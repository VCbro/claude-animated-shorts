## 7.1 Principles of Antimicrobial Therapy

- **Bactericidal** (kill) – **penicillins, cephalosporins, aminoglycosides, fluoroquinolones, vancomycin, metronidazole, rifampicin, INH, cotrimoxazole**; **bacteriostatic** (inhibit growth) – **tetracyclines, macrolides (low dose), chloramphenicol, sulfonamides alone, clindamycin, linezolid**.
- **Mechanisms** – **cell wall synthesis inhibitors** (β-lactams, vancomycin), **protein synthesis** (aminoglycosides, tetracyclines – 30S; macrolides, chloramphenicol, clindamycin, linezolid – 50S), **DNA gyrase** (fluoroquinolones), **folate synthesis** (sulfonamides, trimethoprim), **cell membrane** (polymyxin, amphotericin), **RNA polymerase** (rifampicin).
- **Culture & sensitivity before first dose** (but don't delay in sepsis – **antibiotics within 1 hr in septic shock**); **complete the full course**; correct dose & interval (**maintain blood levels – give on time**); **test dose/ ask allergy history**; **superinfection** (C. difficile – pseudomembranous colitis, oral/ vaginal candidiasis); **antimicrobial resistance (AMR)** – **antimicrobial stewardship**, **Red Line campaign** (India – red vertical line on Rx-only antibiotics), **AWaRe classification (WHO – Access, Watch, Reserve)**.
- **Prophylaxis** – **surgical (cefazolin within 60 min before incision)**, rheumatic fever (benzathine penicillin), meningococcal contacts (rifampicin), PEP.

## 7.2 β-Lactam Antibiotics

| Group | Drugs | Points |
|---|---|---|
| **Natural penicillins** | **Penicillin G (benzylpenicillin – IV/ IM)**, **benzathine penicillin (long-acting depot – IM deep, every 21 days for RF; syphilis 2.4 MU)**, procaine penicillin, penicillin V (oral) | **Penicillin – Alexander Fleming 1928**; **anaphylaxis (most dangerous – test dose, keep adrenaline ready, observe 30 min)**; Jarisch-Herxheimer reaction (syphilis) |
| **Penicillinase-resistant** | **Cloxacillin, flucloxacillin**, methicillin | **Staph aureus** (skin infections) |
| **Aminopenicillins** | **Amoxicillin, ampicillin** | Broad; **rash (esp. in infectious mononucleosis)**, diarrhoea; **amoxicillin + clavulanic acid (β-lactamase inhibitor) – Augmentin** |
| **Antipseudomonal** | **Piperacillin + tazobactam** | Hospital infections |
| **Cephalosporins** – 1st gen | **Cefazolin, cephalexin** | **Surgical prophylaxis**, skin |
| – 2nd gen | Cefuroxime, cefoxitin | RTI |
| – **3rd gen** | **Ceftriaxone (meningitis, typhoid, gonorrhoea; once daily; don't mix with calcium solutions – precipitates in neonates)**, **cefotaxime (neonates)**, **ceftazidime (Pseudomonas)**, **cefixime (oral – typhoid, UTI)**, cefoperazone | – |
| – 4th / 5th gen | **Cefepime** / ceftaroline (MRSA) | – |
| **Carbapenems** | **Meropenem, imipenem (+ cilastatin – seizures)**, ertapenem | **Reserve – ESBL, MDR** |
| **Monobactam** | Aztreonam | Safe in penicillin allergy |

- **Cross-allergy** penicillin ↔ cephalosporins (~1–2 %; avoid if anaphylaxis).
- **Cephalosporins + alcohol** (cefoperazone) – disulfiram-like reaction.

## 7.3 Other Antibacterials

| Drug/ group | Key uses | Key adverse effects / nursing |
|---|---|---|
| **Aminoglycosides – gentamicin, amikacin, streptomycin, tobramycin, neomycin (oral – bowel prep, hepatic coma)** | Gram-negative sepsis, **TB (streptomycin)**, endocarditis (synergy) | **Nephrotoxicity** (monitor creatinine, urine output, hydrate) & **ototoxicity (8th nerve – vestibular & cochlear; irreversible – tinnitus, vertigo, deafness)**, **neuromuscular blockade (apnoea – with anaesthesia/ MgSO₄; myasthenia)**; **once-daily dosing**; **peak & trough levels**; **avoid in pregnancy (fetal deafness)**; **not absorbed orally** |
| **Macrolides – erythromycin, azithromycin, clarithromycin** | **Atypical pneumonia (Mycoplasma), pertussis, diphtheria, chlamydia, penicillin allergy**, H. pylori (clarithro), typhoid & trachoma (azithro) | **GI upset (erythromycin – motilin agonist)**, **QT prolongation**, **enzyme inhibition (↑ warfarin, theophylline, digoxin)**, cholestatic jaundice (erythromycin estolate) |
| **Tetracyclines – doxycycline, tetracycline, minocycline** | **Rickettsia (scrub typhus – DOC), cholera, chlamydia, acne, Lyme, leptospirosis, malaria prophylaxis, brucellosis** | **Teeth discolouration & enamel hypoplasia, bone growth ↓ – avoid < 8 yrs, pregnancy, lactation**; **photosensitivity (sunscreen)**; **oesophageal ulcer – take with full glass of water, stay upright 30 min, not at bedtime**; **chelation – don't take with milk, antacids, iron, calcium** (2 hrs apart); **expired tetracycline – Fanconi syndrome**; superinfection; fatty liver (IV in pregnancy) |
| **Chloramphenicol** | Typhoid (earlier), meningitis (allergy), eye drops | **Gray baby syndrome (neonates)**, **bone marrow suppression – aplastic anaemia (idiosyncratic, fatal)** |
| **Fluoroquinolones – ciprofloxacin, ofloxacin, levofloxacin, moxifloxacin, norfloxacin** | **UTI, typhoid, gonorrhoea, Gram-negative**, **MDR-TB (levo/ moxi)**, traveller's diarrhoea, anthrax | **Tendinitis/ tendon rupture (Achilles – elderly, steroids)**, **arthropathy – avoid in children & pregnancy**, **QT prolongation**, CNS (seizures – with NSAIDs), photosensitivity, dysglycaemia; **chelation with antacids/ iron/ dairy**; ↑ theophylline |
| **Sulfonamides – cotrimoxazole (sulfamethoxazole + trimethoprim)** | **UTI, PCP (Pneumocystis) prophylaxis & Rx in HIV**, toxoplasmosis, nocardia | **SJS/ TEN (rash – stop)**, **crystalluria (drink plenty of fluids ≥ 2 L)**, **kernicterus (neonates, late pregnancy)**, haemolysis (G6PD), bone marrow suppression (folate ↓), hyperkalaemia; photosensitivity |
| **Metronidazole (tinidazole, secnidazole)** | **Anaerobes, amoebiasis (DOC), giardiasis, trichomoniasis, bacterial vaginosis, C. difficile, H. pylori, tetanus**, surgical prophylaxis | **Metallic taste**, nausea, **disulfiram-like reaction with alcohol (avoid alcohol during & 48–72 hrs after)**, dark urine, peripheral neuropathy (prolonged), **↑ warfarin effect** |
| **Nitrofurantoin** | **Uncomplicated UTI** (lower) | **Brown urine**, pulmonary fibrosis, neuropathy; avoid near term (haemolysis), G6PD |
| **Vancomycin** | **MRSA**, **C. difficile (oral)**, endocarditis | **"Red man (vancomycin flushing) syndrome" – infuse slowly over ≥ 60 min**; nephrotoxicity, ototoxicity; **trough levels** |
| **Linezolid** | MRSA, VRE, MDR-TB | Thrombocytopenia, myelosuppression, serotonin syndrome, optic neuropathy |
| **Clindamycin** | Anaerobes, bone, MRSA (skin) | **Pseudomembranous colitis (C. difficile) – report diarrhoea** |
| **Polymyxin B / colistin** | MDR Gram-negative (reserve) | Nephro- & neurotoxicity |
| **Fosfomycin** | UTI (single dose) | – |

## 7.4 Antitubercular & Antileprosy Drugs

| Drug | Key adverse effects |
|---|---|
| **Isoniazid (H)** | **Peripheral neuropathy (give pyridoxine 10–50 mg)**, **hepatitis**, slow acetylators; lupus-like; overdose seizures (pyridoxine antidote) |
| **Rifampicin (R)** | **Orange-red urine, sweat, tears (stains contact lenses)**, **hepatitis**, flu-like syndrome (intermittent), **potent enzyme inducer – OCP failure, ↓ warfarin, ↓ ART (protease inhibitors)**; take empty stomach |
| **Pyrazinamide (Z)** | **Hepatotoxicity (most hepatotoxic)**, **hyperuricaemia – gout/ arthralgia** |
| **Ethambutol (E)** | **Optic (retrobulbar) neuritis – ↓ visual acuity, red-green colour blindness** – check vision; caution in young children |
| **Streptomycin (S)** | **Ototoxicity (vestibular), nephrotoxicity**; avoid in pregnancy |
| **Second-line (MDR-TB)** | **Bedaquiline (QT)**, delamanid, pretomanid, linezolid, moxifloxacin/ levofloxacin, clofazimine, cycloserine (psychosis) |

- **Regimen (NTEP)** – **2HRZE + 4HRE (daily FDC, weight bands)**; **DOT/ 99DOTS**; **Ni-kshay Poshan Yojana ₹1000/month (2024)**; **TPT – INH 6 months/ 3HP**.
- **Leprosy MDT** – **rifampicin (monthly supervised) + clofazimine (skin pigmentation, ichthyosis) + dapsone (haemolysis – G6PD, methaemoglobinaemia, DRESS)**; **PB 6 months, MB 12 months**.

## 7.5 Antifungal Drugs

| Drug | Points |
|---|---|
| **Amphotericin B** | **Systemic fungal infections (DOC – mucormycosis, cryptococcal meningitis), kala-azar**; **infusion reactions – fever, chills, rigors (premedicate – paracetamol, antihistamine, hydrocortisone)**; **nephrotoxicity (most important – hydrate with saline)**, **hypokalaemia, hypomagnesaemia**, anaemia; **liposomal – less toxic**; IV slowly in **5 % dextrose (not saline)**, **protect from light** |
| **Azoles – fluconazole** (candida, cryptococcal), **itraconazole, voriconazole** (aspergillosis – visual disturbances), **ketoconazole** (hepatotoxic, enzyme inhibitor), **posaconazole, isavuconazole** | **Enzyme inhibitors – drug interactions**; hepatotoxicity |
| **Topical azoles – clotrimazole, miconazole** | Skin, oral, vaginal candidiasis |
| **Nystatin** | **Oral thrush (swish & swallow), not absorbed – topical/ GI only** |
| **Griseofulvin** | **Dermatophytes (tinea capitis)**; **take with fatty meal**; long courses; enzyme inducer, photosensitivity, teratogenic |
| **Terbinafine** | **Onychomycosis (nail)**, tinea |
| **Echinocandins – caspofungin, micafungin** | Candidaemia |

| Drug | Use / points |
|---|---|
| **Acyclovir, valacyclovir** | **Herpes simplex, varicella-zoster, HSV encephalitis**; **nephrotoxicity (crystals) – infuse slowly, hydrate** |
| **Ganciclovir, valganciclovir** | **CMV**; bone marrow suppression |

| **Tenofovir (TDF/ TAF), entecavir** | **Hepatitis B**, HIV |
| **Interferon** | HBV/ HCV (old) – flu-like, depression |

### Antiretroviral therapy (ART)
- **Classes** – **NRTIs** (**zidovudine – anaemia, myopathy**; **tenofovir – nephrotoxicity, bone loss**; **lamivudine**, abacavir – hypersensitivity), **NNRTIs** (**nevirapine – rash/ SJS, hepatotoxicity; PPTCT infant prophylaxis**; **efavirenz – CNS (vivid dreams, dizziness), teratogenic (1st trimester)**), **protease inhibitors** (lopinavir/ ritonavir, atazanavir – lipodystrophy, hyperglycaemia, jaundice), **integrase inhibitors** (**dolutegravir – preferred**; raltegravir).
- **First-line (NACO)** – **TLD (Tenofovir + Lamivudine + Dolutegravir)** – single pill daily; **"Test & Treat" – ART for all regardless of CD4 (2017)**.
- **Adherence > 95 %**; **viral load monitoring**; **IRIS (immune reconstitution inflammatory syndrome)**; **PEP – 28 days TLD, start within 72 hrs (ideally 2 hrs)**.
- **Cotrimoxazole prophylaxis**, **INH (TPT)**.

## 7.7 Antimalarial Drugs

| Drug | Points |
|---|---|
| **Chloroquine** | **P. vivax (blood schizonticide) – 25 mg/kg over 3 days**; **pruritus (Africans), retinopathy (long-term – eye checks)**, GI upset, QT; also amoebic liver abscess, RA/ SLE |
| **Primaquine** | **Tissue schizonticide – radical cure of vivax (hypnozoites) – 0.25 mg/kg × 14 days**; **gametocidal – falciparum single dose 0.75 mg/kg**; **haemolysis in G6PD deficiency**; **contraindicated in pregnancy & infants < 1 yr**; give with food |
| **Artemisinins – artesunate, artemether** | **Falciparum (fastest acting)**; **ACT – artesunate + sulfadoxine-pyrimethamine (India, except NE) / artemether-lumefantrine (NE states)**; **injectable artesunate – severe malaria DOC** |
| **Quinine** | Severe malaria (alternative), 1st trimester; **cinchonism (tinnitus, deafness, headache, vertigo)**, **hypoglycaemia (↑ insulin)**, QT; IV slowly in dextrose |
| **Mefloquine** | Prophylaxis; neuropsychiatric effects |
| **Doxycycline** | Prophylaxis (travellers), with quinine |
| **Atovaquone-proguanil** | Prophylaxis |
