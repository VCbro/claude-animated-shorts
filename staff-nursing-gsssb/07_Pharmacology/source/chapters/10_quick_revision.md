## 10.4 Narrow Therapeutic Index & Monitoring

| Drug | Therapeutic level / monitor |
|---|---|
| **Digoxin** | **0.5–2 ng/ml**; apex pulse (hold < 60), K⁺ |
| **Lithium** | **0.6–1.2 mEq/L**; toxic > 1.5 |
| **Phenytoin** | **10–20 µg/ml** |
| **Theophylline** | **10–20 µg/ml** |
| **Warfarin** | **INR 2–3** (2.5–3.5 mechanical valves) |
| **Heparin (UFH)** | **aPTT 1.5–2.5 × control** |
| **Aminoglycosides / vancomycin** | Peak & trough levels, creatinine |
| **Carbamazepine / valproate** | Levels, LFT, CBC |
| **Cyclosporine / tacrolimus** | Trough levels |
| **Insulin** | Blood glucose |
| **MgSO₄** | **4–7 mEq/L**; knee jerk, RR ≥ 12, urine ≥ 30 ml/hr |
| **Potassium (IV)** | Never IV push; ≤ 10 mEq/hr (peripheral) |

- **High-alert drugs (ISMP)** – **insulin, heparin/ anticoagulants, KCl concentrate, opioids, chemotherapy, NM blockers, hypertonic saline (3 %), MgSO₄, IV sedatives, oxytocin (IV), inotropes** – **independent double-check**, labelled & segregated.
- **LASA (look-alike sound-alike)** – tall-man lettering (e.g. hydrOXYzine/ hydrALAZINE).

## 10.6 150 Rapid-Fire One-Liners

:::key 150 one-liners
1. Father of pharmacology – **Oswald Schmiedeberg**; father of modern pharmacology in India – **R. N. Chopra**.
2. Pharmacokinetics – **what body does to drug (ADME)**.
3. Pharmacodynamics – **what drug does to body**.
4. First-pass metabolism avoided by – **sublingual, IV, transdermal, rectal (partly)**.
5. Bioavailability of IV – **100 %**.
6. Main site of metabolism – **liver**; excretion – **kidney**.
7. Half-life – **time for plasma concentration to fall by 50 %**.
8. Steady state – **4–5 half-lives**.
9. Loading dose – **to reach steady state quickly**.
10. Therapeutic index – **TD50/ ED50**; narrow – **digoxin, lithium, warfarin**.
11. Enzyme inducers – **rifampicin, phenytoin, carbamazepine, phenobarbitone, alcohol (chronic), smoking**.
12. Enzyme inhibitors – **cimetidine, erythromycin, ketoconazole, ciprofloxacin, valproate, grapefruit**.
13. Tachyphylaxis – **rapid tolerance (ephedrine, nitrates)**.
14. Idiosyncrasy – **genetically determined abnormal response**.
15. Type I hypersensitivity – **anaphylaxis (IgE)**.
16. Placebo – **inert substance**.
17. Orphan drugs – **for rare diseases**.
18. Schedule H – **prescription-only**; Schedule X – **narcotics/ psychotropics (strict)**.
19. Drugs & Cosmetics Act – **1940**; NDPS Act – **1985**.
20. Pharmacovigilance (India) – **PvPI 2010, IPC Ghaziabad**.
21. Rights of medication – **right patient, drug, dose, route, time + documentation**.
22. Patient identification – **2 identifiers**.
23. IM site of choice adults – **ventrogluteal**; infants – **vastus lateralis**.
24. Deltoid max volume – **1 ml (≤ 2 ml)**.
25. Z-track – **iron dextran**.
26. Intradermal angle – **10–15°**; SC – **45–90°**; IM – **90°**.
27. BCG/ Mantoux – **intradermal**.
28. Insulin & heparin – **subcutaneous**.
29. Heparin site – **abdomen, don't aspirate or massage**.
30. Drops/min = **volume × drop factor / time (min)**.
31. Macro-drip – **15–20 drops/ml**; micro-drip – **60 drops/ml**.
32. 1 grain – **60–65 mg**; 1 tsp – **5 ml**; 1 tbsp – **15 ml**.
33. Young's rule – **age/(age+12) × adult dose**; Clark's – **weight (lb)/150 × adult dose**.
34. Ophthalmic drop – **lower conjunctival sac; press inner canthus 1–2 min**.
35. Ear drops – adult **pinna up & back**; child < 3 yrs **down & back**.
36. Sublingual nitroglycerin – **max 3 tabs, 5 min apart**; store **dark glass bottle**.
37. Cholinergic crisis – **SLUDGE**.
38. Atropine toxicity – **"dry as bone, red as beet, hot as hare, blind as bat, mad as hatter"**.
39. Atropine contraindicated in – **narrow-angle glaucoma, BPH**.
40. Physostigmine – **anticholinergic poisoning**.
41. Neostigmine – **myasthenia, reversal of NM block, post-op urinary retention**.
42. Edrophonium (Tensilon) test – **myasthenia gravis**.
43. Adrenaline in anaphylaxis – **0.5 mg IM thigh (0.01 mg/kg child)**.
44. Dopamine low dose – **renal vasodilation**.
45. Dobutamine – **cardiogenic shock (β1)**.
46. Noradrenaline – **septic shock (first-line)**; extravasation – **phentolamine**.
47. β-blocker contraindicated in – **asthma, heart block, bradycardia**.
48. β-blockers mask – **hypoglycaemia**.
49. Propranolol – **non-selective; migraine, thyrotoxicosis, varices**.
50. ACE inhibitor – **cough (bradykinin)**; contraindicated – **pregnancy, bilateral RAS**.
51. First-dose hypotension – **prazosin, ACEI**.
52. Rebound hypertension – **clonidine (abrupt stop)**.
53. Hydralazine – **lupus-like, reflex tachycardia**.
54. Nitroprusside – **cyanide toxicity; protect from light**.
55. Nitrate tolerance – **nitrate-free interval 10–12 hrs**.
56. Digoxin – **check apex 1 min; hold < 60/min (adult)**.
57. Digoxin toxicity ↑ by – **hypokalaemia**.

59. Amiodarone – **pulmonary fibrosis, thyroid, corneal deposits**.
60. Adenosine – **very short half-life (< 10 sec), rapid IV push + flush**.
61. Furosemide – **loop of Henle; hypokalaemia, ototoxicity**.
62. Spironolactone – **K-sparing, hyperkalaemia, gynaecomastia**.
