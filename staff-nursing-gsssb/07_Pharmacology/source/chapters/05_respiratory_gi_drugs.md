## 5.1 Drugs for Asthma & COPD

| Class | Drugs | Points |
|---|---|---|
| **SABA (short-acting β2 agonist) – reliever** | **Salbutamol**, terbutaline, levalbuterol | **Acute attack – DOC**; inhaled/ nebulised; tremor, tachycardia, palpitation, **hypokalaemia** |
| **LABA** | Salmeterol, formoterol, vilanterol | Maintenance – **always with ICS** in asthma |
| **Anticholinergics** | **Ipratropium (SAMA)**, **tiotropium (LAMA – COPD)** | **COPD – bronchodilator of choice**; dry mouth |
| **Inhaled corticosteroids (ICS) – controller** | **Budesonide, beclomethasone, fluticasone** | **Most effective long-term controller in asthma**; **oral thrush (candidiasis), hoarseness – rinse mouth & gargle after use**, use spacer |
| **Systemic steroids** | **Prednisolone (oral), hydrocortisone, methylprednisolone (IV)** | Acute severe exacerbation |
| **Methylxanthines** | **Theophylline (oral), aminophylline (IV)** | **Narrow TI – therapeutic 10–20 µg/ml** (5–15 now preferred); **toxicity – nausea, vomiting, tachycardia, arrhythmias, seizures**; **aminophylline IV slowly (≥ 20 min)**; **↑ levels – ciprofloxacin, erythromycin, cimetidine; ↓ levels – smoking, rifampicin**; avoid caffeine |
| **Leukotriene antagonists** | **Montelukast** (bedtime), zafirlukast | Aspirin-induced, exercise-induced asthma, allergic rhinitis; neuropsychiatric effects |
| **Mast cell stabilisers** | **Sodium cromoglycate** | Prophylaxis only (not acute) |
| **Anti-IgE / biologics** | Omalizumab, mepolizumab | Severe asthma |
| **MgSO₄ IV** | – | Acute severe asthma |

- **Inhaler order** – **bronchodilator first, wait 5 min, then steroid**.
- **Acute severe asthma** – **O₂, nebulised salbutamol (+ ipratropium), systemic steroids, IV MgSO₄**, (aminophylline).

## 5.2 Cough, Cold & Antihistamines

| Group | Drugs | Points |
|---|---|---|
| **Antitussives (cough suppressants – dry cough)** | **Codeine** (opioid – constipation, dependence), **dextromethorphan**, pholcodine | **Not in productive cough**; codeine avoided < 12 yrs |
| **Expectorants** | **Guaifenesin**, ammonium chloride, potassium iodide | ↑ volume of secretions |
| **Mucolytics** | **Bromhexine, ambroxol, N-acetylcysteine (also paracetamol antidote)**, carbocisteine | Thin sputum |
| **Nasal decongestants** | Oxymetazoline, xylometazoline (topical), pseudoephedrine (oral) | **Rebound congestion (rhinitis medicamentosa) if > 3–5 days** |
| **H1 antihistamines – 1st gen (sedating)** | **Chlorpheniramine (CPM), promethazine, diphenhydramine, hydroxyzine, cyproheptadine** | **Sedation – avoid driving**, anticholinergic (dry mouth, urinary retention), **promethazine – antiemetic, motion sickness**; cyproheptadine – appetite stimulant, serotonin syndrome |
| **H1 – 2nd gen (non-sedating)** | **Cetirizine, levocetirizine, loratadine, fexofenadine**, desloratadine | Allergic rhinitis, urticaria |

## 5.3 Peptic Ulcer & Acid-Related Disorders

| Class | Drugs | Points |
|---|---|---|
| **Antacids** | **Magnesium hydroxide (diarrhoea)**, **aluminium hydroxide (constipation)** – combined to balance; calcium carbonate (constipation, rebound acid, milk-alkali), sodium bicarbonate (systemic alkalosis, CO₂ belching) | **Give 1–3 hrs after meals & at bedtime**; **separate other drugs by 1–2 hrs** (↓ absorption – **tetracyclines, fluoroquinolones, iron, digoxin**); Al – hypophosphataemia; Mg – avoid in renal failure |
| **H2 blockers** | **Ranitidine (withdrawn – NDMA), famotidine**, cimetidine (enzyme inhibitor, gynaecomastia) | Reduce acid |
| **Proton pump inhibitors (PPIs, "-prazole")** | **Omeprazole, pantoprazole, esomeprazole, rabeprazole, lansoprazole** | **Most potent acid suppressants**; **give 30–60 min before breakfast** (empty stomach); GERD, PUD, Zollinger-Ellison, **H. pylori**, stress ulcer prophylaxis, with NSAIDs; long-term – **↓ Mg, B12 deficiency, osteoporosis/ fractures, C. difficile, pneumonia** |
| **Sucralfate** | Coats ulcer base | **Empty stomach (1 hr before meals)**; constipation; separate other drugs |
| **Misoprostol (PGE1)** | Cytoprotective | **NSAID-induced ulcer prevention**; **contraindicated in pregnancy (abortion)**; diarrhoea |
| **Bismuth** | – | Black tongue & stools |

- **H. pylori eradication (triple therapy, 14 days)** – **PPI + clarithromycin + amoxicillin** (or metronidazole); **quadruple** – PPI + bismuth + tetracycline + metronidazole.

## 5.4 Antiemetics

| Class | Drugs | Use / side effects |
|---|---|---|
| **5-HT3 antagonists** | **Ondansetron**, granisetron, palonosetron | **Chemotherapy-induced (DOC), post-op, radiotherapy, gastroenteritis**; headache, constipation, **QT prolongation** |
| **D2 antagonists (prokinetic)** | **Metoclopramide** (crosses BBB), **domperidone** (doesn't cross BBB – fewer EPS) | GERD, gastroparesis, migraine; **metoclopramide – EPS (acute dystonia – esp. young), tardive dyskinesia**; avoid in GI obstruction |
| **Phenothiazines** | **Prochlorperazine**, promethazine, chlorpromazine | Vertigo, nausea; sedation, EPS |
| **Antihistamines/ anticholinergics** | **Promethazine, dimenhydrinate, meclizine, hyoscine** | **Motion sickness** (take 30–60 min before travel) |
| **NK1 antagonists** | **Aprepitant** | Chemo (delayed emesis) |
| **Corticosteroids** | Dexamethasone | Chemo (adjunct) |
| **Doxylamine + pyridoxine** | – | **Nausea & vomiting of pregnancy** |
| **Cannabinoids** | Dronabinol | Chemo |

## 5.5 Laxatives (Purgatives)

| Type | Drugs | Points |
|---|---|---|
| **Bulk-forming** | **Isabgol (psyllium/ ispaghula)**, methylcellulose, bran | **Safest, most physiological**; **take with plenty of water (≥ 1 glass)** – else obstruction; onset 1–3 days |
| **Stool softeners (emollients)** | **Docusate sodium**, liquid paraffin (lubricant) | **Liquid paraffin – lipoid pneumonia (aspiration – avoid at bedtime/ in elderly, children), ↓ fat-soluble vitamins (A, D, E, K) absorption, anal seepage** |
| **Osmotic** | **Lactulose** (**hepatic encephalopathy – ↓ ammonia** – titrate to 2–3 soft stools/day), **magnesium sulphate/ hydroxide (milk of magnesia)**, **polyethylene glycol (PEG – bowel prep)**, sodium phosphate | Dehydration, electrolyte imbalance |
| **Stimulant** | **Bisacodyl, senna, sodium picosulfate**, castor oil | Cramping; chronic use → **cathartic colon**; **bisacodyl – don't chew, avoid with antacids/ milk** |
| **Others** | Lubiprostone, prucalopride | – |

- **Contraindications** – **acute abdomen/ suspected appendicitis, intestinal obstruction**, undiagnosed abdominal pain; **laxative abuse** (eating disorders) → hypokalaemia.
- **Enemas** – **soap water, saline, phosphate (Fleet)**, glycerine suppositories.

## 5.6 Antidiarrhoeals & Others

- **ORS + zinc (mainstay)**, **probiotics**, **racecadotril** (antisecretory – children ok).
- **Loperamide** (opioid – ↓ motility; **not in children < 2–4 yrs, dysentery/ bloody diarrhoea, fever** – toxic megacolon), **diphenoxylate + atropine (Lomotil)**.
- **Antispasmodics** – **dicyclomine, hyoscine butylbromide, drotaverine, mebeverine** (IBS).
- **IBD** – **5-ASA (mesalamine, sulfasalazine – orange urine, oligospermia, folate ↓)**, steroids, azathioprine, biologics (infliximab).
- **Liver** – **lactulose & rifaximin (hepatic encephalopathy)**, ursodeoxycholic acid (gallstones, cholestasis), **propranolol (varices)**, **octreotide/ terlipressin (variceal bleed)**, **vitamin K**.
- **Pancreatic enzymes (pancreatin)** – with meals (cystic fibrosis, chronic pancreatitis).
- **Anorectal** – stool softeners, sitz bath.
