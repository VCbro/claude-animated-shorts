## 8.1 Insulin

- **Discovered – Banting & Best (1921)**; **human/ analogue insulin (recombinant)**; **U-100 (100 units/ml)**.
- **Indications** – **type 1 DM (essential)**, type 2 DM (uncontrolled), **gestational DM (DOC)**, **DKA, HHS** (IV regular), surgery/ critical illness, **hyperkalaemia (with glucose)**.

@fig insulin_profile.svg | Insulin onset, peak & duration – rapid, short, intermediate & long-acting

| Type | Examples | Onset | Peak | Duration | Points |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Rapid-acting** | **Lispro, aspart, glulisine** | **10–15 min** | **~1 hr** | 3–5 hrs | **Give with meal (0–15 min before)** |
| **Short-acting (regular/ soluble)** | **Regular (Actrapid)** – **clear** | **30–60 min** | **2–4 hrs** | 6–8 hrs | **30 min before meals**; **only insulin given IV (DKA)** |
| **Intermediate** | **NPH/ isophane (Insulatard)** – **cloudy** | 1–2 hrs | **4–12 hrs** | 16–24 hrs | Roll gently (don't shake) |
| **Long-acting (basal)** | **Glargine, detemir, degludec** | 1–2 hrs | **No peak** | **~24 hrs (degludec > 40)** | Once daily same time; **don't mix with other insulins** |
| **Premixed** | 30/70 (30 % regular + 70 % NPH), 50/50 | – | Dual | – | Twice daily before meals |

- **Mixing** – **draw CLEAR (regular) before CLOUDY (NPH)** ("**RN – Regular then NPH**"); inject air into NPH first, then regular.
- **Sites** – **abdomen (fastest absorption – except 5 cm around umbilicus)**, thigh, upper arm, buttocks; **rotate within a region** (prevent **lipohypertrophy/ lipodystrophy**); **SC at 90° (45° if thin)**; **don't massage**; insulin pen – hold 10 sec after injection.
- **Storage** – **unopened 2–8 °C (refrigerator, not freezer)**; **in-use vial/ pen at room temperature (< 30 °C) for ~28 days**; protect from heat/ sunlight; **don't use if frozen/ clumped**.
- **Side effects** – **hypoglycaemia (most common & dangerous)**, **weight gain**, lipodystrophy, **hypokalaemia (IV insulin)**, allergy, insulin resistance; **Somogyi effect** (night hypoglycaemia → rebound morning hyperglycaemia – ↓ evening insulin) vs **dawn phenomenon** (early morning ↑ GH/ cortisol – ↑ evening dose/ shift timing).
- **DKA** – **IV regular insulin 0.1 U/kg/hr** (after fluids, K⁺ ≥ 3.3); add dextrose when glucose ~ 250 mg/dl; **monitor K⁺** (insulin drives K⁺ into cells).
- **Sliding scale** – regular insulin by blood glucose readings.

### Hypoglycaemia
- **Blood glucose < 70 mg/dl**; S/S – **adrenergic (sweating – most common, tremor, palpitations, hunger, anxiety)**, **neuroglycopenic (confusion, irritability, blurred vision, slurred speech, seizures, coma)**; **β-blockers mask adrenergic signs (except sweating)**.
- **Conscious – "Rule of 15": 15–20 g fast-acting carbohydrate** (glucose tablets, ½ cup juice, 3–4 tsp sugar, regular soft drink), **recheck after 15 min**, repeat if < 70, then complex carbohydrate snack.
- **Unconscious – 25–50 ml of 50 % dextrose IV (25 % in children; 10 % 2–5 ml/kg in neonates/ children)** or **glucagon 1 mg IM/ SC** (**0.5 mg < 25 kg**); **nothing by mouth**; **sulfonylurea-induced – prolonged (observe 24–72 hrs, octreotide)**.

## 8.2 Oral & Non-Insulin Antidiabetic Drugs

| Class | Drugs | Mechanism | Key points |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Biguanide** | **Metformin** | **↓ hepatic glucose production**, ↑ insulin sensitivity | **First-line T2DM**; **no hypoglycaemia (alone), weight neutral/ loss**; **GI upset (give with meals)**, metallic taste, **B12 deficiency**, **lactic acidosis (rare – stop in renal failure eGFR < 30, contrast, surgery, hypoxia, alcohol)**; PCOS |
| **Sulfonylureas** | **Glibenclamide (glyburide), glimepiride, gliclazide, glipizide** | ↑ insulin release | **Hypoglycaemia (esp. glibenclamide, elderly, renal)**, **weight gain**; give before breakfast; disulfiram-like (chlorpropamide) |
| **Meglitinides** | Repaglinide, nateglinide | ↑ insulin (short) | Take with each meal; skip if meal skipped |
| **DPP-4 inhibitors ("-gliptin")** | **Sitagliptin, vildagliptin, teneligliptin** | ↑ incretin | Weight neutral, low hypo risk; pancreatitis rare |
| **SGLT2 inhibitors ("-gliflozin")** | **Dapagliflozin, empagliflozin, canagliflozin** | **↑ urinary glucose excretion** | **Genital mycotic infections, UTI**, volume depletion, **euglycaemic DKA**; **cardio-renal benefits (HF, CKD)** |
| **Thiazolidinediones** | **Pioglitazone** | ↑ insulin sensitivity (PPAR-γ) | **Fluid retention, weight gain – avoid in heart failure**; bladder cancer, fractures |
| **α-glucosidase inhibitors** | **Acarbose**, voglibose, miglitol | ↓ carbohydrate absorption | **Flatulence, diarrhoea**; take with first bite; treat hypo with **glucose (not sucrose)** |
| **GLP-1 receptor agonists (injectable/ oral)** | **Liraglutide, semaglutide, dulaglutide, exenatide** | ↑ insulin, ↓ glucagon, slow gastric emptying, satiety | **Weight loss**, nausea, pancreatitis; CV benefit |

## 8.3 Thyroid & Antithyroid Drugs

| Drug | Points |
|---|---|
| **Levothyroxine (T4)** | **Hypothyroidism/ myxoedema, cretinism (10–15 µg/kg)**; **take on empty stomach, 30–60 min before breakfast, same time daily**; **separate from iron, calcium, antacids (4 hrs)**; **lifelong**; overdose → hyperthyroid symptoms (palpitations, weight loss, AF, osteoporosis); **start low in elderly/ cardiac patients (angina)**; monitor TSH (6–8 weeks) |
| **Liothyronine (T3)** | Myxoedema coma (IV) |
| **Carbimazole / methimazole** | **Hyperthyroidism (DOC)**; **agranulocytosis – report sore throat & fever (stop drug, check WBC)**, rash; **teratogenic (aplasia cutis) – avoid 1st trimester** |
| **Propylthiouracil (PTU)** | **Thyroid storm & 1st trimester pregnancy**; ↓ T4 → T3 conversion; **hepatotoxicity** |
| **Radioactive iodine (I-131)** | Definitive Rx (Graves', toxic nodule); **contraindicated in pregnancy & lactation**; **hypothyroidism (common late)**; isolation precautions, avoid close contact for days |
| **Lugol's iodine / potassium iodide** | **Pre-operative (↓ vascularity of gland, 10 days)**, thyroid storm (after thionamide), **radiation emergency (protects thyroid)** |
| **Propranolol** | Symptom control (tremor, tachycardia) |

## 8.4 Corticosteroids

- **Glucocorticoids** – **hydrocortisone** (short, + mineralocorticoid – adrenal crisis), **prednisolone** (intermediate, oral), **methylprednisolone** (IV pulse), **dexamethasone & betamethasone** (long-acting, potent, no mineralocorticoid – **cerebral oedema, fetal lung maturity**, antiemetic); **fludrocortisone** (mineralocorticoid – Addison's).
- **Uses** – inflammation/ allergy (**anaphylaxis adjunct, asthma**, urticaria), autoimmune (SLE, RA, IBD), **nephrotic syndrome**, organ transplant, cancer (lymphoma, ALL), **adrenal insufficiency (replacement)**, **cerebral oedema (dexamethasone)**, **antenatal (lung maturity)**, shock (septic – hydrocortisone), skin (topical), eye.
- **Adverse effects (long-term) – "CUSHINGOID"** – **Cushingoid features (moon face, buffalo hump, truncal obesity, striae)**, **hyperglycaemia (steroid diabetes)**, **hypertension, fluid retention**, **hypokalaemia**, **osteoporosis & fractures, avascular necrosis**, **immunosuppression – infections (TB reactivation, fungal), delayed wound healing**, **peptic ulcer (with NSAIDs)**, **cataract, glaucoma**, **growth suppression (children)**, myopathy, **psychosis/ mood changes/ insomnia**, skin thinning, acne, hirsutism, **HPA axis suppression → adrenal crisis on abrupt withdrawal**.
- **Nursing/ teaching** – **give in the morning (mimic diurnal rhythm) with food/ milk** (GI), **never stop abruptly – taper**, **steroid card**, **stress dose ↑** (surgery, illness), monitor **BP, weight, glucose, K⁺**, infection signs, eye check, calcium + vitamin D, low-salt high-protein diet, **avoid live vaccines** (high dose); **ICS – rinse mouth**.
- **Adrenal crisis** – hypotension, shock, hypoglycaemia → **IV hydrocortisone 100 mg + NS/ dextrose**.

## 8.5 Sex Hormones, Contraceptives & Uterine Drugs

- **Oestrogens** (estradiol, ethinylestradiol) – HRT, contraception; **DVT/ PE, breast & endometrial cancer (unopposed), stroke**, nausea, breast tenderness.
- **Progestins** (medroxyprogesterone, levonorgestrel, norethisterone) – contraception, AUB, endometriosis.
- **Combined OCP** (Mala-N) – **inhibits ovulation**; **contraindications – smoker > 35 yrs, DVT history, breast cancer, liver disease, migraine with aura, uncontrolled HTN, < 6 months postpartum breastfeeding**; **warning signs "ACHES"** – Abdominal pain, Chest pain, Headache (severe), Eye (visual) problems, Severe leg pain; **missed pill** – take ASAP; **enzyme inducers (rifampicin, phenytoin, carbamazepine) & some antibiotics ↓ efficacy**.
- **Emergency contraception** – **levonorgestrel 1.5 mg within 72 hrs**; **ulipristal**; Cu-IUD within 5 days.
- **Antiprogestin** – **mifepristone (RU-486) – medical abortion with misoprostol**.
- **Centchroman (ormeloxifene – Chhaya)** – SERM, weekly.
- **Clomiphene citrate, letrozole** – ovulation induction (PCOS).
- **Tamoxifen** – **SERM – breast cancer (ER+)**; **endometrial cancer, DVT, hot flushes**. **Raloxifene** – osteoporosis.
- **Testosterone** – hypogonadism; **finasteride (5α-reductase inhibitor) – BPH**, male-pattern baldness; teratogenic – pregnant women shouldn't handle crushed tablets.
- **Uterine stimulants (oxytocics)** – **oxytocin** (induction, PPH – water intoxication), **methylergometrine** (PPH – contraindicated HTN), **carboprost** (PGF2α – asthma contraindicated), **misoprostol** (PGE1), **dinoprostone** (PGE2 – cervical ripening).
- **Tocolytics (uterine relaxants)** – **nifedipine (first-line)**, **terbutaline/ ritodrine (β2)**, atosiban, indomethacin, **MgSO₄** (neuroprotection).
- **Antenatal steroids** – dexamethasone 6 mg IM × 4 (12-hrly) / betamethasone 12 mg × 2.

## 8.6 Pituitary, Bone & Other Hormones

| Drug | Use / point |
|---|---|
| **Vasopressin (ADH)/ desmopressin (DDAVP)** | **Diabetes insipidus (DOC – desmopressin intranasal/ oral)**, **nocturnal enuresis**, **haemophilia A (mild)/ von Willebrand**, variceal bleeding (vasopressin/ terlipressin); **hyponatraemia, water intoxication** |
| **Octreotide** (somatostatin analogue) | **Acromegaly, carcinoid, variceal bleeding**, sulfonylurea overdose |
| **Growth hormone (somatropin)** | GH deficiency |
| **Bromocriptine, cabergoline** | **Hyperprolactinaemia**, suppress lactation, acromegaly, Parkinson's |
| **Bisphosphonates – alendronate (weekly), zoledronic acid (yearly IV)** | **Osteoporosis, Paget's, hypercalcaemia of malignancy**; **alendronate – empty stomach, with full glass of plain water, stay upright 30 min (oesophagitis)**; osteonecrosis of jaw |
| **Calcium + vitamin D (cholecalciferol)** | Osteoporosis, rickets; calcitriol in CKD |
| **Calcitonin** | Hypercalcaemia, Paget's |
| **Gout – allopurinol** (xanthine oxidase inhibitor – **prophylaxis; not in acute attack; SJS (HLA-B*5801); fluids**), **febuxostat**, **colchicine (acute – diarrhoea)**, NSAIDs (acute), **probenecid** (uricosuric), **rasburicase** (tumour lysis) | – |
