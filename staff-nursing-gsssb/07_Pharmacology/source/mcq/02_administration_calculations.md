:::mcq Level 3 – Advanced (NOT / Match / Assertion-Reason / Calculation)
28. Heparin 25,000 units in 500 ml; order 1000 units/hr – ml/hr –
 (a) 10 (b) 20 (c) 25 (d) 40
29. Assertion (A): Insulin aur heparin site par massage nahi karna chahiye. Reason (R): Massage se absorption change hota hai aur heparin me haematoma ban sakta hai.
 (a) Both A and R true, R explains A (b) Both A and R true, R does not explain A (c) A true, R false (d) A false, R true
30. Medication administration ke rights me NAHI aata –
 (a) Right documentation (b) Right route (c) Right price (d) Right time
:::

:::ans Answer Key & Explanation
**1-b** 2 identifiers (naam + UHID/ DOB) – room number nahi.
**2-b** Ventrogluteal – nerves/ vessels se door.
**3-a** Vastus lateralis.
**4-a** Deltoid – 1 ml (max ~2 ml).
**5-a** ID – 10–15°.
**6-d** IM – 90°.
**7-d** Mantoux 0.1 ml ID.
**8-a** Z-track – iron dextran (skin staining rokta hai).
**9-c** Heparin – na aspirate, na massage (haematoma).
**10-d** STAT – turant.
**11-c** PRN – zarurat pe.
**12-c** HS – hora somni, bedtime.
**13-a** Lower conjunctival sac; phir inner canthus dabana.
**14-a** Adult – up & back.
**15-c** < 3 yrs – down & back.
**16-b** 5 ml.
**17-c** 15 ml.
**18-d** Micro-drip 60.
**19-b** Light & moisture se kharab – dark glass.
**20-c** Pehle patient safety – assess, inform, phir incident report.
**21-b** 1000 × 15 / 480 = 31.25 ≈ 31.
**22-c** 500 × 20 / 240 = 41.7 ≈ 42.
**23-b** 250/500 × 2 = 1 ml.
**24-b** 40 × 15 = 600 mg/day ÷ 2 = 300 mg.
**25-b** 6/(6+12) × 300 = 100 mg.
**26-c** Microdrip me ml/hr = drops/min.
**27-b** Patient ki shanka par ruk kar verify karo.
**28-b** 25,000/500 = 50 units/ml → 1000/50 = 20 ml/hr.
**29-a** Dono sahi, R reason hai.
**30-c** "Right price" koi right nahi.
:::
