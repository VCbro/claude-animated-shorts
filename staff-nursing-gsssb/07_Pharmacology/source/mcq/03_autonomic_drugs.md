**4-a** Pilocarpine – miosis, ↑ aqueous outflow.
**5-a** Atropine (muscarinic) + pralidoxime (enzyme reactivator).
**6-b** SLUDGE + miosis + bradycardia + bronchorrhoea.
**7-b** Mydriasis, tachycardia, dry secretions.
**8-b** Angle closure badh sakta hai.
**9-b** Physostigmine.
**10-b** 0.5 mg IM anterolateral thigh.
**11-c** 1 mg IV 1:10,000.
**12-b** Low dose – D1 renal vasodilation.
**13-c** Noradrenaline.
**14-b** Dobutamine – β1.
**15-c** Phentolamine infiltration.
**16-b** Propranolol.
**17-a** β2 block – bronchospasm.
**18-a** Tamsulosin – uroselective.
**19-b** Bedtime par pehli dose.
**20-d** Atropine 1 mg IV (latest ACLS).
**21-d** Atropinisation end-point.
**22-b** β-blocker adrenergic signs mask karta hai (sweating nahi).
**23-c** Apical pulse < 60 / low BP – hold.
**24-a** Excess secretions = cholinergic crisis.
**25-a** Cholinergic drugs bladder contract karte hain.
**26-c** Antisialagogue + vagolytic.
**27-a** Rebound – taper karna.
**28-d** Atropine se dry mouth hota hai, salivation nahi.
**29-a** Dono sahi, R reason hai.
**30-c** 1 g/1000 ml = 1 mg/ml.
