:::ans Answer Key & Explanation
**1-a** Cervical dilatation – true labour ka hallmark.
**2-a** Onset to full (10 cm) dilatation.
**3-c** Full dilatation to baby birth.
**4-c** 5–15 min; > 30 min retained placenta.
**5-b** 1 hr observation (PPH ka risk).
**6-c** Primi ~12 hrs, multi ~6 hrs.
**7-b** Engagement.
**8-b** Pelvic floor (levator ani) par.
**9-b** Crowning – head peeche nahi jata.
**10-c** WHO 2018 – 5 cm (LCG); India partograph – 4 cm.
**11-b** 1 cm/hr.
**12-c** 4 hrs.
**13-c** Fifths palpable "O".
**14-d** Every 30 min.
**15-a** Every 5 min.
**16-c** Every 4 hrs.
**17-c** 10 IU IM within 1 min.
**18-c** Mediolateral.
**19-c** Separation par uterus firm & globular hota hai.
**20-d** Alert line cross – inform, prepare referral.
**21-d** Delayed cord clamping 1–3 min.
**22-d** Full dilatation + urge.
**23-d** Brandt-Andrews – counter-traction (inversion se bachav).
**24-d** Tone, bleeding, vitals.
**25-a** Full bladder – descent rokta, atony.
**26-a** Standard order.
**27-d** 1st skin/ mucosa, 2nd muscles, 3rd sphincter, 4th rectal mucosa.
**28-a** Dono sahi, R reason hai.
**29-c** 4 cm + 4 hrs × 1 cm = 8 cm.
**30-b** Fundal pressure AMTSL ka part nahi – inversion risk.
