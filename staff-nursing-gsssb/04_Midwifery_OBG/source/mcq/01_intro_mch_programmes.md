:::ans Answer Key & Explanation
**1-c** William Smellie – Father of British midwifery/ obstetrics.
**2-d** Ignaz Semmelweis – chlorinated lime hand washing (1847).
**3-d** MMR = maternal deaths per 1,00,000 live births.
**4-c** PPH sabse common direct cause.
**5-b** IMR – per 1000 live births.
**6-b** JSY – 12 April 2005 (NRHM).
**7-c** JSSK – June 2011.
**8-b** PMSMA – har mahine 9 tarikh.
**9-b** Primigravida – first pregnancy.
**10-c** Grand multipara ≥ 5 deliveries.
**11-a** Nulligravida – kabhi pregnant nahi.
**12-c** 11 April (Kasturba Gandhi jayanti).
**13-c** Puerperium – 6 weeks.
**14-c** LaQshya (2017) – labour room quality.
**15-c** PMMVY – ₹ 5,000 pehle bachche ke liye.
**16-c** India me viability 28 wks (WHO/ new – 24 wks bhi likha jata hai; GSSSB me 28).
**17-a** 1–7 August.
**18-c** Chiranjeevi Yojana – private obstetricians ke saath PPP model.
**19-c** G3 (current incl.), T1, P0, A1, L1.
**20-b** Multiple pregnancy parity me 1 hi count hoti hai.
**21-c** JSSK – sab cashless entitlements.
**22-a** 28 wks – preterm (< 37 wks).
**23-d** SUMAN (2019) – zero preventable deaths.
**24-b** ASHA – motivator & escort.
**25-a** Accidental/ incidental cause maternal death nahi.
**26-a** Accidental causes exclude hote hain.
**27-c** (36 ÷ 40,000) × 1,00,000 = 90.
**28-b** JSY cash, JSSK entitlements, PMSMA 9th, PMMVY maternity benefit.
**29-a** Dono sahi, R reason hai.
**30-c** Nullipara – jisne kabhi viable baby deliver nahi kiya (pregnant ho chuki ho sakti hai).
