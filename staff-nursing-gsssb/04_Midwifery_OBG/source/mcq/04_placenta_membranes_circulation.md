:::ans Answer Key & Explanation
**1-b** ~500 g.
**2-a** 1 : 6.
**3-c** 2 arteries (deoxygenated), 1 vein (oxygenated).
**4-b** ~50 cm.
**5-c** Wharton's jelly.
**6-d** hCG – peak 8–10 wks.
**7-d** hPL (human placental lactogen).
**8-b** IgG – passive immunity.
**9-b** ~800 ml (peak 1000 ml at 36–38 wks).
**10-c** > 2000 ml.
**11-a** AFI < 5 cm.
**12-c** Liquor alkaline → blue.
**13-b** Umbilical vein (~80 % saturation).
**14-a** Pulmonary artery → aorta (lungs bypass).
**15-a** Fossa ovalis.
**16-a** Ligamentum venosum.
**17-a** Membranes par – vasa praevia risk.
**18-d** Battledore – marginal insertion.
**19-a** Meconium – fetal distress possibility – FHS & inform.
**20-c** Sudden decompression – cord prolapse & abruption.
**21-a** Fetal urine nahi – oligohydramnios.
**22-c** Fetus fluid swallow nahi kar pata.
**23-d** Incomplete placenta – PPH/ sepsis.
**24-c** RBCs fetus khud banata hai (liver, spleen, marrow).
**25-c** SUA – associated anomalies ka risk.
**26-a** Sahi remnants.
**27-a** Dono sahi, R reason hai.
**28-b** Accreta < increta < percreta depth.
**29-b** Oxygen placenta deta hai, liquor nahi.
**30-d** 4+5+6+5 = 20 cm – normal (5–25).
