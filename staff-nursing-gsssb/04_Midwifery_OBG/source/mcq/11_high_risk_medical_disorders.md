# High-Risk Pregnancy & Medical Disorders

:::mcq Level 1 – Direct
1. Pregnancy me anaemia (WHO) –
 (a) Hb < 12 (b) Hb < 10 (c) Hb < 7 (d) Hb < 11 g/dL
2. Pregnancy me anaemia ka sabse common cause –
 (a) B12 (b) Folate deficiency (c) Sickle cell (d) Iron deficiency
3. Pre-eclampsia –
 (a) Sirf oedema (b) Fits + HTN (c) 20 wks ke baad HTN + proteinuria (d) 20 wks se pehle HTN
4. Eclampsia ka matlab –
 (a) Hypoglycaemia (b) Pre-eclampsia + convulsions (c) Epilepsy (d) Only HTN
5. Eclampsia me drug of choice –
 (a) Magnesium sulphate (b) Phenytoin (c) Phenobarbitone (d) Diazepam
6. Pritchard regimen loading dose –
 (a) 1 g IM (b) 2 g IV (c) 4 g IV + 10 g IM (5 g each buttock) (d) 10 g IV
7. MgSO₄ toxicity ka antidote –
 (a) Atropine (b) Vitamin K (c) Naloxone (d) 10 % calcium gluconate 10 ml IV
8. MgSO₄ ki next dose se pehle check karte hain –
 (a) Knee jerk present, RR ≥ 16/min, urine ≥ 30 ml/hr (b) Only BP (c) Only FHS (d) Temperature
9. Toxicity me sabse pehle kya gayab hota hai?
 (a) Heart beat (b) Respiration (c) Patellar (knee) reflex (d) Consciousness
10. Pregnancy me antihypertensive first line –
 (a) Enalapril (b) Atenolol (c) Losartan (d) Labetalol
11. HELLP syndrome me "H" –
 (a) Hypertension (b) Haemorrhage (c) Haemolysis (d) Hyperglycaemia
12. GDM ka drug of choice –
 (a) Insulin (b) Sulphonylurea (c) Glibenclamide (d) Acarbose
13. Diabetic mother ke newborn me sabse common problem –
 (a) Hypercalcaemia (b) Hypoglycaemia (c) Hyperglycaemia (d) Anaemia
14. Rh isoimmunisation tab hota hai jab –
 (a) Mother Rh –ve, fetus Rh +ve (b) Mother Rh +ve, fetus Rh –ve (c) Dono Rh +ve (d) Dono Rh –ve
15. Anti-D dose delivery ke baad –
 (a) 50 µg IV (b) 300 µg IM within 72 hrs (c) 1000 µg (d) After 7 days
16. Twins me sabse common complication –
 (a) Preterm labour (b) Macrosomia (c) Post-term (d) Oligohydramnios
17. Hyperemesis gravidarum me dextrose se pehle kya dete hain (Wernicke rokne ke liye)?
 (a) Iron (b) Calcium (c) Thiamine (vitamin B1) (d) Vitamin C
18. IUD me X-ray sign (overlapping skull bones) –
 (a) Kehr's sign (b) Spalding's sign (c) Hegar's sign (d) Robert's sign
:::

:::mcq Level 2 – Priority & Application
19. Eclamptic fit ke dauran nurse ka pehla action –
 (a) Mouth me spoon dalna (b) Restrain karna (c) Left lateral, airway clear, injury se bachav, help bulana (d) Oral fluids
20. MgSO₄ par patient ka RR 10/min aur knee jerk absent. Nurse –
 (a) MgSO₄ rok do, calcium gluconate ready, doctor inform (b) Dose double (c) Ignore (d) Next dose de do
21. Severe pre-eclampsia ka sign –
 (a) BP 130/80 (b) Weight gain 1 kg/ month (c) Mild ankle oedema (d) BP ≥ 160/110, headache, visual disturbance, epigastric pain, oliguria
22. Heart disease wali pregnant woman me sabse critical period –
 (a) Delivery ke turant baad (b) 6 months postpartum (c) 1st trimester (d) 2nd trimester
23. Heart disease wali mother me 3rd stage me kaunsa drug avoid?
 (a) Antibiotic (b) Oxytocin (c) Methylergometrine (d) Paracetamol
24. Heart disease mother ki labour me position –
 (a) Prone (b) Propped up/ semi-Fowler's (c) Trendelenburg (d) Supine flat
25. Rh –ve mother ka ICT (indirect Coombs) –ve hai. 28 wks par –
 (a) Antenatal anti-D prophylaxis (b) Kuch nahi (c) Termination (d) Exchange transfusion
:::

:::mcq Level 3 – Advanced (NOT / Match / Assertion-Reason / Calculation)
26. MgSO₄ toxicity signs ka sahi order (pehle se baad) –
 (a) Loss of patellar reflex → respiratory depression → cardiac arrest (b) Respiratory depression → reflex loss (c) Cardiac arrest → reflex loss (d) Sab ek saath
27. Match: P. Hydatidiform mole  Q. Pre-eclampsia before 20 wks  R. Polyhydramnios  S. Macrosomia — with 1. Oesophageal atresia 2. Diabetes 3. Mole 4. Snowstorm
 (a) P-4, Q-3, R-1, S-2 (b) P-3, Q-4, R-1, S-2 (c) P-4, Q-1, R-3, S-2 (d) P-2, Q-3, R-1, S-4
28. Assertion (A): ACE inhibitors pregnancy me contraindicated hain. Reason (R): Ye fetal renal failure aur oligohydramnios karte hain.
 (a) Both A and R true, R explains A (b) Both A and R true, R does not explain A (c) A true, R false (d) A false, R true
29. Pre-eclampsia ka risk factor NAHI hai –
 (a) Twins (b) Second pregnancy, same partner, pichli pregnancy normal (c) Primigravida (d) Chronic HTN / diabetes
30. GDM screening (DIPSI) result 2 hr = 152 mg/dl. Interpretation –
 (a) Overt diabetes (b) GDM (c) Hypoglycaemia (d) Normal

:::ans Answer Key & Explanation
**1-d** Hb < 11 g/dL (severe < 7).
**2-d** Iron deficiency.
**3-c** HTN (≥ 140/90) + proteinuria after 20 wks.
**4-b** Pre-eclampsia + convulsions.
**5-a** MgSO₄.
**6-c** 4 g IV (20 %) + 10 g IM (50 %).
**7-d** Calcium gluconate 10 ml of 10 % over 10 min.
**8-a** Teeno criteria.
**9-c** Patellar reflex 7–10 mEq/L par jata hai.
**10-d** Labetalol.
**11-c** Haemolysis, Elevated Liver enzymes, Low Platelets.
**12-a** Insulin – placenta cross nahi karta.
**13-b** Neonatal hypoglycaemia (fetal hyperinsulinaemia).
**14-a** Rh –ve mother, Rh +ve fetus.
**15-b** 300 µg within 72 hrs.
**16-a** Preterm labour.
**17-c** Thiamine – Wernicke's encephalopathy prevention.
**18-b** Spalding's sign.
**19-c** Left lateral, airway, safety.
**20-a** Toxicity – stop & antidote.
**21-d** Severe features.
**22-a** Immediately postpartum – autotransfusion.
**23-c** Methergine – sudden ↑ venous return → failure.
**24-b** Propped up.
**25-a** 28 wks antenatal anti-D (non-sensitised).
**26-a** Reflex loss → respiratory → cardiac.
**27-a** Mole – snowstorm; early pre-eclampsia – mole; polyhydramnios – oesophageal atresia; macrosomia – diabetes.
**28-a** Dono sahi, R reason hai.
**29-b** Same partner ke saath pichli normal pregnancy ke baad risk kam hota hai.
**30-b** ≥ 140 = GDM.
