## 18.1 Basics

- **Family planning (WHO)** – "A way of thinking and living adopted **voluntarily** upon the basis of knowledge, attitudes & responsible decisions by individuals and couples, to promote health & welfare of family…".
- India – **first country to launch a National Family Planning Programme (1952)**; now part of **RMNCH+A (Family Planning 2020 → FP 2030 commitments)**.
- **Eligible couple** – currently married couple, **wife aged 15–45 yrs** (reproductive age). **Couple protection rate (CPR)** – % of eligible couples protected by any method; **NFHS-5: CPR ~67 % (any method)**, modern methods ~56 %; **female sterilization most used (~38 %)**.
- **TFR India 2.0 (NFHS-5)**; Gujarat ~1.9; replacement level **2.1**.
- **Birth spacing** – **at least 2–3 years (≥ 24 months birth-to-pregnancy interval – WHO)**; after abortion ≥ 6 months.
- **Pearl Index** – **failures per 100 woman-years (HWY) of exposure** = (Number of accidental pregnancies × 1200) ÷ total months of exposure (**× 1300 if counted in 13 cycles**). Lower = more effective.
- **Ideal contraceptive**: safe, effective, reversible, cheap, acceptable, independent of coitus, long-acting, easy to use.

## 18.3 Barrier Methods

- **Condom (male)** – latex, **Nirodh (1963)**; **only method protecting against STI/ HIV** (+ female condom); Pearl index **2–14 (typical ~13)**; use new each act, check expiry, leave space at tip, hold base while withdrawing; **no oil-based lubricants** (latex damage).
- **Female condom (Femidom)** – polyurethane; **diaphragm** (with spermicide, keep **6 hrs after intercourse**, max 24 hrs; toxic shock), **cervical cap**, **vaginal sponge (Today)**.
- **Spermicides** – nonoxynol-9 (foam, jelly, tablet) – may ↑ HIV risk (mucosal irritation) if used frequently.

## 18.4 Intrauterine Contraceptive Devices (IUCD)

- **Generations**: **1st – inert (Lippes loop – double S, polyethylene)**; **2nd – copper (Cu-T 200, Cu-7, Cu-T 380A, Multiload 250/ 375, Nova-T)**; **3rd – hormone-releasing (Progestasert – progesterone, LNG-IUS/ Mirena – levonorgestrel 20 µg/day, 5 yrs)**.
- **Cu-T 380A – 10 years**; **Cu 375 (Multiload) – 5 years** (GoI). Copper → **sterile inflammatory reaction, toxic to sperm & ovum, prevents fertilization/ implantation**.
- **Timing of insertion**: **within 12 days (10–12) of start of menstruation (interval IUCD)** (os open, less pain, not pregnant), **PPIUCD – within 10 min of placenta (post-placental) / within 48 hrs of delivery / during CS (intra-caesarean)**, or **after 6 weeks**; **PAIUCD – immediately after abortion (within 12 days)**; **emergency contraception – within 5 days (120 hrs) of unprotected intercourse** (most effective EC).
- **Contraindications** (absolute): **suspected pregnancy**, **PID (current/ recent)**, **unexplained vaginal bleeding**, **cancer of genital tract**, **uterine malformations/ fibroids distorting cavity**, **STI**, **Wilson's disease (copper)**, **postpartum sepsis**, **trophoblastic disease** (persistent hCG).
- **Side effects/ complications**: **heavy/ prolonged menstrual bleeding (most common reason for removal) & pain/ dysmenorrhoea**, **spontaneous expulsion (most common in 1st year; first 3 months/ during menses)**, **PID**, **uterine perforation** (rare; at insertion), **ectopic pregnancy (if failure)**, pregnancy with IUCD in situ (remove if threads visible – abortion risk), missing threads.
- **Instructions**: **check thread after each menstruation** (for expulsion), **follow-up – after first period (3–6 wks)**, then **3 months**, then yearly; report **"PAINS"** – **P**eriod late/ abnormal spotting, **A**bdominal pain/ dyspareunia, **I**nfection exposure/ abnormal discharge, **N**ot feeling well/ fever/ chills, **S**tring missing/ shorter/ longer.
- **Ideal IUCD candidate** – parous woman, monogamous, normal menses, wants long-term reversible spacing, no PID history.

## 18.5 Hormonal Contraceptives

### Combined oral contraceptive pills (COC)
- **Mala-N (Govt. – free)** / Mala-D (social marketing): **Levonorgestrel 0.15 mg + Ethinyl oestradiol 0.03 mg** – **21 hormonal tablets + 7 iron (brown) tablets (ferrous fumarate 60 mg)** = 28.
- **Mechanism** – **inhibit ovulation (main – suppress FSH & LH)**, thick cervical mucus, endometrium unsuitable.
- **Start on day 1–5 of menstrual cycle**, **1 tablet daily at same time**; new pack immediately after 28.
- **Missed pill**: **1 pill missed** → take **as soon as remembered** (even 2 in one day) & continue; **≥ 2 pills missed** → take one ASAP, continue + **backup (condom) for 7 days**; if missed in last week of hormonal pills → skip iron tablets, start new pack; consider **ECP** if intercourse in previous 5 days.
- **Pearl index ~0.1–0.3 (perfect)**, typical ~7.
- **Benefits (non-contraceptive)**: regular, lighter, less painful periods, **↓ iron deficiency anaemia**, **↓ ovarian & endometrial cancer**, ↓ ectopic, ↓ PID, ↓ benign breast disease, ↓ functional ovarian cysts, acne improvement.
- **Side effects**: nausea, breast tenderness, **breakthrough bleeding/ spotting**, headache, weight gain, mood changes, **hypertension**, **thromboembolism (DVT/ stroke/ MI)**, **↓ lactation**, cholestatic jaundice.
- **Contraindications (absolute)**: **pregnancy**, **breastfeeding < 6 months** (WHO: < 6 wks absolute; 6 wks–6 months generally not), **thromboembolism/ history of DVT, stroke, IHD**, **hypertension (≥ 160/100)**, **smoker ≥ 35 yrs (≥ 15 cig/day)**, **migraine with aura**, **breast cancer**, **liver disease (active hepatitis, tumour)**, **undiagnosed vaginal bleeding**, diabetes with vascular complications, major surgery with immobilization; drug interaction – **rifampicin, anticonvulsants** (↓ efficacy).
- **Warning signs – "ACHES"**: **A**bdominal pain (severe), **C**hest pain/ breathlessness, **H**eadache (severe), **E**ye problems (blurred vision/ loss), **S**evere leg pain (calf) → stop & report.

### Other hormonal methods
- **Progestin-only pill (POP/ minipill)** – **lactating women** (from 6 wks), **taken daily without break, same time (3-hr window)**; s/e – irregular bleeding.
- **Centchroman (Ormeloxifene – "Chhaya", earlier Saheli)** – **non-steroidal, non-hormonal SERM** (developed by **CDRI Lucknow, India**); **30 mg – twice a week for first 3 months, then once a week**; first pill on **first day of period**; **safe in lactation**; s/e – **delayed periods (oligomenorrhoea)**; no nausea, no weight gain.
- **Injectable – DMPA (Depot medroxyprogesterone acetate – "Antara" programme)** – **150 mg deep IM every 3 months** (grace period 4 wks early/ late; 2 wks early to 4 wks late); safe in lactation (from 6 wks); s/e – **menstrual irregularity/ amenorrhoea**, **delayed return of fertility (~7–10 months)**, weight gain, **↓ bone density (long-term)**. NET-EN (2-monthly), monthly combined injectables (Cyclofem).
- **Implants** – **Jadelle (2 rods LNG – 5 yrs)**, **Nexplanon/ Implanon (single rod etonogestrel – 3 yrs)** – upper arm subdermal; most effective reversible.
- **Vaginal ring (NuvaRing – 3 wks)**, **transdermal patch (Ortho Evra – weekly)**.

### Emergency contraception (EC)
- **Levonorgestrel 1.5 mg single dose (E-pill/ "i-pill")** (or 0.75 mg × 2, 12 hrs apart) **within 72 hrs** (best within 24 hrs; effective up to 120 hrs with lower efficacy) – prevents/ delays ovulation; **not an abortifacient**; **Cu-IUCD within 5 days (most effective EC, 99 %)**; **Ulipristal acetate 30 mg** (within 120 hrs); **Yuzpe method** (combined pills).
- Indications – unprotected intercourse, condom burst, missed pills, **rape**; **not for regular use**.

## 18.6 Natural Methods & LAM

- **Calendar/ rhythm (Ogino-Knaus)**: **first fertile day = shortest cycle − 18**; **last fertile day = longest cycle − 11**; **Standard Days Method (CycleBeads)** – cycles 26–32 days – **avoid days 8–19**.
- **BBT** – rise **0.3–0.5 °C** after ovulation → safe after 3 days of elevated temperature. **Cervical mucus/ Billings method** – fertile when mucus clear, slippery, stretchy (spinnbarkeit). **Symptothermal** – BBT + mucus + calendar.
- **Coitus interruptus (withdrawal)** – oldest method; high failure (pre-ejaculatory fluid).
- **LAM (Lactational Amenorrhoea Method)** – effective (**98 %**) only if **all 3 criteria**: (1) **exclusive/ nearly exclusive breastfeeding (day & night)**, (2) **amenorrhoea (no return of periods)**, (3) **baby < 6 months**. If any one fails → other method.

## 18.7 Permanent Methods (Sterilization)

| | **Tubectomy (female)** | **Vasectomy (male)** |
|---|---|---|
| Procedure | **Minilap** (2–3 cm suprapubic incision – **Pomeroy's technique** – loop ligated with absorbable suture & excised – most common), **Laparoscopic (Falope ring/ Yoon's ring, clips)** | **Conventional (incision)** / **No-Scalpel Vasectomy (NSV – 1974, China; Dr Li Shunqiang)** – vas deferens divided & ligated |
| Site | **Isthmus** of fallopian tube | Vas deferens (scrotum) |
| Timing | **Interval** (within 7 days of menses – after ruling out pregnancy), **postpartum (within 7 days; best 24–48 hrs – minilap)**, **with LSCS**, **post-abortion**; **laparoscopic – interval or after 6 wks postpartum/ with 1st trimester MTP** (not postpartum – enlarged uterus) | Any time |
| Anaesthesia | Local + sedation / spinal | Local |
| Effectiveness | Immediate; failure ~0.5 % | **Not immediately effective** – sperm stored in seminal vesicles → **use condom for 3 months (or ~20 ejaculations) till semen analysis shows azoospermia (2 consecutive)** |
| Complications | Anaesthesia, bleeding, infection, bowel/ bladder injury, **failure → ectopic**, regret | Haematoma, infection, sperm granuloma, **no effect on potency/ libido** |
| Reversal | **Recanalization (tuboplasty)** – ~50–70 % success (Pomeroy/ ring) | Vasovasostomy |
| Eligibility (GoI) | **Married, female 22–49 yrs, male < 60 yrs**, **at least 1 child aged > 1 yr** (sound health), voluntary informed consent (**spouse consent not mandatory per SC**, but encouraged); one of the couple only | Same |

- **Compensation (GoI)** – acceptors ko wage-loss compensation (vasectomy ke liye zyada); **Family Planning Indemnity Scheme** – compensation for death/ failure/ complications.
- **Simpler, safer, cheaper & faster method – vasectomy (NSV)**, yet female sterilization dominates in India.
- **Post-tubectomy care**: rest 24–48 hrs, wound dry, sutures removed day 5–7, **resume intercourse after 1 week (when comfortable)**, light work after 2–3 days, no heavy lifting 2 wks; **report – fever, wound pus, severe pain, missed period (failure/ ectopic)**.
- **Post-vasectomy**: scrotal support, ice pack, avoid heavy work 48 hrs, **condom for 3 months**, semen analysis.
