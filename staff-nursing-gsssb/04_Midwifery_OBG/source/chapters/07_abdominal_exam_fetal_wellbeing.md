## 7.1 Obstetric Terms

@fig fetal_presentations.svg | Lie, presentation aur denominator

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Lie** | Relation of **long axis of fetus to long axis of mother/ uterus** – **longitudinal (99 %)**, transverse, oblique (unstable) |
| **Presentation** | **Part of fetus lying over the pelvic inlet** (first to enter) – **cephalic (~96 %: vertex, brow, face)**, breech, shoulder, compound |
| **Presenting part** | Part felt first on PV (e.g. **vertex – posterior part of parietal bone**) |
| **Attitude** | Relation of fetal parts to each other – normal **universal flexion** (head flexed, limbs flexed) |
| **Denominator** | Arbitrary bony point to name position – **vertex: occiput**, **face: mentum (chin)**, **brow: frontal eminence**, **breech: sacrum**, **shoulder: acromion** |
| **Position** | Relation of **denominator to maternal pelvis** (left/ right, anterior/ posterior/ transverse) |
| **Engagement** | **Widest presenting diameter (BPD in vertex) passes through pelvic inlet** – PV: **station 0 (at ischial spines)**; abdominal: **≤ 2/5 palpable**; primigravida **~36–38 wks**, multigravida in labour |
| **Station** | Level of presenting part relative to **ischial spines (0)** – **−5 to +5 cm** (+ below spines) |
| **Lightening** | Descent of uterus/ head into pelvis **2–3 wks before labour (primi)** → breathing easier, frequency ↑ |

- **Positions of vertex** (6): **LOA (left occiput anterior – most common)**, ROA, LOT, ROT, **LOP, ROP (occipito-posterior – ~10–20 %, prolonged labour)**. **Direct OA/ OP** – rare.
- **Fifths of head palpable (Crichton)**: 5/5 – floating above brim; 3/5; **2/5 – engaged**; 0/5 – deep in pelvis.

## 7.2 Abdominal Palpation

- **Preparation**: explain, **empty bladder**, privacy, **supine with knees slightly flexed (dorsal)**, warm hands, abdomen exposed (xiphisternum to symphysis); avoid prolonged supine (left tilt).
- **Inspection**: size, shape (ovoid – longitudinal; **broad – transverse**), linea nigra, striae, scars (previous LSCS), fetal movements, **flattening below umbilicus – OP**, **pendulous abdomen (grand multipara)**.
- **Fundal height** – with ulnar border of hand (weeks) & **symphysio-fundal height (SFH) tape (cm) ≈ gestational weeks ± 2** (20–36 wks).

@fig fundal_height.svg | Fundal height – kitne hafte me kahan

- **Fundal height > dates**: wrong dates, **multiple pregnancy**, **polyhydramnios**, macrosomia (diabetes), **molar pregnancy**, fibroids. **< dates**: wrong dates, **IUGR**, **oligohydramnios**, IUD, transverse lie.

@fig leopold_grips.svg | Leopold ke 4 grips

:::key Leopold's manoeuvres (4 grips)
1. **Fundal grip** – what occupies fundus → **lie & presentation** (**breech – broad, soft, irregular, not ballotable**; head – hard, round, ballotable).
2. **Lateral (umbilical) grip** – back (smooth, continuous resistance) vs limbs (small knobs) → **position**; **FHS best heard over back**.
3. **Pawlik's grip (first pelvic grip)** – lower pole grasped with **one hand (thumb & fingers)** → **presenting part & engagement** (head mobile = not engaged). **Painful – done gently**.
4. **Pelvic grip (second pelvic)** – **examiner faces patient's feet**, both hands → **flexion (attitude), descent, engagement** (cephalic prominence/ occiput).
:::

- **Auscultation of FHS**: **Pinard fetoscope**/ stethoscope/ **Doppler (handheld)**; **count 1 full minute**; **normal 110–160 bpm (120–160)**, regular; distinguish from maternal pulse (feel radial simultaneously) & **uterine souffle** (maternal – synchronous with maternal pulse) vs **funic souffle** (cord – synchronous with FHS).
 - **Location**: **vertex anterior – below umbilicus on side of back (spinoumbilical line)**; **OP – flank, lateral**; **breech – at/ above umbilicus**; face – on side of limbs (chest).
 - **Fetal bradycardia < 110**, **tachycardia > 160** → fetal distress (hypoxia, maternal fever).

## 7.3 Tests of Fetal Wellbeing

| Test | Details | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| **Daily Fetal Movement Count (DFMC)** | From **28 wks**; **Cardiff "count to 10"** | **< 10 movements in 12 hrs** → NST/ report; **fetal movements stop ~12–48 hrs before IUD** ("movement alarm signal") |
| **NST (Non-Stress Test)** | CTG for **20 min (extend to 40)** – FHR accelerations with fetal movement; after 28–32 wks | **Reactive (normal)** – **≥ 2 accelerations of ≥ 15 bpm lasting ≥ 15 sec in 20 min** ("15 × 15"); **Non-reactive** → BPP/ CST |
| **CST/ OCT (Contraction Stress Test – oxytocin challenge)** | **3 contractions in 10 min** (oxytocin/ nipple stimulation) | **Negative (normal)** – no late decelerations; **Positive** – **late decelerations with ≥ 50 % contractions** → uteroplacental insufficiency |
| **BPP (Biophysical Profile – Manning)** | USG 30 min, **5 parameters × 2 = 10**: **NST, Fetal breathing movements, Gross body movements, Fetal tone, Amniotic fluid (AFI/ SDP)** | **8–10 normal**; **6 equivocal** (repeat 24 hrs); **≤ 4 abnormal – deliver**. **Modified BPP = NST + AFI** |
| **Doppler velocimetry** | **Umbilical artery S/D ratio, MCA, ductus venosus** | **Absent/ reversed end-diastolic flow (AEDF/ REDF)** → IUGR, deliver; **MCA PSV ↑ → fetal anaemia (Rh)** |
| **USG** | Dating (CRL), NT (11–14 wks; > 3 mm – Down), **anomaly scan 18–20 wks**, growth (BPD, HC, AC, FL), AFI, placenta | **AC (abdominal circumference) – best for IUGR** |
| **Amniocentesis** | **15–18 wks** (genetic – karyotype), later – lung maturity, Rh (ΔOD 450); **USG-guided**, **empty bladder (> 20 wks)**/ full (< 20) | Risk – abortion **0.5–1 %**, infection, Rh sensitization (give **anti-D** if Rh –ve) |
| **CVS (Chorionic villus sampling)** | **10–13 wks** – earliest prenatal genetic diagnosis | **Limb reduction defects if < 10 wks**, abortion 1 % |
| **Cordocentesis (PUBS)** | > 18 wks – fetal blood | Fetal anaemia, karyotype |
| **Lung maturity** | **L/S (lecithin/sphingomyelin) ratio ≥ 2** = mature (~35 wks); **phosphatidylglycerol (PG)** present (best) ; shake/ foam test | Steroids if immature |
| **Maternal serum screening** | Double marker (**PAPP-A, free β-hCG** – 11–14 wks), Triple (**AFP, hCG, uE3** – 15–20), Quadruple (+ **inhibin A**) | **Down: AFP ↓, uE3 ↓, hCG ↑, inhibin ↑**; **NTD: AFP ↑** |
| **NIPT (cell-free fetal DNA)** | From 10 wks | Screening for trisomies |

### CTG – intrapartum FHR patterns
- **Baseline 110–160**, **variability 5–25 bpm** (most important sign of fetal wellbeing), accelerations present.
- **Early deceleration** – mirrors contraction (**head compression – benign**).
- **Variable deceleration** – V/U-shaped, abrupt, variable timing (**cord compression**) → **change position**, amnioinfusion.
- **Late deceleration** – begins after peak of contraction, slow return (**uteroplacental insufficiency – ominous**) → **left lateral, O₂, stop oxytocin, IV fluids, inform – expedite delivery**.
- **Sinusoidal pattern** – severe fetal anaemia (Rh), hypoxia.
