# Normal Labour – Stages, Mechanism & Management
<!-- desc: Onset theories, true vs false labour, 3 Ps, stages & duration, mechanism (cardinal movements), partograph, management of each stage, AMTSL, episiotomy, perineal tears -->

## 8.1 Labour – Basics

:::def Normal labour (eutocia)
Labour with **spontaneous onset at term (37–42 wks)**, **vertex presentation**, **without undue prolongation**, natural termination with **minimal aid**, **without complications** to mother & baby. Labour = series of events by which **products of conception (fetus, placenta, membranes)** are expelled from uterus. **Dystocia** = difficult labour.
:::

- **Causes/ theories of onset**: **oxytocin theory** (receptors ↑), **progesterone withdrawal**, **oestrogen ↑** (↑ gap junctions & oxytocin receptors), **prostaglandins (PGE2, PGF2α)**, **fetal cortisol (fetal HPA axis)**, **uterine distension/ stretch**, Ferguson reflex (cervix stretch → oxytocin).
- **Premonitory signs (pre-labour)**: **lightening** (2–3 wks before in primi), **frequency of micturition**, **show (blood-stained mucus plug)**, **false pains**, **cervical ripening**, slight weight loss, burst of energy ("nesting").

| True labour | False labour |
|---|---|
| **Regular, painful contractions**, ↑ frequency, duration & intensity | **Irregular**, no progressive change |
| Pain **back → front (abdomen)**, radiates to thighs | Pain **lower abdomen** only |
| **Not relieved** by rest/ sedation/ enema | **Relieved by rest/ sedation/ walking change**, enema |
| **Cervical effacement & dilatation** (progressive) | **No cervical change** |
| **Show** present | No show |
| Formation of **bag of waters** | No |

- **3 P's (+ more)**: **Powers** (uterine contractions + voluntary bearing down), **Passage** (bony pelvis + soft tissues), **Passenger** (fetus + placenta); + **Psyche**, **Position**.
- **Uterine contractions**: begin at **fundus near cornua (pacemaker)**, **fundal dominance**, spread downward; **retraction** (fibres don't return to original length – permanent shortening of upper segment) → **physiological retraction ring**; **pathological (Bandl's) ring** – obstructed labour.
- Contraction: **intensity 40–60 mmHg** (2nd stage up to 100); frequency – early 10–15 min apart → active **every 2–3 min**, duration **45–60 sec (up to 90)**. **Montevideo units > 200** = adequate.

## 8.2 Stages of Labour

@fig cervical_dilatation.svg | Cervix ka effacement aur dilatation

| Stage | From → To | Primigravida | Multigravida |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1st stage (cervical stage)** | Onset of true labour → **full dilatation (10 cm)** | **~12 hrs** (latent up to 20 hrs) | **~6 hrs** (latent up to 14 hrs) |
| **2nd stage (stage of expulsion)** | Full dilatation → **birth of baby** | **~1 hr (max 2 hrs; 3 with epidural)** | **~30 min (max 1 hr; 2 with epidural)** |
| **3rd stage (placental stage)** | Birth of baby → **expulsion of placenta & membranes** | **15–30 min** (5–15 with AMTSL) | Same |
| **4th stage (observation)** | **1 hr (to 2 hrs) after placenta** – **PPH watch** | | |

- **1st stage phases**: **Latent** – slow, up to **4 cm** (WHO 2018/ LCG – **5 cm**); **Active** – **4 (5) cm → 10 cm**, ~**1 cm/hr** (primi), 1.5 cm/hr (multi); (Friedman – acceleration, maximum slope, deceleration).
- **Rupture of membranes** – usually end of 1st stage; **early ROM** (before full dilatation, in labour), **PROM** (before labour onset).
- **2nd stage signs**: **urge to push (bear down)**, **rectal pressure/ bulging perineum**, **anal gaping**, crowning, show ↑, vomiting; **confirm by PV (full dilatation)**.
- **Crowning** – **biparietal diameter distends vulva, head no longer recedes between contractions**.

## 8.3 Mechanism of Labour (Vertex – LOA)

:::mnemo Cardinal movements – "Every Darn Fool In Egypt Eats Raw Eggs"
**E**ngagement → **D**escent → **F**lexion → **I**nternal rotation → **E**xtension → **R**estitution → **E**xternal rotation → **E**xpulsion
(Descent throughout labour; flexion → smallest diameter suboccipito-bregmatic 9.5 cm presents.)
:::

| Movement | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Engagement** | Sagittal suture in **transverse/ oblique** diameter of inlet (widest); primi before labour |
| **Descent** | Throughout – contractions, bearing down |
| **Flexion** | Head meets resistance (cervix, pelvic floor) → chin to chest → **suboccipito-bregmatic 9.5 cm** |
| **Internal rotation** | Occiput rotates **1/8 of circle (45°) anteriorly** (LOA) at **pelvic floor (levator ani)** → **sagittal suture in AP diameter** of outlet; occiput under symphysis |
| **Crowning & Extension** | Occiput escapes under symphysis (**nape of neck pivots – suboccipital region**) → head born by **extension** – vertex, brow, face, chin sweep perineum |
| **Restitution** | Head **untwists 1/8 circle** (45°) to realign with shoulders (opposite direction of internal rotation) |
| **External rotation** | Shoulders rotate internally to AP of outlet → head turns further **1/8 circle** (occiput towards **mother's thigh of the side of original position – left in LOA**) |
| **Expulsion** | **Anterior shoulder** under symphysis → **posterior shoulder** over perineum (lateral flexion) → trunk |

## 8.4 Management of 1st Stage

- **Admission**: history, vitals, abdominal exam, FHS, **PV examination** (dilatation, effacement, station, membranes, presenting part, position, moulding, pelvis) – **4-hourly PV** (avoid frequent – infection), **strict asepsis**.
- **Partograph** – started at **active phase (4 cm/ 5 cm)**.

@fig partograph.svg | Partograph – alert line & action line

- **Monitoring**: **FHS every 30 min (1st stage; 15 min in active high-risk) & after contraction for 1 min**; **contractions every 30 min** (frequency/ 10 min, duration); **pulse ½ hourly**; **BP & temperature 4 hourly**; **urine output, protein, acetone** – **empty bladder every 2 hrs** (full bladder delays descent); **PV 4 hourly**; liquor colour.
- **Care**: **ambulation & upright positions** (encouraged in latent & early active), **left lateral** when lying; **oral fluids/ light diet** (clear fluids – low-risk); IV fluids if needed; **birth companion** (LaQshya/ respectful maternity care); **no routine enema, no routine shaving** (WHO); **pain relief** – breathing, massage, back rub, warm compress, **epidural (gold standard)**, pethidine (avoid near delivery – neonatal respiratory depression); emotional support, privacy.
- **Avoid**: routine ARM/ oxytocin, frequent PV, **fundal pressure**, lithotomy for long.

## 8.5 Management of 2nd Stage

- **Position** – **upright/ semi-recumbent/ lateral/ squatting** (WHO – woman's choice); traditional lithotomy/ dorsal.
- **Bearing down** – with contractions, **open glottis pushing** preferred; avoid prolonged Valsalva.
- **FHS every 5 min (after each contraction)**.
- **Perineal care** – **perineal support (Ritgen's manoeuvre)** – control head extension (**slow delivery of head between contractions**), **flex head**, protect perineum; **warm compress**.
- **Episiotomy** – **restrictive (not routine)** – indications below.
- After head delivered: **wipe/ clear airway** (suction only if needed – not routine), **feel for cord round neck** (loose → slip over head; tight → **double clamp & cut**), wait for **restitution & external rotation** → **deliver anterior shoulder (gentle downward traction)** → posterior shoulder (upward) → body.
- **Immediate newborn care** – dry, warm, **skin-to-skin**, **delayed cord clamping (1–3 min)**, assess breathing; note **time of birth & sex**.

## 8.6 Management of 3rd Stage

- **Signs of placental separation**: (1) **Gush of blood**, (2) **lengthening of cord** (permanent), (3) **uterus rises & becomes globular, firm, smaller** (fundus rises above umbilicus), (4) **suprapubic bulge**; (Küstner's/ Strassmann's/ Klein's signs – cord doesn't recede on suprapubic pressure).

:::key AMTSL – Active Management of Third Stage of Labour (PPH prevention – most effective)
1. **Uterotonic within 1 minute of birth** (after ruling out 2nd baby): **Inj. Oxytocin 10 IU IM** (drug of choice); alternatives – **misoprostol 600 µg oral**, carbetocin, ergometrine (not in HTN/ heart disease)
2. **Controlled Cord Traction (CCT)** with **counter-traction** (Brandt-Andrews – hand above symphysis pushes uterus up) – **only with contraction**; never without counter-traction (**inversion**)
3. **Uterine massage** after placenta delivery (**every 15 min for 2 hrs**) + check uterus remains contracted
- **Delayed cord clamping (1–3 min)** – now part of essential care (not "early clamping").
- Examine **placenta & membranes for completeness**; inspect **perineum & vagina** for tears; estimate blood loss (**normal < 500 ml vaginal**).
:::

- **Delivery of placenta**: **Brandt-Andrews** (CCT), Crede's method (fundal squeezing – old, not recommended), **manual removal** (retained > 30 min with AMTSL/ bleeding – under anaesthesia).

## 8.7 Fourth Stage & Immediate Postpartum

- Monitor **every 15 min for 1 hr**, then every 30 min for next hour: **pulse, BP, uterine tone (fundus firm, at/ below umbilicus), vaginal bleeding (pads), bladder (full bladder → atony)**, perineum.
- **Breastfeeding within 1 hour**, skin-to-skin, warmth, vitamin K & vaccines for baby, record.

## 8.8 Episiotomy & Perineal Tears

- **Episiotomy (perineotomy)** – surgical incision of perineum to **enlarge the outlet**.
- **Indications** (restrictive): **rigid perineum** (elderly primi), anticipated tear, **instrumental delivery (forceps/ vacuum)**, **breech delivery**, **shoulder dystocia**, **fetal distress (expedite)**, face-to-pubis, previous perineal repair/ 3rd degree tear, preterm (protect head).
- **Timing** – when perineum **thinned out & bulging, ~3–4 cm of head visible** during contraction (**just before crowning**); local anaesthesia (**1 % lignocaine** – pudendal block / local infiltration).
- **Types**: **Mediolateral (most common, preferred in India)** – from midpoint of fourchette **45° (60° at crowning) laterally** – **less risk of extension to anal sphincter**; **Median/ midline** – easy repair, less blood loss, better healing, **but risk of 3rd/4th degree extension**; lateral (obsolete); J-shaped.
- **Repair** (**episiorrhaphy**) – in 3 layers: **vaginal mucosa (continuous, starting 1 cm above apex)** → **perineal muscles (interrupted)** → **skin (subcuticular/ interrupted)**; **absorbable suture – Vicryl rapide/ chromic catgut 2-0/ 3-0**; check **rectum after repair** (stitch through rectal mucosa).
- **Care**: perineal hygiene (front to back), **sitz bath/ warm water**, **ice packs first 24 hrs**, analgesics, change pads, **Kegel exercises**, high fibre (avoid straining), watch for haematoma (severe pain), infection, dehiscence; sutures absorb in 2–3 wks.
- **Perineal tears**:
 - **1st degree** – vaginal mucosa & perineal skin only
 - **2nd degree** – + **perineal muscles** (≈ episiotomy)
 - **3rd degree** – + **anal sphincter** (3a < 50 % EAS, 3b > 50 % EAS, 3c IAS)
 - **4th degree** – + **anal/ rectal mucosa** → repair in OT; stool softeners, antibiotics; risk of **faecal incontinence, rectovaginal fistula**

:::exam PYQ style
- 1st stage of labour ends at – **full cervical dilatation (10 cm)**.
- 2nd stage primi – **~1 hr (max 2)**; 3rd stage – **15–30 min**; 4th stage – **1 hr after placenta**.
- True labour pain – **back to front, not relieved by rest, cervical dilatation**.
- Internal rotation takes place at – **pelvic floor**; occiput rotates – **1/8 of circle** (LOA).
- Smallest presenting diameter – **suboccipito-bregmatic 9.5 cm**.
- Partograph alert line – **1 cm/hr**; action line – **4 hrs to the right**.
- FHS in 2nd stage – **every 5 min**; 1st stage – **every 30 min**.
- AMTSL drug – **oxytocin 10 IU IM within 1 min**.
- Sign of placental separation – **lengthening of cord, gush of blood, uterus globular & rises**.
- Commonest episiotomy – **mediolateral**; timing – **at crowning/ head visible 3–4 cm**.
- 3rd degree tear involves – **anal sphincter**.
:::
