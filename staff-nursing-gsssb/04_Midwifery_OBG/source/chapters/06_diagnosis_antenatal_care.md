## 6.1 Signs of Pregnancy

| Presumptive (subjective) | Probable (objective – examiner) | Positive (diagnostic – fetus) |
|---|---|---|
| **Amenorrhoea** (first & most common symptom) | **Enlargement of abdomen/ uterus** | **Fetal heart sounds** – Doppler **10–12 wks**, stethoscope **18–20 wks** |
| **Morning sickness** (4–14 wks) | **Hegar's sign** (6–10 wks – softening of **isthmus**; bimanual – fingers almost meet) | **Fetal movements felt by examiner** (~20 wks) |
| **Breast changes** (tenderness, Montgomery's) | **Goodell's sign** (cervical softening, 6 wks) | **USG – gestational sac (TVS 4.5–5 wks; TAS 6 wks)**, **fetal pole & cardiac activity (TVS 6 wks)** |
| **Frequency of micturition** | **Chadwick's/ Jacquemier's sign** (bluish vagina, 8 wks) | **Palpation of fetal parts** (~20–24 wks) |
| **Quickening** (first fetal movements felt by mother – 18–20 wks primi, 16–18 multi) | **Osiander's sign** (pulsation in lateral fornix, 8 wks) | **X-ray fetal skeleton** (16 wks – not used) |
| Fatigue, skin pigmentation | **Piskacek's sign** (asymmetric enlargement at implantation site) | |
| | **Palmer's sign** (rhythmic uterine contractions, 4–8 wks) | |
| | **Braxton Hicks contractions** | |
| | **Ballottement** (internal 16–20 wks/ external) | |
| | **Positive pregnancy test (hCG)** | |

- **Pregnancy tests (hCG)**: **urine card test (immunological – Nischay kit, ASHA kit)** – positive ~**1 week after missed period** (urine hCG 25 mIU/ml); **early morning urine** best (concentrated); **serum β-hCG** – **8–11 days after conception** (most sensitive); **doubles every 48 hrs** in early normal pregnancy (not doubling → ectopic/ abortion).

## 6.2 Calculation of Dates

:::key Naegele's Rule (EDD)
**EDD = LMP + 9 months + 7 days** (or LMP − 3 months + 7 days + 1 year) → **280 days (40 wks)** from LMP.
- Example: **LMP 10 March 2026** → EDD **17 December 2026**.
- Example: **LMP 25 Oct 2025** → 25 + 7 = 32 → **1 Aug 2026** (Oct − 3 = July; 32 July = 1 Aug).
- Assumes **28-day regular cycle**; longer cycle → add extra days (cycle length − 28).
- **Only ~4–5 % deliver on EDD**; most within ± 2 wks.
- Gestational age by **first trimester USG (CRL – crown-rump length, 7–13 wks)** – **most accurate dating**.
- Other: date of quickening + 22 wks (primi)/ 24 wks (multi); fundal height.
:::

## 6.3 Antenatal Care (ANC)

- **Aims**: healthy mother & baby, **early detection of high-risk** cases, health education, birth preparedness, prevent complications.
- **First visit/ registration** – **as early as possible, within 12 wks (1st trimester)**.
- **Visits** – **India (MoHFW): minimum 4 ANC visits** – **1st: within 12 wks**, **2nd: 14–26 wks**, **3rd: 28–34 wks**, **4th: 36 wks–term** (+ PMSMA on 9th). **WHO 2016: minimum 8 contacts** (12, 20, 26, 30, 34, 36, 38, 40 wks). Traditional: monthly till 28 wks, fortnightly 28–36, weekly after 36.

### History & examination at first visit
- **History**: LMP (EDD), obstetric history (GTPAL), previous complications (PPH, LSCS, stillbirth, abortions), medical history (DM, HTN, heart disease, TB, epilepsy), family history (twins, DM), drugs, allergies.
- **General exam**: height (**< 140/ 145 cm – CPD risk**), weight, BMI, BP, pulse, **pallor**, oedema, thyroid, breasts & nipples (flat/ inverted), heart & lungs, spine.
- **Obstetric exam** – abdominal palpation (from 2nd trimester), FHS.

### Routine investigations
| Test | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Hb** (each trimester) | Anaemia (< 11 g/dL) |
| **Blood group & Rh** | **Rh –ve → ICT (indirect Coombs) at 28 wks**, anti-D |
| **Urine – albumin & sugar** (every visit) | **Pre-eclampsia (albumin)**, diabetes |
| **Blood sugar – OGTT 75 g (DIPSI)** | **GDM screening** – first visit & **24–28 wks**; **2-hr ≥ 140 mg/dL = GDM** (non-fasting, single step – India) |
| **VDRL/ RPR** | Syphilis |
| **HIV** (with counselling) | PPTCT |
| **HBsAg** | Hepatitis B → baby HBIG + vaccine |
| **Urine routine/ culture** | Asymptomatic bacteriuria |
| **TSH** (high-risk/ universal in some) | Hypothyroidism |
| **Ultrasound** | **Dating (1st trim)**, **NT scan 11–14 wks**, **anomaly scan (TIFFA) 18–20 wks**, growth & placenta (3rd trim) |
| **Double marker (11–14 wks)/ Triple/ Quadruple (15–20 wks)** | **Down syndrome & NTD screening** (MSAFP ↑ – NTD; ↓ – Down) |
| Sickle cell, thalassaemia (HbA2) screening | Endemic areas/ Gujarat tribal |

### Supplements & immunization
- **Td (Tetanus-diphtheria)**: **2 doses – Td1 early in pregnancy, Td2 4 weeks after Td1**; **booster (1 dose)** if previously vaccinated within 3 yrs; (**TT replaced by Td – 2018**). IM deltoid, 0.5 ml.
- **IFA**: **1 tablet (60 mg elemental iron + 500 µg folic acid) daily from 14–16 wks (2nd trimester) for 180 days**, continue **180 days postpartum** (Anaemia Mukt Bharat); anaemic (Hb 7–10.9) – **2 tablets daily**; severe – IV iron/ blood.
- **Folic acid 400 µg (0.4 mg)** daily – **preconception (3 months before) & first trimester** – NTD prevention; previous NTD baby → **4 mg/day**.
- **Calcium**: **1 g elemental (2 × 500 mg tabs) daily from 14 wks** after meals (not with iron – separate by 2 hrs) – pre-eclampsia prevention.
- **Albendazole 400 mg single dose in 2nd trimester** (deworming) – not in 1st trimester.
- **Iodized salt**; Vit D as needed. **Influenza vaccine** (WHO), **COVID**; **live vaccines (MMR, varicella, BCG, yellow fever) contraindicated**.

### Nutrition in pregnancy (ICMR)
- **Extra +350 kcal/day in 2nd & 3rd trimester**; protein **+8–10 g (2nd), +18–23 g (3rd)**; iron rich foods, **folate**, calcium (milk, ragi), vitamins; **small frequent meals**; avoid alcohol, smoking, raw/ undercooked food, excess caffeine (< 200 mg/day).
- **Lactation**: **+600 kcal (0–6 months)**, +520 (6–12 months); protein +13–19 g.

## 6.4 Minor Ailments of Pregnancy & Relief

| Ailment | Cause | Relief |
|---|---|---|
| **Morning sickness** | hCG, oestrogen | **Dry toast/ biscuits before getting out of bed**, small frequent meals, avoid fatty/ spicy foods, ginger, fluids between meals (not with meals), **doxylamine + pyridoxine** |
| **Heartburn** | Progesterone (sphincter relax) + pressure | Small meals, **avoid lying down 1–2 hrs after meals**, head end elevated, antacids |
| **Constipation** | Progesterone, iron, pressure | **Fibre, fluids (2–3 L), exercise**, isabgol; no strong purgatives |
| **Backache** | **Lordosis**, relaxin | **Good posture, low-heeled shoes**, firm mattress, pelvic tilt exercise, proper body mechanics |
| **Leg cramps** | **↓ Calcium/ ↑ phosphorus**, pressure | **Dorsiflex the foot (stretch calf)**, massage, warmth, calcium |
| **Varicose veins** | Venous pressure, progesterone | Elevate legs, elastic stockings, avoid long standing, don't cross legs |
| **Oedema of feet (dependent)** | Venous pressure | Rest with legs elevated, **left lateral**; rule out pre-eclampsia (face/ hands, BP) |
| **Haemorrhoids** | Constipation, pressure | Fibre, sitz bath, ointments |
| **Frequency of micturition** | Bladder pressure | Reassure; rule out UTI (burning); reduce fluids at bedtime |
| **Leucorrhoea** | ↑ vascularity, oestrogen | Hygiene, cotton undergarments; foul/ itchy → treat infection |
| **Fainting/ supine hypotension** | Vasodilation, IVC compression | Left lateral, rise slowly |
| **Insomnia** | Discomfort, movements | Left lateral with pillows, warm milk, relaxation |
| **Ptyalism**, **pica**, **gum bleeding**, **carpal tunnel**, **round ligament pain**, **dyspnoea** | | Reassurance, specific measures |

## 6.5 Danger Signs & Birth Preparedness

:::warn Danger signs in pregnancy – turant hospital
- **Vaginal bleeding** (any amount) · **Leaking of fluid (PROM)**
- **Severe headache, blurring of vision, epigastric pain, swelling of face/ hands** (pre-eclampsia)
- **Convulsions/ fits** (eclampsia) · High fever · Severe abdominal pain
- **Decreased/ absent fetal movements** (< 10 in 12 hrs/ DFMC) · Breathlessness, palpitations
- Severe vomiting · Burning micturition · Labour pains before 37 wks
:::

- **Birth Preparedness & Complication Readiness (BPCR)**: identify **place of delivery** (institution), **skilled birth attendant**, **transport (108/ 102 – Janani Express)**, **money**, **blood donor**, companion, documents (MCP card), know danger signs.
- **Daily fetal movement count (DFMC/ Cardiff "count to 10")** – from **28 wks**; **≥ 10 movements in 12 hrs** normal (or ≥ 3 per hour after meals – Sadovsky); < 10 → report.
- **Health education topics**: diet, rest (**2 hrs day + 8 hrs night, left lateral**), hygiene, exercise (walking), **avoid heavy work & long travel**, **sexual intercourse – avoid in first trimester & last 6 wks** (and in threatened abortion, praevia), clothing (loose, low heels), breast care (nipple – correct flat/ inverted nipples – Hoffman's exercise/ syringe method – not routinely), **drugs only on advice**, X-ray avoid, preparation for breastfeeding, family planning, birth spacing.

- First symptom of pregnancy – **amenorrhoea**.
- Positive sign of pregnancy – **FHS/ fetal parts/ USG fetus**.
- Hegar's sign – **softening of isthmus (6–10 wks)**.
- Quickening primi – **18–20 wks**.
- Naegele's rule – **LMP + 9 months + 7 days**.
- Minimum ANC visits (India) – **4**; first visit – **within 12 wks**.
- IFA prophylaxis – **60 mg iron + 500 µg FA, 180 days**.
- Td doses – **2 (4 weeks apart)**.
- GDM screening (DIPSI) – **75 g glucose, 2-hr ≥ 140 mg/dL**.
- Leg cramps relief – **dorsiflexion of foot**.
- Fetal kick count normal – **≥ 10 in 12 hrs**.
- Morning sickness relief – **dry carbohydrate before getting up**.
:::
