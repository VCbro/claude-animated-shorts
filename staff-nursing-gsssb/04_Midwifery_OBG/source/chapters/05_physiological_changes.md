# Physiological Changes in Pregnancy
<!-- desc: Reproductive, breast, cardiovascular, blood, respiratory, renal, GI, skin, endocrine, metabolic & weight changes; normal values in pregnancy -->

## 5.1 Reproductive Organs

- **Uterus**: weight **50–60 g → ~1000 g**; size 7.5 cm → **35 cm**; capacity ~5 L; growth by **hypertrophy (main) + hyperplasia** (oestrogen) + stretching. Shape: pear → globular (12 wks) → ovoid (term). **Lower uterine segment** forms from isthmus by **32 wks** (thin, passive – **LSCS incision**); upper segment – thick, active, contractile.
- **Braxton Hicks contractions** – **painless, irregular, non-dilating** contractions from **early pregnancy (felt ~20 wks onward)**; **false labour pain** near term.
- **Cervix** – **Goodell's sign (softening, ~6 wks)**, **operculum (mucus plug)** – prevents ascending infection; expelled as **"show"** at labour onset; bluish (Chadwick).
- **Vagina** – **Chadwick's (Jacquemier's) sign** – **bluish/ purplish discolouration** of vagina & cervix (~8 wks) – vascularity; ↑ secretions (**leucorrhoea**), pH ↓ (more acidic – 3.5–6) → **candidiasis common**.
- **Ovaries** – no ovulation; **corpus luteum** active till 10–12 wks.
- **Perineum & vulva** – ↑ vascularity, varicosities.

## 5.2 Breasts

- Early sign (**6–8 wks**): **fullness, tingling, tenderness**; **enlargement**, **prominent veins**; **primary areola** darker; **Montgomery's tubercles** (sebaceous glands) prominent (**8 wks**); **secondary areola** (mottled, **20 wks**); **colostrum** expressible from **12–16 wks**; striae.
- Hormones – **oestrogen (ducts)**, **progesterone (alveoli)**, prolactin, hPL.

## 5.3 Cardiovascular & Blood

| Parameter | Change |
|---|---|
| **Blood volume** | **↑ 40–50 % (~1.5 L)** – peak **30–34 wks** |
| **Plasma volume** | ↑ **50 %** (more than RBC) |
| **RBC mass** | ↑ 20–30 % |
| **Haemoglobin** | **↓ → physiological (dilutional) anaemia** – normal **≥ 11 g/dL** (2nd trimester ≥ 10.5) |
| **Haematocrit** | ↓ (~33–35 %) |
| **Cardiac output** | **↑ 40–50 %** – starts 5 wks, **peak 28–32 wks**; **highest immediately after delivery (~80 % ↑)** → **heart disease patients decompensate after delivery** |
| **Heart rate** | ↑ **10–15 bpm** |
| **Stroke volume** | ↑ |
| **BP** | **↓ in 2nd trimester (diastolic ↓ 5–10 mmHg)**, back to normal at term |
| **Peripheral vascular resistance** | ↓ (progesterone – vasodilation) |
| **Heart** | **Pushed up & to the left** (apex beat 4th ICS), **systolic flow murmur (ejection)**, **S3** may be normal |
| **WBC** | **↑ (up to 15,000; labour 25,000)** – physiological leucocytosis |
| **Platelets** | Slight ↓ (gestational thrombocytopenia) |
| **Coagulation factors** | **↑ fibrinogen (400–650 mg/dL), VII, VIII, IX, X** → **hypercoagulable state** → **DVT risk ↑** (protects from PPH) |
| **ESR** | **↑↑ (not useful)** |
| **Iron requirement** | Total ~**1000 mg** for pregnancy |

- **Supine hypotension syndrome (aortocaval compression)** – late pregnancy, supine → gravid uterus compresses **IVC** → **↓ venous return → hypotension, dizziness, pallor, sweating, fetal distress** → **turn to LEFT lateral position** (or wedge under right hip).
- **Oedema of feet** (physiological – venous pressure ↑) – common, relieved by rest & elevation; **varicose veins, haemorrhoids**.

## 5.4 Respiratory System

- **Tidal volume ↑ 30–40 %**, **minute ventilation ↑ 40–50 %** (progesterone stimulates respiratory centre) → **PaCO₂ ↓ (~30 mmHg)** → **mild compensated respiratory alkalosis** (helps CO₂ transfer from fetus).
- RR unchanged/ slight ↑; **residual volume & FRC ↓ 20 %**; vital capacity unchanged; **diaphragm elevated 4 cm**; **subcostal angle widens (68° → 103°)**; **dyspnoea** common (physiological); **O₂ consumption ↑ 20 %**.
- Nasal congestion, epistaxis (oestrogen – vascular).

## 5.5 Urinary System

- **Kidneys enlarge**; **GFR ↑ 50 %**, renal plasma flow ↑ → **serum urea & creatinine ↓** (creatinine > 0.8 suspicious).
- **Ureters dilate** (progesterone + compression) – **right > left (dextrorotation of uterus)** → **hydroureter/ hydronephrosis** → **UTI/ pyelonephritis risk ↑** (right sided).
- **Frequency of micturition** – **1st trimester** (bladder pressure by uterus) & **last weeks** (presenting part engages); 2nd trimester relief.
- **Glycosuria** (↓ renal threshold) – common, not necessarily diabetes; mild proteinuria (< 300 mg/day).

## 5.6 GI, Skin, Endocrine & Metabolic

- **GI**: **nausea & vomiting (morning sickness – 4–14/16 wks; hCG, oestrogen)**, **ptyalism (excess saliva)**, **pica** (craving non-food – clay, chalk – iron deficiency), **heartburn** (progesterone relaxes cardiac sphincter + pressure), **constipation** (↓ motility – progesterone, iron), **gum hypertrophy/ bleeding (epulis/ pregnancy gingivitis)**, **gallstones** (bile stasis), haemorrhoids.
- **Skin** (melanocyte-stimulating hormone ↑):
 - **Chloasma/ melasma ("mask of pregnancy")** – brown patches on face
 - **Linea nigra** – dark midline on abdomen
 - **Striae gravidarum** – **stretch marks** (pink → silvery **striae albicans** after delivery)
 - Spider naevi, **palmar erythema**, pigmentation of areola, vulva
- **Endocrine**: **pituitary enlarges** (prolactin ↑ – Sheehan's risk with PPH), **thyroid enlarges** (total T4 ↑ – TBG ↑; free T4 normal), **parathyroid ↑**, **adrenal cortisol ↑**, **insulin resistance (hPL, cortisol)** – fasting glucose ↓, post-meal ↑.
- **Musculoskeletal**: **lordosis** (centre of gravity shifts forward) → **backache**; **relaxin** – ligament laxity, pelvic joints mobile (waddling gait); **leg cramps** (calcium ↓/ phosphate ↑).
- **Metabolic**: **BMR ↑ 15–20 %**; **water retention ~6–8 L**; protein, fat storage ↑; **calcium – fetus needs ~30 g (mostly 3rd trimester)**.

### Weight gain in pregnancy
- Average total **10–12 kg (11 kg)** – **1 kg in 1st trimester**, **~5 kg each in 2nd & 3rd trimester**; ~**0.5 kg/week (2 kg/month)** after 20 wks.
- IOM 2009 by BMI: underweight **12.5–18 kg**, normal **11.5–16 kg**, overweight **7–11.5 kg**, obese **5–9 kg**.
- **Sudden excessive weight gain (> 1 kg/week or > 3 kg/month)** → **pre-eclampsia (oedema)** suspicion; **poor gain** → IUGR.
- Distribution at term: **fetus ~3.3 kg**, placenta 0.6, liquor 0.8, uterus 0.9, breasts 0.4, blood 1.5, fluid 1.5–2, fat 3–3.5 kg.
