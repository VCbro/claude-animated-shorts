## 2.1 External Genitalia (Vulva/ Pudendum)

- **Mons pubis**, **labia majora** (homologous to scrotum), **labia minora**, **clitoris** (homologous to penis – erectile), **vestibule**, **urethral meatus**, **vaginal orifice/ introitus**, **hymen**, **Bartholin's glands** (greater vestibular – lubrication; **Bartholin cyst/ abscess**), **Skene's glands** (paraurethral), **fourchette**, **perineum**.
- **Perineum (perineal body)** – pyramid-shaped tissue between **vagina & anus**, ~**4 cm**; muscles meeting – **bulbospongiosus, superficial & deep transverse perinei, levator ani (pubococcygeus), external anal sphincter**. **Levator ani** = main pelvic floor muscle (pubococcygeus, iliococcygeus, puborectalis) – supports pelvic organs.
- **Episiotomy** cuts perineal muscles (mediolateral – bulbospongiosus & transverse perinei).

## 2.2 Internal Genital Organs

@fig female_reproductive.svg | Uterus, tubes, ovaries, cervix aur vagina

### Vagina
- **Fibromuscular canal** from vulva to cervix; anterior wall **7.5 cm**, posterior wall **9 cm** (longer); directed **upward & backward**.
- **Fornices** – anterior, posterior (**deepest – culdocentesis**), 2 lateral.
- **pH 4–5 (acidic)** – **Döderlein's bacilli (lactobacilli)** convert glycogen → lactic acid → protection. pH alkaline in newborn (maternal oestrogen – acidic briefly), childhood & post-menopause.
- **No glands** in vaginal wall.

### Uterus
- **Hollow, pear-shaped, muscular** organ in pelvis between bladder (front) & rectum (behind).
- Size **7.5 × 5 × 2.5 cm** ("3 × 2 × 1 inch"); weight **50–60 g**; cavity **6.5–7 cm** (sound length); capacity non-pregnant ~2–5 ml → term **~5000 ml**; weight at term **~900–1000 g**.
- Position – **anteverted (angle with vagina ~90°) & anteflexed** (body with cervix ~120°–170°).
- Parts: **Fundus** (above tubes), **Body (corpus)**, **Isthmus** (0.5 cm – forms **lower uterine segment** in pregnancy – site of LSCS incision), **Cervix** (2.5 cm – supravaginal & vaginal parts; **internal os & external os**; nulliparous external os **round**, parous **transverse slit**). Body : cervix ratio – **birth/ childhood 1 : 2**, puberty 1 : 1, **adult 2 : 1** (multipara 3 : 1).
- Layers: **Perimetrium** (peritoneum), **Myometrium** – **3 layers**: outer longitudinal, **middle oblique/ interlacing ("living ligatures" – figure-of-8 fibres compress vessels after placental separation → haemostasis)**, inner circular; **Endometrium** – functional (sheds) & basal layer.
- **Supports (ligaments)**: **Transverse cervical (Cardinal/ Mackenrodt's) – main support**, **uterosacral**, **pubocervical**, **round ligament** (maintains **anteversion**; homologous to gubernaculum; through inguinal canal to labia majora), **broad ligament** (peritoneal fold – not a real support), **levator ani** (pelvic floor). **Pouch of Douglas (rectouterine)** – lowest point of peritoneal cavity (pus/ blood collect); uterovesical pouch – anterior.
- Blood supply – **uterine artery** (branch of **internal iliac**) – crosses **above ureter** ("water under the bridge") near cervix (ureter injury risk in hysterectomy); **ovarian artery** (from **aorta**).

### Fallopian (uterine) tubes
- **10 cm** each; parts: **Interstitial** (intramural – narrowest, 1 mm) → **Isthmus** (site of **tubectomy**) → **Ampulla** (widest, longest – **site of fertilisation & commonest ectopic**) → **Infundibulum** with **fimbriae** (ovarian fimbria).
- Ovum transport by **cilia & peristalsis**; ovum reaches uterus in **3–4 days**.

### Ovaries
- **Almond-shaped**, **3 × 2 × 1 cm**, 5–8 g; in **ovarian fossa**; attached by **mesovarium**, **ovarian ligament** (to uterus), **infundibulopelvic (suspensory) ligament** (carries ovarian vessels).
- At birth ~**1–2 million** primordial follicles; puberty ~**3–4 lakh**; only ~**400–500 ovulate** in lifetime.
- Hormones: **oestrogen** (follicle – granulosa/ theca), **progesterone** (corpus luteum), inhibin, small androgens, relaxin.

### Breast
- Modified sweat gland; **15–20 lobes**, lactiferous ducts → **lactiferous sinuses** (behind areola) → nipple; **Montgomery's tubercles** (sebaceous glands on areola – lubrication; prominent in pregnancy); **tail of Spence** (upper outer – axillary); supported by **Cooper's ligaments**.

## 2.3 The Female Pelvis

@fig female_pelvis.svg | Pelvic inlet diameters aur pelvis ke types

- **Bones**: **2 innominate (hip) bones** (each = **ilium + ischium + pubis** – fuse at **acetabulum**), **sacrum** (5 fused vertebrae; **sacral promontory**), **coccyx** (4 fused).
- **Joints**: **2 sacroiliac**, **symphysis pubis**, **sacrococcygeal** – relaxin/ progesterone → slight mobility in pregnancy (coccyx moves back 2–3 cm in 2nd stage).
- **False pelvis** (above brim – supports uterus) vs **True pelvis** (below brim – **birth canal** – obstetric importance).
- **True pelvis planes**: **Inlet (brim)** – bounded by sacral promontory, alae of sacrum, iliopectineal lines, superior pubic rami, upper border of symphysis; **Cavity**; **Outlet** – lower border of symphysis, ischial tuberosities, sacrotuberous ligaments, tip of coccyx. **Plane of least pelvic dimensions** – at **ischial spines** (**Station 0** – engagement reference).
- **Axis of birth canal** – **curve of Carus** (J-shaped) – downward & backward then forward.

| Plane | AP (cm) | Oblique | Transverse |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Inlet** | **11 (true conjugate)** | 12 | **13 (largest)** |
| **Cavity** | 12 | 12 | 12 (round) |
| **Outlet** | **13 (largest)** | 12 | **11 (bi-ischial/ intertuberous)** |

- **Conjugates**: **True/ anatomical conjugate** (promontory → upper border of symphysis) **11 cm**; **Obstetric conjugate** (promontory → **posterior surface** of symphysis – shortest AP, **10–10.5 cm**; clinical importance); **Diagonal conjugate** (promontory → **lower border** of symphysis – **12–12.5 cm** – **measured clinically by PV**; **obstetric conjugate = diagonal − 1.5–2 cm**).
- **Interspinous diameter** – **10.5 cm** (narrowest transverse in mid-pelvis). **Sub-pubic angle** – **90°** (gynaecoid) – "two fingers/ fist"; **intertuberous (bi-ischial) ≥ 10–11 cm** (admit fist – 4 knuckles).
- Head **enters inlet in transverse/ oblique** (widest transverse diameter) and **exits outlet AP** (widest AP) → **internal rotation**.

| Type (Caldwell-Moloy, 1933) | Inlet shape | % | Labour |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Gynaecoid** | **Round/ slightly oval** | **~50 %** | **Best – normal female** |
| **Android** | **Heart/ wedge-shaped** (male type) | ~20 % | Deep transverse arrest, OP, difficult |
| **Anthropoid** | **AP oval** (long AP) | ~25 % | **Direct OP delivery** (face-to-pubis) |
| **Platypelloid** | **Transverse oval (flat)** | ~5 % | Transverse arrest at inlet, **asynclitism** |

- **Pelvic assessment** at **36–37 wks** in primigravida (CPD exclusion). **Contracted pelvis** – one or more diameters reduced ≥ 1 cm; obstetric conjugate < 10 cm. **Rickets, osteomalacia, polio, TB spine, fractures** → pelvic deformity.

## 2.4 Fetal Skull

@fig fetal_skull.svg | Fetal skull – sutures, fontanelles aur diameters

- **Vault bones**: 2 **frontal**, 2 **parietal**, 1 **occipital** (+ parts of 2 temporal) – **membranous ossification** → not fused at birth → **moulding** possible.
- **Sutures**: **Sagittal** (between parietals – **most important, felt PV, longest**), **Coronal** (frontal–parietal), **Lambdoid** (parietal–occipital), **Frontal** (between frontal bones).
- **Fontanelles**:
 - **Anterior (Bregma)** – **diamond-shaped**, **3 × 2.5 cm**, 4 sutures meet (sagittal, frontal, 2 coronal) → **closes 18 months (12–18)**; **bulging – ↑ICP/ meningitis/ hydrocephalus**; **sunken – dehydration**.
 - **Posterior (Lambda)** – **triangular/ Y-shaped**, small, 3 sutures meet → **closes 6–8 weeks** (by 2–3 months).
- **Regions**: **Vertex** (between 2 fontanelles & parietal eminences), **Sinciput (brow)** – anterior fontanelle to root of nose, **Face** (root of nose to chin), **Occiput** (behind posterior fontanelle).
- **Landmarks**: **Glabella, Nasion, Mentum (chin), Occipital protuberance, Parietal eminences, Biparietal**.

### Diameters
| AP diameter | cm | Presentation/ attitude |
|---|---|---|
| **Suboccipito-bregmatic** | **9.5** | **Vertex, well flexed** (normal – smallest) |
| **Suboccipito-frontal** | 10 | Vertex, partially flexed |
| **Occipito-frontal** | **11.5** | Vertex deflexed – **OP position** |
| **Mento-vertical** | **13.5–14 (largest)** | **Brow** – **no vaginal delivery** (average size) |
| **Submento-vertical** | 11.5 | Face, partially extended |
| **Submento-bregmatic** | **9.5** | **Face, fully extended** |

- Transverse: **Biparietal 9.5 cm** (largest transverse; **engagement** when BPD passes inlet), **Bitemporal 8 cm**, **Bimastoid 7.5 cm**. **Bisacromial (shoulders) 12 cm** – adducts to 9.5; bitrochanteric 10 cm.
- **Circumference**: suboccipito-bregmatic ~**33 cm**; occipito-frontal ~**34–35 cm** (head circumference at birth **33–35 cm**).
- **Moulding** – change in skull shape by **overlapping of bones** during labour (parietal over occipital & frontal); grades **0 (separated), +1 (touching), +2 (overlapping, reducible), +3 (overlapping, non-reducible)**; disappears in **24–48 hrs**. Excess → **intracranial haemorrhage (tentorial tear)**.
- **Caput succedaneum** – **oedema of scalp**, **crosses suture lines**, present at birth, disappears in **2–3 days**. **Cephalhaematoma** – **subperiosteal blood**, **does NOT cross sutures**, appears after 12–24 hrs, resolves in **weeks–months** (jaundice risk).

:::mnemo Caput vs Cephalhaematoma
**C**aput **C**rosses sutures, **C**omes at birth, **C**lears in days. **Cephalhaematoma** – **sutures se bandha** (bone ka periosteum), late appear, slow disappear.
:::
