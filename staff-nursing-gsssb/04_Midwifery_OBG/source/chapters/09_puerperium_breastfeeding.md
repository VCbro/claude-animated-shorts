# Normal Puerperium, Postnatal Care & Breastfeeding

## 9.1 Puerperium

:::def Puerperium
Period **following delivery (expulsion of placenta)** during which the **reproductive organs return to pre-pregnant state** – **6 weeks (42 days)**. **Immediate** (first 24 hrs), **Early** (up to 7 days), **Remote/ late** (up to 6 weeks).
:::

@fig uterine_involution.svg | Uterus ka involution aur lochia ke types

### Involution of uterus
- Immediately after delivery – fundus **~at/ just below umbilicus (13.5 cm above symphysis)**, weight **~1000 g**, firm.
- Descends **~1 cm (1 finger breadth)/ day**; **not palpable abdominally by 10th–14th day**; **normal size (50–60 g) by 6 weeks**.
- Weight: 1000 g (birth) → **500 g (1 week)** → **300 g (2 weeks)** → **50–60 g (6 weeks)**.
- Mechanism: **autolysis** (proteolytic enzymes – muscle fibres shrink), **ischaemia** (living ligatures), **phagocytosis**; **after-pains** (contractions – multipara, during breastfeeding – oxytocin).
- **Subinvolution** – slow involution → causes: **retained bits**, **infection (endometritis)**, full bladder, fibroids, no breastfeeding, overdistension (twins); signs – fundus higher than expected, boggy, **persistent lochia rubra**, PPH (secondary).
- **Placental site** heals by exfoliation (no scar) by ~6 wks; **endometrium regenerates** – 3 weeks (except placental site).
- **Cervix** – external os **closes by ~1 week (admits 1 finger)**, becomes **transverse slit** (parous); **vagina** – rugae reappear by 3 wks.

### Lochia
| Type | Days | Colour & content |
|---|---|---|
| **Lochia rubra** | **1–4** | **Red** – blood, shreds of decidua, vernix, lanugo, meconium |
| **Lochia serosa** | **5–9 (4–10)** | **Pinkish-brown/ yellowish** – less RBC, more serum, leucocytes, cervical mucus |
| **Lochia alba** | **10 – 15 days (up to 6 wks)** | **Yellowish-white/ creamy** – leucocytes, decidual cells, mucus |

- Normal lochia – **fleshy, earthy (stale) odour**, alkaline, total ~250 ml. **Foul smell → infection**; **persistent rubra/ return of red lochia → subinvolution/ retained products**; **scanty/ absent with fever → infection (lochiometra)**.

### Other physiological changes
- **Vital signs** – temp slightly ↑ (< 38 °C in first 24 hrs – reactionary); **> 38 °C on 2 occasions (24 hrs apart) after first 24 hrs within 10 days = puerperal pyrexia** → investigate; pulse **normal/ slight bradycardia (50–70) first days** – tachycardia → PPH/ infection.
- **Diuresis** (day 2–5) – fluid excretion; urinary retention possible (oedema, epidural, trauma) – **void within 6 hrs**.
- **Weight loss** ~5–6 kg at delivery, further 2–3 kg in puerperium.
- **Blood** – leucocytosis, ↑ fibrinogen → **DVT risk (first 2 wks – highest)**.
- **Menstruation return** – non-lactating **6–8 weeks**; lactating – delayed (**LAM**); **ovulation may precede first period** → contraception.
- **Postpartum blues ("baby blues")** – **day 3–5**, 50–80 % mothers, weepy, mood swings, **self-limiting (≤ 2 wks)** → reassurance. **Postpartum depression** (10–15 %, > 2 wks) & **postpartum psychosis** (1–2/1000, first 2 wks – emergency) – see complications chapter.

## 9.2 Postnatal Care

- **Postnatal check-ups (India – MoHFW)**: **1st – within 24 hrs (at facility; stay 48 hrs after normal delivery)**, **2nd – day 3**, **3rd – day 7**, **4th – 6 weeks** (+ HBNC ASHA visits 3, 7, 14, 21, 28, 42).
- **WHO**: at least **4 PNC contacts** – first 24 hrs, day 3, day 7–14, 6 weeks.

:::key Postnatal nursing care – "BUBBLE-HE"
**B**reasts (engorgement, nipples, feeding) · **U**terus (fundal height, tone) · **B**ladder (voiding within 6–8 hrs, output) · **B**owels (constipation – fibre; first BM by day 2–3) · **L**ochia (amount, colour, odour) · **E**pisiotomy/ perineum (**REEDA** – Redness, Oedema, Ecchymosis, Discharge, Approximation) · **H**omans' sign/ legs (DVT) · **E**motions (bonding, blues)
:::

- **Vital signs** – first hour every 15 min, then 4 hrly first 24 hrs, then BD.
- **Early ambulation – within 6–8 hrs** (normal delivery) – prevents DVT, ↑ bladder & bowel function, involution.
- **Diet** – normal, nutritious, **+600 kcal**, fluids ≥ 3 L, iron-folic acid (**180 days**), **calcium (6 months)**; no food taboos.
- **Perineal hygiene**, **pads changed every 4–6 hrs**, wash front to back; sitz bath.
- **Rest** – sleep when baby sleeps; **Postnatal exercises** – **deep breathing, Kegel (pelvic floor), leg exercises (DVT)** from day 1; **abdominal exercises (head lift, pelvic tilt)** after 24 hrs/ 2nd day (normal delivery); after LSCS – gradually.
- **Family planning counselling** – **PPIUCD (within 48 hrs/ at LSCS)**, LAM, POP (progestin-only pills – from 6 wks), condoms, **tubectomy (within 7 days postpartum – minilap)**; **combined OCP avoid in lactation < 6 months**.
- **Immunization** of baby, **MCP card**; **danger signs** (heavy bleeding, foul lochia, fever, severe headache/ fits, breast pain with fever, calf pain, breathlessness, depression); **JSY payment**, birth registration (within 21 days).
- **Postnatal sexual activity** – after lochia stops & perineum heals (~6 wks).

## 9.3 Lactation & Breastfeeding

### Physiology
- **Mammogenesis** (breast growth – pregnancy) → **Lactogenesis I** (colostrum from 2nd trimester) → **Lactogenesis II** (copious milk **day 2–4** after placenta delivery – progesterone ↓, prolactin acts) → **Galactopoiesis** (maintenance – **suckling**).
- **Prolactin reflex (milk secretion/ production reflex)** – suckling → **anterior pituitary → prolactin** → milk production (more at **night**; **more suckling = more milk** – "demand & supply").
- **Let-down reflex (milk ejection/ oxytocin reflex)** – suckling/ baby's cry/ sight → **posterior pituitary → oxytocin** → **myoepithelial cells contract** → milk ejection; **inhibited by stress, pain, anxiety, embarrassment**; also causes **uterine contraction (after-pains) → involution**.

| Milk | Time | Features |
|---|---|---|
| **Colostrum** | **First 2–4 days** (10–40 ml/day) | **Yellow, thick**, **high protein, Vit A, IgA (secretory), lactoferrin, leucocytes**, **less fat & lactose**; **"first immunisation"**; laxative (meconium passage, ↓ jaundice) |
| **Transitional milk** | Day 4–14 | |
| **Mature milk** | After 2 weeks (~600–800 ml/day) | **Foremilk** – watery, thirst-quenching (lactose, water); **Hindmilk** – **rich in fat**, satiety, weight gain → **empty one breast fully before switching** |

- Breast milk composition (per 100 ml): **energy ~67–70 kcal**, protein **1.1 g** (whey : casein = **60 : 40**), fat 3.5–4 g, lactose 7 g, iron low but **50 % absorbed (lactoferrin)**; **low in Vit K & D**.
- Advantages – **baby**: ideal nutrition, **immunity (IgA, lactoferrin, lysozyme, bifidus factor)**, ↓ diarrhoea/ pneumonia/ otitis/ allergy/ NEC, ↓ SIDS, better IQ, bonding, always available, clean & warm; **mother**: **↓ PPH (oxytocin)**, faster involution, **LAM contraception**, ↓ **breast & ovarian cancer**, weight loss, economical.

:::key Breastfeeding recommendations (WHO/ IYCF)
- **Initiate within 1 hour of birth** (normal & LSCS – as soon as possible) – **skin-to-skin contact**.
- **Exclusive breastfeeding for 6 months** – **no water, honey, ghutti, prelacteal feeds** (prelacteals harmful).
- **Complementary feeding from 6 months (180 days)** + **continue breastfeeding up to 2 years or beyond**.
- **On demand (baby-led)** – **8–12 times/ 24 hrs** (at least every 2–3 hrs), day & night; **rooming-in**.
- **Baby-Friendly Hospital Initiative (BFHI – 1991, WHO/UNICEF) – 10 steps**; **MAA programme (2016)**; **IMS Act 1992 (amended 2003)** – bans promotion of infant milk substitutes & feeding bottles.
- **World Breastfeeding Week 1–7 August**.
:::

### Positioning & attachment
- **Position**: baby's **head & body in straight line**, **body close to mother (tummy-to-tummy)**, **facing breast – nose opposite nipple**, **whole body supported** (not just head/ shoulders). Holds – **cradle, cross-cradle (preterm), football/ underarm (LSCS, twins)**, side-lying (night, after CS).
- **Good attachment (latch) – 4 signs**: (1) **Chin touching breast**, (2) **mouth wide open**, (3) **lower lip turned outward (everted)**, (4) **more areola visible above than below** the mouth. + **slow deep sucks with pauses, audible swallowing**, no pain.
- **Adequacy of milk**: **urine ≥ 6 times/ day** (after day 5), **weight gain (regains birth weight by 10–14 days; ~20–30 g/day)**, baby satisfied, sleeps 2–3 hrs.
- **Burping** after feeds; breast **not washed with soap** before each feed (normal bathing ok); **apply hindmilk on nipples** after feed (healing).

### Breastfeeding problems
| Problem | Cause | Management |
|---|---|---|
| **Sore/ cracked nipples** | **Poor attachment (most common)** | **Correct attachment**, start with less sore side, apply **hindmilk**, air dry; continue feeding |
| **Breast engorgement** | Milk stasis (day 3–5), infrequent feeds | **Frequent feeding**, **warm compress before feed**, **express milk**, **cold compress after**, supportive bra, analgesics |
| **Blocked duct** | Localized stasis | Massage toward nipple, frequent feeding |
| **Mastitis** | Infection (Staph aureus) via cracks | **Continue breastfeeding (from both breasts)**, antibiotics (cloxacillin/ flucloxacillin), analgesics, rest, fluids |
| **Breast abscess** | Untreated mastitis | **Incision & drainage/ needle aspiration**, antibiotics; feed from healthy side, express affected |
| **Flat/ inverted nipples** | | Attachment (baby takes areola, not nipple); **syringe method** (inverted syringe suction) after birth; **Hoffman's exercises (antenatal) not recommended routinely** |
| **"Not enough milk"** | Perceived; poor attachment/ infrequent feeds | Frequent feeding, correct latch, rest, reassurance, ↑ fluids; galactagogues (no strong evidence) |

- **Expressed breast milk (EBM)**: **hand expression** (thumb & index at areolar edge, press back toward chest, compress, release); store **room temp (≤ 25 °C) – 6–8 hrs (4 hrs per some)**, **refrigerator – 24 hrs (up to 3–5 days)**, **freezer – 3 months**; feed with **katori-spoon/ paladai** (not bottle).
- **Contraindications to breastfeeding**: **galactosemia (classic – baby)**, maternal **HIV (where safe replacement available; in India – breastfeed with ART)**, **active untreated TB** (may breastfeed after 2 wks of Rx/ with mask; baby INH prophylaxis), **anticancer drugs, radioactive iodine**, lithium, amiodarone; **NOT contraindicated**: hepatitis B (after HBIG/ vaccine), mastitis, common cold, maternal fever.
- **Suppression of lactation** (stillbirth/ medical): tight binder, ice packs, avoid stimulation, **cabergoline** (preferred) – bromocriptine (not routinely – stroke/ MI risk).

- Duration of puerperium – **6 weeks**.
- Uterus not palpable abdominally by – **10–14 days**; normal size by – **6 weeks**.
- Lochia rubra – **1–4 days**; serosa – **5–9**; alba – **10 days to 6 wks**.
- Puerperal pyrexia – **≥ 38 °C** on 2 occasions after 24 hrs within 10 days.
- Postpartum blues – **day 3–5**, self-limiting.
- Hormone for milk production – **prolactin**; ejection – **oxytocin**.
- Colostrum rich in – **protein, IgA, Vit A** (less fat).
- Breastfeeding initiation – **within 1 hr**; exclusive – **6 months**.
- Sign of good attachment – **chin touching breast, more areola above**.
- Most common cause of sore nipple – **poor attachment**.
- Mastitis – **continue breastfeeding**.
- EBM in refrigerator – **24 hrs**.
- Absolute contraindication (baby) – **galactosemia**.
:::
