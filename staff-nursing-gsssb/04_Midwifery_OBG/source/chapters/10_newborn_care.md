## 10.1 Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Neonate** | Birth to **28 days** |
| **Early neonatal** | First **7 days** |
| **Term** | 37–42 wks |
| **LBW (Low birth weight)** | **< 2500 g** (irrespective of gestation) |
| **VLBW** | **< 1500 g** |
| **ELBW** | **< 1000 g** |
| **SGA** | Weight **< 10th percentile** for gestational age |
| **LGA** | > 90th percentile (e.g. diabetic mother) |
| **IUGR/ FGR** | Fetus failed to achieve growth potential – **symmetrical** (early – genetic/ infection; head & body both small) vs **asymmetrical** (late – placental insufficiency; **head spared**, AC small – more common) |

- **Average Indian newborn**: weight **~2.8–3 kg**, length **50 cm**, **head circumference 33–35 cm** (> chest by 1–2 cm), chest 31–33 cm. **Weight loss up to 10 % in first week**, **regains birth weight by 10–14 days**; **doubles by 5 months**, **triples by 1 year**.

## 10.2 Immediate / Essential Newborn Care (ENBC)

:::key Immediate care at birth (sequence)
1. **Note time of birth & sex**; call out
2. **Dry the baby immediately with warm towel** (head first), **discard wet towel** – stimulates breathing & prevents hypothermia
3. **Assess breathing/ crying** – if breathing well → **skin-to-skin on mother's abdomen**, cover with dry cloth & cap
4. **Delayed cord clamping 1–3 min** (or when pulsation stops); cut with sterile blade **2–3 cm from abdomen**, clamp/ tie; **dry cord care** (nothing applied)
5. **Initiate breastfeeding within 1 hour**
6. **Eye care** (clean from inner to outer canthus) – prophylaxis per policy
7. **Vitamin K 1 mg IM** (anterolateral thigh) – **0.5 mg if < 1500 g** – prevents **haemorrhagic disease of newborn**
8. **Weigh** (after first feed), **identification band**, examine for anomalies, **APGAR at 1 & 5 min**
9. **Birth dose vaccines** – **BCG** (intradermal, left upper arm, **0.05 ml** if < 1 month; 0.1 ml after 1 month), **OPV-0** (oral 2 drops), **Hepatitis B birth dose** (**within 24 hrs**, IM thigh, 0.5 ml)
10. **Delay bathing – at least 24 hrs** (WHO; Indian – after 24 hrs/ 48 hrs; LBW – later); **don't apply anything on cord/ eyes/ ears**; **no prelacteal feeds**
:::

### APGAR score (Virginia Apgar, 1952)
| Sign | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A**ppearance (colour) | Blue/ pale all over | Body pink, **extremities blue (acrocyanosis)** | Completely pink |
| **P**ulse (heart rate) | Absent | **< 100** | **≥ 100** |
| **G**rimace (reflex irritability) | No response | Grimace | Cry/ cough/ sneeze |
| **A**ctivity (muscle tone) | Limp | Some flexion | Active movement |
| **R**espiration | Absent | Slow, irregular, weak cry | Good, strong cry |

- Done at **1 min & 5 min** (and 10 min if < 7); **7–10 normal**, **4–6 moderate depression**, **0–3 severe asphyxia**. **Resuscitation is NOT delayed for APGAR**. Most babies score 1 for colour (acrocyanosis) → **9 typical**.
- **Heart rate is the most important** sign during resuscitation.

### Neonatal resuscitation (NRP/ NSSK – "Golden Minute")
- **Initial steps (within 30 sec)**: **provide warmth (radiant warmer)**, **position – neck slightly extended ("sniffing")**, **clear airway if needed** (mouth before nose – "**M before N**"), **dry**, **stimulate** (flick soles/ rub back), reposition.
- **Not breathing/ gasping or HR < 100** → **positive pressure ventilation (PPV) with bag & mask within 1 minute (golden minute)** – rate **40–60 breaths/min**, room air (term) / 21–30 % O₂ (preterm); check chest rise (**MR SOPA** for corrections).
- **HR < 60 after 30 sec effective PPV** → **chest compressions** (2-thumb technique, lower third sternum, **3 : 1 ratio – 90 compressions + 30 breaths/min**) + **100 % O₂**.
- **HR < 60 despite** → **adrenaline (epinephrine) 1 : 10,000 – 0.1–0.3 ml/kg IV (umbilical vein)/ 0.5–1 ml/kg ET**.
- **Meconium-stained, non-vigorous baby** → **no routine intubation & tracheal suction** (current guidelines) – start PPV.
- Bag size – **240–250 ml (self-inflating)**; mask covers chin, mouth, nose.

## 10.3 Normal Newborn – Features & Physiological Phenomena

- **Vital signs**: **HR 120–160 bpm** (apical), **RR 30–60/min** (abdominal, irregular, periodic breathing pauses < 15–20 sec normal; **apnoea ≥ 20 sec**), **temperature 36.5–37.5 °C** (axillary), BP ~70/45.
- **Skin**: **vernix caseosa** (don't rub off), **lanugo**, **milia** (white spots on nose – sebaceous), **Mongolian spots** (bluish – sacral; fade), **erythema toxicum** (red rash – day 2–3, benign), **stork bites** (naevus simplex), **desquamation** (post-term), **acrocyanosis** (first 24–48 hrs normal), **physiological jaundice**.
- **Head**: moulding, caput, cephalhaematoma, **fontanelles**.
- **Eyes**: subconjunctival haemorrhage (benign), no tears for first weeks.
- **Mouth**: **Epstein's pearls** (white cysts on palate), natal teeth (rare), tongue-tie.
- **Breast engorgement** (both sexes – maternal hormones, day 3–5) – **don't squeeze** ("witch's milk"); **pseudomenstruation** (vaginal bleeding in girls – day 3–7, oestrogen withdrawal), **white vaginal discharge**.
- **Umbilical cord** – **2 arteries, 1 vein**; falls **5–10 days**.
- **Meconium** – first stool, **greenish-black, tarry, odourless** – passed **within 24 hrs (90 %), all by 48 hrs** (delay → Hirschsprung's, meconium ileus – cystic fibrosis, imperforate anus); **transitional stools** (day 3–5, greenish-yellow); **breastfed – golden yellow, soft, frequent**.
- **Urine** – first void **within 24 hrs** (most by 48 hrs).

### Newborn reflexes
| Reflex | Elicitation / Response | Disappears |
|---|---|---|
| **Moro (startle)** | Sudden drop of head/ loud noise → **arms abduct & extend, then adduct (embrace)**, cry | **3–6 months**; **asymmetric → brachial plexus palsy (Erb's), clavicle fracture** |
| **Rooting** | Stroke cheek → turns head toward stimulus, opens mouth | 3–4 months |
| **Sucking** | Object in mouth → sucks | ~4 months (becomes voluntary) |
| **Palmar grasp** | Finger in palm → grasps | **3–4 months** |
| **Plantar grasp** | Pressure on sole → toes curl | **8–12 months** |
| **Babinski** | Stroke sole → **toes fan, big toe dorsiflexes** (positive normal) | **12–24 months** |
| **Tonic neck (fencing)** | Head turned → arm & leg extend on that side, flex opposite | 4–6 months |
| **Stepping/ walking** | Held upright, feet touching surface → stepping | 2 months |
| **Gallant (trunk incurvation)**, **Blink**, **Swallowing**, **Gag**, **Sneeze** | | |

## 10.4 Thermal Protection

- Newborn loses heat fast (large surface area, thin skin, little fat) – **evaporation (wet), conduction (cold surface), convection (draught), radiation (cold walls)**; heat produced by **brown fat (non-shivering thermogenesis)** – newborns **can't shiver**.
- **Warm chain (WHO 10 steps)**: warm delivery room (**25–28 °C**), immediate drying, **skin-to-skin**, breastfeeding, **postpone bathing & weighing**, appropriate clothing & bedding (cap, socks), mother & baby together (rooming-in), warm transport, warm resuscitation, training.
- **Hypothermia**: **cold stress 36–36.4 °C**, **moderate 32–35.9 °C**, **severe < 32 °C** → skin-to-skin (KMC), radiant warmer, warm room; signs – cold extremities, lethargy, poor feeding, **hypoglycaemia**, apnoea, **sclerema**.
- **Temperature check**: axillary **3 min** (digital as per device); feel **abdomen & feet** (warm abdomen + cold feet = cold stress).

## 10.5 Neonatal Jaundice

| | **Physiological** | **Pathological** |
|---|---|---|
| Onset | **After 24 hrs (day 2–3)** | **Within first 24 hrs** |
| Peak | **Day 3–5 (term)**; day 5–7 (preterm) | Any |
| Serum bilirubin | **< 12–15 mg/dL (term)**; rise < 5 mg/dL/day | **> 15 mg/dL**, rise > 5 mg/dL/day, **conjugated > 2 mg/dL** |
| Duration | **Disappears by 10–14 days (term)**, 3 wks (preterm) | **> 2 weeks (term)**, > 3 wks (preterm) |
| Cause | ↑ RBC breakdown (HbF, short RBC life 70–90 days), **immature liver (glucuronyl transferase ↓)**, ↑ enterohepatic circulation | **Rh/ ABO incompatibility (haemolysis)**, **G6PD deficiency**, sepsis, hypothyroidism, **biliary atresia (clay stools, dark urine)**, breast milk jaundice |

- **Kramer's rule** (cephalocaudal spread): face (~5 mg) → chest (~10) → abdomen/ thigh (~12) → legs (~15) → **palms & soles (> 15 mg/dL)**.
- **Treatment**: **adequate breastfeeding (8–12/day)**, **phototherapy (blue light 425–475 nm)** – converts bilirubin to water-soluble **lumirubin** (excreted); **exchange transfusion** (severe – > 20–25 mg/dL, rising; **double volume – 160–170 ml/kg**).
- **Phototherapy nursing care**: **eyes covered (eye shields – retinal damage)**, **genitals covered (diaper – gonads)**, baby **naked** (maximum skin exposed), **change position every 2–3 hrs**, distance **30–45 cm**, **monitor temperature** (hyper/ hypothermia), **↑ fluids/ breastfeeding (insensible loss ↑ 20 %)**, daily weight, stool (green, loose – normal), skin rash (normal), **bronze baby syndrome** (conjugated hyperbilirubinaemia – stop), check bilirubin 12–24 hrly; **sunlight exposure not recommended**.
- **Kernicterus** – unconjugated bilirubin deposits in **basal ganglia** → lethargy, poor suck, **opisthotonus, high-pitched cry, seizures**, sequelae – **athetoid cerebral palsy, deafness**, gaze palsy.

## 10.6 Preterm & LBW Baby

- **Preterm features**: **< 37 wks**, weight < 2500 g, **large head relative to body**, thin shiny red/ gelatinous skin, **plenty of lanugo & vernix**, **soft ear cartilage (no recoil)**, **flat/ absent breast nodule (< 5 mm)**, **few sole creases (anterior ⅓ only)**, **testes undescended**/ **labia majora don't cover minora**, **poor tone (extended limbs – "frog position")**, weak cry & suck, **poor reflexes**. **New Ballard score** – gestational age assessment.
- **Problems**: **hypothermia**, **hypoglycaemia (< 45 mg/dL)**, **RDS (hyaline membrane disease – surfactant deficiency)**, **apnoea of prematurity (caffeine)**, infections, **jaundice**, feeding difficulty, **NEC**, **IVH**, **ROP (retinopathy of prematurity – O₂ toxicity → screen at 4 wks / < 34 wks or < 2000 g)**, PDA, anaemia, BPD.
- **Antenatal steroids** (mother – preterm labour 24–34 wks) – **dexamethasone 6 mg IM 12 hrly × 4 doses** or **betamethasone 12 mg IM 24 hrly × 2** → **↓ RDS, IVH, NEC**.
- **Feeding**: **breast milk best**; can't suck → **expressed breast milk by paladai/ katori-spoon** (≥ 32 wks) or **orogastric tube** (< 32 wks); start **60–80 ml/kg/day day 1**, ↑ by 15–30 ml/kg/day to **150–180 ml/kg/day**.

:::key Kangaroo Mother Care (KMC)
- Developed **Bogotá, Colombia (1978 – Rey & Martinez)**; for **LBW (< 2000 g – India), stable** babies.
- Components: (1) **Skin-to-skin contact** – baby **naked except nappy, cap & socks**, upright between mother's breasts, **frog position**, head turned to one side, slightly extended (airway), tied with cloth/ binder; (2) **Exclusive breastfeeding**; (3) **Early discharge & follow-up**.
- Duration – **as long as possible (≥ 1 hr per session; ideally 24 hrs – continuous)**; father/ relatives can also give.
- Benefits – **thermal control**, ↓ infection, ↑ breastfeeding & weight gain, bonding, ↓ mortality (~40 %), earlier discharge.
- **WHO 2022 – immediate KMC** right after birth for LBW babies.
:::

- **Newborn screening** – hypothyroidism (TSH), G6PD, CAH, PKU, galactosaemia, hearing (OAE), CCHD (pulse oximetry), hip (Ortolani & Barlow – DDH).
- **Danger signs in newborn (IMNCI)**: **not feeding well**, **convulsions**, **fast breathing (≥ 60/min)**, **severe chest indrawing**, **temperature ≥ 37.5 or < 35.5 °C**, **movement only when stimulated/ no movement**, jaundice on palms & soles/ first 24 hrs, umbilical redness/ pus, bulging fontanelle.

- Neonatal CPR ratio – **3 : 1**.
- Vitamin K dose term – **1 mg IM**.

- Physiological jaundice appears – **after 24 hrs**, peaks **day 3–5**.

:::
