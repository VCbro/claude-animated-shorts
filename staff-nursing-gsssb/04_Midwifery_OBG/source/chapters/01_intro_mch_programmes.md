## 1.1 Midwifery – Basics

:::def Definitions
- **Midwife (ICM/ WHO)**: A person who has successfully completed a recognised midwifery education programme and is qualified & licensed to practise midwifery – gives care during **pregnancy, labour, postpartum, newborn**, conducts normal deliveries on her own responsibility.
- **Obstetrics** – Latin "*obstetrix*" = **midwife** ("one who stands before" the woman). Branch dealing with **pregnancy, labour & puerperium**.
- **Gynaecology** – diseases of female reproductive organs (non-pregnant).
:::

- **Father of Obstetrics** – **William Smellie** (British; forceps & pelvic measurements). **Hippocrates** – father of medicine. **Ignaz Semmelweis** – **puerperal fever** prevention by **hand washing (1847)**. **James Young Simpson** – chloroform in labour. **Chamberlen family** – inventors of **obstetric forceps**. **Dr. Anandibai Joshi** – first Indian woman doctor (1886).
- **ICM** (International Confederation of Midwives) – 1922; **5 May = International Day of the Midwife**.
- India – **Nurse Practitioner in Midwifery (NPM)** cadre (2018 guidelines) – midwifery-led care units; **"Midwifery Initiative" 2018**.

### Important obstetric terms
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Gravida** | Number of **pregnancies** (including present), irrespective of outcome |
| **Para** | Number of pregnancies **delivered beyond viability (≥ 20/ 28 wks)**, alive or dead (twins = 1 para) |
| **Primigravida** | Pregnant for the **first time** |
| **Multigravida** | Pregnant **2nd time or more** |
| **Nullipara** | Never delivered beyond viability |
| **Primipara** | Delivered once |
| **Multipara** | Delivered 2–4 times |
| **Grand multipara** | **Delivered ≥ 5 times** (risk – PPH, rupture, malpresentation) |
| **Elderly primigravida** | First pregnancy at **≥ 30 yrs (FOGSI: 35)** |
| **GTPAL** | **G**ravida, **T**erm, **P**reterm, **A**bortions, **L**iving children |
| **Viability** | Fetus can survive outside uterus – India **28 wks** (MTP/ legal – 20–24 wks; WHO 22 wks / 500 g) |
| **Term** | **37–42 wks** (early term 37–38+6, full term 39–40+6, late 41–41+6, post-term ≥ 42) |
| **Preterm** | **< 37 completed weeks** |
| **Post-term** | **≥ 42 wks** |
| **Puerperium** | **6 weeks (42 days)** after delivery |
| **Abortion** | Termination **before viability (< 20 wks / < 500 g WHO; India < 28 wks)** |
| **Stillbirth** | Fetal death **≥ 28 wks** (born without life) |

:::exam GTPAL example
Woman pregnant now, has 1 term baby (alive), 1 preterm (alive), 1 abortion → **G4 P2 (T1 P1 A1 L2)**. Twins count as **1 para but 2 living**.
:::

## 1.2 Maternal & Child Health Indicators

| Indicator | Definition | Recent India value (SRS) |
|---|---|---|
| **MMR (Maternal Mortality Ratio)** | Maternal deaths **per 1,00,000 live births** | **93 (SRS 2019–21)**; 97 (2018–20); SDG target **< 70 by 2030** |
| **Maternal death (WHO)** | Death during pregnancy or **within 42 days** of termination, from any cause related to/ aggravated by pregnancy (not accidental) | Leading causes – **PPH (most common)**, hypertensive disorders, sepsis, abortion, obstructed labour |
| **IMR (Infant Mortality Rate)** | Infant (< 1 yr) deaths **per 1000 live births** | **28 (SRS 2020)**, **27 (SRS 2021)** |
| **NMR (Neonatal Mortality Rate)** | Deaths < 28 days per 1000 live births | **~19–20** (SRS 2021: 19) |
| **PMR (Perinatal Mortality Rate)** | Stillbirths (≥ 28 wks) + early neonatal deaths (< 7 days) **per 1000 total births** | ~18 |
| **U5MR** | Under-5 deaths per 1000 live births | ~31 (2021) |
| **TFR (Total Fertility Rate)** | Average children per woman | **2.0 (NFHS-5)** – below replacement (2.1) |

- **Kerala** – lowest MMR & IMR; **Assam / MP** – highest MMR/IMR historically.
- **Gujarat MMR** ~57 (SRS 2018–20); **Gujarat IMR** ~23 (2020).
- **Leading cause of maternal death** – **haemorrhage (PPH)**; **neonatal death** – **prematurity/ LBW**, birth asphyxia, sepsis.
- **3 Delays model**: (1) delay in **deciding to seek care**, (2) delay in **reaching** facility, (3) delay in **receiving** care.

## 1.3 National Programmes & Schemes (very important for GSSSB)

| Scheme | Year | Key points |
|---|---|---|
| **RCH-I / RCH-II** | **1997 / 2005** | Reproductive & Child Health programme |
| **NRHM → NHM** | **2005 / 2013** | ASHA (1 per 1000), JSY, VHSNC |
| **Janani Suraksha Yojana (JSY)** | **12 April 2005** | **Cash incentive for institutional delivery** (BPL/ SC/ ST); LPS (low performing states) rural mother **₹1400**, ASHA ₹600; HPS rural ₹700 |
| **Janani Shishu Suraksha Karyakram (JSSK)** | **1 June 2011** | **Free & cashless** delivery incl. **C-section**, drugs, diagnostics, diet (3 days normal, 7 days CS), blood, **free transport** (home → facility → referral → drop back); **sick newborns up to 1 year** |
| **RMNCH+A** | **2013** | Reproductive, Maternal, Newborn, Child + Adolescent – **continuum of care**, "**5×5 matrix**" |
| **Pradhan Mantri Surakshit Matritva Abhiyan (PMSMA)** | **2016** | **Free ANC by specialist/ MO on 9th of every month** (2nd/3rd trimester); stickers – **red = high risk**, green = no risk; **e-PMSMA / Extended PMSMA (2022)** – HRP tracking, ₹ incentives |
| **Pradhan Mantri Matru Vandana Yojana (PMMVY)** | **2017** (Maternity Benefit) | **₹5000** for **first live child** (now 2 instalments; ₹6000 incl. JSY); **PMMVY 2.0 (Mission Shakti, 2022)** – **2nd child girl → ₹6000** |
| **LaQshya** | **2017** | **Labour room & maternity OT quality improvement** |
| **MusQan** | 2021 | Child-friendly quality services in public facilities |
| **SUMAN (Surakshit Matritva Aashwasan)** | **10 Oct 2019** | **Zero tolerance for preventable maternal & newborn deaths**, assured free services, respectful care |
| **Mission Indradhanush / IMI** | **25 Dec 2014** | Full immunization (children & pregnant women) |
| **Anaemia Mukt Bharat** | **2018** | **6×6×6 strategy**; pregnant: **IFA 60 mg + 500 µg folic acid daily from 14–16 wks for 180 days** (+180 PNC) |
| **POSHAN Abhiyaan** | **8 March 2018** | Nutrition mission (Jhunjhunu, Rajasthan) |
| **Home Based Newborn Care (HBNC)** | 2011 | ASHA visits – **institutional delivery: 6 visits (day 3, 7, 14, 21, 28, 42)**; home delivery – **7 (day 1 extra)** |
| **HBYC (Home Based Young Child care)** | 2018 | ASHA visits at 3, 6, 9, 12, 15 months |
| **Navjaat Shishu Suraksha Karyakram (NSSK)** | 2009 | Basic newborn care & resuscitation training |
| **Facility-based newborn care** | – | **SNCU** (district), **NBSU** (FRU), **NBCC** (every delivery point) |
| **MAA (Mothers' Absolute Affection)** | **2016** | **Breastfeeding promotion** |
| **Rashtriya Bal Swasthya Karyakram (RBSK)** | **2013** | **4 Ds** – Defects at birth, Diseases, Deficiencies, Development delays (0–18 yrs) |
| **RKSK** | **2014** | Adolescent health (10–19 yrs) |
| **Mission Parivar Vikas** | 2016 | Family planning in high TFR districts |
| **Kilkari / Mobile Academy** | 2016 | Weekly voice messages to pregnant women |
| **MCP card (Mother & Child Protection card)** | – | Joint MoHFW–MWCD card; ANC, delivery, immunization, growth chart |
| **RCH portal / ANMOL** | – | Name-based tracking of mothers & children |

- **Gujarat schemes**: **Chiranjeevi Yojana (2005)** – PPP model, private obstetricians deliver BPL women free (**UN award**); **Kasturba Poshan Sahay Yojana**; **Bal Sakha Yojana** (newborn care in private hospitals); **Khilkhilat** – free drop-back van service (2012); **108 ambulance** (2007); **Mamta Divas** – VHND (Village Health & Nutrition Day); **Poshan Sudha**; **Mukhyamantri Matrushakti Yojana (2022)** – take-home ration for pregnant & lactating mothers.
- **Days**: **International Day of the Midwife – 5 May**, **National Safe Motherhood Day – 11 April** (Kasturba Gandhi's birthday; White Ribbon Alliance), **World Breastfeeding Week – 1–7 Aug**, **Newborn Care Week – 15–21 Nov**, **World Prematurity Day – 17 Nov**, **World Population Day – 11 July**, **World Contraception Day – 26 Sept**.
