# Bleeding in Pregnancy – Abortion, Ectopic, Mole & APH
<!-- desc: Types of abortion, septic abortion, MTP Act, ectopic pregnancy, hydatidiform mole, cervical incompetence, placenta praevia, abruptio placentae, vasa praevia -->

## 12.1 Early Pregnancy Bleeding – Causes
**Abortion (most common)**, **ectopic pregnancy**, **hydatidiform mole**, implantation bleed, cervical lesions (polyp, ectropion, cancer).

## 12.2 Abortion (Miscarriage)

:::def Abortion
Expulsion/ extraction of products of conception **before viability** – **WHO: < 20 wks or < 500 g**; India (traditional) **< 28 wks**. **Spontaneous abortion** – ~10–15 % of clinical pregnancies; mostly **first trimester (~80 %)**; **most common cause – chromosomal (genetic) anomalies (~50 %)** (trisomy 16 commonest). Second trimester – **cervical incompetence**, uterine anomalies, APLA.
:::

| Type | Bleeding | Pain | Os | Uterus size | Management |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Threatened** | **Slight** | Mild/ none | **Closed** | = dates | **Rest**, avoid intercourse, progesterone (selected), reassurance, USG – pregnancy **continues (~80 %)** |
| **Inevitable** | More | **Painful cramps** | **Open** (products felt/ not yet expelled) | = dates | **Evacuation** (MVA/ suction), oxytocin/ misoprostol |
| **Incomplete** | **Heavy, continuous** | Present | **Open**, products partly passed | **< dates** | **Evacuation (MVA/ D&E)**, misoprostol, blood, antibiotics |
| **Complete** | **Little/ stopped** | Subsides | **Closed** | **Smaller, firm** | USG confirm; no evacuation; anti-D |
| **Missed** (silent) | Brownish discharge/ none | None | Closed | **< dates**, symptoms of pregnancy regress | **Mifepristone + misoprostol** / evacuation; **DIC risk (retained > 4 wks)** – check coagulation |
| **Septic** | Foul discharge | + **fever, tenderness** | Open | – | **Antibiotics (broad-spectrum)**, evacuation after 6 hrs of antibiotics, IV fluids, tetanus; complications – **septic shock, DIC, AKI, peritonitis** |
| **Recurrent (habitual)** | **≥ 2–3 consecutive** spontaneous abortions | | | | Investigate – karyotype, APLA (aspirin + heparin), thyroid, diabetes, uterine anomaly, **cervical incompetence (cerclage)** |

- **Rh-negative** mother – **anti-D** after any abortion (< 12 wks 50 µg; > 12 wks 300 µg).
- **Cervical incompetence (insufficiency)** – **painless dilatation** in **2nd trimester** → recurrent mid-trimester abortion/ preterm (cause – previous D&C, cone biopsy, congenital) → **cervical cerclage at 12–14 wks** – **McDonald's (purse-string – most common, removed at 37 wks)** / **Shirodkar's** (submucosal – may be permanent, LSCS); remove earlier if labour starts/ ROM/ infection.

### MTP Act (Medical Termination of Pregnancy)
- **MTP Act 1971** (effective 1 April 1972); **MTP (Amendment) Act 2021** (rules 2021).

| Gestation | Opinion required |
|---|---|
| **Up to 20 weeks** | **1 Registered Medical Practitioner (RMP)** |
| **20–24 weeks** | **2 RMPs** – for **special categories** (rape survivors, incest, **minors**, change of marital status – widowhood/ divorce, disability, mentally ill, **fetal malformation**, humanitarian/ disaster) |
| **Beyond 24 weeks** | Only for **substantial fetal abnormalities** – **State-level Medical Board** decision |
| **Any time** | To **save life of the woman** – **1 RMP (immediate)** |

- Grounds: risk to life/ grave injury to **physical or mental health**, substantial fetal abnormality, rape (mental anguish), **contraceptive failure – now for any woman (married or unmarried) – 2021**.
- **Consent – only woman's** (if **≥ 18 yrs & sane**); **minor (< 18) or mentally ill – written consent of guardian**. **Husband's consent NOT required**.
- **Confidentiality** – name of woman can't be revealed (except to authorised person) – punishable.
- Place – **government/ approved facility**; **medical abortion (MMA) up to 9 weeks (63 days)** – **Mifepristone 200 mg oral (day 1) → Misoprostol 800 µg buccal/ vaginal/ sublingual after 24–48 hrs (day 3)** – by RMP; follow-up day 15.
- **Surgical**: **MVA (Manual Vacuum Aspiration – up to 12 wks)**, **suction evacuation & curettage (EVA)**, **D&E (2nd trimester)**; 2nd trimester – **misoprostol/ mifepristone**, extra-amniotic ethacridine (old), oxytocin.
- **PCPNDT Act 1994** (amended 2003) – **prohibits sex determination**.

## 12.3 Ectopic Pregnancy

- **Implantation outside uterine cavity**; **most common site – fallopian tube (~95–97 %)** – **ampulla (most common, ~70–80 %)** > isthmus (ruptures earliest – 6–8 wks) > fimbrial > **interstitial (cornual – latest & most dangerous rupture ~12–16 wks, massive bleeding)**; others – ovary, cervix, abdomen, **caesarean scar**.
- **Risk factors**: **previous ectopic (strongest)**, **PID (Chlamydia/ gonorrhoea – most common cause)**, **tubal surgery/ tubectomy failure**, **IUCD in situ** (if pregnancy occurs), **ART/ IVF**, smoking, endometriosis, progestin-only pills.
- **Classical triad**: **amenorrhoea (6–8 wks) + abdominal pain (most common symptom) + vaginal bleeding (spotting)**.
- **Ruptured ectopic** – **sudden severe lower abdominal pain**, **shoulder tip pain (Kehr's sign – diaphragmatic irritation by blood)**, fainting, **haemorrhagic shock (pallor, tachycardia, hypotension)**, **cervical motion tenderness (excitation)**, **tender fullness in pouch of Douglas**, abdominal distension, **Cullen's sign** (periumbilical bluish – rare).
- **Diagnosis**: **serum β-hCG + TVS** – **empty uterus with β-hCG above discriminatory zone (1500–2000 mIU/ml – TVS)** → ectopic; **β-hCG fails to double in 48 hrs**; **culdocentesis** (non-clotting blood from POD – old); **laparoscopy – gold standard**.
- **Management**:
 - **Ruptured – EMERGENCY**: **treat shock (2 large-bore IV, blood, cross-match)** + **immediate laparotomy/ laparoscopy – salpingectomy**; consent, NPO.
 - **Unruptured, stable** – **medical: Methotrexate 50 mg/m² IM** (criteria – haemodynamically stable, **sac < 3.5–4 cm**, **no cardiac activity**, **β-hCG < 5000**) – follow β-hCG day 4 & 7 (≥ 15 % fall); **avoid folic acid, NSAIDs, alcohol, intercourse, sun**; laparoscopic **salpingostomy**; expectant (low hCG, declining).
 - **Anti-D** if Rh –ve.

## 12.4 Gestational Trophoblastic Disease – Hydatidiform Mole

- **Abnormal proliferation of trophoblast**; villi become **grape-like vesicles** ("**bunch of grapes**").
- **Complete mole** – **46 XX (paternal origin – empty ovum fertilized by sperm)**, **no fetus**, **higher malignancy risk (15–20 %)**. **Partial mole** – **triploid (69 XXY)**, fetal parts present, lower risk.
- Risk – **extremes of age (< 20, > 35–40)**, previous mole, Asian, low protein/ carotene diet.
- **Clinical**: **amenorrhoea + vaginal bleeding (most common)** with **passage of grape-like vesicles (pathognomonic)**, **uterus larger than dates (doughy/ soft)**, **absent FHS & fetal parts**, **hyperemesis**, **early pre-eclampsia (< 20 wks)**, **hyperthyroidism**, **theca-lutein cysts** (bilateral ovarian), anaemia.
- **Investigations**: **very high β-hCG** (> 1,00,000 mIU/ml), **USG – "snowstorm appearance" / "bunch of grapes/ honeycomb"**; chest X-ray (metastasis).
- **Management**: **suction evacuation (treatment of choice)** + oxytocin drip after start of evacuation, blood ready, **histopathology**; hysterectomy (older women, family complete); **anti-D**.
- **Follow-up (very important)**: **serum β-hCG weekly till negative × 3 consecutive weeks**, then **monthly for 6 months** (complete mole); **contraception – OCPs/ barrier for 6–12 months (avoid pregnancy & IUCD till hCG normal)**. Persistent/ rising hCG → **GTN (invasive mole/ choriocarcinoma)** → **methotrexate/ actinomycin-D (chemosensitive – high cure)**. **Choriocarcinoma** – metastasis to **lungs (most common)**, vagina, brain, liver.

## 12.5 Antepartum Haemorrhage (APH)

:::def APH
**Bleeding from genital tract after 20 weeks (India – 28 wks) of pregnancy and before birth of baby**. Causes: **placenta praevia (~1/3)**, **abruptio placentae (~1/3 – most common in some data)**, **unclassified/ indeterminate** (local lesions, vasa praevia, show).
:::

:::warn APH – golden rule
**NO PER VAGINAL (PV) EXAMINATION** in APH outside OT (can cause torrential bleeding in placenta praevia) – **only USG/ speculum (gentle)**; PV only in OT with everything ready for LSCS ("**double set-up examination**"). **No rectal exam, no enema**.
:::

| Feature | **Placenta Praevia** | **Abruptio Placentae** (accidental haemorrhage) |
|---|---|---|
| Definition | Placenta **implanted partly/ wholly in lower uterine segment** | **Premature separation of normally situated placenta** |
| Bleeding | **Painless, causeless, recurrent, bright red**, "**warning haemorrhage**" (often first at ~32–34 wks) | **Painful**, dark (old) blood, may be **concealed** (retroplacental) or revealed/ mixed |
| Shock | **Proportionate to visible blood loss** | **Out of proportion** to visible loss (concealed) |
| Uterus | **Soft, relaxed, non-tender** | **Tense, tender, hard ("woody/ board-like")**, ↑ size |
| Fetal parts / FHS | Easily felt; **FHS usually present** | Difficult to feel; **FHS often absent/ distress** |
| Presentation | **Malpresentation common**, **high/ floating head** (not engaged) | Normal, may be engaged |
| Associated | **Multiparity, previous LSCS/ scar, multiple pregnancy, previous praevia, smoking, advanced age** | **Pre-eclampsia/ HTN (most common association)**, trauma, **sudden decompression (polyhydramnios, twin delivery)**, short cord, smoking, cocaine, folic acid deficiency |
| Complications | PPH (LUS poor contractility), **placenta accreta**, preterm, malpresentation | **DIC (hypofibrinogenaemia)**, **Couvelaire uterus** (blood infiltrates myometrium – bluish, may still contract), **AKI**, PPH, **IUD** |
| Diagnosis | **USG (TVS safe)** | Clinical; USG (retroplacental clot – may miss) |

- **Degrees of placenta praevia**: **Type I (low-lying)** – lower segment but not reaching os; **Type II (marginal)** – reaches margin of internal os; **Type III (partial)** – partially covers os; **Type IV (complete/ central)** – completely covers os → **LSCS**. Types I & II anterior – vaginal delivery may be possible; **posterior type II ("dangerous placenta praevia")** – compresses presenting part/ cord.
- **Placenta praevia management**: **expectant – "Macafee & Johnson" regimen** (if bleeding not severe, < 37 wks, mother & fetus stable) – **hospitalization, bed rest, blood available (cross-matched 2 units), haematinics, steroids (< 34 wks), anti-D**, no PV/ intercourse; **deliver at 37 wks (36–37)**; **LSCS** for major degrees; **active (immediate delivery)** – heavy bleeding, labour, term, fetal distress/ death.
- **Abruption management**: **resuscitate (2 large-bore IV, blood, FFP/ cryo)**, **monitor coagulation (fibrinogen, clotting time – bedside clot test)**, **Foley catheter (urine output ≥ 30 ml/hr)**, **CVP**; **ARM (reduces pressure, speeds delivery, ↓ thromboplastin entry)** + oxytocin; **LSCS if fetus alive with distress/ not delivering quickly**; IUD → vaginal delivery preferred (if coagulopathy corrected); **watch PPH**.
- **Vasa praevia** – fetal vessels in membranes over os (velamentous cord) → **ROM → fetal bleeding** (painless bleed + **sudden fetal distress/ sinusoidal FHR**) → **emergency LSCS**; **Apt test** (fetal Hb – alkali resistant).

:::exam PYQ style
- Commonest cause of spontaneous abortion – **chromosomal anomalies**.
- Threatened abortion – **os closed**, slight bleeding; inevitable – **os open**.
- Incomplete abortion – **evacuation**; complete – **os closed, uterus small**.
- Cervical incompetence – **painless mid-trimester abortion**; Rx – **McDonald's cerclage at 12–14 wks**.
- MTP up to 20 wks – **1 RMP**; 20–24 wks (special) – **2 RMPs**; consent – **woman herself**.
- Medical abortion up to – **9 weeks** (mifepristone + misoprostol).
- Commonest site of ectopic – **ampulla**; most dangerous – **interstitial**.
- Classical triad of ectopic – **amenorrhoea, pain, bleeding**; shoulder tip pain – **Kehr's sign**.
- Unruptured ectopic medical Rx – **methotrexate**.
- Snowstorm appearance – **H. mole**; follow-up – **β-hCG**; contraception – **OCP (not IUCD)**.
- APH – **no PV examination**.
- Painless recurrent bright red bleeding – **placenta praevia**; painful with tense tender uterus – **abruptio**.
- Abruption associated with – **pre-eclampsia**; complication – **DIC, Couvelaire uterus**.
:::
