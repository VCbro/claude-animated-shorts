## 4.1 Placenta

@fig placenta_cord.svg | Placenta ki maternal & fetal surface aur cord

- Develops from **trophoblast (chorion frondosum – fetal part)** + **decidua basalis (maternal part)**; **fully formed by 12 weeks**; **functional from ~3rd week**.
- At term: **discoid**, **15–20 cm diameter**, **2.5–3 cm thick** (centre), weight **~500 g** (**ratio placenta : baby ≈ 1 : 6**), **15–20 cotyledons** on maternal surface.
- **Maternal surface** – rough, dark red, **cotyledons** → check for **completeness** (missing cotyledon → **PPH, infection**). **Fetal surface** – smooth, shiny, covered by **amnion**, umbilical vessels radiating.
- **Separation**: **Schultze** (central – **fetal surface first, "Shiny Schultze"**, more common ~80 %, less bleeding, retroplacental clot inside) vs **Matthews Duncan** (marginal – **maternal surface first, "Dirty Duncan"**, more bleeding, membranes may be retained).
- **Utero-placental blood flow at term ~500–750 ml/min**. Exchange in **intervillous space** – maternal & fetal blood **do not mix** (placental barrier).

### Functions of placenta
1. **Respiratory** – O₂ & CO₂ exchange (simple diffusion)
2. **Nutritive** – glucose (facilitated diffusion – main fuel), amino acids (active transport), vitamins, minerals, iron
3. **Excretory** – urea, uric acid, creatinine
4. **Protective/ barrier** – blocks many microorganisms & drugs (but **viruses, TORCH, alcohol, nicotine, many drugs cross**); **IgG crosses** (passive immunity – **only IgG**)
5. **Endocrine** – hormones:

| Hormone | Function / Point |
|---|---|
| **hCG** (human chorionic gonadotropin) | **Maintains corpus luteum**; basis of **pregnancy test**; detectable in serum **8–10 days after ovulation**; **peak 60–70 days (8–10 wks)**; very high in **molar pregnancy, twins**; low in ectopic/ abortion; hyperemesis linked |
| **hPL** (human placental lactogen)/ hCS | **Anti-insulin (diabetogenic)** – ↑ maternal blood glucose for fetus; ↑ with placental mass |
| **Oestrogen** (mainly **oestriol** – needs fetal adrenal & liver) | Uterine growth, breast ducts; oestriol = **fetal wellbeing marker** |
| **Progesterone** | **Maintains pregnancy** – uterine relaxation (quietening), breast alveoli; produced by placenta from ~10–12 wks |
| Relaxin, CRH, PAPP-A | Relaxin – pelvic joints soften; PAPP-A – first trimester screening |

### Placental abnormalities
| Abnormality | Description |
|---|---|
| **Succenturiate lobe** | Accessory lobe connected by vessels → **retained → PPH** (check membranes for torn vessels) |
| **Bipartite/ bilobed** | Two lobes |
| **Circumvallate** | Fetal surface with thick white ring (folded membranes) – APH, preterm |
| **Battledore placenta** | **Cord attached at margin** (edge) |
| **Velamentous insertion** | **Cord inserted into membranes**; vessels run in membranes → **vasa praevia** (fetal bleeding on ROM – fetal death) |
| **Placenta accreta / increta / percreta** | Abnormal adherence – villi into myometrium (**accreta** – superficial, **increta** – into myometrium, **percreta** – through serosa/ bladder); **previous LSCS + placenta praevia** = major risk → PPH, hysterectomy |
| **Placenta membranacea** | Thin, covering most of chorion |
| **Infarcts, calcification** | Post-term, pre-eclampsia |

## 4.2 Membranes & Amniotic Fluid

- **Amnion** (inner, fetal side; tough, avascular, no nerves) & **Chorion** (outer, adjacent to decidua). **Amniotic sac** = "bag of waters".
- **Amniotic fluid (liquor amnii)**: clear, pale straw, **alkaline (pH 7–7.5)**; **98–99 % water**; volume **~1000 ml at 36–38 wks (max)**, **~800 ml at term**, **50 ml at 12 wks**, 400 ml at 20 wks.
- Source – early: **amnion/ maternal transudate**; after 20 wks: **fetal urine (main)** + lung fluid. Removed by **fetal swallowing**.
- **Functions**: protection from trauma, **constant temperature**, free fetal movement (musculoskeletal development), **lung development**, prevents adhesions & cord compression, during labour – **forewaters dilate cervix**, flushes birth canal (antiseptic).
- **Test for ROM**: **Nitrazine paper** (alkaline liquor → **blue**), **fern test** (microscope – arborization), pooling on speculum, AmniSure.
- **Colour**: **clear** (normal), **green/ meconium-stained** (fetal distress, post-term, breech – may be normal), **golden yellow** (Rh isoimmunisation – bilirubin), **red/ blood-stained** (APH), **dark brown/ tobacco juice** (IUD – old), **greenish-yellow & foul** (infection – chorioamnionitis).

| | **Polyhydramnios** | **Oligohydramnios** |
|---|---|---|
| Definition | **> 2000 ml**; **AFI > 24–25 cm**; SDP > 8 cm | **< 200–500 ml**; **AFI < 5 cm**; SDP < 2 cm |
| Causes | **Fetal: oesophageal/ duodenal atresia (can't swallow)**, **anencephaly/ NTD**, twins (TTTS); **maternal diabetes**, Rh isoimmunisation, chorioangioma, idiopathic | **Renal agenesis (Potter's syndrome)**, **posterior urethral valve**, PROM, **IUGR/ placental insufficiency**, post-term |
| Effects | Malpresentation, **cord prolapse**, PROM, preterm labour, **PPH (overdistension)**, abruption (sudden decompression), dyspnoea | **Pulmonary hypoplasia**, limb deformities (club foot), cord compression, fetal distress, Potter's facies |
| Management | **Amniocentesis/ amnioreduction**, **indomethacin** (↓ fetal urine), treat cause; ARM slowly with care | Hydration, **amnioinfusion** (in labour), deliver if term |

- **AFI (Amniotic Fluid Index)** – sum of deepest vertical pockets in **4 quadrants** by USG; **normal 5–25 cm (8–18 optimal)**.

## 4.3 Umbilical Cord (Funis)

- Length **~50 cm (30–100)**; **short cord < 30 cm** (abruption, inversion), **long cord > 100 cm** (prolapse, knots, nuchal cord).
- **2 umbilical arteries** (deoxygenated – fetus → placenta) + **1 umbilical vein** (oxygenated – placenta → fetus) embedded in **Wharton's jelly** (protects from compression); **allantois** remnant.
- **Single umbilical artery** (~1 %) → associated with **renal & cardiac anomalies**, trisomy.
- **True knot** (danger – fetal death) vs **false knot** (vessel loop – harmless).
- Cord **cut** – **delayed cord clamping 1–3 min** (WHO; ↑ iron stores) unless asphyxiated baby needing resuscitation.
- **Cord blood** – **blood group, Rh, Coombs**, bilirubin, stem cell banking.
- Cord stump falls in **5–10 days** (dry cord care – nothing applied; chlorhexidine 7.1 % where high NMR).

## 4.4 Fetal Circulation

@fig fetal_circulation.svg | Fetal circulation – teeno shunts aur unke adult remnants

:::key Fetal circulation – key points
- **Placenta = fetal lung**; oxygenated blood via **umbilical vein** (SaO₂ ~80 % – **highest O₂ in fetus**).
- **3 shunts**:
 1. **Ductus venosus** – umbilical vein → **IVC** (**bypasses liver**) → closes in few days → **ligamentum venosum**
 2. **Foramen ovale** – **right atrium → left atrium** (**bypasses lungs**) → closes functionally at birth (↑ LA pressure) → **fossa ovalis**
 3. **Ductus arteriosus** – **pulmonary artery → aorta** (**bypasses lungs**) → functional closure **10–15 hrs**, anatomical **2–3 weeks** (↑ O₂, ↓ prostaglandins) → **ligamentum arteriosum**
- **Umbilical vein → ligamentum teres** (round ligament of liver); **umbilical arteries → medial umbilical ligaments**; hypogastric arteries proximal part → superior vesical arteries.
- Lungs fluid-filled, **high pulmonary vascular resistance** → blood bypasses lungs.
:::

- **Changes at birth**: first breath → lungs expand → **pulmonary resistance ↓** → more blood to lungs → **LA pressure ↑** → **foramen ovale closes**; cord clamped → placental circulation stops → **SVR ↑**; ↑ PaO₂ → **ductus arteriosus constricts**.
- **PDA** (patent ductus arteriosus) – preterm, rubella → **machinery murmur**; Rx **indomethacin/ ibuprofen/ paracetamol** (prostaglandin inhibitors). **Prostaglandin E1** keeps ductus open (duct-dependent heart disease).
- **Fetal heart rate** – normal **110–160 bpm** (older 120–160).

:::exam PYQ style
- Placenta weight at term – **500 g (1/6 of baby)**; cotyledons – **15–20**.
- Shiny surface delivered first – **Schultze**; maternal first – **Matthews Duncan**.
- Pregnancy test hormone – **hCG**; diabetogenic hormone – **hPL**.
- Only immunoglobulin crossing placenta – **IgG**.
- Amniotic fluid maximum – **~1000 ml at 36–38 wks**; pH – **alkaline**.
- Polyhydramnios – **oesophageal atresia, anencephaly, diabetes**; oligohydramnios – **renal agenesis**.
- Nitrazine paper with liquor – **blue**.
- Cord contains – **2 arteries + 1 vein**; highest O₂ – **umbilical vein**.
- Ductus arteriosus connects – **pulmonary artery to aorta**; remnant – **ligamentum arteriosum**.
- Cord inserted on membranes – **velamentous**; at edge – **battledore**.
:::
