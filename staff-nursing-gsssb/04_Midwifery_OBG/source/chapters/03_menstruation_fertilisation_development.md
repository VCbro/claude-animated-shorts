## 3.1 Menstrual Cycle

@fig menstrual_cycle.svg | Menstrual cycle – hormones, ovary aur endometrium

- **Menarche** – first period (avg **12–13 yrs** India); **menopause** – permanent cessation (avg **45–50 yrs**; confirmed after **12 months** amenorrhoea).
- Cycle **21–35 days (avg 28)**; flow **2–7 days (avg 4–5)**; blood loss **~35–80 ml** (> 80 ml = menorrhagia). Menstrual blood **does not clot** (fibrinolysins).
- **Hypothalamo-pituitary-ovarian axis**: **GnRH** (hypothalamus) → **FSH & LH** (anterior pituitary) → ovary → **oestrogen & progesterone** → endometrium.

| Phase | Days | Ovary | Endometrium | Hormone |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Menstrual** | 1–5 | – | Functional layer **sheds** | ↓ oestrogen & progesterone |
| **Proliferative** (Follicular) | **6–14** | Follicle growth → **Graafian follicle** | Regeneration, thickening | **Oestrogen** (FSH) |
| **Ovulation** | **~14** | Ovum released (**LH surge** 24–36 hrs before) | – | **LH peak** |
| **Secretory** (Luteal) | **15–28** | **Corpus luteum** | Glands tortuous, secretion, **decidual changes** – ready for implantation | **Progesterone** (+ oestrogen) |
| **Ischaemic** (premenstrual) | 27–28 | Corpus luteum degenerates (no pregnancy) → **corpus albicans** | **Spiral arteries constrict** | Both fall |

- **Luteal phase constant 14 days** → **ovulation = cycle length − 14** (35-day cycle → day 21).
- **Signs of ovulation**: **BBT rise 0.3–0.5 °C** (progesterone – thermogenic) after ovulation, **spinnbarkeit** (cervical mucus clear, stretchy, ≥ 10 cm), **fern pattern** (oestrogen), **mittelschmerz** (ovulation pain), LH kit, USG follicular monitoring.
- Corpus luteum – if pregnancy, maintained by **hCG** up to **10–12 wks** (placenta takes over – "luteo-placental shift").

## 3.2 Gametogenesis, Fertilisation & Implantation

- **Spermatogenesis** – ~**74 days**; sperm count normal **≥ 15 million/ml (WHO 2010; 16 M – 2021)**, volume ≥ 1.5 ml; sperm survive in female tract **48–72 hrs (up to 5 days)**. **Capacitation** in female tract.
- **Oogenesis** – ovum (secondary oocyte) arrested in meiosis II, completes after fertilisation; ovum life **12–24 hrs**.
- **Fertilisation** – fusion of sperm & ovum in **ampulla** of tube → **zygote (46 chromosomes)**; **sex determined by sperm** (X – female, Y – male); within **24 hrs** of ovulation. Acrosome reaction → zona reaction (prevents polyspermy).
- **Cleavage** → **morula (16 cells, day 3–4)** enters uterus → **blastocyst (day 4–5)** – inner cell mass (**embryoblast** → embryo) + outer **trophoblast** (→ placenta).
- **Implantation** – **day 6–7 after fertilisation (≈ day 20–21 of cycle)**, complete by day 10–12; usual site – **upper posterior wall of uterus (fundus)**; **implantation bleeding** (Hartman's sign) may occur. **Abnormal site** – tube (ectopic), lower segment (**placenta praevia**).
- **Decidua** = pregnant endometrium: **Decidua basalis** (under embryo → **maternal part of placenta**), **Decidua capsularis** (covers embryo), **Decidua vera/ parietalis** (rest of uterus). Capsularis & vera fuse by **12–14 wks**.
- **Germ layers** (week 3 – gastrulation): **Ectoderm** – skin, nervous system, eye lens, enamel; **Mesoderm** – muscles, bones, heart, blood, kidneys, gonads; **Endoderm** – GI lining, liver, pancreas, lungs, thyroid, bladder lining.

## 3.3 Stages of Development

| Stage | Period |
|---|---|
| **Ovum/ pre-embryonic (germinal)** | **0–2 weeks** after fertilisation |
| **Embryonic** | **3rd–8th week** – **organogenesis – most vulnerable to teratogens** |
| **Fetal** | **9th week to birth** – growth & maturation |

### Fetal development – month-wise highlights
| Gestation | Development |
|---|---|
| **4 weeks** | Heart starts beating (**~day 22**), neural tube closes (**day 26–28**) – **folic acid before conception** prevents NTD |
| **6–7 weeks** | **Cardiac activity on TVS USG** (5.5–6 wks), limb buds |
| **8 weeks** | All major organs formed; **embryo → fetus**; length ~3 cm |
| **12 weeks** | **External genitalia distinguishable**, fetal heart by **Doppler (10–12 wks)**, kidneys produce urine, uterus palpable above symphysis; ~6–7 cm, 14–45 g |
| **16 weeks** | Sex visible on USG, **lanugo** appears, quickening (multigravida 16–18) |
| **20 weeks** | **Quickening (primigravida 18–20 wks)**, **FHS by stethoscope (Pinard) 18–20 wks**, **vernix caseosa**, ~25 cm, ~300 g; fundus at umbilicus (22–24) |
| **24 weeks** | **Surfactant production begins (type II pneumocytes)**, eyelids separate; ~600–700 g; borderline viability (NICU) |
| **28 weeks** | **Viability (India)**; eyes open; ~1000–1100 g; 35 cm |
| **32 weeks** | Subcutaneous fat, ~1600–1800 g, toenails |
| **36 weeks** | ~2500 g; lanugo disappearing; **L/S ratio ≥ 2 (lung maturity) ~35 wks** |
| **40 weeks** | **~3000–3200 g (India avg ~2.8–3 kg), 50 cm length**, testes in scrotum, nails beyond fingertips, sole creases full |

- **Haase's rule** (fetal length): **1st 5 months – (month)² cm**; **6–10 months – month × 5 cm**. (e.g. 3 months = 9 cm, 7 months = 35 cm).
- **Lanugo** – fine body hair (16–20 wks). **Vernix caseosa** – cheesy white protective coating (sebaceous secretion + desquamated cells). **Meconium** – in fetal gut from **16 wks**; passage in utero = **fetal distress** (or post-term).
- **Fetal Hb (HbF)** – higher O₂ affinity; fetal blood Hb ~**17–20 g/dL**.

### Teratogens
- **Drugs**: **Thalidomide** (phocomelia), **warfarin** (fetal warfarin syndrome – nasal hypoplasia), **isotretinoin**, **valproate** (NTD), **phenytoin** (fetal hydantoin syndrome), **tetracycline** (teeth discolouration), **ACE inhibitors**, **methotrexate**, **lithium** (Ebstein anomaly), **DES** (vaginal adenocarcinoma), **misoprostol**, **streptomycin** (deafness), **alcohol (fetal alcohol syndrome – most common preventable cause of mental retardation)**, smoking (LBW).
- **Infections – TORCH**: **T**oxoplasmosis, **O**thers (syphilis, varicella, parvovirus, HIV, Zika), **R**ubella (**cataract, PDA, deafness – Gregg's triad**), **C**MV (most common congenital infection), **H**erpes simplex.
- **Radiation**, maternal diabetes (caudal regression, cardiac anomalies), hyperthermia.
- **FDA pregnancy categories (old)**: A (safe) → B → C → D → **X (contraindicated)** – replaced by PLLR (2015) but still asked.
