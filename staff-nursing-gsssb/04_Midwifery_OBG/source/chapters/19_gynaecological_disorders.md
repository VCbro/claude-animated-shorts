# Gynaecological Disorders & Women's Health

## 19.1 Menstrual Disorders

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Amenorrhoea – primary** | **No menarche by 15 yrs** (with secondary sex characters) / 13 yrs (without) – causes: **Turner's (45 XO) – most common chromosomal**, Müllerian agenesis (MRKH), imperforate hymen (**cryptomenorrhoea** – haematocolpos), Kallmann |
| **Amenorrhoea – secondary** | **No periods for ≥ 3 months** (regular cycles) / 6 months (irregular) in a woman who menstruated – **pregnancy (most common – rule out first)**, lactation, menopause, **PCOS**, hypothyroidism, hyperprolactinaemia, **Sheehan's**, **Asherman's**, stress, anorexia, excessive exercise |
| **Menorrhagia (heavy menstrual bleeding)** | **> 80 ml** or > 7 days, **regular cycles** |
| **Metrorrhagia** | **Irregular, intermenstrual bleeding** |
| **Menometrorrhagia** | Heavy + irregular |
| **Polymenorrhoea** | Cycle **< 21 days** (frequent) |
| **Oligomenorrhoea** | Cycle **> 35 days** (infrequent) |
| **Hypomenorrhoea** | Scanty flow |
| **Dysmenorrhoea** | **Painful menstruation** – **primary** (no pathology, adolescents, **prostaglandins**; starts with period, lasts 48–72 hrs → **NSAIDs (mefenamic acid)**, OCP, heat, exercise) / **secondary** (pathology – **endometriosis**, adenomyosis, PID, fibroids, IUCD) |
| **PMS (premenstrual syndrome)** | Luteal phase symptoms (bloating, irritability, mastalgia), relieved with menses; severe – PMDD |
| **Post-menopausal bleeding (PMB)** | Bleeding **≥ 12 months after menopause** → **rule out endometrial carcinoma** (most important), cervical ca, atrophic vaginitis, HRT |
| **AUB (PALM-COEIN – FIGO)** | Structural: **P**olyp, **A**denomyosis, **L**eiomyoma, **M**alignancy; Non-structural: **C**oagulopathy, **O**vulatory dysfunction, **E**ndometrial, **I**atrogenic, **N**ot classified |

- **PCOS (Polycystic ovary syndrome)** – **Rotterdam criteria (2 of 3)**: (1) **oligo/ anovulation (irregular periods)**, (2) **hyperandrogenism (hirsutism, acne, alopecia)**, (3) **polycystic ovaries on USG (≥ 12/ 20 follicles 2–9 mm or volume > 10 ml – "string of pearls")**; **insulin resistance, obesity, acanthosis nigricans**, infertility, ↑ LH/FSH ratio; long-term – **T2DM, endometrial cancer**, CVD. Rx – **weight loss (first-line)**, **OCP**, **metformin**, **letrozole/ clomiphene (ovulation induction)**, spironolactone (hirsutism).
- **Endometriosis** – **endometrial tissue outside uterus** (**ovary – "chocolate cyst" (endometrioma) – most common site**, POD, uterosacral ligaments); **triad – dysmenorrhoea (secondary, progressive), dyspareunia, infertility**; diagnosis – **laparoscopy (gold standard – powder-burn spots)**; Rx – NSAIDs, OCP, progestins, GnRH analogues, danazol, surgery.
- **Adenomyosis** – endometrium **within myometrium** – multiparous, 40s; **bulky, tender, uniformly enlarged uterus**, menorrhagia, dysmenorrhoea.

## 19.2 Infertility

- **Inability to conceive after 1 year of regular unprotected intercourse** (**6 months if woman ≥ 35**). **Primary** – never conceived; **secondary** – previous conception.
- Causes: **female (~40–50 %)** – **ovulatory (PCOS – most common)**, **tubal (PID, TB – India)**, uterine (fibroids, Asherman's, anomalies), cervical, endometriosis; **male (~30–40 %)** – oligospermia, azoospermia, varicocele, infections, erectile dysfunction; **unexplained (~10–15 %)**.
- **Investigations**: **Semen analysis (first test – easiest)** (**WHO 2021**: volume ≥ 1.4 ml, count ≥ 16 million/ml, total motility ≥ 42 %, progressive ≥ 30 %, normal forms ≥ 4 %; abstinence 2–7 days); **ovulation** – **mid-luteal (day 21) serum progesterone > 3 ng/ml**, BBT, follicular monitoring USG, LH kits; **tubal patency** – **HSG (hysterosalpingography – day 7–10 of cycle, post-menses pre-ovulation)**, **SSG**, **laparoscopy with chromopertubation (gold standard)**; hormones – FSH, LH, AMH (ovarian reserve), TSH, prolactin.
- Treatment – **ovulation induction (clomiphene citrate, letrozole – PCOS first-line)**, gonadotropins, **IUI** (intrauterine insemination – mild male factor, cervical), **IVF-ET** (tubal block – Louise Brown, 1978 – first IVF baby; India – **Durga (Kanupriya Agarwal) 1978, Dr Subhash Mukhopadhyay**), **ICSI** (severe male factor), surgery; **OHSS** – ovarian hyperstimulation syndrome risk.
- **ART (Regulation) Act 2021 & Surrogacy (Regulation) Act 2021** – commercial surrogacy banned; altruistic only.

## 19.3 Fibroids, Prolapse & Displacement

- **Uterine fibroid (leiomyoma/ myoma)** – **most common benign tumour of uterus/ female pelvis**; **oestrogen-dependent**; 30–45 yrs, nulliparous, obesity, African; types – **intramural (most common)**, **subserous**, **submucous (most symptomatic – menorrhagia, infertility, abortion)**, cervical, pedunculated; **S/S – menorrhagia (most common)**, pelvic mass, pressure symptoms (frequency, retention, constipation), infertility, pain (**red degeneration – pregnancy (2nd trimester) – most common degeneration in pregnancy**); **hyaline degeneration – most common overall**; **sarcomatous change – rare (< 0.5 %)**. Rx – observation, tranexamic acid, GnRH analogues, **myomectomy (young, fertility desired)**, **hysterectomy**, UAE; shrink after menopause.
- **Uterovaginal prolapse** – descent of uterus/ vaginal walls; causes – **multiparity, prolonged/ difficult labour, premature bearing down, ↑ intra-abdominal pressure (chronic cough, constipation, obesity), menopause (oestrogen ↓)**, congenital weakness; **supports damaged – cardinal & uterosacral ligaments, levator ani**.
 - **Degrees**: **1st** – cervix descends but **within vagina**; **2nd** – cervix **at/ outside introitus**, body inside; **3rd** – cervix & body outside; **Procidentia** – **whole uterus outside** with vaginal walls (complete inversion of vagina) → **decubitus ulcer**. (**POP-Q** system now.)
 - **Cystocele** (bladder – anterior wall; urinary symptoms), **rectocele** (posterior wall; constipation), **enterocele** (POD – small bowel), **urethrocele**.
 - Rx – **pelvic floor (Kegel) exercises** (prevention & mild), **ring pessary** (unfit for surgery, pregnancy, temporary – change 3–6 monthly), **surgery – Fothergill's (Manchester) operation** (young, wants uterus), **vaginal hysterectomy with pelvic floor repair (anterior colporrhaphy + posterior colpoperineorrhaphy) – common in elderly**, Le Fort's colpocleisis (elderly unfit), sling operations (Shirodkar, Purandare – young nulliparous).
- **Retroverted uterus** – 15–20 % normal; mostly asymptomatic; **knee-chest position exercises**; Hodge pessary.

## 19.4 Reproductive Tract Infections (RTI/ STI) & PID

| Infection | Discharge / Features | Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| **Bacterial vaginosis** (Gardnerella – most common cause of vaginal discharge) | **Thin, grey-white, fishy odour (↑ after intercourse)**, **clue cells**, **whiff test +ve (KOH)**, pH > 4.5; no inflammation | **Metronidazole** (oral/ gel) |
| **Candidiasis (moniliasis)** | **Thick, white, curdy ("cottage cheese")**, **intense itching**, redness; pH normal (< 4.5); **pregnancy, diabetes, antibiotics, OCP, HIV** | **Clotrimazole vaginal**, fluconazole oral (not in pregnancy) |
| **Trichomoniasis** (*T. vaginalis* – protozoa) | **Frothy, greenish-yellow, foul**, itching, **"strawberry cervix"** (punctate haemorrhages), pH > 4.5; **STI – treat partner** | **Metronidazole 2 g single dose / 400–500 mg BD × 7 days (both partners)**; avoid alcohol |
| **Gonorrhoea** (*N. gonorrhoeae*) | Purulent discharge, cervicitis, dysuria; neonatal ophthalmia | **Ceftriaxone IM** + azithromycin |
| **Chlamydia** (*C. trachomatis*) | Often asymptomatic; **most common bacterial STI**; **PID, tubal infertility, ectopic** | **Azithromycin 1 g single/ doxycycline** |
| **Syphilis** (*Treponema pallidum*) | **Painless chancre** (primary), rash (secondary) | **Benzathine penicillin 2.4 MU IM** |
| **Chancroid** (*H. ducreyi*) | **Painful** soft ulcer, bubo | Azithromycin/ ceftriaxone |
| **Genital herpes (HSV-2)** | Painful vesicles/ ulcers; neonatal herpes → CS if active lesions at term | Acyclovir |
| **Genital warts (HPV 6, 11)** | Condyloma acuminata | Podophyllin (not in pregnancy), cryo |
| **LGV / Donovanosis** | | Doxycycline/ azithromycin |

- **Syndromic management (NACO/ NACP – colour-coded STI kits)**: **Kit 1 – Grey** (urethral/ cervical discharge – azithromycin + cefixime), **Kit 2 – Green** (vaginal discharge – secnidazole + fluconazole), **Kit 3 – White** (genital ulcer non-herpetic – benzathine penicillin + azithromycin), **Kit 4 – Blue** (genital ulcer – penicillin allergy – doxycycline + azithromycin), **Kit 5 – Red** (genital ulcer herpetic – acyclovir), **Kit 6 – Yellow** (lower abdominal pain/ PID – cefixime + metronidazole + doxycycline), **Kit 7 – Black** (inguinal bubo – doxycycline + azithromycin). **Suraksha clinics** (DSRC).
- **PID (Pelvic Inflammatory Disease)** – ascending infection (**Chlamydia, gonorrhoea**, anaerobes) → endometritis, **salpingitis**, tubo-ovarian abscess, pelvic peritonitis; S/S – **lower abdominal pain, fever, abnormal discharge, cervical motion tenderness, adnexal tenderness**, dyspareunia; complications – **infertility, ectopic, chronic pelvic pain**; **Fitz-Hugh-Curtis syndrome** (perihepatitis); Rx – antibiotics (both partners), **Fowler's**, rest, remove IUCD if severe.
- **Genital TB** – **India – common cause of tubal infertility**; **fallopian tube most affected (~90–100 %)**, endometrium; amenorrhoea/ infertility; HSG – beaded tubes, **"tobacco pouch"**; ATT 6 months.
- **Vaginal pH** – normal **3.8–4.5**; ↑ in BV & trichomonas.

## 19.5 Gynaecological Cancers & Screening

- **Cervical cancer** – **2nd most common cancer in Indian women** (after breast); **HPV 16 & 18 (~70 %)**; **squamous cell (most common, ~80–90 %)** at **transformation zone (squamo-columnar junction)**; risk – early coitus, multiple partners, **multiparity**, smoking, HIV, low SES, OCP long-term; **CIN (precancer) → invasive**; S/S – **post-coital bleeding (earliest/ commonest)**, intermenstrual bleeding, foul watery discharge, late – pain, VVF, uraemia (**commonest cause of death – uraemia/ renal failure**).
 - **Screening**: **VIA (visual inspection with 3–5 % acetic acid – acetowhite area = +ve)** – India NP-NCD, **30–65 yrs every 5 yrs**; **VILI (Lugol's iodine – unstained area)**; **Pap smear** (Papanicolaou – **Ayre's spatula + cytobrush** from SCJ; **avoid during menses, intercourse/ douching 48 hrs before**; fix in **95 % ethyl alcohol** immediately; from 21/ 25 yrs every 3 yrs); **HPV DNA test (every 5 yrs – 30+)**; **colposcopy** + biopsy (abnormal).
 - **Screen & treat** – **cryotherapy / thermal ablation**, **LEEP/ LLETZ**, cone biopsy.
 - **HPV vaccine** – **girls 9–14 yrs (2 doses 6 months apart)**; **Cervavac (indigenous quadrivalent – Serum Institute, 2022)**; Gardasil (4/ 9-valent), Cervarix (2).
 - Staging **FIGO**; Rx – surgery (**Wertheim's radical hysterectomy** – early), **radiotherapy (brachytherapy + external)** – most stages, chemoradiation (cisplatin).
- **Breast cancer** – **most common cancer in Indian women** (and worldwide); **upper outer quadrant**; risk – **BRCA1/2**, early menarche/ late menopause, nulliparity/ late first pregnancy, no breastfeeding, HRT, obesity, alcohol; **painless hard irregular lump**, peau d'orange, nipple retraction; **screening – BSE (monthly, 5–7 days after menses)**, **CBE**, **mammography (40/ 50+ every 1–2 yrs)**; Rx – surgery (BCS/ MRM), chemo, radiation, hormonal (**tamoxifen**).
- **Endometrial cancer** – **post-menopausal bleeding (cardinal symptom)**; risk – **unopposed oestrogen, obesity, nulliparity, PCOS, diabetes, HTN ("corpus cancer syndrome")**, tamoxifen, late menopause; **endometrial biopsy/ fractional curettage**; Rx – hysterectomy + BSO.
- **Ovarian cancer** – "**silent killer**" – vague abdominal symptoms (bloating, distension), late diagnosis; **epithelial (serous – most common)**; marker **CA-125**; **germ cell tumours (young)** – AFP, hCG, LDH; **Krukenberg tumour** (metastatic from stomach – signet ring cells); risk – BRCA, nulliparity, family history; protective – **OCP, pregnancy, breastfeeding, tubal ligation**. **Dermoid cyst (mature cystic teratoma) – most common benign ovarian tumour in young** (torsion – common complication).
- **Choriocarcinoma** – see Chapter 12.

## 19.6 Menopause

- **Permanent cessation of menstruation** due to loss of ovarian follicular activity – **diagnosed retrospectively after 12 months of amenorrhoea**; **average age 45–50 yrs (India ~46–48)**; **premature menopause < 40 yrs (POI)**; **perimenopause/ climacteric** – transition phase.
- Hormones – **↓ oestrogen, ↓ inhibin → ↑ FSH (> 30–40 IU/L – most sensitive marker)**, ↑ LH.
- **Symptoms**: **vasomotor – hot flushes (most common), night sweats**, palpitations; insomnia, mood swings, irritability, depression, poor memory; **genitourinary syndrome of menopause** – vaginal dryness, **dyspareunia, atrophic vaginitis**, urinary frequency/ urgency/ UTI, **prolapse**; skin thinning, breast atrophy; long-term – **osteoporosis (fractures – vertebra, hip, wrist)**, **cardiovascular disease ↑**, weight gain, dementia.
- **Management**: **lifestyle** (exercise – weight-bearing, **calcium 1200 mg + Vit D**, diet, stop smoking), **HRT (oestrogen ± progesterone – progesterone added if uterus present to prevent endometrial cancer)** – short-term, lowest dose for vasomotor symptoms; **contraindications – breast/ endometrial cancer, undiagnosed vaginal bleeding, thromboembolism, active liver disease, CVD/ stroke**; non-hormonal – SSRIs/ SNRIs, gabapentin, clonidine; vaginal oestrogen/ lubricants; **bisphosphonates** for osteoporosis; **DEXA screening**.
- **18 October = World Menopause Day**.

## 19.7 Common Gynaecological Procedures – Nursing Points

| Procedure | Nursing points |
|---|---|
| **Pap smear** | Not during menses; no douche/ intercourse/ vaginal meds 48 hrs; **lithotomy/ dorsal**; **Cusco's (bivalve) speculum – no lubricant (water only)**; fix immediately |
| **Per speculum (P/S)/ per vaginal (P/V)** exam | **Empty bladder**, lithotomy/ dorsal, privacy, **female chaperone**, warm speculum, explain |
| **D&C / Endometrial biopsy** | NPO, consent, lithotomy, post-op – watch bleeding, perforation, pain |
| **HSG** | **Day 7–10 of cycle (after menses, before ovulation)**; iodine allergy check; antibiotics; mild cramps |
| **Colposcopy** | Magnified cervix view with acetic acid/ iodine |
| **Laparoscopy** | **Trendelenburg** intra-op; **shoulder pain post-op (CO₂ gas – referred)** → reassure, ambulate, analgesics |
| **Hysterectomy** (abdominal – TAH / vaginal – VH / laparoscopic – TLH) | Pre-op – bowel prep, catheter, consent; post-op – **vaginal bleeding (pad check), urine output (ureter injury), catheter 24 hrs**, early ambulation (DVT), **no heavy lifting/ intercourse 6 wks**; vaginal vault care; surgical menopause if ovaries removed (BSO) |
| **Vaginal douche** | Not routinely (disturbs flora); lukewarm, can height 30–60 cm |
| **Sitz bath** | 40–43 °C, 15–20 min – perineal wounds, haemorrhoids |
