## 17.1 Uterotonics (Oxytocics)

| Drug | Dose / Route | Uses | Side effects / Contraindications / Nursing |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Oxytocin (Pitocin/ Syntocinon)** – posterior pituitary hormone (synthetic) | **AMTSL: 10 IU IM**; **induction/ augmentation: low dose IV infusion (2.5–5 IU in 500 ml)** start 1–2 mU/min, ↑ every 30 min; **PPH: 20–40 IU in 500–1000 ml** | **Drug of choice – PPH prevention & treatment**, **induction & augmentation**, after LSCS, incomplete abortion | **Uterine hyperstimulation → fetal distress, rupture uterus**; **water intoxication** (ADH-like – headache, vomiting, convulsions); hypotension (IV bolus – **never IV bolus**); **contraindicated – CPD, obstructed labour, malpresentation, praevia, previous classical CS, fetal distress**; **store 2–8 °C**; **infusion pump, monitor contractions & FHS every 15–30 min** |
| **Carbetocin** | **100 µg IM/ IV** (heat-stable version available) | PPH prevention (CS) | Long-acting oxytocin analogue |
| **Methylergometrine (Methergine)** – ergot alkaloid | **0.2 mg IM/ IV (slow)**; repeat every 15 min (max 5 doses/ 1 mg); oral 0.125 mg TDS (subinvolution) | **PPH (atony)**, subinvolution | **Sustained tonic contraction** – **never before delivery of baby (and 2nd twin)**; **HTN/ vasoconstriction, nausea, vomiting, headache**; **contraindicated – hypertension, pre-eclampsia/ eclampsia, heart disease, peripheral vascular disease, HIV on protease inhibitors**; **light sensitive, refrigerate** |
| **Ergometrine** | 0.2–0.5 mg | As above | Same |
| **Syntometrine** | Oxytocin 5 IU + ergometrine 0.5 mg IM | AMTSL (UK) | |
| **Carboprost (15-methyl PGF2α – Prostodin/ Hemabate)** | **250 µg IM (deep)/ intramyometrial**, every 15–90 min, **max 8 doses (2 mg)** | **Refractory PPH (atony)**, 2nd trimester abortion | **Bronchospasm** – **contraindicated in asthma**; **diarrhoea (most common)**, vomiting, fever, shivering |
| **Misoprostol (PGE1 – Cytotec)** | **PPH prevention: 600 µg oral**; **PPH Rx: 800 µg sublingual**; **induction: 25 µg vaginal 4-hrly / 25 µg oral 2-hrly**; **MMA: 800 µg** after mifepristone; incomplete abortion 400–600 µg | **PPH (no refrigeration – community)**, **cervical ripening/ induction**, **medical abortion**, missed/ incomplete abortion, IUD | **Shivering, fever**, diarrhoea, vomiting; **hyperstimulation/ rupture (scarred uterus – avoid in previous CS for induction)**; cheap, heat-stable |
| **Dinoprostone (PGE2 – Cerviprime gel/ Prostin E2)** | **0.5 mg gel intracervical**, repeat 6-hrly (max 3); vaginal tablet 3 mg; insert 10 mg | **Cervical ripening** (unfavourable Bishop) | Hyperstimulation; **refrigerate**; patient lies down 30 min after |
| **Mifepristone (RU-486)** – antiprogesterone | **200 mg oral** (MMA/ IUD) | Medical abortion with misoprostol, IUD induction, cervical priming | Bleeding, cramps |

## 17.2 Tocolytics (uterine relaxants)

| Drug | Dose | Side effects |
|---|---|---|
| **Nifedipine** (Ca channel blocker) – **first-line** | **20 mg oral loading** → 10–20 mg 6–8 hrly | Headache, flushing, hypotension, tachycardia |
| **Ritodrine/ Isoxsuprine/ Terbutaline** (β₂-agonists) | Terbutaline **0.25 mg SC** (acute – hyperstimulation, inversion) | **Maternal tachycardia (hold if > 120)**, palpitation, tremor, **hyperglycaemia, hypokalaemia, pulmonary oedema** – avoid in heart disease, diabetes |
| **Atosiban** (oxytocin antagonist) | IV infusion | Few side effects; costly |
| **Indomethacin** (PG synthetase inhibitor) | 50–100 mg | **< 32 wks, ≤ 48 hrs** – **premature closure of ductus arteriosus, oligohydramnios** |
| **MgSO₄** | IV | Neuroprotection < 32 wks (weak tocolytic) |

- Purpose – **delay delivery 48 hrs** for **antenatal steroids & in-utero transfer**.

## 17.3 MgSO₄ & Antihypertensives

- **Magnesium sulphate** – **drug of choice for eclampsia & prevention in severe pre-eclampsia**; Pritchard (IM) / Zuspan (IV) – see Chapter 11; **monitor knee jerk, RR ≥ 16, urine ≥ 30 ml/hr**; **antidote – 10 % calcium gluconate 10 ml IV over 10 min**; therapeutic level **4–7 mEq/L**.

| Antihypertensive | Use |
|---|---|
| **Labetalol** | **First-line oral & IV** (20 mg IV, then 40, 80 every 10 min; max 300 mg) – avoid asthma |
| **Nifedipine (immediate/ extended release, oral)** | Acute severe HTN (10–20 mg oral); **not sublingual** |
| **Methyldopa** | **Safe in pregnancy (longest record)**, chronic/ gestational HTN; s/e – sedation, depression, haemolytic anaemia |
| **Hydralazine** | IV for hypertensive emergency (5 mg) |
| **Contraindicated** | **ACE inhibitors (-pril), ARBs (-sartan)** – fetal renal failure, oligohydramnios, skull defects; **atenolol** (IUGR); **diuretics** (except pulmonary oedema) |

## 17.4 Other Important Drugs

| Drug | Use / Dose |
|---|---|
| **Corticosteroids – Dexamethasone 6 mg IM 12-hrly × 4** / **Betamethasone 12 mg IM 24-hrly × 2** | **Fetal lung maturity – preterm 24–34 wks** (↓ RDS, IVH, NEC) |
| **Anti-D immunoglobulin** | **300 µg IM** within **72 hrs** postpartum (baby Rh +ve); **28 wks** antenatal; **50 µg** < 12 wks abortion; IM deltoid; **not IV**; store 2–8 °C |
| **Iron (ferrous sulphate/ fumarate)** | **60 mg elemental + 500 µg FA** prophylaxis; IV **iron sucrose (100–200 mg)**, **ferric carboxymaltose (up to 1000 mg)** |
| **Folic acid** | **0.4 mg (400 µg)** preconception & 1st trimester; **4–5 mg** – previous NTD, epilepsy (valproate), diabetes |
| **Calcium** | **1 g elemental/ day** from 14 wks to 6 months PNC (pre-eclampsia prevention, bone) |
| **Low-dose aspirin** | **75–150 mg at night from 12–16 wks to 36 wks** – pre-eclampsia prevention (high risk), APLA |
| **Heparin/ LMWH (enoxaparin)** | Doesn't cross placenta – **safe**; DVT, mechanical valves, APLA |
| **Warfarin** | **Teratogenic (6–12 wks – embryopathy)**; avoid 1st trimester & near term |
| **Insulin** | **Drug of choice for diabetes in pregnancy** (doesn't cross placenta) |
| **Metformin** | GDM (accepted) |
| **Doxylamine + pyridoxine** | Nausea & vomiting of pregnancy |
| **Antibiotics safe** | **Penicillins, cephalosporins, erythromycin/ azithromycin, nitrofurantoin (avoid near term), metronidazole (after 1st trim)** |
| **Antibiotics avoid** | **Tetracyclines (teeth/ bone)**, **aminoglycosides – streptomycin (ototoxic)**, **fluoroquinolones**, **chloramphenicol (gray baby)**, **co-trimoxazole (near term – kernicterus; 1st trim – folate antagonist)** |
| **Thyroxine** | Hypothyroidism – dose ↑ in pregnancy |
| **PTU (1st trim) → carbimazole** | Hyperthyroidism |
| **Antiepileptics** | **Valproate avoid (NTD)**; lamotrigine/ levetiracetam preferred + folic acid 5 mg |
| **Vitamin K** (newborn) | **1 mg IM** (0.5 mg < 1500 g) |
| **Naloxone** (newborn) | **0.1 mg/kg** – opioid-induced respiratory depression |

- **Drugs in breastfeeding** – most drugs safe; **contraindicated**: **anticancer (cytotoxics)**, **radioactive iodine/ isotopes**, **lithium**, amiodarone, **ergotamine/ bromocriptine (suppress lactation)**, high-dose oestrogen (↓ milk – combined OCP avoid < 6 months), tetracycline (prolonged), chloramphenicol, drugs of abuse. **Safe**: paracetamol, ibuprofen, penicillins, cephalosporins, insulin, heparin, warfarin, thyroxine, most antihypertensives (labetalol, nifedipine, methyldopa, enalapril postpartum), SSRIs (sertraline), ATT, ART.

- Drug of choice for PPH – **oxytocin**; dose in AMTSL – **10 IU IM**.
- Oxytocin side effect (high dose) – **water intoxication**.
- Methergine contraindicated – **hypertension/ pre-eclampsia, heart disease**.
- Carboprost contraindicated – **bronchial asthma**; max dose – **2 mg (8 doses)**.
- Heat-stable uterotonic for community – **misoprostol**.
- Cervical ripening gel – **dinoprostone (PGE2)**.
- Tocolytic first-line – **nifedipine**; β-agonist s/e – **tachycardia, pulmonary oedema**.
- Indomethacin side effect – **premature closure of ductus arteriosus**.
- Antihypertensive contraindicated – **ACE inhibitors**.
- Steroid for lung maturity – **dexamethasone 6 mg IM × 4 doses**.
- Anticoagulant safe in pregnancy – **heparin**; teratogenic – **warfarin**.
- Folic acid dose (high risk NTD) – **4–5 mg**.
- Antibiotic causing gray baby – **chloramphenicol**; teeth staining – **tetracycline**.
