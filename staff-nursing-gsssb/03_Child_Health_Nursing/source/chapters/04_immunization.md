## 4.1 Immunity – Basics

| Type | Example |
|---|---|
| **Natural active** | After infection (measles – lifelong) |
| **Artificial active** | **Vaccines** (live/ killed/ toxoid) – takes time, long-lasting |
| **Natural passive** | **Maternal IgG through placenta**, IgA in breast milk |
| **Artificial passive** | **Immunoglobulins/ antisera** – immediate, short (e.g. **TIG, ARS/ RIG, HBIG, ADS, ASV**) |

| Vaccine type | Examples |
|---|---|
| **Live attenuated** | **BCG, OPV, Measles/ MR/ MMR, Rotavirus, JE (SA-14-14-2), Yellow fever, Varicella, Typhoid oral (Ty21a)** |
| **Killed/ inactivated** | **IPV**, Pertussis (whole cell), Rabies, Hepatitis A, Influenza, JE (JENVAC) |
| **Toxoid** | **Tetanus, Diphtheria** |
| **Subunit/ recombinant** | **Hepatitis B**, HPV, acellular pertussis |
| **Conjugate (polysaccharide + protein)** | **Hib**, **PCV**, typhoid conjugate (TCV), meningococcal |

- **Herd immunity** – protection of unimmunised due to immunity in majority.
- **Cold chain** – system of storing & transporting vaccines at recommended temperature from **manufacturer to beneficiary**.

## 4.2 National Immunization Schedule (UIP)

@fig immunization_schedule.svg | UIP schedule for infants & children with route/ site

| Age | Vaccines |
|---|---|
| **Birth** | **BCG**, **OPV-0** (within 15 days), **Hepatitis B birth dose (within 24 hrs)** |
| **6 weeks** | **OPV-1, Pentavalent-1, RVV-1, fIPV-1, PCV-1** |
| **10 weeks** | **OPV-2, Pentavalent-2, RVV-2** |
| **14 weeks** | **OPV-3, Pentavalent-3, RVV-3, fIPV-2, PCV-2** |
| **9–12 months** | **MR-1, JE-1 (endemic), PCV booster, fIPV-3, Vitamin A (1st dose, 1 lakh IU)** |
| **16–24 months** | **MR-2, JE-2, DPT booster-1, OPV booster, Vitamin A (2nd dose, 2 lakh IU)** |
| **5–6 years** | **DPT booster-2** |
| **10 years** | **Td** |
| **16 years** | **Td** |
| Pregnant women | **Td-1, Td-2 (4 weeks apart) / Td booster** |

- **Pentavalent = DPT + Hepatitis B + Hib**.
- **12 vaccine-preventable diseases (UIP)**: TB, polio, diphtheria, pertussis, tetanus, hepatitis B, Hib (pneumonia/ meningitis), measles, rubella, **rotavirus diarrhoea**, **pneumococcal pneumonia**, **JE** (endemic).
- **Vitamin A** – 9 doses: 9 months (1 lakh IU = 1 ml), then 2 lakh IU (2 ml) every 6 months till **5 yrs**.
- **Fully immunized child** – BCG, 3 doses OPV & Penta, MR-1 (+ OPV-0, HepB-0) **before 1 year**.

| Vaccine | Dose | Route | Site |
|---|---|---|---|
| **BCG** | **0.1 ml (0.05 ml if < 1 month)** | **Intradermal** | **Left upper arm (over deltoid insertion)** |
| **OPV** | **2 drops** | Oral | Mouth |
| **Hepatitis B** | 0.5 ml | IM | **Anterolateral thigh** |
| **Pentavalent / DPT** | **0.5 ml** | **IM** | **Anterolateral mid-thigh (left)** |
| **fIPV** (fractional IPV) | **0.1 ml** | **Intradermal** | **Right upper arm** |
| **Rotavirus (Rotavac)** | **5 drops** | Oral | Mouth |
| **PCV** | 0.5 ml | IM | **Right anterolateral thigh** |
| **MR** | **0.5 ml** | **Subcutaneous** | **Right upper arm** |
| **JE (live SA-14-14-2)** | 0.5 ml | **SC** | **Left upper arm** |
| **Td** | 0.5 ml | IM | Upper arm |
| **Vitamin A** | 1 ml / 2 ml | Oral | – |

- **Diluents**: BCG – **normal saline**; MR – **sterile water**; use **only the diluent supplied** with the vaccine, **cool it** (keep in ILR 24 hrs before use) – warm diluent → vaccine damage.
- **BCG reaction** – papule (2–3 wks) → pustule → ulcer → **scar by 6–12 weeks** (no dressing/ ointment); **no scar does NOT need repeat** (as per UIP). Complications – **BCG adenitis** (axillary), abscess (deep injection/ overdose), BCG-osis (immunodeficient).
- **OPV** – **if child vomits within 30 min, repeat dose**; OPV can be given with diarrhoea (dose not counted in older schedules – just give).
- **Diphtheria/ tetanus/ pertussis vaccine reactions** – fever, local pain, persistent cry; **DPT contraindication** – encephalopathy within 7 days of previous dose (give DT/ Td instead); anaphylaxis.

## 4.3 Cold Chain

| Equipment | Temperature | Level |
|---|---|---|
| **Walk-in cooler (WIC)** | +2 to +8 °C | State/ regional |
| **Walk-in freezer (WIF)** | −15 to −25 °C | State/ regional |
| **ILR (Ice Lined Refrigerator)** | **+2 to +8 °C** | **PHC/ district** – keeps cold **for ~8 hrs without power** |
| **Deep freezer** | **−15 to −25 °C** | Ice packs (and OPV at higher levels) |
| **Cold box** | +2 to +8 °C | Transport, power failure storage (up to ~5–7 days) |
| **Vaccine carrier** | +2 to +8 °C | **Outreach session – with 4 conditioned ice packs**, keep closed, in shade |

- **Most heat-sensitive vaccine – OPV** (then MR/ measles); **freeze-sensitive (never freeze)** – **Hepatitis B, Pentavalent, DPT, Td, IPV, PCV** – **freezing destroys them; Hep B is the most freeze-sensitive**.
- **ILR arrangement** – all vaccines **inside the basket** (never directly on the ILR floor); the **bottom of ILR is coldest** → keep **heat-sensitive (OPV, MR, BCG, JE) at the bottom of the basket** and **freeze-sensitive (Hep B, Penta, DPT, Td, IPV, PCV) in the upper part**, away from walls.
- **Shake test** – to check if a freeze-sensitive vaccine was **frozen** (frozen vial sediments faster & forms flakes → discard).
- **Conditioned ice packs** – kept at room temperature till **"sound of water" on shaking** (prevents freezing of vaccines in carrier).
- **VVM (Vaccine Vial Monitor)** – heat-sensitive label: **inner square lighter than outer circle = USE**; **inner square matches or darker = DISCARD** (stages 1–2 usable; 3–4 discard). Indicates **cumulative heat exposure, not freezing**.
- **Temperature log** twice daily; **thermometer/ eVIN (electronic Vaccine Intelligence Network)**.
- **"Hold the cold"**, **"first expiry first out" (FEFO)**.

### Open Vial Policy (UIP)
- **Can be reused up to 28 days** (if VVM OK, not expired, aseptic, stored +2 to +8 °C, date & time written): **OPV, DPT, Td, Hep B, Pentavalent, PCV, IPV**.
- **Discard 4 hrs after opening/ reconstitution or end of session (whichever earlier)**: **BCG, MR/ measles, JE** (reconstituted live vaccines) – risk of **toxic shock syndrome** (Staph contamination).
- Vitamin A – opened bottle used within 6–8 weeks (as per guidelines).

## 4.4 Adverse Events Following Immunization (AEFI)

- **Minor** – fever, local pain, swelling, irritability (**paracetamol, cold compress**).
- **Serious** – death, hospitalisation, disability, cluster; **anaphylaxis** (within minutes – **adrenaline 1:1000, 0.01 ml/kg IM, max 0.5 ml**, anterolateral thigh) – hence **observe for 30 min after vaccination**.
- **Programme errors** – **toxic shock syndrome** (reuse of reconstituted vial > 4–6 hrs) – **fever, vomiting, watery diarrhoea within hours, death in 24–48 hrs**; abscess (unsterile injection), BCG overdose.
- Other: **VAPP (vaccine-associated paralytic polio – OPV)**, **cVDPV**, **intussusception (rotavirus – rare)**, febrile seizures, hypotonic-hyporesponsive episode (DPT), persistent inconsolable crying.
- **AEFI reporting** – immediately to **MO/ DIO**; **AEFI kit** (adrenaline, syringes, IV set, hydrocortisone) at every session.

## 4.5 General Rules & Contraindications

- **Minimum interval** between doses of same vaccine – **4 weeks**; two live vaccines – same day or **≥ 4 weeks apart**.
- **No maximum interval** – if a dose is missed, **continue schedule, don't restart**.
- **Catch-up**: **Pentavalent up to 1 yr**; after 1 yr give **DPT**; **OPV up to 5 yrs**; **MR up to 5 yrs**; **BCG up to 1 yr**; **Hep B birth dose up to 24 hrs** (not after); **Rotavirus – start before 1 yr**; **PCV – up to 1 yr**; **Vitamin A – up to 5 yrs**.
- **True contraindications** – **anaphylaxis to previous dose**, **live vaccines in immunodeficiency/ immunosuppressive therapy, pregnancy**, DPT – progressive neurological disease/ encephalopathy.
- **NOT contraindications (false)** – **mild fever, cold, cough, mild diarrhoea, malnutrition**, prematurity/ LBW (vaccinate by chronological age), breastfeeding, antibiotics, family history of convulsions, local reaction to previous dose, jaundice.
- **Injection safety** – **AD (auto-disable) syringes**, no recapping, **hub cutter**, red & black bags (BMW), **anterolateral thigh in infants (avoid gluteal – sciatic nerve injury)**.
- **4 key messages to parents** after vaccination – what vaccine & disease it prevents, minor side effects & how to manage, **date of next visit**, keep **MCP card** safe.
