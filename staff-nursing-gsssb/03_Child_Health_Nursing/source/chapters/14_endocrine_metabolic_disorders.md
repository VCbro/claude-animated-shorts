## 14.1 Congenital Hypothyroidism (Cretinism)

- **Most common preventable cause of intellectual disability**; ~1 in 2000–4000 births (higher in India); **thyroid dysgenesis (most common)**, dyshormonogenesis, **iodine deficiency (endemic cretinism)**.
- **Newborn screening – TSH (heel prick) at 48–72 hrs** (cord blood TSH also used); confirm with serum T4/ TSH.
- **S/S (often normal at birth)** – **prolonged physiological jaundice (earliest)**, **large posterior fontanelle**, **macroglossia (large protruding tongue)**, **umbilical hernia**, **constipation**, **hoarse cry**, **lethargy, sleepy, poor feeding**, hypotonia, dry coarse skin, **hypothermia**, puffy face, **delayed milestones & dentition**, short stature (if untreated).
- **Treatment** – **levothyroxine 10–15 µg/kg/day orally** – start **ASAP (within 2 weeks of birth)** for normal IQ; **lifelong**; crush tablet in small amount of breast milk/ water (**not with soy, iron, calcium**); monitor T4/ TSH regularly.
- **Juvenile (acquired) hypothyroidism** – Hashimoto's thyroiditis (commonest); growth retardation, delayed puberty, ↓ school performance.

## 14.2 Type 1 Diabetes Mellitus

- **Autoimmune destruction of β-cells → absolute insulin deficiency**; peak **5–7 yrs & puberty**; most common type in children (T2DM ↑ with obesity).
- **S/S** – **polyuria, polydipsia, polyphagia, weight loss**, **secondary enuresis (bedwetting – often first clue)**, fatigue, **DKA (first presentation in ~25–40 %)**.
- **Diagnosis** – FBS ≥ 126, RBS ≥ 200 mg/dl with symptoms, HbA1c ≥ 6.5 %.
- **Treatment** – **insulin (lifelong)** – **basal-bolus regimen** (long-acting glargine/ detemir + rapid-acting before meals) or split-mixed; **pump**; **target HbA1c < 7 %**.
- **Insulin injection teaching** – **rotate sites** (abdomen, thigh, buttock, upper arm) – lipohypertrophy; **SC at 45–90°**; **draw clear (regular) before cloudy (NPH)**; store unopened in fridge, in-use at room temp ≤ 28 days; **SMBG** (self-monitoring 4–6 times/day).
- **Diet** – balanced, carbohydrate counting, regular meals & snacks, no restriction of calories for growth; **exercise** – snack before, monitor glucose; **sick-day rules – never omit insulin, check ketones, fluids**.
- **Honeymoon period** – transient remission (↓ insulin need) weeks–months after diagnosis.

### Diabetic Ketoacidosis (DKA)
- **Glucose > 200 mg/dl + venous pH < 7.3 or HCO₃ < 15 mmol/L + ketonaemia/ ketonuria**.
- **S/S** – dehydration, **Kussmaul breathing (deep, rapid)**, **fruity (acetone) breath**, abdominal pain, vomiting, drowsiness → coma.
- **Management (ISPAD)**:
  - **Fluids FIRST – 0.9 % NS 10–20 ml/kg over 1 hr** (shock) → deficit replacement **slowly over 48 hrs** (**avoid rapid fluids – cerebral oedema**).
  - **Insulin infusion 0.05–0.1 U/kg/hr – start 1 hr after fluids** (**no bolus**); add **dextrose (5–10 %)** when glucose falls to **250–300 mg/dl**; reduce glucose by **≤ 100 mg/dl/hr**.
  - **Potassium** – add after urine output & K⁺ < 5.5 (**40 mEq/L**) – insulin drives K⁺ into cells (**hypokalaemia**).
  - **Bicarbonate – not routinely**.
  - **Monitor** – hourly glucose, vitals, **neuro observations (GCS hourly)**, I/O; electrolytes, pH 2–4 hourly.
- **Cerebral oedema – most common cause of death in paediatric DKA** – headache, ↓ HR, ↑ BP, irritability, altered sensorium (4–12 hrs into treatment) → **mannitol 0.5–1 g/kg / 3 % hypertonic saline**, head-end elevated, reduce fluids.

### Hypoglycaemia in diabetic child
- **< 70 mg/dl** – sweating, tremor, hunger, pallor, irritability, behaviour change, confusion, seizures.
- **Conscious → "rule of 15"**: **15 g fast-acting sugar** (glucose tablets, juice ½ cup, 3 tsp sugar) → recheck in **15 min** → repeat if < 70 → then complex carbohydrate snack.
- **Unconscious → glucagon IM/ SC (0.5 mg < 25 kg; 1 mg > 25 kg) / IV 10 % dextrose 2 ml/kg**; **nothing by mouth**.

## 14.3 Other Endocrine Disorders

| Condition | Key points |
|---|---|
| **Growth hormone deficiency** | **Short stature with normal proportions**, delayed bone age, chubby, hypoglycaemia (infant), micropenis; **GH stimulation test**; **recombinant GH SC at bedtime** |
| **Short stature causes** | **Familial/ constitutional (commonest – normal growth velocity)**, malnutrition, chronic disease, hypothyroidism, GHD, Turner syndrome, skeletal dysplasia (achondroplasia – disproportionate) |
| **Precocious puberty** | **Girls < 8 yrs, boys < 9 yrs**; central (GnRH dependent – idiopathic in girls, CNS tumour in boys) – **GnRH analogue (leuprolide)** |
| **Delayed puberty** | Girls no breast development by **13 yrs**, boys no testicular enlargement by **14 yrs** |
| **Congenital adrenal hyperplasia (CAH)** | **21-hydroxylase deficiency (90 %)** – autosomal recessive; **ambiguous genitalia in girls**; **salt-wasting crisis (1–3 weeks) – vomiting, dehydration, hyponatraemia, hyperkalaemia, shock**; **↑ 17-OH progesterone**; **hydrocortisone + fludrocortisone lifelong**, stress doses |
| **Diabetes insipidus** | ADH deficiency – **polyuria (dilute urine), polydipsia, hypernatraemia**; **desmopressin** |
| **SIADH** | ↑ ADH – hyponatraemia, fluid restriction (meningitis, pneumonia) |
| **Cushing's syndrome** | Mostly iatrogenic (steroids) – moon face, buffalo hump, growth suppression |

## 14.4 Inborn Errors of Metabolism

| Disorder | Key points |
|---|---|
| **Phenylketonuria (PKU)** | **Phenylalanine hydroxylase deficiency** (autosomal recessive) → phenylalanine ↑; **musty/ mousy odour**, **fair skin, blue eyes, blond hair**, eczema, seizures, **intellectual disability**; screening – **Guthrie test** (after 24–48 hrs of protein feeds); **low-phenylalanine diet (special formula)** – **avoid high-protein foods & aspartame (artificial sweetener)**; started in first weeks, continued lifelong (especially before & during pregnancy) |
| **Galactosaemia** | **GALT deficiency**; **vomiting, jaundice, hepatomegaly, cataract (oil-drop), E. coli sepsis** after milk feeds; **lactose/ galactose-free diet (soy formula)**; **breastfeeding contraindicated** |
| **Maple syrup urine disease** | Branched-chain amino acids; sweet (maple syrup) odour urine |
| **Glycogen storage disease** | Hypoglycaemia, hepatomegaly |
| **Lysosomal storage** (Gaucher, Tay-Sachs – cherry-red spot) | Enzyme replacement (some) |

- **Newborn screening (India – RBSK/ state programmes)** – **congenital hypothyroidism, CAH, G6PD deficiency, galactosaemia, PKU**, hearing, CCHD (pulse oximetry), sickle cell (selected areas).

## 14.5 Chromosomal & Genetic Disorders

### Down Syndrome (Trisomy 21)
- **Most common chromosomal disorder & most common genetic cause of intellectual disability**; ~1 in 700–800 births; **↑ with maternal age > 35 yrs**.
- **Types** – **non-disjunction (~95 % – maternal age related)**, **translocation (~4 % – hereditary, any age; t(14;21))**, mosaicism (~1 %).
- **Features** – **hypotonia (most common neonatal sign – "floppy baby")**, **flat facial profile/ occiput (brachycephaly)**, **upslanting palpebral fissures, epicanthic folds**, **Brushfield spots** (iris), small low-set ears, **protruding tongue**, short neck with excess skin, **single transverse palmar (simian) crease**, **sandal gap** (wide 1st–2nd toe space), short stature, clinodactyly (5th finger).
- **Associated** – **CHD (~40–50 %) – AVSD/ endocardial cushion defect (most characteristic)**, VSD; **duodenal atresia ("double bubble")**, Hirschsprung; **hypothyroidism**; **leukaemia (ALL/ AML ↑ 10–20 times)**; atlanto-axial instability; hearing/ vision problems; early Alzheimer's; infertility (males).
- **Screening/ diagnosis** – **1st trimester combined test (NT ↑ on USG + PAPP-A ↓, free β-hCG ↑)**, quadruple test (AFP ↓, uE3 ↓, hCG ↑, inhibin A ↑), **NIPT**; **confirm – karyotype (CVS/ amniocentesis)**.
- **Nursing** – early intervention & **stimulation programmes**, feeding (hypotonia – support head, protruding tongue), constipation, prevent infections (respiratory), **thyroid check yearly**, cardiac evaluation (echo), parent support groups, special education, **genetic counselling**.

| Disorder | Karyotype | Key features |
|---|---|---|
| **Edwards syndrome** | **Trisomy 18** | **Clenched fists with overlapping fingers, rocker-bottom feet**, micrognathia; most die in 1st year |
| **Patau syndrome** | **Trisomy 13** | **Cleft lip/ palate, polydactyly, holoprosencephaly**, microphthalmia, scalp defects |
| **Turner syndrome** | **45, XO** (female) | **Short stature, webbed neck, shield chest (widely spaced nipples)**, **coarctation of aorta**, lymphoedema of hands/ feet at birth, **streak ovaries – primary amenorrhoea, infertility**; GH & oestrogen therapy |
| **Klinefelter syndrome** | **47, XXY** (male) | **Tall, gynaecomastia, small testes, infertility (azoospermia)**, ↓ testosterone; most common cause of male hypogonadism |
| **Fragile X syndrome** | X-linked (CGG repeat) | **Most common inherited cause of intellectual disability**; long face, **large ears, macroorchidism**, autism features |
| **Cri-du-chat** | **5p deletion** | **Cat-like cry**, microcephaly |
| **Achondroplasia** | Autosomal dominant (FGFR3) | Short-limbed dwarfism, normal intelligence |

- **Inheritance patterns** – **autosomal dominant** (Huntington's, Marfan, achondroplasia, NF-1); **autosomal recessive** (**thalassaemia, sickle cell, cystic fibrosis, PKU, galactosaemia, CAH, albinism**); **X-linked recessive** (**haemophilia A & B, G6PD, DMD, colour blindness**); X-linked dominant (vitamin D-resistant rickets).
- **Autosomal recessive – both parents carriers → each pregnancy: 25 % affected, 50 % carrier, 25 % normal**.
- **Genetic counselling** – **non-directive**, pedigree, risk estimation, prenatal diagnosis options, support; advise against consanguineous marriage where a recessive disorder runs in the family.
