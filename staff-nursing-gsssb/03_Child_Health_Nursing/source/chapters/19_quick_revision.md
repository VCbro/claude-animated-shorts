:::tip Exam strategy – Asha Didi ki last advice
- GSSSB paper me **Child Health se ~10–15 questions** aate hain – **growth & milestones, immunization schedule (dose/ route/ site), IMNCI & diarrhoea (ORS plans), newborn care, CHD aur common disorders** sabse important hain.
- **Numbers** (milestones ki age, vaccine doses, fluid formulas) ki tables roz 10 minute padho; **age-based questions** me pehle socho "ye kaunsa age group hai".
- **Priority questions**: Airway → Breathing → Circulation; bachche me **tachycardia shock ka earliest sign** hai aur **hypotension late sign**.
- MCQ Bank PDF ke saare questions **2 baar** solve karo, galtiyan notebook me likho.
:::
