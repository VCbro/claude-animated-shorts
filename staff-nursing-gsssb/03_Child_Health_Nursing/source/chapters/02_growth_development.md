## 2.1 Definitions & Principles

:::def Growth vs Development
**Growth** – increase in **size/ mass** (physical, **quantitative**, measurable – weight, height). **Development** – increase in **skill & function** (**qualitative**, functional maturity). **Maturation** – genetically determined unfolding. Growth stops in adulthood; development continues lifelong.
:::

- **Principles of growth & development**:
  - Continuous & orderly process; **sequence is same for all children, rate differs**.
  - **Cephalocaudal** – head → toe (head control before sitting/ walking).
  - **Proximodistal** – centre → periphery (shoulder control before fingers).
  - **General → specific** (mass → specific activity), **simple → complex**.
  - Different organs grow at different rates – **brain/ nervous tissue fastest early** (brain 25 % adult weight at birth, ~75 % by 2 yrs, ~90 % by 5 yrs); **lymphoid tissue** peaks before puberty; **reproductive organs** at puberty.
  - **Critical periods** – development most vulnerable at certain times.
  - Development is **predictable** and interrelated (physical, mental, social).
- **Periods of rapid growth** – **infancy** (fastest) and **adolescence (growth spurt)**. Slowest – school age (**latent** period).
- **Factors**: **Genetic** (heredity, sex, race), **environmental** – prenatal (maternal nutrition, infections, drugs, radiation), postnatal (**nutrition – most important**, illness, hormones – GH, thyroid, sex hormones, insulin), socioeconomic status, family, culture, climate, emotional factors (**psychosocial dwarfism** – emotional deprivation).

## 2.2 Physical Growth – Anthropometry

| Parameter | Birth | Milestones |
|---|---|---|
| **Weight** | **~3 kg** (India avg 2.8–3 kg) | Physiological loss **up to 10 % in first week**, regained by **10–14 days**; **doubles at 5 months**, **triples at 1 yr**, **quadruples at 2 yrs**, 5 × at 3 yrs, 6 × at 5 yrs, 10 × at 10 yrs |
| **Length/ height** | **~50 cm** | **75 cm at 1 yr** (+25 cm), 87 cm at 2 yrs, **100 cm at 4 yrs (double)**, ~150 cm at 12 yrs (triple) |
| **Head circumference (OFC)** | **33–35 cm** | **+2 cm/month first 3 months**, 1 cm/month 3–6 months, 0.5 cm/month 6–12 months → **45–47 cm at 1 yr**; 48–49 cm at 2 yrs; ~50 cm at 5 yrs |
| **Chest circumference** | **~31–33 cm** (2–3 cm **less than head**) | **Equals head at 1 yr**, then exceeds head |
| **MUAC** | ~11 cm | **6 months – 5 yrs ≈ constant (> 13.5 cm normal; < 11.5 cm SAM)** |
| **Upper : lower segment ratio** | **1.7 : 1** | 1.3 at 3 yrs, **1 : 1 at 7–10 yrs** |

- **OFC measured** – tape over **occipital protuberance & supra-orbital ridges** (largest circumference).
- **Length** measured **lying (infantometer)** < 2 yrs; **standing (stadiometer)** > 2 yrs.
- **Anterior fontanelle** closes **12–18 months** (diamond); **posterior** – **6–8 weeks** (triangular). **Large AF/ delayed closure** – rickets, hypothyroidism, hydrocephalus, Down syndrome. **Early closure** – microcephaly, craniosynostosis.

### Weight & height formulas (IAP – Weech's)

| Age | Formula |
|---|---|
| **3–12 months** – weight (kg) | **(Age in months + 9) ÷ 2** |
| **1–6 years** – weight (kg) | **Age (yrs) × 2 + 8** |
| **7–12 years** – weight (kg) | **(Age (yrs) × 7 − 5) ÷ 2** |
| **2–12 years** – height (cm) | **Age (yrs) × 6 + 77** |

- E.g. **5-year child**: weight = 5 × 2 + 8 = **18 kg**; height = 5 × 6 + 77 = **107 cm**.
- **Growth charts** – **WHO growth standards (2006)** used in MCP card – **weight-for-age** (underweight), **length/ height-for-age** (**stunting – chronic malnutrition**), **weight-for-height** (**wasting – acute malnutrition**), BMI-for-age. **Z-score < −2 SD = moderate, < −3 SD = severe**.
- **Road to Health chart** (growth chart, **David Morley**) – weight-for-age; flattening/ downward curve = danger.

### Dentition
- **First tooth** – **lower central incisor at 6–7 months**; **all 20 milk teeth by 2½ yrs (24–30 months)**.
- **No. of teeth (6–24 months) = age in months − 6**.
- **Permanent teeth – 32**; **first permanent tooth = first molar at 6 yrs**; last – third molar (17–25 yrs).
- **Delayed dentition (> 13 months)**: rickets, hypothyroidism, Down syndrome, malnutrition.
- **Teething** – drooling, irritability, gum rubbing; **does NOT cause high fever/ diarrhoea** – look for other causes.
- **Natal teeth** (at birth) / neonatal teeth (first month).

## 2.3 Developmental Milestones

@fig milestones.svg | Key milestones – gross motor, fine motor, language & social

| Age | Gross motor | Fine motor | Language | Social/ adaptive |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1 month** | Lifts head momentarily (prone) | Hands fisted | Startles to sound | Watches face |
| **2 months** | Head up 45° in prone | – | **Coos** | **Social smile (6–8 wks)** |
| **3 months** | **Neck holding (head control)** | Hands open, holds rattle | Laughs | **Recognises mother** |
| **4–5 months** | **Rolls over** (front → back 4 m, back → front 5 m) | **Bidextrous reach** | Laughs loud | Enjoys mirror |
| **6 months** | **Sits with support** (tripod) | **Palmar grasp, transfers** objects hand to hand | **Monosyllables** (ba, da) | **Stranger anxiety** begins (6–9 m) |
| **8 months** | **Sits without support** | – | – | – |
| **9 months** | **Crawls, stands with support** | **Pincer grasp (immature 9 m, mature 10–12 m)** | **Bisyllables** (mama, dada) | **Waves bye-bye**, plays peek-a-boo |
| **12 months** | **Walks alone/ with one hand held** (10–15 m) | Releases object | **1–2 meaningful words** | Plays simple ball game |
| **15 months** | Walks well, creeps upstairs | **Tower of 2 cubes**, scribbles | 4–6 words | Points to wants |
| **18 months** | **Runs**, walks upstairs (hand held) | Tower of 3–4 cubes, turns 2–3 pages | 10–20 words | Feeds self with spoon, copies parents |
| **2 years** | **Walks up & down stairs (2 feet/ step)**, kicks ball | **Tower of 6–7 cubes**, turns single page | **2-word phrases**, uses "I, me" | **Parallel play**, dresses with help |
| **3 years** | **Rides tricycle**, climbs stairs alternate feet | **Tower of 9 cubes**, **copies circle** | **Sentences**, tells name, age, sex | **Day + night bladder control** (most), shares toys |
| **4 years** | **Hops on one foot** | **Copies cross (+) & square** | Tells story | Plays cooperatively, goes to toilet alone |
| **5 years** | **Skips** | **Copies triangle** | Fluent speech | Dresses/ undresses |

- **Red flags (refer)**: no social smile by **3 months**, no head control by **4 months**, not sitting by **9 months**, **not walking by 18 months**, no words by **16–18 months**, no 2-word phrases by 2 yrs, **any loss of skills (regression) at any age**.
- **Developmental screening tools**: **DDST-II (Denver Developmental Screening Test, 0–6 yrs)**, **Trivandrum Developmental Screening Chart (TDSC)**, **Baroda Development Screening Test**, **Gesell**, Bayley scales, **Vineland Social Maturity Scale**, **Goodenough Draw-a-Man test** (IQ in children), **Stanford-Binet** (IQ).
- **Toilet training** – readiness **18–24 months** (bladder control signs); **bowel control before bladder**; **day bladder by 2–3 yrs, night by 3–5 yrs**; **enuresis** diagnosed only **after 5 yrs**.

## 2.4 Theories of Development

@fig development_theories.svg | Erikson, Freud, Piaget & play – age-wise

| Age | **Erikson (psychosocial)** | **Freud (psychosexual)** | **Piaget (cognitive)** |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–1 yr | **Trust vs Mistrust** (virtue – hope) | **Oral** | **Sensorimotor (0–2 yrs)** – **object permanence (~9 months)**, reflexes → purposeful |
| 1–3 yrs | **Autonomy vs Shame & Doubt** (will) | **Anal** (toilet training) | **Preoperational (2–7 yrs)** – **egocentrism**, **magical thinking**, **animism**, centration, irreversibility, symbolic play |
| 3–6 yrs | **Initiative vs Guilt** (purpose) | **Phallic** (Oedipus/ Electra complex) | Preoperational |
| 6–12 yrs | **Industry vs Inferiority** (competence) | **Latency** | **Concrete operational (7–11 yrs)** – **conservation**, reversibility, classification, seriation |
| 12–18 yrs | **Identity vs Role confusion** (fidelity) | **Genital** | **Formal operational (11+ yrs)** – **abstract**, hypothetical reasoning |
| Young adult | Intimacy vs Isolation | – | – |
| Middle adult | Generativity vs Stagnation | – | – |
| Old age | Integrity vs Despair | – | – |

- **Kohlberg (moral development)** – **pre-conventional** (punishment–obedience, < 9 yrs) → **conventional** (good boy/ girl, law & order – school age/ adolescence) → **post-conventional** (social contract, universal ethics).
- **Sullivan** – interpersonal theory; **Bandura** – social learning (modelling); **Bowlby** – attachment; **Gesell** – maturational theory.
- **Fears**: infant – strangers (6–9 m); **toddler – separation**; **preschool – body mutilation, dark, ghosts**; school – failure, loss of control, death; adolescent – body image, peers.

## 2.5 Play

- **Functions**: sensorimotor development, intellectual, socialisation, creativity, self-awareness, **therapeutic value** (expresses fears), moral.

| Type of play | Age | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Solitary** | **Infant** | Plays alone – rattles, mobiles, mirror |
| **Onlooker** | Toddler | Watches others play |
| **Parallel** | **Toddler** | **Plays beside, not with** others – push-pull toys, blocks |
| **Associative** | **Preschool** | Plays together without rules/ organisation – dress-up, **make-believe/ dramatic** |
| **Cooperative** | **School age** | Organised, **rules, team games**, competition – board games, sports |

- **Suitable toys**: infant – bright mobiles, rattles, soft toys (large, non-toxic, no small parts); toddler – **push-pull toys**, blocks, picture books; preschool – **dolls, doctor set, crayons, tricycle**, puzzles; school – **board games, collections, books, bicycle**, hobbies; adolescent – music, sports, computer.
- **Therapeutic play** in hospital – **doll play/ medical play** to reduce fear (explaining procedure on doll).
