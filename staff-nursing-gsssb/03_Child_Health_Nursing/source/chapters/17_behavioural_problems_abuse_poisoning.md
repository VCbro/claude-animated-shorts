## 17.1 Common Behaviour Problems

| Problem | Age | Key points & management |
|---|---|---|
| **Thumb sucking** | Normal **< 4 yrs** | Ignore in toddler; after 4–5 yrs – **dental malocclusion**; **positive reinforcement, distraction**; no punishment/ bitter substances forcibly |
| **Nail biting** | School age | Anxiety – address stress, keep nails short |
| **Temper tantrums** | **1–3 yrs (toddler)** – peak 2 yrs ("terrible twos") | **Ignore the behaviour (if safe), stay calm, be consistent**, **time-out (1 min per year of age)**, don't give in, praise good behaviour |
| **Breath-holding spells** | 6 months – 5 yrs | Triggered by anger/ pain; benign; reassure; iron therapy; no punishment |
| **Pica** | > 2 yrs | Eating non-food (mud, chalk, paint) – **iron deficiency, lead poisoning, worms**; investigate & treat |
| **Enuresis** | **≥ 5 yrs** | See GU chapter – alarm, star chart, desmopressin |
| **Encopresis** | **≥ 4 yrs** | Faecal soiling – constipation with overflow; bowel training |
| **Sleep problems** | Night terrors (**non-REM, child doesn't remember**), nightmares (REM, remembers), sleep walking (somnambulism) | Reassure, regular routine, safety |
| **Stuttering (stammering)** | 2–5 yrs (developmental) | Don't interrupt/ criticise; speech therapy if persists |
| **Sibling rivalry** | New baby | Prepare child, involve in care, spend special time |
| **School phobia (refusal)** | School age | Separation anxiety – **gradual return to school** |
| **Lying, stealing** | Preschool (fantasy) normal | Older – identify cause, consistent discipline |
| **Juvenile delinquency** | < 18 yrs | Antisocial acts – **Juvenile Justice Act 2015**, observation homes, counselling |
| **Substance abuse** | Adolescents | Solvent sniffing (whitener, glue), tobacco, alcohol |

## 17.2 Neurodevelopmental Disorders

### ADHD (Attention Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder)
- Onset **before 12 yrs**, symptoms in **≥ 2 settings** (home & school) for **≥ 6 months**; **boys > girls**.
- **Triad – inattention, hyperactivity, impulsivity** (can't sit still, talks excessively, interrupts, careless mistakes, loses things).
- Rx – **behaviour therapy (first-line in < 6 yrs)**, **parent training**, classroom modification (front seat, short tasks, structured routine), **methylphenidate (stimulant – drug of choice)** – side effects: **anorexia, weight loss, insomnia (give in morning), growth suppression**; atomoxetine.

### Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD)
- **Onset in early childhood (< 3 yrs)**; boys 4 : 1.
- **Deficits in social communication & interaction** – **poor eye contact**, doesn't respond to name, **no social smile/ pointing (joint attention)**, no pretend play, prefers to be alone, **delayed/ abnormal speech (echolalia)**; **restricted, repetitive behaviours** – **hand flapping, rocking, spinning**, insistence on sameness (routine), lining up toys, unusual sensory responses.
- **Screening – M-CHAT (16–30 months)**; **INCLEN/ ISAA** (India).
- **Management** – **early intensive behavioural intervention (ABA – applied behaviour analysis)**, speech & occupational therapy (sensory integration), special education; **no cure**; risperidone for irritability; **structured, predictable environment**; **vaccines (MMR) do NOT cause autism**.

### Intellectual Disability (ID – Mental Retardation)
- **IQ < 70** + deficits in adaptive functioning, **onset during developmental period (< 18 yrs)**.
- **IQ = (Mental age ÷ Chronological age) × 100**.

| Severity | IQ | Remarks |
|---|---|---|
| **Mild** | **50–69** | **~85 %**; **"educable"**, achieve ~6th grade, independent living with support |
| **Moderate** | **35–49** | **"Trainable"**, self-care, semi-skilled work under supervision |
| **Severe** | **20–34** | Basic self-care with training, constant supervision |
| **Profound** | **< 20** | Total care, often with physical disabilities |

- **Causes** – **Down syndrome (most common genetic)**, **Fragile X (most common inherited)**, **congenital hypothyroidism (most common preventable)**, birth asphyxia, prematurity, infections (TORCH, meningitis), malnutrition, iodine deficiency, fetal alcohol syndrome, lead poisoning, PKU.
- **Management** – early intervention & stimulation, special education, vocational training, **parental counselling**, **NIOS, disability certificate (RPwD Act 2016 – 21 disabilities)**, institutions – **NIEPID (Secunderabad)**.

### Learning Disability
- **Dyslexia (reading – most common)**, **dysgraphia (writing)**, **dyscalculia (mathematics)**; **normal intelligence**; remedial education.

## 17.3 Child Abuse & Neglect

- **Types** – **physical abuse**, **sexual abuse**, **emotional/ psychological abuse**, **neglect (most common type)**, Munchausen syndrome by proxy (fabricated illness by caregiver).
- **Indicators of physical abuse**:
  - **History inconsistent with injury/ developmental stage**, **delay in seeking care**, changing stories.
  - Bruises of **different ages/ colours** (red-purple fresh → blue → green → yellow → brown), **patterned bruises (belt, hand, bite marks)**, bruises in non-ambulant infant, on **back, buttocks, ears, cheeks, genitals** (non-bony areas).
  - **Burns – cigarette (round), immersion (glove/ stocking), iron-shaped**.
  - **Fractures – spiral (twisting), posterior rib, metaphyseal corner, multiple at different healing stages, skull**.
  - **Shaken baby syndrome (abusive head trauma)** – **< 1 yr**; **subdural haematoma + retinal haemorrhages** (± rib fractures), irritability, vomiting, seizures, no external injury.
- **Sexual abuse** – genital injury/ bleeding, STI, pregnancy, **age-inappropriate sexual behaviour/ knowledge**, regression, fear of particular person.
- **Neglect** – FTT, poor hygiene, missed immunisations, untreated illness, unsupervised child.
- **Behavioural signs** – **"frozen watchfulness"**, withdrawn, fearful of parents/ adults, aggressive, school problems.

### Nurse's role
- **Recognise, document (exact words of child in quotes, body map, photographs), report**; **treat injuries**; **ensure child safety (hospital admission if needed)**; **don't confront or accuse parents**; **don't interrogate repeatedly**; use **non-leading open questions**; inform **CWC (Child Welfare Committee)/ police/ Special Juvenile Police Unit, CHILDLINE 1098**.
- **POCSO Act 2012** – **mandatory reporting of sexual offences against < 18 yrs by any person, including doctors & nurses** – **failure to report is punishable**; child-friendly procedures (statement at child's place, female police officer, no aggressive questioning, in-camera trial, special courts); **POCSO amendment 2019 – death penalty for aggravated penetrative sexual assault**.
- **Medical examination of a female child – by a woman doctor**, in presence of parent/ trusted person.
- **Good touch – bad touch** education in schools.

## 17.4 Accidents & Injuries

- **Accidents – leading cause of death in children > 1 yr** (globally); types by age: **infant – falls, suffocation, aspiration, burns**; **toddler – falls, poisoning, drowning, burns (scalds)** – **most accident-prone (curious, mobile, no sense of danger)**; preschool – road traffic (pedestrian), drowning; school – **RTA, sports, bicycle**; adolescent – **RTA, drowning, violence, suicide**.
- **Prevention** – **"supervision is the best prevention"**; childproofing (**socket covers, stair gates, window guards, cabinet locks**), medicines & chemicals locked & out of reach in **original containers (not in soft-drink bottles)**, **child-resistant caps**, no small objects (< 3.2 cm – coins, beads, button batteries, peanuts) for < 3–4 yrs, **never leave child alone near water (buckets, tubs, ponds)**, car seats (rear-facing infants), helmets, **sleep on back (SIDS)**.
- **Drowning** – toddlers (buckets, tanks); rescue breaths first, CPR, prevent hypothermia, observe 24 hrs (secondary drowning).
- **SIDS (Sudden Infant Death Syndrome)** – **2–4 months**, during sleep; prevention – **"Back to Sleep" (supine)**, firm mattress, no soft bedding/ pillows/ toys, **no smoking**, **room sharing without bed sharing**, avoid overheating, **breastfeeding**, pacifier at sleep.

## 17.5 Poisoning

- **Most common age – 1–3 yrs (toddler, accidental)**; adolescents – intentional.
- **Common agents in India** – **kerosene (most common in children)**, **household products** (detergents, bleach, toilet cleaners), **medicines (iron, paracetamol)**, **organophosphates (insecticides)**, rodenticides, plant (dhatura), **snake/ scorpion bite**.

| Poison | Key points | Specific treatment/ antidote |
|---|---|---|
| **Kerosene/ hydrocarbons** | **Aspiration pneumonitis (main danger)** – cough, choking, tachypnoea, fever | **NO induced vomiting, NO gastric lavage**; O₂, observe 6–8 hrs, CXR; **no antibiotics/ steroids routinely** |
| **Paracetamol** | Hepatotoxicity (toxic dose **> 150 mg/kg**); asymptomatic first 24 hrs | **N-acetylcysteine (NAC)** – best within 8 hrs; levels at 4 hrs (Rumack-Matthew nomogram) |
| **Iron** | **> 60 mg/kg elemental – serious**; vomiting, GI bleeding → apparent recovery → shock, hepatic failure; radiopaque tablets | **Desferrioxamine IV** (vin-rosé coloured urine); **activated charcoal does NOT bind iron** |
| **Organophosphates** | **DUMBELS** (Diarrhoea, Urination, Miosis, Bronchorrhoea/ bradycardia, Emesis, Lacrimation, Salivation), fasciculations | **Atropine** (till secretions dry/ HR > 80) + **pralidoxime (PAM)**; remove clothes, wash skin |
| **Datura (anticholinergic)** | **"Dry as bone, red as beet, hot as hare, blind as bat, mad as hatter"** – dilated pupils, flushing, delirium | Physostigmine (rarely), supportive |
| **Lead** | Pica, old paint; **anaemia (basophilic stippling), abdominal colic, encephalopathy, lead lines** | **Chelation – succimer (DMSA), CaNa₂EDTA, BAL** |
| **Corrosives (acids/ alkalis)** | Burns of mouth/ oesophagus, drooling, stridor | **NO emesis, NO lavage, NO neutralisation**; dilute with small sips of water/ milk only if able to swallow; endoscopy (12–24 hrs) |
| **Benzodiazepines** | Sedation | **Flumazenil** (caution) |
| **Opioids** | **Pin-point pupils, respiratory depression, coma** | **Naloxone** |
| **Snake bite (neurotoxic – cobra/ krait; haemotoxic – viper)** | Ptosis, respiratory paralysis / bleeding (**20-min whole blood clotting test – 20WBCT**) | **Polyvalent ASV**; **immobilise limb, reassure, no tourniquet, no cutting/ sucking**, remove rings; neostigmine + atropine (cobra) |
| **Scorpion sting** | Autonomic storm, **pulmonary oedema**, myocarditis | **Prazosin** (drug of choice), scorpion antivenom |

- **General management** – **ABC first**, decontamination: **activated charcoal 1 g/kg** (within 1 hr; **not for kerosene, corrosives, iron, alcohols, lithium**), **gastric lavage (rarely – within 1 hr, life-threatening, protected airway; contraindicated in corrosives & hydrocarbons)**; **induced emesis (ipecac) – no longer recommended**; enhance elimination; **specific antidote**; supportive care; **psychosocial assessment & prevention education**.
- **Poison control centre** – AIIMS Delhi (National Poison Information Centre).

## 17.6 Adolescent Health

- **Puberty** – **girls 8–13 yrs** (thelarche – first sign; menarche ~2–2.5 yrs later, after peak height velocity), **boys 9–14 yrs** (**testicular enlargement – first sign**); **Tanner staging (SMR 1–5)**.
- **Problems** – **anaemia (most common nutritional)**, acne, **body image issues**, eating disorders (**anorexia nervosa – girls, BMI < 17.5, amenorrhoea, fear of weight gain; bulimia**), **substance abuse**, **teenage pregnancy**, STIs/ HIV, **depression & suicide**, **RTAs**, internet addiction, academic stress, **menstrual problems (dysmenorrhoea)**, PCOS.
- **Services** – **RKSK – AFHCs ("Ujjwal" clinics)**, **peer educators (Saathiya)**, **WIFS (weekly IFA)**, **Menstrual Hygiene Scheme** (sanitary napkins), **Kishori Shakti Yojana/ SABLA (adolescent girls)**, school health & wellness programme (Ayushman Bharat), **HPV vaccine (9–14 yrs girls)**.
- **HEEADSSS assessment** – **H**ome, **E**ducation/ employment, **E**ating, **A**ctivities, **D**rugs, **S**exuality, **S**uicide/ depression, **S**afety – **confidential, non-judgmental**, interview alone.
