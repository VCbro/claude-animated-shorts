## 6.1 Classification of Newborns

| By | Category |
|---|---|
| **Gestational age** | **Preterm < 37 wks** (extremely < 28, very 28–< 32, moderate/ late 32–< 37) · **Term 37–42** · **Post-term ≥ 42 wks** |
| **Birth weight** | **LBW < 2500 g** · **VLBW < 1500 g** · **ELBW < 1000 g** · Macrosomia > 4000 g |
| **Weight for gestation** | **SGA < 10th centile** · AGA 10–90th · **LGA > 90th centile** |

- **High-risk newborn** – preterm, LBW, SGA/ LGA, infant of diabetic mother, birth asphyxia, sepsis, congenital anomalies, jaundice, multiple birth, Rh problems.
- **Gestational age assessment** – **New Ballard Score** (neuromuscular + physical maturity), Dubowitz.

| Feature | **Preterm** | **Term** |
|---|---|---|
| Skin | **Thin, shiny, red/ gelatinous, visible veins**, **lanugo abundant** | Thick, pale, creases, little lanugo |
| Sole creases | **Few/ only anterior** | Cover whole sole |
| Breast nodule | **< 5 mm**, areola barely visible | 5–10 mm, raised areola |
| Ear | **Soft, flat, slow recoil** | Firm, instant recoil |
| Genitalia | **Male – testes undescended, few rugae; female – prominent clitoris & labia minora** | Testes descended, rugae; labia majora cover minora |
| Posture | **Extended, hypotonic (frog-like)** | **Fully flexed** |
| Scarf sign | Elbow crosses midline | Elbow doesn't reach midline |

### Problems of preterm/ LBW
- **Hypothermia** (little brown fat, large surface area), **hypoglycaemia**, **RDS (surfactant deficiency)**, apnoea of prematurity (**caffeine citrate**), **jaundice**, **infections/ sepsis**, feeding problems (poor suck-swallow coordination < 32–34 wks), **NEC**, **IVH**, **PDA**, **ROP**, anaemia, BPD, hypocalcaemia.

## 6.2 Thermoregulation

- Newborns lose heat by **evaporation (most at birth – wet baby)**, **conduction** (cold surface), **convection** (draughts), **radiation** (cold walls/ windows). **Brown fat → non-shivering thermogenesis** (newborns cannot shiver).
- **Normal axillary temperature 36.5–37.5 °C**.

| Hypothermia (WHO) | Temperature |
|---|---|
| **Cold stress (mild)** | **36.0–36.4 °C** |
| **Moderate** | **32.0–35.9 °C** |
| **Severe** | **< 32 °C** |

- **Warm chain (10 steps)** – warm delivery room (**25–28 °C**), **immediate drying**, skin-to-skin, breastfeeding, **postpone bathing (≥ 24 hrs)/ weighing**, appropriate clothing & bedding, mother & baby together, warm transport, warm resuscitation, training/ awareness.
- **Touch method** – **abdomen warm & feet warm = normal**; abdomen warm, feet cold = cold stress; both cold = hypothermia.
- **Radiant warmer** (servo-control, probe on abdomen/ upper quadrant), **incubator**.
- **Hyperthermia** – > 37.5 °C – undress, remove from heat source, frequent feeds.

### Kangaroo Mother Care (KMC)
- **Components** – **continuous skin-to-skin contact** (baby in nappy, cap & socks, between breasts, upright, frog position), **exclusive breastfeeding**, **early discharge with follow-up**.
- **Eligibility** – **stable LBW babies** (especially < 2000 g); can start in **SNCU when stable**; duration – as long as possible (**minimum 1 hr per session**, ideally 24 hrs) until baby reaches ~2500 g/ 40 wks or wriggles out.
- **Benefits** – thermal control, ↑ breastfeeding, weight gain, ↓ infection, ↓ mortality, bonding; **iKMC** (immediate KMC) after birth – WHO 2022.

## 6.3 Feeding the Preterm/ LBW

| Gestation/ weight | Feeding method |
|---|---|
| **< 28 wks / < 1200 g** (or sick) | **IV fluids initially → trophic feeds via OG tube** |
| **28–31 wks** | **Gavage (OG/ NG) feeding** with expressed breast milk |
| **32–34 wks** | **Paladai/ katori-spoon** (± gavage) |
| **≥ 34 wks** | **Direct breastfeeding** (suck-swallow-breathe coordination) |

- **Expressed breast milk (EBM) – best**; human milk fortifier for VLBW; donor milk banks (**"Comprehensive Lactation Management Centres"**).
- Feeding every **2 hrs (VLBW)**/ 2–3 hrs; check **gastric residual** before feeds; abdominal distension → suspect NEC.
- **Supplements** – **vitamin D 400 IU/day**, **iron 2 mg/kg/day from 2 weeks** (preterm) till 1 yr, multivitamin, calcium/ phosphorus (VLBW).

## 6.4 Neonatal Jaundice (Hyperbilirubinaemia)

| | **Physiological** | **Pathological** |
|---|---|---|
| Onset | **After 24 hrs** (day 2–3) | **Within first 24 hrs** |
| Peak | **Day 3–5 (term)**, day 5–7 (preterm) | Any time |
| Duration | **Disappears by 14 days (term)**, 21 days (preterm) | **> 2 weeks (term)** / 3 weeks (preterm) – prolonged |
| Bilirubin rise | < 5 mg/dl/day | **> 5 mg/dl/day** (> 0.5 mg/dl/hr) |
| Max level | < 12–15 mg/dl (term) | Exceeds phototherapy range |
| Conjugated | Unconjugated | **Conjugated > 2 mg/dl (or > 20 %) – always pathological** (cholestasis – pale stools, dark urine) |
| Palms/ soles yellow | No | Yes (severe) |

- **Causes of physiological** – ↑ RBC breakdown (high Hb, short RBC life **70–90 days**), **immature liver (low glucuronyl transferase)**, ↑ enterohepatic circulation.
- **Pathological** – **haemolytic (Rh/ ABO incompatibility, G6PD deficiency)**, sepsis, **hypothyroidism**, breast milk jaundice, biliary atresia (conjugated), cephalhaematoma, polycythaemia.
- **Breastfeeding jaundice** (first week – inadequate intake – feed more) vs **breast milk jaundice** (late, 2nd–3rd week – factors in milk – continue BF).
- **Kramer's rule (dermal zones)** – **face 5 → chest 10 → abdomen 12 → legs 15 → palms & soles > 15 mg/dl** (cephalocaudal progression).
- **Kernicterus (bilirubin encephalopathy)** – unconjugated bilirubin crosses BBB → **basal ganglia** – lethargy, poor suck, hypotonia → **hypertonia, opisthotonus, high-pitched cry, setting-sun eyes, seizures**; sequelae – **choreoathetoid CP, sensorineural deafness, upward gaze palsy, dental enamel dysplasia**.

### Phototherapy – nursing
- **Blue light (wavelength 450–460 nm)** converts bilirubin to water-soluble **lumirubin** (structural isomerisation) excreted in bile/ urine.
- **Baby naked** (only diaper/ minimal), **eyes covered (eye shields – retinal damage)**, genitals covered (debated), **distance 30–45 cm** (LED closer as per manufacturer).
- **Change position every 2 hrs**; **continue breastfeeding** (frequent feeds) – **extra fluids 10–20 %** (insensible loss ↑).
- **Monitor temperature 2–4 hourly** (hypo/ hyperthermia), **daily weight**, hydration, **serum bilirubin 12–24 hourly** (skin colour unreliable during phototherapy).
- **Side effects** – **dehydration (↑ insensible water loss)**, **loose green stools**, skin rash, **bronze baby syndrome (if conjugated ↑ – contraindicated in cholestasis)**, hyper/ hypothermia, retinal damage, separation from mother.
- **Exchange transfusion** – removes bilirubin & antibodies; **double volume = 2 × 80 ml/kg = 160 ml/kg** (removes ~85 % RBCs, ~50 % bilirubin); **umbilical vein**; fresh **O-negative/ compatible blood**; 5–10 ml aliquots (push-pull); monitor vitals, Ca²⁺, glucose; complications – emboli, NEC, infection, hypocalcaemia, electrolyte imbalance, arrhythmia.

## 6.5 Neonatal Sepsis

- **Early-onset (EOS) < 72 hrs** – maternal (vertical) – **PROM > 18–24 hrs, maternal fever, foul liquor, prolonged labour, multiple PV exams**; organisms – **E. coli, GBS, Klebsiella**.
- **Late-onset (LOS) > 72 hrs** – nosocomial/ community – **Klebsiella, Staph aureus**, Pseudomonas, CONS.
- **S/S** – **refusal to feed (earliest)**, **lethargy**, hypo/ hyperthermia (**hypothermia more common**), poor cry, **apnoea**, jaundice, abdominal distension, vomiting, **sclerema**, seizures, bulging fontanelle (meningitis), shock.
- **Investigations** – **blood culture (gold standard)**, **sepsis screen** (TLC < 5000, ANC low, I/T ratio > 0.2, **CRP > 1 mg/dl**, micro-ESR > 15 mm) – **≥ 2 positive = probable sepsis**; LP.
- **Treatment** – **ampicillin/ cloxacillin + gentamicin** (first line); cefotaxime, piperacillin-tazobactam, vancomycin, meropenem as per sensitivity; supportive – warmth, IV fluids, glucose, O₂.
- **Prevention** – **hand hygiene (most important)**, **6 cleans**, exclusive breastfeeding, **dry cord care**, rooming-in, aseptic procedures, **chlorhexidine 7.1 % cord application in high-NMR/ home delivery areas**.
- **Local infections** – **umbilical sepsis (omphalitis)**, skin pustules, **oral thrush (candida – white curdy patches not wiped off → nystatin/ clotrimazole)**, conjunctivitis (**ophthalmia neonatorum – gonococcal day 2–5, chlamydial day 5–14**).

## 6.6 Birth Asphyxia & HIE

- **Failure to initiate/ sustain breathing at birth** (WHO); **APGAR ≤ 3 at 1 min** (NNF – gasping/ no breathing).
- **HIE (Hypoxic Ischaemic Encephalopathy) – Sarnat staging**: **Stage I (mild)** – hyperalert, irritable, normal tone, dilated pupils, no seizures; **Stage II (moderate)** – lethargy, hypotonia, **seizures**, miosis; **Stage III (severe)** – **stupor/ coma**, flaccid, absent reflexes, apnoea.
- Management – **NRP**, maintain temperature (**therapeutic hypothermia 33–34 °C for 72 hrs in moderate-severe HIE – within 6 hrs** in NICUs), glucose, fluids (restrict – SIADH), treat seizures (**phenobarbitone 20 mg/kg loading – first-line**), ventilation.

## 6.7 Respiratory Problems

| Condition | Key points |
|---|---|
| **RDS (Hyaline Membrane Disease)** | **Preterm**, **surfactant deficiency**; onset **within 6 hrs**, ↑ RR, **expiratory grunt**, retractions, nasal flaring, cyanosis; X-ray – **ground glass, air bronchogram**; Rx – **CPAP**, **surfactant (intratracheal – INSURE/ LISA)**; **prevention – antenatal steroids**; **Silverman-Anderson score** (0–10; ≥ 7 impending failure) / **Downes' score** |
| **TTN (Transient tachypnoea)** | **Term, LSCS**, delayed clearance of lung fluid; resolves in **24–72 hrs**; X-ray – fluid in fissures |
| **MAS (Meconium aspiration)** | **Post-term, fetal distress**; X-ray – patchy infiltrates, hyperinflation; risk of **PPHN, pneumothorax**; **routine intrapartum/ tracheal suction no longer recommended for vigorous baby** |
| **Apnoea of prematurity** | Pause **≥ 20 sec** (or shorter with bradycardia/ cyanosis); < 34 wks; **tactile stimulation, caffeine citrate** |

## 6.8 Other Neonatal Problems

- **Neonatal hypoglycaemia** – **blood glucose < 45 mg/dl** (Indian practice; treat < 40–45); at risk – **IDM, LBW, preterm, SGA, sepsis, hypothermia, asphyxia**; S/S – jitteriness, lethargy, poor feeding, apnoea, seizures (may be asymptomatic); Rx – breastfeed/ EBM if asymptomatic; symptomatic – **10 % dextrose 2 ml/kg IV bolus** → infusion **6–8 mg/kg/min** (GIR); **avoid 25 % / 50 % dextrose (rebound, vein damage)**.
- **Hypocalcaemia** – < 7 mg/dl (total) – jitteriness, seizures – **10 % calcium gluconate 2 ml/kg slow IV with cardiac monitoring**.
- **NEC (Necrotising enterocolitis)** – **preterm, formula-fed, asphyxia**; **abdominal distension, bilious aspirates/ vomiting, bloody stools**; X-ray – **pneumatosis intestinalis (gas in bowel wall – pathognomonic)**; Rx – **NPO, NG decompression, IV antibiotics, TPN**; prevention – **breast milk**.
- **IVH (intraventricular haemorrhage)** – preterm < 32 wks, first 72 hrs; prevent – avoid rapid fluid/ osmolar changes, minimal handling, head midline.
- **ROP (Retinopathy of prematurity)** – **< 34 wks / < 1750 g**, oxygen toxicity; **screening by day 20–30 of life** (or 4 weeks); target SpO₂ 90–95 %; Rx – laser photocoagulation, anti-VEGF.
- **Polycythaemia** – venous Hct > 65 %; partial exchange.
- **Infant of diabetic mother (IDM)** – **macrosomia**, **hypoglycaemia (most common)**, hypocalcaemia, RDS, polycythaemia, jaundice, **sacral agenesis/ caudal regression**, cardiomyopathy, birth injuries.
- **Neonatal seizures** – subtle (most common – lip smacking, eye deviation, cycling), clonic, tonic, myoclonic; causes – **HIE (most common)**, hypoglycaemia, hypocalcaemia, sepsis/ meningitis, IVH; Rx – glucose/ calcium if low, **phenobarbitone** (first line).

## 6.9 Levels of Newborn Care (India)

| Unit | Where | Function |
|---|---|---|
| **NBCC** (Newborn Care Corner) | **Every delivery point** (labour room/ OT) | Resuscitation, warmth, early BF, vitamin K |
| **NBSU** (Newborn Stabilisation Unit) | **FRU/ CHC** | Stabilisation (4 beds), referral |
| **SNCU** (Special Newborn Care Unit) | **District hospital** (≥ 12 beds) | Level II care – except ventilation & surgery |
| **NICU** | Medical colleges/ tertiary | Level III – ventilation, surgery |

- **Incubator care** – temperature per weight/ age (**thermo-neutral environment**), humidity, clean daily with soap & water, open portholes only, change every 7 days.
- **Infection control in NICU** – **hand washing (40–60 sec)/ alcohol rub between babies**, gown, minimal handling, no sharing equipment, visitors restricted, cohorting.
