## 1.1 Basic Concepts

:::def Child Health (Paediatric) Nursing
Specialised nursing care of children from **birth to adolescence** – promotion of growth & development, prevention of illness, care during illness and rehabilitation, with **family-centred care** as the core philosophy. Father of Paediatrics – **Abraham Jacobi**.
:::

| Age group | Period |
|---|---|
| **Neonate (newborn)** | **Birth – 28 days** (early neonatal 0–7 days, late 8–28 days) |
| **Infant** | **Birth – 1 year** |
| **Toddler** | **1 – 3 years** |
| **Preschooler** | **3 – 6 years** |
| **School-age** | **6 – 12 years** |
| **Adolescent** | **10 – 19 years** (WHO) · teenager 13–19 |
| Perinatal period | **28 weeks gestation – 7 days after birth** |
| Under-five | 0 – 5 years (most vulnerable) |

- **Children (0–14 yrs) ≈ 31 % of India's population (Census 2011)** – a large vulnerable group.
- **Concept of "child health"** – not just absence of disease but complete physical, mental, social wellbeing + optimal growth & development.
- **Modern trends**: family-centred care, **atraumatic care** (minimise psychological & physical distress – EMLA, play, parents present), rooming-in, **day-care surgery**, home-based newborn care, preventive & promotive focus, evidence-based practice, child rights, **"Child-friendly hospital"**.

### Roles of a paediatric nurse
- Direct **caregiver**, **health educator** (parents), **advocate** (child rights), **counsellor**, **coordinator/ collaborator**, researcher, manager, **case finder** (screening), **play therapist**, consultant, protector (child abuse detection).

## 1.2 Child Health Indicators (India – SRS 2021)

| Indicator | Definition | India |
|---|---|---|
| **IMR** | Infant deaths (< 1 yr) per **1000 live births** | **27** |
| **NMR** | Neonatal deaths (< 28 days) per 1000 live births | **19** |
| **U5MR** | Under-5 deaths per 1000 live births | **31** |
| **Perinatal mortality rate** | Still births (≥ 28 wks) + early neonatal deaths per 1000 **total births** | – |

- **SDG 3.2 (by 2030)**: **NMR ≤ 12**, **U5MR ≤ 25** per 1000 live births.
- **Leading causes of neonatal death** – **prematurity/ LBW (most common)**, birth asphyxia, neonatal sepsis, congenital anomalies.
- **Leading causes of under-5 death** – neonatal causes, **pneumonia**, **diarrhoea**, malnutrition (underlying ~45 %), injuries.
- **IMR is the most sensitive indicator** of health status of a community.

## 1.3 Child Rights & Laws

| Law/ Convention | Key point |
|---|---|
| **UNCRC (UN Convention on Rights of the Child)** | Adopted **20 Nov 1989**; India ratified **1992**; 4 core rights – **survival, development, protection, participation** |
| **Universal Children's Day** | **20 November** |
| **Children's Day (India)** | **14 November** (Pandit Nehru's birthday) |
| **POCSO Act 2012** | Protection of Children from Sexual Offences – child = **< 18 yrs**; **mandatory reporting** (nurse also) |
| **Juvenile Justice (Care & Protection) Act 2015** | Child in conflict with law/ in need of care; **16–18 yrs heinous crimes** – may be tried as adult |
| **Right to Education (RTE) Act 2009** | Free & compulsory education **6–14 yrs** (Article 21A) |
| **Child & Adolescent Labour (Prohibition) Act 1986 (amended 2016)** | No employment **< 14 yrs**; adolescents 14–18 not in hazardous work |
| **Prohibition of Child Marriage Act 2006** | Girls < 18, boys < 21 = child marriage |
| **PCPNDT Act 1994** | Bans sex selection/ sex determination |
| **Infant Milk Substitutes (IMS) Act 1992 (amended 2003)** | Promotion of formula/ feeding bottles banned (breastfeeding protection) |
| **CHILDLINE** | **1098** – 24×7 free emergency helpline for children |
| **NCPCR** | National Commission for Protection of Child Rights (2007) |

## 1.4 National Child Health Programmes

| Programme | Year | Key points |
|---|---|---|
| **EPI** (Expanded Programme on Immunization) | **1978** | Started immunization |
| **UIP** (Universal Immunization Programme) | **1985** | Now 12 VPDs (+ JE in endemic districts) |
| **Pulse Polio** | **1995** | OPV to all < 5 yrs on NIDs; India **polio-free certified 27 March 2014** (last case **13 Jan 2011**, Howrah, WB) |
| **ICDS** (Integrated Child Development Services) | **2 Oct 1975** | Anganwadi; beneficiaries – **children 0–6 yrs, pregnant & lactating women, adolescent girls**; **6 services** – supplementary nutrition, immunization, health check-up, referral, pre-school non-formal education, nutrition & health education |
| **IMNCI** | **2003** (India) | Integrated Management of Neonatal & Childhood Illness – 0–2 months & 2 months–5 yrs |
| **NRHM → NHM** | 2005 / 2013 | **RMNCH+A** strategy (2013) – continuum of care |
| **JSSK** | 2011 | Free care for sick infants (now up to 1 yr) incl. transport |
| **HBNC** (Home Based Newborn Care) | **2011** | ASHA home visits – institutional delivery: **day 3, 7, 14, 21, 28, 42** (6 visits); home delivery – **+ day 1** (7 visits) |
| **HBYC** (Home Based Young Child care) | **2018** | ASHA visits at **3, 6, 9, 12, 15 months** – nutrition, ORS/ zinc, development, WASH |
| **RBSK** (Rashtriya Bal Swasthya Karyakram) | **Feb 2013** | **"4 D's"** – **D**efects at birth, **D**eficiencies, **D**iseases, **D**evelopmental delays incl. disability; 0–18 yrs; mobile health teams; **DEIC** (District Early Intervention Centre) |
| **RKSK** (Rashtriya Kishor Swasthya Karyakram) | **Jan 2014** | Adolescents **10–19 yrs**; 6 themes – nutrition, sexual & reproductive health, NCDs, substance misuse, injuries & violence, mental health; **AFHC** (Adolescent Friendly Health Clinics) |
| **Mission Indradhanush** | **25 Dec 2014** | Full immunization of left-out/ drop-out children; **IMI** (Intensified MI) 2017 onwards |
| **MAA** (Mothers' Absolute Affection) | **2016** | Breastfeeding promotion |
| **POSHAN Abhiyaan** (National Nutrition Mission) | **8 Mar 2018** (Jhunjhunu, Rajasthan) | Reduce stunting, undernutrition, anaemia, LBW; **Poshan Maah – September** |
| **Anaemia Mukt Bharat** | **2018** | 6×6×6 strategy; IFA supplementation by age (see Ch. 3) |
| **National Deworming Day** | 2015 | **10 Feb & 10 Aug** – albendazole 1–19 yrs |
| **SAANS** | **2019** | Social Awareness & Action to Neutralise Pneumonia Successfully |
| **IDCF / STOP Diarrhoea campaign** | 2014 / 2024 | ORS + zinc distribution (pre-monsoon) |
| **PM POSHAN** (Mid-day meal) | **1995** (renamed 2021) | Hot cooked meal for school children |
| **LaQshya, SNCU/ NBSU/ NBCC** | – | Facility newborn care – **NBCC** (every delivery point), **NBSU** (FRU/ CHC), **SNCU** (district hospital) |
| **Gujarat – Bal Sakha Yojana** | 2009 | Newborn care of BPL babies by private paediatricians (PPP) |
