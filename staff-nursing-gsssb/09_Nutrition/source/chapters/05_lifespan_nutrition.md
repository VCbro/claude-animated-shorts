## 5.1 Pregnancy

- **Extra requirements (ICMR 2020)** – **energy +350 kcal/day (2nd & 3rd trimester)**; **protein +9.5 g (2nd) & +22 g (3rd trimester)**; **iron 27 mg + IFA supplementation**; **folate 570 µg**; **calcium 1000 mg** (+ supplement); **iodine 250 µg**; vitamin C +20 mg; vitamin A ~900 µg; **DHA/ ω-3**; **water ~2.5–3 L**.
- **Weight gain (IOM, by pre-pregnancy BMI)** – underweight (< 18.5) **12.5–18 kg**; **normal (18.5–24.9) 11.5–16 kg** (**Indian average ~10–12 kg**); overweight 7–11.5 kg; obese 5–9 kg; **~1–2 kg in 1st trimester, then ~0.5 kg/week (~2 kg/month)**; **sudden excess gain (> 1 kg/week) – suspect pre-eclampsia**; inadequate gain → IUGR/ LBW.
- **Supplements (MoHFW)** – **folic acid 400 µg (0.4 mg) from preconception till 12 weeks**; **IFA (60 mg iron + 500 µg FA) daily for 180 days from 14–16 weeks + 180 days postpartum**; **calcium 500 mg BD + vitamin D 250 IU from 14 weeks till 6 months postpartum**; **albendazole 400 mg single dose in 2nd trimester**; **Td**; **iodised salt**.
- **Dietary advice** – **"eat for one, not two" – one extra small meal**; small frequent meals (nausea, heartburn), **↑ fibre & fluids (constipation)**, GLV, milk (2–3 glasses), pulses, eggs/ meat, seasonal fruits; **avoid alcohol, tobacco, excess caffeine (< 200 mg/day), raw/ undercooked meat & eggs (Toxoplasma, Listeria), unpasteurised milk, papaya/ pineapple myths – counsel scientifically**; **food fads & taboos** – counsel (e.g. avoiding nutritious foods).
- **Problems** – **nausea/ vomiting** (dry crackers in morning, small meals, ginger, avoid fatty/ spicy), **heartburn** (small meals, don't lie down after eating, head-end up), **constipation** (fibre, fluids, exercise), **pica**, **anaemia** (IFA, iron-rich foods + vitamin C), **gestational diabetes** (MNT, low GI, avoid sugar; insulin/ metformin if needed), **PIH** (normal salt advised; adequate protein & calcium; excess salt avoided).
- **PM Matru Vandana Yojana (PMMVY, 2017)** – **₹5000** in instalments for the first child; **₹6000 for a second child if girl (PMMVY 2.0)** – compensate wage loss & improve nutrition; **Take-home ration (THR) – ICDS** for pregnant & lactating women.

## 5.2 Lactation

- **Extra – energy +600 kcal (0–6 months), +520 kcal (6–12 months)**; **protein +17 g / +13 g**; **calcium 1000 mg**; **iodine 250 µg**; vitamin A, C (+40 mg), B12, DHA; **fluids ~3 L/day (drink a glass of water/ milk at each feed)**; galactagogues (traditional – methi, garlic, ajwain, gond laddoo – mostly cultural; **frequent suckling is the best galactagogue**).
- **Avoid** – alcohol, smoking, unnecessary drugs, excess caffeine.
- **Average milk output** – **~500–800 ml/day (~750 ml)** in first 6 months; ~600 ml in 6–12 months.

## 5.3 Breastfeeding

### Composition
| Milk | Features |
|---|---|
| **Colostrum (first 2–4 days)** | **Thick, yellow (β-carotene), small quantity (~10–40 ml/day; ~2–20 ml per feed)**; **rich in protein (~3× mature milk), IgA (secretory), WBCs, lactoferrin, vitamin A, zinc**; **less fat & lactose**; **laxative – helps passage of meconium (↓ jaundice)**; **"first immunisation"** – must not be discarded (pre-lacteal feeds harmful) |
| **Transitional milk** | Day ~5–14 |
| **Mature milk** | **~70 kcal/100 ml (0.67 kcal/ml)**; protein ~1.1 g/100 ml (**whey : casein ~ 60 : 40/ 70 : 30 – easily digestible**), **fat ~3.5–4 g** (DHA, ARA, **lipase**), **lactose ~7 g (higher than cow's milk)**, Ca 30 mg (well absorbed), **iron low but ~50 % absorbed (lactoferrin)**; **low in vitamin D, vitamin K** |
| **Foremilk** | Beginning of feed – **watery, more lactose & protein – quenches thirst** |
| **Hindmilk** | End of feed – **rich in fat → satiety & weight gain** → **empty one breast fully before switching** |

| Per 100 ml | Breast milk | Cow's milk | Buffalo milk |
|---|---|---|---|
| Energy (kcal) | ~65–70 | ~67 | ~117 |
| Protein (g) | **~1.1** | **~3.2** | ~4.3 |
| Fat (g) | ~3.4 | ~4.1 | **~6.5 (highest)** |
| Lactose (g) | **~7.4 (highest)** | ~4.4 | ~5.0 |
| Calcium (mg) | ~28 | ~120 | ~210 |
| Iron (mg) | ~0.2 (well absorbed) | ~0.2 | ~0.2 |

### Advantages of breastfeeding
- **For baby** – **ideal nutrition, easily digested**, **immunological protection (IgA, lactoferrin, lysozyme, bifidus factor – Lactobacillus bifidus, macrophages)** → ↓ **diarrhoea, pneumonia, otitis media, NEC, sepsis**; ↓ allergies, ↓ SIDS; **better IQ & cognitive development (DHA)**; ↓ later obesity, T2DM, hypertension; **sterile, at right temperature, always available, free**; **bonding**; **jaw & teeth development**.
- **For mother** – **↓ PPH (oxytocin → uterine involution)**, **lactational amenorrhoea (LAM – natural contraception: exclusive BF, < 6 months postpartum, amenorrhoeic – ~98 % effective)**, **↓ breast & ovarian cancer**, ↓ T2DM, weight loss, bonding, economical, convenient.
- **Hormones** – **prolactin (milk production/ secretion – "milk-making")** & **oxytocin (milk ejection/ let-down reflex – "milk-ejecting"; inhibited by stress, pain, anxiety, doubt)**; **suckling** stimulates both; **FIL (feedback inhibitor of lactation)** – unemptied breast ↓ production.

### Recommendations (WHO/ IYCF India)
- **Early initiation – within 1 hour of birth (incl. caesarean – as soon as mother responsive)**; **skin-to-skin contact**; **no pre-lacteal feeds (honey, ghutti, sugar water, animal milk)**.
- **Exclusive breastfeeding (EBF) for first 6 months** – **no water**, no other food/ liquid **(except ORS, drops/ syrups of vitamins, minerals, medicines)**.
- **Continue breastfeeding till 2 years or beyond** with complementary foods.
- **Demand feeding (8–12 times/24 hrs incl. night)**; **night feeds** maintain prolactin.
- **Signs of good attachment** – **mouth wide open, lower lip turned outward (everted), chin touching breast, more areola visible above than below**; **good positioning** – baby's **head & body in straight line, body close to mother, facing breast (nose opposite nipple), whole body supported** (newborn).
- **Signs of effective suckling** – slow deep sucks with pauses, audible swallowing, cheeks round (not drawn in), baby releases breast satisfied.
- **Adequate milk intake** – **urine ≥ 6 times/24 hrs (wet nappies)** after day 4–5, **weight gain ~500 g/month or more (~20–30 g/day)**, regains birth weight by **10–14 days**, sleeps 2–3 hrs between feeds.
- **Burping** after feeds (upright on shoulder); position on side/ back after feed (sleep supine – SIDS).
- **Breastfeeding positions** – **cradle, cross-cradle (preterm/ small), football/ underarm (twins, after LSCS, large breasts), side-lying (night, after LSCS)**.
- **Expressed breast milk (EBM)** – hand expression (**C-hold, press back towards chest wall, compress & release**) or pump; **storage – room temperature (≤ 25 °C) 6–8 hrs, refrigerator (4 °C) 24 hrs (up to 3–5 days per some guidelines), freezer (−18 °C) 3 months**; **thaw in warm water, don't boil/ microwave**; **feed with cup/ paladai/ spoon (not bottle)**; working mothers.
- **Contraindications (rare)** – **galactosaemia (classic) in baby**, maternal **HIV (in settings where replacement feeding is AFASS; in India – EBF with ART)**, **active untreated TB (separate till non-infectious ~2 weeks of therapy; give EBM; baby INH prophylaxis)**, **anticancer drugs, radioactive isotopes**, illicit drugs, HSV lesion on breast; **NOT contraindicated – maternal hepatitis B (vaccinate baby + HBIG), fever, common cold, mastitis (continue feeding), jaundice in baby (continue)**.

### Breast problems
| Problem | Management |
|---|---|
| **Sore/ cracked nipples** | **Correct positioning & attachment (main cause – poor attachment)**; apply **hindmilk** after feeds, air dry, start on less sore side; don't use soap |
| **Breast engorgement (day 3–5)** | **Frequent feeding (don't stop), express milk**, **warm compress before & cold after feeds**, gentle massage, supportive bra, analgesics |
| **Mastitis** | **Continue breastfeeding (from both sides)**, empty breast, rest, fluids, antibiotics (cloxacillin), analgesics |
| **Breast abscess** | Incision & drainage/ aspiration; feed from other breast, express affected side; resume when healed |
| **Flat/ inverted nipple** | **Syringe technique (cut 10/ 20 ml syringe)** after delivery, attachment to areola; antenatal nipple exercises not recommended |
| **Insufficient milk (perceived)** | Frequent suckling, night feeds, EBF, relaxation, support (**relactation**) |

- **Baby-Friendly Hospital Initiative (BFHI, 1991)** – **"Ten steps to successful breastfeeding"**; **MAA (Mothers' Absolute Affection) programme (2016)**; **World Breastfeeding Week – 1–7 August**; **IMS Act (Infant Milk Substitutes, Feeding Bottles & Infant Foods Act, 1992; amended 2003)** – bans promotion of formula & feeding bottles.

## 5.4 Complementary Feeding (6–24 months)

- **Start at completed 6 months (180 days)** – breast milk alone insufficient for energy, iron, zinc, vitamin A after 6 months; **continue breastfeeding**.
- **Principles (IYCF – "FATVAH")** – **F**requency, **A**mount, **T**hickness (consistency), **V**ariety (≥ 4–5 food groups – minimum dietary diversity), **A**ctive/ responsive feeding, **H**ygiene.

| Age | Texture | Frequency (+ breastfeeding) | Amount per meal |
|---|---|---|---|
| **6–8 months** | **Thick mashed/ semi-solid (dal-rice, khichdi, mashed banana/ potato, suji kheer)** | **2–3 meals/day** (+ 1–2 snacks) | **Start 2–3 tablespoons → ½ katori (250 ml bowl)** |
| **9–11 months** | **Finely chopped/ mashed, finger foods** | **3–4 meals** + 1–2 snacks | **½ katori** |
| **12–24 months** | **Family foods (chopped/ mashed)** | **3–4 meals** + 1–2 snacks | **¾ to 1 katori** |
| **Non-breastfed** | Add 1–2 extra meals + **1–2 cups milk** | – | – |

- **Energy needs from complementary food** – **~200 kcal (6–8 m), ~300 kcal (9–11 m), ~550 kcal (12–23 m)**.
- **Energy-dense** – **add 1 teaspoon of oil/ ghee to each meal**, sugar/ jaggery; **thick (not watery – dal water/ rice water is low in energy)**; **"spoon test" – food should stay on spoon**.
- **Include** – **animal foods (egg, fish, meat), pulses, GLV & yellow fruits/ veg (vitamin A), iron-rich foods**; **introduce one new food at a time (3–5 days)**; **avoid salt & sugar excess, honey < 1 yr (botulism), tea/ coffee, packaged junk foods, whole nuts (choking)**; **cow's milk as main drink not before 1 yr**.
- **Malted/ germinated flours (amylase-rich food – ARF)** – ↓ viscosity → **more energy-dense liquid gruel**; **roasting** (ready-to-use mixes); **fermentation**.
- **Responsive feeding** – feed slowly & patiently, encourage, **feed directly to infants, help older children**; minimise distractions (no TV/ mobile); **separate plate/ bowl** to monitor amount.
- **Hygiene** – wash hands (mother & child), **clean utensils, cup & spoon (not bottle)**, freshly prepared food (**serve within 1–2 hrs**), safe water.
- **Feeding during illness** – **continue breastfeeding more often**, offer soft favourite foods; **after illness – extra meal/day for ≥ 2 weeks (catch-up growth)**.
- **Weaning foods examples (India)** – **khichdi, dalia, suji upma, ragi porridge, mashed banana, sattu, idli, multi-mix (cereal + pulse + oil + jaggery + vegetables) – e.g. "Bal Amrutham"**, THR (ICDS).
- **Bottle feeding hazards** – **diarrhoea (contamination), malnutrition (over-dilution), otitis media, dental caries ("nursing-bottle caries"), nipple confusion, ↓ bonding, expense**.

## 5.5 Low Birth Weight & Preterm Feeding

- **LBW < 2500 g**; **VLBW < 1500 g**; **ELBW < 1000 g**.
- **Feeding method by gestation/ ability** – **≥ 34 weeks/ good suck – direct breastfeeding**; **32–34 weeks – paladai/ spoon/ cup with EBM**; **28–31 weeks – orogastric/ nasogastric tube (gavage) EBM**; **< 28 weeks/ < 1200 g/ sick – IV fluids then trophic feeds** → advance.
- **EBM (mother's own milk) – best**; **donor human milk (human milk banks – Lactation Management Units/ "Amrit")**; **human milk fortifier** for VLBW; **preterm formula** if no breast milk.
- **Kangaroo Mother Care (KMC)** – skin-to-skin, exclusive breastfeeding, early discharge & follow-up.
- **Supplements for preterm/ LBW** – **vitamin D 400 IU/day**, **iron 2 mg/kg/day from 2–6 weeks (till 1 yr)**, calcium & phosphorus (VLBW), multivitamin.
- **Daily fluid/ feed advancement** – day 1 ~60–80 ml/kg → increase ~20 ml/kg/day up to **150–180 ml/kg/day**.

## 5.6 Preschool, School-Age & Adolescents

- **Preschool (1–5 yrs)** – **"vulnerable/ most nutritionally at-risk group" (PEM, vitamin A deficiency, anaemia)**; slow growth vs infancy; **food fads, picky eating, poor appetite**; small frequent meals (5–6), family foods, milk, egg, fruits; growth monitoring (MCP card – WHO charts).
- **School-age (6–12 yrs)** – energy for growth & activity; **don't skip breakfast**; healthy tiffin; limit junk/ HFSS foods & sugary drinks; **PM POSHAN (mid-day meal)**; school health – IFA, deworming, vision, dental.
- **Adolescents (10–19 yrs)** – **growth spurt (2nd fastest growth after infancy; ~20 % adult height, ~50 % adult weight gained)** → **↑ energy, protein, calcium (bone mass – peak bone mass by ~25–30 yrs), iron (menstruation, ↑ muscle)**; **anaemia** common (girls), eating disorders, junk food, obesity, **early marriage/ pregnancy risks**; **WIFS, RKSK (Rashtriya Kishor Swasthya Karyakram, 2014)**, **Scheme for Adolescent Girls (SAG – ICDS)**.

## 5.7 Adults & Elderly

- **Adults** – maintain healthy weight (BMI 18.5–22.9 for Asians), balanced diet, ↓ salt/ sugar/ fat, ↑ fibre, physical activity ≥ 150 min/week (moderate).
- **Elderly (≥ 60 yrs)** – **↓ BMR & activity → ↓ energy needs (~10–20 % less)**; **same/ ↑ protein (1–1.2 g/kg – prevent sarcopenia)**, **↑ calcium (1200 mg) & vitamin D (800 IU)**, B12 (↓ absorption – atrophic gastritis), fibre & fluids (constipation – ↓ thirst sensation → dehydration), zinc, antioxidants.
- **Problems** – **poor dentition/ dentures (soft foods)**, ↓ taste & smell, dysphagia, ↓ appetite, **constipation**, loneliness/ depression, poverty, chronic diseases, **polypharmacy (drug–nutrient interactions)**, malabsorption, **osteoporosis**, sarcopenia, dehydration.
- **Advice** – **small frequent soft meals**, nutrient-dense, adequate fluids (6–8 glasses even without thirst), fibre, social eating, **MNA (Mini Nutritional Assessment)** screening.
