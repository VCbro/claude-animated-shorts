## 2.1 Carbohydrates

- **Composition** – C, H, O (CH₂O)n; **main & cheapest source of energy (~55–65 % of energy in Indian diet)**; **4 kcal/g**.
- **Classification**:
  - **Monosaccharides** – **glucose (dextrose – blood sugar)**, **fructose (sweetest sugar – fruits, honey)**, **galactose (milk)**.
  - **Disaccharides** – **sucrose (table sugar = glucose + fructose)**, **lactose (milk sugar = glucose + galactose – least sweet)**, **maltose (malt = glucose + glucose)**.
  - **Oligosaccharides** – raffinose, stachyose (pulses – flatulence).
  - **Polysaccharides** – **starch (cereals, tubers – amylose + amylopectin)**, **glycogen (animal starch – liver & muscle)**, **cellulose, hemicellulose, pectin (dietary fibre)**, dextrins.
- **Functions** – **energy (brain & RBCs depend on glucose – ~130 g/day minimum)**, **protein-sparing action**, **prevents ketosis (anti-ketogenic – fat metabolism needs CHO)**, glycogen storage, flavour, fibre for bowel, synthesis of non-essential amino acids, lactose ↑ calcium absorption.
- **Digestion** – salivary amylase → pancreatic amylase → brush-border disaccharidases (**maltase, sucrase, lactase**) → monosaccharides absorbed in **small intestine (jejunum)**.
- **Deficiency** – ketosis, protein breakdown, weight loss, fatigue; **excess** – obesity, dental caries (sugar), hypertriglyceridaemia, type 2 DM.
- **Lactose intolerance** – lactase deficiency → diarrhoea, bloating after milk → **curd/ fermented milk** tolerated better.
- **Glycaemic index (GI)** – rise in blood glucose after a food vs **glucose (GI = 100)**; **high GI ≥ 70** (glucose, white bread, potato, cornflakes, white rice), **medium 56–69**, **low ≤ 55** (whole grains, millets, pulses, most fruits, milk); low-GI foods preferred in **diabetes**; **glycaemic load** = GI × available CHO/ 100.
- **Sweeteners** – nutritive (sugar, honey, jaggery – iron), **non-nutritive/ artificial (saccharin, aspartame – contraindicated in phenylketonuria, sucralose, stevia – natural)**.

## 2.2 Dietary Fibre

- **Non-starch polysaccharides + lignin – not digested by human enzymes**; fermented partly by colonic bacteria.
- **Soluble fibre** – **pectin, gums, mucilage, β-glucan** (oats, barley, **fenugreek/ methi seeds**, isabgol, fruits – apple, legumes) → **↓ blood cholesterol & glucose** (slows absorption), forms gel.
- **Insoluble fibre** – **cellulose, hemicellulose, lignin** (wheat bran, whole grains, vegetables) → **↑ stool bulk, prevents constipation**, ↓ transit time.
- **Benefits** – **prevents constipation, haemorrhoids, diverticulosis, colon cancer**, ↓ cholesterol (CHD), glycaemic control (DM), satiety (obesity), gallstones; **requirement ~25–40 g/day (ICMR ~40 g/2000 kcal)**; with adequate fluids.
- **Excess** – flatulence, ↓ mineral absorption (phytates).
- **Low-residue/ low-fibre diet** – **acute diarrhoea, before colonoscopy/ bowel surgery, IBD flare, intestinal obstruction**.

## 2.3 Proteins

- **Composition** – C, H, O, **N (~16 % – nitrogen × 6.25 = protein)**, S; polymers of **amino acids** (peptide bonds); **4 kcal/g**; **"proteios" – of prime importance (Mulder)**.
- **Functions** – **body building (growth, tissue repair – most important)**, **enzymes, hormones (insulin), antibodies (immunity)**, **haemoglobin, plasma proteins (albumin – oncotic pressure)**, transport (transferrin, lipoproteins), **acid–base buffer**, muscle contraction (actin, myosin), structural (collagen, keratin), energy (when CHO/ fat insufficient), **neurotransmitters**, blood clotting (fibrinogen).
- **Essential amino acids (9) – can't be synthesised – must come from diet**: **histidine, isoleucine, leucine, lysine, methionine, phenylalanine, threonine, tryptophan, valine** – "**PVT TIM HALL**" (P-henylalanine, V-aline, T-hreonine, T-ryptophan, I-soleucine, M-ethionine, H-istidine, A-rginine*, L-eucine, L-ysine) – **arginine & histidine semi-essential (needed in childhood)**.
- **Complete (first-class) proteins** – contain all EAAs in adequate amounts – **animal proteins (egg, milk, meat, fish)** + **soybean**; **incomplete (second-class)** – plant proteins (lack ≥ 1 EAA – **limiting amino acid**).
- **Reference protein – egg (whole egg protein; BV ~94–100, NPU ~96)**; **breast milk** – reference for infants.
- **Protein quality indices** – **biological value (BV – % of absorbed N retained)**, **digestibility coefficient**, **NPU (net protein utilisation = BV × digestibility – best overall)**, **PER (protein efficiency ratio – weight gain per g protein – rats)**, **amino acid (chemical) score**, **PDCAAS/ DIAAS**.
- **Nitrogen balance** – **positive** (growth, pregnancy, convalescence, body building), **negative** (starvation, fever, burns, trauma, surgery, cancer, immobilisation), **equilibrium** (healthy adult).
- **Sources** – **animal** (egg ~13 %, meat ~20 %, fish ~20 %, milk ~3.2 %, paneer ~18 %, cheese ~24 %), **plant** (**soybean ~40–43 % – highest**, pulses ~20–24 %, groundnut ~25 %, cereals ~7–12 %).
- **Deficiency** – **PEM (kwashiorkor, marasmus)**, poor growth, oedema (↓ albumin), poor wound healing, immunodeficiency, anaemia, fatty liver; **excess** – renal load (avoid in CKD), ↑ calcium loss, gout risk (purine-rich).
- **Protein restriction** – **hepatic encephalopathy (acute), CKD (pre-dialysis 0.6–0.8 g/kg)**; **↑ protein** – **burns (1.5–2 g/kg or more), surgery, pregnancy, lactation, nephrotic syndrome (moderate), TB, fever, cancer, dialysis (1.2 g/kg)**.

## 2.4 Fats (Lipids)

- **Most concentrated source of energy – 9 kcal/g**; esters of **fatty acids + glycerol (triglycerides ~95 % of dietary fat)**.
- **Functions** – energy & **storage (adipose)**, **carrier of fat-soluble vitamins (A, D, E, K)**, **essential fatty acids**, **insulation & protection of organs**, **satiety (slow gastric emptying)**, palatability, **cell membranes (phospholipids)**, synthesis of **steroid hormones, bile acids, vitamin D** (cholesterol), myelin sheath, prostaglandins.

| Fatty acids | Features | Sources |
|---|---|---|
| **Saturated (SFA)** – no double bond | **Solid at room temp**; **↑ LDL cholesterol (atherogenic)** | **Ghee, butter, vanaspati, coconut oil (~90 % SFA – highest among oils), palm oil, meat fat, cheese** |
| **Monounsaturated (MUFA)** – 1 double bond (**oleic acid – ω-9**) | ↓ LDL, maintain HDL | **Olive oil, groundnut oil, mustard oil, rice bran oil, sesame, almonds** |
| **Polyunsaturated (PUFA)** – ≥ 2 double bonds | Liquid; **↓ cholesterol** | **ω-6 – linoleic acid (LA)**: sunflower, safflower, corn, soybean oils; **ω-3 – α-linolenic acid (ALA)**: **flaxseed (alsi), mustard, soybean, walnuts, fenugreek**; **EPA & DHA – fatty fish (sea fish), fish oil** |
| **Trans fatty acids** | **Partially hydrogenated oils (vanaspati, bakery, fried & reheated oils, fast foods)**; **↑ LDL & ↓ HDL – most harmful** | **FSSAI limit – ≤ 2 % of total fat in oils & foods (since 2022)**; WHO "REPLACE" – eliminate |

- **Essential fatty acids (EFA)** – **linoleic acid (ω-6) & α-linolenic acid (ω-3)** – can't be synthesised; **arachidonic acid** semi-essential; **deficiency → phrynoderma (toad skin – follicular hyperkeratosis), dermatitis, poor growth, poor wound healing, hair loss**; **DHA – brain & retina development (breast milk, pregnancy)**; ω-3 – **anti-inflammatory, antithrombotic, ↓ triglycerides, ↓ CHD**.
- **Ideal ω-6 : ω-3 ratio ≈ 5–10 : 1**; **use combination/ rotation of 2 or more vegetable oils** (e.g. groundnut + mustard; rice bran + soybean).
- **Cholesterol** – **only in animal foods (egg yolk – richest common source, organ meats/ brain, prawns, butter, ghee)**; **plant foods – zero cholesterol**; body synthesises (~1 g/day – liver); **desirable serum total cholesterol < 200 mg/dl**, **LDL < 100 ("bad")**, **HDL > 40 (men)/ > 50 (women) ("good" – higher is better)**, **triglycerides < 150 mg/dl**; **dietary cholesterol < 300 mg/day** (older advice).
- **Lipoproteins** – **chylomicrons (dietary TG from gut)**, **VLDL (endogenous TG from liver)**, **LDL (cholesterol to tissues – atherogenic)**, **HDL (reverse cholesterol transport – liver; protective)**.
- **Excess fat** – **obesity, atherosclerosis, CHD, hypertension, type 2 DM, gallstones, certain cancers (breast, colon)**, fatty liver; **deficiency** – EFA deficiency, fat-soluble vitamin deficiency, low energy.
- **Rancidity** – oxidation of fats (off-flavour, toxic); prevented by antioxidants (vitamin E, BHA/ BHT), cool dark storage; **reheated oils – avoid (polar compounds, trans fats); FSSAI – Total Polar Compounds ≤ 25 %**.
- **Fat restriction** – **gall bladder disease, pancreatitis, obesity, CHD, hyperlipidaemia, malabsorption/ steatorrhoea** (use **MCT – medium-chain triglycerides – absorbed directly into portal blood, don't need bile/ lipase**).

## 2.5 Water & Electrolytes

- **~60 % of adult body weight (~75–80 % in newborn)**; **ICF ⅔, ECF ⅓**; loss of **10 % water – serious, 20 % – fatal**.
- **Functions** – solvent & transport, **temperature regulation (sweating)**, lubrication (joints, eyes), digestion, excretion, cell shape, chemical reactions.
- **Requirement** – **~1 ml/kcal (adults ≈ 2–3 L/day), 1.5 ml/kcal (infants)**; ↑ in **fever (↑ 10–12 % per °C), hot climate, exercise, diarrhoea/ vomiting, lactation, burns, polyuria**; restrict in **renal failure (oliguric – previous day's output + 500 ml), heart failure, SIADH, hyponatraemia, cirrhosis with ascites**.
- **Sources** – **drinking water & beverages (~60 %)**, **food water (~30 %)**, **metabolic water (~10 %, ~300 ml/day – fat oxidation yields the most)**.
- **Dehydration signs** – thirst, dry mucosa, ↓ skin turgor, sunken eyes/ fontanelle, oliguria, dark urine, tachycardia, hypotension, weight loss; **ORS (WHO low-osmolarity – 245 mOsm/L: Na 75, glucose 75 mmol/L)** + **zinc (10 mg < 6 months, 20 mg > 6 months × 14 days)** in diarrhoea.
- **Sodium** – **main ECF cation**; sources – **common salt (NaCl ~40 % sodium; 5 g salt ≈ 2 g sodium)**, pickles, papad, processed foods, baking soda; **excess → hypertension, oedema**; restrict in **HTN, CHF, renal disease, cirrhosis (ascites), PIH/ oedema, steroid therapy**.
- **Potassium** – **main ICF cation**; sources – **banana, coconut water, citrus/ orange juice, potato, tomato, dry fruits (dates, apricots), legumes, GLV**, tender coconut; **restrict in renal failure/ hyperkalaemia (leaching vegetables – soaking in warm water reduces K⁺)**; supplement with diuretics (loop/ thiazide).
