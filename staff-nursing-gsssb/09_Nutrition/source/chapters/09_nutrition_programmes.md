## 9.1 Integrated Child Development Services (ICDS)

- **Launched 2 October 1975** (33 blocks; Ministry of Women & Child Development – MWCD); **world's largest community-based programme for early childhood care**; now under **"Saksham Anganwadi & Poshan 2.0" (2021–26)**.
- **Beneficiaries** – **children 0–6 yrs, pregnant & lactating women, adolescent girls (11–14 yrs out-of-school – Scheme for Adolescent Girls; aspirational districts/ NE)**.
- **Six services** – **1. Supplementary nutrition**, **2. Immunisation**, **3. Health check-ups**, **4. Referral services**, **5. Pre-school (non-formal) education (3–6 yrs)**, **6. Nutrition & health education (women 15–45 yrs)** – immunisation, health check-ups & referral delivered via **health system (ANM/ PHC)**.
- **Anganwadi Centre (AWC)** – **1 per ~400–800 population (rural/ urban), 1 per 150–400 (mini-AWC/ tribal)**; **Anganwadi Worker (AWW) + helper**; **supervisor (~25 AWCs)**, **CDPO** (block); **Poshan Tracker app** (real-time growth monitoring).

### Supplementary nutrition norms (Poshan 2.0 – per beneficiary per day, 300 days/yr)
| Beneficiary | Energy (kcal) | Protein (g) |
|---|---|---|
| **Children 6 months–3 yrs (take-home ration – THR)** | **500** | **12–15** |
| **Children 3–6 yrs (hot cooked meal + morning snack)** | **500** | **12–15** |
| **Severely underweight/ malnourished children (6 m–6 yrs)** | **800** | **20–25** |
| **Pregnant & lactating women (THR)** | **600** | **18–20** |
| **Adolescent girls (SAG)** | 600 | 18–20 |

- **THR** – fortified micronutrient-rich premix (e.g. "**Bal Bhog**", "**Matrushakti**", "**Purna Shakti**" in Gujarat), **millets promoted**.
- **Growth monitoring** – **WHO growth standards** – monthly weighing (< 3 yrs), MCP card; **Village Health, Sanitation & Nutrition Day (VHSND) – monthly at AWC**.

## 9.2 POSHAN Abhiyaan (National Nutrition Mission)

- **Launched 8 March 2018 (Jhunjhunu, Rajasthan)** – **PM's Overarching Scheme for Holistic Nutrition**; now **Mission Poshan 2.0 (2021)**.
- **Targets (original, by 2022)** – reduce **stunting by 2 % per year**, **undernutrition (underweight) by 2 % p.a.**, **anaemia (children, women, adolescent girls) by 3 % p.a.**, **LBW by 2 % p.a.**; **"Mission 25 by 2022"** – reduce stunting from 38.4 % to 25 %.
- **Pillars** – ICT-enabled real-time monitoring (**Poshan Tracker**), convergence of schemes, **behaviour change – Jan Andolan**, incentives, **Community-Based Events (CBEs – "Godh Bharai", "Annaprashan")**.
- **Poshan Maah – September (every year) (Rashtriya Poshan Maah)**; **Poshan Pakhwada – March**; **National Nutrition Week – 1–7 September**.
- **First 1000 days (conception to 2 yrs)** – critical window of opportunity.

## 9.3 PM POSHAN (Mid-Day Meal Scheme)

- **Started 15 August 1995** as **National Programme of Nutritional Support to Primary Education (NP-NSPE)**; **cooked mid-day meal from 2004 (Supreme Court order 2001)**; **renamed PM POSHAN (Pradhan Mantri Poshan Shakti Nirman) – 2021**; Ministry of Education; **Tamil Nadu – pioneer (1956/ 1982 – K. Kamaraj/ MGR)**.
- **Coverage** – **Bal Vatika (pre-primary) & classes 1–8** in government & aided schools.
- **Norms per child per day** – **primary (classes 1–5): 450 kcal & 12 g protein**; **upper primary (6–8): 700 kcal & 20 g protein**; food grains – 100 g (primary)/ 150 g (upper primary); **micronutrients (iron, folic acid, vitamin A), deworming**; **fortified rice**; **tithi bhojan** (community contribution), **school nutrition gardens**.
- **Objectives** – improve nutritional status, **↑ enrolment, attendance & retention**, socialisation (caste barriers), relief in drought areas.

## 9.4 Micronutrient Programmes

| Programme | Key points |
|---|---|
| **National Vitamin A Prophylaxis Programme (1970)** | **9 doses** – **1 lakh IU at 9 months** (with MR-1), **2 lakh IU at 16–18 months**, then **2 lakh IU every 6 months up to 5 yrs**; biannual rounds (**"Bal Swasthya Poshan Maah"** in some states); treatment of xerophthalmia/ measles/ SAM |
| **Anaemia Mukt Bharat (2018)** (earlier National Nutritional Anaemia Prophylaxis Programme – 1970, NIPI 2013) | **6×6×6 strategy**; IFA – **biweekly syrup (6–59 m)**, **pink weekly (5–9 yrs)**, **blue weekly (10–19 yrs – WIFS)**, **red weekly (WRA)**, **red daily × 180 days (pregnancy & lactation)**; **deworming (NDD – 10 Feb & 10 Aug)**; test & treat; fortified foods; target ↓ anaemia 3 % points/yr |
| **NIDDCP (1992; NGCP 1962)** | **Universal salt iodisation – ≥ 30 ppm production, ≥ 15 ppm consumer**; **ban on non-iodised salt (2006)**; IDD surveys, urinary iodine lab, health education; **Global IDD Prevention Day – 21 October** |
| **Zinc + ORS** | Diarrhoea management (IDCF – Intensified Diarrhoea Control Fortnight / **STOP Diarrhoea Campaign**, June–July) |
| **Calcium & vitamin D supplementation** | Pregnant & lactating women (MoHFW) |
| **Folic acid** | Preconception & pregnancy |
| **Food fortification** | **+F (salt, oil, milk, atta, rice) – fortified rice in PDS/ ICDS/ PM POSHAN** |
| **National Fluorosis Programme (NPPCF)** | Safe water, defluoridation, nutrition (calcium, vitamin C) |

## 9.5 Malnutrition Management & Maternal-Child Schemes

- **Nutrition Rehabilitation Centres (NRCs – NHM, 2008)** – facility-based care of **complicated SAM** (14 days stay, mother's wage compensation, counselling, follow-up); **Community-based Management of Acute Malnutrition (CMAM)**.
- **MAA (Mothers' Absolute Affection, 2016)** – promotion of breastfeeding (early initiation, EBF, IYCF counselling by ASHA/ ANM).
- **Home-Based Newborn Care (HBNC) & Home-Based Care for Young Child (HBYC – 3, 6, 9, 12, 15 months visits – ASHA)** – feeding counselling, growth monitoring.
- **PMMVY (2017)** – **₹5000** conditional cash for first child (₹6000 for second child if girl – PMMVY 2.0); **Janani Suraksha Yojana (2005)** – institutional delivery cash.
- **RBSK (Rashtriya Bal Swasthya Karyakram, 2013)** – screening for **4 Ds: Defects at birth, Diseases, Deficiencies, Development delays** (0–18 yrs).
- **RKSK (2014)** – adolescent health including nutrition (WIFS, counselling).
- **Mission Indradhanush** – immunisation (measles → malnutrition link).
- **LaQshya, SUMAN** – maternal care.
- **Gujarat initiatives** – **"Kuposhan Mukt Gujarat Mahabhiyan", "Mission Balam Sukham" (SAM/ MAM management – Child Malnutrition Treatment Centres – CMTC & Child Development Nutrition Centres)**, **"Purna Yojana" (adolescent girls)**, **"Doodh Sanjivani Yojana"** (fortified milk in schools/ AWCs – tribal), **"Poshan Sudha"** (hot meals to pregnant/ lactating women – tribal), **"Matrushakti, Bal Shakti, Purna Shakti"** (THR), **"Mukhyamantri Matrushakti Yojana"** (2022 – 1000 days nutrition kit).

## 9.6 Food Security Programmes

- **National Food Security Act (NFSA), 2013** – legal entitlement – **up to 75 % rural & 50 % urban population**; **5 kg food grains per person per month at ₹3/2/1 (rice/ wheat/ coarse grains)** – **Antyodaya Anna Yojana (AAY – poorest of poor) – 35 kg per household per month**; **maternity benefit ≥ ₹6000**; ICDS & MDM entitlements; **eldest woman (≥ 18 yrs) – head of household for ration card**.
- **PMGKAY (Pradhan Mantri Garib Kalyan Anna Yojana, 2020)** – **free food grains** (merged with NFSA – free from 1 January 2023, extended 5 yrs till 2028).
- **Targeted Public Distribution System (TPDS, 1997)**; **One Nation One Ration Card (2019)**.
- **Annapurna scheme (2000)** – 10 kg free food grains for destitute elderly not getting pension.
- **National Food Security Mission (agriculture – production)**.
- **Food for Work, Antyodaya**.

## 9.7 Global Goals & Organisations

- **SDG 2 – "Zero Hunger" (end hunger, achieve food security, improved nutrition, sustainable agriculture by 2030)**; SDG 3 (health).
- **WHO Global Nutrition Targets 2025 (World Health Assembly 2012)** – **40 % ↓ in stunting (under-5)**, **50 % ↓ anaemia in women of reproductive age**, **30 % ↓ LBW**, **no ↑ in childhood overweight**, **↑ EBF to ≥ 50 %**, **wasting < 5 %** (extended to 2030).
- **UN Decade of Action on Nutrition (2016–2025)**; **SUN (Scaling Up Nutrition) movement**.
- **Global Hunger Index** (India ranks low; 2023 – 111/125, 2024 – 105/127); **State of Food Security & Nutrition in the World (SOFI – FAO)**.
- **Organisations** – **FAO (Food & Agriculture Organisation, 1945 – Rome; World Food Day 16 Oct)**, **WFP (World Food Programme – Nobel Peace Prize 2020)**, **UNICEF (1946)**, **WHO**, **CARE (Cooperative for Assistance & Relief Everywhere – supplementary feeding)**, **ICMR-NIN (Hyderabad)**, **CFTRI (Central Food Technological Research Institute – Mysuru)**, **National Nutrition Monitoring Bureau (NNMB, 1972 – NIN)**, **Food & Nutrition Board (MWCD)**, **Nutrition Foundation of India (C. Gopalan, Delhi)**, **IIPS Mumbai – NFHS**; **Comprehensive National Nutrition Survey (CNNS 2016–18)** – first national survey of micronutrients in children & adolescents.
- **NFHS-5 (2019–21) key data** – stunting **35.5 %**, wasting **19.3 %**, severe wasting **7.7 %**, underweight **32.1 %**, overweight (U5) **3.4 %**; anaemia – children (6–59 m) **67.1 %**, women **57 %**, pregnant women **52.2 %**, men **25 %**; **EBF (< 6 months) 63.7 %**; early initiation of breastfeeding **41.8 %**; minimum acceptable diet (6–23 m) **11.3 %**; women BMI < 18.5 – **18.7 %**; women overweight/ obese – **24 %**.

## 9.8 Nutrition Education & Nurse's Role

- **Nutrition education** – process of helping people **adopt healthy food habits** using available resources – **change knowledge, attitude & practice (KAP)**.
- **Target groups (priority/ vulnerable)** – **pregnant & lactating women, infants & preschool children (most vulnerable), adolescent girls, elderly, sick & convalescent, low-income families**.
- **Methods** – **individual** (counselling at clinic/ home visits – most effective for behaviour change), **group** (**demonstration – most effective method for teaching cooking/ recipes**, group discussion, role play, **mothers' meetings at AWC, VHSND**), **mass** (radio, TV, posters, exhibitions, social media, Poshan Maah campaigns, **street plays/ nukkad natak**, puppet shows).
- **Audio-visual aids** – **flash cards, flannel graph, charts, posters, models (food models), real foods, videos**.
- **Principles** – start from known to unknown, simple language, local & low-cost foods (**"seasonal, local, affordable"**), cultural acceptability, involve family decision-makers (mother-in-law/ husband), repeat & reinforce, practical demonstration, **dispel food fads & taboos**, evaluation.
- **Key messages** – EBF 6 months, timely complementary feeding, IFA compliance, iodised salt, hand washing, diverse diet (GLV, pulses, milk, eggs), kitchen gardens, safe water, growth monitoring, de-worming, family planning (spacing).
- **Nurse's role** – **nutritional assessment & screening (MUAC, weight, Hb)**, **growth monitoring (MCP card)**, identify & refer malnourished (NRC), **IFA/ vitamin A/ deworming distribution & compliance**, breastfeeding & IYCF counselling, **cooking demonstrations**, diet counselling in diseases, coordination with AWW/ ASHA (convergence – "**AAA – ASHA, AWW, ANM**"), record keeping & reporting, school health, community surveys, health education campaigns, research.
