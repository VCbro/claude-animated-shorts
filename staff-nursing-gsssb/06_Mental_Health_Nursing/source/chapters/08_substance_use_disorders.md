## 8.1 Terminology

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Substance abuse / harmful use** | Pattern of use causing **physical or mental harm** |
| **Dependence (addiction)** | Cluster – **craving (compulsion)**, **loss of control**, **tolerance**, **withdrawal**, **neglect of other interests**, **persistent use despite harm** (≥ 3 in 12 months – ICD-10) |
| **Tolerance** | **Need for increased amounts** for same effect / diminished effect with same dose |
| **Withdrawal** | Characteristic symptoms on stopping/ reducing (usually opposite of drug effect) |
| **Cross-tolerance** | Tolerance to one drug extends to similar drugs (alcohol ↔ BZD – used in detox) |
| **Intoxication** | Reversible state after recent use |
| **Relapse** | Return to use after abstinence |
| **Psychological dependence** | Craving, emotional need; **physical dependence** – withdrawal syndrome |

- **National Survey (Magnitude of Substance Use, 2019, AIIMS)** – **alcohol – most commonly used (~14.6 % of 10–75 yrs)**, then **cannabis (~2.8 %)** & **opioids (~2.1 %)**; **tobacco** – GATS-2: 28.6 % adults.
- **Aetiology** – genetic (alcohol), peer pressure, availability, stress, personality, family dysfunction, **reward pathway – dopamine (mesolimbic, nucleus accumbens)**.

## 8.2 Alcohol

- **CNS depressant**; metabolised in liver – **alcohol dehydrogenase → acetaldehyde → aldehyde dehydrogenase → acetate**; **1 standard drink ~10 g alcohol (India)**.
- **Blood alcohol** – **legal driving limit India 30 mg/100 ml (0.03 %)**; > 400 mg% may be fatal.
- **Screening** – **CAGE** (**C**ut down?, **A**nnoyed by criticism?, **G**uilty?, **E**ye-opener? – **≥ 2 = significant**), **AUDIT** (WHO, 10 items), lab – **↑ GGT (most sensitive)**, ↑ MCV, ↑ AST:ALT > 2, **CDT**.
- **Complications** – **GI** (gastritis, pancreatitis, **fatty liver → alcoholic hepatitis → cirrhosis**, oesophageal varices), **CNS** (**Wernicke-Korsakoff, peripheral neuropathy**, cerebellar degeneration, dementia), **cardiomyopathy**, HTN, **fetal alcohol syndrome**, nutritional deficiencies (**thiamine**, folate), **accidents, violence, suicide**, **Othello syndrome (morbid jealousy)**, social & occupational problems.

### Alcohol withdrawal
| Stage | Time after last drink | Features |
|---|---|---|
| **Uncomplicated withdrawal** | **6–12 hrs** (peak 24–48) | **Tremors (earliest – "shakes")**, anxiety, insomnia, sweating, nausea, tachycardia, ↑ BP, craving |
| **Withdrawal seizures ("rum fits")** | **12–48 hrs** | Generalised tonic-clonic |
| **Alcoholic hallucinosis** | 12–48 hrs | **Auditory hallucinations** in **clear consciousness** |
| **Delirium tremens (DT)** | **48–72 hrs (up to 96)** | **Medical emergency** – **clouded consciousness, disorientation, vivid visual (Lilliputian – small people/ animals) & tactile hallucinations, gross tremor, fever, autonomic hyperactivity (tachycardia, sweating, ↑ BP)**, agitation; mortality ~5 % untreated |

### Treatment
- **Detoxification** – **benzodiazepines (drug of choice)** – **chlordiazepoxide/ diazepam** (long-acting – loading/ tapering), **lorazepam/ oxazepam in liver disease & elderly**; **CIWA-Ar** guided; **IV thiamine 100 mg before glucose** (prevents Wernicke's), multivitamins, fluids & electrolytes (Mg, K), nutrition.
- **DT nursing** – **quiet, well-lit room** (reduce misperceptions), **constant observation**, **re-orientation**, **safety (side rails, avoid restraints if possible)**, vital signs every 1–2 hrs, **fluid & electrolyte balance**, fever control, seizure precautions, high-calorie diet.
- **Wernicke's encephalopathy** – **thiamine (B1) deficiency** – **triad: global confusion, ataxia, ophthalmoplegia (nystagmus, lateral rectus palsy)** – reversible with **parenteral thiamine**.
- **Korsakoff's psychosis/ syndrome** – **anterograde amnesia (recent memory loss), disorientation & confabulation** – largely **irreversible**; mammillary bodies.
- **Relapse prevention (anti-craving/ deterrent)** –
  - **Disulfiram (Antabuse)** – inhibits aldehyde dehydrogenase → **acetaldehyde accumulates → disulfiram-ethanol reaction (flushing, throbbing headache, nausea, vomiting, palpitation, hypotension, breathlessness)**; **start after ≥ 12–24 hrs of abstinence**, **informed consent – never secretly**; avoid alcohol in **cough syrups, mouthwash, aftershave, vinegar sauces**.
  - **Naltrexone** (opioid antagonist – ↓ craving & reward), **acamprosate** (glutamate – ↓ craving; safe in liver disease), baclofen, topiramate.
- **Psychosocial** – **motivational interviewing (stages of change)**, **CBT, relapse prevention**, **Alcoholics Anonymous (AA) – 12-step programme (1935, Bill W. & Dr Bob)**, **Al-Anon (family), Alateen (teenage children)**, family therapy, aversion therapy, **de-addiction centres**.

## 8.3 Other Substances

| Substance | Intoxication | Withdrawal | Specific points |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Opioids** (heroin/ "brown sugar", morphine, codeine cough syrup, tramadol, opium, smack) | Euphoria, drowsiness, **pin-point pupils (miosis)**, **respiratory depression**, constipation; **overdose triad – coma, pin-point pupils, respiratory depression** | **Flu-like – yawning, lacrimation, rhinorrhoea, dilated pupils (mydriasis), piloerection ("cold turkey"), muscle aches, abdominal cramps, diarrhoea, insomnia, craving**; **not life-threatening** | **Overdose – naloxone**; detox – **buprenorphine, methadone, clonidine**; **OST (opioid substitution therapy) – buprenorphine/ methadone**; **naltrexone** (maintenance); IV use – **HIV, HCV**, abscess |
| **Cannabis** (bhang, ganja, charas/ hashish; **THC**) | Euphoria, **↑ appetite ("munchies")**, **conjunctival injection (red eyes)**, dry mouth, tachycardia, time distortion, anxiety/ panic | Irritability, insomnia, ↓ appetite (mild) | **Amotivational syndrome**, **cannabis-induced psychosis**, ↑ schizophrenia risk; "flashbacks" |
| **Tobacco/ nicotine** | Stimulant & relaxant | Irritability, craving, ↑ appetite, poor concentration | **Most common substance used (incl. smokeless)**; **NRT, varenicline, bupropion**; **5 A's (Ask, Advise, Assess, Assist, Arrange)**; quitline **1800-112-356** |
| **Cocaine / amphetamines** (stimulants) | Euphoria, **↑ energy, dilated pupils**, tachycardia, HTN, **paranoid psychosis**, **formication ("cocaine bugs")**, MI, stroke | **"Crash" – depression, hypersomnia, ↑ appetite, suicidal** | Nasal septum perforation (cocaine) |
| **Sedative-hypnotics (BZD, barbiturates)** | Like alcohol – slurred speech, ataxia | **Like alcohol – seizures, delirium (can be fatal)** → **gradual tapering** | Overdose BZD – **flumazenil** |
| **Inhalants/ volatile solvents** (glue, **whitener, petrol**, paint thinner) | Euphoria, dizziness, slurred speech | – | **Street children, adolescents**; sudden sniffing death (arrhythmia), brain/ liver/ kidney damage |
| **Hallucinogens (LSD, mescaline, psilocybin)** | Perceptual distortions, **synaesthesia**, "bad trip", flashbacks | Minimal | – |
| **Caffeine** | Restlessness | Headache, fatigue | – |

## 8.4 Nursing Care & Programmes

- **Assessment** – history (substance, amount, route, duration, last use), withdrawal signs, complications, motivation (**stages of change**), family.
- **Detox phase** – safety, **vital signs**, withdrawal monitoring (CIWA/ COWS), nutrition, hydration, **seizure precautions**, sleep, medications.
- **Rehabilitation** – **therapeutic relationship (non-judgemental)**, **confront denial** gently, **motivational interviewing**, **coping skills, relapse prevention**, **life skills, vocational training**, **self-help groups (AA, NA)**, family involvement, **avoid enabling** (codependency).
- **Common defence mechanisms** – **denial (most common)**, rationalisation, projection, minimisation.
- **Laws & programmes** – **NDPS Act 1985 (Narcotic Drugs & Psychotropic Substances)** – addicts may be sent for treatment (Sec 64A immunity if seeking treatment); **Nasha Mukt Bharat Abhiyaan (15 Aug 2020)**; **National Action Plan for Drug Demand Reduction (NAPDDR)**; **de-addiction centres (Integrated Rehabilitation Centres for Addicts – IRCA)**; **Drug Treatment Clinics**; **International Day against Drug Abuse & Illicit Trafficking – 26 June**; **Gujarat – prohibition state (Bombay Prohibition Act 1949)** – health permits for alcohol.
