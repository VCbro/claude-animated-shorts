# Schizophrenia & Other Psychotic Disorders

## 5.1 Schizophrenia – Basics

:::def Schizophrenia
A **severe, chronic psychotic disorder** characterised by **disturbances in thinking, perception, emotion, behaviour & volition**, with **loss of contact with reality** and **impaired functioning**, in **clear consciousness**. Term by **Eugen Bleuler (1908/1911)** – "split mind" (splitting of psychic functions, **NOT split personality**). Kraepelin – "**dementia praecox**".
:::

- **Prevalence ~1 %** (lifetime); **onset – late adolescence/ early adulthood**: **males 15–25 yrs, females 25–35 yrs** (males earlier & worse prognosis); equal sex incidence.
- **Aetiology** – **genetic** (one parent – ~12 %, **both parents ~40–46 %**, **monozygotic twin ~48 %**), **dopamine hypothesis (excess dopamine in mesolimbic pathway – positive symptoms; ↓ in mesocortical – negative symptoms)**, glutamate (NMDA hypofunction), serotonin; **neurodevelopmental** (obstetric complications, winter births, prenatal infection); **ventricular enlargement**, ↓ frontal activity; **cannabis use**, urban living, migration, stress (**stress–diathesis model**); family – **high expressed emotion → relapse**; "**schizophrenogenic mother**" & **double-bind theory (Bateson)** – historical.

## 5.2 Clinical Features

- **Bleuler's 4 A's (fundamental symptoms)** – **A**ssociation disturbance (loosening), **A**ffective disturbance (**flat/ inappropriate affect**), **A**mbivalence, **A**utism (withdrawal into fantasy) (+ **A**ttention, **A**volition sometimes added).
- **Kurt Schneider's First-Rank Symptoms (FRS)** – highly suggestive (not pathognomonic):
  1. **Audible thoughts (thought echo – "Gedankenlautwerden")**
  2. **Voices arguing or discussing** (third person)
  3. **Voices commenting** on one's actions (running commentary)
  4. **Thought withdrawal**
  5. **Thought insertion**
  6. **Thought broadcasting** (thought diffusion)
  7. **Made feelings, made impulses, made volitional acts** (**passivity phenomena** – controlled by external force)
  8. **Somatic passivity**
  9. **Delusional perception**

| **Positive symptoms** (excess/ distortion) | **Negative symptoms ("5 A's")** |
|---|---|
| **Hallucinations (auditory – most common)** | **Alogia** (poverty of speech) |
| **Delusions** (persecutory, reference, control) | **Avolition** (lack of motivation, apathy) |
| **Disorganised speech** (loosening, word salad, neologisms) | **Affective flattening/ blunting** |
| **Disorganised/ bizarre behaviour**, catatonic behaviour | **Anhedonia** |
| – | **Asociality** (social withdrawal), attention deficit |
| Respond better to antipsychotics; **better prognosis** | Respond poorly; **poor prognosis**, more disability |

- **Diagnosis** – **ICD-10/ 11: symptoms for ≥ 1 month**; **DSM-5: ≥ 6 months total (≥ 1 month active phase)**; **< 1 month – acute & transient psychotic disorder (ICD)/ brief psychotic disorder (DSM)**; **1–6 months – schizophreniform (DSM)**.
- **Phases** – **prodromal** (social withdrawal, decline in functioning, odd beliefs) → **active (acute psychotic)** → **residual**.

## 5.3 Types (ICD-10 – still asked; ICD-11/ DSM-5 removed subtypes)

| Type | Features |
|---|---|
| **Paranoid** | **Most common**; **prominent delusions (persecution/ grandeur) & auditory hallucinations**; personality relatively preserved; **later onset, better prognosis**; may be hostile/ suspicious |
| **Hebephrenic (disorganised)** | **Early onset (15–25 yrs)**; **disorganised speech & behaviour, inappropriate/ silly affect (giggling), mannerisms, grimacing**; **worst prognosis** |
| **Catatonic** | **Psychomotor disturbances – stupor/ excitement, mutism, negativism, posturing, waxy flexibility, echolalia, echopraxia, automatic obedience**; **best prognosis** (responds to **lorazepam & ECT**) |
| **Undifferentiated** | Doesn't fit any type |
| **Residual** | Negative symptoms after active phase |
| **Simple** | **Insidious negative symptoms without positive symptoms** – drifting, vagrancy |
| **Post-schizophrenic depression** | Depression after an episode |

### Prognosis
| **Good prognosis** | **Poor prognosis** |
|---|---|
| **Acute onset**, late onset, precipitating stressor | **Insidious onset**, early onset |
| Female, married | Male, single |
| **Positive symptoms**, catatonic/ paranoid | **Negative symptoms**, hebephrenic/ simple |
| Mood symptoms present | Family history of schizophrenia |
| Good premorbid personality & social support | Poor premorbid adjustment, **high EE family** |
| Good compliance | Substance use, non-compliance, structural brain changes |

- **Course** – ~1/3 recover substantially, ~1/3 intermittent relapses, ~1/3 chronic deterioration; **suicide risk ~5–10 %** (esp. young males after first episode, post-psychotic depression).

## 5.4 Treatment

- **Antipsychotics** (**typical & atypical**; **clozapine for treatment-resistant** – failed ≥ 2 antipsychotics); **depot** for non-compliance; **maintenance – at least 1–2 yrs after first episode, longer/ lifelong for relapses**.
- **ECT** – catatonia, acute excitement, suicidal, resistant.
- **Psychosocial** – **psychoeducation (patient & family)**, **family therapy (↓ expressed emotion)**, **social skills training**, **vocational rehabilitation**, **token economy**, **CBT for psychosis**, **assertive community treatment**, day care, half-way homes, supported employment.

## 5.5 Nursing Care

### Hallucinations
- **Observe for cues** (talking/ laughing to self, listening posture, sudden anger) – **ask directly**: "Are you hearing voices? What are they saying?" (check **command hallucinations** – risk of harm).
- **Don't argue or deny** patient's experience; **don't agree**; **present reality**: "I understand the voices seem real to you, but I don't hear them."
- **Focus on reality-based activities**, **distraction** (music, conversation, activities), **reduce environmental stimuli**, consistent staff.
- Teach **coping** – humming, talking to trusted person, earphones/ music, **voice dismissal**.
- **Safety** – command hallucinations ordering harm → close observation.

### Delusions
- **Don't argue, challenge or try to convince** patient his delusion is false; **don't agree/ reinforce** either.
- **Focus on feelings behind delusion** ("You must feel frightened") & **reality**; **voice doubt** gently; **avoid whispering/ laughing** in front of patient (paranoid).
- **Build trust** – be **honest, consistent**, keep promises, **avoid touching suspicious patient without explanation**.
- **Persecutory delusion & food refusal ("poisoned food")** – **sealed/ packaged food, let patient choose own tray, family-brought food**, eat in common dining room.
- **Engage in reality-based activities**; don't focus on delusional content.

### Withdrawal/ social isolation & negative symptoms
- **Brief, frequent contacts**, accept silence, gradually involve in **one-to-one → small group activities**, **simple tasks**, **positive reinforcement**, assist **self-care (ADL schedule)**.

### Catatonia
- **Physical needs** – nutrition (tube feeding if needed), fluids, **elimination**, **skin care, position change 2-hrly (pressure sores)**, **ROM exercises**, mouth & eye care; **talk to patient (they may be aware)**; **lorazepam, ECT**; watch for **sudden excitement (catatonic excitement)**.

### General
- **Medication compliance** (check swallowing), side effects (EPS, NMS, metabolic), **suicide & violence risk**, **family psychoeducation**, rehabilitation, **relapse prevention (early warning signs – sleep disturbance, withdrawal, suspiciousness)**.

## 5.6 Other Psychotic Disorders

| Disorder | Key points |
|---|---|
| **Persistent delusional disorder** | **Non-bizarre fixed delusion ≥ 3 months (ICD) / ≥ 1 month (DSM)**; **otherwise functioning normally**; types – **persecutory (most common)**, **jealous (Othello syndrome – conjugal paranoia, alcohol)**, **erotomanic (de Clérambault – believes someone of higher status loves them)**, grandiose, **somatic (delusional parasitosis)**; Rx – antipsychotics |
| **Acute & transient psychotic disorder (ATPD)** / brief psychotic disorder | Acute onset (≤ 2 weeks), often after stressor, **< 1 month (DSM)/ < 3 months (ICD)**, full recovery; common in India |
| **Schizoaffective disorder** | Schizophrenia symptoms **+ prominent mood (manic/ depressive) episode** concurrently |
| **Induced delusional disorder (Folie à deux)** | **Shared psychotic disorder** – delusion transferred from dominant (primary) to dependent person; **separate them** |
| **Postpartum (puerperal) psychosis** | **Within 2–4 weeks of delivery**; confusion, hallucinations, **risk of infanticide & suicide** – **psychiatric emergency**; mostly bipolar; Rx – antipsychotics, lithium, **ECT**; supervised contact with baby |
| **Capgras syndrome** | Delusion that a familiar person is replaced by an **impostor** |
| **Fregoli syndrome** | Different people are the **same persecutor in disguise** |
| **Cotard syndrome** | **Nihilistic delusion** – "I am dead/ my organs are rotting" (severe depression) |
| **Koro** | Culture-bound – fear of penis retracting into abdomen (NE India, SE Asia) |
| **Dhat syndrome** | Culture-bound (India) – anxiety about **loss of semen** |
