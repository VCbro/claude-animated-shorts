## 10.1 Delirium

:::def Delirium (acute confusional state)
**Acute onset (hours–days), fluctuating** disturbance of **attention & consciousness (awareness)** with **cognitive changes (disorientation, memory)** and **perceptual disturbances**, caused by an **underlying medical condition, drug intoxication or withdrawal**; usually **reversible**.
:::

- **Causes ("I WATCH DEATH")** – **I**nfections (UTI, pneumonia – common in elderly), **W**ithdrawal (alcohol – DT, BZD), **A**cute metabolic (**hypoglycaemia, electrolyte imbalance – Na, Ca**, uraemia, hepatic failure), **T**rauma (head injury, post-op), **C**NS pathology (stroke, seizures), **H**ypoxia, **D**eficiencies (**thiamine, B12**), **E**ndocrine (thyroid), **A**cute vascular, **T**oxins/ drugs (**anticholinergics, opioids, BZD, steroids**), **H**eavy metals.
- **Features** – **clouding of consciousness**, **↓ attention**, **disorientation (time first)**, **visual hallucinations/ illusions**, agitation or **hypoactive (quiet) delirium – often missed**, **sleep-wake reversal**, **sundowning (worse in evening/ night)**, fluctuating course, lucid intervals.
- **Assessment** – **CAM (Confusion Assessment Method)**.
- **Management** – **identify & treat cause (most important)**; **low-dose haloperidol** for agitation; **avoid BZD** (except alcohol/ BZD withdrawal); **non-pharmacological**.
- **Nursing** – **well-lit room (night light), quiet, familiar objects, clocks/ calendars, frequent re-orientation**, **same staff**, **family presence**, **glasses & hearing aids**, safety (**fall prevention, low bed**), **avoid restraints**, hydration, nutrition, sleep promotion, early mobilisation.

## 10.2 Dementia (Major Neurocognitive Disorder)

- **Chronic, progressive, usually irreversible** decline in **memory & other cognitive functions** (language, judgement, executive function) with **clear consciousness**, interfering with daily life.
- **Types** – **Alzheimer's disease (most common ~60–70 %)**, **vascular dementia (2nd – stepwise deterioration, multi-infarct)**, **Lewy body dementia** (visual hallucinations, parkinsonism, fluctuations – **sensitive to antipsychotics**), **frontotemporal (Pick's disease – personality change, disinhibition, early)**, Parkinson's, HIV, alcohol-related; **reversible causes** – **hypothyroidism, B12 deficiency, normal pressure hydrocephalus (triad: dementia, gait disturbance, urinary incontinence – "wacky, wobbly, wet")**, depression (**pseudodementia**), subdural haematoma, neurosyphilis.
- **Alzheimer's** – **amyloid plaques & neurofibrillary tangles (tau)**, **↓ acetylcholine**, cortical atrophy (hippocampus); risk – **age (strongest)**, family history, **Down syndrome**, APOE-ε4; **4 A's – Amnesia (recent memory first), Aphasia, Apraxia, Agnosia**; stages – mild (forgetfulness) → moderate (confusion, wandering, sundowning) → severe (total dependence, incontinence).
- **Drugs** – **cholinesterase inhibitors – donepezil, rivastigmine (patch), galantamine** (mild–moderate); **memantine (NMDA antagonist – moderate–severe)**; behavioural symptoms – non-drug first; antipsychotics cautiously (↑ stroke/ death in elderly).
- **MMSE (Folstein, 30 points)** – **24–30 normal, 18–23 mild, < 17 severe**; **Mini-Cog, MoCA, clock drawing test**.
- **World Alzheimer's Day – 21 September**.

| | **Delirium** | **Dementia** |
|---|---|---|
| Onset | **Acute (hours–days)** | **Insidious (months–years)** |
| Course | **Fluctuating**, worse at night | **Progressive, stable day-to-day** |
| **Consciousness** | **Clouded/ altered** | **Clear** (until late) |
| **Attention** | **Impaired (key feature)** | Normal (early) |
| Memory | Immediate & recent impaired | **Recent memory first**, later remote |
| Hallucinations | **Common (visual)** | Less common (except Lewy body) |
| Reversibility | **Usually reversible** | **Usually irreversible** |
| Duration | Days–weeks | Months–years |

### Nursing care of dementia
- **Safety** – remove hazards, **ID bracelet (wandering)**, locked exits, **fall prevention**, supervise smoking/ cooking/ medications.
- **Structured routine**, **consistent caregiver**, **simple one-step instructions**, **short sentences, calm tone, eye contact**, avoid arguing/ correcting false memories – **validation therapy** (accept feelings), **reality orientation** (early stages), **reminiscence therapy** (past memories – ↑ self-esteem).
- **ADL support** – dressing (simple clothes, Velcro), feeding (finger foods), toileting schedule (incontinence), hygiene.
- **Sundowning** – ↑ lighting in evening, limit naps, structured activities, avoid caffeine.
- **Agitation** – identify triggers (pain, hunger, toilet), distraction, calm environment.
- **Caregiver support** – **caregiver burden/ burnout**, **respite care**, support groups, education, legal/ financial planning (advance directive – MHCA).

## 10.3 Intellectual Disability (Intellectual Developmental Disorder)

- **IQ < 70** + **deficits in adaptive functioning** with **onset during developmental period (< 18 yrs)**; **IQ = MA/ CA × 100**.
- **Mild (50–69) – ~85 %, educable**; **moderate (35–49) – trainable**; **severe (20–34)**; **profound (< 20)**.
- **Causes** – **genetic (Down syndrome – most common chromosomal; Fragile X – most common inherited)**, metabolic (**PKU, congenital hypothyroidism – most common preventable**), prenatal (**rubella, alcohol, iodine deficiency**), perinatal (**birth asphyxia, prematurity, kernicterus**), postnatal (meningitis, encephalitis, head injury, lead, malnutrition), psychosocial deprivation.
- **Management** – **early stimulation**, **special education**, **vocational training**, behaviour modification (**token economy, operant conditioning**), **parental counselling**, treat comorbidities (epilepsy, behaviour problems – risperidone), **RPwD Act 2016**, **NIEPID (Secunderabad)**; **prevention** – iodised salt, rubella vaccination, folic acid, antenatal care, newborn screening (TSH, PKU).

## 10.4 Childhood Psychiatric Disorders

| Disorder | Key features | Treatment/ nursing |
|---|---|---|
| **Autism spectrum disorder (ASD)** | Onset **< 3 yrs**; **deficits in social interaction & communication** (**poor eye contact, no social smile/ pointing, delayed speech, echolalia, pronoun reversal**), **restricted repetitive behaviours** (**hand flapping, rocking, insistence on sameness**), sensory issues; boys 4 : 1; **Kanner (1943)**; **M-CHAT** screening; **not caused by vaccines** | **Early intensive behavioural intervention (ABA)**, speech & occupational therapy, special education, **structured routine**, risperidone/ aripiprazole for irritability |
| **ADHD** | **Inattention, hyperactivity, impulsivity**; onset **before 12 yrs**, ≥ 2 settings, ≥ 6 months; boys | **Methylphenidate (DOC)**, atomoxetine; **behaviour therapy, parent training**; **structured environment, short tasks, front seat, one instruction at a time, positive reinforcement**, reduce distractions |
| **Conduct disorder** | **Repetitive violation of others' rights/ rules** – aggression to people/ animals, destruction of property, lying, theft, truancy, running away; may → antisocial PD | Parent management training, multisystemic therapy, limit setting |
| **Oppositional defiant disorder** | Angry, argumentative, defiant towards authority (no serious violation) | Parent training |
| **Specific learning disorder** | **Dyslexia (reading – most common)**, dysgraphia, dyscalculia; normal IQ | Remedial education |
| **Tic disorders / Tourette's syndrome** | **Motor + vocal tics > 1 yr**; **coprolalia** (obscene words); onset < 18 | Haloperidol, risperidone, aripiprazole, clonidine; habit reversal |
| **Enuresis** | **≥ 5 yrs** | Alarm, star chart, desmopressin |
| **Encopresis** | **≥ 4 yrs** | Bowel training |
| **Separation anxiety disorder** | Excessive fear of separation from attachment figure; **school refusal** | CBT, gradual return to school |
| **Selective mutism** | Doesn't speak in specific situations (school) | – |
| **Childhood depression** | Irritability rather than sadness, school decline | – |
| **Stuttering** | Childhood-onset fluency disorder | Speech therapy |

- **Child abuse & neglect** – recognise, document, report (**POCSO 2012, CHILDLINE 1098**).
- **Child Guidance Clinic** – multidisciplinary (child psychiatrist, psychologist, social worker, nurse).
- **Play therapy, family therapy, parent training** – key modalities.

## 10.5 Geriatric Psychiatry

- **Common disorders in elderly** – **depression (most common)**, **dementia**, **delirium**, anxiety, substance (BZD, alcohol), late-onset psychosis.
- **Elderly depression** – somatic complaints, **pseudodementia** (cognitive impairment due to depression – reversible; patient complains of memory loss – "don't know" answers), **↑ suicide risk (elderly males highest)**.
- **Principles** – **start low, go slow** (drugs), **avoid anticholinergics & long-acting BZD** (falls, confusion), polypharmacy review, social support, elder abuse screening.
