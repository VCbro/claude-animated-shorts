## 11.1 Psychiatric Emergencies

| Emergency | Key management |
|---|---|
| **Suicidal patient** | **Highest priority – safety**, constant observation, remove harmful objects (see Ch 6) |
| **Violent/ aggressive patient** | See below |
| **Catatonic stupor/ excitement** | **IV/ IM lorazepam** (lorazepam challenge test), **ECT**; nutrition, fluids, prevent pressure sores/ DVT |
| **Neuroleptic Malignant Syndrome** | Stop antipsychotic, cooling, IV fluids, **dantrolene/ bromocriptine**, ICU |
| **Serotonin syndrome** | Stop serotonergic drugs, **cyproheptadine**, supportive |
| **Lithium toxicity** | Stop lithium, IV saline, **haemodialysis** |
| **Acute dystonia** | **IM promethazine/ benztropine/ diphenhydramine** |
| **Delirium tremens** | BZD, thiamine, fluids |
| **Drug overdose** | ABC, decontamination, antidotes (**naloxone – opioids, flumazenil – BZD**), TCA overdose – **sodium bicarbonate** (cardiac) |
| **Panic attack** | Stay with patient, calm, breathing exercises, BZD |
| **Postpartum psychosis** | Admission, safety of mother & baby |
| **Acute psychosis/ excitement** | **Rapid tranquillisation – IM haloperidol + promethazine** (or IM lorazepam/ olanzapine) |
| **Conversion/ dissociative states** | Calm reassurance, avoid secondary gain |
| **Grief/ crisis** | Crisis intervention |

### Management of violence & aggression
- **Predictors** – **history of violence (best predictor)**, substance intoxication, command hallucinations, paranoid delusions, mania, antisocial PD, young male; **warning signs – pacing, clenched fists, loud voice, threats, staring, agitation**.
- **Assault cycle** – **trigger → escalation → crisis → recovery → post-crisis depression**.
- **De-escalation (first-line)** – **remain calm, speak softly & slowly, non-threatening posture**, **maintain safe distance (> arm's length, ~1.5 m)**, **stand at an angle (not face-to-face)**, **keep exit route clear for both**, don't turn back, acknowledge feelings, **offer choices**, set clear limits, avoid arguing/ sudden movements, **reduce stimuli/ remove audience**, **never approach alone – call for help (team)**; remove potential weapons; avoid wearing necklaces/ stethoscope around neck.
- **If de-escalation fails** – **PRN medication** (oral first, then IM), **physical restraint by trained team (minimum 4–5 persons, one for each limb + head)**, as per MHCA (**last resort, documented, monitored every 15 min**).
- **Post-incident** – debriefing of patient & staff, documentation, review care plan.

## 11.2 Community Mental Health

- **Community psychiatry** – prevention, early detection, treatment & rehabilitation **in the community** rather than institutions; **deinstitutionalisation**.
- **Levels of prevention in mental health (Caplan)** – **primary** (prevent occurrence – mental health promotion, life skills education, stress management, genetic counselling, prenatal care, prevent child abuse), **secondary** (early detection & prompt treatment, crisis intervention, screening), **tertiary** (rehabilitation, prevent relapse & disability).
- **NMHP (1982)** – objectives: availability & accessibility of **minimum mental health care for all**, integration with general health care, community participation; **DMHP (1996 – pilot Bellary, Karnataka)** – district-level services, training of PHC staff, IEC, **suicide prevention, life skills, workplace stress, counselling in schools/ colleges**.
- **National Mental Health Policy (2014)**, **Mental Healthcare Act 2017**, **Tele-MANAS (10 Oct 2022) – 14416 / 1-800-891-4416** (24×7, multilingual), **KIRAN helpline (1800-599-0019, 2020)**, **Manodarpan** (students, 2020), **Ayushman Arogya Mandir – mental health as CPHC package**.
- **Rehabilitation services** – **day hospital/ day care centre, half-way home (transitional – between hospital & community), long-stay homes, sheltered workshop, vocational rehabilitation, supported employment, foster care, self-help groups, community-based rehabilitation**.
- **Stigma reduction**, **psychoeducation**, **family support (high EE reduction)**, **assertive community treatment**.
- **Role of community mental health nurse** – case finding, home visits, **medication management (depot injections)**, follow-up, crisis intervention, psychoeducation, liaison, counselling, referral, rehabilitation.

## 11.3 Mental Healthcare Act 2017 (MHCA)

- **Passed – 7 April 2017**; **came into force – 29 May 2018**; replaced **Mental Health Act 1987**; **rights-based**, in line with **UNCRPD**.

| Section/ Provision | Key point |
|---|---|
| **Sec 2 – Mental illness definition** | Substantial disorder of thinking, mood, perception, orientation or memory that grossly impairs judgement, behaviour, capacity to recognise reality (**excludes mental retardation/ ID**) |
| **Sec 4 – Capacity** | Every person presumed to have capacity to make mental health care decisions |
| **Sec 5 – Advance directive** | Person can state **how they wish/ don't wish to be treated** & appoint nominated representative |
| **Sec 14 – Nominated representative** | Person appointed to support decision-making |
| **Sec 18 – Right to access mental health care** | Affordable, good quality, from govt services; free for BPL/ homeless |
| **Sec 19 – Right to community living** | – |
| **Sec 20 – Right to protection from cruel, inhuman & degrading treatment** | **No chaining**, clean environment, privacy, food, sanitation |
| **Sec 21 – Equality & non-discrimination** | **Medical insurance for mental illness on par with physical illness** |
| **Sec 22 – Right to information**, **Sec 23 – confidentiality**, **Sec 27 – free legal aid** | – |
| **Sec 85–86 – Independent admission** | **Voluntary** – person with capacity; can request discharge (**within 24 hrs**) |
| **Sec 89 – Supported admission (up to 30 days)** | High support needs – self-harm/ harm to others/ unable to care for self; **by nominated representative's request + 2 psychiatrists/ MHPs' assessment** |
| **Sec 90 – Supported admission beyond 30 days** | **Up to 90 days → 120 days → 180 days** with **Mental Health Review Board (MHRB)** approval |
| **Sec 94 – Emergency treatment** | **Up to 72 hours** (to prevent death/ harm) by registered medical practitioner |
| **Sec 95 – Prohibited procedures** | **ECT without anaesthesia & muscle relaxant**, **ECT for minors**, **sterilisation as treatment**, **chaining** |
| **Sec 96 – Psychosurgery** | Only with informed consent + MHRB approval |
| **Sec 97 – Restraint & seclusion** | Physical restraint only to prevent imminent harm; **seclusion & solitary confinement prohibited** |
| **Sec 98 – Discharge planning** | Community-based treatment |
| **Sec 100 – Duty of police** | Take person found wandering to public health establishment (within 24 hrs) |
| **Sec 102 – Magistrate** | Can order assessment |
| **Sec 115 – Attempted suicide** | **Presumed to have severe stress; not punishable; govt to provide care & rehabilitation** |
| **Authorities** | **Central Mental Health Authority (CMHA)**, **State Mental Health Authority (SMHA)**, **Mental Health Review Boards (MHRB)** – district level; register mental health establishments & professionals |

## 11.4 Forensic Psychiatry & Legal Terms

- **McNaughten (M'Naghten) rule (1843)** – **legal insanity defence**: at the time of the act, due to unsoundness of mind, the person **did not know the nature of the act or that it was wrong**; India – **IPC Section 84 → BNS 2023 Section 22** ("act of a person of unsound mind").
- **Testamentary capacity** – capacity to make a valid **will** (understand nature, extent of property, claims of relatives); **lucid interval** wills valid.
- **Competency to stand trial**, **criminal responsibility**, **civil responsibility (contracts, marriage)** – mentally ill person's contract voidable.
- **Marriage** – **Hindu Marriage Act 1955** – mental disorder (unfit for marriage/ procreation) ground for **annulment/ divorce**.
- **Narcoanalysis** – IV thiopentone/ sodium amytal (truth serum) – **requires consent** (SC 2010, Selvi case).
- **Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act 2016** – mental illness one of 21 disabilities.
- **NDPS Act 1985**, **POCSO 2012**, **Juvenile Justice Act 2015**.

## 11.5 Ethics in Psychiatric Nursing

- **Autonomy** (informed consent, refusal), **beneficence**, **non-maleficence**, **justice**, **confidentiality** (exceptions – **duty to warn/ protect third party – Tarasoff case**, court order, risk of harm), **veracity**, **fidelity**, **least restrictive environment**, **dignity**, **advocacy**.
- **Informed consent** – capacity, information, voluntariness.
