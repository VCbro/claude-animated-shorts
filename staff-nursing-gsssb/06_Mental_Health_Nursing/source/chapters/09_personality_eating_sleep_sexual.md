## 9.1 Personality Disorders (PD)

- **Personality** – enduring pattern of thinking, feeling & behaving.
- **Personality disorder** – **enduring, inflexible, maladaptive pattern** of inner experience & behaviour, deviating markedly from culture, **pervasive**, **onset in adolescence/ early adulthood**, causing distress/ impairment; usually **ego-syntonic** (patient doesn't see it as a problem) – **poor insight, difficult to treat**.

| Cluster | Type | Key features |
|---|---|---|
| **A – "Odd/ eccentric" (MAD)** | **Paranoid** | **Pervasive distrust & suspiciousness**, grudges, misinterprets benign remarks |
| | **Schizoid** | **Detached, loner, emotionally cold**, indifferent to praise/ criticism, no desire for relationships |
| | **Schizotypal** | **Odd beliefs, magical thinking, eccentric behaviour/ appearance**, ideas of reference (closest to schizophrenia) |
| **B – "Dramatic/ emotional/ erratic" (BAD)** | **Antisocial (dissocial)** | **Disregard for others' rights, lying, cheating, aggression, criminal acts, impulsivity, irresponsibility, LACK OF GUILT/ remorse**; **conduct disorder before 15 yrs**; diagnosed **≥ 18 yrs**; males |
| | **Borderline (emotionally unstable)** | **Unstable relationships, self-image & mood**; **fear of abandonment**, **impulsivity**, **recurrent self-harm (wrist cutting)/ suicidal behaviour**, **chronic emptiness**, intense anger, **splitting (idealisation ↔ devaluation)**, **manipulation**, transient paranoia; females |
| | **Histrionic** | **Attention-seeking, dramatic, seductive**, shallow emotions, suggestible |
| | **Narcissistic** | **Grandiose, need for admiration, lack of empathy**, entitlement, exploitative |
| **C – "Anxious/ fearful" (SAD)** | **Avoidant (anxious)** | **Social inhibition, feels inadequate, hypersensitive to rejection**, wants relationships but avoids |
| | **Dependent** | **Excessive need to be cared for, submissive, clinging**, can't make decisions alone |
| | **Obsessive-compulsive (anankastic)** | **Perfectionism, orderliness, rigidity, preoccupation with rules & details**, stubborn, miserly (ego-syntonic – vs OCD ego-dystonic) |

- **Treatment** – psychotherapy (**Dialectical Behaviour Therapy – DBT – Marsha Linehan – for borderline PD**), CBT, group therapy; medication for symptoms (SSRIs, mood stabilisers, low-dose antipsychotics).

### Nursing care
- **Borderline** – **safety (self-harm precautions)**, **consistent limit setting by all staff** (prevents **splitting** – staff communicate & present unified approach), **clear boundaries**, avoid special favours, **teach emotion regulation & coping**, **don't reinforce manipulation**, help verbalise feelings, structured routine, DBT skills.
- **Antisocial** – **firm, consistent limits & consequences**, **don't argue/ get manipulated**, **hold patient responsible** for behaviour, protect others, teach anger management, positive reinforcement for appropriate behaviour.
- **Manipulative behaviour** – recognise, set & enforce limits consistently, **all staff aware of care plan**, avoid power struggles, don't accept gifts/ flattery, **discuss behaviour directly**.
- **Paranoid** – **honest, matter-of-fact**, explain actions, avoid humour/ whispering, respect space.
- **Dependent** – encourage decision making & self-care, praise independence.

## 9.2 Eating Disorders

| | **Anorexia nervosa** | **Bulimia nervosa** |
|---|---|---|
| Core | **Deliberate weight loss; intense fear of gaining weight; distorted body image** (sees self fat) | **Recurrent binge eating** (large amount, loss of control) **+ compensatory behaviour** (**self-induced vomiting, laxatives, diuretics, fasting, excessive exercise**) – **at least once/ week × 3 months** |
| Weight | **Significantly low – BMI < 17.5 (ICD-10)/ < 18.5** | **Usually normal/ near normal** |
| Age/ sex | **Adolescent girls (13–20 yrs)**, middle/ high SES, perfectionists | Late adolescent/ young women |
| Physical signs | **Amenorrhoea (ICD-10), lanugo hair, bradycardia, hypotension, hypothermia**, dry skin, constipation, osteoporosis, ↓ T3, **hypokalaemia** (if purging), oedema | **Russell's sign (calluses on knuckles)**, **dental enamel erosion**, **parotid enlargement**, **hypokalaemic metabolic alkalosis**, oesophagitis/ Mallory-Weiss tear |
| Mortality | **Highest of all psychiatric disorders** (~5–10 % – cardiac arrhythmia, suicide) | Lower |
| Treatment | **Nutritional rehabilitation (gradual – avoid refeeding syndrome: ↓ phosphate, K, Mg)**, **target weight gain 0.5–1 kg/week (inpatient)**, **family-based therapy (Maudsley – adolescents)**, CBT; hospitalise if BMI very low, electrolyte imbalance, bradycardia | **CBT (first-line)**, **SSRIs (fluoxetine – high dose)**, nutrition counselling |

- **Nursing (anorexia)** – **weigh daily (same time, same scale, after voiding, gown only, back to scale)**, **supervise meals & 1–2 hrs after meals (bathroom observation – prevent vomiting)**, **calorie count & I/O**, **contract for weight gain with privileges (behaviour modification)**, **don't discuss food/ weight excessively or argue about body image**, focus on feelings/ self-esteem, monitor electrolytes & ECG, **refeeding syndrome watch**, limit exercise.
- **Binge eating disorder** – binges without compensatory behaviour → obesity. **Pica** (non-food), **rumination disorder**.

## 9.3 Sleep Disorders

- **Normal sleep** – **NREM (stages N1, N2, N3 – deep/ slow-wave)** & **REM (dreaming, muscle atonia, rapid eye movements)**; cycles ~**90 min**, 4–6 cycles/ night; **REM ~20–25 %** of adult sleep; **newborn ~50 % REM**.

| Disorder | Features |
|---|---|
| **Insomnia** | **Most common**; difficulty initiating/ maintaining sleep or early awakening ≥ 3 nights/ week × 3 months; Rx – **sleep hygiene, CBT-I (first-line)**, short-term hypnotics (zolpidem, BZD) |
| **Hypersomnia** | Excessive sleepiness |
| **Narcolepsy** | **Tetrad – excessive daytime sleepiness (sleep attacks), cataplexy (sudden loss of muscle tone with emotion – laughter), hypnagogic hallucinations, sleep paralysis**; **REM onset sleep**; ↓ hypocretin (orexin); Rx – **modafinil**, stimulants, sodium oxybate |
| **Obstructive sleep apnoea** | Snoring, apnoea, daytime sleepiness, obesity; **CPAP** |
| **Sleepwalking (somnambulism), night terrors (pavor nocturnus)** | **NREM stage 3 (deep sleep)**; children; **no memory** of event; ensure safety |
| **Nightmares** | **REM sleep**; vivid recall |
| **REM sleep behaviour disorder** | Acting out dreams (loss of REM atonia) – Parkinson's risk |
| **Restless legs syndrome** | Urge to move legs at rest/ evening; iron deficiency; dopamine agonists |
| **Bruxism** | Teeth grinding |

- **Sleep hygiene** – **regular sleep-wake schedule**, **avoid daytime naps**, **avoid caffeine, nicotine, alcohol & heavy meals before bed**, exercise (not near bedtime), **bed only for sleep (stimulus control)**, quiet, dark, comfortable room, relaxation, **get up if unable to sleep in 20 min**, **avoid screens before bed**, warm milk.

## 9.4 Sexual Disorders

- **Sexual dysfunctions** – **erectile dysfunction (most common in males)**, **premature ejaculation**, ↓ desire (hypoactive sexual desire – most common in females), female arousal disorder, **anorgasmia**, **vaginismus** (involuntary spasm of vaginal muscles), **dyspareunia** (painful intercourse); **Masters & Johnson** – sexual response cycle (excitement, plateau, orgasm, resolution); **sensate focus therapy**; PDE-5 inhibitors (sildenafil) for ED; SSRIs (delay ejaculation).
- **Paraphilias (disorders of sexual preference)** – **fetishism** (non-living objects), **exhibitionism** (exposing genitals to strangers), **voyeurism** (watching others undress/ sexual acts), **frotteurism** (rubbing against non-consenting person), **paedophilia** (prepubertal children), **sexual sadism** (inflicting pain), **sexual masochism** (receiving pain), **transvestic fetishism** (cross-dressing for arousal), necrophilia, zoophilia.
- **Gender dysphoria (gender incongruence – ICD-11 moved out of mental disorders)** – distress due to incongruence between experienced gender & assigned sex; **Transgender Persons (Protection of Rights) Act 2019**.
- **Homosexuality** – **not a mental disorder** (removed DSM 1973); **Supreme Court of India decriminalised consensual same-sex relations – Sept 2018 (Navtej Singh Johar)**.
- **Dhat syndrome** (culture-bound, India).
