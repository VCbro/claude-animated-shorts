## 2.1 Functions & Bone Basics

- **Functions** – support, **protection** (skull – brain, ribs – heart & lungs), movement (levers), **haemopoiesis (red marrow)**, **mineral storage (99 % calcium, 85 % phosphorus)**, fat storage (yellow marrow), endocrine (osteocalcin).
- **Adult bones – 206** (axial **80** + appendicular **126**); **newborn ≈ 270–300** (fusion later).
- **Largest/ longest & strongest bone – femur**; **smallest – stapes (ear ossicle)**; **longest bone of upper limb – humerus**; **only bone with no articulation with other bone – hyoid**; **most commonly fractured bone – clavicle** (also commonest in birth injury); **first bone to ossify – clavicle**; **hardest substance – tooth enamel**.

| Type | Example |
|---|---|
| **Long** | Femur, humerus, tibia, radius, phalanges |
| **Short** | **Carpals, tarsals** |
| **Flat** | Skull bones, **sternum, ribs, scapula** |
| **Irregular** | **Vertebrae**, hip bone, some facial bones |
| **Sesamoid** (in tendon) | **Patella (largest sesamoid)**, pisiform |
| **Pneumatic** (air sinuses) | Maxilla, frontal, ethmoid, sphenoid |

### Bone structure
- **Long bone** – **diaphysis (shaft)** with **medullary cavity**, **epiphysis** (ends), **metaphysis**, **epiphyseal (growth) plate – longitudinal growth**, **periosteum** (outer – nutrition, growth in thickness, repair; **sensitive to pain**), endosteum, articular (hyaline) cartilage.
- **Compact bone** – **Haversian system (osteon)**: Haversian canal (vessels), lamellae, lacunae (osteocytes), canaliculi; **Volkmann's canals** connect. **Spongy (cancellous)** – trabeculae, **red marrow**.
- **Cells** – **osteoblasts (bone forming)**, **osteocytes (mature)**, **osteoclasts (bone resorbing – multinucleated)**.
- **Ossification** – **intramembranous (flat skull bones, clavicle)**, **endochondral (from cartilage – long bones)**.
- **Red marrow in adults** – **flat bones (sternum, ilium, vertebrae, ribs, skull)**, proximal femur/ humerus; **bone marrow aspiration – posterior superior iliac spine (adult), sternum; tibia in infants**.

## 2.2 Axial Skeleton (80)

### Skull (22 bones) + hyoid + ear ossicles (6)
- **Cranial bones (8)** – **frontal (1), parietal (2), temporal (2), occipital (1), sphenoid (1), ethmoid (1)**.
- **Facial bones (14)** – **maxilla (2), zygomatic (2), nasal (2), lacrimal (2), palatine (2), inferior nasal concha (2), vomer (1), mandible (1 – only movable skull bone, largest & strongest facial bone)**.
- **Sutures** – **coronal** (frontal–parietal), **sagittal** (between parietals), **lambdoid** (parietal–occipital), squamous.
- **Foramen magnum** – in occipital – spinal cord/ medulla passes.
- **Ear ossicles** – **malleus, incus, stapes** (3 each side = 6).

### Fontanelles (newborn)
| Fontanelle | Shape | Closes |
|---|---|---|
| **Anterior (bregma)** | **Diamond**, largest (~2.5 × 3.5 cm) | **12–18 months** |
| **Posterior (lambda)** | **Triangular** | **6–8 weeks (by 2–3 months)** |

- **Bulging** – raised ICP (meningitis, hydrocephalus); **depressed/ sunken** – **dehydration**; delayed closure – **rickets, hypothyroidism, hydrocephalus**; early closure – craniosynostosis/ microcephaly.

### Vertebral column (26 in adult; 33 in child)
| Region | Number | Features |
|---|---|---|
| **Cervical** | **7** | **Foramen transversarium**; **C1 – atlas (no body, "yes" movement)**, **C2 – axis (dens/ odontoid process – "no" rotation)**, **C7 – vertebra prominens** |
| **Thoracic** | **12** | **Articulate with ribs** |
| **Lumbar** | **5** | **Largest bodies**; LP done at **L3–L4/ L4–L5** |
| **Sacrum** | 5 fused → 1 | |
| **Coccyx** | 4 fused → 1 | |

- **Curvatures** – **primary (concave anteriorly) – thoracic & sacral (fetal)**; **secondary – cervical (head holding ~3 months) & lumbar (walking ~12 months)**.
- **Abnormal** – **kyphosis (hunchback – ↑ thoracic)**, **lordosis (↑ lumbar – pregnancy)**, **scoliosis (lateral curvature – most common in adolescent girls)**.
- **Intervertebral disc** – **nucleus pulposus (inner, gel) + annulus fibrosus (outer)**; **disc prolapse commonest at L4–L5/ L5–S1**.

### Thoracic cage
- **Sternum** – **manubrium, body, xiphoid process**; **sternal angle (angle of Louis) – 2nd rib level** (counting ribs, T4/5); **CPR compressions – lower half of sternum**.
- **Ribs – 12 pairs (24)** – **true ribs 1–7** (directly to sternum), **false ribs 8–10** (via costal cartilage of 7th), **floating ribs 11–12**; **cervical rib** (extra – C7) → thoracic outlet syndrome.

## 2.3 Appendicular Skeleton (126)

| Part | Bones |
|---|---|
| **Pectoral girdle** | **Clavicle (collar bone – S-shaped, horizontal long bone with no medullary cavity)**, **scapula (shoulder blade)** |
| **Upper limb (30 each)** | **Humerus**, **radius (lateral – thumb side)**, **ulna (medial; olecranon = elbow tip)**, **8 carpals** (**scaphoid – most commonly fractured carpal**; "**S**ome **L**overs **T**ry **P**ositions **T**hat **T**hey **C**an't **H**andle" – scaphoid, lunate, triquetrum, pisiform, trapezium, trapezoid, capitate, hamate), **5 metacarpals, 14 phalanges** |
| **Pelvic girdle** | **2 hip bones (ilium + ischium + pubis fused at acetabulum)**, sacrum, coccyx; **female pelvis – wider, shallower, round inlet (gynaecoid), subpubic angle > 90°**; male – android, narrow (< 70°) |
| **Lower limb (30 each)** | **Femur** (head, neck – **neck of femur fracture in elderly women – osteoporosis**), **patella**, **tibia (medial, weight-bearing; shin; medial malleolus)**, **fibula (lateral; lateral malleolus; non-weight bearing)**, **7 tarsals (calcaneus – largest, heel; talus – articulates with tibia)**, **5 metatarsals, 14 phalanges** |

- **Ischial tuberosity** – bone we sit on; **iliac crest** – highest point of hip (L4 level – landmark for LP); **anterior superior iliac spine (ASIS)**.
- **Arches of foot** – medial longitudinal (highest), lateral, transverse; **flat foot** – loss of arch.

## 2.4 Joints (Articulations)

| Type (structural) | Movement | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Fibrous** | **Immovable (synarthrosis)** | **Skull sutures**, gomphosis (**teeth in socket**), syndesmosis (distal tibiofibular) |
| **Cartilaginous** | **Slightly movable (amphiarthrosis)** | **Pubic symphysis, intervertebral discs** (secondary/ symphysis); **epiphyseal plate, 1st sternocostal** (primary/ synchondrosis) |
| **Synovial** | **Freely movable (diarthrosis)** | Most limb joints – **synovial membrane, synovial fluid, articular cartilage, capsule, ligaments**, bursae |

| Synovial type | Example |
|---|---|
| **Ball & socket** (most movement – multiaxial) | **Shoulder (most mobile, most commonly dislocated)**, **hip** |
| **Hinge** (uniaxial – flexion/ extension) | **Elbow, knee (largest joint – modified hinge), ankle, interphalangeal** |
| **Pivot** (rotation) | **Atlanto-axial (C1–C2 – "no")**, proximal radio-ulnar |
| **Condyloid (ellipsoid)** | **Wrist (radiocarpal)**, metacarpophalangeal |
| **Saddle** | **Carpometacarpal joint of thumb** (opposition) |
| **Gliding (plane)** | **Intercarpal, intertarsal**, acromioclavicular |

- **Movements** – **flexion** (↓ angle), **extension**, **hyperextension**, **abduction** (away from midline), **adduction** (towards), **rotation**, **circumduction**, **pronation** (palm down), **supination** (palm up – "soup"), **inversion/ eversion** (foot), **dorsiflexion/ plantar flexion**, **protraction/ retraction**, **opposition (thumb)**.
- **Knee** – **menisci (medial more often injured)**, **cruciate ligaments (ACL – sports injury)**, collateral ligaments; **largest & most complex joint**.
- **Ligament** – bone to bone; **tendon** – muscle to bone (**Achilles – strongest/ thickest tendon**).

## 2.5 Bone Physiology & Calcium

- **Normal serum calcium 8.5–10.5 mg/dl**; ↑ by **PTH (parathormone – bone resorption, renal reabsorption, activates vitamin D)** and **calcitriol (active vitamin D – 1,25-(OH)₂D₃, made in kidney; intestinal absorption)**; ↓ by **calcitonin (thyroid C cells – inhibits osteoclasts)**.
- **Vitamin D deficiency** – **rickets (children), osteomalacia (adults)**; **osteoporosis** – ↓ bone mass (post-menopausal – ↓ oestrogen; **DEXA scan; T-score ≤ −2.5**).
- **Bone healing stages** – haematoma → granulation/ inflammation → **soft callus** → **hard (bony) callus** → **remodelling**.
- **Growth** – GH, thyroxine, sex hormones (**puberty – epiphyseal fusion**), vitamin A, C (collagen), D.
