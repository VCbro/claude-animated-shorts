## 7.1 Overview

- **Alimentary canal** (≈ **9 m**, mouth → anus) + **accessory organs** (teeth, tongue, salivary glands, liver, gall bladder, pancreas).
- **Processes** – ingestion, propulsion (**peristalsis**), mechanical digestion, chemical digestion (enzymes – **hydrolysis**), absorption, defaecation.
- **Wall layers (oesophagus → anus)** – **mucosa** (innermost), **submucosa (Meissner's plexus – secretion)**, **muscularis externa (inner circular + outer longitudinal; Auerbach's/ myenteric plexus – motility)**, **serosa/ adventitia**; **stomach has extra oblique layer**.
- **Peritoneum** – largest serous membrane; **greater omentum ("policeman of abdomen" – walls off infection)**, lesser omentum, mesentery; **retroperitoneal organs – kidneys, pancreas (mostly), duodenum (most), ascending & descending colon, aorta, IVC**.

## 7.2 Mouth, Teeth & Salivary Glands

- **Teeth** – **deciduous (milk) – 20** (erupt **6 months – 2.5 yrs**; first – **lower central incisor ~6 months**); **permanent – 32** (first – **first molar ~6 yrs**; last – **third molar/ wisdom 17–25 yrs**); **dental formula – permanent 2-1-2-3 (incisor, canine, premolar, molar) per quadrant**; deciduous **2-1-0-2** (no premolars).
- **Tooth structure** – **enamel (hardest substance in body)**, dentine, pulp (nerves, vessels), cementum; **fluoride** strengthens enamel (excess – fluorosis).
- **Tongue** – skeletal muscle; **taste buds on papillae** (circumvallate – largest, V-shaped, back; fungiform; foliate; filiform – no taste buds); **5 tastes – sweet, salty, sour, bitter, umami**; **taste – facial nerve (anterior ⅔ – chorda tympani), glossopharyngeal (posterior ⅓)**; **motor – hypoglossal (XII)**; general sensation anterior ⅔ – lingual nerve (V3).

| Salivary gland | Duct | Secretion | Point |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Parotid (largest)** | **Stensen's duct – opens opposite upper 2nd molar** | **Serous (watery, amylase)** | **Mumps**; facial nerve passes through |
| **Submandibular** | **Wharton's duct** – floor of mouth | Mixed – **maximum (~70 %) of saliva** | **Commonest site of salivary stones** |
| **Sublingual (smallest)** | Ducts of Rivinus | Mainly mucous | – |

- **Saliva** – **1–1.5 L/day**, pH **~6.8**; contains **salivary amylase (ptyalin – starch → maltose)**, lingual lipase, **lysozyme (antibacterial)**, IgA, mucus; secretion controlled by **ANS (parasympathetic – profuse watery)**.
- **Swallowing (deglutition)** – **oral (voluntary) → pharyngeal (involuntary – soft palate closes nasopharynx, epiglottis closes larynx, breathing stops) → oesophageal (peristalsis)**; **centre – medulla**.

## 7.3 Oesophagus & Stomach

- **Oesophagus** – **~25 cm**, C6 → T11; **upper ⅓ skeletal, middle mixed, lower ⅓ smooth muscle**; **4 constrictions** (at **15 cm – cricopharynx, narrowest**; 22.5 cm – aortic arch; 27.5 cm – left bronchus; **40 cm from incisors – diaphragm/ cardia**); **lower oesophageal sphincter** prevents reflux (GERD if weak); NG tube length – **nose-ear-xiphisternum (NEX)**.
- **Stomach** – J-shaped; **left hypochondrium & epigastrium**; parts – **cardia, fundus, body, antrum, pylorus (pyloric sphincter)**; **capacity ~1–1.5 L (up to 2–4 L)**; **rugae**; newborn capacity ~ **20–30 ml**.

| Gastric cell | Secretion |
|---|---|
| **Parietal (oxyntic)** | **HCl** (pH 1.5–3.5 – kills microbes, activates pepsin) & **intrinsic factor (vitamin B12 absorption – deficiency → pernicious anaemia)** |
| **Chief (peptic/ zymogen)** | **Pepsinogen → pepsin (protein → peptides)**, gastric lipase; **rennin (infants – curdles milk)** |
| **Mucous (neck) cells** | **Mucus + bicarbonate** – protect mucosa |
| **G cells (antrum)** | **Gastrin** – ↑ HCl & motility |
| **D cells** | Somatostatin (inhibits) |

- **Gastric juice ≈ 2–3 L/day**; **phases of secretion – cephalic (sight/ smell – vagus, ~30 %), gastric (food in stomach – most ~60 %), intestinal**.
- **Gastric emptying ≈ 2–4 hrs** – **fastest carbohydrates, slowest fats**; **chyme**; vomiting centre – **medulla (CTZ – area postrema)**.
- **Absorption in stomach** – minimal: **alcohol, water, aspirin**, some drugs.

## 7.4 Small Intestine

- **Length ≈ 6–7 m (longest part of GI tract)**; **duodenum (~25 cm, C-shaped, shortest & widest; 2nd part – ampulla of Vater/ major duodenal papilla – common bile duct + pancreatic duct, with sphincter of Oddi)**, **jejunum (~2.5 m)**, **ileum (~3.5 m – Peyer's patches; ends at ileocaecal valve)**.
- **Surface area ↑ by** – **circular folds (plicae circulares), villi (with lacteals & capillaries), microvilli (brush border)** → ~**200–250 m²**.
- **Succus entericus (intestinal juice)** – ~1.5–3 L/day; **brush-border enzymes** – **maltase, sucrase, lactase (deficiency – lactose intolerance)**, peptidases, enterokinase (activates trypsinogen).
- **Hormones** – **secretin (S cells – stimulates bicarbonate-rich pancreatic juice; first hormone discovered – Bayliss & Starling 1902)**, **cholecystokinin (CCK – I cells – gall bladder contraction & pancreatic enzyme secretion)**, GIP (↑ insulin), motilin (motility).
- **Absorption sites** – **most nutrients – jejunum**; **iron, calcium, folate – duodenum (& upper jejunum)**; **vitamin B12 (with IF) & bile salts – terminal ileum**; **fats – lacteals → lymph (chylomicrons)**; **glucose & galactose – Na⁺-glucose co-transport (SGLT-1 – basis of ORS)**; fructose – facilitated diffusion.
- **Movements** – **segmentation (mixing)**, **peristalsis (propulsion)**; **migrating motor complex**.

## 7.5 Large Intestine

- **Length ≈ 1.5 m**; **caecum (with vermiform appendix – RIF; McBurney's point – ⅓ from ASIS to umbilicus)** → **ascending → transverse → descending → sigmoid colon → rectum (~12–15 cm) → anal canal (~4 cm)**; **taeniae coli (3 bands), haustra, appendices epiploicae**.
- **Functions** – **absorption of water (~1–1.5 L/day) & electrolytes (Na⁺)**, **synthesis of vitamin K & some B vitamins (biotin) by gut flora**, formation & storage of faeces, mucus secretion.
- **Faeces** – ~100–200 g/day; **brown colour – stercobilin**; odour – indole, skatole; ~75 % water.
- **Defaecation reflex** – rectal distension → **internal anal sphincter (involuntary, smooth)** relaxes; **external anal sphincter (voluntary, skeletal – pudendal nerve)**; **gastrocolic reflex** – urge after meals (bowel training).
- **Anal canal** – **pectinate (dentate) line** – above: visceral, painless (internal haemorrhoids); below: somatic, painful (external haemorrhoids).

## 7.6 Liver & Biliary System

- **Liver** – **largest internal organ & largest gland (~1.2–1.5 kg)**; **right hypochondrium, epigastrium**; **4 lobes (right – largest, left, caudate, quadrate)**; **Glisson's capsule**; **functional unit – hepatic lobule (hexagonal; central vein, portal triad – hepatic artery, portal vein, bile duct)**; **Kupffer cells** – macrophages.
- **Dual blood supply** – **portal vein (~75 % – nutrient-rich from gut)** + **hepatic artery (~25 % – oxygenated)**; drains via **hepatic veins → IVC**; receives **~25 % of cardiac output**.
- **Functions** – **bile production (~0.5–1 L/day)**; **carbohydrate metabolism (glycogenesis, glycogenolysis, gluconeogenesis)**; **protein – albumin, clotting factors (I, II, V, VII, IX, X), deamination & urea formation (ornithine cycle)**; **fat – cholesterol, lipoproteins, β-oxidation, ketone bodies**; **storage – glycogen, vitamins A, D, B12, K, iron (ferritin), copper**; **detoxification & drug metabolism**, hormone inactivation, **bilirubin conjugation**, **heat production**, fetal haemopoiesis, Kupffer cell phagocytosis.
- **Regenerates** even after 70 % resection.
- **Bile** – **no digestive enzyme**; contains **bile salts (emulsify fats, help absorption of fat & fat-soluble vitamins A, D, E, K)**, bile pigments (bilirubin), cholesterol, lecithin; **enterohepatic circulation** (~95 % bile salts reabsorbed in terminal ileum).
- **Gall bladder** – pear-shaped, **stores & concentrates bile (~30–50 ml capacity)**; **CCK** → contraction; **ducts – right & left hepatic → common hepatic + cystic → common bile duct (CBD) → ampulla of Vater**; **Murphy's sign** (cholecystitis); **Calot's triangle**; gallstones – "**fat, female, fertile, forty, fair**".

## 7.7 Pancreas

- **Mixed gland** – retroperitoneal, behind stomach; **head (in C-loop of duodenum), neck, body, tail (touches spleen)**; ~ 15 cm.
- **Exocrine (~98–99 %) – acini** – **pancreatic juice ~1.2–1.5 L/day, alkaline (pH ~8, rich in HCO₃⁻)** – **trypsinogen (→ trypsin by enterokinase), chymotrypsinogen, carboxypeptidase**, **pancreatic amylase**, **pancreatic lipase (main fat-digesting enzyme)**, nucleases; **duct of Wirsung (main) & Santorini (accessory)**.
- **Endocrine (~1–2 %) – islets of Langerhans (~1 million)** – **α cells – glucagon (↑ glucose)**, **β cells (~70 %) – insulin (↓ glucose)**, **δ cells – somatostatin**, PP/F cells – pancreatic polypeptide.
- **Serum amylase & lipase ↑ – acute pancreatitis** (lipase more specific).

## 7.8 Digestion Summary & Metabolism

| Nutrient | Enzyme (site) | End product |
|---|---|---|
| **Carbohydrates** | Salivary amylase (mouth) → pancreatic amylase → maltase, sucrase, lactase (brush border) | **Monosaccharides – glucose, fructose, galactose** |
| **Proteins** | **Pepsin (stomach)** → trypsin, chymotrypsin, carboxypeptidase (pancreas) → peptidases | **Amino acids** |
| **Fats** | Bile emulsifies → **pancreatic lipase** (main) | **Fatty acids + glycerol (monoglycerides)** |
| **Nucleic acids** | Nucleases | Nucleotides → bases, sugars |

- **Energy value** – **carbohydrate 4 kcal/g, protein 4 kcal/g, fat 9 kcal/g**, alcohol 7 kcal/g.
- **BMR** – energy at complete rest (~1 kcal/kg/hr; ↑ by thyroxine, fever – 7 % per °F/ ~13 % per °C, pregnancy, children; ↓ in sleep, starvation, hypothyroidism); **highest BMR per kg – infants**.
- **Glycolysis** – glucose → pyruvate (cytoplasm, 2 ATP net); **Krebs (TCA) cycle** – mitochondria; **ETC** – most ATP (~30–32 ATP/glucose total).
- **Ketone bodies** (acetoacetate, β-hydroxybutyrate, acetone) – in **starvation, uncontrolled DM** (fat breakdown).
- **Normal fasting blood glucose 70–100 mg/dl**; post-prandial < 140; **storage form – glycogen (liver & muscle)**.
