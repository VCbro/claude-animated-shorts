## 3.1 Types of Muscle

| Feature | **Skeletal** | **Cardiac** | **Smooth** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Control | **Voluntary** | **Involuntary** | **Involuntary** |
| Striations | **Present** | **Present** | **Absent** |
| Shape / nuclei | Long cylindrical, **multinucleated (peripheral)** | Branched, **1–2 central nuclei**, **intercalated discs** (gap junctions) | **Spindle-shaped, single central nucleus** |
| Nerve supply | Somatic | Autonomic (intrinsic – SA node) | Autonomic |
| Fatigue | Fatigues quickly | **Doesn't fatigue (long refractory period – no tetany)** | Slow, sustained |
| Location | Attached to bones | **Heart only** | **Walls of viscera, blood vessels, iris, uterus** |

- Body has **~600–650 skeletal muscles**; ≈ **40 % of body weight**.
- **Largest muscle – gluteus maximus**; **longest – sartorius**; **smallest – stapedius**; **strongest (by force/ weight) – masseter**; **largest smooth muscle organ – uterus (pregnancy)**; **most active – extraocular muscles**; **broadest – latissimus dorsi**; **hardest working – heart (myocardium)**.

## 3.2 Structure of Skeletal Muscle

- **Muscle → fascicles (perimysium) → muscle fibres/ cells (endomysium) → myofibrils → sarcomeres**; whole muscle covered by **epimysium**.
- **Sarcolemma** (cell membrane), **sarcoplasm**, **sarcoplasmic reticulum (stores Ca²⁺)**, **T-tubules**.
- **Sarcomere – functional (contractile) unit** – between two **Z lines**; **thick filament – myosin (A band – dark, anisotropic)**, **thin filament – actin + troponin + tropomyosin (I band – light, isotropic)**; **H zone** (only myosin), **M line**.

### Neuromuscular junction & contraction
1. Nerve impulse → **acetylcholine (ACh)** released at **NMJ (motor end plate)**.
2. ACh binds **nicotinic receptors** → muscle action potential; ACh broken by **acetylcholinesterase**.
3. Impulse via T-tubules → **Ca²⁺ released from sarcoplasmic reticulum**.
4. **Ca²⁺ binds troponin** → tropomyosin shifts → actin binding sites exposed.
5. **Myosin heads (cross-bridges) bind actin & pull (power stroke) – ATP needed** → **sliding filament theory (Huxley)**: **I band & H zone shorten, A band length constant**.
6. Relaxation – Ca²⁺ pumped back (ATP needed).
- **Rigor mortis** – after death, **no ATP → myosin can't detach** (starts 2–6 hrs, lasts ~24–48 hrs).
- **Myasthenia gravis** – **autoantibodies against ACh receptors** (ptosis, fatigue ↑ evening).

### Energy & physiology
- Sources – **creatine phosphate (first seconds)**, **anaerobic glycolysis → lactic acid (fatigue, cramps)**, **aerobic respiration (most ATP)**; **oxygen debt**; **myoglobin** – O₂ store in muscle (red fibres).
- **Motor unit** – one motor neuron + all fibres it supplies (eye – few fibres; thigh – many).
- **Muscle tone** – partial sustained contraction; **tetanus (physiological)** – sustained contraction from rapid stimuli; **all-or-none law** (single fibre).
- **Isotonic contraction** – length changes (concentric/ eccentric – lifting); **isometric** – tension ↑, length same (**pushing wall – isometric exercises for immobilised patients, quadriceps setting**).
- **Atrophy** (disuse, ↓ size), **hypertrophy** (exercise – ↑ size of fibres), **contracture**, **spasm, cramp**, **fibrillation**, **muscular dystrophy (Duchenne – X-linked, Gower's sign, boys)**.

## 3.3 Important Muscles

| Region | Muscle | Action / point |
|---|---|---|
| Face | **Orbicularis oculi** (closes eye), **orbicularis oris** (lips – "kissing muscle"), **buccinator** (cheek – blowing, sucking), frontalis | **Facial nerve (VII)** |
| Mastication | **Masseter, temporalis, pterygoids** | **Trigeminal (V3)**; masseter – strongest |
| Neck | **Sternocleidomastoid** (turns head to opposite side – **torticollis/ wry neck**), trapezius (shrug – **accessory XI**) | – |
| Respiration | **Diaphragm (main – 75 %, phrenic nerve C3–C5)**, **external intercostals (inspiration)**, internal intercostals (forced expiration); **accessory – SCM, scalene, pectoralis minor** (dyspnoea) | Abdominal muscles – forced expiration, cough |
| Abdomen | **Rectus abdominis** (linea alba, "six-pack"), external & internal oblique, transversus abdominis | – |
| Shoulder | **Deltoid** (abduction 15–90°; **IM site – axillary nerve**), **rotator cuff – SITS: supraspinatus (initiates abduction 0–15°), infraspinatus, teres minor, subscapularis** | – |
| Arm | **Biceps brachii** (flexion of elbow, supination), **triceps** (extension) | Antagonists |
| Back | **Latissimus dorsi** (broadest; "climbing"), erector spinae | – |
| Hip/ buttock | **Gluteus maximus** (extension – largest), **gluteus medius & minimus** (abduction; **ventrogluteal IM site**) | **Sciatic nerve** – avoid in dorsogluteal |
| Thigh | **Quadriceps femoris** (rectus femoris, vastus lateralis, medialis, intermedius – **knee extension**; **vastus lateralis – infant IM site**), **hamstrings** (knee flexion), **sartorius (longest – "tailor's muscle")**, adductors | – |
| Leg | **Gastrocnemius + soleus (calf – plantar flexion; Achilles tendon)**, tibialis anterior (dorsiflexion – foot drop if paralysed – common peroneal nerve) | – |
| Pelvic floor | **Levator ani** (pubococcygeus – **Kegel exercises**) | Supports pelvic organs |

### IM injection sites (anatomy link)
- **Deltoid** – **2–3 fingers (≈ 2.5–5 cm) below acromion**; **max 1 ml (≤ 2)**; avoid **radial & axillary nerve**.
- **Ventrogluteal** – **palm on greater trochanter, index on ASIS, middle finger along iliac crest – inject in V**; **safest (no major nerves/ vessels)**.
- **Dorsogluteal** – **upper outer quadrant** – risk of **sciatic nerve injury** (avoid in < 3 yrs).
- **Vastus lateralis** – **middle third of anterolateral thigh** – **site of choice in infants**.
- **Rectus femoris** – middle third anterior thigh (self-injection).
