## 10.1 The Eye

- **Eyeball** – ~**24 mm** diameter; in **orbit** (7 bones); **protective structures** – eyelids, eyelashes, conjunctiva, **lacrimal apparatus (lacrimal gland – upper outer orbit → puncta → canaliculi → lacrimal sac → nasolacrimal duct → inferior meatus of nose)**; tears contain **lysozyme**.
- **Extraocular muscles (6)** – 4 recti (superior, inferior, medial, lateral) + 2 obliques; **"LR6 SO4, rest 3"** – lateral rectus by VI, superior oblique by IV, others by III.

| Layer | Parts | Points |
|---|---|---|
| **Outer fibrous** | **Sclera** (white, posterior 5/6), **cornea** (transparent, anterior 1/6) | **Cornea – avascular (nourished by aqueous & tears), most refractive surface (~⅔ of power), highly sensitive (CN V) – corneal transplant success**; limbus – junction |
| **Middle vascular (uvea)** | **Choroid** (vascular – nourishes retina), **ciliary body** (ciliary muscle – **accommodation**; ciliary processes – **aqueous humour**), **iris** (coloured; **pupil** – sphincter pupillae (parasympathetic – constriction), dilator pupillae (sympathetic – dilation)) | Uveitis |
| **Inner nervous** | **Retina** – **rods & cones** | – |

### Retina
- **Rods (~120 million)** – **dim light/ night (scotopic) vision**, peripheral, **rhodopsin (visual purple – needs vitamin A → night blindness in deficiency)**.
- **Cones (~6–7 million)** – **bright light, colour vision (photopic) & sharp vision**; 3 types (red, green, blue); **colour blindness – X-linked (red-green most common)** – **Ishihara chart**.
- **Macula lutea** – yellow spot; **fovea centralis – only cones – point of sharpest vision (maximum acuity)**.
- **Optic disc – "blind spot" – no photoreceptors** (optic nerve exit); **papilloedema – ↑ ICP**.
- **Light pathway** – cornea → aqueous humour → pupil → lens → vitreous humour → retina.

### Lens, humours & accommodation
- **Lens** – **biconvex, transparent, avascular, elastic**; held by **suspensory ligaments (zonules)**; **opacity → cataract (commonest cause of blindness in India)**.
- **Aqueous humour** – **formed by ciliary processes** → posterior chamber → pupil → anterior chamber → **drained via trabecular meshwork & canal of Schlemm** (at angle); maintains **IOP 10–21 mmHg (normal)**; ↑ IOP – **glaucoma** (tonometry).
- **Vitreous humour** – jelly behind lens; maintains shape; **not replaced**.
- **Accommodation (near vision)** – **ciliary muscle contracts → zonules relax → lens becomes more convex**, **pupil constricts, eyes converge** ("near triad"); **presbyopia** – age-related loss (~40+ yrs) – **convex lens**.

| Refractive error | Image | Correction |
|---|---|---|
| **Myopia (short-sight)** | Before retina (long eyeball) | **Concave (minus/ diverging) lens** |
| **Hypermetropia (long-sight)** | Behind retina (short eyeball) | **Convex (plus/ converging) lens** |
| **Astigmatism** | Irregular corneal curvature | **Cylindrical lens** |
| **Presbyopia** | Loss of accommodation (age) | **Convex lens (reading glasses)** |

- **Visual pathway** – retina → optic nerve → **optic chiasma (nasal fibres cross)** → optic tract → **lateral geniculate body (thalamus)** → optic radiation → **visual cortex (occipital)**; **pituitary tumour → bitemporal hemianopia**.
- **Normal visual acuity – 6/6 (Snellen chart at 6 m)**; **blindness (WHO/ India) – visual acuity < 3/60 in better eye**; near vision – Jaeger chart.
- **Light reflex** – light in one eye → **both pupils constrict (direct & consensual)**; afferent **II**, efferent **III**.
- **Vitamin A deficiency** – **night blindness (earliest) → conjunctival xerosis → Bitot's spots → corneal xerosis → keratomalacia**.

## 10.2 The Ear

| Part | Structures | Function |
|---|---|---|
| **External ear** | **Pinna (elastic cartilage)**, **external auditory canal (~2.5 cm, S-shaped; ceruminous glands – wax)** | Collects sound |
| **Middle ear (tympanic cavity)** | **Tympanic membrane (eardrum – pearly grey, cone of light antero-inferior)**, **ossicles – malleus (hammer), incus (anvil), stapes (stirrup – smallest bone; footplate on oval window)**; **Eustachian (auditory) tube – connects to nasopharynx – equalises pressure** (shorter, wider, horizontal in children → otitis media); muscles – **stapedius (smallest muscle – facial nerve)**, tensor tympani | **Amplifies (~20 ×) & transmits sound** |
| **Inner ear (labyrinth)** | **Cochlea (2¾ turns – organ of Corti – hearing receptors)**; **vestibule (utricle & saccule – linear acceleration & static balance)**; **semicircular canals (3 – rotational/ angular acceleration – dynamic balance)**; fluids – **perilymph (like ECF) & endolymph (high K⁺)** | **Hearing & balance** |

- **Hearing pathway** – sound → pinna → canal → tympanic membrane → ossicles → **oval window** → cochlear fluid → **basilar membrane → hair cells of organ of Corti** → **cochlear nerve (VIII)** → **auditory cortex (temporal)**; **round window** – releases pressure.
- **Human audible range – 20–20,000 Hz**; **speech 500–2000 Hz**; normal conversation **~60 dB**; **noise > 85 dB prolonged → noise-induced hearing loss (4000 Hz notch)**; pain at ~120–130 dB.
- **Tuning fork tests (512 Hz)** – **Rinne** (normal AC > BC – positive), **Weber** (lateralises to **affected ear in conductive loss**, to **better ear in sensorineural loss**).
- **Conductive deafness** – external/ middle ear (wax, otitis media, otosclerosis); **sensorineural** – cochlea/ nerve (presbycusis, noise, ototoxic drugs, rubella).
- **Ear drops/ examination** – **adult pinna up & back; child < 3 yrs down & back**.
- **Newborn hearing screening** – OAE/ BERA.

## 10.3 Smell & Taste

- **Olfaction** – **olfactory epithelium in roof of nasal cavity** → olfactory receptor cells (**bipolar neurons – the only neurons regularly replaced**) → **olfactory bulb** → olfactory tract → **temporal lobe (uncus)/ limbic system**; **only sense not relayed via thalamus**; **adaptation fast**; anosmia (COVID, head injury).
- **Taste (gustation)** – **taste buds (~10,000)** on papillae (**circumvallate – largest, most taste buds**, fungiform, foliate; **filiform – no taste buds, most numerous**); **5 basic tastes – sweet, salty, sour, bitter, umami**; nerves – **VII (anterior ⅔ via chorda tympani), IX (posterior ⅓), X (epiglottis)**; taste closely linked with smell (flavour); taste cortex – **insula/ parietal**.

## 10.4 Skin (Integumentary System)

- **Largest organ of body** – **~1.5–2 m², ~15–16 % body weight**; thickest – **palms & soles**; thinnest – **eyelids**.

| Layer | Features |
|---|---|
| **Epidermis** (stratified squamous keratinised, **avascular**) | Layers (deep → surface) – **stratum basale (germinativum – dividing cells, melanocytes), spinosum, granulosum, lucidum (only in thick skin – palms/ soles), corneum (dead keratinised – protection)** – "**Come, Let's Get Sun Burned**" (surface → deep); cells – **keratinocytes (90 %), melanocytes (melanin – skin colour, UV protection), Langerhans cells (immune), Merkel cells (touch)**; renewed every ~4 weeks |
| **Dermis** (connective tissue – collagen & elastin) | **Papillary** (fingerprints, Meissner's corpuscles) & **reticular** layers; **blood vessels, nerves, hair follicles, sweat & sebaceous glands**, receptors |
| **Hypodermis (subcutaneous)** | **Adipose & loose connective tissue** – insulation, energy store; **SC injection site** |

- **Glands** – **eccrine sweat glands** (all over, **most on palms, soles, forehead** – thermoregulation; watery), **apocrine sweat glands** (axilla, groin, areola – active after puberty; **body odour** by bacteria), **sebaceous glands (sebum – holocrine; absent on palms & soles; acne)**, **ceruminous (ear wax)**, **mammary (modified sweat glands)**.
- **Functions of skin** – **protection** (barrier, UV, microbes – acid mantle pH 4.5–5.5), **thermoregulation**, **sensation** (touch – Meissner's; pressure/ vibration – **Pacinian**; pain – free nerve endings; cold – Krause; heat – Ruffini), **excretion** (sweat – water, salt, urea), **vitamin D synthesis (7-dehydrocholesterol + UVB → cholecalciferol)**, absorption (lipid-soluble drugs – transdermal patches), **fluid balance**, immunity, storage (fat).
- **Skin colour** – **melanin** (brown-black; **same number of melanocytes in all races – activity differs**), **carotene** (yellow), **haemoglobin** (pink); **albinism – absence of melanin (tyrosinase deficiency)**; **vitiligo** – loss of melanocytes.
- **Hair** – **keratin**; shaft, root, follicle, **arrector pili (goosebumps – sympathetic)**; growth phases anagen (growth), catagen, telogen; **lanugo (fine fetal hair)**, vernix caseosa (cheesy coating of newborn).
- **Nails** – keratin plates; **lunula (white crescent)**; **grow ~3 mm/month (fingernails faster than toenails)**; **clubbing** (chronic hypoxia, lung cancer, bronchiectasis, cyanotic heart disease), **koilonychia (spoon nails – iron deficiency)**, **capillary refill < 2 (–3) s**.

### Body temperature regulation
- **Centre – hypothalamus (anterior – heat loss; posterior – heat conservation/ production)**.
- **Normal core temperature ~37 °C (98.6 °F)**; range **36.5–37.5 °C**; **oral 37 °C, rectal ~0.5 °C higher (most accurate of common sites – core), axillary ~0.5 °C lower**; **lowest ~early morning (2–6 AM), highest ~late afternoon/ evening (circadian)**; ↑ after ovulation (**progesterone, ~0.3–0.5 °C**).
- **Heat production** – **metabolism (liver most at rest)**, **muscle activity/ shivering (most during exercise)**, **brown fat (non-shivering thermogenesis – newborns; between scapulae, around kidneys)**, thyroxine, adrenaline.
- **Heat loss** – **radiation (~60 % – maximum at rest)**, **conduction**, **convection**, **evaporation (sweating – only way when environment hotter than body; main in exercise)**.
- **Fever (pyrexia)** – > 37.5–38 °C (pyrogens – IL-1, PGE2 reset hypothalamic set point); **hyperpyrexia > 41–41.5 °C (106 °F)**; **hypothermia < 35 °C**; newborns at risk of hypothermia (large surface area, little SC fat) – **"warm chain"**.
- **°F = (°C × 9/5) + 32**; **°C = (°F − 32) × 5/9**.
