## 5.1 Blood – General

- **Blood volume** – **~5–6 L in adult (≈ 7–8 % body weight; 70–80 ml/kg)**; **newborn ~80–85 ml/kg**; **pH 7.35–7.45**; specific gravity ~1.055; viscosity 4–5 times water.
- **Composition** – **plasma ≈ 55 %** + **formed elements ≈ 45 % (haematocrit/ PCV)**.
- **Plasma** – **91–92 % water**, proteins **7 % (6–8 g/dl)** – **albumin (3.5–5 g/dl; most abundant – oncotic pressure, transport; made in liver)**, **globulins (antibodies – γ-globulins, transport)**, **fibrinogen (clotting)**; **serum = plasma − clotting factors (fibrinogen)**.
- **Functions** – transport (O₂, CO₂, nutrients, hormones, waste), **protection (immunity, clotting)**, **regulation (pH buffer, temperature, fluid balance)**.
- **Haemopoiesis** – **fetus: yolk sac (3 wks) → liver (main, 2nd trimester) & spleen → bone marrow (from ~5th month)**; **adult – red marrow of flat bones**; all from **pluripotent haemopoietic stem cell**.

## 5.2 Red Blood Cells (Erythrocytes)

- **Biconcave disc, non-nucleated**, diameter **~7.5 µm**; **lifespan 120 days**; destroyed in **spleen ("graveyard of RBCs")**, liver, marrow.
- **Count – male 4.5–5.5 million/µl, female 4–5 million/µl**.
- **Erythropoiesis** – stimulated by **erythropoietin (kidney – in hypoxia)**; needs **iron, vitamin B12, folic acid**, protein, vitamin C, copper; stages – proerythroblast → … → **reticulocyte (0.5–2.5 %)** → RBC (~7 days).
- **Haemoglobin** – **male 13–17 g/dl, female 12–15 g/dl, newborn 15–20 g/dl**; **pregnancy anaemia < 11 g/dl**; **4 haem (iron Fe²⁺) + globin (2α + 2β in HbA)**; **1 g Hb carries 1.34 ml O₂**; **HbF (2α2γ) – higher O₂ affinity**; **HbS – sickle cell (valine replaces glutamic acid)**.
- **Breakdown** – Hb → haem → **biliverdin → bilirubin (unconjugated, fat-soluble) → liver conjugates (glucuronyl transferase) → bile → stercobilinogen (stool colour) & urobilinogen (urine)**; iron recycled (ferritin/ transferrin).
- **Indices** – **MCV 80–100 fl** (↓ **microcytic – iron deficiency, thalassaemia**; ↑ **macrocytic – B12/ folate deficiency**), MCH 27–32 pg, MCHC 32–36 g/dl; **ESR – Westergren (male 0–15, female 0–20 mm/hr)**.
- **Jaundice** when **serum bilirubin > 2–3 mg/dl** (normal 0.3–1.2); **newborn visible > 5 mg/dl**.

## 5.3 White Blood Cells (Leucocytes)

- **Total count – 4,000–11,000/µl**; **leucocytosis > 11,000**; **leucopenia < 4,000**.

| Type | % (DLC) | Function | ↑ in |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Neutrophils** (multilobed nucleus) | **50–70 % (most common)** | **First line – phagocytosis (pus)**, lifespan 6–8 hrs in blood | **Acute bacterial infection** |
| **Lymphocytes** | **20–40 %** | **Immunity – B (antibodies), T (cell-mediated), NK cells** | **Viral infections, TB**, CLL |
| **Monocytes** (largest WBC) | 2–8 % | **Become macrophages** in tissues (Kupffer cells – liver, microglia – brain, alveolar macrophages) | TB, chronic infection |
| **Eosinophils** (bilobed, red granules) | 1–4 % | **Allergy, parasites** | **Allergy, worm infestation, asthma** |
| **Basophils** (least common) | 0–1 % | **Histamine, heparin** (like mast cells) | Allergy, CML |

- **Granulocytes** – neutrophil, eosinophil, basophil; **agranulocytes** – lymphocyte, monocyte.
- "**Never Let Monkeys Eat Bananas**" – order of frequency (N > L > M > E > B).

## 5.4 Platelets & Haemostasis

- **Platelets (thrombocytes)** – fragments of **megakaryocytes**; **1.5–4.5 lakh/µl**; lifespan **7–10 days**; **thrombocytopenia < 1.5 lakh** (bleeding risk < 50,000; spontaneous < 20,000); **thrombopoietin (liver)**.
- **Haemostasis steps** – **1. vascular spasm → 2. platelet plug (adhesion via vWF, aggregation – ADP, TXA2) → 3. coagulation (fibrin clot) → 4. clot retraction → 5. fibrinolysis (plasmin)**.
- **Coagulation** – **intrinsic pathway (aPTT – factors XII, XI, IX, VIII; heparin)**, **extrinsic pathway (PT/ INR – tissue factor + VII; warfarin)** → **common pathway (X, V, II – prothrombin → thrombin; I – fibrinogen → fibrin; XIII stabilises)**.
- **Calcium = factor IV** (needed throughout; **citrate/ EDTA/ oxalate anticoagulants bind calcium**); **vitamin K-dependent factors – II, VII, IX, X** ("**1972**") + protein C & S – made in liver.
- **Normal values** – **bleeding time 2–7 min (Ivy)** (platelet function), **clotting time 4–10 min**, **PT 11–16 s, INR ~1**, **aPTT 25–35 s**.
- **Haemophilia A – factor VIII deficiency (X-linked recessive, males)**; **B (Christmas disease) – factor IX**; **von Willebrand disease – commonest inherited bleeding disorder**.
- **Natural anticoagulants** – **heparin (basophils, mast cells), antithrombin III, protein C & S**.

## 5.5 Blood Groups & Transfusion

- **ABO system – Karl Landsteiner (1900–01)**; **Rh – Landsteiner & Wiener (1940)**; **World Blood Donor Day – 14 June** (Landsteiner's birthday).

| Group | Antigen on RBC | Antibody in plasma | Can donate to | Can receive from |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | A | Anti-B | A, AB | A, O |
| **B** | B | Anti-A | B, AB | B, O |
| **AB** | A & B | **None** | AB | **All – universal recipient** |
| **O** | **None** | Anti-A & anti-B | **All – universal donor (RBC)** | O |

- **Plasma** – **AB – universal plasma donor**; **O – universal plasma recipient**.
- **Commonest blood groups in India – O+ and B+ (varies by region; B+ more in north)**; **Rh-positive ≈ 93–95 % in India**; **Bombay blood group (hh) – no H antigen** (first found in Mumbai, 1952).
- **Rh incompatibility** – **Rh−ve mother + Rh+ve fetus** → sensitisation (first pregnancy usually unaffected) → **haemolytic disease of newborn/ erythroblastosis fetalis** in later pregnancies → **anti-D immunoglobulin (300 µg) within 72 hrs of delivery/ abortion & at 28 weeks**.
- **Donation** – **age 18–65 yrs, weight ≥ 45 kg (350 ml) / ≥ 55 kg (450 ml), Hb ≥ 12.5 g/dl**; interval **3 months (men), 4 months (women)**; whole blood stored **2–6 °C**, with **CPDA-1 – 35 days** (SAGM – 42 days); **platelets 20–24 °C with agitation – 5 days**; **FFP −30 °C or below – 1 year**.
- **Cross-matching** – donor RBC + recipient serum (major); **Coombs test** – direct (antibody on RBC – HDN, AIHA), indirect (antibodies in serum – antenatal).

## 5.6 Lymphatic System

- **Functions** – return of **tissue fluid (~3 L/day) & proteins** to blood, **absorption of fats (lacteals – chyle)**, **immunity (lymphocytes)**.
- **Lymph flow** – capillaries → vessels (valves) → **nodes (filter)** → trunks → **thoracic duct (largest; drains left side + both lower limbs; starts at cisterna chyli, enters left subclavian–internal jugular junction)** & **right lymphatic duct (right upper quadrant)**.
- **Primary lymphoid organs** – **bone marrow (B cells mature)**, **thymus (T cells mature – large in children, involutes after puberty; "T" for thymus)**; **secondary** – lymph nodes, **spleen**, tonsils, **Peyer's patches (ileum)**, MALT, appendix.
- **Spleen** – **largest lymphoid organ**; **left hypochondrium, ribs 9–11**; **not normally palpable**; functions – **destroys old RBCs, filters blood, immune response, reservoir of blood/ platelets, haemopoiesis in fetus**; **post-splenectomy – risk of overwhelming infection (encapsulated – pneumococcus, H. influenzae, meningococcus) → vaccinate**; **ruptured spleen – Kehr's sign (left shoulder pain)**.
- **Tonsils** – palatine (most common "tonsils"), pharyngeal (**adenoids**), lingual.
- **Lymph nodes** – cervical, axillary (**breast cancer spread**), inguinal; **Virchow's node – left supraclavicular (gastric cancer – Troisier's sign)**.

## 5.7 Immunity

| Type | Details |
|---|---|
| **Innate (non-specific, natural)** | **Skin & mucous membranes (first line)**, **phagocytes (neutrophils, macrophages), NK cells, complement, inflammation, fever, interferons (second line)**; no memory |
| **Acquired (adaptive, specific)** | **Third line** – lymphocytes; **memory & specificity** |
| – **Humoral** | **B lymphocytes → plasma cells → antibodies** |
| – **Cell-mediated** | **T lymphocytes – helper (CD4 – HIV target), cytotoxic (CD8), regulatory, memory** – viruses, TB, transplant rejection, cancer, **type IV hypersensitivity (Mantoux)** |

| Acquired immunity | Active (own antibodies, long-lasting, delayed) | Passive (ready-made antibodies, immediate, short) |
|---|---|---|
| **Natural** | **Recovery from infection** | **Maternal antibodies – placenta (IgG) & breast milk/ colostrum (IgA)** |
| **Artificial** | **Vaccination** | **Antisera/ immunoglobulins (ATS, ASV, RIG, HBIG)** |

### Antibodies (immunoglobulins – Y-shaped; plasma cells)
| Ig | Key point |
|---|---|
| **IgG** | **Most abundant (~75 %)**; **only one crossing placenta**; secondary response; long-term |
| **IgM** | **Largest (pentamer)**; **first produced in primary response** (recent infection); ABO antibodies |
| **IgA** | **Secretions – colostrum, breast milk, saliva, tears, mucosa (dimer)** – secretory immunity |
| **IgE** | **Allergy/ anaphylaxis (type I), parasites**; binds mast cells |
| **IgD** | B-cell receptor |

- **Antigen** – substance inducing immune response; **hapten** – incomplete antigen; **vaccine** – live attenuated (BCG, OPV, MR, rotavirus), killed (IPV), toxoid (TT, DT), subunit/ recombinant (hepatitis B).
- **Hypersensitivity** – **Type I – anaphylactic (IgE – asthma, hay fever, anaphylaxis)**; **Type II – cytotoxic (IgG/ IgM – transfusion reaction, HDN, Graves', myasthenia)**; **Type III – immune complex (serum sickness, SLE, PSGN, Arthus)**; **Type IV – delayed, T-cell (Mantoux, contact dermatitis, graft rejection)** – "**ACID**".
- **Autoimmunity** – loss of self-tolerance (SLE, RA, type 1 DM, Hashimoto's).
