## 1.1 Basic Terminology

:::def Definitions
- **Anatomy** – study of **structure** of the body (Greek *ana* = up, *tome* = cutting); **Father of Anatomy – Andreas Vesalius** (*De Humani Corporis Fabrica*, 1543); **Father of Medicine – Hippocrates**.
- **Physiology** – study of **functions** of the body; **Father of (experimental) Physiology – Claude Bernard** ("milieu intérieur"); **William Harvey – discovered blood circulation (1628)**; **Walter Cannon – term "homeostasis"**.
- **Homeostasis** – maintenance of a **constant internal environment**; mostly by **negative feedback** (temperature, BP, blood glucose); **positive feedback** examples – **labour contractions (oxytocin), blood clotting, LH surge, milk ejection**.
:::

- **Levels of organisation** – chemical → **cell (structural & functional unit of life)** → tissue → organ → system → organism.
- **Anatomical position** – body **erect, face forward, arms by side, palms facing forward**, feet together.
- **Directional terms** – **superior/ cranial** (towards head), **inferior/ caudal**, **anterior/ ventral**, **posterior/ dorsal**, **medial** (towards midline), **lateral**, **proximal** (near origin/ trunk), **distal**, **superficial**, **deep**, **ipsilateral/ contralateral**.

| Plane | Divides body into |
|---|---|
| **Sagittal (median/ midsagittal)** | **Right & left** (equal halves if midsagittal) |
| **Frontal (coronal)** | **Anterior & posterior** |
| **Transverse (horizontal/ axial)** | **Superior & inferior** |
| Oblique | At an angle |

### Body cavities
- **Dorsal cavity** – **cranial (brain)** + **vertebral/ spinal canal (spinal cord)**.
- **Ventral cavity** – **thoracic** (2 pleural + **mediastinum** – heart in **pericardial** cavity, trachea, oesophagus, thymus, great vessels) separated by **diaphragm** from **abdominopelvic** cavity.
- **Abdomen – 9 regions** – **right hypochondrium** (liver, gall bladder), **epigastrium** (stomach, pancreas), **left hypochondrium** (spleen), **right lumbar, umbilical, left lumbar**, **right iliac (appendix – McBurney's point)**, **hypogastrium/ suprapubic** (bladder, uterus), **left iliac** (sigmoid colon); **4 quadrants** – RUQ, LUQ, RLQ, LLQ.

## 1.2 The Cell

- **Cell theory – Schleiden & Schwann (1838–39)**; **"cell" word – Robert Hooke (1665)**; human body ≈ **37 trillion cells**.
- **Largest cell – ovum**; **smallest – sperm (male gamete)/ RBC among smallest**; **longest – neuron**; cells without nucleus – **mature RBC, platelets**; multinucleated – **skeletal muscle, osteoclasts**.

| Organelle | Function |
|---|---|
| **Cell (plasma) membrane** | **Fluid mosaic model (Singer & Nicolson)** – phospholipid bilayer + proteins; **selectively permeable** |
| **Nucleus** | **Control centre**; contains DNA (chromatin), **nucleolus** – makes **ribosomes (rRNA)** |
| **Mitochondria** | **"Powerhouse" – ATP by aerobic respiration (Krebs cycle, ETC)**; have own DNA (**maternal inheritance**) |
| **Ribosomes** | **Protein synthesis** (free or on rough ER) |
| **Rough ER** | Protein synthesis & transport |
| **Smooth ER** | **Lipid & steroid synthesis, detoxification (liver), Ca²⁺ storage (sarcoplasmic reticulum in muscle)** |
| **Golgi apparatus** | **Packaging, modification & secretion ("post office")** |
| **Lysosomes** | **"Suicide bags" – digestive enzymes**, autophagy |
| **Peroxisomes** | Break down H₂O₂, fatty acids |
| **Centrosome/ centrioles** | **Spindle formation in cell division** (absent in mature neurons) |
| **Cilia/ flagella** | Movement (respiratory tract cilia, fallopian tube; sperm flagellum) |
| **Cytoskeleton** | Microtubules, microfilaments – shape & movement |

### Transport across membrane
- **Passive (no ATP)** – **diffusion** (high → low concentration; O₂, CO₂), **facilitated diffusion** (carrier – **glucose**), **osmosis** (**water** from low solute → high solute concentration across semipermeable membrane), **filtration** (pressure – **glomerulus, capillaries**).
- **Active (ATP)** – **Na⁺-K⁺ pump (3 Na⁺ out, 2 K⁺ in)**, **endocytosis** (phagocytosis – cell eating, pinocytosis – cell drinking), **exocytosis**.
- **Tonicity** – **isotonic (0.9 % NaCl, 5 % dextrose initially)** – no change; **hypotonic** – cell **swells/ bursts (haemolysis)**; **hypertonic** – cell **shrinks (crenation)**.
- **Main intracellular ion – K⁺ (and phosphate)**; **main extracellular ion – Na⁺ (and Cl⁻)**.

### Cell division & genetics
- **Mitosis** – somatic cells; **2 identical diploid (46) daughter cells**; phases **PMAT – prophase, metaphase (chromosomes best seen – karyotyping), anaphase, telophase**; interphase (G1, S – DNA replication, G2).
- **Meiosis** – gametes; **4 haploid (23) cells**; **crossing over** (prophase I) → variation.
- **Chromosomes – 46 (23 pairs: 22 autosomes + 1 sex)**; **male XY, female XX**; **DNA double helix – Watson & Crick (1953)**; bases **A=T (2 H-bonds), G≡C (3)**; RNA has **uracil** instead of thymine.
- **Gene** – unit of heredity; **genotype vs phenotype**; **dominant/ recessive**; **Down syndrome – trisomy 21**, **Turner – 45 XO**, **Klinefelter – 47 XXY**.

## 1.3 Tissues

| Tissue | Types / examples |
|---|---|
| **Epithelial** (covers, lines; avascular) | **Simple squamous** (alveoli, endothelium, Bowman's capsule), **simple cuboidal** (kidney tubules, thyroid follicles), **simple columnar** (stomach, intestine – with microvilli), **ciliated columnar** (fallopian tube), **pseudostratified ciliated columnar** (trachea – respiratory epithelium), **stratified squamous keratinised (skin)**, **non-keratinised (mouth, oesophagus, vagina, cornea)**, **transitional (urinary bladder, ureter – stretch)** |
| **Connective** (most abundant, supports) | **Loose (areolar), adipose**, dense (tendons, ligaments), **cartilage** (hyaline – most common: costal, trachea, articular; **elastic – ear pinna, epiglottis**; **fibrocartilage – intervertebral disc, pubic symphysis, menisci**), **bone**, **blood (fluid connective tissue)**, lymph |
| **Muscular** | **Skeletal** (voluntary, striated), **cardiac** (involuntary, striated, intercalated discs), **smooth** (involuntary, non-striated – viscera) |
| **Nervous** | Neurons + neuroglia |

- **Membranes** – **mucous** (lines openings to exterior), **serous** (**pleura, pericardium, peritoneum** – 2 layers, parietal & visceral), **synovial** (joints), **cutaneous (skin)**.
- **Glands** – **exocrine** (ducts – sweat, salivary), **endocrine** (ductless – hormones into blood), **mixed – pancreas, gonads**; **merocrine/ apocrine/ holocrine (sebaceous)**.
- **Regeneration** – epithelium, liver, bone, blood regenerate well; **neurons & cardiac muscle – poor**.
