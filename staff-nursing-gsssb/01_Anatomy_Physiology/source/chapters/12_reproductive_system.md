## 12.1 Male Reproductive System

- **Testes** – **in scrotum (temperature ~2–3 °C below body – needed for spermatogenesis)**; ~4–5 cm, oval; covered by **tunica vaginalis & tunica albuginea**; **descend through inguinal canal by 7th–8th month / at birth (gubernaculum)**; failure → **cryptorchidism (undescended – infertility, malignancy risk; orchiopexy by ~1 yr)**.
- **Seminiferous tubules** – **spermatogenesis**; **Sertoli cells (nurse/ sustentacular – support & nourish sperm, blood-testis barrier, inhibin, ABP – FSH-driven)**; **Leydig (interstitial) cells – testosterone (LH)**.
- **Spermatogenesis** – **~64–74 days**; spermatogonium (46) → primary spermatocyte → (meiosis I) → secondary spermatocyte (23) → (meiosis II) → spermatid → **spermiogenesis** → **spermatozoon**; **1 primary spermatocyte → 4 sperms**; begins at puberty, lifelong.
- **Sperm** – **head (nucleus + acrosome – enzymes – hyaluronidase to penetrate ovum)**, **middle piece (mitochondria – energy)**, tail (flagellum – motility); length ~55–60 µm; **viable in female tract ~48–72 hrs (up to 5 days)**.
- **Duct system** – **seminiferous tubules → rete testis → efferent ductules → epididymis (~6 m coiled; sperm maturation & storage, gain motility) → vas (ductus) deferens (~45 cm – passes through inguinal canal; cut in vasectomy) → joins seminal vesicle duct = ejaculatory duct → prostatic urethra**.
- **Accessory glands** – **seminal vesicles (~60–70 % of semen; alkaline, fructose – energy for sperm, prostaglandins)**, **prostate (~20–30 %; slightly acidic/ milky, citric acid, PSA, acid phosphatase; surrounds urethra below bladder – BPH (elderly) → urinary obstruction)**, **bulbourethral (Cowper's) glands (pre-ejaculate mucus – lubrication)**.
- **Semen (WHO 2021)** – **volume ≥ 1.4 ml (~2–5 ml)**, **sperm concentration ≥ 16 million/ml (~39 million total)**, **total motility ≥ 42 %**, progressive ≥ 30 %, normal forms ≥ 4 %; pH 7.2–8.0; **azoospermia** (none), **oligospermia** (low count).
- **Penis** – **corpora cavernosa (2) & corpus spongiosum (1 – urethra; glans)**; **erection – parasympathetic (NO, vasodilation)**, **ejaculation – sympathetic**; **prepuce (foreskin)** – phimosis.
- **Testosterone** – **male secondary sex characters**, muscle & bone mass, libido, spermatogenesis (with FSH), voice change (larynx), **epiphyseal closure**, ↑ RBC.

## 12.2 Female Reproductive System – Organs

### External genitalia (vulva)
- **Mons pubis**, **labia majora** (homologous to scrotum), **labia minora**, **clitoris (erectile – homologous to penis)**, **vestibule** (urethral & vaginal openings), **Bartholin's glands (greater vestibular – lubrication; cyst/ abscess)**, Skene's glands, hymen, **perineum (perineal body – episiotomy, tears)**.

### Internal organs
| Organ | Features |
|---|---|
| **Vagina** | **~7–10 cm fibromuscular canal** (anterior wall ~7.5 cm, posterior ~9 cm); **acidic pH 3.8–4.5 (Döderlein's bacilli/ lactobacilli – lactic acid from glycogen; oestrogen-dependent)**; **posterior fornix deepest** (culdocentesis); stratified squamous non-keratinised; no glands |
| **Uterus** | **Pear-shaped, hollow, muscular**; **~7.5 × 5 × 2.5 cm (3 × 2 × 1 inch), ~50–60 g (non-pregnant) → ~1000 g at term**; capacity ~5–10 ml → ~5 L at term; **normal position – anteverted & anteflexed**; parts – **fundus, body (corpus), isthmus (→ lower uterine segment in pregnancy), cervix**; layers – **perimetrium, myometrium (smooth muscle – "living ligatures" – oblique fibres in middle layer control bleeding after delivery), endometrium (functional layer – shed in menses; basal layer – regenerates)** |
| **Cervix** | **~2.5 cm**; **internal os & external os** (round in nulliparous, transverse slit in parous); **transformation zone (squamocolumnar junction) – site of cervical cancer → Pap smear**; cervical mucus changes (spinnbarkeit/ ferning at ovulation) |
| **Fallopian (uterine) tubes** | **~10–12 cm**; parts (lateral → medial) – **fimbriae, infundibulum, ampulla (widest, longest – site of fertilisation & commonest site of ectopic pregnancy), isthmus (narrowest – tubectomy site), interstitial (intramural)**; **ciliated columnar epithelium** + peristalsis move ovum |
| **Ovaries** | **Almond-shaped, ~3 × 2 × 1 cm**; on **ovarian fossa**; ligaments – ovarian & suspensory (infundibulopelvic – carries ovarian vessels); **cortex (follicles) & medulla**; produce **ova, oestrogen, progesterone**, inhibin, androgens |

- **Uterine supports** – **pelvic floor (levator ani)**, **cardinal (Mackenrodt's/ transverse cervical) ligaments – main support**, uterosacral ligaments, pubocervical; **round ligament (keeps anteversion; passes through inguinal canal)**; **broad ligament (peritoneal fold – not a true support)**; weakness → **uterine prolapse**.
- **Blood supply** – **uterine artery (from internal iliac) – crosses above ureter ("water under the bridge" – injury risk in hysterectomy)**; ovarian artery (from aorta).
- **Pelvis (female/ gynaecoid)** – **round inlet, wide subpubic arch (> 90°), shallow, ischial spines not prominent**; **true pelvis – inlet, cavity, outlet**; **diagonal conjugate ~12.5 cm (true/ obstetric conjugate ≈ diagonal − 1.5 cm ≈ 11 cm)**; **pouch of Douglas (rectouterine pouch) – lowest point of peritoneal cavity (fluid/ pus collects)**.

## 12.3 Oogenesis & Ovarian Cycle

- **Oogenesis begins before birth** – oogonia multiply in fetus; **primary oocytes arrested in prophase I** – **~7 million at 20 weeks fetal life (maximum) → ~1–2 million at birth → ~3–4 lakh at puberty → ~400–500 ovulated in lifetime**.
- **Meiosis I completes just before ovulation** (→ secondary oocyte + 1st polar body); **secondary oocyte arrested in metaphase II – completes meiosis II only if fertilised** (→ ovum + 2nd polar body); **1 primary oocyte → 1 ovum + 3 (or 2) polar bodies**.
- **Follicle development** – primordial → primary → secondary → **Graafian (mature, vesicular) follicle (~18–25 mm)** → **ovulation** → **corpus luteum (progesterone; lasts ~14 days if no pregnancy → corpus albicans)**; in pregnancy maintained by **hCG** till placenta takes over (~10–12 wks).
- **Ovum** – **largest human cell (~120–150 µm)**; **viable ~12–24 hrs after ovulation**; zona pellucida, corona radiata.

## 12.4 Menstrual Cycle

- **Menarche** – first menses (**~11–14 yrs in India**; average ~12–13); **menopause** – cessation for **12 consecutive months (~45–55 yrs; average ~51 worldwide, ~46–48 in India)**.
- **Normal cycle 28 days (21–35)**; **flow 3–5 days (2–7)**; **blood loss ~35 ml (20–80; > 80 ml – menorrhagia)**; **menstrual blood doesn't clot (fibrinolysins/ plasmin)**.

| Phase | Days (28-day cycle) | Ovary | Endometrium | Hormones |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Menstrual** | **1–5** | Corpus luteum regresses; new follicles recruited | **Functional layer shed** | **↓ oestrogen & progesterone** (trigger) |
| **Follicular/ Proliferative** | **6–14** | Follicle grows (FSH) → Graafian | **Proliferation – regeneration & thickening** | **Oestrogen ↑ (dominant)** |
| **Ovulation** | **~Day 14** (**14 days before next period**) | Graafian follicle ruptures | – | **LH surge (peak ~24–36 hrs before ovulation) – positive feedback by high oestrogen** |
| **Luteal/ Secretory** | **15–28** (constant **14 days**) | **Corpus luteum** | **Secretory – glands tortuous, glycogen-rich, oedematous – ready for implantation** | **Progesterone ↑ (dominant)**, oestrogen (2nd peak) |

- **Signs of ovulation** – **rise in BBT by ~0.3–0.5 °C (progesterone – thermogenic)**, **clear stretchy cervical mucus (spinnbarkeit; fern pattern)**, **mittelschmerz (mid-cycle pain)**, LH kits.
- **Fertile period** – ~**day 10–17** (5 days before to 1 day after ovulation); calendar/ standard days method (**days 8–19** – CycleBeads).
- **Oestrogen** – **proliferative phase**, female secondary characters (breast duct growth, fat distribution), **↑ cervical mucus (thin)**, maintains vaginal epithelium, bone protection, ↑ HDL, epiphyseal closure.
- **Progesterone ("pro-gestation" – pregnancy hormone)** – **secretory endometrium, maintains pregnancy (relaxes uterus – ↓ contractility)**, **thick cervical mucus**, **↑ BBT**, breast alveolar development, relaxes smooth muscle (constipation, heartburn, ureteric dilation in pregnancy).
- **Puberty sequence (girls)** – **thelarche (breast budding ~8–13 yrs – first sign) → pubarche → growth spurt → menarche**; Tanner staging.

## 12.5 Breast & Lactation

- **Modified sweat (apocrine) gland**; **over 2nd–6th ribs**, **axillary tail of Spence (upper outer quadrant – most glandular tissue – commonest site of breast cancer)**; **15–20 lobes** → lobules → **alveoli (milk secretion)** → lactiferous ducts → **lactiferous sinuses (under areola)** → nipple; **Montgomery's tubercles** (sebaceous glands on areola – enlarge in pregnancy); **Cooper's ligaments (suspensory – dimpling in cancer)**; **lymph drainage – ~75 % to axillary nodes**.
- **Lactation hormones** – **development: oestrogen (ducts), progesterone (alveoli), hPL, prolactin**; **milk production – prolactin** (inhibited during pregnancy by high oestrogen & progesterone; starts after placenta delivery); **milk ejection (let-down) – oxytocin**; **suckling stimulates both (neuroendocrine reflex)**; prolactin also suppresses ovulation (**LAM – lactational amenorrhoea method, 6 months, exclusive breastfeeding**).
- **Colostrum** – first **2–4 days**, **yellow, thick**, **rich in protein, IgA, vitamin A, WBCs**, less fat & sugar – **"first immunisation"**; transitional milk (~day 5–14) → mature milk; **foremilk (watery – thirst) & hindmilk (fat-rich – satiety, weight gain)**; daily output ~500–800 ml.

## 12.6 Fertilisation, Implantation & Placenta

- **Fertilisation** – fusion of sperm & ovum in **ampulla** of fallopian tube within ~**12–24 hrs of ovulation**; **capacitation** of sperm (in female tract) → **acrosome reaction** → zona pellucida penetration → **zona reaction (prevents polyspermy)** → **zygote (46 chromosomes; sex determined by sperm – X or Y)**.
- **Cleavage** – zygote → 2, 4, 8 cells → **morula (16 cells, ~day 3–4, enters uterus)** → **blastocyst (~day 5; inner cell mass → embryo; trophoblast → placenta)**.
- **Implantation** – **~day 6–7 after fertilisation (complete by ~day 10–12)**; usually **upper posterior wall of uterine body (fundus)**; endometrium now called **decidua** (basalis – maternal placenta, capsularis, parietalis/ vera); implantation bleeding (Hartman's sign).
- **Embryonic period – up to 8 weeks (organogenesis, 3–8 wks – most teratogen-sensitive)**; **fetal period – 9 weeks to birth**; **heart starts beating ~day 22 (5–6 weeks LMP on USG)**.
- **Germ layers** – **ectoderm (skin/ epidermis, nervous system, eye lens, enamel, hair, nails)**, **mesoderm (muscles, bones, heart, blood, kidneys, gonads, dermis)**, **endoderm (lining of GI & respiratory tracts, liver, pancreas, thyroid, bladder lining)**.
- **Placenta** – **discoid, ~15–20 cm diameter, ~2.5–3 cm thick, ~500 g (≈ ⅙ of baby's weight) at term**; **fetal surface – smooth, shiny (amnion), cord inserted (Schultze)**; **maternal surface – rough, 15–20 cotyledons (Duncan)**; functions – **respiratory, nutritive, excretory, barrier (partial), endocrine (hCG, hPL, oestrogen, progesterone), immunological (IgG transfer)**; formed fully by ~12 weeks.
- **Umbilical cord** – **~50 cm (30–100)**, **2 arteries + 1 vein**, **Wharton's jelly**.
- **Fetal membranes** – **amnion (inner)** & **chorion (outer)**; **amniotic fluid ~800–1000 ml at 36–38 wks** (polyhydramnios > 2000 ml, oligohydramnios < 200–300 ml / AFI < 5 cm); **clear, slightly alkaline (pH ~7–7.5 – nitrazine test turns blue)**; functions – cushion, temperature, free movement, lung development, prevents adhesions; **fetal urine is major source in late pregnancy**.
- **Duration of pregnancy – 280 days (40 weeks) from LMP**; **266 days (38 weeks) from fertilisation**; **Naegele's rule – EDD = LMP + 9 months + 7 days**.

## 12.7 Menopause

- **Permanent cessation of menses (12 months amenorrhoea)** due to **ovarian follicle depletion**; **↓ oestrogen → ↑ FSH (diagnostic) & LH**.
- **Features** – **hot flushes (most common)**, night sweats, mood changes, insomnia, **vaginal dryness/ atrophy (pH rises)**, urinary symptoms, **osteoporosis (↑ fracture risk)**, **↑ cardiovascular risk (↓ HDL protection)**, skin changes.
- **Premature menopause** – before 40 yrs; **post-menopausal bleeding – always investigate (endometrial cancer)**.
- **Andropause** – gradual ↓ testosterone in men.
