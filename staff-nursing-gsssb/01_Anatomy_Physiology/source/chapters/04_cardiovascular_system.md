## 4.1 The Heart – Anatomy

- **Location** – **middle mediastinum**, behind sternum, **⅔ to left of midline**; **apex – left 5th intercostal space, mid-clavicular line (~9 cm from midline)** – **apex beat/ PMI**; base – posterior-superior (great vessels).
- **Size** – ≈ **own clenched fist**; weight **~250–300 g (female ~250, male ~300)**.
- **Layers** – **pericardium** (outer fibrous + serous – parietal & visceral; **pericardial fluid 15–50 ml**; excess → **cardiac tamponade – Beck's triad: hypotension, muffled heart sounds, raised JVP**), **myocardium** (muscle – **thickest in left ventricle**), **endocardium** (inner – continuous with valves; infection → endocarditis).
- **Chambers** – **2 atria (receiving)**, **2 ventricles (pumping)**; **interatrial septum** (fossa ovalis – remnant of foramen ovale), **interventricular septum**.

| Valve | Location | Cusps |
|---|---|---|
| **Tricuspid** | Right atrium → right ventricle | 3 |
| **Mitral (bicuspid)** | Left atrium → left ventricle | **2 (only valve with 2 cusps)** |
| **Pulmonary (semilunar)** | RV → pulmonary trunk | 3 |
| **Aortic (semilunar)** | LV → aorta | 3 |

- **AV valves** held by **chordae tendineae + papillary muscles** (prevent eversion); semilunar valves have no chordae.
- **Valves prevent backflow**; **most commonly affected in rheumatic heart disease – mitral**.
- **Pulmonary artery – only artery carrying deoxygenated blood**; **pulmonary veins (4) – only veins carrying oxygenated blood** (in adults); **umbilical vein** – oxygenated (fetus).

### Coronary circulation
- **Right & left coronary arteries** arise from **aortic sinuses (just above aortic valve)**; **LCA → left anterior descending (LAD – "widow maker", most commonly blocked) + circumflex**; **RCA – supplies SA node (~60 %) & AV node (~80–90 %)**, inferior wall.
- Coronary filling occurs mainly during **diastole**.
- Venous drainage – **coronary sinus → right atrium**.

## 4.2 Conduction System & Cardiac Cycle

| Structure | Intrinsic rate |
|---|---|
| **SA node (pacemaker)** – right atrium wall near SVC | **60–100/min** |
| **AV node** – interatrial septum (delay ~0.1 s – allows ventricular filling) | **40–60/min** |
| **Bundle of His → right & left bundle branches** | – |
| **Purkinje fibres** | **20–40/min** |

- **Cardiac muscle – autorhythmicity**; **long refractory period → no tetany**; **Frank-Starling law** – ↑ stretch (preload) → ↑ force of contraction.
- **Cardiac cycle** ≈ **0.8 s at 75 bpm** – **atrial systole 0.1 s, ventricular systole 0.3 s, diastole 0.5 s** (diastole shortens with tachycardia).
- **Heart sounds** – **S1 "lub" – closure of AV (mitral & tricuspid) valves – start of systole (loudest at apex)**; **S2 "dub" – closure of semilunar (aortic & pulmonary) – end of systole**; **S3** – rapid ventricular filling (normal in children/ pregnancy; **heart failure** in adults); **S4** – atrial contraction against stiff ventricle (always abnormal).
- **Auscultation areas** – **aortic: 2nd right ICS at sternal border**; **pulmonary: 2nd left ICS**; **Erb's point: 3rd left ICS**; **tricuspid: 4th–5th left ICS at sternal border**; **mitral: 5th left ICS mid-clavicular line (apex)** – "**All Physicians Eat Too Much**".
- **ECG** – **P wave – atrial depolarisation**; **PR interval 0.12–0.20 s (AV conduction)**; **QRS – ventricular depolarisation (< 0.12 s)** (atrial repolarisation hidden); **T wave – ventricular repolarisation**; ST segment – elevation in MI; **U wave – hypokalaemia**; **tall peaked T – hyperkalaemia**; **standard 12 leads**; paper speed **25 mm/s – small square 0.04 s, large square 0.2 s**; heart rate = **300 ÷ number of large squares between R–R** (or 1500 ÷ small squares).

## 4.3 Cardiac Output & Blood Pressure

- **Stroke volume (SV)** ≈ **70 ml**; **Cardiac output (CO) = SV × HR ≈ 70 × 72 ≈ 5 L/min**; **cardiac index ≈ 3 L/min/m²**; **ejection fraction (EF) = SV/EDV ≈ 55–70 %** (< 40 % – HFrEF); **EDV ≈ 120 ml, ESV ≈ 50 ml**.
- **Preload** – venous return/ EDV; **afterload** – resistance against which ventricle pumps (aortic pressure, SVR); **contractility** – inotropy.
- **Blood pressure = CO × total peripheral resistance (TPR)**; **normal adult ~120/80 mmHg**; **pulse pressure = SBP − DBP (≈ 40)**; **MAP = DBP + ⅓ pulse pressure (≈ 93; normal 70–105; ≥ 65 for organ perfusion)**.
- **Regulation** – **short-term: baroreceptors (carotid sinus & aortic arch – glossopharyngeal & vagus)**, chemoreceptors, **vasomotor centre (medulla)**; **long-term: kidney – RAAS (renin → angiotensin I → ACE (lungs) → angiotensin II – vasoconstriction & aldosterone)**, ADH, ANP (atria – natriuresis, ↓ BP).
- **Factors ↑ BP** – exercise, emotion, ↑ blood volume, ↑ viscosity, age, obesity; **↓ BP** – sleep, haemorrhage, vasodilation.
- **Korotkoff sounds** – **phase I – SBP (first tapping)**, **phase V – DBP (disappearance)**; cuff bladder **width ≈ 40 %, length ≈ 80 % of arm circumference**; small cuff → **falsely high**; large cuff → falsely low.
- **Normal pulse** – **adult 60–100**, newborn 120–160, child 80–120; **bradycardia < 60**, **tachycardia > 100**; **apical-radial pulse deficit (AF)**.

## 4.4 Blood Vessels

| Vessel | Features |
|---|---|
| **Arteries** | Carry blood **away from heart**; **thick muscular tunica media**; elastic (aorta) & muscular; **arterioles – main resistance vessels (regulate BP)** |
| **Capillaries** | **Single layer of endothelium** – **exchange vessels**; slowest flow; largest total cross-section |
| **Veins** | Towards heart; **thin walls, large lumen, valves (prevent backflow – esp. lower limbs)**; **capacitance vessels – hold ~60–70 % of blood volume** |

- **Wall layers** – **tunica intima (endothelium), tunica media (smooth muscle – thickest in arteries), tunica adventitia/ externa (thickest in veins)**.
- **Largest artery – aorta**; **largest vein – inferior vena cava**; **portal vein** – GI tract → liver (**hepatic portal system** – vein between two capillary beds; also **hypothalamo-hypophyseal portal**).
- **Aorta** – ascending (coronaries) → **arch** (**brachiocephalic trunk → right subclavian + right common carotid; left common carotid; left subclavian**) → descending thoracic → abdominal (coeliac trunk, SMA, renal, IMA) → **bifurcates at L4 into common iliacs**.
- **Circle of Willis** – arterial anastomosis at base of brain (internal carotids + vertebrals/ basilar).
- **Veins for cannulation** – **cephalic, basilic, median cubital (venepuncture – antecubital fossa)**, dorsal venous arch of hand; **great saphenous vein – longest vein (CABG graft; varicose veins)**; **jugular – JVP (normal ≤ 3–4 cm above sternal angle at 45°)**.

### Pulse points
- **Radial** (commonest; wrist, thumb side), **carotid** (**CPR in adults/ children > 1 yr**; never both sides), **brachial** (**infants – CPR**; BP), **femoral**, popliteal (behind knee), **posterior tibial** (behind medial malleolus), **dorsalis pedis** (dorsum of foot), temporal, **apical** (infants < 2 yrs – count 1 full minute; digoxin).

## 4.5 Fetal Circulation

- **Umbilical cord – 2 arteries (deoxygenated → placenta) + 1 vein (oxygenated → fetus)** ("**AVA**"); Wharton's jelly.

| Fetal shunt | Connects | After birth becomes |
|---|---|---|
| **Ductus venosus** | Umbilical vein → IVC (bypasses liver) | **Ligamentum venosum** |
| **Foramen ovale** | Right atrium → left atrium (bypasses lungs) | **Fossa ovalis** (functional closure – first breath, ↑ LA pressure) |
| **Ductus arteriosus** | Pulmonary artery → aorta (bypasses lungs) | **Ligamentum arteriosum** (closes 24–72 hrs functionally; **PDA if open – indomethacin/ ibuprofen closes, PGE1 keeps open**) |
| **Umbilical vein** | – | **Ligamentum teres (round ligament of liver)** |
| **Umbilical arteries** | – | **Medial umbilical ligaments** |

- **Highest O₂ saturation – umbilical vein (~80 %)**; fetal Hb (HbF) has **higher O₂ affinity**.
