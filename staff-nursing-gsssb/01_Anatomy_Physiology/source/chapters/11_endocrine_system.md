## 11.1 Hormones – Basics

- **Endocrine glands – ductless**, secrete **hormones** directly into blood; **"Father of Endocrinology" – Thomas Addison**; **first hormone discovered – secretin (1902)**; **"hormone" term – Starling (1905)**.
- **Chemical types** – **peptide/ protein** (insulin, GH, ADH, oxytocin, PTH – water-soluble, **cell-membrane receptors, 2nd messenger cAMP**), **steroids** (cortisol, aldosterone, sex hormones, vitamin D – lipid-soluble, **intracellular/ nuclear receptors**), **amines** (adrenaline, noradrenaline, **thyroid hormones – act like steroids (nuclear receptors)**, melatonin).
- **Regulation** – mostly **negative feedback** (e.g. ↑ T4 → ↓ TSH); positive – oxytocin in labour, LH surge.
- **Master gland – pituitary**; **"master of master" – hypothalamus**; **largest endocrine gland – thyroid**; **mixed glands – pancreas, gonads**.

## 11.2 Hypothalamus & Pituitary

- **Hypothalamus** – **releasing & inhibiting hormones** via **hypothalamo-hypophyseal portal system** to anterior pituitary – **TRH, CRH, GnRH, GHRH, somatostatin (GH-inhibiting), dopamine (prolactin-inhibiting)**; **makes ADH & oxytocin** (supraoptic & paraventricular nuclei) – transported along axons to **posterior pituitary (stored & released)**.
- **Pituitary (hypophysis)** – **pea-sized (~0.5 g), in sella turcica of sphenoid**, attached by infundibulum; **anterior (adenohypophysis – glandular)** & **posterior (neurohypophysis – neural)**.

| Hormone (anterior pituitary) | Action | Hypo | Hyper |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Growth hormone (GH/ somatotropin)** | Growth of bones & tissues (via **IGF-1 from liver**), protein synthesis, ↑ blood glucose (anti-insulin); **secreted mostly during deep sleep** | **Dwarfism (proportionate)** in children | **Gigantism (before epiphyseal fusion)**, **acromegaly (adults – large hands, feet, jaw)** |
| **TSH (thyrotropin)** | Thyroid hormone release | Hypothyroidism | Hyperthyroidism |
| **ACTH (corticotropin)** | Cortisol release (adrenal cortex) | Secondary adrenal insufficiency | **Cushing's disease** |
| **FSH** | **Female – follicle growth, oestrogen; male – spermatogenesis (Sertoli cells)** | Infertility | – |
| **LH (ICSH in males)** | **Female – ovulation (LH surge), corpus luteum; male – testosterone (Leydig cells)** | Infertility | – |
| **Prolactin** | **Milk production (lactogenesis)** | Lactation failure (**Sheehan's syndrome – postpartum pituitary necrosis after PPH**) | **Galactorrhoea, amenorrhoea, infertility** |
| MSH | Melanin (pigmentation) | – | – |

| Hormone (posterior pituitary) | Action | Disorder |
|---|---|---|
| **ADH (vasopressin)** | **Water reabsorption in collecting ducts (aquaporins)**, vasoconstriction | **Deficiency → diabetes insipidus (polyuria, dilute urine, low sp. gravity < 1.005, thirst)**; **excess → SIADH (hyponatraemia, concentrated urine, water retention)** |
| **Oxytocin ("love/ cuddle hormone")** | **Uterine contraction (labour – Ferguson reflex, positive feedback)**, **milk ejection (let-down reflex – suckling)**, bonding | Used in induction & PPH |

- **Pituitary tumour** – **bitemporal hemianopia** (optic chiasma pressure).

## 11.3 Thyroid Gland

- **Largest endocrine gland (~20–25 g)**; **butterfly-shaped**, **2 lobes + isthmus (over 2nd–4th tracheal rings)**, front of neck **C5–T1**; **moves on swallowing** (pretracheal fascia); highly vascular; **functional unit – follicle (colloid – thyroglobulin)**.
- **Follicular cells** – **T4 (thyroxine – ~90 % secreted, prohormone)** & **T3 (triiodothyronine – ~3–5 × more potent, active form; most T4 → T3 in peripheral tissues)**; need **iodine (daily requirement ~150 µg adult, 250 µg pregnancy/ lactation)**; **mostly bound to TBG** in blood.
- **Parafollicular (C) cells** – **calcitonin (↓ blood calcium – inhibits osteoclasts)**; tumour marker for medullary carcinoma.
- **Actions of T3/T4** – **↑ BMR & O₂ consumption, heat production (calorigenic)**, ↑ heart rate & CO, **growth & brain development (essential in fetus & infancy)**, ↑ metabolism of carbs, fats, proteins, ↑ sensitivity to catecholamines.

| Condition | Features |
|---|---|
| **Hypothyroidism – infant: congenital hypothyroidism (cretinism)** | **Mental retardation, short stature, large tongue (macroglossia), umbilical hernia, prolonged jaundice, constipation, coarse cry** → **newborn screening (TSH, heel prick) & early thyroxine** |
| **Hypothyroidism – adult: myxoedema** | **Weight gain, cold intolerance, bradycardia, lethargy, dry skin, hair loss, constipation, menorrhagia, slow reflexes, non-pitting oedema**; **Hashimoto's thyroiditis – commonest cause (autoimmune)** in iodine-sufficient areas; worldwide – iodine deficiency |
| **Hyperthyroidism (thyrotoxicosis)** | **Weight loss despite appetite, heat intolerance, tachycardia/ AF, tremors, sweating, anxiety, diarrhoea, amenorrhoea**; **Graves' disease (commonest – TSH-receptor stimulating antibodies; exophthalmos, pretibial myxoedema, diffuse goitre)** |
| **Goitre** | Enlarged thyroid – **simple/ endemic (iodine deficiency – sub-Himalayan "goitre belt")**; **NIDDCP – iodised salt (≥ 15 ppm at consumer, 30 ppm at production)** |
| **Thyroid storm** | Life-threatening hyperthyroidism – hyperpyrexia, tachycardia |

- **Normal values** – **TSH ~0.4–4.0 mIU/L**, free T4 ~0.8–1.8 ng/dl, T3 ~80–200 ng/dl; **TSH – best screening test**.

## 11.4 Parathyroid Glands

- **4 small glands (2 superior, 2 inferior)** on **posterior surface of thyroid**; **chief cells – parathormone (PTH)**.
- **PTH ↑ blood calcium** – **bone resorption (osteoclasts)**, **↑ renal Ca²⁺ reabsorption (DCT)**, **↓ phosphate reabsorption (phosphaturia)**, **activates vitamin D (1α-hydroxylase in kidney) → ↑ intestinal Ca²⁺ absorption**; secretion controlled by **serum Ca²⁺ (calcium-sensing receptor)**.
- **Hypoparathyroidism** (commonest cause – **accidental removal/ injury during thyroidectomy**) → **hypocalcaemia → tetany, Chvostek's sign (facial twitch on tapping facial nerve), Trousseau's sign (carpal spasm with BP cuff), laryngospasm, seizures, prolonged QT** → **IV calcium gluconate (10 %) at bedside after thyroidectomy**.
- **Hyperparathyroidism** → **hypercalcaemia – "stones (renal), bones (pain, fractures), groans (abdominal – peptic ulcer, pancreatitis, constipation), moans (psychiatric)"**, polyuria.

## 11.5 Adrenal (Suprarenal) Glands

- **Pair, on upper poles of kidneys**, retroperitoneal; **right – pyramidal, left – semilunar**; ~4–5 g each.
- **Adrenal cortex (outer, ~80–90 %, from mesoderm, steroid hormones – essential for life)** – 3 zones "**GFR – Salt, Sugar, Sex – the deeper you go the sweeter it gets**":

| Zone | Hormone | Action |
|---|---|---|
| **Zona glomerulosa (outer)** | **Mineralocorticoids – aldosterone** | **Na⁺ & water retention, K⁺ & H⁺ excretion (DCT)** → ↑ BP; regulated by **RAAS (angiotensin II) & ↑ K⁺** (not much by ACTH); excess → **Conn's syndrome (hypertension + hypokalaemia)** |
| **Zona fasciculata (middle, largest)** | **Glucocorticoids – cortisol (hydrocortisone)** | **"Stress hormone"** – **↑ blood glucose (gluconeogenesis), protein catabolism, fat redistribution, anti-inflammatory & immunosuppressive**, maintains BP (permissive for catecholamines); **diurnal rhythm – highest ~8 AM (early morning), lowest at midnight**; regulated by **ACTH** |
| **Zona reticularis (inner)** | **Androgens (DHEA)** | Pubic/ axillary hair (females), libido |

- **Cushing's syndrome (cortisol excess)** – **moon face, buffalo hump, truncal obesity with thin limbs, purple striae, hypertension, hyperglycaemia, osteoporosis, hirsutism, thin skin, poor wound healing, infections, hypokalaemia**; commonest cause – **iatrogenic (long-term steroids)**; Cushing's disease = pituitary ACTH-secreting adenoma.
- **Addison's disease (primary adrenal insufficiency – ↓ cortisol & aldosterone)** – **weakness, weight loss, hypotension, hypoglycaemia, hyponatraemia, hyperkalaemia, hyperpigmentation (↑ ACTH/ MSH – skin creases, buccal mucosa)**, salt craving; **commonest cause – autoimmune (TB in India historically)**; **Addisonian crisis – IV hydrocortisone + saline**.
- **Congenital adrenal hyperplasia (21-hydroxylase deficiency)** – ambiguous genitalia in female newborns, salt-wasting.
- **Adrenal medulla (inner, from neural crest; modified sympathetic ganglion)** – **chromaffin cells** – **adrenaline (epinephrine ~80 %) & noradrenaline (~20 %)** – **"fight or flight"**: ↑ HR, BP, glucose, bronchodilation, pupil dilation; **not essential for life**; tumour – **phaeochromocytoma (paroxysmal hypertension, headache, sweating, palpitations; urinary VMA/ metanephrines)**.

## 11.6 Pancreas (Islets of Langerhans)

| Cell | Hormone | Action |
|---|---|---|
| **α (alpha) ~20 %** | **Glucagon** | **↑ blood glucose – glycogenolysis, gluconeogenesis** (liver); released in hypoglycaemia, fasting |
| **β (beta) ~70 %** | **Insulin** (51 amino acids – 2 chains A & B; from proinsulin → **C-peptide**) | **↓ blood glucose – ↑ glucose uptake into muscle & fat (GLUT-4), glycogenesis, lipogenesis, protein synthesis; inhibits gluconeogenesis**; **only hypoglycaemic hormone**; **drives K⁺ into cells** |
| **δ (delta)** | **Somatostatin** | Inhibits insulin & glucagon, GH |
| **PP (F) cells** | Pancreatic polypeptide | – |

- **Hyperglycaemic hormones** – **glucagon, cortisol, GH, adrenaline, thyroxine** ("counter-regulatory").
- **Diabetes mellitus** – **type 1 (autoimmune β-cell destruction – absolute insulin deficiency; young; DKA)**; **type 2 (insulin resistance + relative deficiency; adults, obesity; most common ~90–95 %)**; **gestational**; **diagnosis – FBS ≥ 126 mg/dl, 2-hr PPBS/ OGTT (75 g) ≥ 200, random ≥ 200 with symptoms, HbA1c ≥ 6.5 %**; classic triad – **polyuria, polydipsia, polyphagia**.
- **Insulin discovered – Banting & Best (1921)**; **World Diabetes Day – 14 November (Banting's birthday)**.

## 11.7 Other Endocrine Tissues

| Gland/ tissue | Hormone | Key point |
|---|---|---|
| **Pineal (epiphysis cerebri)** | **Melatonin** | **Circadian rhythm, sleep; secreted in darkness**; "third eye"; calcifies with age |
| **Thymus** | **Thymosin, thymopoietin** | **T-cell maturation**; **largest at puberty, then involutes (fat)**; in anterior mediastinum |
| **Ovaries** | **Oestrogen (Graafian follicle), progesterone (corpus luteum)**, inhibin, relaxin | Female sex characters, menstrual cycle, pregnancy maintenance |
| **Testes** | **Testosterone (Leydig/ interstitial cells – LH)**; inhibin (Sertoli) | Male characters, spermatogenesis |
| **Placenta** | **hCG (basis of pregnancy test – detectable ~8–10 days after conception; peaks ~8–10 wks; maintains corpus luteum)**, **hPL (human placental lactogen – anti-insulin)**, oestrogen (oestriol), progesterone, relaxin | Temporary endocrine organ |
| **Kidney** | **Erythropoietin, renin, calcitriol** | – |
| **Heart (atria)** | **ANP (atrial natriuretic peptide)** | ↓ BP, ↑ Na⁺ excretion |
| **GI tract** | **Gastrin, secretin, CCK, GIP, ghrelin (hunger)** | – |
| **Adipose tissue** | **Leptin (satiety)** | – |
| **Liver** | **IGF-1 (somatomedin), angiotensinogen, thrombopoietin** | – |
| **Local hormones** | **Prostaglandins** (inflammation, pain, fever, labour, gastric protection), **histamine, bradykinin, serotonin** | Act locally (autocrine/ paracrine) |
