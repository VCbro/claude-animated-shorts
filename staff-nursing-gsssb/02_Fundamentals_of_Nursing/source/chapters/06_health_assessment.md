## 6.1 Health History

- **Components**: Biographic data → **Chief complaint (CC – patient ke words me)** → **History of present illness (HPI)** → Past history → Family history (genogram) → Personal/ social history (diet, sleep, habits – smoking, alcohol) → Menstrual/ obstetric history → Immunization → Allergies → Review of systems.
- **HPI analysis – OLDCARTS / PQRST**: **O**nset, **L**ocation, **D**uration, **C**haracter, **A**ggravating, **R**elieving, **T**iming, **S**everity.
- Interview phases: **Introduction (orientation)** → **Working** → **Termination**. Privacy, comfortable seating, open-ended questions.

## 6.2 Techniques of Physical Examination

| Technique | Meaning | Use / Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Inspection** | **Dekhna** (+ smell, listen) – systematic observation | **First** technique; good light; compare both sides (symmetry) |
| **Palpation** | **Chhoona** – touch se feel | **Light** (1 cm – tenderness, texture) first, then **deep** (4 cm – organs, masses). Painful area **last**. **Fingertips** – fine discrimination (texture, pulse); **dorsum of hand** – **temperature**; **palm/ ulnar surface** – **vibration (fremitus)**; grasping – size, consistency |
| **Percussion** | **Thapthapana** – tap karke sound sunna | **Indirect/ mediate** (pleximeter finger on body, plexor finger strikes – most common), direct, blunt (fist – kidney/ CVA tenderness) |
| **Auscultation** | **Stethoscope se sunna** | **Diaphragm – high-pitched** (breath, bowel, normal heart S1, S2); **Bell – low-pitched** (murmurs, **S3, S4**, bruits) – placed **lightly** |

:::warn Order of examination
**Normal order: I → P → P → A** (Inspection, Palpation, Percussion, Auscultation).
**Abdomen: I → A → P → P** (Inspection, **Auscultation**, Percussion, Palpation) – kyunki palpation/ percussion bowel sounds badal deta hai.
:::

### Percussion notes
| Note | Intensity / Pitch | Normal site | Abnormal example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Flat** | Soft, high, short | **Muscle (thigh)**, bone | Large pleural effusion |
| **Dull** | Medium, thud-like | **Liver, heart, full bladder, spleen** | **Pneumonia (consolidation)**, pleural effusion, mass |
| **Resonant** | Loud, low, hollow | **Normal lung** | |
| **Hyper-resonant** | Very loud, booming | None in adult (child lung) | **Emphysema, pneumothorax** |
| **Tympanic** | Loud, high, drum-like | **Stomach, gas-filled bowel** | Distended abdomen (gas) |

:::mnemo Percussion – solid se hollow
**Flat (bone/muscle) → Dull (liver) → Resonant (lung) → Hyper-resonant (emphysema) → Tympanic (stomach)** = dense se khokla.
:::

### Equipment
Stethoscope, sphygmomanometer, **thermometer**, **ophthalmoscope** (retina/ fundus), **otoscope** (ear canal, TM), **tuning fork (512 Hz hearing; 128 Hz vibration)**, **percussion (reflex) hammer**, **Snellen chart (20 ft/ 6 m)**, tongue depressor, penlight/ torch, measuring tape, weighing scale, **nasal speculum**, **vaginal speculum** (Cusco's/ Sims'), gloves, **goniometer** (joint angle/ ROM), **skin-fold calliper** (fat), **Doppler** (weak pulses), cotton & pins (sensation).

### Positions for examination
| Position | Use |
|---|---|
| **Sitting** | Head, neck, chest (back & front), upper limbs, vitals |
| **Supine (dorsal)** | **Abdomen** (knees slightly flexed – relax muscles), breast, heart, extremities |
| **Dorsal recumbent** (supine, knees flexed, feet flat) | **Abdomen**, perineum, catheterization (female), vaginal exam |
| **Lithotomy** | **Vaginal/ pelvic exam, Pap smear**, delivery |
| **Sims' (left lateral, right knee flexed)** | **Rectal exam, enema**, vaginal (Sims' speculum) |
| **Prone** | Back, spine, hip joint movement |
| **Knee-chest (genupectoral)** | **Rectal/ prostate, sigmoidoscopy** |
| **Standing** | Gait, posture, spine, hernia, varicose veins, male genitalia |
| **Fowler's/ semi-Fowler's** | Dyspnoeic patient, head/ neck |
| **Lateral recumbent** | Heart (left lateral – mitral murmur better), **lumbar puncture** |

## 6.3 General Survey & Measurements

- General survey: appearance, **level of consciousness**, posture, gait, build, hygiene, speech, mood, **signs of distress**, odours.
- **Height & weight**: same time (morning), same scale, same clothes, after voiding; infants – supine length (infantometer).
- **BMI = Weight (kg) ÷ Height (m)²** (Quetelet index).

| BMI (WHO) | Category | Asian/ Indian cut-offs |
|---|---|---|
| < 18.5 | **Underweight** | < 18.5 |
| 18.5–24.9 | **Normal** | **18.5–22.9** |
| 25–29.9 | **Overweight** | **23–24.9** |
| ≥ 30 | **Obese** | **≥ 25** |

- **Waist circumference** – risk: **men > 90 cm, women > 80 cm** (Asian); **Waist-hip ratio**: men > 0.9, women > 0.85 (abdominal obesity). **MUAC** (children 6–59 months) – **< 11.5 cm = SAM**.
- **Ideal body weight (Broca)** = Height (cm) − 100 (men); women − 105.
- **Mid-arm circumference, skin-fold thickness (triceps)** – nutrition.

## 6.4 Head-to-Toe – Important Findings

### Skin
- **Colour**: **pallor** (anaemia, shock – see conjunctiva, palms, nail beds), **cyanosis** (central – lips/ tongue; peripheral – nail beds), **jaundice** (sclera first – bilirubin > 2–3 mg/dL), erythema, **mottling**.
- **Turgor** – pinch skin over **sternum/ clavicle (elderly)**, forearm/ abdomen; slow return (**tenting**) = **dehydration**.
- **Oedema** – **pitting**: **1+ (2 mm)**, **2+ (4 mm)**, **3+ (6 mm)**, **4+ (8 mm)**. Press over **bony surface (tibia, ankle, sacrum)** for 5 sec.
- **Capillary refill** – press nail bed → normal **< 2–3 sec**.
- **Clubbing** – **angle > 180°** (normal 160°); **Schamroth's window** lost – chronic hypoxia (COPD, bronchiectasis, CHD, lung cancer).
- **Koilonychia** (spoon nails – iron deficiency), **Beau's lines** (transverse grooves – acute illness), **splinter haemorrhage** (endocarditis), **paronychia**.
- Lesions – see MSN notes (macule, papule, vesicle…).

### Head, Eyes, Ears, Nose, Throat
- Eyes: **PERRLA** (Pupils Equal, Round, Reactive to Light & Accommodation); normal pupil **2–6 mm** (3–5); **anisocoria** (unequal); **ptosis**; **nystagmus**; visual acuity (Snellen), **visual fields (confrontation test)**, **corneal reflex** (CN V & VII), conjunctiva (pallor), sclera (jaundice).
- Ears: otoscope – **adult pull pinna up & back; child < 3 yr – down & back**; **Rinne & Weber** tests; whisper test.
- Mouth: lips (cyanosis), tongue (coated – typhoid; beefy red – B12; **magenta – B2**), gums (bleeding – scurvy, gingival hyperplasia – phenytoin), teeth, tonsils (grading 1+ to 4+ – **4+ = touching midline "kissing tonsils"**), **gag reflex (CN IX, X)**, uvula (rises midline – "ah").
- Neck: lymph nodes, **thyroid (from behind)**, **trachea – midline**, **JVP (jugular venous pressure)** – patient at **45°**; normal **< 3–4 cm above sternal angle**.

### Chest & Lungs
- Shape: **normal AP : lateral = 1 : 2**; **barrel chest 1 : 1** (emphysema); pigeon chest (pectus carinatum), funnel chest (pectus excavatum), kyphosis, scoliosis.
- **Tactile fremitus** ("99" / "ninety-nine" – palpate vibration): **↑ in consolidation (pneumonia)**; **↓ in pleural effusion, pneumothorax, emphysema**.
- **Vocal resonance/ bronchophony, egophony** ("ee" → "aa" over consolidation), **whispered pectoriloquy**.
- Breath sounds: **vesicular** (soft, low – most lung fields; inspiration > expiration), **bronchovesicular** (between scapulae, 1st–2nd ICS), **bronchial/ tubular** (loud, high – over trachea/ manubrium; abnormal elsewhere = consolidation).
- Adventitious: **crackles (rales)** – fine/ coarse – fluid (pneumonia, HF); **wheeze** – narrowed airway; **rhonchi** – secretions large airway (clear with cough); **pleural friction rub** – grating (pleurisy); **stridor**.

### Heart
- Auscultation areas (**APETM** – "**All Physicians Enjoy Taking Money**"): **A**ortic (2nd ICS right sternal border) → **P**ulmonic (2nd ICS left SB) → **E**rb's point (3rd ICS left SB) → **T**ricuspid (4th/5th ICS left lower SB) → **M**itral/ apex (5th ICS left MCL – **PMI/ apex beat**).
- **S1 ("lub")** – closure of **AV (mitral & tricuspid)** valves – loudest at **apex**; start of systole. **S2 ("dub")** – closure of **semilunar (aortic & pulmonary)** – loudest at **base**. **S3** – "Ken-tuc-ky" – ventricular gallop (HF; normal in children/ athletes/ pregnancy). **S4** – "Ten-nes-see" – atrial gallop (stiff ventricle – HTN, MI).
- **Murmurs** graded **I–VI** (VI – heard with stethoscope off chest); **thrill** = palpable murmur.

### Abdomen
- 4 quadrants / 9 regions; **bowel sounds 5–30 /min** (listen **5 min** before calling absent); **hyperactive (borborygmi)** – diarrhoea, early obstruction; **hypoactive/ absent** – paralytic ileus, peritonitis.
- **Liver span 6–12 cm (MCL)**; **spleen** – normally not palpable; **shifting dullness & fluid thrill** – ascites; **rebound tenderness (Blumberg)** – peritonitis; **Murphy's sign** – cholecystitis; **McBurney's point** – appendicitis.
- **CVA tenderness** (fist percussion at costovertebral angle) – **pyelonephritis/ kidney**.

### Musculoskeletal & Neuro
- **Muscle strength grading (0–5)**: **0** – no contraction · **1** – flicker/ trace · **2** – movement with gravity eliminated · **3** – against gravity (no resistance) · **4** – against some resistance · **5** – normal full strength.
- **Deep tendon reflexes (0–4+)**: 0 absent · 1+ diminished · **2+ normal** · 3+ brisk · 4+ hyperactive with **clonus**. Biceps (C5–6), triceps (C7), brachioradialis, **patellar/ knee jerk (L2–L4)**, **Achilles/ ankle jerk (S1)**.
- **Babinski** – stroke sole → toes fan & great toe up = **positive (abnormal in adults; normal < 1–2 yrs)** → UMN lesion.
- **Romberg test** (balance – eyes closed, feet together), **finger-nose test** (cerebellum), **heel-shin test**, **gait**.
- **Level of consciousness**: Alert → Lethargic → Obtunded → Stuporous → Comatose. **AVPU** (Alert, Voice, Pain, Unresponsive). **GCS 3–15** (Eye 4, Verbal 5, Motor 6) – detail in MSN notes.
- **Mental status – orientation ×3 (time, place, person)**; **MMSE (30 points)**.

- Physical exam ka first technique – **inspection**.
- Abdomen me auscultation – **inspection ke baad, palpation se pehle**.
- Temperature palpate karte hain – **dorsum (back) of hand** se; vibration – **palm/ ulnar surface**.
- Percussion over liver – **dull**; over stomach – **tympanic**; emphysema – **hyper-resonant**.
- Bell of stethoscope – **low-pitched** sounds (murmurs, S3, S4).
- Skin turgor in elderly – **over sternum/ clavicle**.
- Normal capillary refill – **< 2–3 sec**.
- Muscle power against gravity but not resistance – **grade 3**.
- Clubbing angle – **> 180°**.
- BMI 27 (Indian) – **obese** (≥ 25).
