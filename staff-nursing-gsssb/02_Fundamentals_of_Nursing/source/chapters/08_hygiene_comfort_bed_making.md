# Personal Hygiene, Bed Making & Comfort Devices
<!-- desc: Bed bath, oral care, hair care & pediculosis, eye/ear/nose care, perineal care, back care, types of beds, comfort devices, positions -->

## 8.1 Personal Hygiene – Principles

- Purposes: cleanliness, **comfort**, circulation stimulation (massage), **prevent infection & pressure ulcers**, assessment opportunity (**skin inspection**), self-esteem, relaxation.
- General rules: **privacy** (screen, close door), explain, collect articles, **warm room (no draughts)**, **water temperature 43–46 °C (110–115 °F)** for bath (test with **elbow/ wrist/ thermometer**), clean → dirty, **top → bottom**, **distal → proximal** strokes on extremities (venous return), encourage self-care, **expose only the part being cleaned**.
- **Morning care (AM care)**: toileting, oral care, wash face & hands, bed straightening. **HS care (hour of sleep / evening care)**: toileting, oral care, back rub, bed straightening, comfort for sleep. **Early morning care** – before breakfast.

## 8.2 Bathing

| Type | Description |
|---|---|
| **Complete bed bath** | Nurse bathes **dependent/ bed-ridden** patient fully |
| **Partial bed bath** | Only parts that cause odour/ discomfort – **face, hands, axillae, back, perineum** |
| **Self-help bath / Assisted** | Patient bathes, nurse assists |
| **Tub bath, Shower** | Ambulatory; check temp; non-slip mat |
| **Sponge bath (tepid sponging)** | **Fever reduction** – water 27–34 °C (~1 °C below body temp) |
| **Therapeutic baths** | **Sitz bath** (perineal/ rectal – 40–43 °C, **15–20 min**), **oatmeal/ starch bath** (pruritus), **medicated** (KMnO₄), **saline**, cold bath |
| **Bag bath / chlorhexidine bath** | Pre-moistened no-rinse cloths (ICU) |

:::key Bed bath sequence
**Eyes (inner → outer canthus, plain water, no soap, separate corner of cloth each eye)** → face → ears → neck → **arms (far arm first; distal → proximal, long firm strokes – venous return)** → hands (can soak) → chest (breast – circular) → abdomen → **legs** → feet → **change water** → back & buttocks (**back rub**) → **perineum last** (change water & gloves).
**Change water** – when cool, soapy or dirty, and **before perineal care**.
:::

## 8.3 Oral Care (Mouth care)

- Purposes: remove plaque/ debris, prevent **halitosis, stomatitis, dental caries, parotitis, aspiration pneumonia (VAP)**, improve appetite.
- **Brushing**: soft brush, **45° angle to gum line**, **from gum to crown** (vibrating/ circular – Bass technique), **2 times/day, 2 min**; floss once daily; tongue cleaning.
- **Special mouth care** – for **unconscious, NPO, dehydrated, febrile, O₂ therapy, NG tube, mouth breathers, chemotherapy** patients – every **2–4 hrs**.

:::warn Oral care of UNCONSCIOUS patient
- Position – **side-lying (lateral) with head turned to side / head of bed lowered** → fluid mouth se bahar nikle (**aspiration prevention**) – **most important point**.
- **Suction ready** at bedside.
- Use **padded tongue blade / bite block** to keep mouth open – **never use fingers**.
- **Small amount of solution**; use swab/ toothette; don't pour fluids.
- Lips – **petroleum jelly/ lip balm** (dryness, cracks).
- Solutions: **NS**, **chlorhexidine 0.12–0.2 %**, sodium bicarbonate (1 tsp in 500 ml), **glycothymoline**, H₂O₂ (diluted, 1:4 – short-term, can damage mucosa); **lemon-glycerine swabs ✘** (drying, erode enamel).
:::

- **Dentures**: remove with gauze – **upper first** (break suction by pushing gently), lower next; clean over **basin of water/ towel (prevents breakage)**, **cool/ lukewarm water** (not hot – warping), store in **labelled cup with water**/ solution when out; reinsert – upper first. Unconscious patients – dentures removed & stored.
- **Oral conditions**: halitosis (bad breath), **stomatitis** (inflammation of mouth), **gingivitis** (gums), **glossitis** (tongue), **cheilosis** (cracking of lips/ corners – **B2 deficiency**), **parotitis** (salivary gland), **sordes** (crust on lips/ teeth – fever, dehydration), **dental caries**, **pyorrhoea**, **xerostomia** (dry mouth), **oral thrush/ candidiasis** (white patches – nystatin).

## 8.4 Hair & Scalp Care

- Brush/ comb daily (distribute sebum), from **ends upward to scalp** for tangles; braid long hair (**not tight** – alopecia).
- **Shampoo in bed** – patient **diagonal/ to edge of bed**, Kelly pad/ rubber sheet trough to bucket, cotton in ears, eyes covered; **contraindicated** – neck/ head injury (doctor's order), fresh surgery, very ill.
- **Pediculosis** – lice: **capitis (head)**, **corporis (body – lives in clothing seams)**, **pubis (crab louse – sexually transmitted)**. **Nits** – eggs glued to hair shaft (white, oval, can't be flicked off unlike dandruff).
- **Treatment**: **Permethrin 1 % (head lice – first line)** / 5 % for scabies, **Malathion 0.5 %**, gamma benzene hexachloride (**Lindane** – neurotoxic, not first line); apply as directed (permethrin 10 min, rinse), **fine-tooth comb (nit comb)**, **repeat after 7–10 days**; wash clothes/ linen in **hot water (≥ 60 °C)** or seal in plastic bag **2 weeks**; check all family members; **don't use kerosene**; nurse – gloves, gown, cap.
- **Alopecia** – hair loss; **hirsutism** – excess hair (female); **dandruff** – scaling.

## 8.5 Eye, Ear & Nose Care

- **Eye care**: **clean from inner canthus → outer canthus** (away from nasolacrimal duct), separate swab/ cotton for each eye & each stroke, **NS/ sterile water**. **Unconscious** – no blink reflex → **artificial tears/ lubricant, eye pads/ moist saline gauze, tape eyelids** (corneal abrasion/ ulcer prevention).
- **Contact lens** – remove for unconscious/ surgery; hard lens – slide off; soft lens – pinch off; store in labelled case with solution.
- **Artificial eye (prosthesis)** – remove by pulling lower lid down & pressing; clean with warm NS; store in water/ saline in labelled container.
- **Ear care**: clean external ear with washcloth; **no cotton buds/ hairpins inside canal** (wax impaction, TM perforation); **hearing aid** – turn off & remove battery when not in use (night), clean with dry cloth, **no water**, avoid hairspray.
- **Nose care**: clean nostrils with moist cotton applicator (not too deep); NG/ O₂ tube patients – check pressure areas.

## 8.6 Perineal Care & Foot/ Nail Care

- **Perineal care (pericare)**: Female – **front to back (pubis → anus)**, separate swab each stroke, **labia – outer then inner**, centre last (meatus → down). Male – retract foreskin (uncircumcised), clean **meatus outward in circular motion**, **replace foreskin** (paraphimosis prevention), then shaft, scrotum. Indications – catheter, post-delivery, incontinence, surgery.
- **Foot care**: soak in warm water (not for diabetics – avoid prolonged soaking), dry **between toes**, lotion **but not between toes**. **Nails – cut straight across** (toenails) – corners not cut (ingrown nail); **diabetics/ PVD – no cutting by nurse; podiatrist** (or file).
- Fingernails – **curved (oval)** cut. Nail biting, **paronychia**, **onychomycosis** (fungal).

## 8.7 Back Care / Back Rub (Back massage)

- Purposes: **stimulate circulation**, **prevent pressure ulcers**, relaxation & sleep, comfort, assess skin.
- Position: **prone or side-lying**. Lotion/ powder (not both); duration **3–5 min**; warm hands.
- **Massage strokes**: **Effleurage** (long, smooth, gliding strokes – most common, start & end), **Petrissage** (kneading – lifting/ squeezing muscles), **Tapotement** (percussion – tapping/ hacking), **Friction** (deep circular), **Vibration**.
- **Contraindications**: **reddened bony prominences (don't massage – tissue damage)**, **DVT/ thrombophlebitis** (embolism), rib/ vertebral fractures, burns, **cardiac surgery (some)**, skin lesions.
- Areas to watch: sacrum, scapulae, heels, elbows, occiput, trochanters.

## 8.8 Bed Making

- Purposes: comfort, safety, **clean wrinkle-free bed (pressure ulcer prevention)**, neat appearance, prevent cross-infection.
- **Principles**: **hand hygiene**; **mitred (hospital) corners** – square, neat (**45° angle**); linen **held away from uniform**; **don't shake/ fan linen** (spreads microbes); soiled linen **never on floor**; **bottom sheet tight & wrinkle-free**; **toe pleat** in top sheet (pressure on toes ↓ – footdrop prevention); open end of pillowcase **away from door**; complete **one side first** (save time & energy – body mechanics); bed at **working height (waist level)**, wheels locked; **economy of time, material & energy**.
- Linen order (bottom to top): **mattress → (mattress cover) → bottom sheet → mackintosh (rubber sheet) → draw sheet** (mackintosh & draw sheet placed **under buttocks – shoulder to knee/ middle third**) → top sheet → blanket → counterpane/ bedspread → pillows.
- **Draw sheet** – can be changed without changing whole bed; **mackintosh** – protect mattress (incontinence).

| Type of bed | Purpose / Feature |
|---|---|
| **Closed bed (unoccupied)** | After discharge/ **terminal disinfection**, waiting for new patient; top linen pulled up to head |
| **Open bed (unoccupied)** | Ready for a patient who is up/ new admission – top linen **fan-folded to foot** |
| **Occupied bed** | Patient **can't get out of bed** – make with patient in bed (side to side – roll patient); **2 nurses** ideal |
| **Admission bed** | Ready for new admission – open bed |
| **Post-operative / Surgical / Recovery / Ether bed** | Receive patient from OT – top linen **fan-folded to side (far side/ opposite to door)**, mackintosh at head & middle, **hot water bottles (removed before patient placed)**, **kidney tray, tissue, suction, O₂, BP apparatus, IV stand** ready; pillow **removed / placed vertically at head (headboard protection)** |
| **Cardiac bed** | **Cardiac table/ backrest**, patient sits up (**orthopnoeic/ high Fowler's**), knee rest – **HF, dyspnoea** |
| **Fracture bed** | **Fracture boards (wooden) under mattress**, firm mattress, **Balkan beam/ overhead frame**, monkey bar (trapeze), cradle |
| **Amputation bed** | Top linen in 2 parts (stump exposed for observation), **sandbags, cradle, tourniquet at bedside** |
| **Divided bed** | Top linen in **2 halves** – leg/ lower limb dressing, fracture |
| **Plaster bed** | Fresh plaster cast – **firm mattress, fracture board, pillows with mackintosh**, **cast uncovered** to dry |
| **Burn bed** | Sterile linen, **bed cradle**, **air/ water bed**, warm room |
| **Water bed / Air bed / Alternating pressure mattress** | **Pressure ulcer prevention** (bed-ridden, paralysed) |
| **Circo-electric bed / Stryker frame / Foster frame** | Turning **spinal cord injury** patients (in alignment) |
| **Clinitron (air-fluidized) bed** | Burns, severe pressure ulcers |
| **Rocking bed** | Respiratory (poliomyelitis – historic) |
| **Hammock (sling) bed** | Pelvic fracture |

## 8.9 Comfort Devices

| Device | Use |
|---|---|
| **Backrest (bed rest)** | Fowler's position – dyspnoea, cardiac |
| **Cardiac table (over-bed table)** | Lean forward – **orthopnoea** |
| **Bed cradle (arch)** | Keep **top linen off** – burns, fracture, gout, wound, amputation, cast drying |
| **Footboard / foot rest** | **Prevents footdrop** (plantar flexion), maintains dorsiflexion |
| **Sandbags** | Immobilize/ support limb, prevent rotation |
| **Trochanter roll** | **Prevents external rotation of hip** (rolled blanket from iliac crest to mid-thigh) |
| **Hand roll / hand splint** | **Prevents finger flexion contracture**, functional position of hand |
| **Trapeze bar (monkey bar)** | Patient lifts self – **upper-body strengthening**, repositioning (contraindicated – some spinal/ abdominal surgery) |
| **Side rails** | Safety (fall prevention) – but entrapment risk; not a restraint substitute |
| **Air ring / Donut cushion** | **Old practice – now NOT recommended** (↓ circulation in centre → ulcer) |
| **Knee rest/ knee roll** | Prevents sliding down; but **prolonged knee gatch – DVT (popliteal compression)** |
| **Heel protector / Elbow protector** | Pressure relief |
| **Pillows** | Support, positioning |
| **Hot water bottle** | Warmth – **fill ⅔, expel air, temp 52 °C (adults), 40–46 °C (children/ elderly/ unconscious)**, cover, **never on unconscious/ paralysed/ diabetic** (burns) |
| **Ice cap/ ice collar** | Cold application (post-tonsillectomy, headache) |

:::exam PYQ style
- Eye cleaning – **inner to outer canthus**.
- Unconscious patient oral care – **side-lying position**, suction ready.
- Bath water temperature – **43–46 °C (110–115 °F)**.
- Perineal care female – **front to back**.
- Massage stroke – long gliding = **effleurage**; kneading = **petrissage**.
- Bed after discharge – **closed bed**; post-op bed – top linen **fan-folded to side**.
- Bed cradle used to – **keep weight of linen off the patient**.
- Footdrop prevention – **footboard**; external hip rotation prevention – **trochanter roll**.
- Head lice treatment – **permethrin 1 %**; repeat after **7–10 days**.
- Dentures stored in – **labelled cup with water**; remove **upper first**.
- Linen should not be – **shaken**; mitred corner angle – **45°**.
:::
