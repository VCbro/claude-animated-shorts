## 1.1 Nursing – Definitions

:::def Famous definitions (exam me directly poochhe jaate hain)
- **Virginia Henderson (1966 – ICN accepted)**: "The unique function of the nurse is to **assist the individual, sick or well**, in the performance of those activities contributing to health or its recovery (or to peaceful death) that he would perform unaided if he had the necessary strength, will or knowledge, and to do this in such a way as to help him **gain independence as rapidly as possible**." → "**Mother of modern nursing definition**"; **14 basic needs**.
- **Florence Nightingale (1859, "Notes on Nursing")**: Nursing is "to put the patient in the **best condition for nature to act** upon him." (Environment theory)
- **ICN (2002)**: Nursing encompasses autonomous and collaborative care of individuals of all ages, families, groups and communities, sick or well, and in all settings.
- **ANA (American Nurses Association)**: Nursing is the **diagnosis and treatment of human responses** to actual or potential health problems.
- "Nurse" word – Latin **"Nutrix"** = to nourish / nurture.
:::

### Nursing as a profession – characteristics
- Well-defined **body of knowledge**, **long education**, **service orientation** (altruism), **code of ethics**, **autonomy**, **professional organization**, **licensure/registration**, research-based practice.
- **Registration** – State Nursing Council (Gujarat me – **Gujarat Nursing Council**) – **license to practice**; renewal periodically.
- **Accreditation** – institution ko recognition (INC).
- **Certification** – specialty me competence.

## 1.2 Florence Nightingale – The Lady with the Lamp

:::key Florence Nightingale – must-know facts
- Born **12 May 1820, Florence (Italy)**; died **13 August 1910**, London. Parents – British.
- **Crimean War (1854–56)** – Scutari Barrack Hospital (Turkey) – mortality **42 % → 2 %** by sanitation & hygiene. Soldiers called her "**Lady with the Lamp**" (night rounds). Also "**Angel of Crimea**".
- **1860 – Nightingale Training School for Nurses, St. Thomas' Hospital, London** – first secular nursing school → "**Founder of Modern Nursing**".
- Books: **"Notes on Nursing: What it is and what it is not" (1859)**, "Notes on Hospitals" (1859).
- **Statistician** – invented **Polar area diagram (Coxcomb chart / rose diagram)**; first woman **Fellow of the Royal Statistical Society (1858)**.
- Theory: **Environmental theory** – 10 components (ventilation, warmth, light, noise, cleanliness, bed & bedding, personal cleanliness, variety, food, observation).
- **Order of Merit (1907)** – first woman. **Nightingale Pledge (1893)** – written by **Lystra Gretter** (Farrand Training School, Detroit) – not by Nightingale herself.
- **12 May = International Nurses Day** (ICN since 1974). **Florence Nightingale Award (India)** – by President of India on 12 May.
- Also helped Indian sanitation – **Royal Commission on Health of Army in India (1859)**.
:::

## 1.4 Qualities, Roles & Functions of a Nurse

- **Qualities**: kindness, empathy (not sympathy), honesty, patience, tact, observation skill, sense of responsibility, punctuality, good health, neat appearance, confidentiality, good communication.
- **Empathy** = patient ki feelings ko **samajhna** ("uske jagah khud ko rakh ke") – therapeutic. **Sympathy** = dukh me **sath dukhi** hona – non-therapeutic.
- **Roles**: care-giver, **advocate** (patient ke rights ki raksha – **most important legal-ethical role**), educator, counsellor, communicator, manager, researcher, leader, change agent, coordinator, case manager, consultant.
- **Functions**: **Independent** (nurse ka apna – positioning, health teaching, hygiene), **Dependent** (doctor's order – drugs, IV), **Interdependent/collaborative** (team ke saath).
- **Nursing care delivery models**: **Functional** (task-oriented – one nurse = one task, e.g. medication nurse), **Team nursing** (team leader + members), **Primary nursing** (one nurse **24-hr responsibility** of patient from admission to discharge), **Total patient care / case method** (one nurse – all care during shift; oldest), Case management, Patient-focused care.

## 1.5 Ethics in Nursing

:::def Ethics
**Ethics** = moral principles – kya **sahi** hai aur kya **galat** (right vs wrong conduct). **Law** = legally binding rules. **Morals** = personal beliefs. **Values** = beliefs jo behaviour guide karte hain. **Bioethics** = health care ki ethics.
:::

### Ethical principles
| Principle | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Autonomy** | Patient ka **apna decision lene ka adhikar** (self-determination) | Patient treatment refuse kar sakta hai; **informed consent** |
| **Beneficence** | **Bhalaai karna** (do good) | Pain relief dena |
| **Non-maleficence** | **Nuksaan na karna** ("**do no harm**" – *primum non nocere*) | Side rails up, galat dawa na dena |
| **Justice** | **Fairness** – sabko barabar care | Ameer-gareeb me farak nahi |
| **Veracity** | **Truthfulness** – sach bolna | Patient ko sahi information |
| **Fidelity** | **Promise nibhana**, loyalty | "15 min me aaungi" – to aana |
| **Confidentiality** | Patient ki information **gupt** rakhna | HIV status kisi ko na batana |
| **Accountability** | Apne kaam ki **zimmedari** lena | Galti report karna |
| **Respect for persons / dignity** | | Privacy maintain karna |

:::mnemo "A Big Nurse Just Visited Friends Carefully"
**A**utonomy · **B**eneficence · **N**on-maleficence · **J**ustice · **V**eracity · **F**idelity · **C**onfidentiality

- **Ethical dilemma** – do equally (un)desirable options ke beech choose karna (e.g. terminally ill patient – euthanasia request).
- **Euthanasia** – **mercy killing**. **Active** (lethal injection – **illegal in India**) vs **Passive** (life support withdraw) – **Supreme Court (Aruna Shanbaug case, 2011)** – passive euthanasia allowed under guidelines; **Common Cause vs Union of India (2018)** – **living will / advance directive** valid; simplified procedure **2023**.
- **ICN Code of Ethics (2021)** – 4 elements: **Nurses & patients/people**, **Nurses & practice**, **Nurses & the profession**, **Nurses & global health**. Fundamental responsibilities of nurse: **promote health, prevent illness, restore health, alleviate suffering**.
- **INC Code of ethics / Code of professional conduct** – respect, confidentiality, competence, accountability.

## 1.6 Legal Aspects of Nursing

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Negligence** | Expected standard of care na dena (**omission or commission**) – unintentional; e.g. side rails up na karna → fall |
| **Malpractice** | **Professional negligence** – professional dwara negligence |
| **Elements of malpractice** (4 D's) | **D**uty, **D**ereliction (breach) of duty, **D**amage (injury), **D**irect cause (causation) |
| **Tort** | Civil wrong (not crime) – **intentional** (assault, battery, false imprisonment, defamation, invasion of privacy) / **unintentional** (negligence) |
| **Assault** | **Threat** to touch / harm (without touching) – "Dawa nahi li to injection lagaungi" |
| **Battery** | **Actual touching without consent** – consent ke bina procedure/ injection |
| **False imprisonment** | Bina legal authority ke patient ko rokna – **unnecessary restraints**, discharge rokna (bill ke liye) |
| **Defamation** | Kisi ki reputation ko nuksaan – **Libel** (written) / **Slander** (spoken) |
| **Invasion of privacy** | Bina permission photo, information share |
| **Crime** | Against society/state – e.g. intentional overdose, theft of narcotics |
| **Res ipsa loquitur** | "**The thing speaks for itself**" – e.g. sponge left in abdomen |
| **Respondeat superior** | "**Let the master answer**" – employer bhi responsible for employee's negligence (vicarious liability) |
| **Good Samaritan law** | Emergency me **free/ voluntary help** karne wale ko **legal protection** (India – **Good Samaritan guidelines 2015/ MV Amendment Act 2019 Section 134A**) |
| **Statute of limitations** | Case file karne ki time limit |
| **Subpoena** | Court me hazir hone ka order |
| **Affidavit** | Written statement under oath |
| **Plaintiff / Defendant** | Case karne wala / jis par case |

### Consent
- **Informed consent** – patient ko procedure ka **nature, purpose, risks, benefits, alternatives** samjha ke, **voluntary**, **written**, **signed**.
- Valid consent ke liye: **adult (≥ 18 yrs)**, **mentally competent**, **conscious, not under sedation/ drugs**, **voluntary (no force)**, **informed**, language samajh me aaye.
- **Minor (< 18)** – parent/ guardian; **emergency (life-threatening)** – **implied consent** (no time) – 2 doctors' opinion; unconscious – nearest relative.
- **Responsibility**: explain karna – **doctor/surgeon**; nurse – **witness** & check ki patient ne samjha hai. Doubts ho to nurse doctor ko inform kare.
- Consent **anytime withdraw** ho sakta hai.
- Types: **Express** (oral/ written) vs **Implied** (action se – arm forward karna injection ke liye).

:::warn Legal protection – nurse kya kare?
- **Accurate, timely, legible documentation** ("not documented = not done")
- **Follow hospital policy**, doctor's written order (verbal order – emergency only, **read back**, sign within 24 hrs)
- Galat/ doubtful order → **clarify with doctor** (blindly follow nahi)
- **Incident report** – fill karo (patient record me nahi lagate, mention nahi karte)
- Know limits – **scope of practice**, continuing education, **professional liability insurance**
- Patient's belongings/ valuables – documented, witnessed handover
:::

### Patient's Bill of Rights (AHA 1973) / Charter of Patients' Rights (NHRC, India 2018)
Right to information, records & reports, **emergency medical care**, informed consent, confidentiality, **dignity & privacy**, second opinion, transparency in rates, non-discrimination, safety & quality, choose alternative treatment, choose source of medicines/tests, proper referral & transfer, protection in clinical trials, discharge (body cannot be detained for bills), patient education, grievance redressal.
