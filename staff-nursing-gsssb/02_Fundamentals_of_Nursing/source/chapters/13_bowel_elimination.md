## 13.1 Defecation – Basics

- **Defecation reflex** – rectum stretch → **parasympathetic (S2–S4)** → urge; **internal anal sphincter (involuntary)**, **external (voluntary)**. **Gastrocolic reflex** – strongest **after breakfast/ meals** (best time for bowel training).
- **Valsalva manoeuvre** – forced expiration against closed glottis (straining) → **↑ intrathoracic pressure → ↓ venous return → bradycardia** (vagal) → **dangerous in cardiac, ↑ICP, post-eye/ abdominal surgery patients** → stool softeners.
- Normal frequency – **3 times/day to 3 times/week**; normal stool ~100–200 g/day.

### Stool characteristics
| Feature | Normal | Abnormal & cause |
|---|---|---|
| **Colour** | **Brown** (stercobilin); infant breast-fed – **yellow**, formula-fed – pale yellow | **Black tarry (melena)** – upper GI bleed, **iron supplements (black, not tarry)**, bismuth; **bright red (haematochezia)** – lower GI bleed, haemorrhoids, beetroot; **clay/ pale/ white (acholic)** – **obstructive jaundice (no bile)**, barium; **green** – rapid transit, spinach, infection |
| **Consistency** | Soft, formed (Bristol type 3–4) | **Hard (constipation)**, liquid (diarrhoea), **ribbon/ pencil-like (rectal obstruction/ cancer)** |
| **Odour** | Pungent (bacterial) | Very foul – infection, malabsorption, blood |
| **Constituents** | Water 75 %, undigested fibre, bacteria, bile | **Blood, mucus, pus, fat (steatorrhoea – frothy, floating, greasy – pancreatic/ malabsorption)**, worms, **rice-water (cholera)**, **red-currant jelly (intussusception)** |

- **Bristol Stool Chart** – **7 types**: type 1–2 constipation, **3–4 normal (ideal 4 – smooth snake)**, 5–7 diarrhoea.
- **Meconium** – first stool of newborn – **greenish-black, tarry, sticky**, passed **within 24–48 hrs**; then transitional (green-yellow).

## 13.2 Common Bowel Problems

- **Constipation** – < 3 BMs/week, hard dry stool, straining. Causes: **low fibre, low fluids, immobility, ignoring urge**, opioids/ iron/ antacids (Al, Ca)/ anticholinergics, pregnancy, elderly, hypothyroidism, hypokalaemia.
 - Management: **fluids 2–3 L/day**, **high-fibre diet (fruits, vegetables, whole grains, bran – 25–30 g)**, **exercise/ ambulation**, **regular toilet time (after breakfast – gastrocolic reflex)**, privacy, sitting/ squatting position (knees above hips), warm drinks in morning, prune juice; laxatives (last).
- **Faecal impaction** – hardened stool in rectum can't be passed; sign – **oozing of liquid stool (paradoxical/ overflow diarrhoea)** around mass; Rx – **oil retention enema** → **digital removal (gloved, lubricated finger)** (vagal stimulation risk – **stop if bradycardia/ dizziness**; doctor's order; contraindicated in cardiac patients).
- **Diarrhoea** – ≥ 3 loose stools/day; risk – **dehydration, electrolyte loss (K⁺, HCO₃⁻ → metabolic acidosis)**, skin excoriation; **ORS**, fluids, BRAT diet (old), zinc (children), hand hygiene, perianal skin care; **C. difficile** – contact precautions + soap & water handwash.
- **Faecal incontinence** – skin care, bowel training, pads, rectal tube/ faecal management system (short-term).
- **Flatulence** – gas accumulation (abdominal distension); ambulation, **flatus tube**, avoid gas-forming foods (beans, cabbage, carbonated drinks), **carminatives**; **tympanites** – distension with gas.
- **Haemorrhoids** – dilated rectal veins; sitz bath, high fibre.

### Laxatives
| Type | Example | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **Bulk-forming** | **Isabgol (psyllium)**, methylcellulose, bran | **Safest, most natural** – take with **full glass of water** (else obstruction) |
| **Stool softener (emollient)** | **Docusate sodium** | Post-MI, post-surgery, haemorrhoids – avoid straining |
| **Osmotic** | **Lactulose**, PEG, **magnesium hydroxide (milk of magnesia)**, magnesium sulphate | Lactulose – hepatic encephalopathy; Mg – avoid in renal failure |
| **Stimulant** | **Bisacodyl (Dulcolax)**, senna | **Bisacodyl – don't crush, don't take with milk/ antacid** (enteric-coated) |
| **Lubricant** | **Liquid paraffin (mineral oil)** | ↓ fat-soluble vitamin absorption; lipid aspiration pneumonia (elderly) – give at bedtime upright |

## 13.3 Enema

:::def Enema
Introduction of **fluid/ solution into the rectum & sigmoid colon** through the anus. Greek – "**to send in / inject**".
:::

### Types
| Type | Purpose / Solution | Volume (adult) |
|---|---|---|
| **Cleansing – Soap suds enema (SSE)** | Evacuate bowel – **castile/ mild soap (5 ml in 1000 ml water)** – irritant → peristalsis | **500–1000 ml** (750–1000) |
| **Tap water (hypotonic)** | Cleansing – **don't repeat** (water intoxication, electrolyte loss) – esp. infants | 500–1000 ml |
| **Normal saline (isotonic)** | **Safest** – infants, children, elderly, repeated enemas | 500–1000 ml |
| **Hypertonic – Fleet/ phosphate enema** | **Draws water into colon** – small volume, quick; **avoid in renal failure, dehydration, children < 2** (phosphate toxicity) | **120–250 ml (120 ml)** – retained **5–10 min** |
| **Oil retention enema** | **Softens hard stool** – olive oil, mineral oil, cottonseed oil – **constipation, impaction** | **90–150 ml (100–200)** – retain **1–3 hrs (≥ 30 min)** |
| **Carminative enema** | **Expel flatus** – e.g. **MGW enema: Magnesium sulphate 30 g + Glycerine 60 ml + Water 90 ml** (1-2-3) / turpentine | 60–180 ml |
| **Medicated/ therapeutic** | **Kayexalate (hyperkalaemia)**, **neomycin/ lactulose (hepatic encephalopathy)**, steroid (IBD), anthelmintic | |
| **Nutrient enema** | Historic – glucose/ fluids | |
| **Barium enema** | **Diagnostic – X-ray of colon** | |
| **Return-flow (Harris flush/ colonic irrigation)** | **Flatus removal** – 100–200 ml in & out repeatedly | |
| **Sedative enema** | Paraldehyde/ chloral hydrate (historic) | |
| **Anthelmintic** | Worms | |
| **Astringent** | Bleeding | |

- **Retention enemas** (oil, medicated, nutrient) – **retained for prolonged time**; **evacuant/ non-retention** (cleansing) – expelled quickly (retain **5–15 min**).

:::key Enema procedure – golden numbers
- **Position – LEFT lateral (Sims') with right knee flexed** (follows natural anatomy of **sigmoid colon & descending colon on left side** – gravity flow). Children – dorsal recumbent; patient unable – dorsal recumbent on bedpan (less effective).
- **Solution temperature – 40.5–43 °C (105–110 °F)** for adults (cleansing); **37.7 °C (100 °F) for children**; oil retention – **body temperature (37 °C)**. Too hot → mucosal burn; too cold → cramps.
- **Rectal tube size**: **adult 22–30 Fr**, child 12–18 Fr, infant 10–12 Fr.
- **Insertion length**: **adult 7.5–10 cm (3–4 inch)**, **child 5–7.5 cm (2–3 inch)**, **infant 2.5–3.75 cm (1–1.5 inch)**.
- Lubricate tip **5 cm**; ask patient to **take deep breath / bear down (relaxes external sphincter)**; insert **towards umbilicus** (direction of rectum).
- **Height of can/ bag: 30–45 cm (12–18 inch) above anus** (adult) – **max 45–60 cm**; child ~7.5 cm (3 inch) above hips. Higher → faster flow → cramps, injury.
- Flow – **slow** (5–10 min for 1000 ml); **cramps → stop/ lower bag/ clamp**, deep breaths, resume.
- **Expel air** from tube before insertion (prime) – air → cramps/ distension.
- Ask patient to **retain 5–15 min** (cleansing); bedpan/ commode/ bathroom ready.
- Observe **result (returns)** – amount, colour, consistency; **"Enema till clear"** – **max 3 enemas** (fluid/ electrolyte imbalance).
- **Contraindications**: **acute abdomen/ appendicitis, intestinal obstruction, recent rectal/ colon/ prostate surgery, ↑ICP (Valsalva), severe haemorrhoids, rectal bleeding, perforation**, cardiac arrhythmia (vagal).
:::

| Age | Volume (cleansing) |
|---|---|
| Infant (< 18 months) | **50–150 ml (≤ 250)** |
| Toddler/ preschool (18 months–5 yrs) | **250–350 ml** |
| School-age (5–12 yrs) | **300–500 ml** |
| Adolescent | 500–750 ml |
| **Adult** | **750–1000 ml** |

- **Bowel wash** – repeated enemas till clear return (pre-op, before colonoscopy/ barium).
- **Complications**: **vagal stimulation (bradycardia, fainting)**, fluid & electrolyte imbalance (water intoxication – tap water), **rectal mucosal injury/ perforation**, cramps.

## 13.4 Suppository & Flatus Tube

- **Suppository** – cone-shaped solid medication (cocoa butter/ glycerine base) melting at body temp. **Glycerine suppository** – laxative (softens, lubricates); **bisacodyl (Dulcolax)** – stimulant (works **15–60 min**).
- Procedure: **left lateral (Sims')**, gloves, lubricate, ask to breathe deep/ bear down, insert **beyond internal sphincter** along **rectal wall** (**not into faecal mass** – won't absorb), **adult 7.5–10 cm (3–4 inch; index finger length)**, **child/ infant 5 cm (2 inch) – little finger**; **pointed (tapered) end first** (Indian textbooks ki standard teaching); retain **15–20 min** (laxative) / till absorbed; refrigerate store (melting).
- **Contraindications**: rectal surgery/ bleeding, cardiac (vagal), ↓ platelets/ neutropenia (rectal route avoid).
- **Flatus tube (rectal tube)** – relieve flatulence; size **22–30 Fr** adult; insert **10 cm (4 inch)**; free end in **water container/ bag (bubbling shows gas)**; leave **max 20–30 min** (sphincter damage if longer); repeat after 2–3 hrs if needed.

## 13.5 Stool Specimen & Ostomy Basics

- **Stool specimen**: **1 inch (2.5 cm) formed / 15–30 ml liquid** in clean container with spatula; **not mixed with urine or toilet paper**; **ova & parasites – warm, send within 30 min (fresh)**; culture – sterile container; **occult blood (guaiac/ FOBT)** – avoid **red meat, iron, vitamin C, NSAIDs, aspirin 3 days** before (false +/−); 3 samples on 3 days; label & send promptly.
- **Ostomy** – surgical opening on abdomen: **colostomy** (colon – formed stool, sigmoid), **ileostomy** (ileum – **liquid, continuous, enzyme-rich – skin excoriation, dehydration**). Healthy stoma – **beefy red, moist**; **pale – anaemia; dusky/ purple/ black – ischaemia → report**. Pouch emptied when **⅓–½ full**; opening cut **2–3 mm (⅛ inch) larger** than stoma. Detail – MSN notes.

:::exam PYQ style
- Enema position – **left lateral (Sims')**.
- Rectal tube insertion adult – **7.5–10 cm (3–4 inch)**.
- Enema can height – **30–45 cm (12–18 inch)** above anus.
- Enema solution temperature – **40.5–43 °C (105–110 °F)**.
- Oil retention enema – retain **30 min–3 hrs**; purpose – **soften stool**.
- Soap suds enema – **5 ml castile soap in 1000 ml water**.
- Hypertonic (Fleet) enema volume – **120 ml**.
- Patient complains of cramps during enema – **stop/ lower the can, deep breaths**.
- Melena – **upper GI bleed** (black tarry); clay stool – **obstructive jaundice**.
- Valsalva is dangerous in – **cardiac & ↑ICP** patients.
- Faecal impaction sign – **liquid stool oozing**.
- Flatus tube kept for max – **20–30 min**.
:::
