:::ans Answer Key & Explanation
**1-a** WHO 1946 me bana, definition 1948 se lagu.
**2-a** WHO founding day, 7 April.
**3-a** Discharge planning admission se shuru hoti hai.
**4-b** Do identifiers – naam + DOB/ UHID.
**5-b** Morse ≥ 45 = high risk.
**6-b** Circulation & skin har 15–30 min check.
**7-c** Har 2 hrs release, ROM, toilet.
**8-d** Rescue, Alarm, Contain, Extinguish.
**9-a** Code Blue = arrest.
**10-b** Code Pink = infant/ child abduction.
**11-b** 3–6 ft cross-infection rokne ke liye.
**12-a** Electrical fire par paani nahi.
**13-a** WHO, 2019 se.
**14-b** Early diagnosis = secondary prevention.
**15-d** Move karne se pehle assess karo (spinal/ head injury ho sakti hai).
**16-b** Autonomy ka respect, document karo; rokna false imprisonment hai.
**17-a** Movable frame se – side rail neeche karne par injury nahi.
**18-d** Low bed, locked wheels, call bell sabse effective.
**19-b** Ambulatory pehle (jaldi niklenge), phir wheelchair, phir bed-ridden.
**20-c** Comfort, safety, orientation, assessment.
**21-d** Open question + ID band match.
**22-a** Bed bath safety goal nahi hai.
**23-a** Red fire, Blue arrest, Pink abduction, Black bomb.
**24-a** Dono sahi, R reason hai.
**25-c** Class A = ordinary combustibles.
**26-d** Symptom experience pehla stage.
**27-b** METHOD = Medications, Environment, Treatment, Health teaching, Outpatient, Diet.
:::
