:::ans Answer Key & Explanation
**1-b** Lydia Hall, 1955.
**2-d** Assessment – data collection.
**3-a** Patient primary source hai.
**4-a** Patient jo bataye = subjective.
**5-a** Measurable sign = objective.
**6-b** 2002 se NANDA-International.
**7-c** Problem, Etiology, Signs/symptoms.
**8-a** AEB = defining characteristics.
**9-b** Risk diagnosis me S nahi hota.
**10-d** Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Realistic, Time-bound.
**11-b** Physiological base par hai.
**12-d** Self-actualization top par.
**13-d** Stridor = airway – ABC priority.
**14-c** Measurable verb + criteria + time.
**15-d** Stable, routine task delegate ho sakta hai.
**16-b** Response + etiology + signs; medical term etiology nahi.
**17-a** Reassess & revise – process cyclic hai.
**18-b** Condition badal sakti hai, pehle reassess.
**19-d** Nurse khud initiate karti hai.
**20-c** Doctor ke order par = dependent.
**21-c** Process flexible & dynamic hai.
**22-b** "Understand" measure nahi kar sakte.
**23-d** Assessment data, diagnosis judgement, planning goals, evaluation outcome.
**24-b** Dono sahi, lekin R, A ko explain nahi karta.
**25-c** Salary delegation ka right nahi.
**26-a** Gordon – 11 patterns.
**27-a** Outcome (patient ka change) sabse important.
:::
