## 10.1 Glands & Hormones

@fig endocrine_glands.svg | Endocrine glands aur unke hormones

:::key Feedback & facts
- Most hormones **negative feedback** se control hote hain (e.g. ↑ T4 → ↓ TSH).
- **Pituitary = master gland**, but controlled by **hypothalamus**. Anterior pituitary = **adenohypophysis**, posterior = **neurohypophysis** (stores ADH & oxytocin made in hypothalamus).
- **Pancreas** – both endocrine & exocrine; **Islets of Langerhans**: α (glucagon), **β (insulin – ~70 %)**, δ (somatostatin).
- **Normal fasting blood sugar 70–100 mg/dL**; post-prandial (2 hr) **< 140 mg/dL**.
:::

## 10.2 Diabetes Mellitus (DM)

:::def Diabetes Mellitus
Chronic metabolic disorder with **hyperglycaemia** due to **insulin deficiency, insulin resistance or both**.
:::

### Diagnosis (ADA)
| Test | Diabetes | Pre-diabetes |
|---|---|---|
| **Fasting plasma glucose** (≥ 8 hr fast) | **≥ 126 mg/dL** | 100 – 125 (IFG) |
| **2-hr OGTT (75 g glucose)** | **≥ 200** | 140 – 199 (IGT) |
| **HbA1c** | **≥ 6.5 %** | 5.7 – 6.4 % |
| Random plasma glucose + classic symptoms | **≥ 200** | – |

- **HbA1c (glycosylated Hb)** = average blood sugar of **past 2–3 months** (RBC lifespan 120 days); target in diabetics **< 7 %**; **no fasting needed**.

### Type 1 vs Type 2
| | **Type 1** | **Type 2** (~90–95 %) |
|---|---|---|
| Cause | **Autoimmune destruction of β cells** → absolute insulin deficiency | **Insulin resistance** + relative deficiency |
| Age | Children/young (< 30) | Adults > 40 (now younger too), **obese** |
| Onset | Sudden | Gradual |
| Body | Thin | **Obese** (central) |
| Ketosis | **DKA prone** | HHS prone; rarely DKA |
| Treatment | **Insulin – always** | Diet, exercise, **oral hypoglycaemics**, insulin later |

- **Gestational DM** – first detected in pregnancy (24–28 weeks screening); risk of Type 2 later.
- Classic symptoms (**3 P's**): **Polyuria, Polydipsia, Polyphagia** + weight loss (Type 1), fatigue, blurred vision, slow wound healing, recurrent infections (candidiasis), pruritus vulvae.

### Insulin

| Type | Example | Onset | Peak | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Rapid-acting** | **Lispro, Aspart**, Glulisine | **10–15 min** | 1 hr | 3–4 hr |
| **Short-acting (Regular)** | **Regular (Actrapid) – clear** | **30–60 min** | 2–4 hr | 6–8 hr |
| **Intermediate** | **NPH (Insulatard) – cloudy** | 1–2 hr | 4–12 hr | 12–18 hr |
| **Long-acting (basal)** | **Glargine (Lantus)**, Detemir, Degludec | 1–2 hr | **No peak** | **24 hr** (Degludec 42 hr) |

:::warn Insulin administration – golden rules
- **Only Regular (clear) insulin can be given IV** (DKA, hyperkalaemia).
- Mixing: "**Clear before Cloudy**" – Regular (clear) **pehle draw**, then NPH (cloudy) – **"RN"** (Regular → NPH). Air pehle NPH me daalo, phir Regular me.
- **Glargine – never mix** with other insulins.
- Route **SC**, angle **45–90°**; **abdomen = fastest absorption** (preferred) > arms > thighs > buttocks; **2 inches (5 cm) away from umbilicus**; **rotate sites** within one area (**lipodystrophy/lipohypertrophy** prevention); don't massage.
- Storage: unopened vials **refrigerator (2–8 °C)**, never freeze; in-use vial room temperature up to **28–30 days**; **roll cloudy insulin gently (don't shake)**.
- Insulin syringe **U-100** (100 units/ml); also pens.
- **Somogyi effect** – night **hypoglycaemia (2–3 am)** → rebound morning hyperglycaemia → **reduce bedtime insulin/give snack**. **Dawn phenomenon** – early morning hyperglycaemia due to GH/cortisol (no night hypo) → **increase/adjust evening insulin**. Check **3 am blood sugar** to differentiate.
:::

### Oral hypoglycaemic agents
| Group | Drug | Key point |
|---|---|---|
| **Biguanide** | **Metformin** (first-line Type 2) | **Lactic acidosis**, GI upset, B12 deficiency; **no hypoglycaemia alone**; **stop 48 hrs before/after iodine contrast** & surgery |
| **Sulfonylureas** | Glibenclamide, **Glimepiride**, Gliclazide | **Hypoglycaemia** (most), weight gain; before meals |
| **Meglitinides** | Repaglinide | Take with meal; skip meal = skip dose |
| **DPP-4 inhibitors (-gliptin)** | Sitagliptin, Vildagliptin | Weight neutral |
| **SGLT-2 inhibitors (-gliflozin)** | Empagliflozin, Dapagliflozin | **Genital/urinary infections**, euglycaemic DKA, heart/kidney protective |
| **Thiazolidinediones** | Pioglitazone | Fluid retention – avoid in HF |
| **α-glucosidase inhibitor** | Acarbose | Take with **first bite**; flatulence; treat hypo with **glucose (not sucrose)** |
| **GLP-1 agonists (injectable)** | Liraglutide, Semaglutide | Weight loss, nausea |

### Diet & exercise
- Diet: complex carbs (**50–60 %**), protein 15–20 %, fat < 30 %, **high fibre**, **low glycaemic index** foods, avoid simple sugars, **regular meal timing** (don't skip), artificial sweeteners in moderation.
- Exercise: **150 min/week** moderate; best time **1 hr after meals**; check sugar before; **avoid if sugar > 250 mg/dL with ketones** (or < 100 → take snack); carry sugar; don't inject insulin in exercising limb.

## 10.3 Acute Complications of Diabetes

### Hypoglycaemia (Blood sugar < 70 mg/dL)
- Causes: **too much insulin/OHA, skipped/delayed meal, excess exercise, alcohol**, vomiting.
- S/S (**adrenergic**): **sweating, tremors, palpitation, tachycardia, hunger, anxiety, pallor**; (**neuroglycopenic**): headache, confusion, blurred vision, slurred speech, irritability, **seizures, coma**. **Cold, clammy skin**. Beta-blockers **mask** symptoms. "**Cold & clammy → need some candy.**"

:::key Rule of 15 (conscious patient)
Give **15 g fast-acting carbohydrate** (½ cup / 120 ml fruit juice, 3–4 glucose tablets, 1 tbsp sugar/honey, regular soda ½ cup) → **recheck after 15 min** → still < 70 → repeat → when normal give **complex carb + protein snack** (if next meal > 1 hr).
**Unconscious**: **IV 50 % dextrose (D50) 20–50 ml** or **Glucagon 1 mg IM/SC** (turn patient on side – vomiting). **Nothing by mouth** to unconscious patient.
:::

### DKA vs HHS
| | **DKA (Diabetic Ketoacidosis)** | **HHS (Hyperosmolar Hyperglycaemic State)** |
|---|---|---|
| Type | **Type 1** | **Type 2** (elderly) |
| Cause | Infection (most common), missed insulin, stress | Infection, dehydration |
| Onset | **Rapid (< 24 hr)** | Slow (days) |
| Blood sugar | **> 250 mg/dL** (usually 300–800) | **> 600 mg/dL** (often > 1000) |
| Ketones | **Present (++)** | Absent / minimal |
| pH | **< 7.3 (metabolic acidosis)**, HCO₃⁻ < 18 | > 7.3 |
| Osmolality | Variable | **> 320 mOsm/kg** (very high) |
| Breathing | **Kussmaul**, **fruity (acetone) breath** | Normal |
| Others | Abdominal pain, nausea, vomiting, dehydration | **Severe dehydration**, neuro signs (seizures, hemiparesis, coma) |
| Mortality | Lower | Higher |

:::key DKA management – order matters
1. **Fluids FIRST – 0.9 % NS** (1 L in first hour) → later 0.45 % NS; **when glucose falls to ~200–250 mg/dL → add dextrose (D5 ½NS)** (hypoglycaemia & cerebral oedema prevention)
2. **IV Regular insulin infusion** (0.1 unit/kg/hr) – start **after K⁺ ≥ 3.3** checked; drop sugar **50–75 mg/dL/hr** (not faster)
3. **Potassium** – insulin pushes K into cells → **hypokalaemia** risk → K replacement (once urine output adequate)
4. Bicarbonate only if pH < 6.9
5. Monitor: hourly blood glucose, K⁺, ABG, I/O, neuro status, ECG
:::

## 10.4 Chronic Complications of Diabetes
- **Macrovascular**: CAD/MI (**most common cause of death**), stroke, PVD (gangrene).
- **Microvascular**: **Retinopathy** (most common cause of blindness in adults – yearly fundus exam), **Nephropathy** (most common cause of ESRD – **microalbuminuria** first sign; ACE-I), **Neuropathy** (most common complication – **glove & stocking** numbness; foot ulcers; autonomic – gastroparesis, impotence, orthostatic hypotension, silent MI).

:::tip Diabetic foot care – teaching
- **Daily inspect feet** (use mirror) – cuts, blisters, redness
- Wash with **lukewarm water** (check temp with elbow/thermometer), **dry between toes**, moisturize but **not between toes**
- **Cut nails straight across** (podiatrist best); never cut corns yourself
- **Never walk barefoot**, well-fitting closed shoes, cotton socks, check inside shoes
- **No hot water bottles / heating pads**, no tight socks; stop smoking
- Report any wound immediately (gangrene/amputation risk)
:::

- **Sick day rules**: **never stop insulin** (stress ↑ sugar), check sugar every **3–4 hrs** & **urine ketones**, fluids **every hour** (sugar-free if high, sugar-containing if not eating), soft foods/liquids for carbs, call doctor if vomiting, sugar > 250 persistent, ketones +.

## 10.5 Thyroid Disorders

| Feature | **Hyperthyroidism** (e.g. **Graves' disease** – most common) | **Hypothyroidism** (e.g. **Hashimoto's** – most common; iodine deficiency) |
|---|---|---|
| Metabolism | **↑↑** (everything fast) | **↓↓** (everything slow) |
| Lab | **↓ TSH, ↑ T3/T4** (primary) | **↑ TSH, ↓ T4** (primary) |
| Weight | **Loss** despite ↑ appetite | **Gain**, ↓ appetite |
| Heat/cold | **Heat intolerance**, sweating, warm moist skin | **Cold intolerance**, dry coarse skin |
| Heart | **Tachycardia**, palpitation, **AF**, ↑ SBP (wide pulse pressure) | **Bradycardia**, ↓ cardiac output, hyperlipidaemia |
| GI | **Diarrhoea** | **Constipation** |
| Neuro | **Nervous, irritable, tremors**, insomnia, hyperreflexia | **Lethargy, slow speech, depression**, memory ↓, slow reflexes |
| Eyes/face | **Exophthalmos** (Graves), lid lag, stare | **Periorbital puffiness**, myxoedema face, **loss of outer 1/3 eyebrow** |
| Hair | Fine, soft, hair loss | **Coarse, brittle**, hair loss |
| Menstruation | Amenorrhoea / scanty | **Menorrhagia** |
| Crisis | **Thyroid storm** | **Myxoedema coma** |

:::mnemo Hyper = fast, Hypo = slow
Hyper patient ko **"garam, bhookha, patla, bechain"** socho. Hypo patient ko **"thanda, mota, sust, sona chahta"** socho.
**Cretinism** = congenital hypothyroidism in children (mental retardation) – newborn screening TSH.
:::

### Hyperthyroidism management
- **Antithyroid drugs**: **Methimazole** (Carbimazole), **PTU (Propylthiouracil – preferred in 1st trimester of pregnancy & thyroid storm)**; side effect – **agranulocytosis** (sore throat, fever → report), hepatotoxicity (PTU). 
- **Beta-blockers (Propranolol)** – symptomatic (tachycardia, tremors).
- **Iodine (Lugol's solution / SSKI)** – ↓ vascularity before surgery (**mix in juice/milk, use straw – stains teeth**), 10–14 days pre-op.
- **Radioactive iodine (I-131)** – **contraindicated in pregnancy & lactation**; avoid close contact/children & pregnant women for a few days, flush toilet 2–3 times, separate utensils; risk of **hypothyroidism later** (lifelong thyroxine).
- Diet: **high-calorie, high-protein**, frequent meals; avoid caffeine & stimulants; cool quiet environment.
- **Exophthalmos care**: **elevate HOB**, artificial tears, **tape eyelids closed at night**/eye patches, dark glasses, ↓ salt (oedema).

:::warn Thyroid storm (Thyrotoxic crisis)
Life-threatening – precipitated by surgery, infection, stress, stopping drugs. **Very high fever (> 38.5–41 °C)**, severe tachycardia (> 130), delirium, vomiting, HF, shock.
Rx: **PTU** → (1 hr later) **iodine**, **propranolol**, **hydrocortisone**, **cooling (paracetamol – NOT aspirin**; aspirin frees T4), O₂, fluids.
:::

### Thyroidectomy – post-op care
- Position: **semi-Fowler's/Fowler's**, head & neck **supported with sandbags/pillows**, avoid neck hyperextension/flexion; support neck (hands behind head) while moving.
- Bedside: **tracheostomy set, O₂, suction, calcium gluconate IV**.
- Watch for:
 - **Haemorrhage** – check **back of neck & dressing** (blood pools behind), tight dressing, frequent swallowing.
 - **Respiratory obstruction** (oedema, haematoma, laryngeal nerve injury) – **stridor** = emergency.
 - **Recurrent laryngeal nerve damage** → **hoarseness**; check voice every 2–4 hrs (patient bole "**ee**"); minimize talking (but some hoarseness normal first days due to ET tube).
 - **Hypocalcaemia/tetany** (parathyroid removal/damage) – **tingling around mouth & fingers** (first sign), Chvostek, Trousseau → **IV calcium gluconate** (usually 1–7 days post-op).
 - **Thyroid storm**.
- Total thyroidectomy → **lifelong levothyroxine**.

### Hypothyroidism management
- **Levothyroxine (T4)** – **empty stomach, morning, 30–60 min before breakfast**, same time daily; **lifelong**; start low in elderly & cardiac patients ("start low, go slow" – angina/MI risk); s/e – symptoms of hyperthyroidism (tachycardia, palpitation) if overdose; check TSH every 6–8 weeks initially.
- Low-calorie, **high-fibre** diet (constipation), warmth, skin care. Sedatives/opioids – **very sensitive** (avoid/small doses).
- **Myxoedema coma** – hypothermia, bradycardia, hypoventilation, hyponatraemia, coma; precipitated by cold, infection, sedatives → **IV levothyroxine**, hydrocortisone, ventilatory support, **passive rewarming (blankets – no heating pads)**.
- **Goitre** – enlarged thyroid; endemic (iodine deficiency) – **iodized salt (≥ 15 ppm at consumer level)**; NIDDCP. **21 October = Global Iodine Deficiency Disorders Prevention Day**.

## 10.6 Parathyroid Disorders
- **PTH** → ↑ serum Ca (bone resorption, kidney reabsorption, Vit D activation), ↓ phosphate.
- **Hyperparathyroidism** – adenoma; **hypercalcaemia** – **"bones, stones, groans, moans, psychic overtones"** (bone pain/fractures, renal stones, abdominal pain/constipation, depression/confusion). Rx: **fluids 3–4 L**, mobility (weight bearing), **cranberry juice**, furosemide, calcitonin, bisphosphonates, **parathyroidectomy**.
- **Hypoparathyroidism** – most common cause **accidental removal during thyroidectomy**; **hypocalcaemia + hyperphosphataemia** → **tetany**, Chvostek & Trousseau +, laryngospasm, seizures. Rx: **IV calcium gluconate (acute)**, oral calcium + **Vit D (calcitriol)**, **high-calcium, low-phosphorus diet** (avoid milk excess, cola, processed foods), phosphate binders.

## 10.7 Adrenal Disorders

| | **Addison's disease (Adrenal insufficiency – hypo)** | **Cushing's syndrome (↑ cortisol – hyper)** |
|---|---|---|
| Cause | **Autoimmune** (most common), TB (India), **sudden stoppage of steroids** | **Long-term corticosteroid therapy (most common – iatrogenic)**; Cushing's **disease** = pituitary adenoma (ACTH ↑) |
| Hormones | **↓ Cortisol, ↓ Aldosterone** | **↑ Cortisol** |
| Skin | **Bronze/hyperpigmentation** (↑ ACTH/MSH) – skin creases, gums | **Purple striae** on abdomen, thin fragile skin, bruising, acne, **hirsutism** |
| Body | Weight loss, weakness, fatigue | **Moon face, buffalo hump, truncal (central) obesity**, thin arms & legs |
| BP | **Hypotension** | **Hypertension** |
| Sugar | **Hypoglycaemia** | **Hyperglycaemia** |
| Na / K | **↓ Na, ↑ K** (salt craving) | **↑ Na, ↓ K** (fluid retention) |
| Others | Nausea, vomiting, **salt craving** | Osteoporosis, infections, poor wound healing, mood swings, muscle wasting |
| Rx | **Lifelong hydrocortisone + fludrocortisone**; **↑ dose during stress/illness**; medical ID bracelet; **high-Na, low-K diet** | Treat cause, taper steroids gradually, adrenalectomy/transsphenoidal surgery; **low Na, high K, high protein, low calorie diet**; infection & fall prevention |

:::warn Addisonian crisis (Acute adrenal crisis)
Severe **hypotension, shock**, hypoglycaemia, hyperkalaemia, severe abdominal/back/leg pain, vomiting, confusion → **IV hydrocortisone (100 mg bolus) + IV NS & dextrose** rapidly; treat precipitating cause (stress, infection, surgery, abrupt steroid withdrawal).
**Steroid therapy rule**: never stop suddenly – **taper**; take **with food** (morning – mimic body rhythm); watch infection, sugar, BP.
:::

- **Primary hyperaldosteronism (Conn's syndrome)** – **HTN + hypokalaemia** + hypernatraemia, metabolic alkalosis; Rx – spironolactone, adrenalectomy.
- **Pheochromocytoma** – tumour of **adrenal medulla** → excess **catecholamines** → **paroxysmal severe HTN**, headache, palpitation, sweating (**triad**), anxiety, hyperglycaemia. Test: **24-hr urine VMA / metanephrines** (avoid coffee, tea, banana, chocolate, vanilla, aspirin before test). **Avoid palpation of abdomen** (crisis). Rx: **α-blocker first (phenoxybenzamine)** then β-blocker → **adrenalectomy**.

## 10.8 Pituitary Disorders

| | **Diabetes Insipidus (DI)** | **SIADH** |
|---|---|---|
| Problem | **↓ ADH** (central – head injury, pituitary surgery) or kidney not responding (nephrogenic – lithium) | **↑ ADH** (excess) – **small cell lung cancer**, head injury, meningitis, drugs (carbamazepine, SSRIs) |
| Urine | **Polyuria – large volume (4–20 L/day), very dilute (SG < 1.005)** | **Oliguria, concentrated (SG ↑)** |
| Serum | **↑ Na (hypernatraemia)**, **↑ osmolality** | **↓ Na (dilutional hyponatraemia < 135)**, ↓ osmolality |
| Body | **Dehydration**, severe thirst (polydipsia) | **Water retention**, weight gain **without oedema**, confusion, seizures |
| Rx | **Desmopressin (DDAVP – nasal/oral/IV)**, vasopressin; fluids; I/O, daily weight | **Fluid restriction (500–1000 ml/day – first-line)**, 3 % NS slowly (severe), **demeclocycline**, tolvaptan, furosemide; seizure precautions |

:::mnemo DI vs SIADH
**DI = "Dry Inside"** → paani bahar nikal jata hai (pee a lot, dehydrated, Na high).
**SIADH = "Soaked Inside"** → paani andar ruk jata hai (pee little, Na low).
:::

- **Acromegaly** – **↑ GH in adults** (after epiphyseal closure) → enlarged hands, feet (shoe/ring size ↑), jaw (prognathism), coarse facial features, thick lips/tongue, DM, HTN. **Gigantism** – ↑ GH in children (before epiphyseal closure). **Dwarfism** – ↓ GH in children.
- **Transsphenoidal hypophysectomy** – post-op: **HOB 30°**, **avoid coughing, sneezing, blowing nose, bending, straining** (CSF leak); **check nasal drainage for glucose (halo sign/ glucose > 30 mg/dL = CSF)**; mouth care without tooth-brushing (10 days); watch **DI** (urine output).
- **Hyperprolactinaemia** – galactorrhoea, amenorrhoea, infertility → bromocriptine/cabergoline.
