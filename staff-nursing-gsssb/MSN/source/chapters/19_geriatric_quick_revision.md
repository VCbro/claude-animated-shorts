## 19.1 Geriatric Nursing

- **Geriatrics** = medical care of elderly; **Gerontology** = study of ageing. India – elderly = **60+ yrs** (MWPSC Act 2007 – Maintenance and Welfare of Parents & Senior Citizens). **NPHCE (2010–11)** – National Programme for Health Care of the Elderly. **1 October = International Day of Older Persons**.

| System | Normal ageing changes | Nursing implication |
|---|---|---|
| **Cardiovascular** | ↓ CO, stiff arteries (↑ SBP), ↓ baroreceptor response | **Orthostatic hypotension** – rise slowly |
| **Respiratory** | ↓ vital capacity, ↓ cough reflex, ↑ residual volume | Pneumonia risk – deep breathing, vaccines |
| **GI** | ↓ saliva, ↓ taste, ↓ peristalsis | **Constipation** – fibre, fluids |
| **Renal** | ↓ GFR, ↓ bladder capacity | **Nocturia**, drug toxicity – dose adjust |
| **Musculoskeletal** | ↓ bone mass, ↓ muscle mass (**sarcopenia**) | **Falls & fractures** |
| **Neuro/ Sensory** | Slower reaction, **presbyopia, presbycusis**, ↓ pain & temperature sensation | Safety, burns risk, good lighting |
| **Skin** | Thin, dry, ↓ subcutaneous fat, ↓ sweat | Pressure ulcers, **hypothermia**; less frequent bathing, moisturize |
| **Immune** | ↓ immunity; **fever may be absent** in infection | **Confusion may be first sign of infection** (UTI, pneumonia) |

- **Polypharmacy** – ≥ 5 drugs → adverse effects; **Beers criteria** (potentially inappropriate drugs in elderly); "**start low, go slow**".
- **Falls** – most common cause of injury/ accidental death in elderly → **Morse Fall Scale**; prevention: **non-slip footwear, good lighting/ night light, remove rugs/ clutter, grab bars, bed in lowest position, call bell within reach**, regular toileting, review sedatives, hip protectors, exercise (tai chi).
- **Elder abuse** – physical, psychological, financial, neglect (most common).

### Delirium vs Dementia vs Depression
| | **Delirium** | **Dementia** | **Depression** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Onset | **Acute/ sudden (hours–days)** | **Insidious, gradual (months–years)** | Recent, may be sudden |
| Course | **Fluctuating, worse at night** | Progressive, stable day-to-day | Diurnal (worse morning) |
| Consciousness/ attention | **Altered/ ↓ attention** | Normal till late | Normal |
| Reversible? | **Usually reversible** (treat cause – infection, drugs, hypoxia, dehydration, electrolyte) | **Irreversible** (Alzheimer's) | Treatable |
| Assessment tool | **CAM** (Confusion Assessment Method) | **MMSE** (Mini-Mental State – 30; < 24 impaired) | GDS (Geriatric Depression Scale) |

| **ACE inhibitors** | **Dry cough**, angioedema, hyperkalaemia |

## 19.6 Top 100 One-Liners (MSN) – Revision before exam

:::key One-liners 1–25
1. Normal adult pulse – **60–100/min**; RR – **12–20/min**.
2. Pain is the **5th vital sign**.
3. Nursing process – **ADPIE**; nursing diagnosis – **NANDA**.
4. Abdominal assessment order – **Inspection, Auscultation, Percussion, Palpation**.
5. Most accurate indicator of fluid balance – **daily weight** (1 kg = 1 L).
6. Main ICF cation – **K⁺**; main ECF cation – **Na⁺**.
7. Fluid compatible with blood – **0.9 % NS**.
8. IV KCl – **never IV push**.
9. Tall peaked T wave – **hyperkalaemia**; U wave – **hypokalaemia**.
10. Trousseau & Chvostek – **hypocalcaemia**.
11. Antidote of MgSO₄ – **10 % calcium gluconate**.
12. ROME – Respiratory Opposite, Metabolic Equal.
13. Vomiting → **metabolic alkalosis**; diarrhoea → **metabolic acidosis**.
14. Kussmaul breathing – **DKA**.
15. Neurogenic shock – **hypotension + bradycardia**.
16. Anaphylaxis drug of choice – **adrenaline IM 1:1000**.
17. Shock position – **modified Trendelenburg (legs raised)**.
18. Opioid antidote – **naloxone**.
19. Most effective infection control – **hand hygiene**.
20. Airborne precautions – **TB, measles, chickenpox (N95, negative pressure)**.
21. Sharps – **white puncture-proof container**; glass – **blue**.
22. Autoclave – **121 °C, 15 psi, 15–20 min**.
23. Wound evisceration – **cover with sterile saline-soaked dressing**.
24. Pre-op hair removal – **clipper, just before surgery**.
25. Post-spinal headache – **flat position**, fluids.
:::

:::key One-liners 26–50
26. Malignant hyperthermia – **dantrolene**.
27. Post-op fever 24–48 hrs – **atelectasis**.
28. Most common cause of MI – **coronary thrombosis/ atherosclerosis**; artery – **LAD**.
29. Most specific MI marker – **troponin**.
30. MONA order of giving – **O₂, Aspirin, Nitrate, Morphine**.
31. NTG – **max 3 tablets, 5 min apart**.
32. Digoxin toxicity – **yellow-green vision**, nausea; antidote **Digibind**.
33. Left HF – **lungs (crackles, orthopnoea)**; Right HF – **body (oedema, JVD)**.
34. ACE inhibitor side effect – **dry cough**.
35. Heparin – **aPTT, protamine**; Warfarin – **PT/INR, Vit K**.
36. Pericarditis pain relieved by – **sitting up & leaning forward**.
37. Beck's triad – **cardiac tamponade**.
38. Arterial ulcer – legs **dependent**; venous ulcer – legs **elevated**.
39. Adult CPR – **30:2, 100–120/min, 5–6 cm**.
40. Shockable rhythms – **VF, pulseless VT**.
41. COPD O₂ – **low flow 1–2 L / Venturi**, SpO₂ 88–92 %.
42. Pink puffer – **emphysema**; blue bloater – **chronic bronchitis**.
43. Asthma rescue drug – **salbutamol**.
44. Mantoux read at – **48–72 hrs**; positive **≥ 10 mm**.
45. Rifampicin – **orange urine**; INH – **B6**; Ethambutol – **eye**.
46. Tension pneumothorax – **trachea shifts to opposite side**; needle at **2nd ICS MCL**.
47. Chest tube continuous bubbling in water seal – **air leak**.
48. Tracheal suction – **max 10–15 sec**, hyperoxygenate.
49. Duodenal ulcer pain – **relieved by food**; gastric – **worsened by food**.
50. Most common cause of peptic ulcer – **H. pylori**.
:::

:::key One-liners 51–75
51. Appendicitis pain point – **McBurney's**; Rovsing's – LLQ pressure → RLQ pain.
52. Suspected appendicitis – **no heat, no enema/laxative**.
53. Dumping syndrome – **no fluids with meals, lie down after meals, low carb**.
54. NG tube placement – **X-ray (gold standard)**, pH ≤ 5.
55. Healthy stoma – **beefy red, moist**.
56. Colostomy irrigation height – **45–50 cm**.
57. Hepatitis A & E – **faeco-oral**; B, C, D – **blood**.
58. Hepatic encephalopathy – **lactulose**; sign – **asterixis**.
59. Varices bleeding – **band ligation**, octreotide, SB tube.
60. Cholecystitis – **Murphy's sign**; pain to **right shoulder**.
61. Pancreatitis – **lipase**; position – **sitting leaning forward/ fetal**.
62. Most common cause of CKD – **diabetes**.
63. AV fistula patency – **thrill & bruit**; no BP on that arm.
64. PD complication – **peritonitis (cloudy effluent)**.
65. HD complication – **hypotension**; disequilibrium syndrome.
66. Calcium oxalate stones – avoid **spinach, tomato, chocolate, tea**.
67. Nephrotic syndrome – **massive proteinuria, hypoalbuminaemia, oedema, hyperlipidaemia**.
68. AGN – **cola-coloured urine, HTN, periorbital oedema**.
69. TURP – **CBI**, TUR syndrome = **hyponatraemia**.
70. Foley balloon – **sterile water**.
71. Diabetes FBS – **≥ 126 mg/dL**; HbA1c – **≥ 6.5 %**.
72. Only IV insulin – **regular**; mix – **clear before cloudy**.
73. Hypoglycaemia – **rule of 15**; unconscious – **D50 / glucagon**.
74. DKA first – **IV 0.9 % NS**, then insulin; watch **K⁺**.
75. Somogyi – **night hypo → morning hyper**.
:::

:::key One-liners 76–100
76. Graves' – **exophthalmos**; drug – methimazole/ PTU.
77. Post-thyroidectomy bedside – **tracheostomy set, calcium gluconate**.
78. Addison's – **bronze skin, ↓ Na, ↑ K, hypotension**.
79. Cushing's – **moon face, buffalo hump, striae, ↑ sugar**.
80. DI – **polyuria, ↑ Na, desmopressin**; SIADH – **↓ Na, fluid restriction**.
81. GCS – **3–15**; ≤ 8 – **intubate**.
82. Earliest sign of ↑ ICP – **change in LOC**; late – **Cushing's triad**.
83. ↑ ICP position – **HOB 30°, neck neutral**.
84. Epidural haematoma – **lucid interval** (middle meningeal artery).
85. Stroke thrombolysis – **within 4.5 hrs** after CT rules out bleed.
86. Seizure – **never put anything in mouth**; status – **IV lorazepam**.
87. Phenytoin – **gingival hyperplasia**.
88. Autonomic dysreflexia – **sit up, check bladder**.
89. Parkinson's – **↓ dopamine, pill-rolling tremor**; MG – **ptosis, pyridostigmine**.
90. Compartment syndrome – **pain on passive stretch**, limb at heart level, fasciotomy.
91. Fat embolism – **long bone fracture, petechiae, hypoxia**.
92. THR – **no hip flexion > 90°, no crossing legs, abduction pillow**.
93. Crutches – weight on **hands**; stairs **up with good, down with bad**.
94. Cane – **opposite (strong) side**.
95. Parkland – **4 ml × kg × %TBSA (RL)**, half in **first 8 hrs**.
96. Burns – adequacy of fluid – **urine output 0.5 ml/kg/hr**.
97. Transfusion reaction – **stop transfusion first**, keep line open with NS.
98. Platelets storage – **20–24 °C with agitation**, 5 days.
99. Glaucoma – **atropine contraindicated**; open-angle – **tunnel vision**.
100. Epistaxis – **sit up, lean forward, pinch nose 10–15 min**.
