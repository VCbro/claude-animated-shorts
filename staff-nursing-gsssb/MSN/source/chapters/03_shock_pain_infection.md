## 3.1 Shock

:::def Definition
**Shock** = Ek life-threatening condition jisme **tissue perfusion inadequate** ho jata hai → cells ko O₂ & nutrients nahi milte → anaerobic metabolism → **lactic acidosis** → organ failure.
:::

### Types of Shock

| Type | Cause | Key feature |
|---|---|---|
| **Hypovolaemic** (most common) | Haemorrhage, burns, severe vomiting/diarrhoea, dehydration | ↓ blood volume; **cold clammy skin**, tachycardia |
| **Cardiogenic** | **MI** (most common), arrhythmia, cardiomyopathy | Heart pump fail; **crackles, JVD**, ↑CVP |
| **Obstructive** | Cardiac tamponade, tension pneumothorax, massive PE | Blood flow me rukawat |
| **Distributive – Septic** | Infection (Gram –ve bacteria most common) | Early: **warm flushed skin, fever**; late: cold |
| **Distributive – Anaphylactic** | Allergy (penicillin, bee sting, food) | Bronchospasm, stridor, urticaria, angioedema |
| **Distributive – Neurogenic** | **Spinal cord injury** (T6 ke upar), spinal anaesthesia | **Hypotension + BRADYCARDIA**, warm dry skin |

:::exam Neurogenic shock ki pehchaan
Sab shocks me **tachycardia** hoti hai – **sirf Neurogenic shock me BRADYCARDIA** (sympathetic tone loss). Skin **warm & dry**.
:::

### Stages of Shock

| Stage | Features |
|---|---|
| **1. Initial** | Cellular level changes, no clinical signs |
| **2. Compensatory** | BP normal (body compensate karta hai), **tachycardia**, tachypnoea, cold pale skin, ↓ urine, restlessness/anxiety (**earliest sign = restlessness/change in mental status**) |
| **3. Progressive** | **BP gir jata hai** (SBP < 90), MAP < 65, oliguria, metabolic acidosis, confusion, weak pulse |
| **4. Refractory (Irreversible)** | Treatment ka response nahi, MODS (multi-organ failure), death |

### Management of Shock
- **ABC** – airway, high-flow **O₂**
- Position: **Modified Trendelenburg** (supine with **legs raised 20–30°**) – head-down nahi
- **2 large bore IV cannula (14–16 G)** – fluids: crystalloids (**NS/RL**), blood for haemorrhage
- Vasopressors: **Noradrenaline** (septic shock – drug of choice), Dopamine, Dobutamine (cardiogenic – inotrope)
- **Anaphylaxis: Adrenaline (Epinephrine) 1:1000, 0.3–0.5 mg IM** (anterolateral thigh) – **drug of choice**
- Septic: blood culture **before** antibiotics, broad-spectrum antibiotics within 1 hour
- Monitor: vitals every 5–15 min, **urine output (Foley, hourly ≥ 30 ml/hr)**, CVP (normal **2–8 mmHg** / 5–10 cmH₂O), mental status, ABG, lactate

:::warn Shock me kya NAHI karna
- Hypovolaemic shock me pehle **fluids** – vasopressor nahi (tank khali hai to pump kya karega).
- Cardiogenic shock me **fluids carefully** – overload → pulmonary oedema.
- Patient ko garam kambal se **over-heat mat karo** (vasodilation) – sirf normal warmth.
:::

## 3.2 Pain

:::def Definition (IASP)
"Pain is an unpleasant sensory and emotional experience associated with actual or potential tissue damage." – **Pain is whatever the patient says it is** (Margo McCaffery). Pain = **5th vital sign**.
:::

| Type | Description |
|---|---|
| **Acute** | < 3–6 months, sudden, protective, e.g. post-op |
| **Chronic** | > 3–6 months, persistent, e.g. arthritis, cancer |
| **Referred** | Kisi aur jagah mehsoos, e.g. MI → left arm/jaw; gallbladder → right shoulder; appendix → periumbilical |
| **Phantom limb** | Amputated part me dard (real pain hai) |
| **Neuropathic** | Nerve damage, burning/shooting – e.g. diabetic neuropathy |
| **Visceral** | Internal organs – diffuse, cramping |

### Pain assessment
- **PQRST**: **P**rovoking factors, **Q**uality, **R**egion/Radiation, **S**everity (0–10), **T**iming
- Scales: **Numeric Rating Scale (0–10)**, Visual Analogue Scale (10 cm line), **Wong-Baker FACES** (children ≥ 3 yrs), **FLACC** (infants, non-verbal), Behavioural pain scale (ICU)
- **Gate Control Theory** – **Melzack & Wall (1965)**: massage, TENS se "gate" band → pain kam.

### WHO Analgesic Ladder (cancer pain)
1. **Step 1 – Mild pain**: Non-opioid (Paracetamol, NSAIDs) ± adjuvant
2. **Step 2 – Moderate pain**: Weak opioid (**Codeine, Tramadol**) + non-opioid ± adjuvant
3. **Step 3 – Severe pain**: Strong opioid (**Morphine**, Fentanyl) + non-opioid ± adjuvant
- Dawa **"by the clock"** (regular interval) – PRN nahi; **by mouth** preferred.

:::warn Opioids – nursing care
- Side effects: **respiratory depression** (most serious – RR < 12 par hold), **constipation** (most common – laxative do), nausea, sedation, urinary retention, pinpoint pupils.
- **Antidote = Naloxone (Narcan)**.
- **PCA** (Patient Controlled Analgesia) – sirf patient button dabaye, family nahi.
:::

## 3.3 Inflammation & Wound Healing

- **5 cardinal signs of inflammation**: **Rubor** (redness), **Calor** (heat), **Tumor** (swelling), **Dolor** (pain), **Functio laesa** (loss of function – Virchow).
- First 4 = **Celsus**.

### Phases of wound healing
1. **Haemostasis** – minutes (platelet plug, clot)
2. **Inflammatory phase** – day 0–4 (neutrophils, macrophages; redness, swelling)
3. **Proliferative phase** – day 4–21 (**granulation tissue**, collagen, epithelialization)
4. **Maturation / Remodelling** – 21 days to 1–2 years (scar strong hota hai)

| Intention | Example |
|---|---|
| **Primary intention** | Clean surgical wound, edges approximated (sutured) – minimal scar |
| **Secondary intention** | Wound open chhoda, granulation se bharta hai – pressure ulcer, burns, infected wound – bada scar |
| **Tertiary (delayed primary)** | Pehle open rakhte hain (infection control), phir 3–5 din baad suture |

:::key Wound healing ke liye zaroori nutrients
**Protein** (most important), **Vitamin C** (collagen synthesis), **Vitamin A**, **Zinc**, adequate calories & hydration. Delay karne wale: diabetes, steroids, smoking, infection, malnutrition, old age, poor circulation.
:::

### Wound complications
- **Dehiscence** = wound edges ka khulna (usually 5–10 post-op day)
- **Evisceration** = wound se **intestine/organs bahar aa jaye** → **EMERGENCY**: patient ko **low Fowler's with knees bent**, organs ko **sterile saline-soaked dressing** se dhako, NPO, surgeon ko turant inform; pushing back mat karo.
- Prevention: **splinting** the incision with a pillow while coughing.

## 3.4 Infection Control

### Chain of Infection (6 links)
**Infectious agent → Reservoir → Portal of exit → Mode of transmission → Portal of entry → Susceptible host**. Kisi bhi ek link ko todo → infection ruk jayega. **Hand hygiene** = single most effective method.

:::key WHO – 5 Moments of Hand Hygiene
1. **Before** touching a patient
2. **Before** clean/aseptic procedure
3. **After** body fluid exposure risk
4. **After** touching a patient
5. **After** touching patient surroundings
Hand wash duration: soap-water **40–60 sec**; alcohol rub **20–30 sec**. Steps: **6 (WHO)**.
:::

### Asepsis
- **Medical asepsis** (clean technique) – microorganisms ki sankhya kam karna (hand washing, gloves).
- **Surgical asepsis** (sterile technique) – **saare** microorganisms + spores khatam (OT, catheterization, IV).
- **Sterilization** – all microbes + spores destroy: **Autoclave (moist heat) 121 °C, 15 psi, 15–20 min** (or 134 °C for 3–4 min); dry heat (hot air oven) 160 °C × 2 hr; ETO gas (heat sensitive items); gamma radiation (disposables).
- **Disinfection** – spores ko chhod ke baaki microbes: glutaraldehyde 2 % (Cidex – endoscopes), sodium hypochlorite 1 % (blood spills), alcohol 70 %.

:::warn Sterile field rules
- Sterile field ke **1 inch border = non-sterile**.
- **Waist level ke neeche** & peeth ke peeche = non-sterile.
- Sterile object doubt ho to **non-sterile maano**.
- Sterile field ko kabhi **peeth mat dikhao** / **unattended mat chhodo**.
- Wet sterile drape = contaminated (strike-through).
:::

### Isolation precautions (CDC)
| Precaution | Diseases | PPE / Room |
|---|---|---|
| **Standard** | **Sab patients** | Hand hygiene, gloves, mask/gown as needed |
| **Airborne** (< 5 µm) | **TB, Measles, Chickenpox**, disseminated herpes zoster | **N95 respirator**, **negative pressure room** (6–12 air changes/hr), door closed |
| **Droplet** (> 5 µm, 3 feet/1 m) | **Influenza, Mumps, Rubella, Diphtheria, Pertussis, Meningococcal** meningitis | Surgical mask, private room |
| **Contact** | **MRSA, VRE**, C. difficile, scabies, wound infection, herpes simplex | Gloves + gown, dedicated equipment |

:::mnemo Airborne = "MTV"
**M**easles, **T**B, **V**aricella (chickenpox) → Airborne + N95.
:::

- PPE **donning** order: Gown → Mask/N95 → Goggles/face shield → **Gloves** (last).
- PPE **doffing** order: **Gloves** (first, most contaminated) → Goggles → Gown → Mask → hand hygiene.

### Biomedical Waste Management (BMW Rules 2016, amended 2018)
| Colour | Waste |
|---|---|
| **Yellow** | Human anatomical waste, soiled dressings/cotton, expired medicines, chemical waste, micro-lab waste → **incineration** |
| **Red** | **Contaminated recyclable plastic** – IV sets, catheters, tubing, syringes (without needle), gloves, urine bags → autoclave & recycle |
| **White (translucent, puncture-proof)** | **Sharps** – needles, blades, scalpels |
| **Blue** (cardboard box) | **Glassware** – broken/discarded vials, ampoules, metallic implants |

- **Needle stick injury** → wash with soap & running water (do NOT squeeze/scrub), report, check source status, **PEP within 2 hours (max 72 hrs)** for HIV; Hep B vaccine status check.
- Needle ko **recap mat karo** (if needed, one-hand scoop technique).
