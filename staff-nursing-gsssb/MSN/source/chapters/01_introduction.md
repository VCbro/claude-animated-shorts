## 1.1 Medical-Surgical Nursing kya hai?

:::def Definition
**Medical-Surgical Nursing (MSN)** = Adult patients (18+ years) ki nursing care jo *medical illness* (dawai se treat) ya *surgical condition* (operation se treat) ke kaaran hospital me hain. Isme health promotion, illness prevention, cure aur rehabilitation sab aata hai.
:::

- **Medical** = Diagnosis & treatment by drugs, diet, rest (e.g. Diabetes, Hypertension, Pneumonia)
- **Surgical** = Treatment by operation (e.g. Appendectomy, Cholecystectomy, Fracture fixation)
- Founder of modern nursing: **Florence Nightingale** (Lady with the Lamp, born *12 May 1820*) → 12 May = *International Nurses Day*.

### Health & Illness – important definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Health (WHO 1948)** | "A state of complete physical, mental and social well-being and not merely the absence of disease or infirmity" |
| **Disease** | Body ke structure/function me abnormal change (objective) |
| **Illness** | Patient ki apni feeling ki wo bimar hai (subjective) |
| **Acute illness** | Achanak, short duration (< 6 months), e.g. appendicitis |
| **Chronic illness** | Dheere, long duration (> 6 months), e.g. DM, CKD |
| **Incidence** | *Naye* cases in a time period |
| **Prevalence** | *Total* (old + new) cases at a time |
| **Prognosis** | Disease ka expected outcome |
| **Etiology** | Disease ka cause |
| **Iatrogenic** | Treatment / health worker ki wajah se hui problem |
| **Nosocomial** | Hospital acquired infection (48 hrs after admission) |
| **Idiopathic** | Cause unknown |

### Levels of Prevention (Leavell & Clark)

| Level | Goal | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Primordial** | Risk factor hi na aaye | Healthy lifestyle in children |
| **Primary** | Disease hone se pehle rokna | Immunization, health education, chemoprophylaxis |
| **Secondary** | Early diagnosis & prompt treatment | Screening (Pap smear, BP check, mammography) |
| **Tertiary** | Disability limitation & rehabilitation | Physiotherapy after stroke, prosthesis |

## 1.2 Roles of Medical-Surgical Nurse

- **Care giver** – direct bedside care
- **Educator** – patient & family ko health teaching
- **Advocate** – patient ke rights ki raksha
- **Counsellor**, **Manager/Coordinator**, **Researcher**, **Change agent**, **Leader**

## 1.3 Nursing Process – ADPIE

:::mnemo ADPIE (5 steps)
**A**ssessment → **D**iagnosis → **P**lanning → **I**mplementation → **E**valuation
Nursing process ka concept: **Lydia Hall (1955)** ne diya. 5 steps me nursing diagnosis step **NANDA** (1973) se aaya.
:::

### Step 1 – Assessment (data collection)
- **Subjective data** = patient jo *bolta* hai (symptoms) – e.g. "mujhe dard ho raha hai"
- **Objective data** = nurse jo *dekhti/measure* karti hai (signs) – e.g. BP 150/90, wound redness
- **Primary source** = patient khud (best source); **Secondary** = family, records, lab reports
- Methods: Interview, Observation, Physical examination

:::key Physical Examination ka order
**I**nspection → **P**alpation → **P**ercussion → **A**uscultation (IPPA)
**Abdomen me exception:** Inspection → **Auscultation** → Percussion → Palpation (taaki palpation se bowel sounds change na ho).
:::

### Step 2 – Nursing Diagnosis (NANDA-I)
- Nursing diagnosis = patient ka *response* to health problem (doctor ki tarah disease ka naam nahi).
- **PES format**: **P**roblem + **E**tiology ("related to") + **S**igns/Symptoms ("as evidenced by")
- Example: *Acute pain related to surgical incision as evidenced by pain score 7/10 and facial grimacing.*

| Type | Example |
|---|---|
| **Actual** | Impaired gas exchange r/t … AEB … |
| **Risk** | Risk for infection r/t invasive lines (no AEB part) |
| **Health promotion / Wellness** | Readiness for enhanced nutrition |
| **Syndrome** | Rape-trauma syndrome |

:::warn Doctor vs Nurse diagnosis
Medical diagnosis = **disease** (e.g. Pneumonia) – treatment ke saath badalta nahi.
Nursing diagnosis = **patient ka response** (e.g. Ineffective airway clearance) – din me bhi badal sakta hai.
:::

### Step 3 – Planning
- **Priority setting** – *Maslow's Hierarchy* use karo: Physiological → Safety → Love/belonging → Esteem → Self-actualization.
- **ABC rule**: **A**irway → **B**reathing → **C**irculation (sabse pehle airway!)
- Goals **SMART** hone chahiye: **S**pecific, **M**easurable, **A**chievable, **R**ealistic, **T**ime-bound.

### Step 4 – Implementation
- **Independent** actions – nurse khud (positioning, health education, turning)
- **Dependent** – doctor ke order par (medicine, IV fluid)
- **Interdependent / Collaborative** – team ke saath (physiotherapist, dietician)

### Step 5 – Evaluation
Goal met / partially met / not met → plan revise karo. Nursing process **cyclic & dynamic** hai.

:::tip Maslow & ABC ka exam me use
Jab question pooche "**Nurse ki first / priority action kya hai?**" – to hamesha socho: (1) Airway, (2) Breathing, (3) Circulation, (4) Safety, phir comfort, phir teaching. Assessment hamesha intervention se pehle aata hai – jab tak emergency (jaise cardiac arrest) na ho.
:::

## 1.4 Vital Signs – Normal Values (Adult)

| Vital sign | Normal | Abnormal terms |
|---|---|---|
| Temperature (oral) | 36.5 – 37.5 °C (97.7–99.5 °F) | Pyrexia >37.5 °C; Hyperpyrexia >41 °C; Hypothermia <35 °C |
| Pulse | 60 – 100 /min | Tachycardia >100; Bradycardia <60 |
| Respiration | 12 – 20 /min | Tachypnoea >20; Bradypnoea <12; Apnoea = no breathing |
| BP | <120/<80 mmHg | Hypertension ≥140/90 (JNC-7) |
| SpO₂ | 95 – 100 % | Hypoxaemia <90 % |
| Pain | "5th vital sign" | Scale 0–10 |

- **Pulse pressure** = Systolic − Diastolic (normal ~40 mmHg)
- **MAP (Mean Arterial Pressure)** = (SBP + 2 × DBP) ÷ 3 → normal **70–100 mmHg**; organ perfusion ke liye ≥ 65 chahiye.
- Pulse lene ke liye best site: **Radial**; cardiac arrest me **Carotid**; infants me **Brachial**; apex beat: *Left 5th intercostal space, mid-clavicular line* (1 full minute).
- Rectal temperature oral se **~0.5 °C zyada**, axillary **~0.5 °C kam**.

:::exam Exam me aksar
- **Pulse deficit** = Apical rate − Radial rate (Atrial fibrillation me milta hai). Do nurses ek saath count karti hain.
- **Cheyne-Stokes breathing** = deep-shallow-apnoea cycle → near death, heart failure, ↑ICP.
- **Kussmaul breathing** = deep, rapid → **DKA** (metabolic acidosis).
- **Biot's breathing** = irregular with apnoea → meningitis, brain damage.
:::

## 1.5 Important Patient Positions

@fig patient_positions.svg | Important positions – kab kaunsa use karein

:::key Positions – quick list
- **Fowler's** (45–60°) / **High Fowler's** (90°) → breathlessness, heart failure, after thyroidectomy, NG insertion
- **Semi-Fowler's** (30°) → raised ICP, post-abdominal surgery, enteral feeding (aspiration rokne ke liye)
- **Supine** → post spinal anaesthesia (headache rokne ke liye flat), spinal injury
- **Prone** → ARDS ("proning"), after lumbar spine surgery
- **Left lateral / Sim's** → enema, rectal examination, unconscious patient (aspiration rokta hai)
- **Right lateral** → **after liver biopsy** (pressure se bleeding rukti hai)
- **Lithotomy** → vaginal exam, delivery, TURP, cystoscopy
- **Knee-chest** → sigmoidoscopy, cord prolapse
- **Trendelenburg** (head down) → pelvic surgery; shock me ab **modified Trendelenburg** (sirf legs 20–30° up)
- **Reverse Trendelenburg** → head up – hiatus hernia, GERD
:::

## 1.6 Documentation & Records

- Rule: *"Not documented = Not done."*
- Ink pen se likho, error par **single line** kheecho, "error" likh ke sign karo; **white-ink / overwriting nahi**.
- **SOAP** = Subjective, Objective, Assessment, Plan (SOAPIE me + Intervention, Evaluation)
- **SBAR** (handover) = **S**ituation, **B**ackground, **A**ssessment, **R**ecommendation
- **Kardex** = quick reference card of patient care plan
- **Incident report** = koi accident/error hone par (patient file me nahi lagate)
- **Informed consent** – doctor ki responsibility, nurse **witness** banti hai. Patient adult (18+), conscious, sedation-free hona chahiye; minor me parent/guardian.
