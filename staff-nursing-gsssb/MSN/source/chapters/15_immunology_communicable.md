## 15.1 Immunity – Basics

| Type | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Innate (natural)** | Born with; non-specific | Skin, mucosa, phagocytes, fever |
| **Active natural** | Disease hone ke baad | Chickenpox ke baad immunity |
| **Active artificial** | **Vaccine** | BCG, OPV, MR – long-lasting |
| **Passive natural** | Mother se | **Placenta (IgG)**, breast milk (**IgA**) |
| **Passive artificial** | Ready-made antibodies | **Immunoglobulins – ATS/TIG, anti-rabies Ig, HBIG, anti-D, antivenom** – immediate but short |

- **Humoral immunity** – **B-lymphocytes** → plasma cells → antibodies. **Cell-mediated** – **T-lymphocytes** (CD4 helper, CD8 cytotoxic).
- Immunoglobulins: **IgG** (most abundant, **crosses placenta**, secondary response), **IgM** (largest, **first** antibody in primary response – acute infection), **IgA** (secretions, **colostrum**), **IgE** (**allergy, parasites**), IgD.

### Hypersensitivity reactions (Gell & Coombs)
| Type | Mechanism | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **I – Immediate / Anaphylactic** | **IgE**, mast cells, histamine | **Anaphylaxis, asthma, hay fever, urticaria, penicillin allergy, bee sting** |
| **II – Cytotoxic** | IgG/IgM against cells | **ABO transfusion reaction, Rh incompatibility**, Goodpasture, myasthenia gravis |
| **III – Immune complex** | Ag-Ab complexes deposit | **SLE, post-streptococcal GN, serum sickness**, RA, Arthus reaction |
| **IV – Delayed (cell-mediated)** | T cells; 24–72 hrs | **Mantoux (tuberculin) test**, contact dermatitis (poison ivy, nickel), **transplant rejection** |

:::mnemo ACID
Type **I – A**naphylactic · Type **II – C**ytotoxic · Type **III – I**mmune complex · Type **IV – D**elayed
:::

### Anaphylaxis
- Severe life-threatening type I reaction – **within minutes**; triggers: drugs (**penicillin – most common drug**, NSAIDs, contrast), foods (peanuts, shellfish), insect stings, **latex**.
- S/S: urticaria, flushing, **angioedema**, **stridor/wheeze**, dyspnoea, **hypotension**, tachycardia, nausea, feeling of impending doom.
- Rx: **Stop the trigger** → **Adrenaline 1:1000 (1 mg/ml) 0.5 mg IM (0.01 mg/kg; child max 0.3 mg) – anterolateral thigh**, repeat every 5–15 min → airway & **high-flow O₂** → supine with legs raised (breathing difficulty → sit up) → IV fluids → **antihistamines (chlorpheniramine), hydrocortisone**, nebulized salbutamol.
- Prevention: **test dose before penicillin/ATS/antivenom** (as per policy), allergy band, **EpiPen** for known allergies, **wait 15–30 min after injections/vaccines**.
- **Latex allergy** – banana, avocado, kiwi, chestnut cross-sensitivity.

### SLE (Systemic Lupus Erythematosus)
- Chronic **autoimmune** multisystem disease; **young women (15–45), 9 : 1**.
- S/S: **butterfly (malar) rash** across cheeks & nose (sun-sensitive – **photosensitivity**), **joint pain (arthralgia)**, fatigue, fever, **lupus nephritis** (most serious), pleuritis, pericarditis, oral ulcers, alopecia, anaemia, Raynaud's.
- Tests: **ANA (screening, most sensitive)**, **anti-dsDNA & anti-Smith (most specific)**, ↓ complement.
- Rx: **hydroxychloroquine** (eye exams), NSAIDs, **corticosteroids**, immunosuppressants; **avoid sun (sunscreen SPF 30+)**, rest, infection prevention, report fever.
- **Drug-induced lupus** – hydralazine, procainamide, isoniazid.

## 15.2 HIV / AIDS

:::def HIV/AIDS
**HIV** = Human Immunodeficiency Virus – **retrovirus** (RNA, **reverse transcriptase** enzyme), attacks **CD4+ T-helper cells**. **AIDS** = advanced stage – **CD4 < 200 cells/mm³** OR presence of **AIDS-defining opportunistic infection/cancer**. Normal CD4 = **500–1500 cells/mm³**.
First case in India – **1986, Chennai** (by Dr. Suniti Solomon). **1 December = World AIDS Day**.
:::

- **Transmission**: **sexual (most common in India ~85 %)**, blood & blood products, sharing needles, **mother to child (vertical – pregnancy, delivery, breastfeeding)**, needle-stick (risk ~0.3 %).
- **NOT transmitted** by: hugging, shaking hands, sharing utensils/toilets, mosquitoes, saliva/tears/sweat (casual contact).
- Body fluids with high virus: **blood, semen, vaginal fluid, breast milk**, CSF, amniotic fluid.
- **Window period** – infection to detectable antibodies (**~3–12 weeks**, up to 3 months) – test negative but can transmit.

### Stages (WHO)
1. **Acute HIV infection (seroconversion)** – 2–4 weeks; flu-like / mononucleosis-like (fever, sore throat, rash, lymphadenopathy); **very high viral load**.
2. **Clinical latency (asymptomatic)** – years (avg 8–10); virus replicating.
3. **Symptomatic** – fever, night sweats, weight loss, chronic diarrhoea, oral thrush, herpes zoster.
4. **AIDS** – opportunistic infections.

### Opportunistic infections
| Infection | Note |
|---|---|
| **Tuberculosis** | **Most common OI in India & most common cause of death** in PLHIV |
| ***Pneumocystis jirovecii* pneumonia (PCP)** | Most common OI (west); dry cough, dyspnoea; **Cotrimoxazole** prophylaxis (CD4 < 200/350) |
| **Oral/oesophageal candidiasis** | White patches; often first sign |
| **Kaposi's sarcoma** | **Purple-red skin lesions** – HHV-8; most common AIDS cancer |
| **Toxoplasmosis (brain)**, **Cryptococcal meningitis**, **CMV retinitis** (blindness), **MAC**, cryptosporidiosis (diarrhoea), herpes zoster, **HIV wasting syndrome**, **AIDS dementia complex** | |

### Diagnosis
- **ELISA / Rapid tests** – screening; India uses **3 different tests (antigen/principle) – Strategy III** for adults. **Western blot** – confirmatory (older).
- **Infants < 18 months**: **HIV DNA PCR** (maternal antibodies interfere with ELISA) – at 6 weeks.
- **CD4 count** – immune status; **Viral load (PCR)** – treatment response (goal: **undetectable < 50 copies/ml** → "**U = U**: Undetectable = Untransmittable").
- **Pre-test & post-test counselling**, **informed consent & confidentiality** mandatory (HIV/AIDS Act 2017).

### ART (Antiretroviral Therapy)
- **"Test and Treat" – start ART in all HIV-positive regardless of CD4** (India since 2017).
- **Preferred first-line (NACO): TLD = Tenofovir (TDF) 300 mg + Lamivudine (3TC) 300 mg + Dolutegravir (DTG) 50 mg** – 1 tablet daily.
- Drug classes: NRTIs (zidovudine – **anaemia**; tenofovir – **renal toxicity**), NNRTIs (efavirenz – **vivid dreams, CNS**; nevirapine – **rash/SJS, hepatotoxicity**), PIs (lipodystrophy), Integrase inhibitors (dolutegravir).
- **Adherence > 95 %** essential (resistance); lifelong; same time daily.
- **PEP (Post-Exposure Prophylaxis)** – start **as early as possible, within 2 hrs ideal, max 72 hrs**; **28 days** of ART (TLD); follow-up testing at 6 weeks, 3 & 6 months.
- **PPTCT/PMTCT** – ART to all pregnant positive women (lifelong); infant **Nevirapine syrup for 6 weeks** (12 weeks if high-risk – + AZT); **exclusive breastfeeding** recommended with maternal ART (no mixed feeding); safe institutional delivery.
- **NACP (National AIDS Control Programme)** – **phase V (2021–2026)**; **NACO** est. 1992. **95-95-95 target** (by 2025/2030): 95 % know status, 95 % on ART, 95 % virally suppressed.
- Red ribbon = AIDS awareness symbol. Helpline **1097**.

:::key Nursing care of PLHIV
- **Standard precautions** for all patients (not special isolation); protective isolation if severe neutropenia
- Oral care (thrush – nystatin), nutrition (**high calorie, high protein**, small frequent meals; food safety)
- Skin care, diarrhoea management (fluids, electrolytes), fever management
- Psychological support, **confidentiality**, **non-judgmental attitude**, address stigma
- Teach **safe sex (condoms)**, no sharing needles/razors/toothbrushes, adherence, recognise OI signs
:::

## 15.3 Important Communicable Diseases

| Disease | Agent / Vector / Route | Key features | Treatment / Nursing / Prevention |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Dengue** | **Dengue virus (4 serotypes, Flavivirus)**; ***Aedes aegypti*** (**day biter**, breeds in **clean stagnant water** – coolers, tyres, pots); "**Tiger mosquito**" | **High fever, severe headache, retro-orbital pain, myalgia/arthralgia ("breakbone fever")**, rash; **warning signs** – abdominal pain, persistent vomiting, bleeding, lethargy; **Dengue haemorrhagic fever / shock syndrome** – **↓ platelets, ↑ Hct (> 20 % = plasma leak)**; **Tourniquet test** (≥ 10 petechiae/sq inch) | **NS1 antigen (day 1–5)**, IgM ELISA (after day 5); **paracetamol only – NO aspirin/NSAIDs/steroids**; **oral/IV fluids**, monitor platelets & Hct; mosquito nets; **source reduction**; **16 May = National Dengue Day** |
| **Chikungunya** | Alphavirus; *Aedes* | Fever + **severe, prolonged joint pain** ("bent over") | Symptomatic |
| **Malaria** | ***Plasmodium*** – **P. vivax** (most common India), **P. falciparum** (most dangerous – **cerebral malaria**), P. malariae, P. ovale, P. knowlesi; **female *Anopheles*** (**night biter**, dusk–dawn; clean water) | **Paroxysm: Cold stage (chills, rigors) → Hot stage (high fever) → Sweating stage**; fever every **48 hrs (tertian – vivax, falciparum)**, 72 hrs (quartan – malariae); anaemia, splenomegaly; falciparum – cerebral malaria, blackwater fever, ARDS, hypoglycaemia | **Peripheral blood smear (thick & thin – gold standard)**, **RDT**; **Vivax: Chloroquine 3 days + Primaquine 14 days (0.25 mg/kg)** (check **G6PD** – haemolysis; avoid in pregnancy & infants < 6 months); **Falciparum: ACT – Artesunate + Sulfadoxine-Pyrimethamine (AS+SP)** (NE states: AL – Artemether-Lumefantrine) + Primaquine single dose; **Severe: IV/IM Artesunate**; **Chloroquine – retinopathy**; **Quinine – cinchonism, hypoglycaemia**; LLIN/ITN bed nets, IRS; **25 April = World Malaria Day**; NVBDCP → **NCVBDC** |
| **Typhoid (Enteric fever)** | ***Salmonella Typhi***; **faeco-oral** (contaminated food/water); carriers (gallbladder – "Typhoid Mary") | **Step-ladder fever**, **relative bradycardia** (Faget's sign), **rose spots** (trunk), **coated tongue**, abdominal pain, constipation/diarrhoea, splenomegaly; **complication – intestinal perforation & haemorrhage (3rd week)** | **Blood culture (1st week – best)**, **Widal (after 1st week)**, stool culture (3rd week), **bone marrow culture (most sensitive)**; **Ceftriaxone, azithromycin**; fluids, soft high-calorie diet, **enteric precautions, hand hygiene**; **Typhoid conjugate vaccine** |
| **Cholera** | ***Vibrio cholerae***; faeco-oral (water) | **Painless profuse "rice-water stools"**, vomiting, **severe dehydration**, muscle cramps, shock | **ORS (WHO low-osmolarity: 245 mOsm/L)**, IV RL, **doxycycline** (single dose) / azithromycin; zinc (children) |
| **Tetanus** | ***Clostridium tetani*** (spores in soil, dust, animal faeces; anaerobic); wounds, umbilical stump (**neonatal tetanus**), unclean delivery/abortion | **Trismus (lockjaw) – first sign**, **risus sardonicus** (sardonic smile), **opisthotonus** (arched back), muscle rigidity & **painful spasms triggered by light, noise, touch**, dysphagia; consciousness intact | **Quiet dark room**, minimal handling, **TIG (Human tetanus immunoglobulin) 3000–6000 IU IM** / ATS (test dose), **metronidazole**, **diazepam** (spasms), wound debridement, airway (tracheostomy), **Td vaccine**; NG feeding; **India eliminated maternal & neonatal tetanus (2015)** |
| **Rabies (Hydrophobia)** | **Rabies virus (Rhabdovirus – bullet-shaped)**; bite/scratch/lick on broken skin of **rabid dog (~95 %)**, cat, monkey, bat; incubation 1–3 months (shorter for face/neck bites) | Prodrome – **pain/paresthesia at bite site**, fever → **hydrophobia (fear of water – laryngeal spasm)**, aerophobia, hyperexcitability, hypersalivation, delirium → paralysis, coma, death (**~100 % fatal once symptoms appear**) | **Wound: wash immediately with soap & running water for 15 min** + povidone-iodine/alcohol; **don't suture** (if needed – after RIG, loose); **Category III (bites/scratches with bleeding, licks on broken skin/mucosa) → RIG (20 IU/kg human RIG, infiltrated into wound) + ARV**; **ARV IM (Essen): days 0, 3, 7, 14, 28** (deltoid; **never gluteal**) / **ID (Updated Thai Red Cross): days 0, 3, 7, 28** (2 sites); observe dog 10 days; patient care – **quiet dark room**, avoid water sound, contact precautions, staff PrEP; **28 September = World Rabies Day** (Louis Pasteur) |
| **Leprosy (Hansen's disease)** | ***Mycobacterium leprae*** (acid-fast; **slowest growing**; affects **skin & peripheral nerves**); **droplet/nasal secretions**, prolonged close contact; incubation 3–5 years | **Hypopigmented/ reddish skin patches with definite loss of sensation**, **thickened tender peripheral nerves** (ulnar, common peroneal), deformities (claw hand, foot drop, lagophthalmos); **Paucibacillary (≤ 5 patches)** vs **Multibacillary (≥ 6 patches/ nerve involvement / smear +ve)** | **MDT (WHO, 2018 uniform): Rifampicin (monthly supervised) + Clofazimine + Dapsone** – **PB: 6 months, MB: 12 months** (all 3 drugs both); **Clofazimine → reddish-brown skin discoloration**; self-care of anaesthetic hands/feet (soak, oil, protective footwear, check injuries); **30 January = Anti-Leprosy Day (Gandhiji)**; NLEP; **Sparsh** awareness campaign |
| **Measles** | Paramyxovirus; **airborne** | **3 C's – Cough, Coryza, Conjunctivitis** + fever → **Koplik's spots** (buccal mucosa – pathognomonic, before rash) → maculopapular rash (behind ears → face → body) | **Vitamin A** (2 doses), supportive; **MR vaccine** (9–12 & 16–24 months) |
| **Chickenpox (Varicella)** | VZV; airborne & contact | **Pleomorphic rash (macule→papule→vesicle→crust all at once)**, centripetal (trunk), itching; infectious till all lesions crusted | **Acyclovir**, calamine, **no aspirin (Reye's syndrome)**, trim nails |
| **Mumps** | Paramyxovirus; droplet | **Parotitis (painful swelling)**, complications – **orchitis**, meningitis, pancreatitis | Symptomatic, soft diet, avoid sour foods |
| **Diphtheria** | ***Corynebacterium diphtheriae***; droplet | **Greyish-white pseudomembrane** in throat (bleeds on removal), **bull neck**, myocarditis | **ADS (antitoxin)**, penicillin/erythromycin; **DPT/Pentavalent** |
| **COVID-19** | **SARS-CoV-2**; droplet, aerosol, contact | Fever, cough, loss of taste/smell, dyspnoea, ARDS; risk – elderly, comorbid | **RT-PCR (gold standard)**, RAT; O₂, **awake proning**, steroids (hypoxic), anticoagulation; masks, distancing, vaccines (Covishield, Covaxin) |
| **Leptospirosis** | *Leptospira*; **rat urine** contaminated water (floods, paddy fields) | Fever, **calf muscle pain**, conjunctival suffusion; **Weil's disease** (jaundice + renal failure + bleeding) | **Doxycycline / penicillin** |
| **Japanese encephalitis** | Flavivirus; ***Culex*** (paddy fields); **pigs = amplifier host** | Encephalitis in children | JE vaccine (UIP) |
| **Filariasis** | *Wuchereria bancrofti*; **Culex** (dirty water) | Lymphoedema, **elephantiasis**, hydrocele | **DEC (diethylcarbamazine) + Albendazole** (MDA) ± Ivermectin |
| **Kala-azar** | *Leishmania donovani*; **sandfly** | Fever, **massive splenomegaly**, skin darkening | Liposomal amphotericin B (single dose) |
| **Hepatitis** | See Chapter 8 | | |

:::mnemo Mosquito vectors
- **Anopheles** → **Malaria** (female; night)
- **Aedes** → **Dengue, Chikungunya, Zika, Yellow fever** (day biter; clean water)
- **Culex** → **Filariasis, Japanese encephalitis, West Nile** (night; dirty water)
- **Mansonia** → Brugian filariasis
:::
