# Perioperative Nursing
<!-- desc: Pre-op preparation, anaesthesia, OT team, intra-op safety, post-op care & complications -->

:::def Perioperative = 3 phases
**Pre-operative** (surgery ka decision → OT table par transfer) → **Intra-operative** (OT table → recovery room/PACU) → **Post-operative** (PACU → full recovery/follow-up).
:::

### Classification of surgery
| By urgency | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Emergency** | Turant, life-saving | Ruptured spleen, gunshot, obstructed hernia |
| **Urgent** | 24–48 hrs me | Acute cholecystitis, kidney stone |
| **Required/Planned** | Few weeks–months | Cataract, BPH |
| **Elective** | Patient ki marzi, delay se harm nahi | Hernia repair, cosmetic |
| **Optional** | Personal choice | Cosmetic surgery |

| By purpose | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Diagnostic** | Biopsy, exploratory laparotomy |
| **Ablative** | Diseased part remove – "-ectomy" (appendectomy) |
| **Palliative** | Symptoms relieve, cure nahi (colostomy in inoperable ca) |
| **Reconstructive** | Function/appearance restore (skin graft) |
| **Transplant**, **Constructive** (congenital defect repair – cleft lip) | |

:::key Surgical suffixes
**-ectomy** = removal · **-otomy** = cutting into · **-ostomy** = artificial opening · **-plasty** = repair · **-scopy** = looking inside · **-pexy** = fixation · **-orrhaphy** = suturing/repair (herniorrhaphy)
:::

## 4.1 Pre-operative Care

### Physical preparation checklist
- **Informed consent** signed (surgeon explain kare; nurse witness) – sedation/pre-medication se **pehle**.
- **NPO**: solids **6–8 hrs**, clear fluids **2 hrs** before anaesthesia (ASA guidelines) – aspiration rokne ke liye.
- **Investigations**: CBC, blood group & cross-match, BT/CT, PT/INR, blood sugar, RFT, electrolytes, ECG (> 40 yrs), chest X-ray, HIV/HBsAg.
- **Skin preparation**: bath with antiseptic (chlorhexidine) previous night; **hair removal by clipper, just before surgery** (razor → nicks → infection).
- **Bowel preparation**: enema/laxatives for GI surgery.
- **Bladder**: void before going to OT (or Foley catheter).
- Remove **jewellery, dentures, contact lenses, hairpins, nail polish/make-up** (nail colour se SpO₂ & cyanosis check nahi hota), prosthesis; hearing aid can stay if needed.
- **Medications**: anticoagulants (warfarin 5 days, aspirin 7 days pehle stop – as per order), insulin dose adjust, antihypertensives usually continue with sip of water.
- **ID band**, site marking (by surgeon), allergy band.
- **Pre-medication**: given **30–60 min before** – atropine/glycopyrrolate (secretions ↓), midazolam (anxiety ↓), opioid, antacid/ranitidine, antiemetic. Pre-med ke baad **side rails up**, bed par rehne do.

### Pre-operative teaching (post-op complications rokne ke liye)
- **Deep breathing & coughing** exercises, **incentive spirometry** (atelectasis/pneumonia prevention)
- **Leg exercises**, early ambulation (DVT prevention)
- **Splinting incision** with pillow while coughing
- Turning in bed, pain scale use, PCA use

:::tip Smoking & surgery
Surgery se kam se kam **4–8 weeks pehle smoking chhodne** ki salah – lung complications & wound healing problem kam hote hain.
:::

## 4.2 Anaesthesia

| Type | Details | Nursing points |
|---|---|---|
| **General** | Complete loss of consciousness; IV (Propofol, Thiopentone, Ketamine) + inhalation (Sevoflurane, Isoflurane, N₂O) + muscle relaxant (Succinylcholine, Vecuronium) | Airway, aspiration risk; **Malignant hyperthermia** risk |
| **Spinal (Subarachnoid block)** | Drug (bupivacaine) in **subarachnoid space**, **L3–L4 / L4–L5** level | Post-spinal **headache** → keep **flat/supine 6–8 hrs**, fluids; hypotension watch |
| **Epidural** | Drug in **epidural space** (catheter – continuous) | Labour analgesia, post-op pain |
| **Local / Regional / Nerve block** | Lignocaine (Xylocaine) – max 3 mg/kg (7 mg/kg with adrenaline) | **Lignocaine with adrenaline** – fingers, toes, nose, ear, penis me **NOT** (necrosis) |
| **Conscious (moderate) sedation** | Midazolam + fentanyl – patient airway maintain karta hai | Endoscopy, minor procedures |

### Stages of General Anaesthesia (Guedel's)
1. **Stage I – Analgesia/Induction**: start → loss of consciousness
2. **Stage II – Excitement/Delirium**: irregular breathing, struggling – **stimulus avoid karo (quiet OT)**
3. **Stage III – Surgical anaesthesia**: surgery isi stage me hoti hai
4. **Stage IV – Medullary paralysis/Overdose**: respiratory arrest – **danger**

:::warn Malignant Hyperthermia
Genetic reaction to **succinylcholine** & inhalation anaesthetics → sudden **↑ temperature (up to 43 °C)**, muscle rigidity (jaw/masseter), tachycardia, **↑ETCO₂ (earliest sign)**, hyperkalaemia.
**Drug: Dantrolene sodium IV**; stop triggering agent, 100 % O₂, cooling.
:::

## 4.3 Intra-operative Care

### OT Team
- **Sterile team (scrubbed)**: Surgeon, assistant surgeon, **scrub nurse** (instruments handle, count, passes instruments)
- **Unsterile team**: Anaesthetist, **circulating nurse** (OT manage, documentation, specimen, outside supplies, count verify), technicians

### OT zones
**Unrestricted** (street clothes allowed) → **Semi-restricted** (scrubs + cap) → **Restricted** (scrubs, cap, **mask**, sterile procedures – theatre itself).
- OT temperature **20–24 °C**, humidity **30–60 %**, positive pressure, ≥ 15–20 air changes/hr.

:::key Surgical safety
- **WHO Surgical Safety Checklist (2008)**: **Sign In** (before induction) → **Time Out** (before skin incision) → **Sign Out** (before patient leaves OT).
- **Swab/sponge, needle & instrument count**: before surgery, before closure of cavity, at skin closure – scrub & circulating nurse dono milke.
- Surgical scrub: **2–6 min** (as per policy), hands **elbows se upar** rakhte hain, fingertips → elbow.
- **Electrosurgery**: grounding pad (dispersive electrode) – fleshy, dry, hairless area par (thigh/buttock) – burns rokne ke liye.
:::

### Intra-op positions
- **Supine** – most abdominal surgeries; **Lithotomy** – gynaec/urology/perineal; **Prone** – spine; **Lateral (kidney position)** – nephrectomy; **Trendelenburg** – pelvic/lower abdominal; **Reverse Trendelenburg** – head & neck, gallbladder.
- Pressure points ko padding; extremities ko 90° se zyada abduct na karo (**brachial plexus injury**).

## 4.4 Post-operative Care

### Immediate (PACU / Recovery room)
- **Airway first**: patient ko **lateral / side-lying (recovery) position** with head turned until gag reflex returns (aspiration rokne ke liye); oropharyngeal airway tab tak rakho jab tak patient khud na nikal de.
- Vitals **every 15 min** (first hour), then 30 min, then hourly.
- **Aldrete score ≥ 9** → PACU se ward transfer (Activity, Respiration, Circulation, Consciousness, O₂ saturation – each 0–2).
- Watch: bleeding (dressing, drains), urine output (≥ 30 ml/hr), pain, temperature, nausea/vomiting.
- Patient ko pehli baar void karna: **6–8 hrs** ke andar.

### Post-operative complications – Timeline ("**Wind, Water, Wound, Walk, Wonder drugs**")

| Day | Complication | Prevention / Management |
|---|---|---|
| **Day 1–2 – Wind** | **Atelectasis** (most common, first 48 hrs – fever), pneumonia | Deep breathing, coughing, **incentive spirometry**, early ambulation |
| **Day 3–5 – Water** | **UTI** (catheter) | Remove catheter early, fluids |
| **Day 5–7 – Wound** | **Wound infection**, dehiscence | Asepsis, nutrition, splinting |
| **Day 7–10 – Walk** | **DVT / Pulmonary embolism** | Early ambulation, leg exercises, TED stockings, low-molecular-weight heparin |
| **Any time – Wonder drugs** | Drug fever, reactions | Review drugs |

:::mnemo 5 W of post-op fever
**W**ind (lungs, 24–48 h) · **W**ater (UTI, 3–5 d) · **W**ound (5–7 d) · **W**alking (DVT, 7–10 d) · **W**onder drugs (any time)
:::

### Other post-op problems
- **Haemorrhage** – Primary (during surgery), **Reactionary** (within 24 hrs – BP ↑ → ligature slip), **Secondary** (7–14 days – infection).
- **Hypovolaemic shock** – tachycardia, ↓BP, cold clammy skin.
- **Paralytic ileus** – absent bowel sounds, distension; NPO + **NG tube** + IV fluids; ambulation. Bowel sounds return → **"flatus pass"** → oral fluids start.
- **Urinary retention** – first void within 8 hrs; bladder scan, warm water, privacy, catheter if needed.
- **Hiccups** (phrenic nerve irritation), **post-op nausea & vomiting** (ondansetron).
- **Pulmonary embolism** – sudden **dyspnoea, chest pain, tachycardia, anxiety** → High Fowler's, O₂, inform, anticoagulants.
- **DVT** – calf pain, swelling, warmth; **Homan's sign** (not reliable now) – **massage mat karo**.

:::warn Drains & tubes
- **Drain output** chart karo; **> 100 ml/hr** bright red blood → inform surgeon.
- **T-tube** (after CBD exploration) – bile drain, normal 300–500 ml first day; bag gallbladder level ke neeche.
- Hemovac/Jackson-Pratt – suction maintain (compress & close).
:::
