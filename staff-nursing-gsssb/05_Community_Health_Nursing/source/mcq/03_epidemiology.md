 (a) Epizootic (b) Iatrogenic (c) Zoonosis (d) Nosocomial

 (a) Exotic (b) Nosocomial (c) Opportunistic (d) Iatrogenic

 (a) Ratio (b) Prevalence (c) Proportion (d) Incidence

 (a) P = I × D (b) I = P × D (c) P = I / D (d) P = I + D

 (a) Attack rate (b) Case fatality rate (c) Prevalence (d) Incidence

 (a) Prevalence (b) Crude rate (c) CFR (d) Secondary attack rate

 (a) Incidence (b) Relative risk (c) Attributable risk (d) Odds ratio

 (a) Relative risk / incidence (b) Prevalence only (c) None (d) Odds ratio

 (a) Case report (b) RCT (c) Case-control (d) Cross-sectional
13. Cross-sectional study kya batata hai?
 (a) Prevalence (b) Incidence (c) Relative risk (d) Odds ratio
14. Sensitivity –
 (a) TP / (TP + FN) (b) TP / (TP + FP) (c) TN / (TN + FP) (d) TN / (TN + FN)
15. Specificity –
 (a) TP / (TP + FN) (b) FP / (FP + TN) (c) TN / (TN + FP) (d) TP / (TP + FP)
16. Epidemiological triad –
 (a) Cause, effect, outcome (b) Rate, ratio, proportion (c) Agent, host, environment (d) Time, place, person
17. Bradford Hill criteria me sabse important –
 (a) Coherence (b) Temporal association (c) Specificity (d) Analogy

 (a) Write report (b) Test hypothesis (c) Treat all (d) Verify diagnosis
