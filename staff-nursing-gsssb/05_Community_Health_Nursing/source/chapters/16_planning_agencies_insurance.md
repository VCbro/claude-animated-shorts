## 16.1 Health Planning

- **Planning** – deciding in advance what to do, how, when & by whom; **planning cycle** – **analysis of health situation → establishment of objectives & goals → assessment of resources → fixing priorities → write-up of formulated plan & programmes → programming & implementation → monitoring → evaluation** (→ back to start).
- **Goal** (ultimate desired state, not time-bound), **objective** (specific, measurable, time-bound – **SMART**: Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant/ Realistic, Time-bound), **target** (quantified), **activities**.
- **Monitoring** – day-to-day follow-up of activities during implementation; **evaluation** – assessing **relevance, adequacy, accessibility, acceptability, effectiveness, efficiency, impact** (done at end/ periodically).
- **Planning Commission (1950)** → replaced by **NITI Aayog (1 Jan 2015)** – chaired by PM; **Five-Year Plans (1951–2017; 12th last)**; now **15-year vision, 7-year strategy, 3-year action agenda**.

### Management techniques
| Technique | Use |
|---|---|
| **PERT (Program Evaluation & Review Technique)** | Network analysis – time estimation (optimistic, pessimistic, most likely) |
| **CPM (Critical Path Method)** | Longest path – minimum project duration |
| **Cost-benefit analysis** | Benefits in money terms |
| **Cost-effectiveness analysis** | Cost per unit outcome (e.g. per life saved) |
| **Systems analysis** | Choose best among alternatives |
| **Management by Objectives (MBO)** – Peter Drucker | Joint goal setting & review |
| **SWOT analysis** | Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, Threats |
| **Input–output analysis**, **network analysis**, **Gantt chart** | Scheduling |
| **Work sampling, Delphi technique (expert consensus)** | – |

- **Functions of management (POSDCORB – Luther Gulick)** – **P**lanning, **O**rganising, **S**taffing, **D**irecting, **CO**ordinating, **R**eporting, **B**udgeting.
- **Supervision** – guiding, helping & teaching staff to do their best (**supportive supervision**); tools – checklists, **supervisory visits**, records review, feedback; **span of control**.
- **Leadership styles** – **autocratic** (crisis), **democratic/ participative** (best for health teams), **laissez-faire** (free rein), situational.
- **Delegation**, **decision making**, **conflict management**, **time management**, **budgeting**, **inventory control** (**ABC analysis – value; VED – vital, essential, desirable**).
- **Indian Public Health Standards (IPHS)** – first 2007, revised **2012 & 2022**; **NQAS (National Quality Assurance Standards)**, **Kayakalp (2015 – cleanliness awards)**, **LaQshya**, **MusQan** (child-friendly).

## 16.3 Health Insurance & Financing

- **Health financing in India** – govt health expenditure ~**1.9–2.1 % of GDP**; **out-of-pocket expenditure (OOPE)** high (~**39–40 %** of total health expenditure, declining).
- **Health insurance schemes**:

| Scheme | Key points |
|---|---|
| **CGHS (Central Government Health Scheme)** | **1954, Delhi**; central govt employees & pensioners |
| **ESIS (ESI Scheme)** | **1948 Act** – industrial workers (see occupational health) |
| **AB-PMJAY** | **23 Sept 2018** – **₹5 lakh per family per year**, secondary & tertiary hospitalisation, **cashless & portable**; **all ≥ 70 yrs covered (2024)**; ~1,900+ packages; **National Health Authority** |
| **RSBY (Rashtriya Swasthya Bima Yojana)** | 2008 – ₹30,000 for BPL (merged into PMJAY) |
| **PMSBY (Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana)** | Accident insurance ₹2 lakh @ **₹20/yr** |
| **PMJJBY (Jeevan Jyoti)** | Life insurance ₹2 lakh @ ₹436/yr |
| **Community-based health insurance** | **SEWA (Ahmedabad), Yeshasvini (Karnataka)** |
| **Private insurance** | IRDAI regulated |

- **Universal Health Coverage (UHC)** – all people receive needed health services without financial hardship; **UHC Day – 12 Dec**.
