## 8.1 RMNCH+A Strategy (2013)

- **Reproductive, Maternal, Newborn, Child & Adolescent Health** – **"continuum of care"** across **life stages** and **levels of care (community → facility)**.
- **"+"** – adolescent health, and linking maternal & child health to reproductive health, family planning, life cycle approach.
- **High Priority Districts (HPDs)** – focused support.
- **5 × 5 matrix** – 5 high-impact interventions in 5 life stages.

### Maternal health services (community nurse role)
- **Early registration (within 12 wks) & minimum 4 ANC visits** (1st – ≤ 12 wks, 2nd – 14–26 wks, 3rd – 28–34 wks, 4th – 36 wks–term); **MCP card**; **Td 2 doses**, **IFA 180 days**, **calcium 1 g**, **albendazole (2nd trimester)**, Hb, urine albumin/ sugar, BP, weight, **HIV, syphilis, HBsAg, blood group**, **GDM test (75 g OGTT)**.
- **Birth preparedness & complication readiness**; **institutional delivery**; **SBA (Skilled Birth Attendant)** – ANM/ LHV/ SN trained; **identify high-risk pregnancy (HRP)** – age < 18/ > 35, height < 145 cm, Hb < 7, BP ≥ 140/90, previous CS, multiple pregnancy, malpresentation, bleeding.
- **PNC** – **within 48 hrs, then day 3, 7, 42** (minimum); **HBNC visits by ASHA**.
- **Maternal Death Surveillance & Response (MDSR)**; **MAA, SUMAN, LaQshya**, **Midwifery led care units**.

| Scheme | Key benefit |
|---|---|
| **JSY (2005)** | **Cash for institutional delivery** – LPS (rural ₹1,400, urban ₹1,000) & HPS (₹700/ ₹600); ASHA incentive |
| **JSSK (2011)** | **Free & cashless** delivery, CS, drugs, diagnostics, diet (3 days normal/ 7 days CS), blood, **transport (home–facility–home)** for pregnant women & sick infants |
| **PMSMA (2016)** | **Free ANC by specialists on 9th of every month** at PHCs/ CHCs |
| **PMMVY (2017)** | **₹5,000** for first child (2 instalments, **DBT**); **₹6,000 for second child if girl** (PMMVY 2.0, 2022) |
| **SUMAN (2019)** | Zero tolerance for preventable maternal/ newborn deaths – assured free services |
| **LaQshya (2017)** | Quality improvement in labour room & maternity OT |

### Child health services
- **Essential newborn care**, **HBNC**, **HBYC**, **immunization (UIP)**, **IMNCI**, **RBSK** (4 D's – mobile health teams screen 0–6 yrs at AWCs twice/ yr, 6–18 yrs in schools once/ yr), **SNCU/ NBSU/ NBCC**, **NRC (Nutrition Rehabilitation Centre)** for SAM, **IDCF/ STOP Diarrhoea campaign**, **SAANS**, **Vitamin A**, **Anaemia Mukt Bharat**, **National Deworming Day**.
- **Child Death Review (CDR)**.

### Adolescent health – RKSK (2014)
- **10–19 yrs**; **6 strategic priorities** – nutrition, sexual & reproductive health, non-communicable diseases, substance misuse, injuries & violence (incl. GBV), mental health.
- **AFHC (Adolescent Friendly Health Clinics) – "Ujjwal" clinics**; **peer educators ("Saathiya")** – 4 per village (2 boys + 2 girls); **Saathiya Salah app/ resource kit**; **WIFS (weekly blue IFA tablet + biannual albendazole)**; **Menstrual Hygiene Scheme** (subsidised sanitary napkins by ASHA); **Adolescent Health Day (AHD)** quarterly.

## 8.2 Family Planning

- **Family planning (WHO)** – way of thinking & living adopted voluntarily to promote health & welfare of family.
- **History** – **India – first country in the world to launch a national family planning programme – 1952**; renamed **Family Welfare Programme (1977)**; **"cafeteria approach"** → **"Basket of choice"**; **target-free approach (1996)**; **Mission Parivar Vikas (2016)** – high-TFR districts.
- **Eligible couple**, **CPR**, **unmet need** (~9.4 % NFHS-5).

| Method | Key points |
|---|---|
| **Condom (Nirodh)** – barrier | Free; also STI/ HIV protection; failure ~ 2–15 % |
| **Combined OCP – Mala-N (free), Mala-D (social marketing)** | 21 hormonal + 7 iron tablets; **start day 1–5 of cycle**; inhibits ovulation |
| **Centchroman – Chhaya (Saheli)** | Non-steroidal, **twice weekly × 3 months then weekly** |
| **Injectable DMPA – Antara** | **150 mg IM every 3 months** |
| **Emergency contraceptive pill (E-pill)** | **LNG 1.5 mg within 72 hrs** |
| **IUCD – Cu-T 380A (10 yrs), Cu 375 (5 yrs)** | Insert within 12 days of menses; **PPIUCD (within 48 hrs of delivery)**, PAIUCD |
| **Female sterilisation (tubectomy)** – **minilap/ laparoscopic** | **Most popular method in India**; Pomeroy technique; postpartum within 7 days |
| **Male sterilisation – NSV (No-Scalpel Vasectomy)** | Simple, OPD, **backup contraception for 3 months (or 20 ejaculations/ semen analysis)** |
| **Natural** | **LAM** (exclusive BF + amenorrhoea + < 6 months), **Standard Days Method (CycleBeads – days 8–19)**, calendar, BBT, cervical mucus (Billings) |

- **Spacing methods** – condoms, OCPs, IUCD, injectables, Chhaya; **terminal** – sterilisation.
- **Delivery of contraceptives by ASHA at doorstep** (Home Delivery of Contraceptives – 2011); **Family Planning Indemnity Scheme**; **compensation for sterilisation** (loss of wages); **Family Planning – Logistics Management Information System (FP-LMIS)**.
- **World Population Day – 11 July** (population fortnight: "Dampati Samparka Pakhwada" + "Seva Pakhwada").

## 8.3 Related Laws

| Law | Key point |
|---|---|
| **MTP Act 1971 (amended 2021)** | **≤ 20 wks – 1 RMP**; **20–24 wks – 2 RMPs (special categories: rape survivors, minors, incest, disabled, foetal anomaly etc.)**; beyond 24 wks for **substantial foetal anomalies – Medical Board**; **contraceptive failure** ground extended to **unmarried women**; confidentiality |
| **PCPNDT Act 1994 (amended 2003)** | **Prohibits sex selection & disclosure of sex of foetus**; registration of USG machines; Form F; **Beti Bachao Beti Padhao (22 Jan 2015, Panipat)** |
| **Prohibition of Child Marriage Act 2006** | Girls 18, boys 21 |
| **Maternity Benefit (Amendment) Act 2017** | **26 weeks paid maternity leave** (for first 2 children); crèche facility (≥ 50 employees) |
| **Surrogacy (Regulation) Act 2021** | Only altruistic surrogacy |
