# Health of Special Groups – School, Urban, Tribal, Elderly, Disabled & Others

## 14.1 School Health

- **First school medical inspection in India – Baroda (Vadodara), Gujarat – 1909**.
- **School Health Committee (Renuka Ray) – 1960**; **school health services** recommended by Bhore Committee.
- **Objectives** – promote positive health, prevent disease, early diagnosis & treatment, **health-conscious citizens**, healthy school environment.
- **Aspects of school health services** – **health appraisal** (medical examination at entry & periodically – every 4 yrs (older), teachers' observation daily), **remedial measures & follow-up**, **prevention of communicable diseases (immunization – Td at 10 & 16 yrs)**, **healthful school environment**, **nutritional services (PM POSHAN)**, first aid & **emergency care**, **mental health**, **dental health**, **eye health (vision screening – refractive errors – most common problem detected)**, **health education**, **education of handicapped children**, **school health records** (cumulative).
- **School environment standards** – **location** away from noise/ traffic, **site 10 acres (higher secondary)**, **classroom – 10 sq ft per student (floor space)**, **≤ 40 students per class**, **desks – "minus" type (desk overhangs seat)**, **window area ≥ 25 % (1/4) of floor area**, lighting from left side, **drinking water**, **separate latrines (1 urinal per 60 students, 1 latrine per 100 students)**.
- **Common health problems in school children** – **malnutrition, anaemia, infections (worms, skin – scabies), dental caries, refractive errors, ear infections**, behaviour problems, learning disorders.
- **Ayushman Bharat – School Health & Wellness Programme (Feb 2020, MoHFW + MoE)** – **2 teachers per school as "Health & Wellness Ambassadors"**; **11 thematic areas** (growing up healthy, emotional well-being, interpersonal relationships, values & citizenship, gender equality, nutrition & hygiene, substance misuse, healthy lifestyle, reproductive health & HIV, violence & injury, internet safety); **Tuesdays as Health & Wellness Day**; **RBSK screening** for 6–18 yrs.
- **Kayakalp, Swachh Vidyalaya**.

## 14.2 Urban Health

- **Urbanisation** – India ~**31 % urban (Census 2011)**; **slums – 17 % of urban population** (Census 2011); problems – overcrowding, poor sanitation, **air & water pollution**, vector-borne diseases (dengue, malaria – *An. stephensi*), TB, RTI, NCDs, substance abuse, violence, migrants.
- **NUHM (1 May 2013)** – **UPHC per 50,000** (slum/ vulnerable), **UCHC per 2.5–5 lakh**, **urban ASHA (per 1,000–2,500 slum population)**, **Mahila Arogya Samiti (MAS – 50–100 households)**, **outreach sessions (UHND)**, **Urban AAM (Ayushman Arogya Mandir – urban) per 15,000–20,000**.
- **Smart Cities Mission, AMRUT, PMAY (housing)**.

## 14.3 Tribal Health

- **Tribal population ~8.6 % (Census 2011)**; Gujarat ~14.8 % (Dangs district majority tribal).
- **Problems** – **malnutrition, anaemia, sickle cell disease (high in Gujarat tribal belts)**, malaria, TB, skin & worm infections, **high IMR/ MMR**, poor access, superstitions, alcohol, geographic isolation.
- **Special norms – SC per 3,000, PHC per 20,000, CHC per 80,000**; **mobile medical units**, **birth waiting homes**, **ASHA per habitation**, **Eklavya schools**, **Van Bandhu Kalyan Yojana (Gujarat, 2007)**, **National Sickle Cell Anaemia Elimination Mission (2023)**, **PM-JANMAN (2023) for PVTGs**.

## 14.4 Geriatric (Elderly) Health

- **Elderly – ≥ 60 yrs** (India); **~8.6 % (Census 2011) → ~10.5 % (2022) of population** (projected ~20 % by 2050); **"feminisation of ageing"**.
- **Problems** – **NCDs (HTN, DM, CVD, arthritis – osteoarthritis, osteoporosis)**, **cataract, hearing loss**, **dementia (Alzheimer's)**, depression, **falls & fractures (hip)**, incontinence, **polypharmacy**, malnutrition, **loneliness, neglect & elder abuse**, financial dependency.
- **NPHCE (2010)**; **Maintenance & Welfare of Parents & Senior Citizens Act 2007** (maintenance tribunals); **National Policy on Older Persons 1999**; **National Council for Senior Citizens**; **Indira Gandhi National Old Age Pension Scheme (IGNOAPS)**; **Rashtriya Vayoshri Yojana** (aids & assistive devices); **Atal Vayo Abhyuday Yojana**; **Elder Line 14567**; **Ayushman Vay Vandana – PMJAY for all ≥ 70 yrs (2024)**; **International Day of Older Persons – 1 Oct**.
- **Nursing** – comprehensive geriatric assessment (**ADL – Katz index, IADL – Lawton**), fall prevention (lighting, grab bars, non-slip mats), nutrition (calcium, vit D, fibre, fluids), immunization (influenza, pneumococcal), medication review, social support, day-care centres, palliative care.

## 14.5 Disability & Rehabilitation

- **Disability** – WHO ICF; ~2.2 % (Census 2011), ~**15 % globally (WHO)**.
- **RPwD Act 2016** – **21 types**, benchmark ≥ 40 %, **UDID card**, reservation (jobs 4 %, education 5 %), **Accessible India Campaign (Sugamya Bharat Abhiyan, 2015)**.
- **Community-Based Rehabilitation (CBR)** – WHO strategy (1978) – rehabilitation, equalisation of opportunities & social inclusion **using community resources** – **CBR matrix: health, education, livelihood, social, empowerment**.
- **Rehabilitation types** – medical, vocational, social, psychological; **District Disability Rehabilitation Centres (DDRC)**, **ADIP scheme** (aids & appliances).
- **Mental health in community** – **DMHP**, early identification, reduce stigma, **community care (half-way homes, day care)**, ASHA/ ANM training (**mhGAP**), suicide prevention.
