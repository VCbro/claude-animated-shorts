## 10.1 Water

- **Safe & wholesome water** – free from pathogens & harmful chemicals, pleasant to taste, usable for domestic purposes.
- **Requirement** – **150–200 litres/ capita/ day (urban)**; **Jal Jeevan Mission (15 Aug 2019) – "Har Ghar Jal" – 55 litres/ person/ day functional household tap connection** in rural areas by 2024 (extended to 2028).
- **Sources** – rain (purest natural), surface (rivers, lakes, ponds – most polluted), **ground water (wells, springs – deep wells safest)**.
- **Water quality standards (BIS 10500)** – **pH 6.5–8.5**; **turbidity < 1 NTU (max 5)**; **fluoride 1.0 mg/L (permissible 1.5)**; **nitrate ≤ 45 mg/L (blue baby syndrome – methaemoglobinaemia)**; **arsenic ≤ 0.01 mg/L** (WB – skin lesions, cancer); **total hardness ≤ 200 mg/L**; **coliforms – 0 per 100 ml (E. coli – indicator of faecal contamination)**.
- **Hardness** – **temporary (bicarbonates – removed by boiling/ Clark's method – lime)**, **permanent (sulphates/ chlorides – removed by base exchange/ permutit process)**.

### Purification of water
- **Large scale** – **storage** (sedimentation – 90 % suspended impurities settle; reduces bacteria ~90 % in 5–7 days; **Schistosoma cercariae die in 48 hrs**) → **filtration** → **chlorination**.

| | **Slow sand (biological/ English) filter** | **Rapid sand (mechanical/ American) filter** |
|---|---|---|
| Rate | **2–3 m³/m²/day** (slow) | **80–200 m³/m²/day** |
| Pre-treatment | Plain sedimentation | **Coagulation (alum) + rapid mixing + flocculation** |
| Key layer | **Vital layer/ Schmutzdecke (zoogleal layer) – heart of filter** | – |
| Cleaning | **Scraping top layer** (weeks–months) | **Backwashing** (daily) |
| Bacterial removal | **99.9–99.99 %** | 98–99 % |
| Space/ use | More space, small towns | Less space, **big cities** |

- **Chlorination** – kills pathogens (not spores, cysts, some viruses); **chlorine demand** satisfied first; **free residual chlorine 0.5 mg/L (ppm) after 1 hour contact** (minimum; **0.2 mg/L at consumer end**); **break-point chlorination** – point where chlorine demand is fully met & free residual chlorine appears; **super-chlorination** (excess then dechlorination); **orthotolidine (OT) test** – residual chlorine (**yellow** colour; **OT-arsenite test** distinguishes free & combined chlorine).
- **Horrock's apparatus** – estimates **dose of bleaching powder** needed to disinfect water (6 white cups, 1 black cup, **starch-iodide indicator – blue colour**).
- **Bleaching powder (chlorinated lime) – 33 % available chlorine** (unstable – store in dark, dry, airtight container); **high-test hypochlorite (HTH) – 60–70 %**; **chlorine tablets** (0.5 g tablet for 20 L); **perchloron**.
- **Household purification** – **boiling (rolling boil 5–10 min – best, kills all pathogens incl. spores in some time)**, chlorination (tablets/ liquid), filtration (ceramic candles – **Pasteur–Chamberland, Berkefeld, Katadyn (silver)**), **SODIS** (solar), alum (fitkari) for turbidity, RO.
- **Well disinfection** – best method: **bleaching powder** (based on Horrock's test); steps – find volume (**π r² h; 1 m³ = 1000 L**), dissolve bleaching powder in bucket (paste → milky solution), let lime settle, pour supernatant into well, **contact 1 hr**, **OT test (0.5 mg/L)**; best done **at night**; **sanitary well** – parapet wall, apron/ platform, drainage, covered, hand pump, **≥ 15 m (50 ft) from latrine/ source of contamination**.
- **Rain water harvesting**.

### Water-borne & water-related diseases
- **Water-borne (faeco-oral)** – **cholera, typhoid, hepatitis A & E, polio, diarrhoea, dysentery**, giardiasis, amoebiasis.
- **Water-washed** (lack of water for hygiene) – scabies, trachoma, skin infections.
- **Water-based** (aquatic host) – **schistosomiasis, guinea worm (dracunculiasis – India eradicated 2000)**.
- **Water-related insect vector** – malaria, dengue, filaria.
- **Chemical** – **fluorosis, arsenicosis, nitrates (blue baby), lead**.

## 10.2 Air & Ventilation

- **Air pollutants** – **SO₂ (acid rain, respiratory)**, **CO (carboxyhaemoglobin – affinity ~200–250 × O₂)**, NO₂, ozone, **PM₂.₅ & PM₁₀ (most harmful – lungs)**, lead, hydrocarbons; **indoor air pollution** – biomass fuel (chulha) – **COPD, pneumonia in children, LBW**; **PM Ujjwala Yojana (2016)** – LPG to BPL women.
- **AQI (Air Quality Index)** – **0–50 good**, 51–100 satisfactory, 101–200 moderate, 201–300 poor, 301–400 very poor, **401–500 severe**; **National Clean Air Programme (NCAP, 2019)**.
- **Greenhouse gases** – **CO₂ (main)**, methane, N₂O, CFCs → global warming; **ozone layer depletion – CFCs** (Montreal Protocol 1987).
- **Comfort indices** – **corrected effective temperature**, **Kata thermometer** (cooling power of air), **wet bulb globe temperature (heat stress)**; **comfortable zone in India 25–27 °C CET**.
- **Ventilation** – **air change 2–3 times/hr (living rooms)**; **cross ventilation**; **window area ≥ 1/5 floor area**; **floor space/ person ≥ 50–100 sq ft**.
- **Overcrowding** – > 2 persons per room / persons per room ratio; spreads TB, meningitis, scabies, ARI.

## 10.3 Excreta Disposal

- **Faecal-borne diseases** (cholera, typhoid, hepatitis, worms) – **"F diagram"** – **F**aeces → **F**ingers, **F**lies, **F**luids, **F**ields → **F**ood → new host; **sanitation barrier** blocks primary transmission.
- **Sanitary latrine** – **excreta should not contaminate surface/ ground water, not accessible to flies/ animals, no odour, simple, cheap, acceptable**.
- **Types (unsewered areas)** – **service type** (banned – **manual scavenging prohibited – Act 2013**), **bore-hole latrine**, **dug-well/ pit latrine**, **water-seal latrine (RCA type/ PRAI type)** – **water seal 2 cm (¾ inch)**, **twin-pit pour-flush latrine (Sulabh)** – most recommended for rural, **septic tank** (individual households – capacity **20–40 gallons (90–150 L)/ person**, retention ~1–3 days; anaerobic digestion; **sludge removed every 1–3 yrs**), **aqua privy**, **chemical closet** (aircraft, trains).
- **Sewered areas** – **water carriage system**; sewage treatment – **primary (screening, grit chamber, sedimentation)**, **secondary (biological – trickling filter, activated sludge process)**, **oxidation ponds/ stabilisation ponds (algae + bacteria – symbiosis)** – cheap for small towns.
- **BOD (Biochemical Oxygen Demand)** – **measure of organic pollution of sewage (5 days at 20 °C)**; domestic sewage ~ 300 mg/L; effluent standard ≤ 30 mg/L.
- **Swachh Bharat Mission – 2 Oct 2014**; **India declared ODF (Open Defecation Free) – 2 Oct 2019**; **SBM-G Phase II – ODF Plus** (solid & liquid waste management).

## 10.4 Solid Waste (Refuse) Disposal

- **Methods** – **composting** (**Bangalore method – anaerobic, hot fermentation**; **Indore method – aerobic, manual turning**; **mechanical composting**; **vermicomposting**), **sanitary landfill (controlled tipping)** – most satisfactory for cities (refuse compacted, covered with earth daily ~ 20–30 cm), **incineration** (hospital waste – best for infectious refuse), **dumping** (unsatisfactory), **manure pits** (rural), burial.
- **Solid Waste Management Rules 2016** – **segregation at source – green (wet/ biodegradable) & blue (dry)**, domestic hazardous.

## 10.5 Biomedical Waste Management Rules 2016 (amended 2018, 2019)

@fig bmw_colour.svg | BMW Rules 2016 – four colour categories & treatment

| Category | Container | Waste | Treatment |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Yellow** | **Non-chlorinated yellow plastic bag** | **Human anatomical**, animal, **soiled waste** (dressings, plaster casts, blood bags), **expired/ discarded medicines**, chemical waste, **chemical liquid**, discarded linen/ mattresses with blood, **microbiology lab & blood bank waste (pre-treated by autoclave/ NaOCl on site)** | **Incineration / plasma pyrolysis / deep burial** (rural, as per rules) |
| **Red** | **Non-chlorinated red plastic bag/ container** | **Contaminated recyclable waste** – tubing, bottles, **IV sets, catheters, urine bags, syringes (without needles)**, **gloves**, vacutainers | **Autoclave/ microwave/ hydroclave + shredding → recycling** (not incinerated) |
| **White (translucent)** | **Puncture-proof, leak-proof, tamper-proof container** | **Sharps** – needles, syringes with fixed needles, needles from needle tip cutter, **scalpels, blades**, metal sharps | Autoclave/ dry heat sterilisation + **shredding/ mutilation/ encapsulation** in metal container/ sharp pit |
| **Blue** | **Cardboard box with blue marking** (puncture-proof) | **Glassware – broken/ discarded/ contaminated glass incl. vials, ampoules**; **metallic body implants** | Disinfection (soak in **1 % NaOCl**)/ autoclave → **recycling** |

- **Key rules** – **segregation at source**; **untreated BMW not stored > 48 hrs**; **bar-code labelling** of bags/ containers; **pre-treatment of lab/ microbiology waste on-site**; **phase out of chlorinated plastic bags** (prevents dioxin on burning); **biohazard symbol** (and cytotoxic symbol for cytotoxic waste – yellow bag with "cytotoxic" label); **annual report to SPCB** by 30 June; **training & immunization (Hep B) of all health workers**; **records for 5 yrs**; **CBWTF (Common Bio-Medical Waste Treatment Facility)** – within **75 km**.
- **Liquid waste** – disinfected before discharge into drains.
- **Needle-stick injury** – wash with soap & water (don't squeeze), report, PEP evaluation.
- **Incinerator** – **double chamber; primary 800 ± 50 °C, secondary 1050 ± 50 °C**.

## 10.6 Housing, Noise, Radiation & Entomology

- **Healthy housing criteria (WHO)** – **floor area per person ≥ 50 sq ft (min) – 100 sq ft ideal**, **room height ≥ 3 m (10 ft)**, **window area 1/5 of floor**, **lighting – daylight factor ≥ 1 %**, separate kitchen, safe water, sanitary latrine.
- **Noise** – unit **decibel (dB)**; **normal conversation ~60 dB**; **> 85 dB (8 hrs) – hearing loss (occupational)**; **> 120 dB – pain threshold**; effects – **auditory (temporary/ permanent threshold shift, noise-induced hearing loss – 4000 Hz notch)**, non-auditory (sleep, BP, stress); **Noise Pollution Rules 2000**; residential limit **day 55 dB, night 45 dB**; **silence zone (hospitals, schools) – day 50 dB, night 40 dB** (within **100 m**).
- **Radiation** – **ionising** (X-rays, γ-rays) – cancer, genetic effects; **ALARA principle**; **lead apron**; **radiation unit – sievert (Sv)**; **TLD badge/ film badge** for workers; **maximum permissible dose – 20 mSv/yr (5-yr average) occupational**; **Atomic Energy Regulatory Board (AERB)**. **Non-ionising** – UV (cataract, skin cancer), microwaves.
- **Medical entomology** –

| Arthropod | Diseases |
|---|---|
| **Anopheles** | Malaria |
| **Aedes** | Dengue, chikungunya, Zika, yellow fever |
| **Culex** | **Filaria (bancroftian), JE**, West Nile |
| **Mansonia** | **Brugian filaria** (Pistia plant) |
| **Housefly** | Typhoid, cholera, diarrhoea, trachoma (mechanical) |
| **Sandfly** | **Kala-azar**, oriental sore, sandfly fever |
| **Rat flea (Xenopsylla cheopis)** | **Plague**, murine typhus; **cheopis index > 1 – plague risk** |
| **Louse** | **Epidemic typhus**, relapsing fever, trench fever |
| **Tick** | **KFD (Kyasanur Forest Disease)**, Lyme, tick typhus |
| **Trombiculid mite (chigger)** | **Scrub typhus** |
| **Itch mite** | Scabies |
| **Cyclops** | Guinea worm, fish tapeworm |
| **Tsetse fly** | Sleeping sickness (Africa) |

- **Insecticides** – **DDT** (organochlorine – banned for agriculture; restricted vector control), **malathion**, **temephos (Abate – larvicide in potable water)**, **synthetic pyrethroids (deltamethrin, cyfluthrin – IRS, LLINs)**, **pyrethrum (space spray/ fogging)**; **IGR**; biological – **Gambusia, Guppy fish, BTI**.
