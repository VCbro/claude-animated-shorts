# National Programmes – NCDs & Others
<!-- desc: NP-NCD (NPCDCS), cancer screening, NPCBVI, NMHP & Tele-MANAS, NTCP & COTPA, NPHCE, NPPCD, NIDDCP, fluorosis, oral health, burns, climate change, IDD, important days -->

## 7.1 Non-Communicable Diseases – Overview

- **NCDs cause ~63–65 % of deaths in India** – **cardiovascular diseases (leading)**, cancers, chronic respiratory disease, diabetes.
- **Common modifiable risk factors** – **tobacco, alcohol, unhealthy diet (salt, sugar, fat), physical inactivity**, air pollution → obesity, ↑ BP, ↑ sugar, ↑ lipids.
- **WHO "25 by 25"** – 25 % reduction in premature NCD mortality by 2025; **SDG 3.4 – one-third reduction by 2030**.

## 7.2 NP-NCD (National Programme for Prevention & Control of NCDs)

- **NPCDCS** (Cancer, Diabetes, CVD & Stroke) – **2010** → **renamed NP-NCD (2023)**.
- **Population-Based Screening (PBS)** – **all persons ≥ 30 yrs** at **AAM** for **hypertension, diabetes, oral cancer, breast cancer, cervical cancer** (+ COPD, CKD, NAFLD added); **CBAC form** (Community-Based Assessment Checklist – ASHA fills; score > 4 → priority).
- **Screening methods** – **BP** (≥ 140/90), **random blood sugar (glucometer – ≥ 140 mg/dl → refer)**, **oral visual examination (OVE)**, **clinical breast examination (CBE)** (women ≥ 30), **VIA (visual inspection with 3–5 % acetic acid)** – women **30–65 yrs, every 5 yrs**.
- **NCD clinics** at district hospital & CHC; **"75/25 initiative" (2023)** – **75 million people with HTN/ diabetes on standard care by 2025**; **India Hypertension Control Initiative (IHCI)**.
- **Cancer** – **commonest in Indian men – lip/ oral cavity & lung**; **women – breast, then cervix**; **tertiary cancer care centres**, **Tata Memorial**; **HPV vaccine (9–14 yr girls)** being introduced.

## 7.3 National Programme for Control of Blindness & Visual Impairment (NPCBVI)

- **Launched 1976** (100 % centrally sponsored); **cataract – leading cause of blindness** in India; others – refractive errors (leading cause of visual impairment), glaucoma, corneal opacity, diabetic retinopathy.
- **Blindness (WHO)** – **presenting visual acuity < 3/60** in better eye; **visual impairment < 6/18**.
- Activities – **free cataract surgery (IOL)**, **school eye screening** (free spectacles), **eye donation** (**Eye Donation Fortnight 25 Aug – 8 Sept**), **eye banks**, diabetic retinopathy & glaucoma management, **trachoma eliminated (2024)**.
- **Vitamin A** – prevents nutritional blindness.
- **World Sight Day** – 2nd Thursday of October.

## 7.4 National Mental Health Programme (NMHP)

- **NMHP – 1982**; **DMHP (District Mental Health Programme) – 1996**; **Mental Healthcare Act 2017** (rights-based, **decriminalised suicide attempt – Section 115**, advance directives, nominated representative, **no ECT without anaesthesia; no ECT in minors**); **National Suicide Prevention Strategy (2022)**; **Tele-MANAS (Oct 2022) – toll-free 14416 / 1-800-891-4416**; **Kiran helpline**; **World Mental Health Day – 10 October**.
- **Rights of Persons with Disabilities (RPwD) Act 2016** – **21 disabilities** (incl. mental illness, autism, intellectual disability, acid attack, thalassaemia, haemophilia, sickle cell, Parkinson's); **benchmark disability ≥ 40 %**; reservation **4 % in govt jobs, 5 % in higher education**; **UDID card**.

## 7.5 Tobacco Control – NTCP & COTPA

- **NTCP – 2007–08**; **COTPA (Cigarettes & Other Tobacco Products Act) – 2003**:
  - **Section 4 – ban on smoking in public places** (since 2 Oct 2008).
  - **Section 5 – ban on advertisement**.
  - **Section 6(a) – no sale to persons < 18 yrs**; **6(b) – no sale within 100 yards of educational institutions**.
  - **Section 7 – pictorial health warnings (85 % of pack surface)**.
- **Tobacco Quitline – 1800-112-356**; **mCessation (SMS)**; **Prohibition of Electronic Cigarettes Act 2019** (e-cigarettes banned); **WHO FCTC (2003; India ratified 2004)**; **World No Tobacco Day – 31 May**; **GATS-2 (2016–17) – 28.6 % adults use tobacco** (smokeless more common).
- **Tobacco Free Educational Institutions (ToFEI)** guidelines.

## 7.6 Other Programmes

| Programme | Year | Key points |
|---|---|---|
| **NPHCE** (National Programme for Health Care of the Elderly) | **2010** | **Elderly ≥ 60 yrs**; geriatric clinics at CHC/ DH, **Regional Geriatric Centres**, **National Centres for Ageing (AIIMS Delhi, MMC Chennai)**; home-based care; **Maintenance & Welfare of Parents & Senior Citizens Act 2007**; **Elder line 14567**; **International Day of Older Persons – 1 October** |
| **NPPCD** (Prevention & Control of Deafness) | **2006** | Early detection (newborn hearing screening – OAE), hearing aids; **noise > 85 dB → hearing loss**; **World Hearing Day – 3 March** |
| **NIDDCP** (National Iodine Deficiency Disorders Control Programme) | **1992** (earlier **NGCP 1962**) | **Iodised salt – 30 ppm at production, 15 ppm at consumer/ household**; IDD – goitre, cretinism, deaf-mutism, still births, ↓ IQ; **urinary iodine excretion** – best indicator of recent intake; **Global IDD Prevention Day – 21 October**; **ban on non-iodised salt for human consumption** |
| **NPPCF** (Prevention & Control of Fluorosis) | **2008–09** | **Fluoride > 1.5 mg/L** – dental mottling, skeletal fluorosis (**genu valgum**); **Nalgonda technique** – defluoridation (alum + lime); Gujarat, Rajasthan, Andhra affected |
| **National Oral Health Programme** | 2014 | Dental caries (fluoride < 0.5 mg/L), oral cancer screening |
| **NPPMBI** (Burn Injuries) | 2010 | Burn units |
| **NPCCHH** (Climate Change & Human Health) | 2019 | Heat-related illnesses, air pollution |
| **National Programme for Palliative Care** | 2012 | – |
| **National Tobacco Testing Labs, NPPC** | – | – |
| **National Sickle Cell Anaemia Elimination Mission** | **1 July 2023 (Shahdol, MP)** | Screening of **7 crore people (0–40 yrs)** in tribal areas by 2025–26; **elimination by 2047**; sickle cell genetic status card |
| **Anaemia Mukt Bharat** | 2018 | **6 × 6 × 6 strategy** – 6 beneficiary groups, 6 interventions, 6 institutional mechanisms; **Test-Treat-Talk (T3) camps** |
| **National Deworming Day** | 2015 | **10 Feb & 10 Aug – albendazole 1–19 yrs** |
| **Pradhan Mantri National Dialysis Programme** | 2016 | Free dialysis at district hospitals |
