## 9.1 Nutrients – Key Facts

| **Protein** | **4 kcal/g**; **RDA adult ~0.83 g/kg/day** (ICMR 2020); **egg protein – reference protein (NPU ~ 96, biological value ~ 94)**; **cereal + pulse = mutual supplementation** (lysine in pulse, methionine in cereal) |

- **ICMR–NIN RDA 2020** – **reference man 65 kg, reference woman 55 kg**; adult **sedentary man ~2110 kcal/day, woman ~1660 kcal/day** (moderate man ~2710); **pregnancy + 350 kcal (2nd & 3rd trimester)**, **lactation + 600 (0–6 m)/ + 520 (6–12 m)**; protein pregnancy +9.5 g (2nd) / +22 g (3rd).

- **Balanced diet** – contains all nutrients in correct proportion for health; **food groups** – cereals, pulses, milk/ animal foods, vegetables & fruits, fats & sugars.

## 9.2 Malnutrition – Community Burden & Assessment

- **Double burden** – undernutrition + overweight/ obesity.
- **NFHS-5 (2019–21) under-5** – **stunting 35.5 %**, **wasting 19.3 %**, **severe wasting 7.7 %**, **underweight 32.1 %**, **overweight 3.4 %**; **anaemia children 6–59 m – 67.1 %**, **women 57 %**.
- **Methods of nutritional assessment ("ABCD")** – **A**nthropometry (weight, height, MUAC, skinfold, BMI), **B**iochemical (Hb, serum albumin, urinary iodine), **C**linical examination (signs), **D**ietary survey (24-hr recall, weighment, food frequency) + vital statistics, ecological factors.
- **Weight-for-age** – underweight; **height-for-age** – stunting (chronic); **weight-for-height** – wasting (acute); **MUAC < 11.5 cm** (6–59 m) – SAM.
- **BMI (Quetelet's index) = weight (kg) / height² (m²)**; **Asian cut-offs: normal 18.5–22.9, overweight 23–24.9, obese ≥ 25**; WHO international: overweight 25–29.9, obese ≥ 30. **Waist circumference** – Asian men > 90 cm, women > 80 cm (abdominal obesity).
- **Growth monitoring** – **MCP card growth chart** monthly (AWW) – **flattening/ falling curve** = action.

## 9.4 Food Hygiene, Fortification & Adulteration

- **Food fortification** – adding micronutrients to commonly consumed food (**iodised salt, vitamin A & D in edible oil & milk, fortified rice/ wheat flour**); **"+F" logo (FSSAI)**.
- **Food enrichment** – restoring nutrients lost in processing.
- **Food adulteration** – **Food Safety & Standards Act (FSSA) 2006** → **FSSAI (2008)** (replaced PFA Act 1954); **Eat Right India** campaign.

| Adulterant | Food | Health effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Kesari dal (Lathyrus sativus)** | Arhar/ besan | **Lathyrism – spastic paraplegia (neurolathyrism) – BOAA toxin** |
| **Argemone oil** | Mustard oil | **Epidemic dropsy** (oedema, glaucoma) |
| **Metanil yellow** | Turmeric, sweets, dal | Carcinogenic, liver damage |
| **Lead chromate** | Turmeric | Anaemia, brain damage |
| **Papaya seeds** | Black pepper | Liver problems |
| **Starch** | Milk, paneer | – |
| **Brick powder** | Chilli powder | GI problems |
| **Aflatoxin (Aspergillus flavus)** | **Groundnut, maize (mouldy)** | **Hepatocellular carcinoma**, liver damage |
| **Ergot (Claviceps purpurea)** | Bajra, rye | Ergotism – gangrene |

- **Milk hygiene** – **pasteurisation** (holder 63 °C × 30 min; HTST 72 °C × 15 sec); **phosphatase test – checks adequacy of pasteurisation**; **methylene blue reduction test – bacterial quality**; milk-borne diseases – TB, brucellosis, typhoid, Q fever.
- **Food poisoning** – **Staph aureus (1–6 hrs, preformed toxin)**, **Bacillus cereus (fried rice)**, **Clostridium botulinum (canned food – descending paralysis; most fatal)**, Clostridium perfringens, Salmonella (12–24 hrs).
