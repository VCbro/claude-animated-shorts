# Home Visit, Family Health Care, Records & Reports

## 13.1 Family Health Nursing

- **Family – basic unit of society & of community health nursing service**. Types – **nuclear**, **joint (extended)**, **three-generation**, single-parent, blended.
- **Functions of family** – reproductive, **socialisation**, economic, affectional, **health care**, protection, status.
- **Family health nursing process** – **assessment (family health assessment – family folder/ family health card)** → **nursing diagnosis (family health problems)** → **planning with family** → implementation → evaluation.
- **Family Health Tree/ genogram**, **eco-map**.
- **Family life cycle (Duvall – 8 stages)** – beginning family → childbearing → preschool → school-age → teenage → launching → middle-aged parents → ageing family.

## 13.2 Community Diagnosis & Survey

- **Community diagnosis** – identification & quantification of health problems of a community, based on data (morbidity, mortality, environment, resources) – **basis of community health planning**.
- **Community survey** steps – define objectives, area, prepare tools (**schedule/ questionnaire**), pre-test, train surveyors, **map** the area, collect data (house-to-house), analyse, report, plan.
- **Community needs assessment** – **felt needs** (people's perceived) vs **real needs** (professionally identified); **community participation** essential.
- **PRA (Participatory Rural Appraisal)** – **social mapping, seasonal calendar, transect walk, Venn diagram**.
- **Windshield survey** – quick observation driving/ walking through community.

## 13.3 Home Visit

:::def Home visit
A **family-nurse contact** that allows the health worker to **assess the home & family situation** to provide necessary nursing care & health-related activities. **Backbone/ cornerstone of community health nursing**.
:::

- **Purposes** – give care to sick, **health education in family setting**, observe home environment, **case finding & follow-up**, **contact tracing**, referral, MCH services (ANC/ PNC, newborn), build rapport, demonstrations.
- **Principles** – **purposeful (definite objective)**, **planned in advance**, flexible, **based on family needs**, **family participation**, **regular & continuous**, use of resources, **records maintained**, health education integral, **courtesy & respect for customs**, punctuality, confidentiality.
- **Steps** – **(1) Initiation** (contact, appointment), **(2) Pre-visit preparation** (review records, plan, prepare bag), **(3) In-home activities** (introduce, greet, explain purpose, **establish rapport**, assess, **care using bag technique**, health education, plan next visit), **(4) Termination** (summarise, plan follow-up), **(5) Post-visit – recording & reporting**, evaluation.
- **Priority of home visits** – **acute communicable disease/ sick > newborn & postnatal mother > antenatal > infant > under-5 > others**; visit **non-infectious cases first and infectious cases last** in a day (to prevent spread).

### Bag technique
- **Bag technique** – tool by which the nurse, during home visit, **performs nursing procedures with ease & deftness, saving time & effort, without contaminating the bag & its contents**; **purpose – prevent cross-infection** (nurse ↔ patient ↔ family ↔ community).
- **Principles** – bag to have **all necessary articles**; **place bag on a clean, flat, high surface** (never on bed/ floor), **on paper lining**; **hand washing before & after care**; **open bag only after washing hands**; **separate clean & dirty articles**; **apron worn with clean side inside & folded with soiled side in**; take out only what is needed; **clean & disinfect reusable articles before putting back**; bag **cleaned regularly & contents replenished**.
- **Contents (typical)** – **thermometers (oral & rectal)**, **BP apparatus, stethoscope**, **scissors (2)**, **forceps**, **kidney tray**, syringes & needles, **gauze, cotton**, **spirit, savlon/ antiseptic**, **soap in soap dish, hand towel, apron**, **newspapers**, **gloves**, mackintosh, **urine testing kit (Benedict's/ dipsticks)**, test tubes, spirit lamp, **ORS, IFA, paracetamol, contraceptives**, **MCP cards, records, IEC material**, measuring tape, **weighing scale** (separately).

## 13.4 Clinics in Community

- **MCH clinic, ANC clinic, under-5 clinic, immunization session (VHND – Village Health & Nutrition Day; now VHSND)**, family planning, NCD clinics, TB/ leprosy clinics.
- **VHSND (Village Health, Sanitation & Nutrition Day)** – **monthly at Anganwadi**; ANM, ASHA, AWW – immunization, ANC, growth monitoring, health education, contraceptives, referral.
- **Under-5 clinic (Morley's concept)** – care in illness, **immunization**, **growth monitoring (Road to Health chart)**, oral rehydration, family planning, health education; **"Road to Health card"** – symbol: triangle of care.

## 13.5 Records & Reports

- **Record** – **written account of an event/ activity** (permanent, legal document); **report** – summary of records/ activities **communicated to others** (higher authority) in a prescribed form.
- **Importance** – continuity of care, **legal document**, communication, evaluation, **planning & policy**, research, statistics, supervision, teaching.
- **Principles** – **accurate, complete, concise, legible, timely (written promptly), signed & dated**, **confidential**, in ink/ digital, no erasures (single line cross & initial), standardised forms.

| Registers/ records at Sub-Centre (ANM) | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Eligible Couple Register (ECR)** | Couples with wife 15–49 yrs; **updated every April (annual)**; FP methods |
| **MCH register / RCH register** | Pregnant women, deliveries, PNC, child immunization |
| **Birth & death register** | Vital events |
| **Immunization/ due list** | Tracking |
| **Village/ family health register** (family folder) | Household survey |
| **Stock register** (drugs, vaccines, equipment) | Inventory |
| **Communicable disease registers** (fever – malaria, TB, leprosy) | Surveillance |
| **NCD register (CBAC)** | Screening |
| **MCP card** (with mother) | Home-based record |

- **Digital systems** – **HMIS (Health Management Information System, 2008)**, **RCH portal (earlier MCTS – Mother & Child Tracking System, 2009)** – name-based tracking with **RCH ID**, **Ni-kshay**, **IHIP**, **ANMOL app (ANM Online)**, **U-WIN** (digital immunization registry, 2023–24), **eVIN**, **ABHA**, **TeCHO+ (Gujarat – Technology Enabled Community Health Operations)**.
- **Monthly reports** – ANM → PHC (by 5th of month approx.) → block → district; **weekly IDSP S-form**.

## 13.6 Referral System

- **Referral** – transferring a patient to a higher/ specialised level for better management; **two-way (referral + feedback/ back-referral)**.
- **Referral slip** – patient details, findings, treatment given, reason, contact; **ASHA escorts**; **108 ambulance (emergency)**, **102 (pregnant women & sick infants – JSSK)**.

## 13.7 Roles of ANM/ Female Health Worker & CHO (Job responsibilities)

- **ANM (FHW)** – **MCH** (ANC, delivery, PNC, newborn care, HBNC support), **immunization**, **family planning** (IUCD insertion, OCP, condoms), **nutrition** (IFA, vitamin A, ORS, zinc), **communicable disease control** (fever surveillance, RDT, DOT, leprosy), **NCD screening**, **health education**, **records & reports** (registers, RCH portal), **VHSND**, supervise/ support **ASHA & dais**, **school health**, coordinate with AWW, **first aid**.
- **MPW (male)** – vector control, malaria (RDT/ smear), sanitation, chlorination of wells, TB/ leprosy, environmental sanitation, health education to men, NSV motivation.
- **LHV (Health Supervisor – female)** – **supervision & guidance of ANMs** (~4–6 SCs), conducts clinics, checks records, training, conducts difficult deliveries, liaison with MO.
- **CHO (Community Health Officer, AAM-SC)** – **team leader**; delivers **CPHC 12 packages**, NCD screening & follow-up, **teleconsultation (eSanjeevani)**, dispensing essential drugs, wellness sessions, **performance-linked incentives**; supervises ASHAs & MPWs at SC level.
