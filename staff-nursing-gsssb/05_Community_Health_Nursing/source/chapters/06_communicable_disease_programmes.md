## 6.1 Tuberculosis – NTEP

- **Mycobacterium tuberculosis** (Robert Koch, **24 March 1882 – World TB Day**); **droplet nuclei (airborne)**; **one untreated sputum +ve patient infects 10–15 people/ year**; India has **~26–27 % of global TB burden** (highest).
- **Programme history** – **NTP 1962** → **RNTCP 1997 (DOTS)** → **NTEP (National TB Elimination Programme) – 1 Jan 2020**; **National Strategic Plan 2017–25 – "End TB by 2025"** (India's target; SDG 2030); **Pradhan Mantri TB Mukt Bharat Abhiyaan (Sept 2022)** – **Ni-kshay Mitra** (community support/ food baskets); **100-day intensified campaign (Dec 2024)**.
- **Presumptive TB** – **cough ≥ 2 weeks**, fever, weight loss, night sweats, haemoptysis, any abnormal CXR; **contacts, PLHIV – any duration cough**.
- **Diagnosis** – **NAAT (CBNAAT/ GeneXpert, Truenat) – upfront for all** (also detects **rifampicin resistance**); sputum smear microscopy (**ZN stain**, LED fluorescence); **2 sputum samples** (one spot, one early morning); CXR; culture (gold standard – LJ medium/ MGIT).
- **Treatment (drug-sensitive)** – **Intensive phase 2 months HRZE + continuation phase 4 months HRE (daily, fixed-dose combination, weight bands)**; **6 months total**; extended continuation in TBM/ bone TB.
- **Drug side effects** – **INH: peripheral neuropathy (give pyridoxine), hepatitis**; **Rifampicin: orange-red urine/ tears, hepatitis, drug interactions (OCP failure)**, flu-like; **Pyrazinamide: hepatotoxicity, hyperuricaemia (gout)**, arthralgia; **Ethambutol: optic neuritis (red-green colour blindness)**; **Streptomycin: ototoxicity, nephrotoxicity**.
- **DR-TB** – **MDR-TB (resistant to H & R)**; **XDR**; **shorter oral bedaquiline-containing regimen / BPaLM (bedaquiline, pretomanid, linezolid, moxifloxacin – 6 months)**.
- **TPT (TB preventive therapy)** – household contacts (all ages, after ruling out active TB), PLHIV – **6H (INH 6 months)** or **3HP (INH + rifapentine weekly × 12 weeks)**.
- **Ni-kshay Poshan Yojana (2018)** – **DBT ₹500/month** → **enhanced to ₹1,000/month (Nov 2024)** during treatment; **Ni-kshay portal** (case notification – **mandatory notification of TB (2012)**, including private sector).
- **Nurse role** – case finding, **DOT (Directly Observed Treatment) / treatment supporter**, adherence (**99DOTS, MERM**), contact tracing, **cough etiquette**, sputum collection (open air, deep cough, 2–5 ml), nutrition, follow-up sputum at end of IP & treatment.
- **BCG** – protects children against severe TB (TBM, miliary).

## 6.2 Leprosy – NLEP

- **Mycobacterium leprae** (**Hansen, 1873**); **longest incubation (avg 3–5 yrs)**; **droplet (nasal discharge) – main route**; affects **skin & peripheral nerves**; **not highly infectious**; **lepromin test** (prognostic, not diagnostic).
- **Cardinal signs (any one = leprosy)**: (1) **hypopigmented/ reddish skin patch with definite loss of sensation**, (2) **thickened/ enlarged peripheral nerve with loss of sensation/ weakness** of muscles supplied, (3) **AFB in slit-skin smear**.
- **Nerves commonly thickened** – **ulnar (most common)**, common peroneal (lateral popliteal), posterior tibial, great auricular, radial cutaneous, median, facial.
- **Classification (WHO – for treatment)**: **PB (paucibacillary) – 1–5 skin lesions, no nerve / only 1 nerve, smear negative**; **MB (multibacillary) – ≥ 6 lesions / > 1 nerve / smear positive**.
- **MDT (Multi-Drug Therapy – 1982 WHO; India 1983)** – **rifampicin (monthly supervised) + clofazimine + dapsone**; **PB – 6 months**, **MB – 12 months**; (WHO now recommends the **3-drug regimen for PB as well**); blister packs; **rifampicin – red urine; clofazimine – skin pigmentation (reddish-black), dryness; dapsone – haemolysis (G6PD), DRESS**.
- **Reactions** – **Type 1 (reversal)** – existing lesions inflamed, **neuritis** → **prednisolone**; **Type 2 (ENL – erythema nodosum leprosum)** – MB, painful nodules, fever → prednisolone, **thalidomide**, clofazimine.
- **Deformities** – **claw hand (ulnar), foot drop (peroneal), lagophthalmos (facial)**, **plantar ulcers**; **self-care (soak, scrape, oil)**, **MCR footwear**, reconstructive surgery (**RCS**) – incentive.
- **Elimination as public health problem – prevalence < 1 per 10,000** (national level achieved **Dec 2005**); **SDR-PEP (single-dose rifampicin post-exposure prophylaxis)** to contacts; **LCDC (Leprosy Case Detection Campaign)**; **SPARSH Leprosy Awareness Campaign (30 Jan – Anti-Leprosy Day, Gandhiji's death anniversary)**; **National Strategic Plan – zero transmission by 2030**.

## 6.3 Vector-Borne Diseases – NCVBDC

- **NCVBDC (National Center for Vector Borne Diseases Control)** (earlier **NVBDCP – 2003/04**; NMEP 1958; NMCP 1953) – **6 diseases: malaria, dengue, chikungunya, Japanese encephalitis, kala-azar, lymphatic filariasis**.

| Disease | Vector | Key points |
|---|---|---|
| **Malaria** | **Female Anopheles** (night biter; *An. culicifacies* – rural, *An. stephensi* – urban) | **P. vivax (most common), P. falciparum (most dangerous – cerebral)**; **RDT/ blood smear (thick & thin)**; vivax – **chloroquine 3 days + primaquine 14 days**; falciparum – **ACT (AS + SP; AL in NE states) + primaquine single dose**; severe – **injectable artesunate**; **LLINs, IRS (indoor residual spraying)**, larvivorous fish; **NFME 2016 – malaria elimination by 2030**; **ABER (annual blood examination rate) ≥ 10 %**; **API (annual parasite incidence) – positives per 1000 population**; **World Malaria Day 25 April** |
| **Dengue** | **Aedes aegypti** (day biter, **breeds in clean stored water** – coolers, tyres, pots) | Fever, myalgia, rash, thrombocytopenia; **NS1 (early)/ IgM ELISA**; **no NSAIDs/ aspirin**; **source reduction – "dry day" weekly**; **National Dengue Day – 16 May** |
| **Chikungunya** | Aedes aegypti/ albopictus | Fever + **severe polyarthralgia** ("bends up") |
| **Japanese encephalitis (JE)** | **Culex tritaeniorhynchus/ vishnui** (rice fields); **pigs – amplifier**, ardeid birds – reservoir | AES; **JE vaccine (2 doses – 9 m & 16–24 m) in endemic districts**; man – dead-end host |
| **Lymphatic filariasis** | **Culex quinquefasciatus** (dirty water); *Wuchereria bancrofti* (most), *Brugia malayi* (Mansonia) | **Nocturnal periodicity – night blood smear (8:30 PM – 12 midnight / 10 PM–2 AM)**; lymphoedema, **hydrocele, elephantiasis**; **MDA – DEC + albendazole (DA)** or **IDA (+ ivermectin)** annually; **morbidity management (MMDP)** – limb hygiene, hydrocele surgery; target elimination |
| **Kala-azar (visceral leishmaniasis)** | **Sandfly – Phlebotomus argentipes** (cracks in mud walls) | *Leishmania donovani*; Bihar, Jharkhand, UP, WB; **fever, massive splenomegaly, darkening of skin, pancytopenia**; **rK39 RDT**; **single-dose liposomal amphotericin B**; **IRS (synthetic pyrethroid)**; **elimination target < 1 case/10,000 at block level – achieved in all endemic blocks (2023)**; **PKDL** – post kala-azar dermal leishmaniasis |

- **Integrated vector management** – **source reduction (most effective, cheapest)**, **larvivorous fish – Gambusia, Guppy**, **temephos (Abate) – larvicide in drinking water**, **BTI**, fogging (adult control, outbreaks), personal protection (repellents, nets, full sleeves).

## 6.4 HIV/ AIDS – NACP

- **NACO (National AIDS Control Organisation) – 1992**; **NACP phase I 1992** → **phase V (2021–26)**; **goal – end AIDS as public health threat by 2030**; **95-95-95 targets** (95 % know status, 95 % of them on ART, 95 % of them virally suppressed).
- **Transmission** – **sexual (most common in India ~85–90 %)**, **parent-to-child**, blood, needle sharing (IDUs). **Highest risk per exposure – blood transfusion (~90 %)**.
- **Window period** – ~3–12 weeks (antibody tests negative though infectious).
- **Diagnosis (NACO)** – **3 different rapid tests (E/R/S – different antigens/ principles)** for diagnosis in adults; infants – **DNA PCR (EID) at 6 weeks**; **ICTC (Integrated Counselling & Testing Centre)** – pre-test & post-test counselling, **informed consent & confidentiality**.
- **"Test & Treat" policy (2017)** – ART for all PLHIV regardless of CD4; **first-line – TLD (tenofovir + lamivudine + dolutegravir)**; viral load monitoring.
- **PPTCT** – universal screening of pregnant women; lifelong ART; infant **nevirapine prophylaxis**.
- **Targeted interventions (TI)** for **high-risk groups** – FSW, MSM, IDU, transgender, migrants, truckers; **OST (opioid substitution)**; condom promotion; **STI clinics – "Suraksha" (colour-coded kits)**; **blood safety – NAT-tested, voluntary donors**.
- **HIV/ AIDS (Prevention & Control) Act 2017** – **prohibits discrimination**, informed consent, confidentiality; **Ombudsman** in each state.
- **World AIDS Day – 1 December**; **Red ribbon**; **toll-free 1097**.
- **PEP (post-exposure prophylaxis)** – start **within 2 hrs (best), ≤ 72 hrs**, **28 days** of ART (TLD); wash wound with soap & water (**don't squeeze/ scrub**).

## 6.5 Other Communicable Disease Programmes

| Disease/ Programme | Key points |
|---|---|
| **Polio (Pulse Polio – 1995)** | **India polio-free – 27 March 2014** (last case 13 Jan 2011, Howrah); **NIDs/ SNIDs**; **AFP surveillance < 15 yrs**; bOPV + fIPV in UIP; **cVDPV** risk; National Immunization Day |
| **National Viral Hepatitis Control Programme (NVHCP)** | **28 July 2018 (World Hepatitis Day)**; **eliminate hepatitis C & reduce HBV by 2030**; free diagnosis & treatment; Hep B birth dose; HAV & HEV – faeco-oral (HEV – fatal in pregnancy) |
| **Rabies – NRCP** (National Rabies Control Programme) | **Dog bite – 96 %**; **"Zero by 30"** (zero human rabies deaths by 2030); **wound wash 15 min with soap & running water** (first aid); **category I** (touch/ feed/ lick intact skin – no PEP), **II** (minor scratches without bleeding/ nibbling – **vaccine**), **III** (transdermal bites/ licks on broken skin/ mucosa/ bat – **vaccine + RIG**); ARV **IM (Essen – days 0, 3, 7, 14, 28)** or **ID (Updated Thai Red Cross – 0.1 ml at 2 sites on days 0, 3, 7, 28)**; **World Rabies Day – 28 Sept** (Louis Pasteur) |
| **Cholera** | **Vibrio cholerae O1/ O139**; rice-water stools; **ORS mainstay**; **doxycycline single dose (adults)**; chemoprophylaxis not recommended for mass |
| **Typhoid** | Salmonella Typhi; faeco-oral; **Widal/ blood culture**; **typhoid conjugate vaccine (TCV)**; carriers |
| **COVID-19** | SARS-CoV-2; first case in India – **30 Jan 2020 (Kerala)**; WHO pandemic declared **11 March 2020**; **vaccination started 16 Jan 2021 (Covishield, Covaxin)**; CoWIN |
| **Antimicrobial resistance (AMR)** | **National Action Plan on AMR (2017)**; **Red Line campaign** (antibiotics with red vertical line – Rx only); Kerala KARSAP |
| **Leptospirosis, scrub typhus, Nipah, KFD, plague** | Emerging/ re-emerging zoonoses; **One Health** approach |
