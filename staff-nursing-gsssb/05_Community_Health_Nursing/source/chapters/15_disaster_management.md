## 15.1 Disaster – Basics

:::def Disaster (DM Act 2005)
A **catastrophe, mishap, calamity or grave occurrence** arising from natural or man-made causes, resulting in **substantial loss of life, human suffering, damage to property/ environment**, beyond the **coping capacity of the affected community**. **Risk = Hazard × Vulnerability / Capacity**.
:::

| Natural | Man-made |
|---|---|
| **Earthquake** (Bhuj, Gujarat – **26 Jan 2001**, ~20,000 deaths), **cyclone** (Gujarat coast – Tauktae 2021, Biparjoy 2023), **flood**, **drought**, landslide, tsunami (**26 Dec 2004**), heat wave, cold wave, avalanche, lightning, epidemics/ pandemics | **Industrial/ chemical (Bhopal 1984)**, **nuclear (Chernobyl 1986, Fukushima 2011)**, fire, building collapse (**Morbi bridge collapse, Gujarat – 30 Oct 2022**), road/ rail/ air accidents, **stampede**, war, terrorism, bioterrorism |

- **India** – ~**58.6 % landmass prone to earthquakes**, ~12 % floods, ~68 % drought, **5,700 km coast – cyclones**. **Gujarat** – earthquake (Kutch – **Zone V – highest seismic zone**), cyclones, floods, droughts, industrial hazards.

## 15.2 Disaster Management Cycle

@fig disaster_triage.svg | Disaster management cycle & START triage colour codes

| Phase | Activities |
|---|---|
| **Mitigation (prevention)** | Reduce risk/ impact – **structural** (earthquake-resistant buildings, embankments, cyclone shelters) & **non-structural** (land-use planning, building codes, insurance, awareness) |
| **Preparedness** | **Disaster plans**, **early warning systems**, **mock drills**, stockpiling, **training**, community awareness, hospital disaster plan, resource inventory, communication network |
| **Response (impact/ emergency)** | **Rescue (search & rescue)**, **evacuation**, **triage**, first aid, **mass casualty management**, shelter, food, water, sanitation, **disease surveillance**, identification & disposal of dead |
| **Recovery (rehabilitation & reconstruction)** | **Rehabilitation** (restoration of services, psychosocial support, livelihood) & **reconstruction** ("**build back better**"); long-term development |

- **Pre-disaster** – mitigation & preparedness; **post-disaster** – response & recovery.
- **Sendai Framework for DRR (2015–2030)** – 4 priorities, 7 targets; replaced **Hyogo Framework (2005–15)**.

## 15.3 Disaster Management Structure in India

- **Disaster Management Act – 2005** (enacted 23 Dec 2005).
- **NDMA (National Disaster Management Authority)** – **chaired by Prime Minister**; policies, plans, guidelines.
- **NEC (National Executive Committee)** – chaired by **Union Home Secretary**; **Ministry of Home Affairs – nodal ministry** (drought – Agriculture; epidemics/ biological – **MoHFW**).
- **NDRF (National Disaster Response Force)** – specialised force (~16 battalions); **NIDM (National Institute of Disaster Management)** – training & research (New Delhi).
- **SDMA** – chaired by **Chief Minister**; **GSDMA (Gujarat State Disaster Management Authority) – 2001 (after Bhuj earthquake)**; **Gujarat State Disaster Management Act 2003** (first state DM Act); **SDRF**.
- **DDMA** – chaired by **District Collector/ DM**; **EOC (Emergency Operation Centre)** at each level.
- **National Policy on DM – 2009**; **National DM Plan – 2016** (first; updated 2019).
- **Disaster helpline – 1078/ 1070**; **International Day for Disaster Risk Reduction – 13 Oct**.

## 15.4 Mass Casualty Management & Triage

- **Triage** – sorting casualties by **priority of treatment** based on severity & likelihood of survival – **"to do the greatest good for the greatest number"**.
- **START (Simple Triage & Rapid Treatment)** – based on **RPM: Respiration (> 30/min), Perfusion (CRT > 2 sec / no radial pulse), Mental status (can't follow simple commands)**; **walking wounded → green**; **JumpSTART** for children.

| Colour | Priority | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Red** | **Immediate (I)** – life-threatening but salvageable | Airway obstruction, tension pneumothorax, shock, severe haemorrhage, RR > 30 |
| **Yellow** | **Delayed/ Urgent (II)** – serious but stable, can wait | Fractures (closed), moderate burns without shock, abdominal injuries stable |
| **Green** | **Minor (III)** – "walking wounded" | Minor cuts, abrasions, sprains |
| **Black** | **Dead / expectant (IV)** – unsalvageable | No breathing after airway opening, massive head injury |

- **Field care** – **search & rescue**, **triage**, **first aid**, **stabilisation**, **evacuation** (transport to appropriate facility), **tagging (triage tags)**; **"golden hour"** (first hour after injury).
- **Hospital disaster plan** – **Incident Command System (ICS)**, surge capacity (discharge stable patients, cancel elective surgeries), triage area, communication, **mock drills**.

## 15.5 Public Health Problems after Disasters

- **Water-borne** – **diarrhoea, cholera, typhoid, hepatitis A & E** (floods – contaminated water); **vector-borne** – malaria, dengue (stagnant water – after 2–3 weeks); **leptospirosis (floods – rat urine)**; **ARI, measles** (overcrowding in camps – **measles vaccination priority in camps**, vitamin A); **malnutrition**; **injuries, tetanus (earthquakes)**; **snake bites (floods)**; **mental health – PTSD, depression, anxiety, grief**; NCDs interrupted care; **heat stroke/ hypothermia**.
- **Relief camp priorities** – **safe water (minimum 15 L/person/day – Sphere standards)**, **sanitation (1 latrine per 20 persons)**, shelter (**3.5 m² covered area/ person**), food (**2100 kcal/ person/ day**), immunization (measles), **disease surveillance & early warning (IDSP)**, vector control, health education, MCH care, **psychological first aid**.
- **Management of dead bodies** – **dead bodies don't cause epidemics** (unless cholera/ etc.); identification, dignified disposal; mass graves avoided.
- **Water disinfection in floods** – chlorination of wells, **chlorine tablets**, boiling.

## 15.6 Nurse's Role in Disaster

- **Preparedness** – participate in planning, **training (first aid, triage, CPR)**, mock drills, community awareness, maintain emergency supplies, vulnerable population list (pregnant, elderly, disabled).
- **Response** – **triage**, first aid, emergency care, **infection control**, **care of vulnerable groups**, immunization, nutrition, **disease surveillance & reporting**, sanitation supervision in camps, **psychosocial support (psychological first aid – "Look, Listen, Link")**, documentation.
- **Recovery** – rehabilitation, follow-up, community mental health, home visits, rebuild health services.
- **Self-care** of responders (burnout, compassion fatigue).

## 15.7 Emergency Medical Services

- **108 – Emergency Response Service (EMRI – GVK)** – **started in Gujarat – Aug 2007** (also AP 2005); free; **free ambulance for medical, police & fire emergencies**; **Basic Life Support (BLS) & Advanced Life Support (ALS) ambulances**.
- **102** – **JSSK free transport** for pregnant women & sick infants ("**Khilkhilat**" in Gujarat – drop back home for mother & newborn).
- **104** – health helpline (information, counselling); **1097** – AIDS helpline; **1098** – CHILDLINE; **181** – women helpline; **112** – national emergency number (Emergency Response Support System).
- **First aid principles** – **ABC**, **DR ABC** (Danger, Response, Airway, Breathing, Circulation), stop bleeding (**direct pressure**), immobilise fractures, prevent shock, treat burns (cool water 20 min), **don't give anything by mouth to unconscious**, arrange transport.
