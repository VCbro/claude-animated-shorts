## 8.1 Immunity – Overview

| Type | Components | Features |
|---|---|---|
| **Innate (natural, non-specific)** | **Skin & mucosa (1st line), lysozyme (tears, saliva), gastric acid, cilia, normal flora**; **phagocytes (neutrophils, macrophages), NK cells, complement, inflammation, fever, interferons (2nd line)** | Present from birth, rapid, **no memory** |
| **Acquired (adaptive, specific – 3rd line)** | **B cells → antibodies (humoral)**; **T cells (cell-mediated)** | **Specificity, memory, diversity**; slower first time (primary response) |

| Acquired | Active (own immune system; lasting; lag period) | Passive (ready-made antibodies; immediate; short ~weeks–months) |
|---|---|---|
| **Natural** | **Clinical/ subclinical infection** | **Transplacental IgG, breast milk/ colostrum IgA** |
| **Artificial** | **Vaccines (live, killed, toxoid, subunit)** | **Immunoglobulins & antisera – ATS/ TIG, ADS, ASV, RIG, HBIG, VZIG, anti-D** |

- **Herd immunity** – immunity of a population (enough immune individuals interrupt transmission); threshold depends on R₀ (**measles ~95 %**, polio ~80–85 %).
- **Primary immune response** – after first exposure; lag ~7–10 days; **mainly IgM**, low titre; **secondary (anamnestic/ booster) response** – faster, higher, longer, **mainly IgG** (basis of booster doses).
- **Antigen** – substance inducing specific immune response (proteins – best); **epitope** – antigenic determinant; **hapten** – low-molecular substance, antigenic only with carrier (drugs – penicillin); **adjuvant** – enhances immunogenicity (**aluminium salts – alum** in DPT, TT, Hep B; **shake test** for freeze damage).
- **Antibody (immunoglobulin)** – **Y-shaped glycoprotein; 2 heavy + 2 light chains; Fab (antigen-binding) & Fc (effector)**; **IgG (75 %, crosses placenta, longest half-life ~21–23 days)**, **IgM (pentamer, largest, first in primary response, best complement activator, ABO isohaemagglutinins)**, **IgA (secretory – dimer, colostrum, mucosa)**, **IgE (allergy, parasites – mast cells)**, IgD (B-cell receptor); **made by plasma cells**.
- **Complement** – ~30 serum proteins; **classical (antigen-antibody), alternative, lectin pathways**; functions – lysis (MAC – C5b-9), opsonisation (C3b), chemotaxis (C5a), inflammation (C3a, C5a – anaphylatoxins).
- **MHC (HLA)** – class I (all nucleated cells – CD8), class II (APCs – CD4); transplant matching.
- **Hypersensitivity (Gell & Coombs)** – **Type I – immediate/ anaphylactic (IgE)**; **Type II – cytotoxic (IgG/ IgM against cell antigens – blood transfusion reaction, Rh HDN, autoimmune haemolytic anaemia, Goodpasture, Graves', myasthenia)**; **Type III – immune complex (serum sickness, Arthus, SLE, PSGN, RA)**; **Type IV – delayed, T-cell mediated (Mantoux, contact dermatitis, graft rejection, granuloma – TB)**.
- **Immunodeficiency** – primary (DiGeorge – thymic aplasia; Bruton's agammaglobulinaemia; SCID) & secondary (**HIV, malnutrition (commonest worldwide), steroids, chemotherapy, diabetes, splenectomy**).

## 8.2 Antigen–Antibody Reactions & Lab Tests

| Reaction | Principle | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Agglutination** | **Particulate antigen + antibody → clumping** | **Blood grouping & cross-matching, Widal (typhoid), Weil-Felix (rickettsia), latex agglutination (RA factor, CRP, ASO), Coombs test (antiglobulin), TPHA (haemagglutination), Paul-Bunnell**, MAT (leptospira) |
| **Precipitation** | **Soluble antigen + antibody → precipitate** | **VDRL & RPR (flocculation – syphilis)**, **Elek's gel test (diphtheria toxin)**, Ouchterlony double diffusion, radial immunodiffusion |
| **Complement fixation** | Complement consumed | Wassermann (old – syphilis) |
| **Neutralisation** | Antibody neutralises toxin/ virus | **Schick test (diphtheria immunity)**, ASO (antistreptolysin O) |
| **ELISA (enzyme-linked immunosorbent assay)** | Enzyme-labelled antibody → colour change | **HIV (screening), HBsAg, dengue NS1/ IgM, hepatitis, many**; highly sensitive |
| **Immunofluorescence (IFA)** | Fluorescent-labelled antibody | **Rabies (corneal smear/ brain), syphilis (FTA-ABS)**, autoantibodies (ANA) |
| **Rapid tests – immunochromatography (lateral flow)/ card tests** | Coloured lines | **Malaria RDT, HIV rapid, pregnancy (hCG) test, dengue, filaria ICT, COVID antigen**, rK39 (kala-azar), HBsAg rapid |
| **Western blot** | Antibodies to specific separated proteins | HIV (old confirmatory), Lyme |
| **Molecular – PCR, RT-PCR, CBNAAT/ TrueNat, LAMP** | Nucleic acid amplification – **most sensitive & specific** | **TB (GeneXpert), COVID-19 (RT-PCR – gold standard), HIV viral load/ EID, HBV/ HCV viral load** |

- **Sensitivity** – ability to detect true positives (few false negatives) – **screening tests should be highly sensitive (ELISA)**; **specificity** – ability to detect true negatives (few false positives) – **confirmatory tests highly specific (Western blot, TPHA)**.

## 8.3 Vaccines

| Type | Examples | Points |
|---|---|---|
| **Live attenuated** | **BCG, OPV (bOPV), MR/ MMR, measles, rotavirus (Rotavac), varicella, yellow fever (17D), JE (SA 14-14-2), typhoid oral (Ty21a), influenza (intranasal)** | **Single dose often gives lasting immunity**, **mimic natural infection**; **contraindicated in pregnancy & severe immunodeficiency** (except specific); **most heat-sensitive (OPV – most heat-sensitive vaccine)**; **2 live injectable vaccines – give same day or ≥ 4 weeks apart** |
| **Killed/ inactivated** | **IPV (Salk), whole-cell pertussis, rabies (cell-culture), hepatitis A (inactivated), JE (inactivated – JENVAC), influenza (injectable), cholera (oral killed), Covaxin** | **Multiple doses + boosters needed**; safe in immunocompromised |
| **Toxoid** | **Tetanus toxoid (TT/ Td), diphtheria toxoid** | **Formalin-detoxified exotoxin**; **freeze-sensitive (never freeze – shake test)** |
| **Subunit/ recombinant/ conjugate/ polysaccharide** | **Hepatitis B (recombinant HBsAg – yeast)**, **HPV**, **Hib (conjugate)**, **PCV (pneumococcal conjugate)**, **TCV (typhoid conjugate)**, meningococcal, Vi polysaccharide | **Polysaccharide vaccines poor in < 2 yrs → conjugated to protein carrier** |
| **Newer** | **mRNA (COVID – Pfizer, Moderna; India – Gemcovac)**, **viral vector (Covishield)**, **DNA (ZyCoV-D)** | – |

### Universal Immunization Programme (UIP) – National Immunization Schedule (India)
| Age | Vaccines |
|---|---|
| **Birth** | **BCG, OPV-0 (within 15 days), Hepatitis B birth dose (within 24 hrs)** |
| **6 weeks** | **OPV-1, Pentavalent-1 (DPT + HepB + Hib), Rotavirus-1, fIPV-1, PCV-1** |
| **10 weeks** | **OPV-2, Pentavalent-2, Rotavirus-2** |
| **14 weeks** | **OPV-3, Pentavalent-3, Rotavirus-3, fIPV-2, PCV-2** |
| **9–12 months** | **MR-1, JE-1 (endemic), PCV booster, fIPV-3 (9 months – added 2023), Vitamin A 1st dose (1 lakh IU)** |
| **16–24 months** | **MR-2, JE-2, DPT booster-1, OPV booster, Vitamin A 2nd dose (2 lakh IU) – then every 6 months till 5 yrs (total 9 doses)** |
| **5–6 years** | **DPT booster-2** |
| **10 years & 16 years** | **Td** |
| **Pregnant women** | **Td-1 (early), Td-2 (4 weeks later), or Td booster** (if vaccinated within last 3 yrs) |

- **Routes & sites** – **BCG – ID, left upper arm (0.1 ml; 0.05 ml < 1 month)**; **OPV – oral 2 drops**; **Pentavalent, PCV, Hep B – IM anterolateral thigh (0.5 ml)**; **fIPV – ID 0.1 ml right upper arm**; **Rotavac – oral 5 drops**; **MR – SC right upper arm (0.5 ml)**; **JE live – SC left upper arm**; **DPT booster – IM thigh**; **Td – IM upper arm**; **Vitamin A – oral (1 ml = 1 lakh IU)**.
- **Mission Indradhanush (2014)**, **Intensified MI (IMI)**; **U-WIN** (digital registry); **National Immunization Day (Pulse Polio)**; **UIP started 1985**; **EPI 1978**.
- **Missed doses** – don't restart series, continue; **Pentavalent up to 1 yr, DPT booster up to 7 yrs**; **BCG up to 1 yr**; **OPV up to 5 yrs**.

### Cold chain
- **Cold chain** – system of storing & transporting vaccines at recommended temperature from manufacturer to beneficiary.
- **Equipment** – **walk-in coolers/ freezers (state/ regional)**, **ILR (ice-lined refrigerator – PHC; +2 to +8 °C; vaccines stored in baskets)**, **deep freezer (−15 to −25 °C – ice packs; OPV at state/ district level)**, **cold box**, **vaccine carrier (with 4 conditioned ice packs; keeps cold ~12 hrs+)**, **conditioned ice packs** (kept outside till "sweating"/ water sound – prevents freezing of vaccines).
- **Storage temperature** – **all UIP vaccines at +2 to +8 °C at PHC/ session level**; **OPV – −15 to −25 °C at state/ district (can be +2 to +8 °C at PHC for short)**.
- **Most heat-sensitive – OPV (then measles/ MR, BCG)**; **freeze-sensitive (never freeze) – Hep B, Pentavalent, DPT, TT/ Td, PCV, IPV, Rotavirus** ("**T-series & HepB**") – store in **ILR basket, not near bottom/ walls**; in ILR – **freeze-sensitive vaccines on top (in basket), heat-sensitive (OPV, BCG, MR) at bottom of basket**.
- **VVM (vaccine vial monitor)** – heat-sensitive label (square inside circle); **use if inner square lighter than outer circle**; **discard if square matches or darker than circle (stage 3/ 4)**.
- **Shake test** – detects **freeze damage** of adsorbed vaccines (DPT, TT, Hep B) – frozen vaccine sediments faster (flakes).
- **Open vial policy** – opened vials of **OPV, Pentavalent, DPT, TT/ Td, Hep B, IPV, PCV, Rotavirus** can be used in subsequent sessions up to **4 weeks** (if VVM OK, not contaminated, cold chain maintained, expiry not passed); **reconstituted BCG, MR, JE – discard after 4 hrs or end of session** (whichever earlier); **write date & time of opening**.
- **Diluent** – same manufacturer, cooled (+2 to +8 °C) before reconstitution; **never use water/ other diluent**.
- **Reverse cold chain** – transporting **stool samples (AFP)** in cold boxes to lab.
- **eVIN** – electronic vaccine intelligence network (real-time stock & temperature).

### AEFI (Adverse Events Following Immunisation)
- **Types** – **vaccine product-related, vaccine quality defect, immunisation error-related (programmatic – commonest preventable: wrong diluent, contaminated vial, wrong site/ route, reuse of syringe)**, immunisation anxiety-related, coincidental.
- **Minor** – fever, local pain/ swelling, irritability; **BCG – papule → ulcer → scar (normal, 2–6 weeks, heals 6–12 weeks; don't apply anything)**; **BCG adenitis**.
- **Serious** – **anaphylaxis (within minutes – observe 30 min after vaccination; adrenaline 1:1000 IM 0.01 ml/kg in AEFI kit)**, **toxic shock syndrome (contaminated reconstituted vial – MR; within hours)**, encephalopathy, **VAPP (OPV)**, death, hospitalisation, cluster → **report & investigate within 24 hrs (district AEFI committee)**.
- **AD (auto-disable) syringes (0.5 ml & 0.1 ml)** – prevent reuse; **hub cutter** for disposal.
- **Contraindications** – **anaphylaxis to previous dose**; **live vaccines – pregnancy, severe immunosuppression**; **DPT – encephalopathy within 7 days of previous dose (give DT)**; **mild illness/ fever, malnutrition, diarrhoea are NOT contraindications**.

## 8.4 Immunoglobulins & Antisera (Passive Immunisation)

| Product | Use |
|---|---|
| **HTIG (human tetanus Ig) 250–500 IU (prophylaxis), 3000–6000 IU (Rx)** / **ATS (equine 1500 IU – test dose)** | Tetanus prone wounds in non-immunised / treatment |
| **RIG – HRIG 20 IU/kg, ERIG 40 IU/kg**; rabies monoclonal antibodies | Category III animal bites |
| **HBIG** | Newborn of HBsAg+ mother, needle-stick (unvaccinated) |
| **VZIG** | Varicella exposure in immunocompromised, pregnant, newborn |
| **Anti-D (Rho-D) 300 µg** | Rh-negative mother within 72 hrs of delivery/ abortion, 28 weeks |
| **ADS (anti-diphtheritic serum)** | Diphtheria treatment |
| **ASV (polyvalent anti-snake venom – Big 4: cobra, common krait, Russell's viper, saw-scaled viper)** | Snakebite with envenomation |
| **IVIG** | Kawasaki disease, ITP, GBS, immunodeficiency |
| **Anti-thymocyte globulin** | Transplant rejection |

- **Serum sickness** (type III) 7–14 days after animal serum; **anaphylaxis** – keep adrenaline ready.
