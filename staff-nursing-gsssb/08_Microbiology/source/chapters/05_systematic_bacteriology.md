## 5.1 Gram-Positive Cocci

| Organism | Key features | Diseases | Lab/ notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Staphylococcus aureus** | Grape-like clusters; **coagulase +ve**, catalase +ve; **golden-yellow colonies**; β-haemolysis; carried in **anterior nares** | **Boils, furuncles, carbuncles, abscess (pus – commonest pyogenic), impetigo, wound/ SSI**, osteomyelitis (commonest cause), septic arthritis, **food poisoning (preformed enterotoxin – 1–6 hrs, vomiting; milk products)**, **toxic shock syndrome (TSST-1 – tampons)**, **scalded skin syndrome (infants)**, pneumonia (post-influenza), endocarditis (IV drug users – tricuspid), **neonatal sepsis, breast abscess** | **MRSA – vancomycin/ linezolid**; **mupirocin nasal** for carriers |
| **S. epidermidis (CoNS)** | Coagulase −ve; **skin flora**; **biofilm** | **Prosthetic devices, IV catheter, CSF shunt infections**; commonest blood-culture contaminant | – |
| **S. saprophyticus** | Coagulase −ve, novobiocin-resistant | **UTI in young sexually active women (2nd after E. coli)** | – |
| **Streptococcus pyogenes (Group A β-haemolytic, GAS)** | Chains; **bacitracin-sensitive**; M protein | **Pharyngitis/ tonsillitis**, **scarlet fever (erythrogenic toxin – strawberry tongue)**, impetigo, **erysipelas, cellulitis**, necrotising fasciitis, puerperal sepsis; **non-suppurative sequelae – rheumatic fever (after throat infection – 2–3 wks) & acute glomerulonephritis (after throat or skin)** | **ASO titre ↑**; **penicillin DOC**; RF prophylaxis – benzathine penicillin |
| **S. agalactiae (Group B Strep)** | Bile-tolerant; vaginal flora | **Neonatal sepsis & meningitis (early onset – commonest in West)** | Antenatal screening 35–37 wks |
| **Viridans streptococci (S. mutans, S. sanguis)** | **α-haemolytic**, optochin-resistant, mouth flora | **Dental caries (S. mutans)**, **subacute bacterial endocarditis (damaged valves after dental extraction)** | Antibiotic prophylaxis |
| **Streptococcus pneumoniae (Pneumococcus)** | **Lanceolate (flame-shaped) Gram +ve diplococci**, **capsulated**, α-haemolytic, **optochin-sensitive, bile-soluble**; **Quellung reaction** | **Lobar pneumonia (commonest cause of community-acquired pneumonia – rusty sputum)**, **otitis media**, sinusitis, **meningitis (adults – commonest)**, **post-splenectomy sepsis** | **PCV vaccine (UIP)**, PPSV23 |
| **Enterococcus faecalis** | Group D, gut flora; grows in **6.5 % NaCl, bile esculin** | **UTI, endocarditis, HAI**; **VRE** | Intrinsic resistance |

## 5.2 Gram-Negative Cocci – Neisseria

| Organism | Key features | Diseases |
|---|---|---|
| **N. meningitidis (Meningococcus)** | **Kidney/ bean-shaped diplococci (intracellular in neutrophils)**, capsulated; nasopharyngeal carriers; **droplet** | **Meningococcal meningitis (epidemic – cerebrospinal fever; children/ young adults, hostels, Hajj)**, **meningococcaemia – petechial/ purpuric rash**, **Waterhouse-Friderichsen syndrome (adrenal haemorrhage)**; **Rx – penicillin/ ceftriaxone**; **contacts – rifampicin/ ciprofloxacin prophylaxis**; vaccine (A, C, Y, W-135) |
| **N. gonorrhoeae (Gonococcus)** | Kidney-shaped diplococci (intracellular); non-capsulated; **Thayer-Martin medium**; **sexual transmission** | **Gonorrhoea – urethritis (purulent discharge – "clap"), cervicitis, PID (infertility, ectopic), ophthalmia neonatorum (onset 2–5 days)**, septic arthritis; **Rx – ceftriaxone (+ azithromycin)**; **no vaccine, no lasting immunity** |

## 5.3 Gram-Positive Bacilli

| Organism | Key features | Disease & points |
|---|---|---|
| **Corynebacterium diphtheriae** | **Club-shaped, Chinese-letter arrangement**, **metachromatic (volutin) granules – Albert's stain**; **Loeffler's serum slope** (rapid), **potassium tellurite (black colonies)**; **Elek's gel precipitation test (toxigenicity)**; **Schick test (immunity – historical)** | **Diphtheria – grey-white pseudomembrane (bleeds on removal) on tonsils/ pharynx**, **bull neck**, airway obstruction; **exotoxin → myocarditis (commonest cause of death), neuritis (palatal palsy)**; **Rx – antidiphtheritic serum (ADS – immediately, test dose) + penicillin/ erythromycin**; **droplet**; vaccine – **DPT/ Pentavalent, Td** |
| **Bacillus anthracis** | **Largest pathogenic bacterium**, **"bamboo stick/ box-car" chains, central spore, polypeptide capsule (D-glutamic acid)**, non-motile; **"Medusa head" colonies** | **Anthrax – zoonosis (cattle, sheep, goats – wool/ hide)**: **cutaneous (malignant pustule – painless black eschar, commonest)**, **pulmonary (woolsorter's disease – most fatal)**, intestinal; **bioterrorism agent**; Rx – ciprofloxacin/ penicillin/ doxycycline |
| **Bacillus cereus** | Spore-former | **Food poisoning – fried rice (emetic type, 1–6 hrs)** |
| **Clostridium tetani** | **Obligate anaerobe**, **"drumstick" terminal round spore**, motile; spores in **soil, dust, animal faeces, rusty nails** | **Tetanus – tetanospasmin blocks release of inhibitory neurotransmitters (glycine, GABA)** → **trismus/ lockjaw (first sign), risus sardonicus, opisthotonus, painful spasms (triggered by light/ noise), dysphagia**; **neonatal tetanus (unclean cord cutting – "8th day disease")**; Rx – **TIG (human tetanus immunoglobulin 3000–6000 IU)/ ATS, wound debridement, metronidazole/ penicillin, diazepam, dark quiet room, airway**; **prevention – TT/ Td vaccine; Td in pregnancy (2 doses/ booster)** |
| **Clostridium perfringens (welchii)** | Anaerobe, **non-motile**, capsulated, **double zone haemolysis**, **Nagler's reaction (α-toxin – lecithinase)**; stormy fermentation of milk | **Gas gangrene (myonecrosis – crepitus, foul smell, war wounds, crush injury)** – **Rx – debridement, penicillin, hyperbaric O₂**; **food poisoning (meat, 8–24 hrs)**; necrotising enteritis |
| **Clostridium botulinum** | Anaerobe, subterminal spore | **Botulism – canned/ preserved food (home-canned), infant botulism (honey)** → **descending symmetrical flaccid paralysis**, diplopia, ptosis, dysphagia, **no fever, normal sensorium**; **botulinum toxin – most potent toxin known (blocks ACh release)**; Rx – antitoxin; therapeutic Botox |
| **Clostridioides (Clostridium) difficile** | Spore-former | **Antibiotic-associated diarrhoea & pseudomembranous colitis (clindamycin, cephalosporins, fluoroquinolones)**; Rx – **oral vancomycin/ fidaxomicin**, (metronidazole); **soap & water hand wash** |
| **Listeria monocytogenes** | **Tumbling motility (at 25 °C)**, grows at **4 °C (refrigerator)** | **Neonatal meningitis, listeriosis in pregnancy (abortion)**, unpasteurised cheese; Rx – ampicillin |

## 5.4 Gram-Negative Bacilli – Enterobacteriaceae & Others

| Organism | Key features | Diseases & points |
|---|---|---|
| **Escherichia coli** | **Lactose fermenter (pink on MacConkey)**, motile, **commonest gut aerobe**; **indicator of faecal contamination of water** | **UTI (commonest cause – community & hospital)**, **neonatal meningitis (with GBS)**, **diarrhoea** – **ETEC (traveller's diarrhoea; commonest in children of developing countries), EPEC (infantile), EIEC (dysentery-like), EHEC (O157:H7 – haemolytic uraemic syndrome; undercooked beef), EAEC**; sepsis, SSI, **peritonitis**, cholecystitis |
| **Klebsiella pneumoniae** | **Lactose fermenter, mucoid colonies (capsule)**, non-motile | **Pneumonia (alcoholics, diabetics – "red currant jelly" sputum, upper lobe cavities)**, UTI, **neonatal sepsis & HAI (ESBL/ carbapenemase – NDM)** |
| **Proteus** | **Swarming growth**, NLF, **urease +ve** (alkaline urine), fishy odour | **UTI – struvite (staghorn) renal stones**; Weil-Felix test antigen |

| **Vibrio cholerae** (O1 – classical & El Tor; O139 Bengal) | **Comma-shaped, highly motile (darting – "shooting star"/ "fish in stream"), monotrichous**; **alkaline media – alkaline peptone water (enrichment), TCBS (yellow colonies)**; VR/ Cary-Blair transport; **string test** | **Cholera – cholera toxin ↑ cAMP → profuse painless "rice-water" stools (fishy odour) + vomiting → severe dehydration, hypovolaemic shock, acidosis, hypokalaemia**; **no fever**; incubation **hours–5 days**; **Rx – rehydration (ORS; IV Ringer lactate – best IV fluid)**, **doxycycline 300 mg single dose (adult)/ azithromycin (children, pregnancy)**; **oral cholera vaccine (Shanchol/ Euvichol – 2 doses)** |
| **V. parahaemolyticus** | Halophilic | **Seafood food poisoning** |

| **Acinetobacter baumannii** | Coccobacilli, non-fermenter | **ICU HAI (VAP, BSI) – MDR/ carbapenem-resistant** |

| **Haemophilus ducreyi** | "School of fish" | **Chancroid – soft, painful genital ulcer (soft sore)** + bubo |
| **Bordetella pertussis** | **Coccobacilli**, **Bordet-Gengou medium ("mercury drop/ bisected pearl" colonies)**, **cough plate/ pernasal swab** | **Whooping cough (pertussis/ "100-day cough")** – **catarrhal (most infectious) → paroxysmal (whoop, post-tussive vomiting, apnoea in infants) → convalescent**; **lymphocytosis**; complications – pneumonia (commonest cause of death), seizures, sub-conjunctival haemorrhage, hernia; **Rx – azithromycin/ erythromycin**; **DPT/ Pentavalent vaccine, aP in pregnancy (some countries)** |

| **Yersinia pestis** | **Bipolar ("safety-pin") staining**, rat flea (Xenopsylla cheopis) | **Plague – bubonic (commonest), pneumonic (droplet – most infectious), septicaemic**; Rx – streptomycin/ gentamicin, doxycycline; Surat 1994 outbreak |
| **Brucella** | Coccobacilli, capnophilic; zoonosis – **unpasteurised milk**, goats, cattle | **Brucellosis (undulant/ Malta fever)**, sacroiliitis, orchitis; occupational (vets, farmers) |
| **Legionella pneumophila** | Water systems, air-conditioners, cooling towers | **Legionnaires' disease (atypical pneumonia), Pontiac fever**; urinary antigen |

## 5.5 Mycobacteria

### Mycobacterium tuberculosis
- **Robert Koch (24 March 1882)** – **World TB Day 24 March**; **slender, slightly curved, acid-fast bacilli (AFB – mycolic acid)**, **non-motile, non-sporing, non-capsulated**, **obligate aerobe (upper lobes – high O₂)**; **slow growing (generation time ~14–18 hrs; culture 4–8 weeks on LJ medium – rough, buff, "cauliflower/ breadcrumb" colonies)**; **resistant to drying (survives in dried sputum for weeks), killed by sunlight/ UV, boiling, autoclave**; **5 % phenol** for sputum disinfection (or autoclave/ burn).
- **Transmission – airborne droplet nuclei** (cough, sneeze); **M. bovis – unpasteurised milk** (intestinal TB).
- **Pathology** – **Ghon focus** (primary lesion – lower part of upper lobe/ upper part of lower lobe) + hilar lymph nodes = **primary (Ghon) complex**; **caseous necrosis**, granuloma (**Langhans giant cells**, epithelioid cells); **secondary/ reactivation TB – apical cavitation**; **miliary TB** (haematogenous); **extra-pulmonary – lymph node (commonest – scrofula), pleural, abdominal, spine (Pott's spine – thoracic), meningitis (children), genitourinary, bone**.
- **Diagnosis** – **sputum smear microscopy (ZN/ fluorescent – 2 samples, spot + early morning)**; **CBNAAT/ GeneXpert (MTB + rifampicin resistance in 2 hrs) & TrueNat – now first-line (NTEP)**; **culture (LJ – gold standard; liquid MGIT – faster 10–14 days)**; **LPA (line probe assay – INH & RIF resistance)**; chest X-ray; **Mantoux (TST) – 5 TU PPD, 0.1 ml intradermal, read at 48–72 hrs, ≥ 10 mm induration positive (≥ 5 mm in HIV)** – type IV hypersensitivity, indicates infection not disease; **IGRA (QuantiFERON)**; ADA (pleural/ CSF).
- **BCG vaccine** – live attenuated **M. bovis (Bacille Calmette-Guérin, Danish 1331 strain in India)**; **at birth (up to 1 yr), 0.05 ml (< 1 month)/ 0.1 ml, intradermal, left upper arm (insertion of deltoid)**; reconstituted with **normal saline**, use within **4 hrs**; protects mainly against **severe childhood TB (miliary, meningitis)**; **scar** forms in 6–12 weeks.
- **Treatment (NTEP)** – **2HRZE + 4HRE (daily, FDC by weight)**; **DOTS / 99DOTS**; **MDR-TB** (resistant to H & R) – BPaLM/ shorter oral bedaquiline regimens; **XDR-TB**; **Ni-kshay portal; Ni-kshay Poshan Yojana**; **TB Mukt Bharat target** (End TB by 2025 in India – SDG 2030 globally).

### Mycobacterium leprae
- **Gerhard Armauer Hansen (1873)** – **Hansen's disease**; **acid-fast (weakly – 5 % H₂SO₄ decolouriser)**, **"cigar bundles"/ globi** in macrophages; **can't be cultured in artificial media – mouse foot pad, nine-banded armadillo**; **longest generation time (~12–14 days)**; **incubation 3–5 yrs (long)**; **prefers cooler body parts (skin, peripheral nerves, nose, testes, ear lobes)**; transmission – **nasal droplets** (main), prolonged close contact; **man – only reservoir**.
- **Classification (WHO)** – **paucibacillary (PB – 1–5 skin lesions, smear −ve)** & **multibacillary (MB – ≥ 6 lesions, nerve involvement > 1, smear +ve)**; **Ridley-Jopling – TT, BT, BB, BL, LL** (tuberculoid – good CMI; lepromatous – poor CMI, many bacilli, **leonine facies**, madarosis, saddle nose).
- **Cardinal signs** – **hypopigmented/ erythematous anaesthetic skin patch**, **thickened/ tender peripheral nerves (ulnar – commonest, common peroneal, great auricular)**, **slit-skin smear +ve**.
- **Lepromin test** – prognostic (not diagnostic) – +ve in tuberculoid.
- **Treatment – MDT (WHO 1982)**: **rifampicin (monthly supervised) + dapsone (daily) + clofazimine (monthly + daily)** – **PB 6 months, MB 12 months** (uniform 3-drug for both since 2018); **lepra reactions (type 1 – reversal; type 2 – ENL)**; **NLEP – Sparsh Leprosy Awareness Campaign (30 January – Anti-Leprosy Day)**.
- **Atypical mycobacteria (NTM)** – M. avium complex (HIV), M. marinum (fish tank granuloma), M. ulcerans (Buruli ulcer), M. fortuitum (post-surgical, injection abscess).

## 5.6 Spirochaetes

| Organism | Disease & points |
|---|---|
| **Treponema pallidum** | **Syphilis** – **sexual, transplacental (congenital – after 16 weeks), blood**; **can't be cultured**; seen by **dark-ground microscopy**; **primary – painless hard chancre (indurated ulcer, ~3 weeks)** → **secondary (rash on palms & soles, condylomata lata, mucous patches, lymphadenopathy – most infectious)** → latent → **tertiary (gumma, cardiovascular – aortitis, neurosyphilis – tabes dorsalis, Argyll Robertson pupil)**; **congenital – Hutchinson's teeth, saddle nose, snuffles, sabre tibia, interstitial keratitis, 8th nerve deafness (Hutchinson's triad)**; **tests – non-treponemal screening VDRL/ RPR (titres – monitor response; false +ve in pregnancy, SLE, leprosy, malaria)**, **treponemal confirmatory – TPHA, FTA-ABS** (remain +ve lifelong); **Rx – benzathine penicillin G 2.4 MU IM** (Jarisch-Herxheimer reaction); **antenatal VDRL screening (eliminate congenital syphilis)** |
| **Leptospira interrogans** | **Leptospirosis (Weil's disease)** – **zoonosis – rat urine** contaminated water/ soil (**floods, paddy fields, farmers, sewer workers**); enters via abraded skin/ mucosa; **fever, myalgia (calf tenderness), conjunctival suffusion**, jaundice, renal failure, pulmonary haemorrhage; **hooked ends, dark-ground**; **MAT (microscopic agglutination test – gold standard)**, IgM ELISA; **Rx – doxycycline/ penicillin**; **chemoprophylaxis – doxycycline 200 mg weekly** (high-risk exposure); common in **Gujarat (south – Surat, Valsad, Navsari)**, Kerala, Maharashtra |
| **Borrelia** | B. recurrentis – **relapsing fever (louse-borne)**; B. burgdorferi – **Lyme disease (tick – erythema migrans)** |

## 5.7 Rickettsia, Chlamydia & Mycoplasma

| Organism | Features & diseases |
|---|---|
| **Rickettsia** (obligate intracellular, vector-borne) | **Epidemic typhus (R. prowazekii – louse)**, endemic/ murine typhus (rat flea), **Indian tick typhus (R. conorii)**, **Rocky Mountain spotted fever**; **scrub typhus (Orientia tsutsugamushi – larval trombiculid mite "chigger"; eschar – painless black scab)**; **Q fever (Coxiella burnetii – no vector, inhalation; not rickettsia now)**; **Weil-Felix test (Proteus OX19, OX2, OXK – heterophile)**; **Rx – doxycycline (DOC)**; rash, fever, headache |
| **Chlamydia trachomatis** (obligate intracellular – elementary & reticulate bodies; inclusion bodies – Halberstaedter-Prowazek) | **Trachoma (serotypes A–C) – leading infectious cause of blindness** (follicles, Herbert's pits, trichiasis, entropion; **SAFE strategy – Surgery, Antibiotics (azithromycin), Facial cleanliness, Environmental improvement**); **genital (D–K) – commonest bacterial STI, non-gonococcal urethritis, cervicitis, PID, infertility, ectopic; inclusion conjunctivitis & pneumonia in newborn (5–14 days)**; **lymphogranuloma venereum (L1–L3)**; Rx – **azithromycin/ doxycycline** |
| **Chlamydophila pneumoniae / psittaci** | Atypical pneumonia; **psittacosis (parrots/ birds)** |
| **Mycoplasma pneumoniae** | **Smallest free-living organism, no cell wall (penicillin & cephalosporins ineffective)**, pleomorphic, **"fried egg" colonies**; **primary atypical pneumonia ("walking pneumonia") – young adults**; **cold agglutinins (IgM)**; Rx – **macrolides/ doxycycline**; M. hominis, Ureaplasma – genital |
