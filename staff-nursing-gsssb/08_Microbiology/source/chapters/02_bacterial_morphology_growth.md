## 2.2 Bacterial Cell Structure

| Structure | Features / importance |
|---|---|
| **Cell wall** | **Peptidoglycan (murein)** – rigidity & shape; **Gram-positive – thick peptidoglycan (teichoic acid)**; **Gram-negative – thin peptidoglycan + outer membrane with lipopolysaccharide (LPS = endotoxin – lipid A)**; **target of penicillins, cephalosporins, vancomycin**; **lysozyme** (tears, saliva) breaks it; **L-forms/ protoplasts – wall-deficient**; **Mycoplasma – no wall** |
| **Cell (cytoplasmic) membrane** | Selective permeability, respiration (no mitochondria), **mesosomes** |
| **Capsule** | Polysaccharide (polypeptide in B. anthracis) outer layer – **virulence factor – prevents phagocytosis**; **India ink/ negative staining**; **Quellung reaction (capsule swelling – pneumococcus)**; capsulated – **pneumococcus, Klebsiella, H. influenzae type b, meningococcus, B. anthracis, Cryptococcus (fungus)** |
| **Slime layer/ biofilm** | **Adherence to catheters, prosthetic valves, IV lines** (S. epidermidis, Pseudomonas) – antibiotic-resistant |
| **Flagella** | **Motility** (protein – flagellin, **H antigen**); types – **monotrichous (one – Vibrio, Pseudomonas)**, **lophotrichous (tuft at one end)**, **amphitrichous (both ends)**, **peritrichous (all over – E. coli, Salmonella, Proteus – swarming)**; **atrichous – non-motile (Shigella, Klebsiella, staph, strep)**; detected by **hanging drop method** |
| **Fimbriae/ pili** | **Adhesion (common pili – E. coli in UTI, gonococcus)**; **sex pili – conjugation (transfer of DNA/ plasmids)** |
| **Ribosomes** | **70S** – protein synthesis (aminoglycosides, tetracyclines, macrolides target) |
| **Nucleoid** | Single circular dsDNA, no nuclear membrane |
| **Plasmids** | Extrachromosomal circular DNA – **R plasmids (drug resistance)**, toxin genes; transferred by conjugation |
| **Inclusion granules** | **Volutin/ metachromatic granules (Babes-Ernst) – C. diphtheriae (Albert's stain)**; glycogen, lipid |
| **Spores (endospores)** | **Highly resistant dormant form** (heat, drying, disinfectants) – **dipicolinic acid + calcium**; **only Gram-positive bacilli – Bacillus (aerobic) & Clostridium (anaerobic)**; **survival form, not reproduction (1 cell → 1 spore)**; position – **central (B. anthracis), subterminal, terminal "drumstick" (C. tetani), oval terminal "tennis racket" (C. tertium)**; **killed by autoclave** – used as **biological indicators** of sterilisation |

## 2.3 Staining Methods

| Stain | Type / use |
|---|---|
| **Simple stain** | Methylene blue – shape & arrangement |
| **Gram stain** | **Differential** – crystal violet (primary) → **Gram's iodine (mordant)** → **acetone/ alcohol (decolouriser – critical step)** → **safranin (counterstain)**; **Gram +ve – violet/ purple; Gram −ve – pink/ red** |
| **Ziehl-Neelsen (acid-fast)** | **Carbol fuchsin (heat) → 20 % H₂SO₄ (decolouriser; 5 % for M. leprae) → methylene blue**; **acid-fast bacilli – red/ pink on blue background**; **mycolic acid** in wall; **TB, leprosy, Nocardia (weakly – 1 %), oocysts of Cryptosporidium/ Isospora** |
| **Albert's stain** | **Metachromatic granules – C. diphtheriae** (green bacilli, bluish-black granules) |
| **Negative stain (India ink, nigrosin)** | **Capsule – Cryptococcus neoformans (CSF)** |
| **Giemsa / Leishman** | **Blood smears – malaria parasites**, Leishmania (LD bodies), Chlamydia inclusions |
| **Spore stain** | Malachite green (Schaeffer-Fulton) |
| **Fontana (silver impregnation)** | Spirochaetes |
| **KOH mount (10–20 %)** | **Fungi – skin, nail, hair** (dissolves keratin) |
| **Lactophenol cotton blue** | **Fungal culture morphology** |
| **Auramine-rhodamine** | Fluorescent – TB |

- **RNTCP/ NTEP sputum grading (ZN)** – scanty (1–9 AFB/100 fields), **1+ (10–99/100 fields), 2+ (1–10/field), 3+ (> 10/field)**.

## 2.4 Bacterial Growth & Physiology

- **Reproduction – binary fission**; **generation (doubling) time** – **E. coli ~20 min**; **M. tuberculosis ~14–18 hrs** (slow – culture takes 4–8 weeks); **M. leprae ~12–14 days** (**can't be cultured in artificial media – armadillo, mouse foot pad**).

### Bacterial growth curve (in batch culture)
| Phase | Features |
|---|---|
| **1. Lag phase** | **Adaptation, no increase in number**, ↑ size & metabolic activity |
| **2. Log (exponential) phase** | **Rapid multiplication**; **most susceptible to antibiotics**; ideal for staining/ biochemical tests |
| **3. Stationary phase** | Growth = death; nutrient depletion; **spores & exotoxins formed** |
| **4. Decline (death) phase** | Death > growth; involution forms |

### Growth requirements
- **Oxygen** – **obligate aerobes (M. tuberculosis, Pseudomonas, B. subtilis)**; **obligate anaerobes (Clostridium, Bacteroides – killed by O₂; lack catalase/ SOD)**; **facultative anaerobes (most – E. coli, staph)**; **microaerophilic (Campylobacter, H. pylori)**; capnophilic (↑ CO₂ – Brucella, Neisseria).
- **Temperature** – **mesophiles (25–40 °C; most pathogens – optimum 37 °C)**; psychrophiles (cold – Listeria grows at 4 °C – refrigerated food); thermophiles (> 55 °C – Geobacillus).
- **pH** – most 7.2–7.6; **Vibrio cholerae – alkaline (pH 8.2–8.6)**; lactobacilli – acidic.
- **Moisture, nutrients, salt** (S. aureus tolerates 7–10 % NaCl – **halotolerant**; Vibrio parahaemolyticus – halophilic).
- **Anaerobic culture** – **McIntosh & Fildes' jar**, **GasPak**, **Robertson's cooked meat (RCM) medium**, thioglycollate broth.

## 2.5 Culture Media

| Type | Examples |
|---|---|
| **Simple/ basal** | **Nutrient broth, nutrient agar, peptone water** |
| **Enriched** (+ blood, serum, egg) | **Blood agar (5–10 % sheep blood – haemolysis)**, **chocolate agar (heated blood – Haemophilus, Neisseria)**, Loeffler's serum slope (diphtheria – rapid growth 6–8 hrs) |
| **Enrichment (liquid – favour pathogen, inhibit flora)** | **Selenite F broth / tetrathionate (Salmonella from stool)**, **alkaline peptone water (Vibrio)** |
| **Selective (solid)** | **Lowenstein-Jensen (LJ – TB)**, **TCBS (Vibrio cholerae – yellow colonies)**, **DCA, XLD, SS agar (Salmonella, Shigella)**, **Thayer-Martin (gonococcus)**, **potassium tellurite (diphtheria – black colonies)**, Wilson-Blair (Salmonella typhi – black metallic sheen), Sabouraud's dextrose agar (**fungi**) |
| **Differential** | **MacConkey agar – lactose fermenters (pink – E. coli, Klebsiella) vs non-fermenters (colourless/ pale – Salmonella, Shigella, Proteus)**; blood agar (haemolysis) |
| **Transport media** | **Stuart's / Amies (swabs – gonococcus)**, **Cary-Blair, VR medium (Vibrio)**, buffered glycerol saline (stool – Salmonella/ Shigella) |
| **Anaerobic** | **Robertson's cooked meat**, thioglycollate |
| **Blood culture** | **Brain-heart infusion (BHI) broth, BACTEC/ BacT/ALERT (automated)**; ratio blood : broth **1 : 5 to 1 : 10** |
| **Special** | **Agar – solidifying agent (from seaweed; melts at ~90–95 °C, sets at ~40 °C; 1.5–2 %)**; **LJ – egg-based, inspissated (85 °C), malachite green** |

- **Haemolysis on blood agar** – **α (partial, greenish – S. pneumoniae, viridans strep)**, **β (complete, clear zone – S. pyogenes, S. aureus)**, **γ (none – Enterococcus)**.
- **Culture methods** – streak plate (isolation of pure colonies), pour plate (colony count), lawn culture (**antibiotic sensitivity – Kirby-Bauer disc diffusion on Mueller-Hinton agar**), stab culture; **MIC** – minimum inhibitory concentration.

## 2.6 Bacterial Toxins

| Feature | **Exotoxin** | **Endotoxin** |
|---|---|---|
| Source | Secreted by living bacteria – **mostly Gram-positive** (also some Gram −ve – cholera, E. coli LT) | **Part of cell wall (LPS) of Gram-negative** – released on death/ lysis |
| Chemistry | **Protein** | **Lipopolysaccharide (lipid A)** |
| Heat | **Heat-labile** (mostly) | **Heat-stable** |
| Toxicity | **Highly potent**, specific action | Less potent; **fever, shock, DIC** (septic shock) |
| **Toxoid** | **Can be converted (formalin) → toxoid – vaccines (tetanus, diphtheria)** | No toxoid |
| Examples | **Tetanus (tetanospasmin – blocks inhibitory glycine/ GABA release → spasms)**, **botulinum (most potent toxin – blocks ACh release → flaccid paralysis)**, **diphtheria (inhibits protein synthesis – EF-2)**, **cholera (↑ cAMP → rice-water diarrhoea)**, staph enterotoxin, TSST-1, **S. pyogenes (erythrogenic – scarlet fever)** | E. coli, Salmonella, Meningococcus (Waterhouse-Friderichsen) |

- **Pyrogen-free (endotoxin-free)** IV fluids; **LAL (Limulus amoebocyte lysate) test** detects endotoxin.

## 2.7 Bacterial Genetics & Drug Resistance

- **Gene transfer** – **transformation** (uptake of naked DNA – Griffith's pneumococcus experiment), **transduction** (via **bacteriophage**), **conjugation** (**sex pilus – R plasmids, most common mode of spread of multidrug resistance**), transposons ("jumping genes").
- **Mutation** – spontaneous (TB drug resistance → multidrug therapy).
- **Resistance mechanisms** – **enzymatic inactivation (β-lactamase/ ESBL, carbapenemase – NDM-1 "New Delhi metallo-β-lactamase")**, altered target (**MRSA – PBP2a, mecA gene**), ↓ permeability, **efflux pumps**, bypass pathways.
- **Important resistant organisms** – **MRSA, VRE (vancomycin-resistant Enterococcus), ESBL-producing E. coli/ Klebsiella, CRE (carbapenem-resistant Enterobacterales), MDR/ XDR-TB, Acinetobacter baumannii, Pseudomonas**; **WHO priority pathogens list**.
- **Prevention of AMR** – rational antibiotic use (**antimicrobial stewardship**), complete course, **culture & sensitivity before antibiotics**, infection control, hand hygiene, no OTC antibiotics (**Schedule H1, Red Line campaign**), **NAP-AMR (India, 2017)**; **World AMR Awareness Week – 18–24 November**.
