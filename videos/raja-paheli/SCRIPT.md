# Raja ki paheli (Part 1) – sher ka pinjra ya andha kuan

## Source / originality
- Classic liar's-choice logic riddle (public-domain folk paradox, told in many versions for centuries).
- Inspired only by the *format* of a reel the creator shared (riddle + "answer in Part 2"). Our own setting
  (lion cage + blind well instead of the reference's punishments), our own lines, our own 3D court and characters.
  No footage, art, characters, lines, channel name or handle from the reference.

## Answer (for Part 2)
Prisoner says: **"Mujhe andhe kuyein mein daala jaayega."**
- If that is TRUE → rule says truth = lion cage → but then he isn't put in the well → the line becomes false. Contradiction.
- If that is FALSE → rule says lie = well → but then he IS put in the well → the line becomes true. Contradiction.
- The king can't apply either punishment without breaking his own word, so he has to let him go.
