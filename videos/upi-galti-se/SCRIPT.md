# Galti se paise bhej diye? Ruko. (UPI fake-credit SMS scam)

## Facts + sources
- The scam: a fake "credited" SMS arrives, then a caller says the money went to you by mistake and asks you to send it back. No money ever arrived. Advice: don't send to the number or UPI ID the caller gives, and check your own account. PhishGuard: https://www.phishguard.co.in/blog/money-received-message-real-or-fake-read-the-sms
- Check incoming payments in your bank account or official UPI app, not in SMS, screenshots or payer notifications. PhishGuard (citing NPCI guidance) and PhonePe Business: https://business.phonepe.com/articles/fake-upi-payment-scams-how-to-identify-and-prevent-fraud
- SMS sender IDs are labels, not verified identities, so a bank-like name proves nothing. PhishGuard (same article).
- A wrong UPI transfer is reversed through the sender's bank, UPI app or NPCI dispute process (npci.org.in). The bank can contact the receiver. PhonePe: https://www.phonepe.com/blog/trust-and-safety/how-to-reverse-upi-payments-when-money-is-wrongly-transferred-or-is-in-pending-status/ · Paytm: https://paytm.com/blog/money-transfer/upi-money-transfer/recover-money-transferred-to-a-wrong-upi-address/
- Police advise against forwarding money that was "sent by mistake" (it can be crime money) and to report it to the bank instead. Gulf News / Dubai Police: https://gulfnews.com/uae/crime/dubai-police-urges-public-to-stay-alert-as-wrong-transfer-could-be-a-scam-1.500188777
- 1930 is India's National Cyber Crime Helpline (cybercrime.gov.in).
- No city or case count is claimed, because no specific Indian case could be verified. The proof beat is the SMS itself.
