# Phone 100% charge mat karo, battery ko 80 pasand hai

## Facts + sources
- Li-ion wear rises with charge voltage, and with time held at full charge plus heat. A partial range of about 40-80 % gives the longest life (Battery University, as quoted): https://forums.electricbikereview.com/threads/charging-to-80-without-a-satiator.24763/page-6 · https://chargie.org/why-charging-to-80-percent-extends-battery-life-science/
- iPhone: Settings > Battery > Battery Health & Charging. Optimized Battery Charging holds at 80 % and finishes later. iPhone 15 and newer also have an 80 % Limit: https://www.macrumors.com/2023/09/19/iphone-15-80-percent-battery-limit-option/
- Samsung Galaxy: Protect battery caps the charge at 85 % (newer One UI: 80 % or a chosen level): https://www.samsung.com/in/support/mobile-devices/battery-protection-feature-in-samsung-galaxy-phones · https://www.samsung.com/us/support/troubleshoot/TSG10013504/
- Not claimed: no "2x/3x life" multiplier (unverified), and no exact battery-health drop per year.
