## Facts + sources
- Mehrangarh (Jodhpur) has seven gates (pols); Loha Pol is the iron gate, and iron spikes were set in the gate wood against
  elephant attacks. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mehrangarh · https://atlasobscura.com/places/mehrangarh-fort
- Indian forts often had rows of sharp iron spikes on gates to stop war elephants ramming them. https://wiki.krisyotam.com/content/wikipedia_en_all_nopic_2026-03/Forts_in_India
- The approach through the gates is steep with twists and turns to hinder enemy elephants. https://thrillingtravel.in/mehrangarh-fort-jodhpur-part-3.html
- Hill forts and loopholes in walls for defenders are general fort features (no specific number claimed).
- Not claimed: exact spike heights, machicolations, speeds (the km/h-free race shot is illustrative).
