# घड़ी कैसे काम करती है? — script & facts

## Facts used (checked)
- Mechanical watches store energy in a coiled **mainspring** inside the barrel; winding the crown turns the ratchet wheel and tightens it.
- The **gear train** carries that energy: centre wheel = 1 turn/hour (minute hand), fourth wheel = 1 turn/minute (small seconds).
  The film's train uses real ratios: centre 60 → third pinion 8 (7.5×), third 64 → fourth pinion 8 (8×) → 60:1.
- The **balance wheel + hairspring** oscillate at a fixed rate (here 2.5 Hz = 18,000 beats/hour, a classic hand-wound rate).
  The **escapement** (escape wheel + pallet fork) releases the train one tooth per beat → the tick. Escape → fourth = 10:1, so the
  seconds hand really turns once a minute at 5 beats/s.
- **Motion works** under the dial give hour hand = minute hand ÷ 12.
- A **quartz** watch: battery → circuit (IC) → the quartz tuning-fork crystal vibrates at **32,768 Hz** (2^15); the IC divides by
  2^15 to get 1 pulse per second → stepper-motor coil → rotor turns 180° → second hand jumps one step each second.
