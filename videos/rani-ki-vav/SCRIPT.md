## Facts + sources
- Stepwell in Patan, Gujarat, on the Saraswati; UNESCO World Heritage since 22 June 2014. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rani_ki_Vav · https://artsandculture.google.com/entity/rani-ki-vav/m047cnbf
- Commissioned by Queen Udayamati in memory of Chaulukya (Solanki) king Bhima I (Bhimdev I), 11th century. Same sources.
- Inverted-temple design, seven levels of stairs with carved panels; more than 500 principal sculptures and 1000+ minor ones. Same sources.
- Silted over by Saraswati floods; rediscovered in the 1940s, restored by the Archaeological Survey of India in the 1980s. Same sources.
- Shown on the back of the ₹100 banknote (2018 series). (Widely reported; RBI note design.) We don't draw the note.
- Not claimed: exact depth/length, exact start year (sources differ: c. 1063).
