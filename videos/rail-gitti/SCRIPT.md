# Railway track pe pathar kyun? (track ballast)

## Facts + sources
- The crushed stone (ballast) under the sleepers spreads the train's load over a wide area of the ground below. https://the-contact-patch.com/book/rail/r1602-the-roadbed · https://civilengineeringnotes.com/ballast-railway-types-functions
- It drains rainwater quickly so water doesn't pool under the track. Same sources + https://www.mawsons.com.au/quarry-products/railway-ballast
- It holds the sleepers in place against sideways and lengthwise movement. https://civilengineeringnotes.com/ballast-railway-types-functions
- It keeps vegetation down. https://kureansiklopedi.com/detay/ballast-28641
- Angular stones interlock and resist movement better than rounded ones. https://taylorandfrancis.com/knowledge/Engineering_and_technology/Mechanical_engineering/Ballast
- Not claimed: train weights or speeds.
