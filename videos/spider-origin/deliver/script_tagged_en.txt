THE BITE: The Origin of Spider-Man (a mockumentary, ~5 min). ElevenLabs Eleven v3, tags in [ ] are not spoken.
On-screen disclaimer (not spoken): A fictional mockumentary based on Marvel's 1962 comic. Spider-Man © Marvel.

[whispers] New York. Nineteen sixty-two.
[curious] A shy teenager walks into a science exhibition... and walks out with a secret no one would believe.
[tense] Within weeks, a masked figure is seen climbing the walls of Manhattan... with his bare hands.
[serious] Police reports would call it impossible. Newspapers would call it a hoax.
[curious] But what really happened that evening? And how did one small bite change a city forever?

[serious] Chapter one. The quiet boy from Queens.
[calm] His name was Peter Parker. He lived in Forest Hills, Queens, with his aunt May and his uncle Ben.
[warm] His parents were gone. His aunt and uncle raised him like their own son.
[calm] At school, Peter was brilliant at science... and invisible everywhere else.
[sad] The other students laughed at him. They called him a bookworm. Nobody invited him anywhere.
[curious] Nobody, except science.

[serious] Chapter two. The exhibition.
[calm] One evening, Peter went alone to a public demonstration of radioactivity.
[tense] Scientists aimed a beam of radiation between two machines, to show the audience how it worked.
[whispers] No one noticed a small spider, drifting down on a single thread... straight into the beam.
[tense] The spider absorbed a massive dose of radiation. In its final moments, it fell onto Peter's hand.
[dramatic] And it bit him.
[calm] He felt a burning pain. Then a strange, dizzy rush of energy. He walked out into the night, confused.

[serious] Chapter three. The change.
[tense] On the street, a car came straight at him. Peter jumped... and cleared it, landing on a wall above the sidewalk.
[awed] His fingers stuck to the brick. He was climbing, like a spider.
[excited] He crushed a steel pipe in one hand, as easily as paper.
[curious] Somehow, the spider's abilities had passed into him. Its strength. Its balance. Its grip.
[impressed] But Peter added something the spider never had. A scientist's mind.
[calm] At home, he mixed a sticky fluid of his own and built two small devices for his wrists. Web shooters.
[excited] Now he could swing between buildings.

[serious] Chapter four. The masked star.
[curious] Peter did not think about heroes. He thought about money for his family.
[calm] Hiding his face behind a mask, he beat a professional wrestler, and an agent put him on television.
[proud] Overnight, the masked teenager became a sensation.
[tense] Then, one night at the studio, a thief ran past him, chased by a guard.
[cold] Peter could have stopped him with one move. He just stepped aside.
[serious] It wasn't his problem, he said.

[serious] Chapter five. The night that changed everything.
[sad] Days later, Peter came home to police cars outside his house.
[sad] A burglar had broken in. Uncle Ben was dead.
[angry] Peter put on his costume and swung after the killer, across the rooftops, to an abandoned warehouse.
[tense] He cornered the man in the dark... and saw his face.
[whispers] It was the same thief. The one he had let go.

[calm] That night, Peter Parker understood something he would never forget.
[serious] In the words that would follow him for the rest of his life...
[slow] With great power, there must also come great responsibility.
[proud] The world would come to know him by another name. Spider-Man.

[curious] If one small choice could change your whole life... what would you have done that night at the studio? Tell us in the comments.
