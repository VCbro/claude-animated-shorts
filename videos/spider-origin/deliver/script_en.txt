New York. Nineteen sixty-two.
A shy teenager walks into a science exhibition... and walks out with a secret no one would believe.
Within weeks, a masked figure is seen climbing the walls of Manhattan... with his bare hands.
Police reports would call it impossible. Newspapers would call it a hoax.
But what really happened that evening? And how did one small bite change a city forever?

Chapter one. The quiet boy from Queens.
His name was Peter Parker. He lived in Forest Hills, Queens, with his aunt May and his uncle Ben.
His parents were gone. His aunt and uncle raised him like their own son.
At school, Peter was brilliant at science... and invisible everywhere else.
The other students laughed at him. They called him a bookworm. Nobody invited him anywhere.
Nobody, except science.

Chapter two. The exhibition.
One evening, Peter went alone to a public demonstration of radioactivity.
Scientists aimed a beam of radiation between two machines, to show the audience how it worked.
No one noticed a small spider, drifting down on a single thread... straight into the beam.
The spider absorbed a massive dose of radiation. In its final moments, it fell onto Peter's hand.
And it bit him.
He felt a burning pain. Then a strange, dizzy rush of energy. He walked out into the night, confused.

Chapter three. The change.
On the street, a car came straight at him. Peter jumped... and cleared it, landing on a wall above the sidewalk.
His fingers stuck to the brick. He was climbing, like a spider.
He crushed a steel pipe in one hand, as easily as paper.
Somehow, the spider's abilities had passed into him. Its strength. Its balance. Its grip.
But Peter added something the spider never had. A scientist's mind.
At home, he mixed a sticky fluid of his own and built two small devices for his wrists. Web shooters.
Now he could swing between buildings.

Chapter four. The masked star.
Peter did not think about heroes. He thought about money for his family.
Hiding his face behind a mask, he beat a professional wrestler, and an agent put him on television.
Overnight, the masked teenager became a sensation.
Then, one night at the studio, a thief ran past him, chased by a guard.
Peter could have stopped him with one move. He just stepped aside.
It wasn't his problem, he said.

Chapter five. The night that changed everything.
Days later, Peter came home to police cars outside his house.
A burglar had broken in. Uncle Ben was dead.
Peter put on his costume and swung after the killer, across the rooftops, to an abandoned warehouse.
He cornered the man in the dark... and saw his face.
It was the same thief. The one he had let go.

That night, Peter Parker understood something he would never forget.
In the words that would follow him for the rest of his life...
With great power, there must also come great responsibility.
The world would come to know him by another name. Spider-Man.
