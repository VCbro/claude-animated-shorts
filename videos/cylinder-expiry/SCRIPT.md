# Tumhara cylinder expire to nahi?

About 30 s. Voice text in `VOICE.txt`. Built to the skill's "Reach rules v2": stake in frame 1, bright open, one action at the end.

## Facts and sources
- The letter + two digits painted on the inner side of one collar stay is the cylinder's **test due date**, not an
  expiry of the gas: A = Jan-Mar, B = Apr-Jun, C = Jul-Sep, D = Oct-Dec, digits = year. Indian Oil has said LPG
  cylinders have no expiry date; the code marks when the cylinder must next be tested.
  BOOM fact-check quoting Indian Oil: https://www.boomlive.in/world/do-gas-cylinders-explode-after-passing-their-expiry-dates-not-really-9307
  Indian Oil note: https://iocl.com/NewsDetails/499
  TotalEnergies explainer: https://totalenergies.in/blogs/how-check-your-lpg-cylinder-due-date
- "Don't accept a cylinder past its date, ask for another" is common consumer advice in news explainers; sources differ
  on the exact procedure, so the script only says to ask the delivery person for another one.
