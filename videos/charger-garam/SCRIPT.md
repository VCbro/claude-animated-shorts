# Charger garam kyun hota hai?

Hindi voice-over (the creator's ElevenLabs take), about 44 seconds. The voice text is in `VOICE.txt`.

## Sources
- Mental Floss, "Why Do Phone and Computer Chargers Get So Hot?": conversion from wall AC to low-voltage DC is not perfectly efficient, the lost energy becomes heat. https://www.mentalfloss.com/posts/why-do-chargers-get-hot
- Reolink, "Why is my charger hot": warm is normal, too hot to touch is a warning; fast charging adds heat. https://reolink.com/blog/why-is-my-charger-hot/
- Electrical Safety First (UK), counterfeit charger testing: fakes contain few of the components needed for safety. https://electricalsafetyfirst.org.uk/guidance/product-safety/iphone-chargers
- Which? (UK), unsafe chargers sold online. https://www.which.co.uk/policy-and-insight/article/phone-chargers-sold-on-amazon-haul-bq-and-ebay-can-electrocute-and-explode-which-warns-aa7Rq0y8duOB

The inside layout (fuse, bridge rectifier, bulk capacitor, controller + MOSFET, flyback transformer, output diode and
capacitors, optocoupler) is the standard flyback switch-mode charger design; it was modelled from general knowledge,
not from one specific product.
