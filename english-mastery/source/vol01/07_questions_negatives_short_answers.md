:::goals Learning Goals · सीखने के लक्ष्य – इस chapter के बाद आप
- make **Yes/No questions** by moving the helping verb before the subject;
  <span class="hl">सहायक क्रिया को कर्ता से पहले लाकर हाँ/ना वाले सवाल बनाएँगे</span>
- make **Wh-questions** in the correct order (**Q-A-S-V**);
  <span class="hl">What, Where, Why जैसे सवाल सही क्रम (Q-A-S-V) में बनाएँगे</span>
- use **do / does / did** correctly in questions and negatives;
  <span class="hl">सवाल और नकारात्मक वाक्य में do/does/did सही लगाएँगे</span>
- form **negatives** with *not*, *never* and *no*, and use contractions;
  <span class="hl">*not, never, no* से नकारात्मक वाक्य और छोटे रूप (don't) बनाएँगे</span>
- give natural **short answers** (*Yes, I do. / No, she isn't.*).
  <span class="hl">स्वाभाविक छोटे उत्तर देंगे – *Yes, I do. / No, she isn't.*</span>
:::

:::warm Warm-up · शुरुआत – गलती पकड़िए
*"You are coming tomorrow?"* · *"Why you didn't call me?"* · *"She don't like tea."*
These sound normal in casual Indian speech, but in standard English they are wrong. Correct forms: ***Are you** coming tomorrow?* · *Why **didn't you** call me?* · *She **doesn't** like tea.* This chapter shows you why.
@hi "तुम कल आ रहे हो?", "तुमने फ़ोन क्यों नहीं किया?", "उसे चाय पसंद नहीं" – इनका सीधा अनुवाद बोलचाल में सुनाई देता है, पर standard English में सही रूप हैं: *Are you coming…? · Why didn't you…? · She doesn't like…*
:::

## 7.1 Helping verbs · सहायक क्रियाएँ – सवाल और नकार की चाबी

:::rule RULE – find the helping (auxiliary) verb first · पहले सहायक क्रिया ढूँढिए
| Helping verb family | Forms |
|---|---|
| **be** | am, is, are, was, were |
| **have** (perfect tenses) | have, has, had |
| **do** | do, does, did |
| **modals** | can, could, will, would, shall, should, may, might, must |
**Questions:** the helping verb moves **before** the subject. **Negatives:** *not* goes **after** the helping verb.
If there is **no helping verb** (present simple / past simple), bring in **do / does / did**.
@hi सहायक क्रियाएँ: **be** (am, is, are, was, were), **have** (have, has, had), **do** (do, does, did) और **modals** (can, will, should…)। **सवाल** में सहायक क्रिया कर्ता से **पहले** जाती है; **नकार** में *not* सहायक क्रिया के **बाद**। सहायक क्रिया न हो तो **do / does / did** लगाइए।
:::

## 7.2 Yes/No questions · हाँ/ना वाले सवाल

:::rule RULE – Yes/No question = Helping verb + Subject + Main verb …? · सहायक क्रिया + कर्ता + मुख्य क्रिया
| Statement | Question |
|---|---|
| She **is** a teacher. | **Is** she a teacher? |
| They **were** at home. | **Were** they at home? |
| He **can** drive. | **Can** he drive? |
| You **have** finished. | **Have** you finished? |
| You **like** coffee. (no helper) | **Do** you **like** coffee? |
| She **likes** coffee. | **Does** she **like** coffee? *(likes → like)* |
| They **went** home. | **Did** they **go** home? *(went → go)* |

**Golden rule:** after **do / does / did**, the main verb is always in the **base form** (no *-s*, no *-ed*).
:::

:::hindi Hindi Help – आसान भाषा में
हिंदी में सिर्फ़ "क्या" लगाकर या आवाज़ ऊपर करके प्रश्न बन जाता है: "तुम कल आ रहे हो?" English में **helping verb को subject से पहले** लाना ज़रूरी है: "**Are you** coming tomorrow?" अगर helping verb न हो तो **do/does/did** लगाइए, और main verb की **पहली form** (base form) रखिए।
:::

## 7.3 Wh-questions · क्या/कहाँ/क्यों वाले सवाल

:::rule RULE – the Q-A-S-V order · सवाल का क्रम
**Q**uestion word + **A**uxiliary (helping verb) + **S**ubject + main **V**erb + rest?  (remember **Q-A-S-V**)
| Q-word | Auxiliary | Subject | Main verb | Rest |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Where** | **do** | you | live | ? |
| **What** | **is** | she | cooking | ? |
| **Why** | **did** | they | leave | early? |
| **When** | **will** | the results | come out | ? |
| **How** | **can** | I | help | you? |

@hi क्रम: **प्रश्नवाचक शब्द + सहायक क्रिया + कर्ता + मुख्य क्रिया + बाक़ी?** *Where do you live?* = तुम कहाँ रहते हो? · *Why did they leave early?* = वे जल्दी क्यों चले गए?
:::

:::example EXAMPLES – the question words · प्रश्नवाचक शब्द
| Word | Asks about | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **What** | thing / action | *What are you reading?* |
| **Which** | choice from a group | *Which bus goes to the airport?* |
| **Who** | person (subject) | *Who called you?* |
| **Whom** | person (object, formal) | *Whom did you meet?* (informal: *Who did you meet?*) |
| **Whose** | owner | *Whose phone is this?* |
| **Where** | place | *Where is the ATM?* |
| **When** | time | *When does the train leave?* |
| **Why** | reason | *Why are you late?* |
| **How** | manner / condition | *How do you go to work?* |
| **How much / How many** | quantity (uncountable / countable) | *How much milk? · How many eggs?* |
| **How long / How often / How far** | duration / frequency / distance | *How often do you exercise?* |

@hi What = क्या · Which = कौन-सा (चुनाव) · Who = कौन (कर्ता) · Whom = किसको (कर्म, औपचारिक) · Whose = किसका · Where = कहाँ · When = कब · Why = क्यों · How = कैसे · How much = कितना (न गिनी जाने वाली चीज़) · How many = कितने (गिनी जाने वाली) · How long = कितनी देर · How often = कितनी बार · How far = कितनी दूर
:::

:::rule RULE – subject questions (no do/does/did) · कर्ता के बारे में सवाल
When **Who** or **What** is the **subject** of the question, use **statement order** and **no do/does/did**.
| Object question (who receives?) | Subject question (who does?) |
|---|---|
| *Who **did** Ravi **call**?* → Ravi called **his mother**. | *Who **called** Ravi?* → **His mother** called Ravi. |
| *What **did** you **break**?* | *What **broke** the window?* → **A ball** broke it. |
✗ *Who did call you?* → ✓ *Who called you?*
@hi जब *Who/What* ख़ुद **कर्ता** हो (काम किसने किया?), तो कथन वाला क्रम रखिए और **do/does/did मत लगाइए**: *Who called Ravi?* = रवि को किसने फ़ोन किया? पर *Who did Ravi call?* = रवि ने किसको फ़ोन किया? (यहाँ who कर्म है)।
:::

## 7.4 Negatives · नकारात्मक वाक्य

:::rule RULE – making negatives · नकार कैसे बनाएँ
| Helper | Negative | Contraction |
|---|---|---|
| am | am not | I'm not *(no "amn't")* |
| is / are | is not / are not | isn't / aren't |
| was / were | was not / were not | wasn't / weren't |
| have / has / had | have not … | haven't / hasn't / hadn't |
| can / will / should | cannot / will not / should not | can't / won't / shouldn't |
| (no helper) present | **do not / does not** + base verb | don't / doesn't |
| (no helper) past | **did not** + base verb | didn't |
*He **doesn't** like spicy food.* (not *doesn't likes*) · *We **didn't** see him.* (not *didn't saw*)
@hi सहायक क्रिया के बाद **not** (*is not / isn't*)। सहायक क्रिया न हो तो वर्तमान में **don't / doesn't**, भूतकाल में **didn't** + **मूल क्रिया**: *He doesn't like* (*likes* नहीं), *We didn't see* (*saw* नहीं)। *I am not* का छोटा रूप *I'm not* (कभी "amn't" नहीं)।
:::

:::note NOTE – one negative is enough · एक ही नकार काफ़ी है
Standard English uses **only one negative word** in a clause.
✗ *I don't know nothing.* → ✓ *I don't know **anything**.* / *I know **nothing**.*
✗ *She never doesn't come late.* → ✓ *She **never** comes late.*
✗ *Nobody didn't help me.* → ✓ ***Nobody** helped me.*
@hi हिंदी में "मुझे **कुछ नहीं** पता" में दो नकार जैसे शब्द चलते हैं, पर standard English में एक clause में **एक ही** नकारात्मक शब्द: *I don't know anything* या *I know nothing* – *I don't know nothing* गलत।
:::

## 7.5 Short answers · छोटे उत्तर

:::rule RULE – Yes/No + subject pronoun + helping verb · हाँ/ना + सर्वनाम + सहायक क्रिया
| Question | Yes | No |
|---|---|---|
| *Are you ready?* | Yes, I **am**. | No, I'm **not**. |
| *Is she your sister?* | Yes, she **is**. | No, she **isn't**. |
| *Do you live here?* | Yes, I **do**. | No, I **don't**. |
| *Does he work here?* | Yes, he **does**. | No, he **doesn't**. |
| *Did they win?* | Yes, they **did**. | No, they **didn't**. |
| *Can you swim?* | Yes, I **can**. | No, I **can't**. |
| *Have you eaten?* | Yes, I **have**. | No, I **haven't**. |
Positive short answers are **not contracted**: ✗ *Yes, I'm.* → ✓ *Yes, I am.*
@hi छोटा उत्तर = *Yes/No* + सर्वनाम + **सवाल वाली सहायक क्रिया**: *Do you…?* → *Yes, I do.* · *Is she…?* → *No, she isn't.* हाँ वाले उत्तर में छोटा रूप नहीं: *Yes, I am* (कभी *Yes, I'm* नहीं)।
:::

:::mistake COMMON MISTAKES – questions and negatives · आम गलतियाँ
| ✗ Wrong | ✓ Right | Why · क्यों |
|---|---|---|
| You are coming tomorrow? | **Are you** coming tomorrow? | helper before subject (in writing) · सहायक क्रिया कर्ता से पहले |
| Why you are late? | Why **are you** late? | Q-A-S-V order · सवाल का क्रम |
| Where she works? | Where **does she work**? | no helper → add *does*; base verb · does लगाओ, मूल क्रिया |
| Did you went to school? | Did you **go** to school? | *did* + base verb · did के बाद मूल क्रिया |
| He don't eat eggs. | He **doesn't** eat eggs. | he/she/it → *does* · he/she/it के साथ does |
| She doesn't likes tea. | She doesn't **like** tea. | *does* already shows the *-s* · -s does में आ चुका |
| Who did break the glass? | Who **broke** the glass? | subject question – no *did* · कर्ता वाला सवाल, did नहीं |
| What you want? | What **do you want**? | add the helper *do* · सहायक do जोड़ो |
:::

:::compare COMPARE & UNDERSTAND – Indian, British and American habits · भारतीय, ब्रिटिश और अमेरिकी आदतें
| Situation | Common Indian English | Standard (British & American) |
|---|---|---|
| Checking information | *You are from Pune, **no**?* / *…, **isn't it**?* | *You're from Pune, **aren't you**?* (question tags – Ch 30) |
| Asking someone's name | *What is your **good name**?* | *What's your name? / May I have your name?* |
| Short "yes" | *Yes, I'm.* | *Yes, I am.* |
| Have you got? | *Do you have a pen?* (fine) | BrE also: *Have you got a pen?* · AmE: *Do you have a pen?* |
Rising intonation without inversion (*You're coming?*) is fine in **casual speech** in all varieties – but in **exams and formal writing**, use full question order.
@hi भारत में आम *"…, no?"* या *"…, isn't it?"* की जगह सही question tag लगाइए (*You're from Pune, aren't you?*)। *"What is your good name?"* की जगह *"May I have your name?"*। बोलचाल में आवाज़ ऊपर करके *You're coming?* चलता है, पर परीक्षा और लेखन में पूरा सवाल वाला क्रम।
:::

:::tip EXAM TIP · परीक्षा टिप
In error-spotting, check three things in every question/negative: (1) Is there a helping verb **before** the subject? (2) After **do/does/did**, is the main verb in **base form**? (3) Is there **only one** negative word?
@hi गलती ढूँढते समय तीन चीज़ें जाँचिए: (1) सहायक क्रिया कर्ता से **पहले** है? (2) do/does/did के बाद **मूल क्रिया** है? (3) नकारात्मक शब्द **एक ही** है?
:::

:::revision QUICK REVISION · जल्दी दोहराइए
- Yes/No question: **Helper + Subject + Verb?** No helper → **Do/Does/Did + base verb**.
- Wh-question: **Q-A-S-V** – Question word + Auxiliary + Subject + Main verb.
- Subject question (*Who called?*) → statement order, **no did**.
- Negative: helper + **not**; no helper → **don't / doesn't / didn't + base verb**. Only **one** negative per clause.
- Short answer: **Yes, I am. / No, she doesn't.** – never *Yes, I'm.*
@hi हाँ/ना सवाल: सहायक + कर्ता + क्रिया? · Wh-सवाल: Q-A-S-V · कर्ता वाला सवाल: did नहीं · नकार: don't/doesn't/didn't + मूल क्रिया, एक ही नकार · छोटा उत्तर: *Yes, I am*।
:::
