## 3.1 Capital letters · बड़े अक्षर

:::rule RULE – Use a capital letter for … · capital letter कहाँ लगाएँ
| # | Use | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The **first word of a sentence** | **T**he bus is late. **W**here are you? |
| 2 | The pronoun **I** (always) | Yesterday **I** said that **I** was busy. |
| 3 | **Names of people** and pets | **R**ahul **S**harma, **D**r **M**ehta, our dog **T**ommy |
| 4 | **Places**: cities, states, countries, rivers, mountains, roads | **A**hmedabad, **K**erala, **I**ndia, the **G**anga, **M**ount **A**bu, **M**.**G**. **R**oad |
| 5 | **Days, months, festivals** | **M**onday, **J**anuary, **D**iwali, **E**id, **C**hristmas |
| 6 | **Languages and nationalities** | **E**nglish, **H**indi, **G**ujarati, **I**ndian, **J**apanese food |
| 7 | **Titles** before names | **M**r **S**ingh, **M**rs **K**apoor, **M**s **R**ao, **P**rofessor **J**oshi, **P**rime **M**inister **M**odi |
| 8 | **Organisations, companies, brands** | **S**tate **B**ank of **I**ndia, **I**ndian **R**ailways, **T**ata **M**otors |
| 9 | **Main words in titles** of books, films, headings | *The **J**ungle **B**ook*, *A **R**eport on **W**ater **S**aving* |
| 10 | **Short forms (abbreviations/acronyms)** | **ATM, PIN, SMS, UPI, PM, ID** |

@hi Capital letter लगाइए: (1) वाक्य का पहला शब्द, (2) **I** (मैं) हमेशा, (3) लोगों/पालतू जानवरों के नाम, (4) जगहें – शहर, राज्य, देश, नदी, सड़क, (5) दिन, महीने, त्योहार, (6) भाषाएँ और राष्ट्रीयता, (7) नाम से पहले पद (Mr, Dr), (8) संस्थाएँ/कंपनियाँ, (9) किताब/फ़िल्म के शीर्षक के मुख्य शब्द, (10) संक्षिप्त रूप (ATM, UPI)।
:::

:::rule RULE – Do NOT use a capital letter for … · यहाँ capital नहीं
- **Seasons**: summer, winter, monsoon (not *Summer*), unless at the start of a sentence.
- **Common nouns** in the middle of a sentence: my **m**other, the **d**octor, a **c**ity, the **p**rincipal (but **P**rincipal **S**harma as a title before a name).
- **Directions** used as directions: go **n**orth, the **e**ast side (but **N**orth **I**ndia, the **M**iddle **E**ast as names of regions).
- **Subjects** (except languages): I like **m**aths and **s**cience, but I also like **E**nglish.
:::

:::hindi Hindi Help – आसान भाषा में
मौसम (summer), साधारण संज्ञा (mother, doctor), दिशा (go north) और विषय (maths, science – पर English भाषा होने से capital) में capital नहीं लगता।

**Capital letter** हर sentence की शुरुआत में, **I** (मैं) में, और हर **नाम** (व्यक्ति, जगह, दिन, महीना, त्योहार, भाषा, कंपनी) में लगता है। **मौसम** (summer, winter) और साधारण शब्द (mother, teacher, city) के लिए sentence के बीच में small letter लिखें।
:::

## 3.2 End marks: . ? ! · वाक्य के अंत के चिह्न

:::rule RULE – Ending a sentence · वाक्य कैसे ख़त्म करें
| Mark | Name | Use | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **.** | **full stop** (British/Indian) / **period** (American) | statements and polite commands | I live in Surat. Please sit down. |
| **?** | **question mark** | direct questions | What is your name? Can you help me? |
| **!** | **exclamation mark** | strong feeling, surprise, a shout | What a beautiful day! Stop! Wow! |

@hi **.** (full stop / पूर्ण विराम) – सामान्य वाक्य और विनम्र आदेश। **?** (प्रश्नचिह्न) – सीधे सवाल। **!** (विस्मयादिबोधक) – तेज़ भावना, हैरानी या चिल्लाना।
:::

:::mistake COMMON MISTAKES – end marks · अंत के चिह्नों की गलतियाँ
| ✗ Wrong | ✓ Right | Why · क्यों |
|---|---|---|
| Where is the station. | Where is the station**?** | A direct question needs **?** · सीधे सवाल में ? |
| I asked him where the station was? | I asked him where the station was**.** | This is an **indirect** question inside a statement → full stop · अप्रत्यक्ष सवाल = full stop |
| It's so nice!!!!! | It's so nice**!** | Use only **one** exclamation mark in formal writing · औपचारिक लेखन में एक ही ! |
| Please send the report? | Please send the report**.** | A polite request in statement form ends with a full stop · कथन वाला अनुरोध = full stop |
:::

## 3.3 The comma ( , ) · अल्पविराम

:::rule RULE – Six everyday uses of the comma · comma के 6 रोज़ के उपयोग
1. **Lists** (3 or more items): *I bought milk**,** bread**,** eggs and butter.*
2. **After an opening word or phrase**: *Yes**,** I can come. / However**,** the shop was closed. / In the morning**,** we went to the temple.*
3. **Calling someone (vocative)**: *Rahul**,** please close the door. / Thank you**,** sir.*
4. **Before a quotation**: *She said**,** "I'm ready."*
5. **Joining two full sentences with *and, but, so, or*** (when they are long): *I called the office**,** but nobody answered.*
6. **Dates, numbers and addresses**: *15 August**,** 1947 / Monday**,** 3 March / 12**,**50**,**000 (Indian) / 1**,**250**,**000 (international) / Navrangpura**,** Ahmedabad*
@hi Comma लगाइए: (1) तीन या ज़्यादा चीज़ों की सूची में, (2) शुरुआती शब्द के बाद (*Yes, However, In the morning*), (3) किसी को पुकारते समय (*Rahul, …*), (4) उद्धरण से पहले, (5) *and/but/so/or* से जुड़े दो लंबे वाक्यों में, (6) तारीख़, संख्या और पते में।
:::

:::compare COMPARE & UNDERSTAND – a comma can change the meaning · एक comma से अर्थ बदलता है
| Sentence | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *Let's eat**,** Grandma!* | We are calling Grandma to come and eat. |
| *Let's eat Grandma!* | (Funny/wrong) We are going to eat Grandma! |
| *My brother**,** who lives in Pune**,** is a doctor.* | I have **one** brother; extra information about him. |
| *My brother who lives in Pune is a doctor.* | I have **more than one** brother; this one lives in Pune. |

@hi *Let's eat, Grandma!* = दादी, चलो खाना खाएँ! पर comma हटाओ तो *Let's eat Grandma!* = चलो दादी को खा जाएँ! 😄 इसी तरह commas के साथ *My brother, who lives in Pune,* = मेरा एक ही भाई है; बिना comma = कई भाइयों में से वह जो पुणे में रहता है।
:::

:::note NOTE – Oxford (serial) comma and number style · Oxford comma और संख्या लिखने का तरीक़ा
- **Oxford comma** = a comma before *and* in a list: *red, white**,** and blue*. It is common in **American** and academic writing; Indian/British writing often leaves it out (*red, white and blue*). **Both are correct** – be consistent, and use it when it prevents confusion.
- **Numbers:** Indian style groups as **lakh/crore** (*12,50,000*); international style groups in thousands (*1,250,000*). Use the style your exam, office or client expects.
@hi सूची में *and* से पहले वाला comma (Oxford comma) अमेरिकी लेखन में आम है, भारतीय/ब्रिटिश में अक्सर नहीं – दोनों सही। संख्या: भारतीय तरीक़ा लाख/करोड़ (12,50,000), अंतरराष्ट्रीय तरीक़ा हज़ार में (1,250,000)।
:::

## 3.4 The apostrophe ( ' ) · ऊपर का अल्पविराम

:::rule RULE – Two jobs of the apostrophe · apostrophe के दो काम
**1. Short forms (contractions)** – the apostrophe shows **missing letters**:
| Full | Short | Full | Short |
|---|---|---|---|
| I am | I**'**m | do not | don**'**t |
| you are | you**'**re | does not | doesn**'**t |
| he is / he has | he**'**s | cannot | can**'**t |
| it is / it has | it**'**s | will not | won**'**t |
| we have | we**'**ve | I would / I had | I**'**d |

**2. Possession (belonging)** – *Riya's bag* = the bag of Riya:
- Singular noun → **'s**: *the teacher**'s** desk, my friend**'s** phone*
- Plural noun ending in -s → **'** only: *the students**'** books, my parents**'** house*
- Plural not ending in -s → **'s**: *children**'s** park, women**'s** day*
(Full detail in Chapter 11.)
@hi **काम 1 – छोटा रूप:** apostrophe वहाँ लगता है जहाँ अक्षर हटाए गए हैं (do n**o**t → don't)। **काम 2 – "का/की/के":** एक वचन में **'s** (*teacher's desk* = टीचर की मेज़), -s वाले बहुवचन में सिर्फ़ **'** (*students' books* = छात्रों की किताबें), बिना -s वाले बहुवचन में **'s** (*children's park*)।
:::

:::mistake COMMON MISTAKES – apostrophe · apostrophe की गलतियाँ
| ✗ Wrong | ✓ Right | Why · क्यों |
|---|---|---|
| The dog wagged **it's** tail. | The dog wagged **its** tail. | *its* = belonging to it (no apostrophe); *it's* = it is / it has · its = उसका; it's = it is |
| **Your** welcome. | **You're** welcome. | *you're* = you are; *your* = belonging to you · you're = तुम हो; your = तुम्हारा |
| Apple**'s** 50 per kg | Apple**s** ₹50 per kg | A simple plural never takes an apostrophe · साधारण बहुवचन में ' नहीं |
| Do'nt | **Don't** | The apostrophe goes where the letter (*o*) is missing: do n**o**t → don't · हटे अक्षर की जगह ' |
| the boys's room | the **boys'** room | plural ending in -s → apostrophe only · -s वाले बहुवचन में सिर्फ़ ' |
:::

## 3.5 Quotation marks, colon, semicolon, hyphen, dash, brackets · अन्य चिह्न

:::rule RULE – Other useful marks · दूसरे उपयोगी चिह्न
| Mark | Name | Main use | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **" "** or **' '** | quotation marks (inverted commas) | exact spoken words; titles of short works | Mother said, "Drink your milk." |
| **:** | colon | before a **list** or an **explanation** | Bring three things: a pen, a photo and your ID card. |
| **;** | semicolon | joins two **closely related** full sentences | It was raining; the match was cancelled. |
| **-** | hyphen (short) | joins words into one idea | a **well-known** actor, **twenty-five**, **mother-in-law**, a **5-year-old** boy |
| **–** | dash (longer) | a sudden break or added comment | He was tired – very tired. |
| **( )** | brackets (parentheses) | extra information | Call me tomorrow (after 5 p.m.). |
| **…** | ellipsis | words left out / speech trailing off | I was going to say… never mind. |

@hi **" "** = किसी के बोले हुए सटीक शब्द · **:** (colon) = सूची या व्याख्या से पहले · **;** (semicolon) = दो जुड़े हुए पूरे वाक्यों के बीच · **-** (hyphen) = दो शब्दों को एक बनाना (*well-known*) · **–** (dash) = अचानक रुकना/टिप्पणी · **( )** = अतिरिक्त जानकारी · **…** = बात अधूरी छोड़ना।
:::

:::note NOTE – British/Indian vs American punctuation · भारतीय/ब्रिटिश बनाम अमेरिकी
| Point | Indian / British | American |
|---|---|---|
| Titles | **Mr, Mrs, Dr** (no full stop) – *Mr.* also accepted in India | **Mr., Mrs., Dr.** (with full stop) |
| Quotation marks | often single: 'Hello' (double also common in India) | double: "Hello" |
| Comma/full stop with quotes | outside if not part of the quote: *He called it 'urgent'.* | usually inside: *He called it "urgent."* |
| Time | 10.30 a.m. or 10:30 a.m. | 10:30 a.m. |
All of these are acceptable – follow one style consistently in a document.
@hi भारत/ब्रिटेन में *Mr, Dr* (बिना बिंदु) और अमेरिका में *Mr., Dr.* – दोनों सही। एक document में एक ही शैली रखें।
:::

:::compare COMPARE & UNDERSTAND – hyphen or no hyphen? · hyphen लगे या नहीं?
| Without hyphen | With hyphen |
|---|---|
| The boy is **five years old**. (after the noun) | a **five-year-old** boy (before the noun, joined as one adjective; *year* stays singular) |
| a **man eating** fish (a man who is eating fish) | a **man-eating** fish (a fish that eats people!) |
| **re cover** – not a word | **re-cover** the sofa (cover again) vs **recover** from illness |

@hi संज्ञा से **पहले** उम्र एक विशेषण बनती है: *a five-year-old boy* (year एकवचन)। संज्ञा के **बाद**: *The boy is five years old.* *man-eating fish* = आदमख़ोर मछली, पर *man eating fish* = मछली खाता हुआ आदमी!
:::

:::tip EXAM TIP · परीक्षा टिप
Exams often test: **its/it's**, **your/you're**, **capital letters for days, months, languages but not seasons**, **? after direct questions only**, and **apostrophe with plural possessives** (*students'*). Read the whole sentence before choosing.
@hi परीक्षा में अक्सर पूछा जाता है: its/it's, your/you're, दिन-महीने-भाषा में capital पर मौसम में नहीं, ? सिर्फ़ सीधे सवाल में, और students' जैसे बहुवचन possessive।
:::

:::revision QUICK REVISION · जल्दी दोहराइए
- Capitals: sentence start, **I**, names, places, days, months, festivals, languages, nationalities, titles, organisations, short forms. **Not** seasons or common nouns.
- **.** statement · **?** direct question · **!** strong feeling (only one).
- Commas: lists, opening words, calling someone, before quotes, joining long sentences, dates/numbers/addresses.
- **it's** = it is; **its** = belonging to it. **you're** = you are; **your** = belonging to you.
- Colon before a list; semicolon between two linked sentences; hyphen in *five-year-old*.
@hi Capital: शुरुआत, I, नाम, जगह, दिन, महीने, त्योहार, भाषा (मौसम नहीं) · . ? ! · comma: सूची, पुकार, तारीख़ · it's = it is, its = उसका · you're = you are, your = तुम्हारा।
:::

**1-b** वाक्य की शुरुआत और शहर के नाम में capital; मौसम (*winter*) में नहीं।
**2-c** हफ़्ते के दिन हमेशा capital से। *summer, teacher, city* साधारण शब्द हैं।
**3-c** सीधा सवाल → **?**
**4-a** *I wonder what time…* एक कथन है जिसके अंदर अप्रत्यक्ष सवाल है → full stop।
**5-b** *its* = उसका। *it's* = it is।
**6-c** *You're* = you are (तुम हो)। *Your* = तुम्हारा।
**7-b** -s वाला बहुवचन → s के बाद सिर्फ़ apostrophe: *students'*।
**8-c** *will not* का छोटा रूप *won't* (अनियमित)।
**9-a** जिसे पुकार रहे हैं उसके नाम के बाद comma।
**10-b** Colon पूरे clause के बाद और सूची से पहले आता है। (a), (c), (d) में colon साधारण वाक्य को तोड़ देता है।
**11-a** संज्ञा से पहले पूरी उम्र hyphen से जुड़ती है और *year* एकवचन रहता है।
**12-a** अमेरिकी शैली में *Mr.*; भारतीय/ब्रिटिश में अक्सर *Mr* बिना बिंदु।
**13-c** मौसम (*monsoon*) में capital नहीं। भाषा, त्योहार, देश में capital।
**14-b** दो जुड़े हुए पूरे वाक्य → semicolon (या full stop, या *, so*)। (a) comma splice है (Ch 43)।
