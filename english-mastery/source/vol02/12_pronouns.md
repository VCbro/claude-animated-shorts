# Pronouns & Pronoun Reference

:::goals Learning Goals · सीखने के लक्ष्य – इस chapter के बाद आप
- use **subject** and **object** pronouns correctly (*I/me, he/him, they/them*);
  <span class="hl">कर्ता और कर्म वाले सर्वनाम सही लगाएँगे</span>
- use **reflexive** pronouns (*myself, themselves*) only where needed;
  <span class="hl">myself, themselves सिर्फ़ ज़रूरत पर लगाएँगे</span>
- use **this/that/these/those** and **someone/anyone/everyone**;
  <span class="hl">संकेतवाचक और अनिश्चयवाचक सर्वनाम सही लगाएँगे</span>
- make every pronoun **agree** with its noun and point **clearly** to it;
  <span class="hl">सर्वनाम को सही संज्ञा से मिलाएँगे और अर्थ साफ़ रखेंगे</span>
- avoid errors like *Me and Ravi went*, *between you and I*, *Myself Priya*.
  <span class="hl">*Me and Ravi went*, *Myself Priya* जैसी गलतियों से बचेंगे</span>
:::

:::warm Warm-up · शुरुआत
*Ravi called **Ravi's** mother because **Ravi** wanted **Ravi's** mother to bring **Ravi's** charger.* – Too many *Ravi*s! → *Ravi called **his** mother because **he** wanted **her** to bring **his** charger.*
@hi बार-बार संज्ञा दोहराने की बजाय हम **सर्वनाम (pronoun)** लगाते हैं – *he, his, her*। सर्वनाम संज्ञा की **जगह** आता है और वाक्य को छोटा और साफ़ बनाता है।
:::

## 12.1 Personal pronouns · पुरुषवाचक सर्वनाम

:::rule RULE – subject, object, possessive, reflexive
| Person | Subject (does the action) | Object (receives the action) | Possessive adj. / pronoun | Reflexive |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1st sing. | **I** | **me** | my / mine | myself |
| 2nd | **you** | **you** | your / yours | yourself / yourselves |
| 3rd sing. male | **he** | **him** | his / his | himself |
| 3rd sing. female | **she** | **her** | her / hers | herself |
| 3rd sing. thing/animal | **it** | **it** | its / – | itself |
| 1st plural | **we** | **us** | our / ours | ourselves |
| 3rd plural | **they** | **them** | their / theirs | themselves |

@hi **Subject form** (कर्ता) क्रिया से पहले: *I, he, she, we, they* (मैं, वह, हम, वे)। **Object form** (कर्म) क्रिया या preposition के बाद: *me, him, her, us, them* (मुझे, उसे, हमें, उन्हें)। *She called **me**.* = उसने **मुझे** फ़ोन किया। *with **him*** = उसके साथ।
:::

:::example EXAMPLES – subject or object?
- ***She** teaches **us** English.* (she = subject, us = object)
- *Please give **them** the keys.* · *Come with **me**.* (after a preposition → object)
- *My brother and **I** are twins.* (part of the subject → *I*)
- *The teacher praised my brother and **me**.* (part of the object → *me*)
@hi पहचान की आसान तरकीब: दूसरा नाम हटाकर देखिए। *My brother and I are twins* → *I am…* ✓। *The teacher praised my brother and me* → *praised me* ✓ (*praised I* ✗)।
:::

:::rule RULE – polite order and "It's me"
- Put **yourself last**: *Ravi and **I*** (not *I and Ravi*), *you and me*, *my wife and I*.
- After **between, with, for, to, except** → object form: *between you and **me***, *for him and **her***.
- *It's **me**.* / *That's **him**.* is normal English. Very formal: *It is **I**.* (rare)
- Comparisons: *She is taller than **me**.* (everyday) = *She is taller than **I am**.* (formal) – both correct.
@hi शिष्टता: अपने आप को **आख़िर** में रखिए – *Ravi and I*, *I and Ravi* नहीं। preposition के बाद object form: *between you and me* (तुम्हारे और मेरे बीच) – *between you and I* गलत। *It's me* (मैं हूँ) सामान्य English है।
:::

## 12.2 Reflexive and emphatic pronouns · निजवाचक सर्वनाम

:::rule RULE – myself, yourself, themselves …
- **Reflexive**: the subject and object are the **same person**: *I hurt **myself**.* · *She looked at **herself** in the mirror.* · *Enjoy **yourselves**!*
- **Emphatic**: to stress "**that person, nobody else**": *I **myself** saw it.* · *The Principal **herself** came.*
- **by + reflexive** = alone / without help: *He lives **by himself**.* · *I made it **by myself**.*
- **Don't** use a reflexive as a normal subject or object: ✗ *Myself Priya.* ✗ *Please contact myself.*
@hi **Reflexive:** कर्ता और कर्म एक ही व्यक्ति – *I hurt myself* (मुझे ख़ुद चोट लगी)। **Emphatic:** ज़ोर देने के लिए – *I myself saw it* (मैंने ख़ुद देखा)। *by myself* = अकेले / बिना मदद। परिचय में *Myself Priya* **गलत** है – कहिए *I am Priya* या *My name is Priya*।
:::

:::mistake COMMON MISTAKES – personal and reflexive pronouns
| ✗ Wrong | ✓ Right | Why · क्यों |
|---|---|---|
| Me and Ravi went to the market. | **Ravi and I** went to the market. | subject form + yourself last · कर्ता = I, ख़ुद को आख़िर में |
| Myself Priya from Surat. | **I am** Priya from Surat. / **My name is** Priya. | reflexive is not a subject · Myself से परिचय नहीं |
| Please send it to myself. | Please send it to **me**. | normal object → *me* · सामान्य कर्म = me |
| between you and I | between you and **me** | after preposition → object · preposition के बाद me |
| He himself he did it. | He did it **himself**. / He **himself** did it. | no double subject · कर्ता दो बार नहीं |
| The book, it is on the table. | The book is on the table. | no extra pronoun after the noun · संज्ञा के बाद फिर it नहीं |
| I enjoyed at the party. | I enjoyed **myself** at the party. / I enjoyed the party. | *enjoy* needs an object · enjoy के बाद कर्म चाहिए |
:::

## 12.3 Demonstrative, indefinite and reciprocal pronouns · संकेतवाचक, अनिश्चयवाचक, परस्पर

:::rule RULE – this / that / these / those
| | Near (यह / ये) | Far (वह / वे) |
|---|---|---|
| Singular | **this** – *This is my seat.* | **that** – *That is our bus.* |
| Plural | **these** – *These are fresh.* | **those** – *Those are old.* |
On the phone: ***This is** Ravi speaking.* · *Is **that** Meena?*
@hi पास की एक चीज़ = **this**, पास की कई = **these**, दूर की एक = **that**, दूर की कई = **those**। फ़ोन पर अपना परिचय: *This is Ravi* (मैं रवि बोल रहा हूँ)।
:::

:::rule RULE – indefinite pronouns
| some- (positive) | any- (questions, negatives) | no- (= not any) | every- (all) |
|---|---|---|---|
| someone / somebody | anyone / anybody | no one / nobody | everyone / everybody |
| something | anything | nothing | everything |
| somewhere | anywhere | nowhere | everywhere |
- They take a **singular verb**: *Everyone **is** here. Nobody **knows**. Something **has** happened.*
- Use **one** negative only: *I didn't see **anyone**.* = *I saw **no one**.*
- Later reference: *Someone left **their** bag.* (*his or her* is formal) – both accepted.
@hi *someone* = कोई, *anyone* = कोई भी (सवाल/नकार में), *no one / nobody* = कोई नहीं, *everyone* = सब। *something* = कुछ, *nothing* = कुछ नहीं, *everything* = सब कुछ। इनके साथ क्रिया **एकवचन**: *Everyone **is** here* (सब यहाँ हैं – पर English में *is*)।
:::

:::rule RULE – reciprocal: each other / one another
*Ravi and Sita love **each other**.* · *The players congratulated **one another**.*
**each other** = usually two people; **one another** = more than two (in everyday English both are used for any number).
@hi *each other / one another* = एक-दूसरे को। *They help each other* = वे एक-दूसरे की मदद करते हैं। (*They help themselves* = वे अपनी ख़ुद मदद करते हैं – अर्थ अलग!)
:::

## 12.4 Interrogative and relative pronouns (first look) · प्रश्नवाचक और संबंधवाचक

:::note NOTE – who, whom, whose, which, what, that
- **Questions**: ***Who** is calling? **Whom** did you invite? **Whose** is this? **Which** do you want? **What** happened?*
- **Joining clauses (relative)**: *the man **who** helped me · the girl **whose** phone was lost · the book **which/that** I bought.*
Full study of relative clauses in **Chapter 34**.
@hi सवाल में: *who* (कौन), *whom* (किसको), *whose* (किसका), *which* (कौन-सा), *what* (क्या)। वाक्य जोड़ने में: *the man **who** helped me* = वह आदमी **जिसने** मेरी मदद की। विस्तार Chapter 34 में।
:::

## 12.5 Agreement and clear reference · मेल और साफ़ संदर्भ

:::rule RULE – a pronoun must agree with its noun and point clearly to it
1. **Number and gender agree**: *The **girls** forgot **their** bags.* · *The **company** changed **its** logo.* (company = it)
2. **Each / every / either / neither + singular**: *Each student must bring **his or her** / **their** ID.* (verb stays singular: *Each student **has** …*)
3. **Clear reference** – if two nouns could fit, repeat the noun:
   ✗ *Ravi told Amit that **he** had won.* (who won?) → ✓ *Ravi told Amit, "**You** have won."* / *Ravi told Amit that **Amit** had won.*
4. **Vague *this / it*:** make it clear what *this* refers to: ✗ *The bus was late and it rained. **This** made me angry.* → ✓ ***This delay** made me angry.*
@hi (1) सर्वनाम अपनी संज्ञा से **वचन और लिंग** में मेल खाए: *The company changed **its** logo* (कंपनी = it)। (2) *each, every, either, neither* के साथ एकवचन क्रिया। (3) अगर *he/she* से दो लोग समझ आ सकते हैं, तो नाम दोहराइए – अर्थ साफ़ रहे।
:::

:::compare COMPARE & UNDERSTAND
| | |
|---|---|
| *They blamed **themselves**.* (each blamed self) | *They blamed **each other**.* (A blamed B, B blamed A) |
| *I did it **myself**.* (no help) | *I did it **by myself**.* (alone, no help) – same idea |
| *Who did you call?* (everyday) | *Whom did you call?* (formal) |
| *She is older than **me**.* (everyday) | *She is older than **I (am)**.* (formal) |
@hi *themselves* = अपने आप को, *each other* = एक-दूसरे को। *Who/me* रोज़ की बोलचाल में, *Whom/I am* औपचारिक लेखन में – दोनों सही।
:::

:::tip EXAM TIP · परीक्षा टिप
Common exam errors: **between you and I**, **Me and my friend went**, **Each of the boys have done their**, **One must do his duty** (✓ formal; *one's duty* also ✓), **Myself**, and **He and me went**. Test by removing the other person.
@hi परीक्षा में अक्सर: *between you and I* (गलत), *Me and my friend went* (गलत), *Each of the boys have* (गलत – *has*)। जाँच का तरीक़ा: दूसरा व्यक्ति हटाकर पढ़िए।
:::

:::revision QUICK REVISION · जल्दी दोहराइए
- Subject: I, he, she, we, they · Object: me, him, her, us, them.
- *Ravi and I* (subject) · *Ravi and me* (object) · *between you and **me***.
- Reflexive only when subject = object or for emphasis; never *Myself Priya*.
- this/these (near) · that/those (far) · everyone/nobody + **singular verb**.
- Pronoun agrees with its noun and must have **one clear** reference.
@hi कर्ता: I/he/they · कर्म: me/him/them · ख़ुद को आख़िर में · myself सिर्फ़ ज़रूरत पर · everyone + एकवचन क्रिया · संदर्भ साफ़।
:::
