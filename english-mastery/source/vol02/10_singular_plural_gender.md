:::goals Learning Goals · सीखने के लक्ष्य – इस chapter के बाद आप
- make **regular plurals** with -s, -es, -ies and -ves;
  <span class="hl">-s, -es, -ies, -ves से नियमित बहुवचन बनाएँगे</span>
- learn the common **irregular plurals** (*man → men, child → children*);
  <span class="hl">अनियमित बहुवचन याद करेंगे</span>
- use nouns that are **always plural** (*scissors, trousers*) or **look plural but are singular** (*news, mathematics*);
  <span class="hl">हमेशा बहुवचन और "दिखने में बहुवचन" वाली संज्ञाएँ सही इस्तेमाल करेंगे</span>
- say *a five-star hotel* but *five stars*;
  <span class="hl">संख्या के साथ बहुवचन का सही प्रयोग करेंगे</span>
- use **masculine, feminine and gender-neutral** nouns correctly.
  <span class="hl">पुल्लिंग, स्त्रीलिंग और लिंग-निरपेक्ष शब्द सही लगाएँगे</span>
:::

:::warm Warm-up · शुरुआत
One **book** → two **books**. One **box** → two **boxes**. One **child** → two … ? Not *childs* – **children**! One **sheep** → two … **sheep**!
@hi ज़्यादातर शब्दों में *-s* लगाकर बहुवचन बनता है, पर कुछ शब्द बिल्कुल अलग रूप लेते हैं (*child → children*), और कुछ बदलते ही नहीं (*sheep → sheep*)। आइए सब नियम देखें।
:::

## 10.1 Regular plurals · नियमित बहुवचन

:::rule RULE – Five spelling patterns
| Ending of the singular | Add | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| most nouns | **-s** | book**s**, girl**s**, phone**s**, day**s**, key**s** |
| **-s, -ss, -sh, -ch, -x, -z** | **-es** | bus**es**, class**es**, dish**es**, watch**es**, box**es**, quiz**zes** |
| consonant + **y** | **y → ies** | baby → bab**ies**, city → cit**ies**, party → part**ies** |
| vowel + **y** | **-s** | boy**s**, toy**s**, monkey**s**, holiday**s** |
| **-f / -fe** (many words) | **-ves** | leaf → lea**ves**, knife → kni**ves**, wife → wi**ves**, half → hal**ves**, shelf → shel**ves**, life → li**ves**, thief → thie**ves**, wolf → wol**ves** |
| **-f** (some words) | **-s** | roof**s**, chief**s**, belief**s**, proof**s**, chef**s**, cliff**s** |
| consonant + **o** (many) | **-es** | potato**es**, tomato**es**, hero**es**, echo**es**, mango**es** (also *mangos*) |
| **-o** (short, foreign, vowel + o) | **-s** | photo**s**, piano**s**, radio**s**, video**s**, kilo**s**, zoo**s**, studio**s** |

@hi ज़्यादातर शब्दों में **-s**। *s, sh, ch, x, z* पर ख़त्म शब्दों में **-es** (बोलने में एक syllable बढ़ जाता है: *box-es*)। **व्यंजन + y** → **ies** (*city → cities*); **स्वर + y** → सिर्फ़ **-s** (*boys*)। कई *-f/-fe* वाले शब्द → **-ves** (*knife → knives*), पर *roof, chief, belief* में सिर्फ़ *-s*। *potato, tomato, hero* → **-es**; *photo, piano, radio, video* → **-s**।
:::

## 10.2 Irregular plurals · अनियमित बहुवचन

:::rule RULE – Learn these by heart
| Change of vowel | -en | Same form for both | Foreign (formal/academic) |
|---|---|---|---|
| man → **men** | child → **children** | sheep → **sheep** | criterion → **criteria** |
| woman → **women** | ox → **oxen** | deer → **deer** | phenomenon → **phenomena** |
| foot → **feet** | | fish → **fish** (usual) | analysis → **analyses** |
| tooth → **teeth** | | aircraft → **aircraft** | crisis → **crises** |
| goose → **geese** | | series → **series** | basis → **bases** |
| mouse → **mice** | | species → **species** | medium → **media** |
| louse → **lice** | | | datum → **data** (often used as uncountable) |
| person → **people** (usual) | | | cactus → **cacti** / cactuses |

@hi इन्हें रट लीजिए: *man → men* (आदमी), *woman → women* (औरतें – उच्चारण "विमिन"), *foot → feet* (पैर), *tooth → teeth* (दाँत), *mouse → mice* (चूहे), *child → children* (बच्चे), *person → people* (लोग)। *sheep, deer, fish, series, species, aircraft* का एकवचन और बहुवचन एक जैसा रहता है।
:::

:::note NOTE – pronunciation of -s / -es
- "**s**" after *p, t, k, f*: cup**s**, cat**s**, book**s**, chief**s**
- "**z**" after vowels and *b, d, g, l, m, n, r, v*: day**s**, bag**s**, pen**s**, car**s**
- "**iz**" (extra syllable) after *s, z, sh, ch, j sounds*: bus**es**, rose**s**, dish**es**, watch**es**, page**s**
@hi *-s* की आवाज़ तीन तरह की होती है: *cups* में "स", *bags* में "ज़", और *buses, pages* में "इज़" (एक हिस्सा बढ़ जाता है)।
:::

## 10.3 Special groups · ख़ास संज्ञाएँ

:::rule RULE – always plural, plural-looking singular, and collective
| Group | Examples | Verb |
|---|---|---|
| **Always plural** (two-part things) | scissors, trousers, jeans, pants, shorts, glasses (spectacles), spectacles, pyjamas, tongs, pliers | **plural**: *My jeans **are** new.* · Count with **a pair of**: *a pair of scissors* |
| **Always plural** (others) | clothes, goods, thanks, congratulations, belongings, surroundings, outskirts, premises, police, cattle, people | **plural**: *The police **are** here. The cattle **are** grazing.* |
| **Look plural but singular** | news, mathematics, physics, economics, politics (subject), athletics, measles, diabetes | **singular**: *Mathematics **is** easy. Measles **is** infectious.* |
| **Collective nouns** | family, team, class, staff, committee, government | **singular** (as one unit) in Indian/American English: *The team **is** strong.* BrE also uses **plural** (members): *The team **are** arriving.* |

@hi **हमेशा बहुवचन:** दो हिस्सों वाली चीज़ें – *scissors* (कैंची), *trousers* (पतलून), *jeans, glasses* (चश्मा) – और *clothes, goods, police, cattle, people*। इन्हें गिनने के लिए *a pair of* (*a pair of scissors* = एक कैंची)। **दिखने में बहुवचन, पर एकवचन:** *news, mathematics, physics, economics, measles* – क्रिया *is*। समूहवाचक (*team, family*) भारत/अमेरिका में आम तौर पर एकवचन (*The team **is**…*)।
:::

:::rule RULE – numbers, measurements and compound nouns
1. **Number + noun as an adjective (before another noun) → singular**: a **five-star** hotel, a **ten-rupee** note, a **two-week** holiday, a **six-foot** wall.
   But as a normal noun → plural: *The hotel has five **stars**. The holiday lasted two **weeks**.*
2. **hundred, thousand, lakh, crore, dozen, million** after a number → no -s: *two **hundred** people, five **lakh** rupees, three **dozen** eggs*. (*hundreds of people* = an unknown large number.)
3. **Compound nouns**: make the **main word** plural: brother**s**-in-law, passer**s**-by, commander**s**-in-chief; noun + noun: bus stop**s**, toothbrush**es**, girlfriend**s**.
@hi (1) संख्या + संज्ञा जब दूसरी संज्ञा से पहले **विशेषण** बने तो एकवचन: *a five-star hotel* (पाँच-सितारा होटल), *a ten-rupee note*। (2) *hundred, thousand, lakh, crore, dozen* संख्या के बाद *-s* नहीं लेते: *five lakh rupees*। (3) यौगिक शब्द में **मुख्य शब्द** बहुवचन बनता है: *brothers-in-law* (साले/जीजा लोग), *passers-by* (राहगीर)।
:::

## 10.4 Gender of nouns · संज्ञा का लिंग

:::rule RULE – four genders in English grammar
| Gender | Meaning | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Masculine** | male | man, boy, father, king, actor, uncle, nephew, bull |
| **Feminine** | female | woman, girl, mother, queen, actress, aunt, niece, cow |
| **Common** | male **or** female | teacher, doctor, student, friend, cousin, parent, child, baby, person |
| **Neuter** | non-living things / no gender | table, book, tree, city, phone, idea |

@hi English में संज्ञा का लिंग **असली लिंग** से तय होता है, शब्द से नहीं। हिंदी में *किताब* स्त्रीलिंग और *पेड़* पुल्लिंग है, पर English में *book, tree, table* – सब **neuter** (नपुंसक) हैं और इनके लिए **it** आता है। *teacher, doctor, friend* पुरुष या महिला दोनों हो सकते हैं – **common gender**।
:::

### Masculine – feminine pairs · पुल्लिंग – स्त्रीलिंग
| Masculine | Feminine | Masculine | Feminine |
|---|---|---|---|
| man | woman | husband | wife |
| boy | girl | bridegroom | bride |
| father | mother | son | daughter |
| brother | sister | nephew | niece |
| uncle | aunt | king | queen |
| prince | princess | lord | lady |
| host | hostess | hero | heroine |
| lion | lioness | tiger | tigress |
| bull / ox | cow | horse / stallion | mare |
| dog | bitch (use: *female dog*) | cock / rooster | hen |
| gentleman | lady | Mr | Mrs / Ms / Miss |

:::note NOTE – modern gender-neutral words
Modern English, especially in offices and exams, prefers **one word for everyone**:
| Older | Modern (preferred) |
|---|---|
| chairman / chairwoman | **chairperson**, **chair** |
| salesman / saleswoman | **salesperson**, sales executive |
| policeman / policewoman | **police officer** |
| fireman | **firefighter** |
| air hostess / steward | **flight attendant**, cabin crew |
| mankind | **humankind**, **people** |
| actress, authoress, poetess | **actor** (both), **author**, **poet** (both) |
**Ms** (pronounced "miz") is used for a woman when marital status is unknown or not relevant.
@hi आज की औपचारिक English में सबके लिए एक ही शब्द बेहतर माना जाता है: *chairperson, police officer, firefighter, flight attendant*। *actor, author, poet* अब पुरुष और महिला दोनों के लिए। विवाहित है या नहीं – पता न हो तो महिला के लिए **Ms** ("मिज़") लिखिए।
:::

:::mistake COMMON MISTAKES – plural and gender
| ✗ Wrong | ✓ Right | Why · क्यों |
|---|---|---|
| two childs / childrens | two **children** | irregular; *children* is already plural · children पहले से बहुवचन |
| many peoples came | many **people** came | *people* = plural of *person* · people ख़ुद बहुवचन |
| a ten-rupees note | a **ten-rupee** note | number + noun before a noun → singular · विशेषण बना तो एकवचन |
| five lakhs rupees | five **lakh** rupees | no -s after a number · संख्या के बाद lakh में -s नहीं |
| My trouser is torn. | My **trousers are** torn. | always plural · हमेशा बहुवचन |
| a scissor | **a pair of scissors** / scissors | always plural · a pair of लगाइए |
| Mathematics are my favourite subject. | Mathematics **is** … | subject names in -ics are singular · विषय का नाम एकवचन |
| I bought new furnitures and cloths. | … new **furniture** and **clothes**. | *clothes* = कपड़े (पहनने के); *cloths* = कपड़े के टुकड़े · अर्थ अलग |
| The book is on the table. She is very old. (about the book) | **It** is very old. | non-living things are neuter → *it* · निर्जीव के लिए it |
:::

:::compare COMPARE & UNDERSTAND
| | |
|---|---|
| **cloth** (uncountable: material) – *cotton cloth* | **clothes** (plural: things you wear) – *new clothes* |
| **people** (plural of person) – *ten people* | **peoples** (nations / ethnic groups) – *the peoples of Asia* |
| **fish** (usual plural) – *I caught five fish.* | **fishes** (different species) – *the fishes of the Indian Ocean* |
| **hundreds of** people (unknown number) | **two hundred** people (exact number) |
@hi *cloth* = कपड़ा (सामग्री), *clothes* = पहनने के कपड़े। *people* = लोग, *peoples* = जातियाँ/राष्ट्र। *five fish* = पाँच मछलियाँ, *fishes* = मछलियों की अलग-अलग प्रजातियाँ। *hundreds of* = सैकड़ों (अनिश्चित), *two hundred* = ठीक दो सौ।
:::

:::tip EXAM TIP · परीक्षा टिप
Watch for: **lakh/crore/dozen/hundred** with -s after a number, **number-noun adjectives** (*a two-hour journey*), **always-plural nouns + singular verb**, and **-ics subjects + plural verb**. These four are the most common plural errors in SSC/bank papers.
@hi परीक्षा में सबसे ज़्यादा पूछे जाने वाली चार गलतियाँ: संख्या के बाद *lakhs/dozens*, *a two-hours journey*, *trousers/scissors* के साथ *is*, और *mathematics/economics* के साथ *are*।
:::

:::revision QUICK REVISION · जल्दी दोहराइए
- Plural: **-s** · **-es** (s, sh, ch, x, z, many -o) · consonant+y → **ies** · many -f/-fe → **ves**.
- Irregular: men, women, children, feet, teeth, mice, people; same: sheep, deer, fish, series, species.
- Always plural: scissors, trousers, jeans, glasses, clothes, police, cattle (*a pair of …*). Singular: news, mathematics, measles.
- *a five-star hotel* · *two hundred / five lakh* (no -s) · brothers-in-law.
- Gender follows real sex; things = **it**; prefer **chairperson, police officer, flight attendant**.
@hi बहुवचन के नियम · अनियमित रूप · हमेशा बहुवचन/एकवचन वाले शब्द · संख्या-विशेषण एकवचन · निर्जीव = it।
:::
