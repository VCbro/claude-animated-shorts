# Degrees of Comparison
<!-- level: Intermediate -->
<!-- hi: तुलना की अवस्थाएँ – positive, comparative, superlative -->
<!-- desc: Positive, comparative, superlative; -er/-est and more/most spelling rules; irregular forms (good/better/best, far/farther/further); as…as, not so/as…as; than + object/clause; the + superlative; double comparatives (the more…the more, bigger and bigger); changing degrees without changing meaning; elder/older, later/latter, less/fewer -->

:::goals Learning Goals · सीखने के लक्ष्य – इस chapter के बाद आप
- make the three degrees: **tall – taller – tallest**, **beautiful – more beautiful – most beautiful**;
  <span class="hl">तुलना की तीनों अवस्थाएँ बनाएँगे</span>
- use the **irregular** forms (*good – better – best, bad – worse – worst*);
  <span class="hl">अनियमित रूप याद करेंगे</span>
- use **as … as**, **than**, **the … -est / the most**;
  <span class="hl">as…as, than और the + superlative सही लगाएँगे</span>
- change one degree into another **without changing the meaning** (exam favourite);
  <span class="hl">अर्थ बदले बिना एक degree से दूसरी में बदलेंगे</span>
- avoid errors like *more better*, *most tallest*, *senior than*.
  <span class="hl">*more better*, *senior than* जैसी गलतियों से बचेंगे</span>
:::

:::warm Warm-up · शुरुआत
Delhi is **big**. Mumbai is **bigger** than Pune. Which is the **biggest** city in India?
@hi *big* = बड़ा (सामान्य), *bigger than* = से बड़ा (दो की तुलना), *the biggest* = सबसे बड़ा (तीन या ज़्यादा में)। हिंदी में "से" और "सबसे" लगाते हैं, English में शब्द का रूप बदलता है।
:::

## 16.1 Three degrees · तीन अवस्थाएँ

:::rule RULE – positive, comparative, superlative
| Degree | Use | Pattern | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Positive** | describes one thing / equal things | adj · **as + adj + as** | *Ravi is **tall**. Ravi is **as tall as** Amit.* |
| **Comparative** | compares **two** | adj**-er** / **more** adj + **than** | *Ravi is **taller than** Amit. This book is **more interesting than** that one.* |
| **Superlative** | one out of **three or more** | **the** adj**-est** / **the most** adj (+ in / of) | *Ravi is **the tallest** boy **in** the class. It's **the most interesting** book **of** all.* |
@hi **Positive** – सामान्य (*tall* = लंबा; *as tall as* = जितना लंबा)। **Comparative** – दो की तुलना (*taller than* = से लंबा)। **Superlative** – तीन या ज़्यादा में सबसे (*the tallest* = सबसे लंबा)। Superlative के साथ **the** ज़रूरी, और जगह/समूह के लिए *in* (*in the class*), बाक़ी के लिए *of* (*of all*)।
:::

## 16.2 Forming comparatives and superlatives · रूप कैसे बनाएँ

:::rule RULE – spelling patterns
| Adjective | Rule | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| one syllable | **-er / -est** | tall → taller → tallest · cheap → cheaper → cheapest |
| ending in **-e** | **-r / -st** | large → larger → largest · nice → nicer → nicest |
| one vowel + one consonant | **double** the consonant | big → bigger → biggest · hot → hotter → hottest · thin → thinner |
| two syllables ending in **-y** | **y → ier / iest** | happy → happier → happiest · easy → easier · busy → busiest |
| two syllables (most others) and **three+ syllables** | **more / most** | careful → more careful · modern → more modern · expensive → most expensive |
| some two-syllable words | either | clever → cleverer / more clever · simple → simpler · quiet → quieter · narrow → narrower |
| **-ed / -ing** adjectives (any length) | **more / most** | tired → more tired · bored → more bored · boring → most boring |
@hi एक syllable वाले शब्द – **-er/-est** (*cheaper, cheapest*)। *-e* पर ख़त्म – **-r/-st**। एक स्वर + एक व्यंजन – व्यंजन **दोहराइए** (*bigger*)। *-y* वाले दो syllable – **ier/iest** (*happier*)। बाक़ी लंबे शब्द – **more/most** (*more expensive* = ज़्यादा महँगा)।
:::

:::rule RULE – irregular forms
| Positive | Comparative | Superlative |
|---|---|---|
| good / well | **better** | **best** |
| bad / badly / ill | **worse** | **worst** |
| much / many | **more** | **most** |
| little (amount) | **less** | **least** |
| far | **farther / further** | **farthest / furthest** |
| old | **older / elder** | **oldest / eldest** |
| late | **later / latter** | **latest / last** |
@hi *good → better → best* (अच्छा → बेहतर → सबसे अच्छा), *bad → worse → worst* (बुरा → और बुरा → सबसे बुरा), *much/many → more → most*, *little → less → least*, *far → farther/further*।
:::

:::compare COMPARE & UNDERSTAND – easily confused forms
| | |
|---|---|
| **elder / eldest** – only for **family members**, before a noun: *my **elder** brother, the **eldest** son* (✗ *elder than*) | **older / oldest** – people or things, with *than*: *He is **older than** me. the **oldest** temple* |
| **farther** – physical distance: *Pune is **farther** than Lonavala.* | **further** – distance **or** "more, additional": ***further** details, **further** studies* |
| **later / latest** – time: *I'll call you **later**. the **latest** news (newest)* | **latter / last** – order: *Ravi and Amit came; the **latter** (Amit) was late. the **last** bus (final)* |
| **fewer** – countable: *fewer mistakes* | **less** – uncountable: *less sugar* |
@hi **elder/eldest** सिर्फ़ परिवार के लिए और संज्ञा से पहले (*my elder brother* = मेरा बड़ा भाई); *than* के साथ **older**। **farther** = ज़्यादा दूर; **further** = दूर या "और अधिक" (*further details* = और जानकारी)। **latest** = सबसे नया; **last** = आख़िरी; **latter** = दो में से बाद वाला।
:::

## 16.3 Comparison patterns · तुलना के ढाँचे

:::rule RULE – useful patterns
| Pattern | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **as + adj + as** | equal | *This phone is **as good as** that one.* |
| **not as / not so + adj + as** | less | *Bus travel is **not as fast as** train travel.* |
| **twice / three times + as … as** | multiple | *My room is **twice as big as** yours.* |
| **comparative + than** | more / less | *Gold is **costlier than** silver.* · *This is **less expensive than** that.* |
| **much / far / a lot / a bit + comparative** | degree of difference | *much better, far cheaper, a bit taller* (✗ *very better*) |
| **comparative + and + comparative** | continuous change | *It's getting **hotter and hotter**. Prices are **more and more** expensive.* |
| **the + comparative, the + comparative** | one change causes another | ***The more** you practise, **the better** you speak.* |
| **one of the + superlative + plural noun** | among the best | *She is **one of the best players** in India.* |
| **superlative + ever / I have ever …** | experience | *This is **the best** biryani **I have ever eaten**.* |
@hi *as good as* = उतना ही अच्छा · *not as fast as* = उतना तेज़ नहीं · *twice as big as* = दुगना बड़ा · *much better* = कहीं बेहतर (*very better* गलत) · *hotter and hotter* = और-और गरम · *The more you practise, the better you speak* = जितना ज़्यादा अभ्यास, उतना अच्छा बोलना · *one of the best players* = सबसे अच्छे खिलाड़ियों में से एक (बहुवचन संज्ञा!)।
:::

:::rule RULE – than + object or subject
- Everyday English: *She is taller **than me**.*
- Formal / exams: *She is taller **than I (am)**.* · *I like you more **than he** does.* vs *I like you more **than him**.* (different meanings!)
- Compare **like with like**: ✗ *The climate of Shimla is cooler than Delhi.* → ✓ *… cooler than **that of** Delhi.* / *… than Delhi's.*
@hi बोलचाल में *than me*; औपचारिक लेखन में *than I am*। तुलना एक जैसी चीज़ों में हो: शिमला की **जलवायु** की तुलना दिल्ली की **जलवायु** से – *than that of Delhi*, सिर्फ़ *than Delhi* नहीं।
:::

## 16.4 Changing the degree (without changing the meaning) · degree बदलना

:::rule RULE – transformation (very common in school & competitive exams)
| Superlative | Comparative | Positive |
|---|---|---|
| *Everest is **the highest** mountain in the world.* | *Everest is **higher than any other** mountain in the world.* | ***No other** mountain in the world is **as high as** Everest.* |
| *Mumbai is **one of the biggest** cities in India.* | *Mumbai is **bigger than most other** cities in India.* | ***Very few** cities in India are **as big as** Mumbai.* |
| – | *Ravi is **taller than** Amit.* | *Amit is **not as tall as** Ravi.* |
Key words: **any other + singular noun** · **most other + plural noun** · **No other … as … as** · **Very few … as … as**.
@hi Superlative → Comparative: *the highest* → *higher than any other* (किसी भी दूसरे से ऊँचा – *any other* + एकवचन)। → Positive: *No other … as high as* (कोई दूसरा उतना ऊँचा नहीं)। "One of the" वाले वाक्य में *most other* और *very few* लगते हैं।
:::

:::mistake COMMON MISTAKES – comparison
| ✗ Wrong | ✓ Right | Why · क्यों |
|---|---|---|
| This is more better. | This is **better**. / **much better** | no double comparative · दो बार तुलना नहीं |
| She is the most tallest. | She is **the tallest**. | no double superlative · दो बार superlative नहीं |
| He is elder than me. | He is **older** than me. | *elder* not with *than* · than के साथ older |
| He is senior than me. | He is **senior to** me. | *senior/junior/superior/inferior/prior* + **to** · इनके साथ to |
| I prefer tea than coffee. | I prefer tea **to** coffee. | *prefer … to* · prefer के साथ to |
| very taller | **much / far** taller | *very* doesn't go with comparatives · very नहीं, much |
| one of the best player | one of the best **players** | *one of the* + plural · बहुवचन संज्ञा |
| Ram is better than any boy in the class. | … than **any other** boy … | Ram is also a boy → *other* · खुद को भी गिन लेंगे |
| Who is the eldest, you or your brother? | Who is **older / the elder** …? | two people → comparative · दो में comparative |
:::

:::tip EXAM TIP · परीक्षा टिप
Error spotting: look for **double comparatives** (*more easier*), **senior/junior/prefer + than** (should be *to*), **elder than**, **any + noun** (needs *other*), and **one of the + singular noun**. Transformation questions: keep the **tense** and **meaning**; use *any other + singular* and *no other … as … as*.
@hi Error spotting में देखिए: *more easier*, *senior than* (*to* होना चाहिए), *elder than*, *any boy* (*any other boy*), *one of the best player* (*players*)। Transformation में काल और अर्थ वही रखिए।
:::

:::revision QUICK REVISION · जल्दी दोहराइए
- **-er/-est** (short) · **more/most** (long) · doubling (*bigger*) · y→i (*happier*).
- Irregular: **better/best, worse/worst, more/most, less/least, farther/further**.
- **as…as** (equal) · **not as…as** (less) · **-er than** · **the -est / the most** · **much better** (not *very*).
- *elder* (family, no *than*) · *senior **to*** · *prefer A **to** B* · *one of the + plural*.
- Transform: **the highest = higher than any other = No other … as high as**.
@hi तीन अवस्थाएँ · अनियमित रूप · as…as, than, the + superlative · elder/older, senior to, prefer to · degree बदलना।
:::
