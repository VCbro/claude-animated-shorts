:::practice Section 1 – Nouns, plurals and possessives (Ch 9–11) · संज्ञा, बहुवचन, possessive
1. Choose the correct sentence.
 (a) She gave me many useful advices. (b) She gave me some useful advice. (c) She gave me an useful advice. (d) She gave me a useful advices.
2. The news ______ very encouraging.
 (a) are (b) were (c) is (d) have
3. The plural of **leaf** is:
 (a) leafs (b) leaves (c) leafes (d) leavs
4. Choose the correct phrase.
 (a) a five-stars hotel (b) a five-star hotel (c) a five star's hotel (d) a fives-star hotel
5. My new trousers ______ too tight.
 (a) is (b) are (c) was (d) has
6. The ______ room is on the second floor. (for many teachers)
 (a) teacher's (b) teachers' (c) teachers's (d) teachers
7. This pen isn't mine. Is it ______?
 (a) your's (b) yours (c) you're (d) yours'
8. He is a cousin of ______.
 (a) me (b) my (c) mine (d) I

:::practice Section 2 – Pronouns and articles (Ch 12–13) · सर्वनाम और article

 (a) I (b) me (c) myself (d) mine

 (a) a (b) the (c) an (d) no article

**3-b** *-f* → *-ves*। (Ch 10)

**5-b** *trousers* हमेशा बहुवचन। (Ch 10)

**11-b** *Everyone* + एकवचन क्रिया। (Ch 12)
