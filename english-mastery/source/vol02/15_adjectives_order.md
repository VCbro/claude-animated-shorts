:::warm Warm-up · शुरुआत
Which sounds natural? **(a)** *a red big old car* **(b)** *a big old red car*
Native speakers say **(b)** without thinking. English has an **order** for adjectives – and you will learn it today.
@hi (b) *a big old red car* (एक बड़ी पुरानी लाल गाड़ी) सही लगता है। English में कई विशेषणों का एक **तय क्रम** होता है – आज वही सीखेंगे।
:::

## 15.1 What adjectives do and where they go · विशेषण क्या करते हैं, कहाँ आते हैं

:::rule RULE – adjective positions
An **adjective** describes or gives more information about a **noun** or **pronoun**.
| Position | Pattern | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Attributive** (before the noun) | adjective + noun | *a **tall** building, **fresh** vegetables, an **honest** man* |
| **Predicative** (after a linking verb) | be / look / seem / feel / sound / taste / smell / become / get + adjective | *The building **is tall**. You **look tired**. The soup **tastes good**. She **became famous**.* |
- Adjectives **never take -s** in English: *two **big** houses* (✗ *bigs houses*).
- Some adjectives are used **only after** the verb: *afraid, alone, alive, asleep, awake, alike* → *The baby is **asleep**.* (✗ *an asleep baby* → ✓ *a **sleeping** baby*).
@hi **Adjective (विशेषण)** संज्ञा या सर्वनाम की विशेषता बताता है। दो जगह आता है: संज्ञा से **पहले** (*a tall building* = एक ऊँची इमारत) या *is, look, feel, taste, become* जैसी क्रियाओं के **बाद** (*You look tired* = तुम थके लगते हो)। English में विशेषण का बहुवचन नहीं होता – *big houses*, *bigs* नहीं। *asleep, afraid, alone, alive* सिर्फ़ क्रिया के बाद आते हैं।
:::

:::note NOTE – linking verbs take adjectives, not adverbs
*The food **smells good**.* (✗ *smells well*) · *She **looks happy**.* (✗ *looks happily*) · *I **feel bad** about it.* (✗ *feel badly* – in exams)
**But** *well* is also an adjective meaning **healthy**: *I don't **feel well** today.* ✓
@hi *look, feel, smell, taste, sound* के बाद **विशेषण** (adverb नहीं): *The food smells good* (खाने की ख़ुशबू अच्छी है)। पर *well* का अर्थ "स्वस्थ" भी है: *I don't feel well* = मेरी तबीयत ठीक नहीं।
:::

## 15.2 Kinds of adjectives · विशेषण के प्रकार

:::rule RULE – main kinds
| Kind | Answers | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Quality / descriptive** | what kind? | good, kind, red, round, Indian, cotton, intelligent |
| **Quantity** | how much? | some, much, little, enough, whole, all (Ch 14) |
| **Number** (definite / indefinite / ordinal) | how many? / which in order? | one, two, ten · many, several, few · first, second, last |
| **Demonstrative** | which one? | this, that, these, those |
| **Possessive** | whose? | my, your, his, her, its, our, their |
| **Interrogative** | in questions | which, what, whose – *Which bus? What time? Whose bag?* |
| **Proper** (from proper nouns) – capital! | – | Indian, Gujarati, British, Japanese, Mughal, Victorian |
@hi **गुणवाचक** (कैसा? – *good, red, Indian*), **परिमाणवाचक** (कितना? – *some, much*), **संख्यावाचक** (कितने? कौन-सा क्रम? – *two, many, first*), **संकेतवाचक** (*this, that*), **सार्वनामिक/possessive** (*my, your*), **प्रश्नवाचक** (*which, what*)। देश/भाषा से बने विशेषण (*Indian, Gujarati*) हमेशा capital से।
:::

## 15.3 -ed and -ing adjectives · bored और boring

:::rule RULE – how you feel vs what causes the feeling
| **-ed** = how a **person feels** | **-ing** = what **causes** the feeling (person or thing) |
|---|---|
| *I am **bored**.* (I feel bored) | *The lecture is **boring**.* (it makes me bored) |
| *She was **interested** in the offer.* | *It was an **interesting** offer.* |
| *We were **excited** about the trip.* | *The trip was **exciting**.* |
| *He felt **tired** after work.* | *Work was **tiring** today.* |
| ***confused, surprised, worried, annoyed, embarrassed, satisfied, amazed, frightened, disappointed*** | ***confusing, surprising, worrying, annoying, embarrassing, satisfying, amazing, frightening, disappointing*** |
⚠ *I am **boring**.* = I am a boring person (others find me dull)! You probably mean *I am **bored**.*
@hi **-ed** = व्यक्ति कैसा **महसूस** करता है (*I am bored* = मैं ऊब गया हूँ)। **-ing** = जो चीज़ या व्यक्ति वह भावना **पैदा** करता है (*The lecture is boring* = lecture उबाऊ है)। सावधान: *I am boring* का मतलब है "मैं एक उबाऊ इंसान हूँ"!
:::

## 15.4 Order of adjectives · विशेषणों का क्रम

:::rule RULE – OSASCOMP (Opinion → Size → Age → Shape → Colour → Origin → Material → Purpose + NOUN)
| Det. | **O**pinion | **S**ize | **A**ge | **S**hape | **C**olour | **O**rigin | **M**aterial | **P**urpose | Noun |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| a | lovely | small | old | round | brown | Indian | wooden | dining | table |
| a | – | big | new | – | red | – | – | – | car |
| two | beautiful | – | – | – | – | Kashmiri | silk | – | shawls |
| an | expensive | – | – | – | black | Italian | leather | – | bag |
| a | – | – | – | – | – | – | steel | lunch | box |
- In real English we rarely use more than **2–3 adjectives** together.
- **Opinion** (*nice, beautiful, ugly*) usually comes **first**; **purpose** (*sleeping bag, running shoes*) is **closest** to the noun.
- Two adjectives of the **same type** → use a comma or *and*: *a **cheap, comfortable** hotel*; *The room was **clean and quiet**.*
@hi क्रम याद रखने का सूत्र **OSASCOMP**: **राय** (सुंदर) → **आकार** (छोटा) → **उम्र** (पुराना) → **बनावट/आकृति** (गोल) → **रंग** (भूरा) → **मूल/देश** (भारतीय) → **पदार्थ** (लकड़ी का) → **उपयोग** (खाने की) + संज्ञा: *a lovely small old round brown Indian wooden dining table*। असल में 2–3 विशेषण ही साथ आते हैं।
:::

@fig ch15_adjective_order.svg | The order of adjectives – OSASCOMP

:::example EXAMPLES – natural combinations
- *a **beautiful old** temple* (opinion + age) · *a **tall young** man* (size + age)
- *a **small black** puppy* (size + colour) · *a **new Japanese** car* (age + origin)
- *a **lovely cotton** saree* (opinion + material) · *my **old red** school bag* (age + colour + purpose)
@hi अर्थ: एक सुंदर पुराना मंदिर · एक लंबा युवक · एक छोटा काला पिल्ला · एक नई जापानी गाड़ी · एक प्यारी सूती साड़ी · मेरा पुराना लाल स्कूल बैग।
:::

## 15.5 Compound adjectives and nouns as adjectives · यौगिक विशेषण

:::rule RULE – compound adjectives (with hyphens before a noun)
| Pattern | Examples |
|---|---|
| number + noun (singular!) | a **five-year-old** girl, a **10-minute** walk, a **two-day** trip, a **500-rupee** note |
| adjective/adverb + past participle | a **well-known** actor, a **well-dressed** man, a **brown-haired** boy, a **kind-hearted** lady |
| adjective + -ing participle | a **good-looking** actor, a **long-lasting** battery, a **time-saving** idea |
| noun + -ing / past participle | a **heart-breaking** story, a **man-made** lake, a **hand-made** basket |
| others | a **part-time** job, a **full-time** course, a **last-minute** change, a **world-famous** singer |
**Nouns as adjectives** stay singular: *a **shoe** shop, a **vegetable** market, a **bus** stop, a **tooth**brush* (✗ *shoes shop, vegetables market*).
@hi दो-तीन शब्दों को hyphen से जोड़कर एक विशेषण: *a well-known actor* (एक मशहूर अभिनेता), *a five-year-old girl* (पाँच साल की लड़की – *year* एकवचन!), *a part-time job*। जब संज्ञा विशेषण का काम करे तो **एकवचन** रहती है: *a shoe shop* (जूतों की दुकान), *shoes shop* नहीं।
:::

:::mistake COMMON MISTAKES – adjectives
| ✗ Wrong | ✓ Right | Why · क्यों |
|---|---|---|
| She has a black long hair. | She has **long black** hair. | size before colour; *hair* uncountable · आकार फिर रंग |
| I am very boring in the class. | I am very **bored** in the class. | feeling → *-ed* · भावना = -ed |
| The film was very bored. | The film was very **boring**. | cause → *-ing* · कारण = -ing |
| The soup tastes deliciously. | The soup tastes **delicious**. | linking verb + adjective · taste के बाद विशेषण |
| They are goods students. | They are **good** students. | adjectives never take -s · विशेषण में -s नहीं |
| a ten-years-old boy | a **ten-year-old** boy | number + noun adj → singular · एकवचन |
| He bought a leather Italian black bag. | … a **black Italian leather** bag. | colour → origin → material · क्रम |
| a vegetables shop | a **vegetable** shop | noun as adjective → singular · एकवचन |
| a asleep child | a **sleeping** child / The child is **asleep**. | *asleep* only after the verb · asleep सिर्फ़ क्रिया के बाद |
:::

:::compare COMPARE & UNDERSTAND
| | |
|---|---|
| *an **economic** crisis* (related to the economy) | *an **economical** car* (saves money / fuel) |
| *a **historic** day* (very important in history) | *a **historical** film* (about history) |
| *She is **sensible**.* (wise, practical) | *She is **sensitive**.* (easily hurt / aware of feelings) |
| *I'm **ashamed** of my mistake.* (feel bad) | *I'm **shy** to speak.* (nervous with people) |
@hi *economic* = अर्थव्यवस्था से जुड़ा / *economical* = किफ़ायती · *historic* = ऐतिहासिक महत्व वाला / *historical* = इतिहास के बारे में · *sensible* = समझदार / *sensitive* = संवेदनशील · *ashamed* = शर्मिंदा / *shy* = शर्मीला।
:::

:::tip EXAM TIP · परीक्षा टिप
Exams test: **-ed/-ing** (*bored/boring*), **linking verb + adjective** (*looks happy*, not *happily*), **adjective order** in "rearrange" questions, **number-noun compounds** (*a three-day*), and confusing pairs (**economic/economical**, **sensible/sensitive**).
@hi परीक्षा में: *bored/boring*, *looks happy* (happily नहीं), विशेषणों का क्रम, *a three-day trip*, और *economic/economical* जैसे जोड़े।
:::

:::revision QUICK REVISION · जल्दी दोहराइए
- Adjective before a noun or after **be / look / feel / taste / seem / become**; no -s.
- **-ed** = how I feel · **-ing** = what causes it.
- Order: **Opinion – Size – Age – Shape – Colour – Origin – Material – Purpose + noun**.
- Compounds: *a two-day trip, a well-known actor* · noun as adjective stays singular: *a shoe shop*.
@hi विशेषण संज्ञा से पहले या linking verb के बाद · -ed = भावना, -ing = कारण · OSASCOMP क्रम · *a two-day trip, a shoe shop*।
:::
