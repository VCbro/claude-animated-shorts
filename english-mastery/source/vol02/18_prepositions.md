- use **at / on / in** for time and place;
  <span class="hl">समय और जगह के लिए at/on/in सही लगाएँगे</span>
- use **for / since**, **by / until**, **during / while**;
  <span class="hl">for/since, by/until, during/while का फ़र्क़ समझेंगे</span>
- choose **between / among**, **into / in**, **across / through**;
  <span class="hl">between/among, into/in, across/through चुनेंगे</span>
- learn **fixed prepositions** after verbs, adjectives and nouns (*depend on, good at*);
  <span class="hl">क्रिया, विशेषण और संज्ञा के बाद आने वाले तय prepositions याद करेंगे</span>
- avoid extra prepositions (*discuss about, reach to*).
  <span class="hl">फ़ालतू prepositions (*discuss about, reach to*) से बचेंगे</span>
:::

:::warm Warm-up · शुरुआत
*The meeting is **at** 10 a.m. **on** Monday **in** June.* · *The keys are **on** the table **in** the kitchen.*
@hi हिंदी में "में, पर, को, से, के नीचे" जैसे शब्द संज्ञा के **बाद** आते हैं (*मेज़ **पर***)। English में **preposition** संज्ञा से **पहले** आता है (***on** the table*)। सबसे ज़्यादा गलतियाँ *at/on/in* में होती हैं।
:::

## 18.1 Prepositions of time · समय

:::rule RULE – at, on, in (from small to big)
| **at** – exact time / point | **on** – days and dates | **in** – longer periods |
|---|---|---|
| **at** 7 o'clock, **at** 5.30 p.m. | **on** Monday, **on** Sundays | **in** the morning / afternoon / evening |
| **at** noon, **at** midnight, **at** night | **on** 15 August, **on** my birthday | **in** June, **in** summer, **in** the monsoon |
| **at** the weekend (BrE) | **on** the weekend (AmE) | **in** 2026, **in** the 1990s, **in** the 21st century |
| **at** Diwali / Christmas (the festival period) | **on** Diwali day, **on** Independence Day | **in** two hours (= after two hours from now) |
| **at** the moment, **at** present, **at** the same time | **on** Monday morning, **on** a hot day | **in** time (not late) · **on** time (punctual) |
**No preposition** before *today, tomorrow, yesterday, this/next/last/every + time*: ✗ *on next Monday* → ✓ *next Monday*.
@hi **at** = घड़ी का समय, एक बिंदु (*at 7 o'clock, at night*)। **on** = दिन और तारीख़ (*on Monday, on 15 August*)। **in** = लंबा समय – महीना, साल, मौसम, सुबह/शाम (*in June, in 2026, in the morning*)। *next, last, every, this, today, tomorrow* से पहले कोई preposition नहीं: *next Monday* (*on next Monday* गलत)।
:::

:::rule RULE – for / since, by / until, during / while, before / after, within
| Pair | Use | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **for** | a **period** of time (how long) | *I have lived here **for** five years.* |
| **since** | a **starting point** (from when) | *I have lived here **since** 2021 / **since** Monday.* |
| **by** | **not later than** a time (deadline) | *Submit the form **by** Friday.* |
| **until / till** | **up to** a time (continuing) | *The shop is open **until** 9 p.m. Wait **till** I come.* |
| **during** + noun | in the course of an event | *He slept **during** the film.* |
| **while** + clause | at the same time as | *He slept **while** the film was running.* |
| **within** | inside a period (not more than) | *We deliver **within** 24 hours.* |
| **from … to / till** | start and end | *Office hours are **from** 10 **to** 6.* |
@hi **for** = कितने समय से (*for five years* = पाँच साल से); **since** = कब से (*since 2021* = 2021 से)। **by** = तक, उससे पहले (deadline – *by Friday* = शुक्रवार तक जमा कर दें); **until/till** = तब तक लगातार (*open until 9* = 9 बजे तक खुली)। **during** + संज्ञा, **while** + पूरा clause। **within** = के अंदर।
:::

## 18.2 Prepositions of place · स्थान

:::rule RULE – at, on, in for place
| **at** – a point / a place for an activity | **on** – a surface / a line | **in** – inside / an area |
|---|---|---|
| **at** the bus stop, **at** the door | **on** the table, **on** the wall, **on** the floor | **in** the box, **in** the room, **in** the car |
| **at** home, **at** work, **at** school, **at** college | **on** the 2nd floor, **on** M.G. Road | **in** Pune, **in** Gujarat, **in** India |
| **at** the station, **at** the airport (as a point) | **on** the bus / train / plane / bike | **in** a taxi / car / auto (small vehicles) |
| **at** 25, Shanti Nagar (exact address) | **on** the page, **on** the screen, **on** the phone | **in** the newspaper, **in** a book, **in** the photo |
| **at** the top / bottom / end of | **on** the left / right | **in** the middle, **in** the corner (of a room) |
@hi **at** = एक बिंदु या काम की जगह (*at the bus stop, at home, at work*)। **on** = सतह या रेखा पर (*on the table, on the wall, on the 2nd floor*) और बड़े वाहन (*on the bus, on the train*)। **in** = अंदर या क्षेत्र (*in the box, in Pune, in India*) और छोटे वाहन (*in a car, in an auto*)।
:::

:::rule RULE – other place prepositions
| Preposition | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **above / over** | higher than (*over* = also covering / across) | *The fan is **above** the bed.* · *Put a blanket **over** the baby.* |
| **below / under** | lower than (*under* = directly below / covered) | *The cat is **under** the table.* · *The temperature is **below** zero.* |
| **next to / beside / by** | at the side of | *Sit **next to / beside** me.* · *a house **by** the sea* |
| **between** | in the middle of **two** (or clearly separate items) | *The bank is **between** the school and the post office.* |
| **among** | in a **group** (three or more, not separate) | *He was **among** the crowd.* · *Share the sweets **among** the children.* |
| **in front of / behind** | ahead of / at the back of | *There is a garden **in front of** the house and a garage **behind** it.* |
| **opposite** | facing, on the other side | *The bank is **opposite** the hospital.* |
| **near / close to / far from** | distance | *We live **near** the station, **far from** the airport.* |
@hi **above** = ऊपर (छुए बिना), **over** = ऊपर / ढकते हुए · **under** = नीचे · **next to / beside** = बगल में · **between** = दो के बीच · **among** = समूह में (तीन या ज़्यादा) · **in front of** = सामने · **behind** = पीछे · **opposite** = ठीक सामने (आमने-सामने) · **near** = पास।
:::

## 18.3 Prepositions of movement · दिशा और गति

:::rule RULE – movement
| Preposition | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **to** | towards a destination | *I go **to** college by bus.* |
| **into / out of** | moving inside / outside | *She went **into** the room. He jumped **out of** the car.* |
| **onto / off** | moving onto / away from a surface | *The cat jumped **onto** the table. Get **off** the bus.* |
| **across** | from one side to the other (a surface) | *Walk **across** the road / the bridge.* |
| **through** | from one end to the other (inside something) | *The train went **through** a tunnel. We walked **through** the forest.* |
| **along** | following a line | *Walk **along** the river / this road.* |
| **up / down** | to a higher / lower place | *Go **up** the stairs. Come **down** the hill.* |
| **past** | going by | *Walk **past** the temple and turn left.* |
| **towards** | in the direction of | *He ran **towards** me.* |
| **round / around** | in a circle | *The earth goes **round** the sun.* |
@hi **to** = की ओर / को (मंज़िल) · **into** = अंदर की ओर जाना · **out of** = बाहर निकलना · **onto** = ऊपर चढ़ना · **across** = आर-पार (सतह पर – सड़क, पुल) · **through** = बीच से होकर (सुरंग, जंगल) · **along** = किनारे-किनारे · **past** = आगे से निकलना · **towards** = की तरफ़।
:::

:::compare COMPARE & UNDERSTAND
| | |
|---|---|
| *He is **in** the room.* (position) | *He went **into** the room.* (movement) |
| *Walk **across** the road.* (surface) | *Walk **through** the park.* (inside, with things around) |
| *Divide it **between** Ravi and Amit.* (two) | *Divide it **among** the five friends.* (group) |
| ***Beside** = next to: Sit **beside** me.* | ***Besides** = in addition to: **Besides** English, she speaks French.* |
| *I'll finish it **by** 5.* (at the latest) | *I'll work **until** 5.* (continuously up to 5) |
| *I've been waiting **for** an hour.* | *I've been waiting **since** 3 o'clock.* |
@hi *in* = अंदर है, *into* = अंदर गया · *across* = सतह पर आर-पार, *through* = बीच से होकर · *between* = दो, *among* = समूह · *beside* = बगल में, *besides* = के अलावा · *by 5* = 5 बजे तक (उससे पहले), *until 5* = 5 बजे तक लगातार।
:::

## 18.4 Other uses · अन्य उपयोग

:::rule RULE – by, with, like, as, about, for
- **by** + transport / means / doer: *by bus, by email, by cheque* · *a novel **by** Premchand* · *paid **by** card*
- **with** + instrument / company: *cut it **with** a knife* · *I went **with** my sister*
- **like** = similar to: *She sings **like** a professional.* · **as** = in the role of: *She works **as** a nurse.*
- **about** = on the topic of: *a book **about** India* · *What are you talking **about**?*
- **for** = purpose / benefit: *a gift **for** you, a bus **for** Delhi*
- **on foot** (not *by foot*), **in** cash, **by** cheque / card
@hi **by** = साधन या कर्ता (*by bus, a novel by Premchand* = प्रेमचंद का उपन्यास) · **with** = औज़ार या साथ (*with a knife* = चाकू से, *with my sister* = बहन के साथ) · **like** = की तरह · **as** = के रूप में (*works as a nurse* = नर्स के रूप में काम करती है) · पैदल = **on foot**।
:::

## 18.5 Dependent prepositions · निश्चित prepositions

:::rule RULE – verb / adjective / noun + fixed preposition (learn them together!)
| Verb + prep | Adjective + prep | Noun + prep |
|---|---|---|
| **depend on**, **rely on** | **good at**, **bad at** | **reason for** |
| **listen to**, **belong to**, **reply to** | **interested in** | **solution to**, **answer to** |
| **agree with** (a person), **agree to** (a plan) | **afraid of**, **proud of**, **fond of**, **full of** | **increase / decrease in** (prices) |
| **apply for** (a job), **wait for**, **pay for**, **ask for** | **famous for**, **responsible for** | **demand for** |
| **believe in**, **succeed in**, **specialise in** | **angry with** (a person), **angry about** (a thing) | **interest in** |
| **look at**, **laugh at**, **arrive at** (place) / **in** (city) | **married to**, **similar to**, **used to** | **advantage of**, **cause of** |
| **think about / of**, **dream of**, **consist of** | **different from**, **free from**, **absent from** | **relationship with**, **problem with** |
| **congratulate on**, **insist on**, **concentrate on** | **aware of**, **capable of**, **tired of** | **respect for**, **need for** |
| **prevent from**, **suffer from**, **protect from** | **satisfied with**, **busy with**, **pleased with** | **access to**, **visit to** |
@hi ये जोड़े **साथ में** याद कीजिए: *depend on* (पर निर्भर), *listen to* (सुनना), *good at* (में अच्छा), *interested in* (में रुचि), *afraid of* (से डर), *famous for* (के लिए प्रसिद्ध), *married to* (से शादी हुई – *with* नहीं!), *different from* (से अलग), *congratulate on* (पर बधाई), *suffer from* (से पीड़ित), *reason for* (का कारण)।
:::

:::rule RULE – NO preposition after these verbs
| ✗ Wrong (extra preposition) | ✓ Right |
|---|---|
| discuss **about** the plan | **discuss** the plan |
| reach **to** the station | **reach** the station (but *arrive **at*** the station) |
| enter **into** the room | **enter** the room |
| return **back** | **return** / **come back** |
| ask **to** him | **ask** him |
| marry **with** her | **marry** her / **get married to** her |
| order **for** a pizza | **order** a pizza |
| go **to** home / come **to** home | **go home** / **come home** |
| resemble **to / with** | **resemble** (*He resembles his father.*) |
| request **for** (as a verb) | **request** something (but *a request **for*** – noun) |
@hi इन क्रियाओं के बाद **कोई preposition नहीं**: *discuss* (*discuss about* गलत), *reach* (*reach to* गलत), *enter*, *marry*, *order*, *resemble*, *go home*। हिंदी के "के बारे में", "तक", "से" के कारण ये गलतियाँ होती हैं।
:::

:::mistake COMMON MISTAKES – prepositions
| ✗ Wrong | ✓ Right | Why · क्यों |
|---|---|---|
| I was born in 15 August. | I was born **on** 15 August. | date → *on* · तारीख़ के साथ on |
| See you on next Friday. | See you **next Friday**. | no prep before *next* · next से पहले कुछ नहीं |
| I am living here since five years. | I **have been** living here **for** five years. | period → *for* (+ perfect tense) · अवधि = for |
| She is married with a doctor. | She is married **to** a doctor. | *married to* · married के साथ to |
| He is good in maths. | He is good **at** maths. | *good at* · good at |
| Let's discuss about it. | Let's **discuss** it. | no preposition · discuss के बाद कुछ नहीं |
| We reached to Delhi at night. | We **reached** Delhi at night. | no preposition · reach के बाद कुछ नहीं |
| Divide the cake between the five kids. | … **among** the five kids. | group → *among* · समूह = among |
| He jumped in the river. | He jumped **into** the river. | movement → *into* · अंदर जाना = into |
| I came by foot. | I came **on foot**. | fixed phrase · पैदल = on foot |
:::

:::tip EXAM TIP · परीक्षा टिप
Fill-in-the-blank and error questions on prepositions are among the **most frequent** in SSC, bank and state exams. Memorise the **dependent preposition table** and the **no-preposition list**. Read the whole sentence: *agree **with** someone* but *agree **to** a proposal*.
@hi Prepositions के सवाल SSC, बैंक और राज्य परीक्षाओं में सबसे ज़्यादा आते हैं। तय prepositions की तालिका और "कोई preposition नहीं" वाली सूची रट लीजिए।
:::

:::revision QUICK REVISION · जल्दी दोहराइए
- Time: **at** 7, **on** Monday / 5 May, **in** June / 2026 / the morning · no prep before *next/last/this/every*.
- **for** (period) / **since** (start) · **by** (deadline) / **until** (continuing) · **during** + noun / **while** + clause.
- Place: **at** a point, **on** a surface, **in** an area · **between** two / **among** many · **in** (position) / **into** (movement).
- Fixed: *depend on, good at, interested in, married to, different from, congratulate on, suffer from*.
- No prep: *discuss, reach, enter, marry, order, resemble, go home*.
@hi समय और जगह में at/on/in · for/since · by/until · between/among · into · तय prepositions · discuss/reach/enter के बाद कुछ नहीं।
:::
