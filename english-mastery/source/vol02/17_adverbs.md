:::warm Warm-up · शुरुआत
*She sings **beautifully**.* (how?) · *She sings **here**.* (where?) · *She sings **every day**.* (when / how often?) · *She sings **very** well.* (how much?)
@hi **Adverb (क्रिया-विशेषण)** बताता है काम **कैसे**, **कहाँ**, **कब**, **कितनी बार** या **कितना** हुआ। *beautifully* = सुंदर ढंग से, *here* = यहाँ, *every day* = रोज़, *very* = बहुत।
:::

## 17.1 What adverbs do · क्रिया-विशेषण क्या करते हैं

:::rule RULE – adverbs modify verbs, adjectives, other adverbs and whole sentences
| Modifies | Example |
|---|---|
| a **verb** | *He drives **carefully**.* |
| an **adjective** | *The film was **really** good. She is **extremely** busy.* |
| another **adverb** | *He drives **very** carefully. She speaks **quite** fast.* |
| a whole **sentence** | ***Luckily**, nobody was hurt. **Honestly**, I don't know.* |
@hi Adverb क्रिया (*drives carefully* = सावधानी से चलाता है), विशेषण (*really good* = सच में अच्छा), दूसरे adverb (*very carefully* = बहुत सावधानी से) या पूरे वाक्य (*Luckily* = क़िस्मत से) के बारे में बताता है।
:::

## 17.2 Kinds of adverbs · प्रकार

:::rule RULE – main kinds
| Kind | Question | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Manner** | how? | slowly, quickly, carefully, well, fast, hard, loudly, happily |
| **Place** | where? | here, there, inside, outside, upstairs, abroad, everywhere, nearby |
| **Time** | when? | now, today, yesterday, tomorrow, soon, later, then, recently, already, yet, still |
| **Frequency** | how often? | always, usually, often, sometimes, rarely, seldom, never, every day, twice a week |
| **Degree** | how much? to what extent? | very, too, quite, rather, fairly, extremely, almost, nearly, enough, so, hardly |
| **Sentence / comment** | speaker's view | luckily, unfortunately, honestly, actually, probably, certainly, of course |
@hi **रीति** (कैसे – *slowly*), **स्थान** (कहाँ – *here, upstairs*), **समय** (कब – *now, soon*), **आवृत्ति** (कितनी बार – *always, never*), **मात्रा** (कितना – *very, too, almost*), **टिप्पणी** (वक्ता की राय – *luckily, honestly*)।
:::

## 17.3 Forming adverbs · क्रिया-विशेषण बनाना

:::rule RULE – adjective + -ly (with spelling changes)
| Adjective | Rule | Adverb |
|---|---|---|
| slow, quick, careful, loud | + **-ly** | slowly, quickly, carefully, loudly |
| happy, easy, busy | **y → ily** | happily, easily, busily |
| simple, gentle, possible | **-le → -ly** | simply, gently, possibly |
| basic, tragic | **-ic → -ically** | basically, tragically (but *public → publicly*) |
| true, due, whole | drop **e** | truly, duly, wholly |
@hi ज़्यादातर विशेषणों में **-ly** जोड़िए: *slow → slowly* (धीमा → धीरे)। *-y* → *ily* (*happily*), *-le* → *-ly* (*simply*), *-ic* → *-ically* (*basically*)।
:::

:::rule RULE – irregular and confusing adverbs
| Adjective | Adverb | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **good** | **well** | *She is a **good** singer. She sings **well**.* (✗ *sings good*) |
| **fast** | **fast** | *a **fast** car · He drives **fast**.* (✗ *fastly*) |
| **hard** | **hard** | *a **hard** worker · He works **hard**.* · **hardly** = almost not: *I **hardly** know him.* |
| **late** | **late** | *a **late** arrival · She came **late**.* · **lately** = recently: *Have you seen him **lately**?* |
| **early** | **early** | *an **early** train · I get up **early**.* |
| **daily / weekly / monthly** | same | *a **daily** newspaper · It comes out **daily**.* |
| **friendly, lovely, lonely, silly, ugly** | – (these are **adjectives** in -ly) | use *in a friendly way*: *She smiled **in a friendly way**.* |
@hi **good** (विशेषण) → **well** (adverb): *She sings well* (*sings good* गलत)। *fast, hard, late, early* दोनों रूपों में एक जैसे – *fastly* कोई शब्द नहीं। सावधान: **hardly** = मुश्किल से / लगभग नहीं (*I hardly know him* = मैं उसे मुश्किल से जानता हूँ); **lately** = हाल में। *friendly, lovely* विशेषण हैं – adverb के लिए *in a friendly way*।
:::

## 17.4 Position of adverbs · क्रिया-विशेषण की जगह

:::rule RULE – front, mid and end position
| Position | Which adverbs | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Front** (start of the sentence) | comment and connecting adverbs; time (for emphasis) | ***Unfortunately**, the train was cancelled.* · ***Tomorrow** I'll call you.* |
| **Mid** (with the verb) | **frequency** (always, usually, often, never…), *already, just, still, almost, also, even, only, probably* | *I **always** drink tea.* · *She has **already** left.* |
| **End** | manner, place, time (order: **manner → place → time**) | *He worked **hard in the office yesterday**.* |
@hi **शुरुआत में:** टिप्पणी वाले (*Unfortunately*) और ज़ोर देने के लिए समय। **बीच में:** आवृत्ति वाले (*always, never*) और *already, just, still*। **आख़िर में:** रीति → स्थान → समय (Chapter 4 का S-V-O-P-M-T याद कीजिए)।
:::

:::rule RULE – the exact mid position
| Verb | Adverb goes … | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **be** (am/is/are/was/were) | **after** *be* | *She **is always** on time.* · *They **were never** late.* |
| one-word main verb | **before** the main verb | *I **usually get** up at 6.* · *He **never eats** meat.* |
| helping verb + main verb | **after the first helping verb** | *I **have never been** to Goa.* · *You **can always call** me.* |
| negative | frequency **after** *not* (usually) | *I **don't often** go out.* (but *I **usually don't** …* is also used) |
✗ *I go always to school by bus.* → ✓ *I **always go** to school by bus.*
✗ *He speaks very well English.* → ✓ *He speaks English **very well**.* (never put an adverb between the verb and its object)
@hi **be** के बाद (*She is always on time*), मुख्य क्रिया से पहले (*I usually get up at 6*), पहली सहायक क्रिया के बाद (*I have never been*)। adverb को **क्रिया और कर्म के बीच** कभी न रखें: *speaks English very well* ✓।
:::

## 17.5 very, too, enough, so, such; already, yet, still · ख़ास शब्द

:::rule RULE – degree words
| Word | Meaning | Pattern | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **very** | a lot (no problem) | very + adj/adv | *The tea is **very** hot.* |
| **too** | more than is good (problem) | too + adj/adv (+ to …) | *The tea is **too** hot **to drink**.* |
| **enough** | as much as needed | adj/adv + **enough** / **enough** + noun | *Is he **old enough** to vote? We have **enough time**.* |
| **so** | very (strong feeling / result) | so + adj/adv (+ that …) | *I was **so** tired **that** I slept at 8.* |
| **such** | very (with a noun) | such (a/an) + adj + noun | *It was **such a** good film! They are **such** nice people.* |
| **quite / rather / fairly** | moderately | quite + adj | *The exam was **quite** easy.* |
@hi **very** = बहुत (कोई दिक़्क़त नहीं) · **too** = ज़रूरत से ज़्यादा (समस्या!) – *too hot to drink* = इतनी गरम कि पी न सकें · **enough** = काफ़ी (विशेषण के **बाद**: *old enough*) · **so + विशेषण** / **such + (a) + विशेषण + संज्ञा** = इतना… (*so tired that…*, *such a good film*)।
:::

:::rule RULE – already, yet, still, just
| Word | Use | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **already** | sooner than expected (positive, mid) | *I have **already** paid the bill.* |
| **yet** | until now (negatives & questions, end) | *Have you finished **yet**? I haven't eaten **yet**.* |
| **still** | continuing, not finished (mid) | *She is **still** at the office. I **still** don't understand.* |
| **just** | a very short time ago (mid) | *He has **just** left.* |
@hi **already** = पहले ही (*I have already paid* = मैं पहले ही भुगतान कर चुका हूँ) · **yet** = अभी तक (नकार/सवाल में, आख़िर में – *I haven't eaten yet* = मैंने अभी तक नहीं खाया) · **still** = अब भी (*She is still at the office* = वह अब भी ऑफ़िस में है) · **just** = अभी-अभी।
:::

:::compare COMPARE & UNDERSTAND
| | |
|---|---|
| *He works **hard**.* (with effort) | *He **hardly** works.* (almost never works!) |
| *I came home **late**.* (not on time) | *I haven't seen her **lately**.* (recently) |
| *The box is **too** heavy for me.* (I can't lift it) | *The box is **very** heavy.* (but maybe I can lift it) |
| *It was **so** cold.* (+ adjective) | *It was **such** a cold day.* (+ noun) |
@hi *hard* = मेहनत से / *hardly* = मुश्किल से ही; *late* = देर से / *lately* = हाल में; *too heavy* = इतना भारी कि उठा न सकूँ / *very heavy* = बहुत भारी; *so* + विशेषण / *such* + संज्ञा।
:::

:::mistake COMMON MISTAKES – adverbs
| ✗ Wrong | ✓ Right | Why · क्यों |
|---|---|---|
| She sings very good. | She sings very **well**. | verb + adverb · क्रिया के साथ well |
| He drives very fastly. | He drives very **fast**. | *fast* is the adverb · fastly शब्द नहीं |
| I go always to the gym. | I **always go** to the gym. | frequency before main verb · क्रिया से पहले |
| She speaks fluently English. | She speaks English **fluently**. | not between verb and object · क्रिया-कर्म के बीच नहीं |
| He is enough old to drive. | He is **old enough** to drive. | adjective + enough · विशेषण के बाद enough |
| It is very hot to drink. | It is **too** hot to drink. | *too … to* = so much that not · too…to |
| It was so a nice day. | It was **such a** nice day. | *such a* + adj + noun · संज्ञा के साथ such |
| I have finished my work yet. | I have **already** finished my work. | positive → *already* · सकारात्मक में already |
| I hardly worked and passed the exam. (meaning: with effort) | I worked **hard** and passed the exam. | *hardly* = almost not · hardly का अर्थ उल्टा |
:::

:::tip EXAM TIP · परीक्षा टिप
Watch for: **good/well**, **hard/hardly**, **late/lately**, **too/very**, **so/such**, **enough** position, **already/yet**, and frequency adverbs **after be** but **before** the main verb.
@hi परीक्षा में: good/well, hard/hardly, late/lately, too/very, so/such, enough की जगह, already/yet, और आवृत्ति वाले adverb की सही जगह।
:::

:::revision QUICK REVISION · जल्दी दोहराइए
- Adverbs tell **how, where, when, how often, how much**.
- adj + **-ly** · **good → well** · *fast, hard, late, early* (same form) · *hardly, lately* = different meanings.
- Frequency: **after be**, **before the main verb**, **after the first helping verb**. Never between verb and object.
- **very** (a lot) · **too** (problem) · **adj + enough** · **so + adj** · **such (a) + adj + noun**.
- **already** (+) · **yet** (– / ?) · **still** (continuing) · **just** (a moment ago).
@hi adverb = कैसे/कहाँ/कब/कितनी बार/कितना · good→well · hard/hardly · always की जगह · too/very · so/such · already/yet/still।
:::
