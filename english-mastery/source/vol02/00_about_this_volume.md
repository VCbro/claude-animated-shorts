:::goals What you will learn in this volume · इस volume में आप क्या सीखेंगे
In Volume 1 you met the **8 parts of speech** for the first time (Chapter 4). In this volume you study each one in detail: **nouns, pronouns, articles, determiners, adjectives, adverbs, prepositions, conjunctions and interjections** – the words that make up every English sentence.

@hi Volume 1 (Chapter 4) में आपने 8 शब्द-भेदों की पहली झलक देखी थी। इस volume में हर शब्द-भेद को विस्तार से सीखेंगे – संज्ञा, सर्वनाम, a/an/the, some/any जैसे शब्द, विशेषण, क्रिया-विशेषण, संबंधसूचक, समुच्चयबोधक और विस्मयादिबोधक। इन्हीं से हर English वाक्य बनता है।
:::

:::practice Quick check – Volume 1 (5 minutes) · Volume 1 की छोटी जाँच
1. Choose the correct phrase.
 (a) a hour (b) an university (c) an honest man (d) a umbrella
2. Choose the correct order.
 (a) I every day drink milk. (b) I drink milk every day. (c) I drink every day milk. (d) Milk I drink every day.
3. ______ your brother live in Pune?
 (a) Do (b) Is (c) Does (d) Are
4. Which group of words is a **phrase**?
 (a) the shop opens at nine (b) because it rained (c) in the old house (d) when she came
5. Choose the correct sentence.
 (a) She don't like tea. (b) She doesn't likes tea. (c) She doesn't like tea. (d) She not like tea.
6. Choose the correct sentence.
 (a) I am having two sisters. (b) I have two sisters. (c) I has two sisters. (d) I am have two sisters.
7. Choose the correct sentence.
 (a) It's tail is long. (b) Its tail is long. (c) Its' tail is long. (d) It tail is long.
8. There ______ many people at the station.
 (a) is (b) was (c) were (d) has
:::

## How to study this volume · यह volume कैसे पढ़ें

:::tip Study plan · पढ़ाई की योजना
- Study **one chapter in 2 days**: Day 1 – rules, examples, mistakes; Day 2 – practice and answer key.
- After Chapters 9–14 (nouns to determiners), make a **one-page summary** in your notebook.
- Take the **Part B Revision Quiz** at the end of the volume. Aim for **70%** or more.
- Keep a **"My Mistakes" page**: write every wrong answer and its correct form.

:::

:::ans Answer Key – Quick check · उत्तर
**1-c** *honest* में h silent है → *an*। (Ch 1)
**2-b** कर्ता + क्रिया + कर्म + समय। (Ch 4)
**3-c** *your brother* = he → *Does*। (Ch 7)
**4-c** कर्ता + क्रिया नहीं → phrase। (Ch 5)
**5-c** *doesn't* + मूल क्रिया। (Ch 7)
**6-b** "पास होना" में -ing नहीं। (Ch 8)
**7-b** *its* = उसका (बिना apostrophe)। (Ch 3)
**8-c** *many people* बहुवचन → *were*। (Ch 8)
