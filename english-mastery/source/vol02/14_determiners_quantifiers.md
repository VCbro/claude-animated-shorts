:::warm Warm-up · शुरुआत
**(a)** *I have **a few** friends in Delhi.* 🙂 **(b)** *I have **few** friends in Delhi.* 😟
One small word *a* changes the feeling: (a) = some friends – good! (b) = almost no friends – sad.
@hi (a) *a few friends* = कुछ दोस्त हैं (अच्छी बात) · (b) *few friends* = लगभग कोई दोस्त नहीं (दुख की बात)। छोटा-सा *a* पूरा भाव बदल देता है।
:::

## 14.1 What is a determiner? · निर्धारक क्या है?

:::rule RULE – the word before a noun that tells "which / how many / how much / whose"
| Type | Examples |
|---|---|
| articles | a, an, the |
| demonstratives | this, that, these, those |
| possessives | my, your, his, her, its, our, their, Ravi's |
| quantifiers | some, any, much, many, few, little, all, both, each, every, either, neither, no, enough, several |
| numbers | one, two, first, second |
**Use only one** of the main determiners before a noun: ✗ *the my book* ✗ *a this pen* → ✓ *my book*, ✓ *this pen*, ✓ *a friend of mine*.
@hi **Determiner** = संज्ञा से पहले आने वाला शब्द जो बताए कौन-सा, कितना, कितने या किसका। एक संज्ञा से पहले एक ही मुख्य determiner: *the my book* गलत, *my book* सही।
:::

## 14.2 Some and any · some और any

:::rule RULE – some vs any
| | **some** | **any** |
|---|---|---|
| Positive statements | *I have **some** money. There are **some** eggs.* | – |
| Negatives | – | *I don't have **any** money. There aren't **any** eggs.* |
| Questions | **offers & requests** (you expect *yes*): *Would you like **some** tea? Can I have **some** water?* | **real questions**: *Do you have **any** questions? Is there **any** milk?* |
| Special meaning | – | **any = it doesn't matter which**: *Take **any** seat. Call me **any** time.* |
Both work with **plural** and **uncountable** nouns: *some books, some milk; any books, any milk*.
@hi **some** = कुछ – सकारात्मक वाक्यों में और जब कुछ पेश कर रहे हों या माँग रहे हों (*Would you like some tea?* = थोड़ी चाय लेंगे?)। **any** = कोई / कुछ भी – नकारात्मक और सामान्य सवालों में (*Do you have any questions?*)। सकारात्मक वाक्य में *any* = "कोई भी, फ़र्क़ नहीं पड़ता" (*Take any seat* = कोई भी सीट ले लो)।
:::

## 14.3 Much, many, a lot of · much, many, a lot of

:::rule RULE – amount and number
| | Countable (plural) | Uncountable |
|---|---|---|
| Questions | **How many** people? | **How much** time? |
| Negatives | *not **many** people* | *not **much** time* |
| Positive (everyday) | ***a lot of / lots of** people* | ***a lot of / lots of** time* |
| Positive (formal) | ***many** people* | ***a great deal of** time* (*much* is rare in positive) |
| Too / so | ***too many** cars, **so many** questions* | ***too much** noise, **so much** work* |
@hi **many** = गिनने वाली चीज़ें (बहुत सारे), **much** = न गिनी जाने वाली (बहुत सारा)। सवाल और नकार में *many/much*; सकारात्मक बोलचाल में **a lot of** दोनों के साथ। *too much noise* = बहुत ज़्यादा शोर, *too many cars* = बहुत ज़्यादा गाड़ियाँ।
:::

## 14.4 Few / a few, little / a little · बहुत बड़ा फ़र्क़

:::rule RULE – the meaning changes with "a"
| | Countable (plural) | Uncountable | Meaning / feeling |
|---|---|---|---|
| with **a** | **a few** friends | **a little** money | **some** – enough, positive 🙂 |
| without **a** | **few** friends | **little** money | **almost none** – not enough, negative 😟 |
| with **the** | **the few** friends (that) I have | **the little** money (that) I had | **all of the small number/amount** |
| with **quite** | **quite a few** = many | – | (informal) a fairly large number |
@hi **a few / a little** = थोड़े/थोड़ा है – सकारात्मक भाव (*I have a little money* = मेरे पास थोड़े पैसे हैं, काम चल जाएगा)। **few / little** = लगभग नहीं – नकारात्मक भाव (*I have little money* = मेरे पास पैसे लगभग नहीं हैं)। **the few / the little** = जो थोड़े-से थे, वे सब (*I spent the little money I had* = जो थोड़े पैसे थे, सब ख़र्च कर दिए)।
:::

:::example EXAMPLES
- *Hurry up! We have **a little** time.* (some time – we can still make it) / *We have **little** time.* (almost none – it's urgent)
- ***Few** people understood the joke.* (almost nobody laughed)
- *He gave **the few** books he had to the library.* (all of them)
@hi अर्थ: जल्दी करो! थोड़ा समय है / समय लगभग नहीं है। · बहुत कम लोग चुटकुला समझे। · उसके पास जो थोड़ी-सी किताबें थीं, सब library को दे दीं।
:::

## 14.5 Each, every, all, both, either, neither, none · हर एक, सब, दोनों, कोई भी नहीं

:::rule RULE – group words
| Word | Meaning | Noun / verb | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **each** | every one **separately** (2 or more) | singular noun, singular verb | ***Each** student **has** a locker.* · *two pens **each*** |
| **every** | all, seen as a group (3 or more) | singular noun, singular verb | ***Every** room **is** clean.* · ***every** day, **every** 2 hours* |
| **all** | the whole number/amount | plural/uncountable, plural/singular verb | ***All** the students **are** here.* · ***All** the milk **is** gone.* |
| **both** | the two together | plural noun, plural verb | ***Both** (the) brothers **are** doctors.* |
| **either** | one **or** the other of two | singular noun | *You can sit on **either** side.* |
| **neither** | **not** one and **not** the other of two | singular noun, singular verb (formal) | ***Neither** answer **is** correct.* |
| **no / none** | not any | **no** + noun; **none** alone or *none of* | *There is **no** sugar. **None** of the shops **is/are** open.* |
@hi **each** = हर एक, अलग-अलग (*Each student has a locker* = हर छात्र का अपना लॉकर है) · **every** = हर (समूह के रूप में) · **all** = सब · **both** = दोनों (बहुवचन) · **either** = दोनों में से कोई एक · **neither** = दोनों में से कोई नहीं · **no / none** = कोई नहीं। *each/every/either/neither* के साथ **एकवचन संज्ञा और क्रिया**।
:::

:::rule RULE – other quantifiers
- **several** (more than two, not many): *I called **several** times.*
- **enough** (as much as needed) – before a noun: ***enough** chairs, **enough** money* · after an adjective: *old **enough**, big **enough***.
- **plenty of** (more than enough): *We have **plenty of** time / food.*
- **a number of** + plural verb (many): ***A number of** students **were** absent.* · **the number of** + singular verb: ***The number of** students **is** 40.*
- **another** (= one more / a different one, + singular): ***another** cup of tea* · **other** (+ plural / with *the*): ***other** people, **the other** side* · **others** (pronoun, no noun): *Some like tea; **others** prefer coffee.*
@hi **several** = कई · **enough** = काफ़ी (संज्ञा से पहले, पर विशेषण के बाद: *old enough* = काफ़ी बड़ा) · **plenty of** = भरपूर · **a number of** = बहुत से (बहुवचन क्रिया) / **the number of** = की संख्या (एकवचन क्रिया) · **another** = एक और / दूसरा (एकवचन) · **other** + बहुवचन संज्ञा · **others** = दूसरे लोग (अकेला)।
:::

:::compare COMPARE & UNDERSTAND – confusing pairs
| | |
|---|---|
| ***Each** of my two hands has five fingers.* (two → *each*, not *every*) | ***Every** student in India knows this.* (large group) |
| ***Either** day is fine for me.* (one of the two – OK) | ***Neither** day is fine.* (none of the two) |
| ***Another** student came.* (one more) | ***Other** students came.* (more, plural) · ***The other** student left.* (the second of two) |
| ***A number of** shops **are** closed.* (many) | ***The number of** shops **is** growing.* (the count) |
@hi दो चीज़ों के लिए *each* (हर एक), *every* नहीं। *either* = कोई भी एक (ठीक है), *neither* = कोई भी नहीं। *another* = एक और, *other* + बहुवचन, *the other* = दोनों में से दूसरा।
:::

:::mistake COMMON MISTAKES – determiners
| ✗ Wrong | ✓ Right | Why · क्यों |
|---|---|---|
| I don't have some money. | I don't have **any** money. | negative → *any* · नकार में any |
| How much students are there? | How **many** students …? | countable → *many* · गिने जाने वाले → many |
| There is too many traffic. | There is too **much** traffic. | uncountable → *much* · uncountable → much |
| Every students has a book. | Every **student has** a book. | *every* + singular · every के बाद एकवचन |
| Each of the boys have a bike. | Each of the boys **has** a bike. | *each* → singular verb · each → has |
| Both of them is coming. | Both of them **are** coming. | *both* → plural · both → बहुवचन |
| I have less friends than him. | I have **fewer** friends … | *fewer* + countable (*less* + uncountable) · गिने जाने वालों के लिए fewer |
| He gave me an another chance. | He gave me **another** chance. | *another* already has *an* · another में an पहले से |
| The my bag is blue. | **My** bag is blue. | one determiner only · एक ही determiner |
| I am not enough tall. | I am not **tall enough**. | *enough* after an adjective · विशेषण के बाद enough |
:::

:::tip EXAM TIP · परीक्षा टिप
SSC/bank questions repeatedly test: **few vs a few vs the few**, **little vs a little**, **much/many**, **each/every + singular**, **a number of / the number of**, **fewer/less**. Read for **meaning** (positive or negative?) before choosing.
@hi परीक्षा में बार-बार: few / a few / the few, little / a little, much / many, each/every + एकवचन, a number of / the number of, fewer / less। पहले भाव समझिए – सकारात्मक है या नकारात्मक?
:::

:::revision QUICK REVISION · जल्दी दोहराइए
- **some** (+, offers) · **any** (–, ?, "any one at all").
- **many** + countable · **much** + uncountable · **a lot of** both.
- **a few / a little** = some (+) · **few / little** = almost none (–) · **the few / the little** = all of a small number.
- **each / every / either / neither** + singular · **both** + plural · **a number of** + plural verb · **the number of** + singular verb.
- **another** + singular · **other** + plural · **others** alone · **fewer** (count) vs **less** (amount).
@hi some/any · many/much · a few (कुछ) बनाम few (लगभग नहीं) · each/every + एकवचन · both + बहुवचन · another/other/others · fewer/less।
:::

 (a) little (b) a little (c) few (d) a few

 (a) a few (b) few (c) a little (d) little
6. She spent ______ money she had on books.
 (a) little (b) a little (c) the little (d) few
7. ______ child needs love and care.
 (a) All (b) Every (c) Both (d) Several
8. Each of the players ______ a medal.
 (a) get (b) are getting (c) gets (d) have got
9. ______ my parents are teachers.
 (a) Either (b) Both (c) Each (d) Every
10. ______ of the two answers is correct; both are wrong.
 (a) Either (b) Neither (c) Both (d) None
11. ______ number of people were injured in the accident.
 (a) The (b) A (c) An (d) no article
12. Can I have ______ cup of coffee, please?
 (a) other (b) another (c) others (d) an another
13. There are ______ cars on the road today than yesterday.
 (a) less (b) fewer (c) little (d) much
14. Is the water warm ______ for a bath?
 (a) enough (b) plenty (c) many (d) several
