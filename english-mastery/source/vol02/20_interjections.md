## 20.1 What is an interjection? · विस्मयादिबोधक क्या है?

:::rule RULE – a word or short phrase that shows sudden feeling
- An **interjection** expresses a **sudden feeling or reaction**. It is **not grammatically connected** to the rest of the sentence.
- It usually comes at the **beginning**.
- **Punctuation**: strong feeling → **exclamation mark**: ***Wow!** What a view!* · mild feeling → **comma**: ***Oh,** I forgot.* / ***Well,** let me think.*
@hi **Interjection** अचानक आई भावना या प्रतिक्रिया दिखाता है और व्याकरण में बाक़ी वाक्य से जुड़ा नहीं होता। आम तौर पर शुरुआत में आता है। तेज़ भावना → **!** (*Wow! What a view!*), हल्की भावना → **comma** (*Oh, I forgot.*)।
:::

## 20.2 Common interjections by feeling · भावना के अनुसार

:::rule RULE – interjections and their meanings
| Feeling | Interjections | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Joy / celebration** | Hurray! / Hurrah! / Yay! / Yippee! | ***Hurray!** We won the match!* |
| **Surprise / wonder** | Wow! / Oh! / Oh my God! / Really? / Whoa! | ***Wow!** Your house is beautiful.* |
| **Pain** | Ouch! / Ow! / Ah! | ***Ouch!** That hurts.* |
| **Sorrow / sympathy** | Alas! (old, literary) / Oh no! / Oh dear! | ***Oh no!** I missed the train.* · ***Alas!** He is no more.* |
| **Disgust / dislike** | Ugh! / Yuck! / Eww! / Pooh! | ***Yuck!** This milk has gone sour.* |
| **Mistake / small accident** | Oops! / Whoops! / Uh-oh! | ***Oops!** I sent the message to the wrong group.* |
| **Relief** | Phew! | ***Phew!** The exam is finally over.* |
| **Attention / calling** | Hey! / Excuse me! / Look! / Listen! | ***Hey!** Wait for me!* |
| **Greeting / goodbye** | Hello! / Hi! / Bye! / Cheers! (BrE) | ***Hi**, how are you?* |
| **Hesitation / thinking** | Um… / Er… / Hmm… / Well… | ***Hmm**, let me check my calendar.* |
| **Agreement / disagreement** | Yes! / Yeah / OK / Sure! / No way! / Nah | ***Sure!** I'll help you.* · ***No way!** That's too expensive.* |
| **Praise / encouragement** | Bravo! / Well done! / Good job! / Way to go! | ***Well done!** You topped the class.* |
| **Silence / quiet** | Shh! / Hush! | ***Shh!** The baby is sleeping.* |
@hi **ख़ुशी:** Hurray!, Yay! (वाह!) · **हैरानी:** Wow!, Oh! (अरे!) · **दर्द:** Ouch! (आह!) · **दुख:** Alas! (हाय!), Oh no! · **घृणा:** Ugh!, Yuck! (छी!) · **गलती:** Oops! (उफ़!) · **राहत:** Phew! (चलो!) · **ध्यान खींचना:** Hey! · **सोचना:** Hmm…, Well… · **शाबाशी:** Bravo!, Well done! · **चुप:** Shh!
:::

:::dialogue DIALOGUE – a day at the market
**Neha:** ***Wow!*** Look at these mangoes. They're huge!
**Shopkeeper:** ₹200 a dozen, madam.
**Neha:** ***What!*** That's too much. ***Hmm…*** ₹150?
**Shopkeeper:** ***Oh,*** OK, madam. For you, ₹160.
**Neha:** ***Great!*** ***Oops!*** I forgot my bag. Can you give me a carry bag?
**Shopkeeper:** ***Sure!***
@hi अर्थ: नेहा – वाह! ये आम देखो, कितने बड़े हैं! · दुकानदार – ₹200 दर्जन। · नेहा – क्या! बहुत ज़्यादा है। हम्म… ₹150? · दुकानदार – अच्छा, आपके लिए ₹160। · नेहा – बढ़िया! उफ़! थैला भूल गई। एक carry bag दे देंगे? · दुकानदार – ज़रूर!
:::

## 20.3 Formal and informal use · औपचारिक और अनौपचारिक

:::rule RULE – where to use interjections
| Situation | Use interjections? |
|---|---|
| Speaking with friends and family, chats, stories, dialogue | ✓ Yes – natural and expressive |
| Formal emails, applications, essays, reports, exam answers | ✗ Avoid (except in a quoted dialogue or a story) |
| Polite formal alternatives | *Wow!* → *That is impressive.* · *Oops!* → *I apologise for the error.* · *Yay!* → *I am delighted to …* |
**Chat & text**: *lol, omg, ugh, yay, hmm* are common in WhatsApp but **not** in formal writing.
@hi दोस्तों से बात, chat, कहानी और संवाद में interjections स्वाभाविक हैं। **औपचारिक email, आवेदन, निबंध, report और परीक्षा** में इनसे बचिए। *Oops!* की जगह औपचारिक रूप: *I apologise for the error* (गलती के लिए क्षमा चाहता हूँ)।
:::

:::compare COMPARE & UNDERSTAND – similar interjections, different feelings
| | |
|---|---|
| ***Oh!*** – mild surprise / realising: *Oh, it's you!* | ***Oh no!*** – something bad happened: *Oh no, I lost my wallet!* |
| ***Wow!*** – amazed, positive | ***Whoa!*** – surprised, slow down: *Whoa! Drive slowly!* |
| ***Oops!*** – small mistake (by you) | ***Uh-oh!*** – you see a problem is coming |
| ***Ah!*** – understanding: *Ah, now I get it.* | ***Aha!*** – discovery / caught you: *Aha! I found the key.* |
| ***Hey!*** – calling / friendly (informal) | ***Excuse me!*** – calling politely (formal) |
@hi *Oh!* = अरे! (हल्की हैरानी), *Oh no!* = अरे नहीं! (बुरी ख़बर) · *Wow!* = वाह! (तारीफ़), *Whoa!* = रुको! (हैरानी) · *Oops!* = उफ़! (छोटी गलती), *Uh-oh!* = लगता है गड़बड़ होने वाली है · *Hey!* अनौपचारिक, *Excuse me!* विनम्र।
:::

:::note NOTE – Indian, British and American habits
- Indian English often uses *Arre!*, *Accha!*, *Oho!*, *Hai!* in speech; in standard English use ***Oh!***, ***I see!***, ***Oh dear!***.
- *Cheers!* = thanks / goodbye in British English; in India and the US it is mostly used for a toast.
- *Alas!* sounds old and literary – use *Unfortunately* or *Oh no* in modern English.
@hi भारतीय बोलचाल के *अरे!, अच्छा!, ओहो!* की जगह standard English में *Oh!, I see!, Oh dear!*। *Alas!* पुराना साहित्यिक शब्द है – आज *Unfortunately* बोलिए।
:::

:::mistake COMMON MISTAKES – interjections
| ✗ Wrong | ✓ Right | Why · क्यों |
|---|---|---|
| Wow what a beautiful dress. | **Wow!** What a beautiful dress**!** | strong feeling → ! · भावना के बाद ! |
| Hurray we won the cup! | **Hurray!** We won the cup! / **Hurray,** we won… | separate the interjection · अलग कीजिए |
| Dear sir, Oops! I made a mistake in the form. | Dear Sir, **I apologise** for an error in the form. | too informal for an email · औपचारिक email में नहीं |
| Alas! I won the lottery! | **Hurray!** I won the lottery! | *Alas* = sorrow · Alas दुख के लिए |
| Ouch! What a lovely song! | **Wow!** What a lovely song! | *Ouch* = pain · Ouch दर्द के लिए |
| Wow!!!!! | Wow! | one ! in writing · एक ही ! |
:::

:::tip EXAM TIP · परीक्षा टिप
School exams ask you to **identify** the interjection, **match** it with a feeling (*Alas* – sorrow, *Hurray* – joy, *Bravo* – praise, *Ouch* – pain), or **punctuate** a sentence. Competitive exams rarely test them directly, but exclamatory sentences (Ch 6) are common.
@hi School परीक्षाओं में: interjection पहचानना, भावना से मिलाना (*Alas* – दुख, *Hurray* – ख़ुशी, *Bravo* – शाबाशी, *Ouch* – दर्द) और विराम-चिह्न लगाना।
:::

:::revision QUICK REVISION · जल्दी दोहराइए
- Interjection = sudden feeling, usually at the start, not grammatically linked.
- Strong → **!** · mild → **,**
- Joy: Hurray, Yay · Surprise: Wow, Oh · Pain: Ouch · Sorrow: Alas, Oh no · Disgust: Ugh, Yuck · Mistake: Oops · Relief: Phew · Praise: Bravo, Well done · Quiet: Shh.
- Natural in speech and chats; **avoid** in formal writing.
@hi अचानक की भावना · ! या comma · हर भावना के शब्द · बोलचाल में हाँ, औपचारिक लेखन में नहीं।
:::
