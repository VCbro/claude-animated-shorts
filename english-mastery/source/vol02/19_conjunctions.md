# Conjunctions
<!-- level: Intermediate -->
<!-- hi: समुच्चयबोधक / योजक शब्द – and, but, because, although… -->
<!-- desc: Coordinating conjunctions (FANBOYS) and commas; correlative pairs (both…and, either…or, neither…nor, not only…but also, whether…or); subordinating conjunctions of time, reason, contrast, condition, purpose and result; because vs because of, although vs despite; no double conjunctions (although…but) -->

:::goals Learning Goals · सीखने के लक्ष्य – इस chapter के बाद आप
- join words and sentences with **and, but, or, so, yet, for, nor**;
  <span class="hl">and, but, or, so से शब्द और वाक्य जोड़ेंगे</span>
- use **pairs** like *both … and, either … or, neither … nor, not only … but also*;
  <span class="hl">जोड़ी वाले योजक सही लगाएँगे</span>
- use **because, although, when, if, unless, so that** to make complex sentences;
  <span class="hl">because, although, if, unless से मिश्र वाक्य बनाएँगे</span>
- choose **because / because of**, **although / despite**;
  <span class="hl">because और because of, although और despite में चुनाव करेंगे</span>
- avoid **double conjunctions** like *Although … but*.
  <span class="hl">*Although … but* जैसी दोहरी गलती से बचेंगे</span>
:::

:::warm Warm-up · शुरुआत
*It was raining. We went out.* → *It was raining, **but** we went out.* → ***Although** it was raining, we went out.*
@hi **Conjunction (योजक)** दो शब्दों, वाक्यांशों या वाक्यों को **जोड़ता** है और उनके बीच का **संबंध** बताता है – और, लेकिन, क्योंकि, हालाँकि, अगर।
:::

## 19.1 Coordinating conjunctions – FANBOYS · समान स्तर के योजक

:::rule RULE – For, And, Nor, But, Or, Yet, So
| Conjunction | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **and** | addition | *I bought milk **and** bread.* · *She sings, **and** her brother plays the tabla.* |
| **but** | contrast | *The phone is good, **but** it is expensive.* |
| **or** | choice / alternative | *Do you want tea **or** coffee?* · *Hurry up, **or** you'll miss the bus.* (= otherwise) |
| **so** | result | *It was late, **so** we took a taxi.* |
| **yet** | surprising contrast (= but still) | *He is rich, **yet** he lives simply.* |
| **for** | reason (formal, old style) | *She was tired, **for** she had worked all day.* |
| **nor** | and not (after a negative) | *He doesn't smoke, **nor** does he drink.* (note the question order after *nor*) |
**Comma rule:** when FANBOYS joins **two full sentences**, a comma usually comes **before** it (*…, so we took a taxi*). No comma for short words: *tea or coffee*.
@hi **and** = और · **but** = लेकिन · **or** = या / वरना · **so** = इसलिए · **yet** = फिर भी · **for** = क्योंकि (पुरानी, औपचारिक शैली) · **nor** = और न ही। दो पूरे वाक्य जोड़ें तो योजक से पहले comma।
:::

## 19.2 Correlative conjunctions (pairs) · जोड़ी वाले योजक

:::rule RULE – pairs and verb agreement
| Pair | Meaning | Example | Verb agrees with … |
|---|---|---|---|
| **both … and** | the two | ***Both** Ravi **and** Amit **are** absent.* | plural |
| **either … or** | one of the two | ***Either** you **or** your sister **has** to come.* | the **nearer** subject |
| **neither … nor** | not this and not that | ***Neither** the manager **nor** the clerks **were** there.* | the **nearer** subject |
| **not only … but also** | not just this, also that | *She is **not only** smart **but also** hardworking.* | the nearer subject |
| **whether … or** | it doesn't matter which / doubt | *I don't know **whether** he will come **or** not.* | – |
| **as … as / so … that / such … that** | comparison / result | *He was **so** tired **that** he slept early.* | – |
**Parallel structure:** put the **same kind of word** after each part: ✗ *She is **not only** smart **but also** works hard.* → ✓ *She **not only** is smart **but also** works hard.* / *She is **not only** smart **but also** hardworking.*
@hi **both…and** = दोनों (बहुवचन क्रिया) · **either…or** = या तो…या · **neither…nor** = न…न · **not only…but also** = न केवल…बल्कि · **whether…or** = …या नहीं। *either/neither* में क्रिया **पास वाले** कर्ता से मेल खाती है। दोनों हिस्सों के बाद **एक जैसा** शब्द-भेद रखिए (parallel)।
:::

## 19.3 Subordinating conjunctions · आश्रित उपवाक्य जोड़ने वाले योजक

:::rule RULE – they start a dependent clause (Chapter 5)
| Meaning | Conjunctions | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Time** | when, while, before, after, as soon as, until/till, since, whenever, once | *Call me **as soon as** you reach.* · *Wait here **until** I come back.* |
| **Reason** | because, as, since, now that | *I stayed home **because** I was ill.* · ***As** it was late, we left.* |
| **Contrast** | although, though, even though, whereas, while | ***Although** he is rich, he is not happy.* |
| **Condition** | if, unless (= if not), provided (that), in case, as long as | ***Unless** you hurry, you will miss the bus.* |
| **Purpose** | so that, in order that | *Speak slowly **so that** everyone can understand.* |
| **Result** | so … that, such … that | *It was **so** hot **that** we stayed in.* |
| **Place / manner** | where, wherever, as, as if, like (informal) | *Sit **wherever** you like.* · *He talks **as if** he knows everything.* |
@hi **समय:** when (जब), while (जबकि/दौरान), as soon as (जैसे ही), until (जब तक) · **कारण:** because, as, since (क्योंकि) · **विरोध:** although, though (हालाँकि), whereas (जबकि) · **शर्त:** if (अगर), unless (जब तक नहीं / अगर नहीं), in case (अगर ऐसा हो तो) · **उद्देश्य:** so that (ताकि) · **परिणाम:** so…that (इतना…कि)।
:::

:::rule RULE – special points
1. **unless = if … not** – don't add another *not*: ✗ *Unless you don't study, you will fail.* → ✓ ***Unless** you study, you will fail.* = ***If** you do**n't** study, …*
2. **No future tense after time/condition conjunctions**: ✗ *when I will reach* → ✓ *I'll call you **when I reach**.* · ✓ *If it **rains**, …*
3. **One conjunction per link**: ✗ *Although he is poor, **but** he is honest.* → ✓ ***Although** he is poor, he is honest.* / *He is poor, **but** he is honest.* Also ✗ *Because … so*.
4. **lest** (formal) = so that … not, followed by **should**: *Walk carefully **lest** you **should** fall.* (✗ *lest you should not fall*)
5. **No sooner … than**, **Hardly / Scarcely … when** (formal time pairs): *I had **no sooner** reached **than** it started raining.*
@hi (1) **unless** में "नहीं" पहले से है – फिर से *not* मत लगाइए। (2) *when, if, as soon as* के बाद भविष्य काल (*will*) नहीं: *when I reach*। (3) एक ही जोड़ के लिए दो योजक नहीं: *Although … but* गलत। (4) *lest* के बाद *should*, *not* नहीं। (5) *No sooner … than*, *Hardly … when* (औपचारिक जोड़े)।
:::

:::compare COMPARE & UNDERSTAND – conjunction + clause vs preposition + noun
| Conjunction + **clause** (subject + verb) | Preposition + **noun / -ing** |
|---|---|
| ***because** it was raining* | ***because of** the rain* |
| ***although** he was ill* | ***despite / in spite of** his illness* · *despite **being** ill* |
| ***while** we were travelling* | ***during** the journey* |
| ***before** I left* | ***before** leaving* (also a preposition) |
✗ *Despite of the rain* → ✓ *Despite the rain* / *In spite of the rain*.
@hi योजक के बाद **पूरा उपवाक्य** (कर्ता + क्रिया): *because it was raining*। Preposition के बाद **संज्ञा या -ing**: *because of the rain, despite the rain*। *despite of* गलत – या *despite* या *in spite of*।
:::

:::example EXAMPLES – real-life sentences
- *I'll book the tickets **as soon as** I get my salary.* (time)
- *Take an umbrella **in case** it rains.* (precaution)
- *You can borrow my bike **as long as** you return it by 6.* (condition)
- *My brother loves cricket, **whereas** I prefer football.* (contrast)
- *She saved money **so that** she could buy a laptop.* (purpose)
@hi अर्थ: तनख़्वाह मिलते ही टिकट बुक कर दूँगा। · बारिश हो जाए तो, इसलिए छाता ले लो। · शाम 6 बजे तक लौटा दो, तो मेरी बाइक ले सकते हो। · भाई को क्रिकेट पसंद है, जबकि मुझे फ़ुटबॉल। · उसने पैसे बचाए ताकि laptop ख़रीद सके।
:::

:::mistake COMMON MISTAKES – conjunctions
| ✗ Wrong | ✓ Right | Why · क्यों |
|---|---|---|
| Although it was late, but we continued. | **Although** it was late, we continued. | one conjunction only · एक ही योजक |
| Because he was ill, so he didn't come. | **Because** he was ill, he didn't come. | one conjunction only · because…so दोनों नहीं |
| Unless you don't work hard, you won't pass. | **Unless** you work hard, you won't pass. | *unless* = if not · unless में not नहीं |
| I'll call you when I will reach. | I'll call you when I **reach**. | no *will* after *when* · when के बाद will नहीं |
| Despite of the traffic, we were on time. | **Despite** the traffic, … | *despite* without *of* · despite के साथ of नहीं |
| Neither he or his wife came. | Neither he **nor** his wife came. | *neither … nor* · neither के साथ nor |
| Both Ravi as well as Amit came. | **Both** Ravi **and** Amit came. | *both … and* · both के साथ and |
| No sooner did I reach then the bus left. | No sooner did I reach **than** the bus left. | *no sooner … than* · than, then नहीं |
:::

:::tip EXAM TIP · परीक्षा टिप
Error spotting: **Although…but**, **Because…so**, **Unless…not**, **when/if + will**, **despite of**, **neither…or**, **both…as well as**, **no sooner…then / hardly…than** (should be *than / when*), **lest…not**. Also check **parallel structure** after correlatives.
@hi परीक्षा में सबसे आम: Although…but, Because…so, Unless…not, when + will, despite of, neither…or, both…as well as, no sooner…then, lest…not, और जोड़ी वाले योजकों के बाद parallel structure।
:::

:::revision QUICK REVISION · जल्दी दोहराइए
- **FANBOYS**: for, and, nor, but, or, yet, so (comma before when joining two sentences).
- Pairs: **both…and** (plural) · **either…or / neither…nor** (verb with nearer subject) · **not only…but also** (parallel).
- Subordinators: time, reason, contrast, condition (**unless = if not**), purpose (**so that**), result (**so…that**).
- **because / although** + clause · **because of / despite** + noun. One conjunction per link. No *will* after *when/if*.
@hi FANBOYS · जोड़ी वाले योजक · unless = if not · because + clause, because of + संज्ञा · एक जोड़ में एक योजक · when/if के बाद will नहीं।
:::

 (a) Because (b) Although (c) Unless (d) So

 (a) Although (b) Despite (c) Despite of (d) Because

 (a) If (b) Unless (c) When (d) As
9. I will call you as soon as I ______ home.
 (a) will reach (b) reach (c) reached (d) am reaching
10. She works hard ______ she can support her family.
 (a) so that (b) because of (c) although (d) unless
11. Take a jacket ______ it gets cold.
 (a) unless (b) in case (c) although (d) so that
12. No sooner had the guests arrived ______ the lights went out.
 (a) then (b) when (c) than (d) that
13. Walk carefully lest you ______ slip.
 (a) will (b) should (c) should not (d) would not
14. Choose the correct sentence.
