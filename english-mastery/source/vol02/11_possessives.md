:::goals Learning Goals · सीखने के लक्ष्य – इस chapter के बाद आप
- show possession with **'s** and **s'** correctly;
  <span class="hl">'s और s' से "का/की/के" सही दिखाएँगे</span>
- choose between **'s** and **of** (*Riya's bag* vs *the door of the room*);
  <span class="hl">'s और of में सही चुनाव करेंगे</span>
- use **my/your/his** and **mine/yours/his** correctly;
  <span class="hl">my/your और mine/yours का फ़र्क़ समझेंगे</span>
- write **joint** and **separate** possession (*Ram and Sita's house*);
  <span class="hl">साझा और अलग-अलग स्वामित्व लिखेंगे</span>
- use time expressions like *a week's leave, two hours' journey*.
  <span class="hl">समय के साथ possessive (*a week's leave*) लगाएँगे</span>
:::

:::warm Warm-up · शुरुआत
*This is **Riya's** phone.* = This phone belongs to Riya. · *This phone is **hers**.*
@hi हिंदी में "रिया **का** फ़ोन", "उसका फ़ोन" कहते हैं। English में यह काम **'s** (*Riya's*), **of** (*the name of the shop*) और possessive शब्द (*her, hers*) करते हैं।
:::

## 11.1 Apostrophe + s · 's और s'

:::rule RULE – where to put the apostrophe
| Owner | Rule | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| singular noun | **'s** | the **boy's** bag (one boy) · my **sister's** room · **Ramesh's** shop |
| singular noun ending in -s | **'s** (usual) or **'** | **James's** car / **James'** car · the **boss's** office |
| plural noun ending in -s | **'** only | the **boys'** bags (many boys) · my **parents'** house · the **teachers'** room |
| plural noun **not** ending in -s | **'s** | the **children's** park · **women's** day · **men's** toilet · **people's** opinion |
| compound noun | **'s** on the **last** word | my **mother-in-law's** house · the **Prime Minister's** speech |

@hi **एक मालिक** → **'s**: *the boy's bag* (लड़के का बैग)। **-s वाले बहुवचन मालिक** → सिर्फ़ **'**: *the boys' bags* (लड़कों के बैग)। **बिना -s वाले बहुवचन** (*children, women, men, people*) → **'s**: *children's park*। यौगिक शब्द में **आख़िरी** शब्द पर 's: *my mother-in-law's house* (मेरी सास का घर)।
:::

:::example EXAMPLES – one letter, different meaning
- *my **friend's** wedding* = the wedding of **one** friend
- *my **friends'** wedding* = the wedding of **two or more** friends (they are marrying each other)
- *the **student's** marks* = one student · *the **students'** marks* = many students
@hi apostrophe की जगह से अर्थ बदलता है: *friend's* = एक दोस्त का, *friends'* = कई दोस्तों का। *student's marks* = एक छात्र के अंक, *students' marks* = सभी छात्रों के अंक।
:::

## 11.2 's or of? · 's लगाएँ या of?

:::rule RULE – choosing 's or "of"
| Use **'s** mainly with … | Use **of** mainly with … |
|---|---|
| **people and animals**: *Meena's idea, the dog's tail* | **things and places**: *the door **of** the car, the roof **of** the house* |
| **organisations, countries, cities**: *the company's profit, India's population* (of also OK) | **long owner phrases**: *the name **of** the man who called yesterday* |
| **time**: *today's paper, a week's holiday* | **parts, amounts, positions**: *the end **of** the film, the top **of** the page, a cup **of** tea* |
Compound nouns also work for things: *the car door, the kitchen window, the table leg*.

@hi **'s** आम तौर पर **लोगों और जानवरों** के साथ (*Meena's idea*), संस्था/देश के साथ (*India's population*) और **समय** के साथ (*today's paper* = आज का अख़बार)। **of** आम तौर पर **निर्जीव चीज़ों** के साथ (*the door of the car* = गाड़ी का दरवाज़ा), लंबे वाक्यांशों और हिस्से/मात्रा के साथ (*the end of the film, a cup of tea*)। चीज़ों के लिए दो संज्ञाएँ साथ भी चलती हैं: *the car door*।
:::

:::rule RULE – possessive with time and distance
**a day's** work · **a week's** leave · **today's** news · **tomorrow's** meeting · **two hours'** drive · **three months'** salary · **a stone's throw** (very near)
Compare: *a **two-hour** drive* (number + noun adjective, Ch 10) = *two **hours'** drive* – both correct.
@hi समय और दूरी के साथ भी possessive लगता है: *a week's leave* (एक हफ़्ते की छुट्टी), *two hours' drive* (दो घंटे का सफ़र), *a stone's throw* (बिल्कुल पास)। *a two-hour drive* भी सही है।
:::

## 11.3 Joint, separate and double possession · साझा, अलग और दोहरा स्वामित्व

:::rule RULE – two owners
- **Joint** (they own it together) → **'s on the last name only**: *Ram and Sita's house* (one house, both own it).
- **Separate** (each owns his/her own) → **'s on each name**: *Ram's and Sita's phones* (two different phones).
- **Double possessive** = *a/an/this/some + noun + of + possessive*: *a friend **of mine**, a cousin **of Priya's**, this book **of yours**.*
@hi **साझा:** दोनों का एक ही घर → आख़िरी नाम पर 's (*Ram and Sita's house*)। **अलग-अलग:** हर एक की अपनी चीज़ → दोनों नामों पर 's (*Ram's and Sita's phones*)। **"मेरा एक दोस्त"** = *a friend of mine* (*a my friend* नहीं)।
:::

## 11.4 Possessive adjectives and possessive pronouns · my और mine

:::rule RULE – my vs mine
| Person | Possessive **adjective** (+ noun) | Possessive **pronoun** (no noun) |
|---|---|---|
| I | **my** book | The book is **mine**. |
| you | **your** bag | The bag is **yours**. |
| he | **his** car | The car is **his**. |
| she | **her** phone | The phone is **hers**. |
| it | **its** tail | – (rarely used) |
| we | **our** house | The house is **ours**. |
| they | **their** tickets | The tickets are **theirs**. |
**No apostrophe** in any of these: ✗ *your's, her's, our's, their's, it's (for possession)*.

@hi **my, your, his, her, its, our, their** हमेशा संज्ञा से **पहले** आते हैं (*my book* = मेरी किताब)। **mine, yours, his, hers, ours, theirs** **अकेले** आते हैं, उनके बाद संज्ञा नहीं (*The book is mine* = किताब मेरी है)। इनमें कभी apostrophe नहीं लगता – *yours*, *your's* नहीं।
:::

:::compare COMPARE & UNDERSTAND – sound-alike pairs
| Possessive (belonging) | Short form (contraction) |
|---|---|
| **its** – *The bird hurt **its** wing.* | **it's** = it is / it has – ***It's** raining.* |
| **your** – *Is this **your** pen?* | **you're** = you are – ***You're** late.* |
| **their** – ***Their** house is big.* | **they're** = they are – ***They're** coming.* · **there** = place – *Sit **there**.* |
| **whose** – ***Whose** bag is this?* | **who's** = who is / who has – ***Who's** at the door?* |
@hi बोलने में एक जैसे, अर्थ अलग: *its* = उसका / *it's* = it is; *your* = तुम्हारा / *you're* = you are; *their* = उनका / *they're* = they are / *there* = वहाँ; *whose* = किसका / *who's* = who is।
:::

:::mistake COMMON MISTAKES – possessives
| ✗ Wrong | ✓ Right | Why · क्यों |
|---|---|---|
| This is my fathers car. | This is my **father's** car. | apostrophe needed · 's लगाइए |
| the girls's hostel | the **girls'** hostel | plural in -s → ' only · -s वाले बहुवचन में सिर्फ़ ' |
| the childrens' toys | the **children's** toys | *children* has no -s → 's · children में 's |
| Is this pen your's? | Is this pen **yours**? | no apostrophe in possessive pronouns · yours में ' नहीं |
| He is a my friend. | He is **a friend of mine** / **my friend**. | double possessive · "मेरा एक दोस्त" |
| the table's leg | the **leg of the table** / the **table leg** | things → *of* or compound · निर्जीव के लिए of |
| Ram's and Sita's house (one house) | **Ram and Sita's** house | joint ownership → 's once · साझा = एक बार 's |
| Who's car is this? | **Whose** car is this? | *whose* = belonging · किसका = whose |
:::

:::tip EXAM TIP · परीक्षा टिप
In error spotting, check: (1) **'s vs s'** – is the owner one or many? (2) **its/it's, whose/who's** (3) **yours/hers/theirs** without apostrophe (4) *a friend of mine* (not *a my friend* / *a friend of me*).
@hi जाँचिए: मालिक एक है या कई (*'s* या *s'*)? *its/it's*, *whose/who's* सही है? *yours* में apostrophe तो नहीं? *a friend of mine* सही रूप है।
:::

:::revision QUICK REVISION · जल्दी दोहराइए
- One owner **'s** · plural in -s **'** · *children's, women's* **'s** · compound: *mother-in-law's*.
- People/animals/time → **'s**; things/parts → **of** (or *car door*).
- Joint: *Ram and Sita's* · separate: *Ram's and Sita's* · double: *a friend of mine*.
- my/your/his/her/its/our/their + noun · mine/yours/his/hers/ours/theirs alone · **no apostrophe**.
@hi 's / s' · लोगों के लिए 's, चीज़ों के लिए of · साझा और अलग स्वामित्व · my + संज्ञा, mine अकेला · yours, its, whose में apostrophe नहीं।
:::
