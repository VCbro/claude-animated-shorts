# How a water motor works — script

Format: 1080x1920 (9:16), 60 fps, ~55.5 s. English narration (Kokoro `af_heart`, offline neural TTS).
Subject: a monoblock centrifugal pump ("water motor"): induction motor + impeller pump on one shaft.

Facts used:
- A 2-pole induction motor on 50 Hz mains has a synchronous speed of 3,000 rpm; with slip it runs at ~2,850-2,900 rpm.
- The stator's three-phase (or split-phase) windings make a rotating magnetic field; the rotor is dragged along (induction).
- A centrifugal impeller adds velocity; the volute's widening passage turns that velocity into pressure.
- A standard centrifugal pump cannot move air (air is ~800x lighter than water), so it must be primed.
