# Stage 0 — story and science

## Beat shape (works for almost every topic)

```
HOOK      0–3 s    the everyday action, photoreal person, close and fast
DIVE      3–8 s    camera enters the body through the obvious door (mouth, nose, skin, eye)
BEAT 1-3  8–35 s   three effects, ordered by time or by route through the body
TURN      35–42 s  the long-term picture (repeat it for years → what accumulates)
OUT       42–48 s  pull back out to the person; end on recovery or the choice
```

Order beats by **time after the action** ("10 seconds", "20 minutes", "1 year") when the topic has a timeline, otherwise by **route** (mouth → lungs → blood → heart → brain). Time-ordered reels get the "0 sec / 10 sec / 20 min" timestamp overlay in the editor.

## Fact rules

- Only state effects that are standard textbook / public-health-agency knowledge (WHO, CDC, NHS level). If unsure, describe the mechanism without a number.
- Mark every number you use in the beat table with its basis so the user can check it.
- Never show a disease the action does not cause, never imply one exposure causes a chronic disease.
- Health-risk topics end with what improves when the habit stops, if that is true.
- No needles-into-eyes, gore, open surgery or real-patient imagery. Inside-body visuals stay clean, glossy, "museum-quality medical visualization".

## Deliverable for Stage 0

A beat table — time range, scale level, what is seen, the fact behind it — then the voiceover script in the user's language, timed to the beats. Voiceover is about 2.5 words per second in Hindi, so a 45 s reel is about 100–115 words. Write it short, spoken, second person ("aapke lungs…").
