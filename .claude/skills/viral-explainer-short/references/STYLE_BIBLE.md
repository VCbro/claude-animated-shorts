# Style bible: dark 3D Hinglish explainer reel ("chori / scam / sach" format)

Measured on 2026-11-04 from two viral Instagram reels the user supplied: a petrol-pump "jump trick" meter fraud
(81.5 s) and a stolen phone traced by IMEI (72.9 s). Both are 720x1280, 30 fps, 48 kHz.
The reels are study material only. Never re-upload them, never copy their lines, shots or branding, and never
say their channel name. Every topic, script, shot, channel name and voice is our own.

## 1. Format
| | Measured | Our target |
|---|---|---|
| Size / fps | 720x1280, 30 fps | 1080x1920, 30 fps (2K 1440x2560 optional) |
| Length | 73-82 s | 45-80 s. 60-75 s is the sweet spot for this format |
| Loudness | **-14.0 LUFS** integrated (both) | -14 LUFS, peak ≤ -1 dBTP |
| Cuts | A: 31 visual cuts in 81 s (every 2.6 s); B: 11 hard cuts + continuous morphs | a new picture every 1.5-3 s; never hold one layout > 4 s |
| Luma | B median .05 (very dark), A median .18, p95 .37 / .81 | median .06-.20: dark ground, bright subject |
| Ground | rgb(9-13, 12-18, 11-18), near-black with a green-grey tint | `#0A0D0C` + soft radial lift |
| Accent | lime ≈ rgb(194,240,59) on everything "smart / true / you" | `#C4F23C` |
| Danger | red ≈ rgb(219,107,82) only for loss, thief, fine, blocked | `#E8573F` |
| Warm | out-of-focus amber bokeh for real-world places (bazaar, pump at night) | `Bokeh hue="warm"` |

## 2. Voice
- One male narrator, Hinglish, talks straight to the viewer as **"tum"** ("tumhara phone", "tum kya karoge?").
- Fast and punchy: ~3 words/s, sentences of 3-8 words, many one-word punches ("Gaye.", "Phas gaya.").
- Present tense, story first and lesson second. Numbers said in Hindi ("bees rupaye", "chaudah lakh").
- No intro and no "hello doston": the first word is the story.

## 3. Script beats (both reels follow this exactly)
| # | Beat | Time | Ref A (petrol) | Ref B (phone) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Hook**: a scene already in motion, then a twist with "par" | 0-4 s | someone says "zero dekh lo", then "par chori zero ke baad hoti hai" | a hand in a pocket and the phone is gone |
| 2 | **Stakes**: what you lose, concretely | 4-10 s | the ₹20 of fuel never reached the tank | photos, contacts, Mummy's call cut, SIM thrown away |
| 3 | **Name it**: "Ise kehte hain ___" / "par ek cheez chor bhool gaya" | ~10 s | "jump trick" | "IMEI, phone ka Aadhaar" |
| 4 | **Inside look**: an x-ray of the mechanism | 10-25 s | the chip inside the dispenser, where 1 L shown means 100 ml short | the motherboard, 15 digits rising out of it |
| 5 | **Scale math**: "Socho…" with counters | ~25 s | ₹20 a fill becomes ₹2,000 a year | blacklisted on every network within 24 h |
| 6 | **Proof**: real cases with a city on the dot map, plus the consequence | 25-50 s | machines seized in two cities, ₹50,000 fine, SEALED | a new SIM 3 days later, the network checks, "Phas gaya.", 14 lakh+ phones recovered |
| 7 | **"Toh 3 cheezein yaad rakho"**: three numbered actions | 50-65 s | watch 2 s, ask for the 5 L measure, complaint book + helpline | report + block portal, never insert a new SIM, *#06# |
| 8 | **Callback mantra**: 2-4 words shown as lime pills | 65-70 s | "Do second. Paanch litre." | "*#06#. Save karo." |
| 9 | **CTA** aimed at a specific person, plus a comment question | last 5-8 s | follow, comment, save, "jo bhi gaadi chalata hai usse bhejo" | "Mummy ko bhejo", then "Kabhi tumhara phone chori hua? Haan mila / Nahi mila" |

Why it goes viral: it protects the viewer's own money or phone, a named trick makes the viewer feel clever, it carries an
actionable 3-step list (saves), it gives a "send this to ___" reason (shares), and it ends on a yes/no question (comments).

## 4. Picture vocabulary
- **HUD on almost every shot.** Top-left: a pulsing lime dot and `PLACE · TIME` in mono caps ("BAZAAR · SHAAM 7:10",
  "PHONE KE ANDAR"). Top-right: a grey micro label ("CHORI KE BAAD") over a lime timer ("00:02", "DIN 3").
  The HUD is the story clock and the reason it feels like an investigation.
- **Kinetic captions.** Bold geometric sans in 2 lines, top-left (y ≈ 15-25 %), white with 1-2 keywords in lime
  (or red for loss). Words appear as they are spoken. Big standalone words like "14 lakh+" and "IMEI" get their own shot.
- **Chips.** Solid dark boxes with lime/red/white text ("JUMP TRICK", "₹50,000 FINE"), or lime pills with black text
  for the mantra.
- **3D hero objects.** Glossy "Blender product render" look: a dark PBR body, soft key light, a cool rim light, specular
  sweeps, a shallow DOF floor and a slow dolly or orbit. Shown as the real object (dispenser, scooter, phone, SIM, measuring can).
- **X-ray / inside.** A wireframe lime outline of the device, its parts drawn in thin lime lines, glowing dotted lime
  paths for the flow (fuel, data, money) and a perspective lime grid floor.
- **Dot-matrix India.** A halftone map in grey dots: city pings (lime dot + ring + mono label), red spread for
  "blacklist / every network", and an arc for movement between cities.
- **Phone UI mockups.** Contacts, incoming call, lock screen clock, dialer, chat, notification and a verdict card
  ("Request mil gayi", "Phone wapas mil gaya ✓").
- **Data walls.** Columns of IDs or numbers in mono, with one row highlighted lime or red ("Phas gaya." + a BLOCKED tag).
- **Stamps.** SEALED, BLOCKED and DARJ ✓, slammed in with a shake.
- **Numbered steps.** Lime discs 1-2-3 placed on the 3D scene.
- **End card.** A phone with your profile and Follow → Following ✓, or a yes/no poll on the phone.

## 5. Motion
- Hard cuts on new ideas. Inside a shot the camera always creeps: push 3-8 % per shot, a few degrees of 3D tilt drift,
  or a dolly in 3D. No static frames.
- Every hit (chip, stamp, counter, bubble) lands 0-3 frames before its word.
- Impacts: a flip shake decaying over 8 f, music dropping out for ~0.5 s, and a low boom.
- Transitions: a hard cut, plus a whoosh in the sound only. No fancy wipes.

## 6. Sound
- A minimal dark pulse bed (minor key, ~100-110 BPM, sub + filtered plucks + light hats), ducked under the voice.
- SFX: whoosh on cuts, soft UI clicks on pops, a boom on impacts, risers into reveals, beeps for dialers and counters.
- Silence works as a tool: the bed cuts out on the punch word.
